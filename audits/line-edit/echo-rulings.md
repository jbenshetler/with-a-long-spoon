# Echo rulings — the durable ledger (line edit, Stage 0)

Authorial rulings on cross-chapter echo candidates from
`audits/line-edit/echo-inventory.md` (which regenerates and holds no
decisions; this file does). Per-chapter line editors: **PROTECTED echoes must
never be flagged or varied**; **FIX-AT entries** are applied during the owning
chapter's review (keep the listed occurrences, vary/cut the rest — replacement
wording is drafted at review time and approved by the author); **IGNORE**
entries are settled noise. Rulings re-open only by the author.

**Scope (author ruling 2026-08-04): this line-edit pass covers Volume One
only** — the 49 chapters in the epub roster / STATUS.md. The echo inventory
spans all of `scenes/` (cross-volume repetition informs keeper choices), but
FIX-AT work is applied only in Volume One chapters. This ledger records no
rulings about chapters outside the volume — later volumes re-harvest and
re-rule against their then-current text.

## Batch 1 (ruled 2026-08-03)

1. **"cracked its two inches" — PROTECTED.** The dorm-room window, cracked
   two inches in winter against an unbalanced HVAC system: a set detail doing
   continuity work. The fixed wording is the fixed fact; do not vary.
2. **"a beat past" (8× / 7 ch) — FIX-AT.** Timing tic. Keep the 2–3 strongest
   occurrences (chosen at review time); vary the rest as their chapters come

3. **"the small private" (7× / 7 ch) — FIX-AT.** Same-shaped rendering each
   time; vary all but the strongest.
   **Applied 2026-08-06: `two-towels`** ("Something in her dropped, small and
   private." → "Something in her dropped.").
   **Open question raised at that review, not yet ruled:** the occurrences
   split into two kinds — four are Pace's recurring *tell*, "the small private

   holds, the real FIX-AT set is `seconds` alone. Awaiting an author ruling —
   until then treat the four smiles as unruled, not as licensed.
4. **"high on the inside of" (6× incl. a-round) — FIX-AT wording, PROTECT the

7. **"plain fact of" (6× / 6 ch) — FIX-AT.** Interiority formula; vary.
