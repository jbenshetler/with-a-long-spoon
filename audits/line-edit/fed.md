3. **Repetition — heat up the throat, lines 23 / 37.** "the heat started up her throat" and, fourteen lines later, the standalone "The heat came up in her throat." The second is the chapter's emotional payoff; the first spends it early with nearly the same wording. 7. **Repetition — "A beat, fond" (31) / "A beat, warm" (63).** The same fragment-beat construction twice, and line 63 already has "She looked at her, warm" two sentences earlier — "warm" lands three times in lines 63–67. 8. **Overwork — the not-admitting move done twice, lines 61 / 69.** "told herself none of it meant anything in particular, and had known, even telling herself, that it was a lie of a particular size" (61) and "how much she'd wanted exactly that, she didn't quite admit either" (69). Same interiority mechanism (self-deception named as such) twice in nine lines; the line-61 version is the stronger. Recommend cutting or recasting the "she didn't quite admit either" clause at line 69.

9. **Rhythm — line 69 ends one beat late.** "The friend she'd wanted at fifteen and stopped expecting to get" is the paragraph's true last sentence; the trailing "Who liked her, some days, better than she managed to like herself — and how much she'd wanted exactly that, she didn't quite admit either" extends past it and overlaps with finding 8. Recommend ending the paragraph at "get" (or at most at "herself").

10. **Precision — "gone still and full listening," line 23.** "Randi had gone still and full listening" — "full listening" reads as a grammatical near-miss rather than compression; the chapter's fragments elsewhere are cleaner. Recommend adjusting so "full" and "listening" don't jam ("still" alone may carry it).
