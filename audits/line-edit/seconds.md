## Findings

1. **`:3`** — "Outside the glass the yard was still dark. A pale moon was coming up over the black line of the mountains … and the porch glass held all of it: the dark yard, the moon, her own face faintly doubled in it where the angle caught."
   *Class: overwork.* The colon-list re-inventories the two images the preceding sentences just placed; only the doubled face is new.
   *Rec:* Cut the re-listed "the dark yard, the moon" so the reflection arrives without a recap.

2. **`:3`** — "her own face faintly doubled in it where the angle caught"
   *Class: word precision.* "faintly" softens the one image in the paragraph that should be exact, and "where the angle caught" explains the optics after the doubling has landed.
   *Rec:* Drop one of the two hedges — the modifier or the explanatory clause.

3. **`:5`** — "smelled like him because it had been on his bed before he had put it out here for the cooler months"
   *Class: overwork.* The smell is the fact; the causal provenance is housekeeping, and it flattens the sentence's best clause ("the steady weight of a man who was not in a hurry to be anywhere else") by delaying it behind admin.
   *Rec:* Cut the "because…" sourcing and let the quilt just smell like him.

4. **`:11`** — "she did not know the rule was a rule until she had bumped against it from the inside, and now she knew"

   *Rec:* End the clause on "from the inside."

5. **`:17`** — "His hand was on her shin under the quilt; the cup was warm against her palm."

   *Rec:* Cut the hand half of the sentence and let the cup carry the pre-speech stillness.

6. **`:23`** — "She had thought she would have to find the sentence in the moment, that the actual saying would require her to find the words again, and she found instead that the words were already there in her mouth where she had been keeping them. She did not have to search."
