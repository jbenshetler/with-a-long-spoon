## Findings

1. **fix** — Line 17: "she had Vee by the arm before the girl who'd let her in was even out of the room." Contradicts line 13, where the blonde "said, and went" — she has already left the room, and Brooke has since entered and had a whole exchange with Vee (line 15). By the time Randi comes down the stairs, the greeter cannot still be on her way out. Recommended fix: re-anchor the timing beat to someone actually present (e.g., before Brooke is out of the room) or cut the comparison.

2. **clarification** — Line 127: "both of them still frayed from their separate bad days." Vee's bad day is the scene; Randi's is not established anywhere in it — she is mid-unpacking and ends the visit laughing, and her New Year story is weeks old (the only shadow is the "we went up" swerve, which is not "a day"). A reader will stumble on what Randi's bad day was. Recommended fix: either seed a small sign of Randi's own frayed day earlier, or reword so the fray attaches to something the scene has shown.

3. **nitpick** — Line 13: "'I'll get her,' she said, and went." No name has been spoken; the greeter is a stranger to Vee, yet knows exactly whom the crisis belongs to. Defensible (a sorority sister may know Randi's friends by sight), but a beat of recognition would close the gap. Recommended fix: optionally give the greeter one flicker of recognizing Vee as Randi's.

4. **nitpick** — Line 49: "pushed the wet hair off Vee's face." Vee's hair being wet is unestablished (no rain or snow on the walk; tears wet a face more than hair), and "wet hair" was just used of the blonde at the door (line 13), inviting a cross-wire. Recommended fix: tie the wetness to the crying ("tear-stuck") or drop "wet."

5. **nitpick** — Line 95: "she was up and reaching for her coat, the day's coat." Vee is "inside her coat" in the front hall (line 15) and is never shown taking it off upstairs, so the reach for it lands on an object the reader last saw on her body. Recommended fix: show or imply the coat coming off when she settles on the bed.

6. **nitpick** — Line 11: "Cassie's side was stripped and bare, her family off in another state and not due back till the weekend." Grammatically the family is off and the family is not due back; the intended subject of "not due back" is Cassie. Recommended fix: reword so Cassie is the one away with her family and the one due back.

7. **nitpick** — Line 45: "he didn't call you and you didn't call him" vs. lines 9/23, where the silence is framed as texting ("He hadn't texted. Not once"). Colloquial "call" plausibly covers reaching out, but a literal reader could hear a new fact (no phone calls either) being asserted by Randi, who is only paraphrasing Vee. Recommended fix: none required, or align the verb with "texted."
