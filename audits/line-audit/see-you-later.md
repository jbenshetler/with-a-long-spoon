# Line audit — see-you-later (2026-07-31)

**Verdict:** 6 issues found — 2 fix, 2 clarification, 2 nitpick.

1. **Seating contradiction (fix).** Opening paragraph: "took her usual two-thirds of the way back, **on the aisle**, with Cassie beside her." Later: "Randi dropped into the seat on Vee's other side, **the aisle side**, so that Vee was between her and Cassie now." If Vee is in the aisle seat, there is no seat on her aisle side for Randi to drop into — and the seats are "bolted in pairs," so a third adjacent seat isn't obviously there either. Compounding it: Randi is "already sidestepping in past two annoyed people," which describes entering mid-row, not taking an aisle-end seat reachable directly from the aisle. The planned geometry (condensed brief: Randi takes the aisle seat, Vee between her and Cassie) requires Vee to be one seat in, not on the aisle. Recommended fix: change the opening so Vee's usual seat is one in from the aisle (or drop "on the aisle"), and reconcile the "past two annoyed people" sidestep with wherever Randi's seat actually is.

2. **"Morning" at ~12:15 (fix).** Closing beat: "Randi turned and went up Draper in the bright cool **morning**." Class ends at "eleven-fifteen" (Randi's own line), then two blocks' walk, ordering, and "the best hour she'd had in months" at the high table — the sidewalk parting is roughly 12:15–12:30, past noon. Recommended fix: change "morning" to a time-neutral or midday word.

3. **"That house" has no antecedent (clarification).** "Randi wasn't only in **that house**, she was near the top of it" — no house, sorority, or Greek-life mention precedes this in the scene; the conversation on the page only established that Randi has her own room. Reads like an editing seam where the sorority came up in trimmed material. Recommended fix: add a one-beat surface mention (Randi naming or gesturing at the house) before Vee's inference, or rephrase the inference so it doesn't point at an unintroduced referent.

4. **Oat-milk latte vs. "the lavender thing" (clarification).** "Randi had ordered her an oat-milk latte" is immediately followed by Randi handing it over with "They had the lavender thing." A reader can reconcile these as one drink (lavender oat-milk latte), but on first pass it reads as two different drinks named for the same cup. Recommended fix: make the drink one thing in both sentences (e.g., name it once).

5. **Ceramic-coded cup dropped in a trash bin (nitpick).** Interior described as "the tables and the cups all white" with spoons ringing "against the insides of cups" — for-here ceramic coding — yet Vee walks out "holding an empty white cup" and "dropped the cup in the bin on the corner." Works only if hers was a to-go cup, which nothing establishes. Recommended fix: mark Vee's cup as paper/to-go at the handoff, or lose the bin drop.

6. **Chronology metadata mismatch (nitpick, meta not prose).** `meta/meta-plan-chronology.md` line 46 lists `present: Vee, Randi`, but Cassie is a full on-page participant (and Meg is named in flashback). Recommended fix: add Cassie to the entry's `present:` field.
