   - Severity: **fix** (authorial decision required — surface, don't silently resolve).
   - Recommendation: either move the chronology pin to a late-September Wednesday (and re-space the following scenes), or change the scene's "late-September" season dressing (dogwood rust, turned maple) and "Wednesday-afternoon" to match early-September Tuesday; the "four weeks in" line should be reconciled with whichever date wins.

   - Severity: **clarification**.
