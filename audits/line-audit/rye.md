Not a contradiction (it can read as Cassie riffing on the true-crime cliché), but it faintly signals a later day than the chronology gives the scene.
   *Recommended fix:* either accept as Cassie's genre-parody register or swap in a same-day marker; no prose change strictly required.

   *Recommended fix:* none required if the doubling is intentional; otherwise move the kit beat's "got to the top" phrasing so the summit arrives once.
