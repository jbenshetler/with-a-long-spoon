1. **"How old are you?" she asked … "Twenty-two." / "You're not." / "I am." / "You're getting a doctorate."** (lines 91–103, and the incredulity running through 111)
   Cross-scene contradiction: in `scenes/what-to-wear.md:21` (Thu Oct 1, nineteen days before this scene) Vee tells Kayla and Meg, "He's getting his doctorate. Math. Twenty-two and almost finished, which is obnoxious" — she already knows his age and has bragged about it. Here she asks as if for the first time and reacts with disbelief. The chronology entry for {{Rock}} does plan the college-at-fifteen talk for this scene, so the conflict is between two drafted scenes and needs an authorial call.
   Severity: **fix.** Recommended: reframe the beat so she's pulling the how (college at fifteen), not discovering the age/doctorate combination she already knew — or surface the conflict to the author for which scene owns the reveal.

2. **"…and found, high on the inside of his thigh, a place where the skin was not like the rest. A seam of it, old, raised and smooth, longer than her hand. Her fingers went along it before she thought about whether to."** (line 247)
   The scar is rendered as a fresh discovery, but per `scenes/famished.md:79` (First Night, Fri Oct 9) she already found and traced it and he moved her hand away; `scenes/we-find-out.md:91` later confirms "the one she had found that first night and traced." The chronology entry for {{Rock}} itself says she "asks about the scar she's traced since First Night." The deferral beat ("Not tonight") is planned and fine; the discovery framing is not.
   Severity: **fix.** Recommended: adjust the touch so it reads as returning to a known place (and first asking aloud), not first contact.

3. **"All evening she had heard him undersell things — the hall, the fifteen, the dormitory full of men — and this was that same voice…"** (line 213)
   Sequence error: he does not say "I was fifteen in a dormitory full of men" until line 229, sixteen paragraphs later, in the lifting exchange. At line 213 the only dormitory image so far is Vee's own narration (line 127), which he never voiced, so she cannot have "heard him undersell" it yet.
   Severity: **fix.** Recommended: drop the dormitory item from the line-213 list or swap in something he has actually said by that point (e.g., the tutor arrangement).

4. **"She set the mug down on the low table." (line 151) … "She smiled down into the mug … She turned the mug." (line 201) … "She set the mug on the table" (line 217)**
   Prop continuity: her mug is set down at 151 and never shown picked back up, yet is in her hands at 201 and set down a second time at 217.
   Severity: **nitpick.** Recommended: add a pickup beat before 201 or cut one of the two set-downs.

5. **"His hand moved up from her ankle, under the quilt…"** (line 245)
   His hand was placed on her ankle at line 89, but since then she has repositioned twice — shoulder to his arm (151), then fitted along his side with his arm coming around her (217, 221) — and the original posture had her feet tucked under his thigh (5). Two hands can technically cover it, but a reader tracking bodies may stumble over whether the ankle hand is still plausibly there after she turned along his side.
   Severity: **nitpick.** Recommended: re-anchor the hand on the ankle (or another location) somewhere after the line-217 reposition so the line-245 move has a live antecedent.
