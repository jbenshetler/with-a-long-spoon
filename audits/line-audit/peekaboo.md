Severity: **nitpick**. Recommended fix: none required; if desired, tie the later sentence back to the earlier failure (found-by-touch what the eye couldn't) so the pair reads as designed rather than forgotten.

She is barefoot with the hem pooling on the floor; on a step, the pooled length would keep the lower calf/ankle partly draped, so "whole leg … calf to hip" slightly overstates what the slit can show at this height. Severity: **nitpick**. Recommended fix: none required, or trim the exposure claim to match the un-heeled hem (mid-calf up, say).
