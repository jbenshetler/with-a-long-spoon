## Findings

1. **Drawer inventory contradicts the unpacking.** Line 7: "the brush and the cream and the cardboard form of green scrunchies and the loose pins, a whole drawer of her set down in a clean man's house" — these items *live in the drawer* and "never had to be packed at all." Line 33: "she knelt over the bag at last to put herself away into the drawer, the cream and the pins, the few things that still traveled." The cream and the pins can't both permanently live in the drawer and travel in the bag the same night. **Severity: fix.** Recommended: change one of the two lists so the traveling items and the resident items are disjoint (e.g., name different small items at line 33).

2. **The re-scenting schedule collides with Thanksgiving break.** Line 53: "She'd carry it back to the dorm Sunday night full of him, and sleep in him through the nights that weren't his, and bring it out next Friday gone faint again to be filled." Per `meta-plan-chronology.md`, this scene is Fri Nov 20; Thanksgiving break runs Sat Nov 21–Sun Nov 29 with "Vee home with her mother," and the next rendered return is Sun Nov 29 ({{Cropped}}). So Sunday Nov 22 she'd go home, not to the dorm, and "next Friday" (Nov 27) she's mid-break at her mother's, not driving out to Pace's. **Severity: fix.** Recommended: either loosen the sentence to the habitual pattern without the concrete "dorm Sunday night / next Friday" anchors, or have her anticipation acknowledge break (the chronology, which owns dates, would otherwise need to move the scene).

3. **Cross-doc: "five nights" apart vs. {{In His Hands}} placement.** Line 9: "It had been five nights" (apart since ~Sun Nov 15). The chronology dates {{In His Hands}} — an overnight, in-bed scene — "~mid-to-late November · the week before Thanksgiving break," which reads as the week of Nov 16–20, inside the five-night gap. If {{In His Hands}} is meant as the prior weekend (Nov 14–15) there is no conflict, but the chronology phrasing doesn't pin it. **Severity: clarification** (cross-doc, not a prose error). Recommended: pin {{In His Hands}}'s date in the chronology to on-or-before Sun Nov 15.

4. **"a slug of the wine" (line 21) precedes the wine's introduction.** The definite article points at a wine the reader hasn't met; the Nebbiolo and its pouring arrive only at line 25 ("She'd been drinking the wine he'd poured her, a Nebbiolo, he'd said"). A first-pass reader briefly has no antecedent. **Severity: nitpick.** Recommended: introduce the bottle/pour before the sauce paragraph or make line 21's reference indefinite.

5. **"The mushroom gone soft and giving, holding the wine" (line 29).** The wine went into the tomato reduction (line 21); the mushrooms were sliced raw and scattered on top (line 23), above the sauce, for a fifteen-minute bake. "Holding the wine" implies the mushrooms absorbed it, which the assembly as staged doesn't quite support. **Severity: nitpick.** Recommended: attribute the wine note to the sauce, or stage the mushrooms in contact with it.
