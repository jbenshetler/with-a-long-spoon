## Findings

1. **"The bartender, the older lady, closing the place down that night without looking up from the glasses…"** (line 45). The antecedent of "that night" is genuinely ambiguous within this scene: the only night named earlier is "the night of the dance" (line 19), but the quoted lines (*he doesn't take advantage… wasn't him they were jealous of*) belong to the first bar night, which is never identified here. A reader can wrongly bind "that night" to the dance. Severity: **clarification**. Recommended fix: anchor the referent (e.g., identify it as the first night at the bar) or drop the demonstrative.

2. **"going on six in the morning there, and Randi would still be up; glamorous, wild Randi wouldn't go to bed on New Year's Eve, not out there"** (line 27). At ~6 a.m. in Gstaad it is already January 1 — Randi's midnight passed six hours ago, and Vee's framing ("wouldn't go to bed on New Year's Eve") never registers that Randi's new year has already turned. The arithmetic itself ("six hours ahead") is correct (EST→CET), and staying up through the night is a coherent reading, so this is plausibly in-character credulity rather than an error. Severity: **nitpick**. Recommended fix: none required; if wanted, a half-beat acknowledging Randi's midnight already came would close the seam.
