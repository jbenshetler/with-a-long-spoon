## 7. CONFLICTS & CONTRADICTIONS

### CONFLICT 1: Circumstances of the First Kiss (Chapter 8 vs. Chapter 32)
* **Chapter 8 establishes**: Under the tree on the campus path, Pace stops and turns to Vee. Vee speaks her personal rule: *"I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days."* Pace does not ask to kiss her; he smiles, drops the matter, and begins to walk away. Vee stops him and initiates the kiss herself:
  > *"He didn't argue. He didn't do anything she'd braced for — no lean, no charm turned up, no patient little wait for her to change her mind. He just smiled, like she'd told him something true and fine about herself, and let it close, and started them gently back along the path, her hand drawn along with his. She didn't go... 'But,' she said... 'I'm making an exception.'"* (Ch 8)
  *(Reaffirmed by Vee in Ch 9: "He was already walking away and I pulled him back and I did it. Me"; in Ch 10: "I kissed him. Two days I'd known him... and I just did it"; and in Ch 13: "I told him flat out I don't kiss a man I've known two days. Then I backed him into a tree and we made out.")*
* **Chapter 32 contradicts**: The text states that Pace asked if he could kiss her:
  > *"He looked at her the way he had looked at her that first evening, under the tree, when he'd asked if he could kiss her and she'd told him she didn't kiss a man she'd known a few days, and he had smiled as though she'd handed him something true and fine about herself."* (Ch 32)
* **Finding**: Direct contradiction. In Chapter 8, Pace never asked to kiss her and began walking away; in Chapter 32, the narrative states he asked if he could kiss her.

### CONFLICT 2: Day of Week of Chapter 1 vs. Chapter 2 & Chapter 10
* **Chapter 1 establishes**: Randi arrives at Pace's house after chapter dinner:
  > *"I left right after chapter dinner, Pace. I told Brooke I had a paper." / "Do you?" / "It's not due until Thursday."* (Ch 1)
* **Chapter 2 establishes**: Randi returns to the sorority house the morning after Chapter 1 in "last night's clothes" on a **Saturday morning**, and Brooke references seeing her leave "last night" (Friday night):
  > *"wanted to be at the stairs and up them before the house registered that she was only now coming in, in last night's clothes, on a Saturday... Saturday morning, before ten... Brooke looked up... 'I was going to ask about it last night and you'd already left.'"* (Ch 2)

  > *"'We missed you Thursday,' Brooke said to Randi, warm, and it was only friendly, and still it set down on the table the fact of a Thursday Vee knew nothing about..."* (Ch 10)
* **Finding**: Chronological mismatch. If Chapter 1 occurred on the night of chapter dinner and Randi returned Saturday morning in "last night's clothes" (Friday night), Chapter 1 took place Friday. If Chapter 1 took place after chapter dinner on Thursday (as Brooke states in Ch 10 and Randi's paper comment in Ch 1 implies), Randi would have returned Friday morning, contradicting Chapter 2's explicit "on a Saturday" setting.

Do yetis shop here?' Randi laughed, and went to top it. Vee lost sight of her in the sale racks; when she looked up Randi was already coming back down the aisle toward her... in pink rhinestones... Vee lost it — laughed till her legs quit and plopped onto the try-on bench."* (Ch 23)
* **Chapter 24 contradicts**: Randi tells Pace that Vee actually put the shoe on, walked down the aisle, stomped, and growled:
  > *"'She's ragging on my feet all afternoon — yeti this, sasquatch that — so she finds the biggest, ugliest shoe in the place, a canoe, a sole like a snow tire, and gets it on, and does the whole walk for me. The stomping, arms out, the little growl. The full bigfoot.' The laugh climbed. 'And there's an older couple stopped dead at the end of the aisle the whole time, watching, and she has no idea — she's mid-stomp — and then she turns around and sees them.' Randi pushed her face into his chest. 'She went so red I thought she'd go up.'"* (Ch 24)
* **Finding**: Factual discrepancy between the scene as narrated in Chapter 23 (where Vee never put the shoe on her foot or walked in it) and Randi's dialogue in Chapter 24. *(Note: This may represent in-character embellishment by Randi, but is recorded here as an objective factual divergence in the text).*

### CONFLICT 4: Identity of the Date at the Rusty Nail (Chapter 26/28 vs. Chapter 49)
* **Chapters 26 & 28 establish**: On Thursday night before Thanksgiving, Pace brings **Vee** (who has copper-red hair) to the Rusty Nail. Sheri hugs Pace when they enter, and Vee becomes jealous of the greeting:
  > *"two women came off the rail with their arms already open, and there was a round of hugs... This is Vee, he said, each time... One of them kept a hand on his arm and tipped her head at Vee. 'Did he tell you he taught the class?'"* (Ch 26)
  > *"Sheri rode on his left... 'After you left Thursday,' Sheri said, 'I kept an eye on her.' ... 'Vee,' he said. 'Vivienne.'"* (Ch 28)
* **Chapter 49 contradicts**: Sheri refers to the woman Pace brought dancing whom she hugged hello as a **brunette** from the sorority:
  > *"'Anyhow.' She waved a fry, closing the folder. 'That brunette you brought dancing before Thanksgiving — the sorority one. Easy on the eye, I'll give her that.' A pull at the straw. 'Bet she's a lot, though.' / 'You met her?' / 'Y'all came in dancing, I hugged you hello. She looked at me like I was one more of your harem applicants. Never did learn my name. But I got to see her.'"* (Ch 49)
  *(Later in the exact same scene, Sheri distinguishes Vee separately: "I have never seen you gone on a girl like you're gone on this redhead. / Her name is Vee.")*
* **Finding**: Direct contradiction. In Chapter 26 and Chapter 28, the date Pace walked in with whom Sheri hugged hello was Vee (the redhead). In Chapter 49, Sheri claims the date she hugged hello when they entered dancing was "that brunette... the sorority one" (Randi), while treating the redhead as an entirely separate woman she saw only briefly at the bar.
