## CONFLICT

### Shoe-shopping incident: who wore the joke shoe and was embarrassed?

**Direct scene — Vee presents the giant shoe; Randi wears the pink platforms; the couple merely watches both women:**

> “A minute later Vee came back from the end wall with a single shoe the size of a canoe, a sole like a snow tire, and set it down in front of her without a word. ‘Found your size. Do yetis shop here?’” (Ch. 23)

> “When she looked up Randi was already coming back down the aisle toward her, gone a full head taller and taking it as her usual altitude: six-inch platforms bedazzled toe to ankle in pink rhinestones…” (Ch. 23)

> “Somewhere in it she caught an older couple stopped by the boots to watch them — a little flush, there and gone, just two girls being silly over shoes.” (Ch. 23)

**Randi’s later account — Vee puts on the giant shoe, performs a Bigfoot walk, notices the couple, and becomes intensely embarrassed:**

> “She's ragging on my feet all afternoon — yeti this, sasquatch that — so she finds the biggest, ugliest shoe in the place, a canoe, a sole like a snow tire, and gets it *on*, and does the whole walk for me. The stomping, arms out, the little growl. The full bigfoot.” (Ch. 24)

> “And there's an older couple stopped dead at the end of the aisle the whole time, watching, and she has no idea — she's mid-stomp — and then she turns around and *sees them*. She went so red I thought she'd go up. Hand over her mouth, wanting the floor to take her.” (Ch. 24)

These versions cannot both be literal accounts of the same event. Chapter 24 may depict Randi deliberately reshaping the story for Pace, but the factual ledger must preserve the contradiction rather than decide that intention.
