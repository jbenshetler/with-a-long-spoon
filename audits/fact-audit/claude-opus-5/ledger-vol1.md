## IX. CONFLICTS — findings

**CONFLICT 1 — the date count.**
- Reading A (Ch.11, internal): *"Girls in trucks. What kind of. Three dates. Three dates and you'd — your mother."* The hike is the third outing (froyo, dinner, hike) — internally consistent.
- Reading B (Ch.26, after the dance): *"She was angry about the girls, and angrier that she'd let herself be played: three dates, and she'd spread her legs like a slut."* By Ch.26 the outings are at minimum froyo (6), dinner (8), hike (11), the house/fitting (14–15), the first night (18), the afternoon (21), the porch night (25), the dance (26). "Three dates" is not supportable at Ch.26.
- Additional strain: at Ch.26 Vee and Pace have already had sex (Ch.18, 19, 21), so "spread her legs" as a single recent event is also off. **Both readings recorded. Marked CONFLICT.**

**CONFLICT 2 — Randi's first encounter with Vee: who apologised.**
- Ch.3 (Pace watching): the collision is Randi's engineered work — *"it was the redhead's hand that flew to her own mouth. It was the redhead who pulled up short, stricken, apologizing."* Then *"Randi's hand went to the girl's arm — no, no, my fault, it's fine."*
- Ch.4 (Vee's memory): *"it had genuinely not been clear, one of those collisions where both people apologize and neither one did it. Vee had looked up to say sorry and forgotten to."*
- These are reconcilable as differing perceptions (that is the design — Randi's execution "looked like nothing"), but note Ch.4's "Vee had looked up to say sorry and **forgotten to**" sits against Ch.3's explicit "It was the redhead who… apologiz[ed]." **Recorded as a soft conflict / possible intentional unreliability.**

**CONFLICT 3 — Vee's statement about Meg in Ch.4 vs. Ch.13.**
- Ch.4: Vee was with "Meg from her floor, dark-haired, easygoing Meg" outside the dining hall.
- Ch.3: Randi's target is walking "with another girl," unnamed, and after the collision "the friend laughed too."
- No conflict; recorded for completeness because Ch.3 and Ch.4 render the same scene from two POVs and the friend is unnamed in one.
