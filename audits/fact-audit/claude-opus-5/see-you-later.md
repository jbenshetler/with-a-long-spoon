- **"the wrong textbook back in her bag"** with no mention of the right one. Omission, not conflict. Rejected.
- **Class time, "yesterday," "See you Friday," "eleven-fifty," "a lab at noon," "two hours."** Out of scope per instructions (dates, weekdays, intervals) — not evaluated.
