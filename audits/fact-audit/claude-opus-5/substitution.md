## FINDING 1 — Randi's seat at the table

**WHERE**
> "She picked up her coffee and held it between both hands and looked **across the table at Randi**, who had her laptop open in front of her and was scrolling something"

**AGAINST** (same chapter, two later passages, plus Pace's seat)
> "**Beside her, Randi's hand moved to her coffee cup.** Then Vee looked at the man who was looking at her."

> "He turned back to Vee. **Beside her, Randi's hand went to her coffee cup**, held it with both hands a second longer than the sip required, then set it down."

> "He pulled out the empty chair at their table without dragging it and sat." … "**He sat across from her** and listened."

**WHY** Randi cannot be across the table from Vee and beside Vee, and the seat across from Vee is explicitly given to Pace once he sits — so the opening placement and the two "beside her" beats describe two different tables.

**CONFIDENCE** MEDIUM — "across the table" can just barely be read as "over the tabletop" rather than as a seating position, but the chapter uses "beside her" twice and hands the opposite chair to Pace, which is the signature of one line not being updated.
