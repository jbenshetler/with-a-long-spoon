## NOTED (not errors)

- **"a low sheet of water leaping the whole gap to break light over Vee's head" vs. "It barely got her."** — The likeliest edit-seam in the chapter, so I read it closely. The very next clause reconciles them on the page: "outrage out of all proportion to the actual amount of water, which was the point." The sheet is rendered for its *visual* size ("break light"), the follow-up for its *volume*. Both can hold; rejected.
- **Geometry of the splash.** Cassie is "as far as Cassie went," at mid-ribs, and splashes "from ten feet off"; Vee then crosses "the ten feet of deep… into the shallow." That requires Cassie to be standing essentially at the drop-off, which is what "as far as Cassie went" means. Consistent; rejected.
- **Cassie's word count.** She speaks ~30 words here, against Ch 4's "says about four words a day." That is Vee's hyperbole about a temperament, not a stated fact; rejected.
- **"Who designed this? Genuinely. What man?" vs. "it didn't bother her one bit."** — The complaint is about the garment's construction, not about how she looks in it; the text distinguishes these in the same paragraph. Rejected.
- **"it fit" vs. "there'd been less of her to fill it."** — Same suit at two body sizes; "it fit, and it gave her nowhere to hide" is the point of the line, not a size claim contradicting the ledger's "quit when her body developed." Rejected.
- **Towel on "the bench," later "up on the deck with the rolled towel."** — A poolside bench is on the deck; also distinct from the Ch 1 object of that name, which is not in play here. Rejected.
- **Bleachers occupied by hecklers**, vs. Ch 37's bleachers "folded back against the wall on weekdays." Different visits; Ch 7 does not assert a weekday. Rejected (and the day itself is out of scope).
- **No lane lines**, vs. Ch 37's lane lines and "third lane in from the wall." Ch 7 shows open-swim use; nothing asserts lanes are permanent. Rejected.
- **"Legs for *days*"** aimed at Vee, where the ledger gives "long dancer's legs" to Randi. Vee is recorded as tall and lavishly built; long legs are not attributed exclusively. Rejected.
- **Lifeguard "at the desk to check" vs. "the lifeguard's radio"** heard from the water. One is the door check, one is ambient room sound; no claim they are the same post. Rejected.
- **"fifty yards of held breath"** for the walk past the bleachers — figurative measure of the walk's duration, and no ledger entry fixes the deck's length. Rejected.
- **Presence/knowledge.** Only Vee and Cassie are present; nothing here shows either knowing anything not yet learned by this point (no reference to Pace, Randi, or the bench). Nothing to flag.
