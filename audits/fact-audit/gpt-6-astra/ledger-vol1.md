**Unresolved facts and attribution limits**

- **Exclusivity:** No explicit agreement establishing exclusivity between Pace and Vee is shown. Vee repeatedly believes she is his only partner; the reader knows of his concurrent involvement with Randi. The text does not establish when, or whether, that involvement ends. **Chs. 24, 28, 38, 46, 49, 51–52.**
- **Randi’s assurances about other women:** Her assertion to Vee that Pace has no other woman is an assurance from a concealed participant, not reliable independent evidence of exclusivity. **Ch. 51.**
- **Pace’s scar:** Location and texture are known; cause, treatment, and origin remain undisclosed. “Surgeon’s line” is descriptive wording, not an account of the event that caused it. **Chs. 18, 25, 32, 35.**
- **Randi’s bracelet:** Its constant wear and locked fit are established; giver, occasion, and significance are not. **Chs. 17, 47.**
- **Randi’s money:** The project links an adverse record to her father in Vee’s understanding. No full company name, sum, adjudication, or proof that Randi participated in wrongdoing is supplied. **Chs. 40, 48.**
- **Thermostat preparation:** Pace’s house is repeatedly warm; Vee repeatedly attributes this to advance preparation for her. Narration from Pace’s side also establishes that he likes maintaining warmth. Exact adjustments and settings are not shown. **Chs. 1, 14–16, 24, 46, 50.**
- **Wood species:** The initial den furniture Vee examines is pale white oak. Pace later recounts her examining a walnut chair. The latter is not explicitly identified as the same piece or the same visit, so these are not necessarily contradictory. **Chs. 14, 28.**
- **Retold shoe-store antics:** Ch. 23 shows Vee presenting an oversized shoe and later noticing an older couple; Randi’s Ch. 24 account adds Vee wearing it and doing a bigfoot imitation. That added action is not shown in the first account, but omission alone does not prove it could not have happened.
- **Mouth kisses:** Vee initially explains Randi’s kiss as cultural friendliness, then increasingly participates and extends it. No explicit mutual conversation naming a romantic relationship between them occurs. **Chs. 17, 20, 27, 30, 34, 39, 47, 51.**
- **Parents at the induction:** Vee initially expects her mother to attend and father to miss it for work. The ceremony chapter explicitly places Cassie there but does not state whether her mother attends. **Chs. 11, 31.**
- **John:** New Haven law study and a city residence are his statements. Vee’s later billionaire image is her invention; no billionaire status is established. **Chs. 47–48, 51.**

**CONFLICT C1 — Gown’s hem without shoes**

- **Ch. 21:** “The hem pooled at her feet; the dress wanted a height she wasn't giving it, and more of one than she owned.”
- **Ch. 23:** “She came out of the bathroom in it, bare-shouldered, the hem just off the floor without the shoes, and stood in the middle of the room and let Randi look.”

The completed gown pools around her bare feet in one scene and clears the floor without shoes in the other. No intervening alteration is reported. Ch. 31 again makes the correct hem position dependent on the heels.

**CONFLICT C2 — Statistics-room desks**

- **Ch. 4:** “the seats bolted in pairs to a shared table that ran the length of each tier, chairs that swung out and swung back and went nowhere else”
- **Ch. 30:** “Then Randi shifted beside her, and her knee came to rest against Vee's knee under the little fold-out desk”

The recurring lecture room is furnished with continuous shared tables in one description and a small folding desk in the other.

**CONFLICT C3 — Whether Vee has asked about Pace’s scar**

- **Ch. 25:** “Her fingers went along it before she thought about whether to.  
  ‘This?’ she said.”
  
  Pace answers: “‘Not tonight,’ he said.”
- **Ch. 35:** “the old scar was a paler line in the gloom that she'd still never asked about and still meant to keep”

She has asked about it, although he has not answered. The scar’s origin remaining unknown is consistent; the assertion that she has never asked is not.

**CONFLICT C4 — Whether Pace has seen Vee deliberately standing naked in heels**

- **Ch. 33:** “Ten steps in four-inch heels and nothing else, lamplight standing at the end of it, the house's warmth moving over every part of her at once so that there was no forgetting, anywhere, what she wasn't wearing.”
  
  She then tells him: “‘And this is what I had on under it,’ she said.”
- **Ch. 52:** “he had never once seen her like this, stood up on purpose in a lit room with her heels on, made into an offering and knowing she'd made herself one”

The deliberate, illuminated display in heels has already occurred. The new shoes and coat reveal are new particulars; the categorical prior-experience claim is contradicted.

**CONFLICT C5 — Drinks when Vee leaves Pace at the Rusty Nail**

- **Ch. 26:** “She set the bottle on the ledge and walked off before he could say anything”
  
  Subsequently: “He stood where she'd left him with his bottle.”
- **Ch. 27:** “‘Then I walked off — didn't let him get a word out, left him standing there holding both our drinks. And from across the room I watched him. He didn't come after me. He didn't get angry. He stood there a while, then set the drinks down and left. Slow.’”

The enacted scene leaves Vee’s bottle on the ledge and Pace holding his own. Her later account places both drinks in his hands. This is a conflict between the event and Vee’s retelling; the ledger does not determine whether the discrepancy is intentional.
