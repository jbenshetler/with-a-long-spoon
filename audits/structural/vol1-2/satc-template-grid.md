**What the grid shows.** The beats already change meal to meal, and most of the
changes are the arc — who orders, who pays, who touches first, who prolongs the
kiss, Cassie's slide from present to absent. The defects are almost all in the
*narration of the recurring beat*, where a later telling copies an earlier
sentence instead of compressing or changing it.
