## Randi answers Brooke's demand in public (author 2026-10-07)

Added against {{My Friend Randi}}'s new panel two, where Brooke tells Randi
*"You've got a position in this house. You need to choose"* and **leaves without
waiting for a reply**. That choice stays open for thirteen chapters and is
answered here.

**The exchange.** Mid-dossier, Randi's arm comes around Vee's waist — *"there,
where a second before there had been nothing"* — and she answers out loud:
*"I chose."* Brooke: *"I can see."* That is **the first time Brooke drops the
warmth**, and it is a receipt, not an escalation: she demanded an answer, she got
one in front of witnesses, she logged it. She then re-arms the smile for Vee at
once (*"Of *course*, sweetheart… Any friend of Randi's"*), so Vee sees only
warmth.

**The gesture is a progression inside the scene.** Eight lines earlier Randi's
*"hand warm and sure against her spine"* is already on the page; the waist is the
next rung on the same axis, and it gives *"I chose"* something Brooke can
physically see, which is what makes *"I can see"* land.

**The exchange goes over Vee's head, and nothing glosses the miss.** She is lit
all through about the dossier and braced for the Jan 15 wreck to be read out.
Her non-reaction *is* the misfile; do not narrate it. Her asking afterward is the
only evidence it snagged.

## "As my date" — the cost sits on the word

> "You." Randi's arm tightened at her waist, and the rest of it came a beat late.
> "As my date."

**The word is the one Randi refused at the invite.** At `:9` she says *"I get a
plus-one,"* and she lets Vee's *"You could get a date… Easily"* go by without
picking it up, answering instead with *"You're more fun than any guy… You I just
get to have."* She could not say it three weeks ago. **Saying it now, after being
told to choose, is the brave act, and it is measurable because the reader watched
her dodge it.** So the hesitation must land **on the word itself** — *the rest of
it came a beat late* — and nothing may explain the hesitation.

**Vee's delight is at being picked, never at the romance of the word.** *Randi's
date. Glamorous, beautiful Randi, who could have walked in on the arm of anybody
in the place, had picked her.* `glamorous` is Vee's established word for Randi
({{Dear}}, {{Rock}}, {{Old Acquaintances}} — which also has her piling the
adjectives this way, *"glamorous, wild Randi"*), and the lift is in *could have
walked in on the arm of anybody in the place*: the most glamorous person in her
world had the whole room available and took her — which is also, exactly, what
Randi just did and at what price. **Her *what-we-do* frame must survive**, and it
does because her delight attaches to being chosen rather than to the word; the
frame has nine chapters to carry.

**Deliberately avoided:** the formulation at `:99` (*"the thousand glamorous
things Randi knew that she didn't"*) is only twenty-eight lines earlier, so
reusing it would read as repetition rather than return.

## What this loads

- **The three-beat arc.** Defiant here → the bill taken *"without flinching"* at
  {{Inversion}}, which now reads as **an invoice she asked for** rather than
  caving. It also makes {{Inversion}}'s *"the humiliation exists only because of
  Vee"* literal: she told Brooke she had chosen Vee, in public, and the docket is
  the bill. And the Vee-shaped half of Brooke's case is now dramatized rather than
  asserted.
- **The word's three-stage pass.** Randi says it as a **choice** → Vee repeats it
  flat to Pace at {{Boyfriend}} (*"I went as her date"*; *"it was only the word for
  the thing, a plus-one, a favor"* — she reaches back for Randi's own deflection)
  → **Pace's inch recharges it** (*"the inch made her hear the word again, hear it
  the way he'd just heard it"*). Vee strips out a charge that was put there in
  front of her, and Pace's body restores it. Do not pre-charge the word here;
  Randi's beat-late pause is the whole of it.
- **`:129` flips without being touched.** Vee braces to hear the Jan 15 wreck read
  into the room, Brooke declines, and Vee concludes she *"was kind after all."*
  With the January panel upstream, that omission is **a prosecutor holding
  evidence.** `meta-triage-on-her-floor.md` already protects Brooke's
  double-reading as a confirmed positive; this makes the second reading load-bearing
  rather than merely available.
