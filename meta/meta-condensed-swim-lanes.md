## Summary

The twin of {{Tannin}}, one riser up: Randi's debrief of the rain hike.

He has driven home in the wet shirt and stood in the shower until the cold came out of
his back. Her text is waiting — *on my way.* He puts on a henley and jeans, turns the heat
up, puts her music on (Blondie, low in the front rooms), leaves the door unlocked. **Then
he is hungry.** He has not eaten since the rock, and he is standing at the open
refrigerator when he hears the car.

The deadbolt goes over anyway. He comes through and sees **the chair by the door with
nothing on it**, and sets that aside. *"How was it?"* — asked into his mouth, with no room
for the answer; she takes it back off his tongue before he can get a word onto it, and
walks him backward out of the kitchen and through the bedroom door.

Every garment is an interruption: his hands come off her only for as long as it takes the
next thing to leave her body. Then she puts a hand flat on his chest and pushes him down,
comes up over him, and **does not take him** — she holds him against herself and looks at
his face until he understands he is meant to watch, and lets herself down by inches, a
stop between each, until he says her name.

*"Tell me."* He knows it is not the trail she wants, and he is glad to give it: *"She wears
gardenia… Gardenia and musk. On her throat, and lower. It's been warm on her since morning, so
by now it's mostly the musk… Her hair smells shampoo-clean, and subtly, underneath it, a hint
of chlorine. Like a pool."* The grip of her
tightens; the laugh that comes out of her is thin, and not her laugh. *"Tell me."* /
*"Her stomach is soft."* He does not say how he knows — she had given him herself in the
truck with the rain coming down — and he keeps it.

Then the hard clench, all at once, eyes shut, nails set in his chest, held long enough
that he says her name again; and then she drives, her belly tight as a board under the
heel of his hand. He takes it for the want, too big for her, with nowhere else to go.
***"I want us,"*** at his ear, torn out of her, unglossed. She comes first and takes him
with her.

**And then she skips the afterglow** — the part she keeps for herself, gone loose on his
chest — and the chill takes her fast. He offers food and warmth; *"No thanks,"* bright,
from inside the camisole going over her head. *"Stay anyway. Get warm."* / *"I'm good."*
She dresses, kisses him thoroughly at the bed and again at the door, says *"See you,"*
turns the deadbolt back and lets herself out.

She had asked him how it was. He never got to it, and she never asked again.

**Close:** she has never left after — not once since the first night. He sets the empty
chair and the leaving side by side and they will not come out; *it was the want*, he
thinks, more than she could lie still under, and that is as far as he can take it, leaving
him in his own doorway with something low and plain in his chest he was not ready for. He
shuts the door, eats alone at the table, and is still hungry after, and makes more.
