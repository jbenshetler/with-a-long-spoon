# The forged-message reveal (planning note)

*A thread across volumes: Randi's two forged midnight messages to Vee from Pace's phone, and how Pace finds out. Sections marked **Author ruling** are decided (2026-11-23). Everything else is a proposal that waits on the author. Nothing here is in the chronology yet, apart from the {{Nachtmusik}} guards and the {{Made-Up}} payoff.*

## The first message — on the page

- **{{Nachtmusik}}** (`nachtmusik.md:137-167`). While Pace sleeps, Randi unlocks his phone with Vee's birthday (she has it from Vee's license, `fed.md`, C9). She forwards Vee's induction photo to herself. She types *breasts*, deletes it, chooses *tits*, and sends Vee *The way that dress hugs your tits makes me wish I was a dress.* Vee replies *PACE.*, *who are you and what did you do with him??*, *I'm keeping this forever*. Randi deletes every message on his side, and the forward too. Vee's phone keeps the whole exchange.
- **{{Made-Up}}** (`made-up.md:61-65`, Sat Oct 31). After the first costume, Vee quotes the message back to him with a shake of her shoulders: "The way this dress hugs my tits — does it still make you wish you were a dress?" His face goes still. She reads it as shyness: "a man who'd written that alone in the dark with her picture in one hand." Behind the bathroom door she rereads the thread and keeps it, so as not to make him look at it. She never raises it again.
- **Pace's side is off the page.** He heard words he never wrote and could not place them. It is Halloween, she has just called the evening's outfits costumes, and he files it as Vee putting words in his mouth as part of the game. When nothing fits, he keeps silent (`meta-craft-pace.md`, Console rules).
- **Randi's side.** Vee never mentioned it, and Pace behaved as usual, so as far as Randi can tell it worked. That impunity is what causes the second message.

## The second message and the reveal — Author ruling

1. **It must come after {{Gesso}}** (Sat Feb 6). At Gesso, Vee shows Randi the {{Still Life}} photo. Randi seeing it there needs its full weight, and the scene gives Randi an innocent way to know the photo, so knowing it proves nothing. What exposes her is a message sent **from Pace's phone**.
2. **Stealing the photo is Randi possessing Vee.** At Gesso she sees it with permission. Afterward she takes it from his phone.
3. **The second message is another midnight message from Randi pretending to be Pace**, in the same voice, and it uses *tits* again. The word is what ties it to the first message.
4. **Vee takes the message back to Pace.**
5. **It is not a reminder on the phone screen.** Months of texts have buried the first exchange, and nobody scrolls back that far.
6. **Pace finds it in his Recently Deleted.** The {{Nachtmusik}} deletions are long past the 30-day window. This one is fresh. Nobody checks that folder without a reason, and now he has one.

## Proposed shape

- **Vee brings it to him**, quoting it or showing her phone. In front of her he covers, a lie to Vee he would feel. That answers the vol1 structural finding that Pace is never uneasy about deceiving Vee.
- **Alone, he checks Recently Deleted** and finds the same set Randi deleted at Nachtmusik:
  - the message he never wrote;
  - Vee's replies;
  - **the Still Life photo forwarded to Randi's number.**

  The forward is both the proof and the possession made visible. The timestamps put everything at an hour when his phone was on the bedside charger and Randi was in his bed.
- **His memory brings {{Made-Up}} back** in one italic recall: the hall, the shake inside the silk, *does it still make you wish you were a dress?*, and his own face going still. He had filed it under the costume game. Now he knows the first one was hers too. The recall carries a reader who forgot. The two scenes are 35 chapters and about 126,000 words apart ({{Made-Up}} is ch32, {{Gesso}} ch68), so the reveal must not depend on the reader remembering.
- **Deed before words.** He changes his passcode (Vee's birthday) before he says anything to Randi, per his rule of deeds, not words.
- **Don't seed it in between.** No rereads or passing mentions of the first message between {{Made-Up}} and the reveal. The payoff depends on nobody watching that thread.

## Open questions for the author

1. **Why does Vee bring this one to him, when she kept the first?** Either she's bolder now and shows him delighted ("you did it again"), or the message oversteps. For example, it knows something only Vee and Randi share. That would be stronger, since it's another of Randi's slips, but it risks pointing Vee at Randi, and Vee must not learn.
2. **Whose POV when she brings it?** In Pace's, his cover is the after-register, everything held in. In Vee's, she sees only that he's pleased, or quiet.
3. **Where does the confrontation go?** The window runs from after Feb 6 to before the summer threesome (`meta-plan-chronology.md:509-529`). Inside it are the spring Pace/Randi staircase ({{My Pleasure}} → {{For You}} → {{In Her Place}}) and Randi taking the scrunchies (`meta-condensed-randi-takes-scrunchies.md`). A confrontation changes what Pace knows going into {{In Her Place}} and the threesome planning. It might supply In Her Place's punishment frame ("misbehaved… so he'll make her pay," `meta-note-in-her-place.md:28`); check that against the note before choosing.
4. **What Randi says when confronted**, and whether her want for Vee shows to Pace there, measured against what he already knows when the threesome is planned.

## Guards

- **Vee never learns** that Randi sent either message.
- **"Never the same beat twice"**: the second theft must not repeat {{Nachtmusik}}'s mechanics on the page. Its message should be shown briefly, or only through Vee and then through the folder.
- **Keep Vee's {{Made-Up}} reading as hers.** On a first read, "she'd made him shy" stands. The reread charge comes from the reveal, not from any hint in between.
