## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **`:73` "That's like asking me to choose between sunrise and sunset" — stands.**
  Flagged as too-polished by three of five cold readers (sol, kimi, opus:
  "composed for quotation"). Ruled design, not flaw: it is Pace performing a
  rehearsed gallantry that Vee immediately punctures ("She gave him an eyebrow"),
  after which he lands the real answer ("The other is for the world. This is just
  for me."). sol and kimi both explicitly credit the puncture-then-real-answer as
  the book "knowing its own man"; the too-smooth line is the setup the eyebrow
  needs, and the over-perfection is load-bearing for the Pace architecture (the
  reader is meant to "hear the perfection as a tell").
- **`:43` "She watched him make himself do it" — stands.** Editor flagged it as
  the abstract middle of three restraint-renderings (wine / this / `:51`).
  **Reader-praised:** gpt-5.6-sol named this line *and* `:51` together as "the
  peaks for me. Restraint becomes visibly difficult, and therefore erotic." And
  they are two distinct beats bracketing the walk — `:43` is him making himself
  *sit back down*; `:51` is him *not touching* after it — not one echo.
- **`:63` "He hadn't left the chair. She'd put him there, and he'd stayed put." —
  stands.** Flagged as one fact tripled. Ruled the voice: the period stages a
  realization-beat (external observation → *I did that* → he obeyed), the same
  arc as `:51`'s "she understood that she had gotten this exactly right." The
  pairing "She'd put him there, and he'd stayed put" is reader-praised (gpt-5.5:
  "That matters").
- **`:63` "so that there was no forgetting, anywhere, what she wasn't wearing" —
  stands.** Existential + stacked negatives flagged (also the style linter,
  `#aa97516b88fe`). Kept: the double negative enacts the effortful, can't-switch-
  off all-over awareness of nakedness; the trailing comma-set "anywhere" gives
  the awareness a creeping hesitation a smoother recast loses. **Acked**
  (`there-was-were` `#aa97516b88fe`). The `warm` at `:63` ("the house's warmth")
  is load-bearing warm-house-on-bare-skin — **acked** (`warm` `#e911c05acff7`).
- **`:69` "a man being handed something and taking his time about the taking" —
  stands.** Editor flagged the appositive as restating "let the looking be the
  touch" and the doubled "taking." **Reader-praised:** kimi-k3 quoted it and
  wrote *"The taking. There it is,"* reading the doubled *taking* as the tell
  planted inside the tenderness (Pace's governing mode). The repeat is the
  feature; sol called the sentence "especially effective," kimi "the sentence of
  the chapter."
- **`:15` "The heels that finished it" — stands (the "finished/finished thing"
  proximity).** Flagged as repeating the protected thesis line's "finished."
  Ruled canon motif: Sorority frames the heels as making "the line of her
  **finishing** itself" (`sorority:95`), and `made-up:65` echoes it ("the long
  line finishing itself"); Pace's spoken "They complete it" (`:47`) keeps
  *complete* as his word. So "finish (the line)" is the heels' canonical verb and
  the two `:15` uses are different senses (heels-finish-the-line vs. the-finished-
  object). Not varied to "complete" precisely because that would pre-empt Pace's
  `:47` verdict.
- **`:15` "he had never seen under it at all" — stands.** Flagged as spatially
  wrong (heels at the feet, not under the dress). Kept: "under the dress" is the
  idiomatic under-layer, and — the deciding reason — it arms the `:67` payoff,
  where the second costume's line is *"And this is what I had on under it,"*
  (nothing). The chapter's two-costume turn pivots on *under it*: the innocent
  thing under it (the heels) at `:15`, the erotic thing under it (nothing) at
  `:67`. "on her" would break the bracket.
- **`:49` "and to her it was louder" — stands.** Flagged as a metaphor explained
  after it lands. Kept: "louder" is a paradox that completes the thought, not a
  restatement — one silent man outweighing the induction's whole murmuring room,
  the chapter's private-viewing-over-public spine (`:39`, `:77`).
- **`:7` "boys in whatever had been within reach at nine o'clock" — stands.**
  sol: "announces its cleverness." Single reader, light; it's an establishing
  beat *before* the intimacy narrows in, and it seeds the title/costume frame
  ("everyone out being somebody else").
- **Lyricism-reach cluster (`:17`, `:33`) — stands (the voice).** opus flagged a
  density of reaching-for-lyricism constructions ("her body on its feet while the
  rest of it was still arriving," "clean and enormous," "loud in all that
  quiet"). Ruled the register, not a fault: `:17` is the book's body-before-mind
  signature (protected); "clean and enormous" was reader-called "lovely." One
  reader, self-marked "minor." (Cross-volume echo candidate logged separately —
  see below.)
