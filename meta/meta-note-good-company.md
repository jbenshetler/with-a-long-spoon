## Why this scene exists (the Vol-1-end oracle)

The cold-read oracle at Volume One's close floored Randi on every reader-axis — **sympathy 2, identification 1, believability 3** — and the reader named the cause each time: *her interior is almost entirely withheld, so she reads as a mechanism, not a woman.* Her only humanity-crack in the whole fall is {{Fairytale}} (very late). This scene surfaces her warm interior **early**, so the reader banks her humanity across the volume instead of in one late beat. It lifts **both** leads: Randi (de-mechanized) and Pace (his sincerity and his falling shown warmly). (Results: `reviews/cold-read/claude-opus-4-8/oracle/`.)

## What the scene is

Post-coital Pace and Randi, in the warm afterglow, both **buzzing about how great Vee is** — the happy-romantic-friendship view of the configuration, *from the inside.* Pace is falling for Vee; Randi is genuinely delighting in the friendship (the best thing in her life) **and** her own attraction leaks. Warm and true on first read; cold only on reread.

## POV — Pace close third (pays the visual debt)

Randi is assembled from **surface tells** — the soft smile, the unperformed laugh (canon at {{Sorority}}), how she says Vee's name, and **what she leaves out.** This is also the licensed frame for rendering Randi's body sensually (the "visual debt," first paid at {{The Bench}}) — a different occasion, same frame.

## Randi's desire — laundered, in her voice (leak-and-rewrap)

She surfaces her attraction the way she talks — **open, frank, sexual** — never by declaring it: *she's so much fun; those curves under that cardigan — but you already know.* That single line is the villain-guard's warm-leak in her register: the desire **leaks**, the wrap is instant (deflected onto Pace's shared knowledge, folded into the shared-plan frame). It's her **laundering** on the page — she can only voice wanting Vee *through* the acceptable frame (for Pace's threesome), because want she controls and need she can't (canon: 🔒 The Need Engine; the threesome is the largest instance). Continuity: "curves under that cardigan" fits Vee's canon (the lush body she *hides*) — Randi sees what Vee conceals; "you already know" is clean (Pace and Vee are lovers by this slot).

## What Pace knows / doesn't

He has known Randi **wants** Vee since {{The Pointing Game}} — that layer is open between them; they can speak the shared wanting warmly. He is blind to the **sadism**, the **cost / need-not-want depth**, and the **hate.** Randi gives him the **sanitized** friendship version (canon: `meta-arch-randi.md`); the **omission of the sadism** is load-bearing on reread — the reader watches her curate what she hands him.

## The three razors (make or break)

1. **Admiration as *love*, not *acquisition*.** "How great she is" must read as *I'm falling for her / she's the best friend I've had* — never two hunters cataloging prey ("perfect for the plan"). Delight, not appetite-for-the-plan.
2. **No awareness-of-the-deception on the page.** No "she has no idea" wink. Their happiness is sincere and non-cruel; the cold is only in what the reader knows and they don't. The instant either enjoys Vee's ignorance, it curdles to villainy.
3. **Pace hot, never calculating.** His interior runs warm/loving (canon: hot fantasist, not cool strategist). One computed line cools him — and the `pov-reliability` oracle showed his POV already reads *cooler* than Vee's view of him, so guard it hard.

## Planned beat order

1. **Afterglow (frame).** Post-coital in Pace's bed — warm, sated, tangled, low-lit. Render their genuine ease as *lovers* (the first tender Pace/Randi; the Bench was BDSM); the ordinary intimacy is what makes the configuration read warm. Pace-POV, embodied, quiet.
2. **Vee drifts in — the sanitized friendship.** Talk wanders to Vee (the boutique / a brunch). Randi gives the *friendship version only* — closeness, fun; never the extraction or the sadism. The **omission** is load-bearing on reread; warm on first.
3. **Randi gushes — the love leaking (a beat too long).** She can't stop — how funny, how brave, how alive Vee is: her **friendship-love** escaping. Pace watches her light up on the name, fond. It runs past where an ordinary friend would stop. *(Her love — what de-villains her; a manipulator has appetite, a friend has this.)*
4. **The cardigan → the shame exchange → the desire leaks.** Randi puzzles at the cardigan (*"I don't know why she hides in that cardigan"*); Pace answers with **tenderness, not appetite** — *"She's ashamed. Somebody taught her to be ashamed of her body"* (spare, certain, no analysis; he doesn't preen). Randi credits *him* — *"And you're showing her she doesn't have to be. That's why she's so gone over you"* — and gives him a **soft, brief kiss on the lower lip** (her protective fondness for Vee, shown through the body; a *pulse* of fondness, lighter than the earlier lingering kiss). **Then** her appetite can't stay in the noble register and leaks — *"God. What she keeps under there… It's criminal, Pace. But you already know"* — with the breast-drag. The sequence is the **leak-and-rewrap in physical form**: protective tenderness (the kiss) tips into wanting (the drag). Bounds the mood-dip to one line and lets the heat carry into the sex. *Reread payload:* Pace's benefactor myth in Randi's mouth, and the un-shaming as the on-ramp to Vee being used — tender-and-instrumental, never adjudicated; no thumb on first read. (The shame-exchange also de-cools Pace — the oracle's pov-reliability flag — by showing him *seeing* Vee outside a sexual frame.)
5. **Pace clocks the desire (his kink fires).** He reads it — sees the threesome-frame for the fig leaf, sees her *choosing* to want a woman out loud — and it makes him **smile and heat**: a woman transgressing her own rules by her own choice (his core appetite). Fond and turned on, not analytical. He sees the **desire** (layer *a*); he is **blind to the need/cost/sadism** under it (layer *b*) — his seeing is partial, catching what arouses, missing what would horrify (the tragic irony of his gift).
6. **He answers with her aliveness, not her body.** He doesn't join the body-cataloging (that stays Randi's register; his *love* is Vee's spirit) — *she brings a room to life, the air sparkles around her.* Both engines: hot at Randi's wanting, tender at Vee's light. "Brings a room to life" lands on Randi's exact need (aliveness she cannot self-supply).
7. **The gladness / the threesome, anticipated.** Pace buoyant that they fit; anticipating what's coming — rendered as shared **delight/beauty** (*"I can't wait to give you both that"*), never efficiency / good-for-the-plan. Randi agrees *too enthusiastically* — he's named her need and her want. The air charges.
8. **Turn back into sex.** Charged from the talk, they kiss and go again — warm, real, tender-charged; pays the **visual debt** (Randi's body, Pace-POV). Dark on reread (Vee the unknowing fuel); sexy and loving on first. Close on the warmth — **no doom telegraph.**

**Author-approved exception (observation-only).** The line *"Something in Randi went quiet at that, and softened past it"* renders a hair of Randi's interior directly rather than as pure Pace-observed surface. The author has **signed off** on this as a deliberate light touch — do **not** re-flag it in continuity passes as an observation-only breach. (The rest of the scene holds Randi to surface tells; this one mild interior brush is intentional.)

**Calibrations.** Pace clocks Randi's *desire* (hot, on-canon: "Randi is the wanting") but never the *need/cost/sadism* (his blindness); arousal aimed at her *chosen transgression*, never at Vee's *ignorance* (no-deception-wink). Randi's *love* must surface (beats 2–3), not just desire. The "too long" / "too enthusiastically" play as **warmth on first read**, over-investment only on reread — and Pace reads them as warmth-and-heat, not as anything to be suspicious of.
