# Condensed — Another Round

*Compaction of the drafted scene (`another-round.md`); surface only — the unrendered craft/subtext lives in `meta-note-another-round.md`. Sat Jan 23, after {{Among Friends}}, before {{Grace}}; a taproom in town. Vee POV. Vee and Sheri's first meeting on the page.*

- **The kitchen hinge (two nights after the roadhouse).** At his sink — Vee mining the party (Danny's tub of egg whites, Pace's fingerprints per Meg) — Pace says, to the window: *"There's somebody I'd like you to meet." "Sheri."* The name detonates the Christmas invention (gold hair, tan leg); the knowing (*I built her on the nights I felt stupid*) turns out to be a coat of paint — the cold arrives anyway. His facts, handed over like dried spoons: *"Small." "Loud." "We cycle." "She dances."* — the *we* and the blade both received in silence.
- **The armor.** She dresses **for the woman her jealousy built, knowing it, doing it anyway**: Meg's keyhole shirt, a hall girl's short skirt, and — past the boots — the **induction heels** (four inches of thin strap, bought on a card with nothing behind it), buckled on against January. The production runs long; Pace waits in the lobby and says nothing about the time, then or after.
- **Arrival — the jealousy dies hard.** Coats onto pegs; nothing woolen to hide behind. Vee is still hunting the imagined blonde over the tables when Pace calls out *"Sheri"* at a glad public volume she's never heard him spend — a hot spike. Sheri comes off the stool standing: five foot nothing, two-tone perm, too much makeup worn like a dare. The blonde fails to find anywhere to stand — and the heat *stays*: it never needed the blonde.
- **"You're late" + the hug taxonomy.** Whole-weight hug for Pace (cheek flat, eyes shut one count; his hand knows its place on her back); the lateness is Vee's fault, ten minutes buckled to her feet. The heat sits up confused — wrong body, but that was no lab-partner hug.
- **The look, the laugh, the motorboat.** Squared off like a mismatched prizefight, Sheri gives Vee a full unhurried appreciative scan — Vee braces for the pricing look and gets a mouth rounding to a soundless *oh*, stuck at the induction shoes (*the most naked thing in the room — a few thin straps against January*). Understanding lands: the look was *enjoying* her — and the month of jealousy leaves in one long laugh. *"I'm Vee."* / *"I know who you are... Come here, you"* — fast fierce hug; *"You've got him happy. That's the whole book."* Then, to Pace, at no lowered volume: the motorboat aside — and Pace **blushes**, past anything deniable; Vee has seen him easy everywhere, even in the dark, and never once seen him blush.
- **Settling.** Hard cider for Vee; Pace's one witnessing beer. The **philosophy-of-food** beat: Sheri reports that Pace told her Vee *gets the philosophy of his cooking* — *"what I get is derailleurs... He does go on about you"* — and Vee warms in a place the drink hadn't reached: she is the one person he tells things to, told to the one person he tells things to.
- **Testimony, part one (first drink-run) — how he dances.** Every man in the hall dances as a down payment; Pace shows up alone, drinks maybe one, dances because the dancing is the point, hands where the dance puts them, every time. You don't praise a man at his own table. Plus the invite: Saturday, the dance hall, *"Bring your opinions"* — and the migrated Christmas callback (*"Bet those legs feel like Christmas — what'd I tell you back in December"*).
- **Testimony, part two (second drink-run) — what he did.** Out of Vee's *"How did you two get to be friends?"*: not the dancing — *"I came out last March."* The inventory (family, church, everyone), the tablecloth line, tears then not now (*"so don't you start either"*); strangers' easy words while the ones who owed her words spent none; crying in the dance hall — the one place left with her name on it; the deeds morning (coffee and donuts, the lock promised since August, the faucet, the bike and Sundays, a bacon cheeseburger; no false words). *"He was there. That's all a body can be for you at a point like that."* Then the **ration**: *"Went bad with his daddy. He hasn't been home since he was sixteen... Two years he's been my friend, and that is every word of it I've got."* Folder shut at full volume (the porter that girl's been promising since March).
- **Told, then shown.** The girls get drunk; Pace, sober, walks Vee out on his arm (*it read as affection, it worked as ballast — with him, those had never been two different things*), hands her up into the cab, drives them home. Sheri sings filthy versions over two radio songs — never quoted; rendered as permanence: two songs ruined for Vee for life.
- **The drop-offs.** Sheri's half-a-duplex on an unpaved road, headlights held on her door till the porch light; her goodnight (*"Saturday, both of you, and leave them shoes home — can't two-step on stilts"*). Then the cab: *"I like her." / "She likes you." / "She told me things... Not telling you which."* — *"Bacon cheeseburgers,"* to the window, to nobody. Final image: her temple on his shoulder, his hands at ten and two, *like something full he was carrying, and had no intention of spilling.*

**Function:** Pace's decency rendered on the page through a witness — he never opens up, his goodness is *testified,* then enacted the same night. The sealed world opened a notch (his people before his house; the Cabin key, a month on, deepens it to dominion). The Christmas jealousy dissolved experientially, not by fact alone — it dies hard, only after the scan and the laugh, as physical relief. Reader-side, never on the page: the realer his constancy, the deeper Vee's bargain and the louder the unsaid *I love you.* Full craft + guards: `meta-note-another-round.md`.
