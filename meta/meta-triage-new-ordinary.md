# Triage — The New Ordinary (line edit, 2026-08-12)

## New prose — the hall strip sequence (author, workshopped this session)

The single largest change. The old rendering stated his slowness and her
urgency (*"He undressed her with the unhurried hands he brought to everything
… the patience of it was its own small cruelty tonight, because she was
already long past patience"*) and summarised the sex. It is now **staged**: a
continuous kissing/undressing sequence travelling the canonical hall — foyer
(coat, boots, her hands on him) → bathroom door (her sweater, his buttons) →
office (the jeans defeat her; she leans back into him for balance and he
monetises the contact; *"You're not helping." / "I'm helping."*) → the socks →
the corner (he holds her bra and makes her unwind out of it) → the bedroom.

Design points, recorded so a later pass doesn't undo them:

- **The bench is the payload.** Vee throws the green thong onto "the low table
  of odd proportions" and never looks. She does not know what it is; the reader
  knows Randi has been laying her underwear there since `the-bench:119`.
  Chekhov's bench, deliberate. **Pace does not react** — no flicker, no pause;
  a wink would telegraph. The register is sanctioned by
  `meta-note-vee-on-the-bench.md` ("the disguised table as her half-noticed
  glance is fine").
- **It also re-charges the chapter title.** "The New Ordinary" now also reads
  as *the ordinary is already contaminated and she can't see it.*
- **Playfulness is the mode, not comic relief** — per `meta-craft-pace.md`
  ("with Vee, teasing is erotically load-bearing"). Pace speaks once. The
  wetness stays in the **evidentiary placeholder** ("the proof of her") per
  Vee's rendering rule — do not concretise it.
- **The socks are the anti-lingerie**, and the laugh is *hers* — the one beat
  in the sequence she wins, balancing five stations where he has the upper hand.
- **He stays mostly dressed** (two buttons, shirt hanging open). Deliberate:
  the trail is hers being stripped, and who is losing is part of the point.
- Continuity verified against `meta-plan-pace-house.md`: hall order (bathroom →
  office → corner → bedroom), the bench at the room's centre ~three steps in,
  the green satin bra+thong set (`famished:7–11`, bought together).
