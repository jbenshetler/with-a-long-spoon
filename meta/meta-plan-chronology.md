Christmas break Sat Dec 19–Mon Jan 18. **Spring semester begins Tue Jan 19** — the real VT date, classes beginning the day after MLK Day (Mon Jan 18). Spring break Sat Mar 6–Sun Mar 14. **End of spring adopted tentatively** (real VT dates): last day of classes Wed May 5, reading day Thu May 6, finals Fri May 7–Wed May 12, commencement Wed May 12. **Summer 2027** (~mid-May–mid-Aug) is the climax act (Volume Three); the novel's coda is Vee's return in **early fall 2027**.

**Open decision — extend the spring by a week?** The fall was pushed a week later than the real calendar; the spring, as of this ruling, is not. Shifting spring to a Mon Jan 25 start would restore the symmetry *and* restore the First Weekend Back hard pin (continuity flag 7 below). Until ruled, the spring runs on the real dates above.

**Hidden-year device (never surfaced to the reader):** the fall semester is pinned internally to **2026** (spring, summer, and the early-fall coda to **2027**) *solely* to fix the day-of-week ↔ date mapping — so weekends, weeknights, and "stats day (MWF)" land on real weekdays. The year is an authoring tool only: it is **never named or implied on the page**, and only the *day of week* and *time of year* it produces are load-bearing, never the specific dates. (2026 also quietly lengthens Randi's window of PPP-money enjoyment.) Anchors here reflect the real 2026–27 calendar: Nov 1 2026 = Sun; Thanksgiving = Thu Nov 26; finals week = Mon Dec 14–Fri Dec 18.
