# Craft note — Between

*SCENE · Tue Jan 26 · Vee POV · SATC/confidante · Draft complete · slug: between · drafted 2026-09-12*

**Title.** *Between* — first read: the innocuous slot. Between classes, between the Sunday kitchen and the Friday appointment, the loft between floors; the ordinary Tuesday nothing-much. Reread: what passes **between** the two of them and gets named by neither — and where Vee is standing without knowing it, between the two people running her. The title takes no position; it only marks the gap.

## What the scene is

The chapter the {{Hangover}} anticipation was written toward, and the on-page home of the {{Barely Stings}} seed. It is a **down-beat with two small charges** — mostly comedy, one covered Randi slip and one Vee tell, then practical talk — which is what resets the meter before the {{Barely Stings}}/{{Still Life}} diptych and {{On Her Floor}}.

**Opens mid-meal, by author ruling (2026-09-12): no class, no dressing, no arrival.** {{Covering}} and {{A Clean Plate}} both spend a full runway (the dressing-and-redressing, the Marsh lecture, the walk, the stair, Randi already there with her coat on the saved seat). Cutting all of it does three things at once: it varies the **container** of the retelling without varying the **set** (the track's stated anti-sag discipline), it puts the reader in an already-warm room so **Randi spends no opening story of her own** (*"Randi goes first until she doesn't need to"*), and it keeps a short chapter short.

## The beat

- **Movement 1 — the Sheri telling, played for comedy.** Vee arrives having *composed* this (see {{Hangover}}), and the order is hers: the invented midnight blonde laughed out of the room first (*five foot nothing — in boots*), Randi demanding she start over and go slower, then the treaty-signing where Vee hands over the win. Randi's **"Fear needs a face, baby. You gave it one."** is the live echo of her own {{My Friend Randi}} talk-down (`my-friend-randi.md` :57, :65) — she gets to be right out loud, and Vee enjoys letting her.
  - **The reveal lands last: she's gay.** A month of dread spent on a woman who could not have wanted him. Keep it delighted and clean. The **evidence is Vee's own public observation only** — Sheri watching the bartender walk off "like a woman watching a parade," at the table, in front of Pace (`another-round.md` :71).
  - **The disclosure boundary (author ruling 2026-09-17) — the standing rule for every SATC recount.** Sheri's coming-out is a **confidence**: told only while Pace is up at the bar, shut with no seam the moment he's back, capped *"that is every word of it I've got"* (`another-round.md` :105–117) — and it carries **Pace's** history too (*went bad with his daddy*). Vee does not retail it. She names **only** that it happened and went badly, says out loud that the rest isn't hers, and spends the fact **as the runway for praising Pace**: he showed up when nobody else did — the drive home, the coffee on her porch the next morning, the lock her landlord had been promising since August. The pride is **rendered, never named** — *"That man. Hers."*, Sheri's own road-condition dismissal turned over into possession. **Also barred: the ex's name.** *Paige* comes from `the-usual.md` :39, a Pace/Sheri two-hander (chronology :244) — Vee was not there and cannot know it. What Sheri gave Vee in confidence does not travel to Randi.
- **Movement 2 — the friction. One covered control-slip, and the first thing Vee hides from Randi.**
  - Vee **reports the look as event, not as appetite** — *it happened*, never *I appreciated it.* She is riding the laugh and gets there without deciding to.
  - **Told as comedy — built, then reversed (author ruling 2026-09-16).** Vee lays the *wrong* reading down first: the pricing look she braced for at `another-round.md` :51 — the outfit added up, a number on you before the door finishes closing — and she stands there waiting for a grade that never comes. Then the itemized flip, keyhole → bare leg → feet, closing on *"She never graded me at all, Randi. She was enjoying herself."* The setup is what makes the reversal land; without it the beat is only a description of a look.
  - **The Vee tell.** Randi's *"No complaints there"* arrives with her eyes up on Vee's **and staying**; Vee **can't hold it — giggles, drops her eyes, and finishes the story to the tablecloth.** The division of labor matters: Randi's look is *volitional and deniable* (a friend flattering a friend), and the **involuntary** thing on the page belongs to **Vee**. Render Randi's look in her own register — never Pace's *looking straight at her* (`barely-stings.md` :10) — or the two seducers' vocabularies blur (`meta-arch-bible.md`, Global Craft Rules). Unpointed: Vee is telling a story about a woman looking at her *while a woman looks at her*, and the second look is the one she can't meet.
  - **The slip:** Randi has Vee's hand across the cloth, turning the fingers over one at a time, and her **thumb stops mid-pass.** Involuntary, physiological, small, nothing she could have chosen (`meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` :347) — and *less* choosable than a hand halting over a plate, which a reader could take for second thoughts about the food. Vee has it **under her skin before she has it anywhere else** (body before mind). Then the register drops flat and quiet — *"And you liked it."* — her established interrogation voice, the one that undercuts the polished version.
  - **The lie:** *"No."* Then, hearing the speed of it, *"No. Not my thing."* — Vee's voice **regressing downward** under pressure, per her Console rules. Body before mind: she drinks before she answers.
  - **The misread, which is the point.** Randi's stillness holds a beat too long, and Vee files it as Randi *still standing guard for her about Pace* — and is warmed by being looked after that hard. She reassures Randi that Sheri was never a threat. **Vee never once considers that the jealousy is pointed at herself.**
  - **The cover is warm and is an action, not a statement:** Randi's thumb takes up where it left off, goes on over the knuckles, and **stays** — she keeps the hand. **"I know she isn't, gorgeous."** Truth-flag honored — the sentence carrying the reserved word is true, and true in the register Vee cannot hear. Randi never goes cold; going cold is the tell she doesn't make.
  - **A moment, not an issue** (author ruling 2026-09-11). The scene does not stop for it, nobody returns to it, and Vee walks home untroubled. This is the background friction the spring found-hair beat needs — and the first time Vee conceals a *want* from Randi, which is what arms the {{On Her Floor}} cover-up: she has already practiced not owning this.
- **Movement 3 — the {{Barely Stings}} seed.** Saved for the end because it is the thing Vee actually needs, and because the friction has to pass under it. Pace's unexplained Sunday kitchen ask, reported; then Randi's **three-nudge cluster** live on the page — *have a drink first / Sondra by name, Draper Street / "Barely stings" / "the most expensive thing in the room"* — **verbatim as `barely-stings.md` :31–35 recalls it.** Any future edit to those lines must be made in both files.
  - **The omission is rendered as pure absence.** Nothing about the turn-over, and — critically — **no beat drawing attention to the gap**: no held breath, no almost-saying, no narrator's glance. A signalled omission is the signal the track forbids. The reread charge is the reader's alone, on arrival at *"the part Randi hadn't said."*
  - Vee says *"All of it. Bare"* without knowing what all of it covers — which is the seed of `barely-stings.md` :81 (*"without once asking what the whole of it was"*). Unpointed.
- **The parting and close.** Physical staircase **rested** — no new riser. The ratified neck rung recurs in fresh language, unnamed by either of them; **the banked phrase *"how they let each other go now"* is NOT spent here** (reserved until the spring seam by author ruling). The two-tier signature is rendered in **fresh wording** — the debut's *"a small drawing-back, the barest pause… a second press that had something in it the first one hadn't"* (`how-its-done.md` :172) must never be re-quoted (`meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` :109); here the second tier runs on **duration**, the staircase's legal channel.
  - **Vee arrives first (author ruling 2026-09-17).** She brings her face up and around *before Randi has started to*, and stops there **with her eyes already closed**, waiting on the hand — which comes to the **hinge of her jaw**, the debut gesture. Eyes shut for a kiss that hasn't arrived is the asking, and nothing in the narration touches it. **Not a riser:** {{All the Time}} (Sat Nov 14) already had her refuse to let the kiss end (`all-the-time.md` :133), so a passively-received kiss here would be a *regression*, not restraint. Its job is structural — this is the **last parting before {{On Her Floor}}**, and the very next rung is the friction backtrack (*still does it, but lightly, without the prior enthusiasm*). A backtrack needs a high-water mark to fall from. She never notices or files any of it (the collapse guard, `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` :89).
  - Closes on Vee writing *Sondra. Draper St.* on the back of her hand in her own stairwell — and not writing the other part down, because she has it cold. Innocent on first read; the believed lie is the last thing in the chapter.

## Guards

- **Earn the dark by being light.** This is a funny, warm chapter and must read that way on first pass. The single cold thing in it is four words long and Vee explains it to herself immediately.
- **Never inside Randi.** Her interior shows only as the stopped hand, the flat register, and the timing of the recovery. No access, no narration of motive.
- **Never name.** Nobody says jealousy, nobody says lie, and the prose does not gloss Vee's misreading as a misreading.
- **No second trace.** The found-hair thread is a single spring beat (`meta-arch-bible.md` :400); this chapter is its *upstream friction* and seeds no trace of its own.
- **Randi's intake is precise, and she never touches Vee's food (author ruling 2026-09-17).** She eats **exactly half the fish** — to the middle of the fillet, fork laid down across the plate, the other half going nowhere. *Precision, not abstinence:* a quantity decided in advance and executed, the alimentary version of the bound ponytail (`meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` :432). **Not** one token bite — that reads as *not eating* and drifts toward the diet register the track explicitly bans (**no calorie narration**). And **never reaching into Vee's plate**: it inverts her canon, where she *offers* and rations herself (`one-bite.md` :139, :143), and it is beneath a presentation she does not drop. Her hands go to **Vee's hand** instead — which is also what makes the slip available. Vee eats everything.
