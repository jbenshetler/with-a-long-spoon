## Guards (for the draft)

- **Register — warmth is primary (volume-governing).** First read is warm, playful, sincere, and mutually hungry: a couple lit up about the woman they both want. The reader should want the configuration and be torn. The reread charge comes from sequence, never explanatory narration. If the scene reads cold, predatory, or scheming on first pass, it has failed the volume.
- **POV and body-first logic.** Pace close third; body before mind. Randi’s need, love, scheme, resentment, and mirror-identification are performed through body, voice, timing, motion, and sparse dialogue, never supplied as her interior. Pace catches her hiding but not its depth; he gives her room rather than exposing or stage-managing her.
- **Love and instrumentality stay simultaneous.** Randi truly loves and admires Vee, and she also routes Vee toward the configuration. Neither truth cancels the other. Her delight in Vee’s humor and agency is real; no deception-wink lets anyone enjoy Vee’s ignorance.
- **Cruel track absent.** The operational sadism, humiliation, extraction, and full {{The Pointing Game}} wound remain outside Pace’s perception and outside the page. The repeated-kiss tease may carry only a fractional unread edge. Randi’s laughter targets Pace’s lost composure, never Vee.
- **Choice is the charge.** Pace is aroused by a woman’s willing choice to break her own rule and experiences opening pleasure as a gift. Never render him excited by coldness, coercion, hurt, or rule-breaking imposed from outside.
- **No body comparison.** Pace never measures Randi’s slight body against Vee’s abundant one or treats Randi as insufficient. The small/large contrast reinforces Randi’s command over his power.
- **Ambiguity is load-bearing.** “She’s not the only one in love” stays unresolved for reader, Pace, narration, and Randi’s visible behavior. Do not turn it into either confession or observation.
- **Descent exists only in sequence.** Randi’s move from command into self-appointed service gains its reread meaning from placement after the love line. Pace does not perceive a lowering, and narration never names hurt, jealousy, displacement, rank, or a missed confession. Randi rises and restores command herself.
- **Fixation ladder.** This scene may transpose Vee’s neck, breasts, self-taste curiosity, and ass onto Randi’s body. Direct fixation on Vee’s mouth belongs to {{For You}} and must not be pre-run here.
- **Dialogue sparse, broken by breath and motion.** Vee-talk runs through sex rather than becoming a fluent briefing. Explicit, embodied, heat-3.
