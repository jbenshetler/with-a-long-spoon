# Free sample & discovery platforms — Volume 1

*Status: assistant guidance recorded 2026-07-28 from a positioning session; options and rationale, not yet authorial decisions except where marked. Companion to `meta-blurb.md` (genre/positioning rulings) and `meta-cover.md`.*

## Free opening chapters — yes, and more than two

Give the opening chapters away free (own site sample; retail Look Inside
exposes ~10% regardless — through roughly chapter 4).

- An excerpt does not compromise the book's "unpublished" status for the few
  doors where that matters (manuscript prizes, trade review outlets).
- **The wrinkle:** chapter 1 is {{The Bench}} — a full explicit Pace/Randi
  BDSM scene. As a sample it is a superb genre handshake (heat register,
  prose register, interiority claim, honest self-selection) — but a
  two-chapter sample ends before Vee exists, i.e. before the engine the
  blurb promises is visible.
- **Therefore: run the free sample at least through {{The Pointing Game}}**
  (chapter 3) — the moment the game is declared and Vee is pointed at; where
  blurb-promise and page fuse and the one-more-chapter compulsion ignites.
  Free-through-the-hook beats free-N-chapters; the conversion asset is
  accumulation, so generosity up front is cheap.

## Platform map — free archives (discovery via serialization)

Serialize early chapters with a pointer to where the whole book lives; all
under the pen name.

- **Literotica** — the giant; tens of millions of readers; strong
  series-following culture and author pages. Median content is functional
  stroke fiction but a real literary sub-audience exists (Novels & Novellas).
  Self-promo tolerated in author bios. Volume 1's opening chapters outclass
  the shelf there.
- **StoriesOnline**, **Lush Stories** — same model, smaller; SOL skews to
  committed serial readers who binge long works.
- **Archive of Our Own** — accepts original work; explicit fully welcome;
  excellent tag-driven discovery; original fiction swims against the fanfic
  current.
- **Wattpad — ruled out:** prohibits explicit sexual content; unpostable.
- **Reddit** — intelligence more than posting: r/EroticAuthors is the
  genre's working business forum (pricing/platform/market data from active
  sellers); reader subs are word-of-mouth surface.

## Prestige venues (reader-facing credential + discovery)

The lane that fits the positioning better than any archive:

- ***Erotic Review*** (relaunched as a literary-erotica magazine) — exactly
  the venue for the Gaitskill-shelf reader.
- ***Best Women's Erotica of the Year*** (Cleis Press anthologies) —
  submission-based, paid, real in-genre prestige.
- **The move:** adapt a standalone short from **{{Two Towels}} + {{A Round}}
  combined** and submit. {{A Round}} alone is all payoff, no contract — a
  cold reader needs what {{Two Towels}} builds (the house revealing him, the
  heat turned up before she came, the sun-porch offer) so the measuring is
  *elected*, not merely occurring. Combined arc: arrival → trust → offer →
  the body betraying its wanting → deferred ending. The deferral is a
  feature for the literary lane (ending on the unfinished dress, not
  consummation), and the pairing makes the "more than sex seduces her"
  argument portable — the excerpt markets the actual book.
  Reaches actual buyers of literary erotica; earns a bio line; costs a
  submission email. Verify each venue's current excerpt/reprint terms.
- **Adaptation is compression, not concatenation:**
  - Target ~2.5–6k words (venue norms); two full chapters run ~7–9k. Trim
    {{Two Towels}} to only what the fitting spends — a few load-bearing
    rooms, the heat, the offer.
  - **Strip the series cargo** — above all the second towel, a reread plant
    whose meaning requires Randi to exist; in a standalone it's noise. Audit
    every beat whose charge is deferred to later chapters; keep only what
    detonates inside the story's own walls.
  - Untouchable: the "Oops," "Keep still" at audible cost, the knee-walk
    circuit — the scene's own engine.
