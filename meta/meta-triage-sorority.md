# Triage — Sorority (line-edit pass, 2026-08-07)

Source: `audits/line-edit/sorority.md` (14 findings + 1 pre-ruled FIX-AT),
reviewed with the author item by item. Enriched with five cold reads
(claude-fable-5, claude-opus-4-8, gpt-5.5, gpt-5.6-sol, gpt-5.6-terra) —
the most uniformly praised chapter of the pass so far. Two readers named the
same friction the editor found: the boutique rationalization pressing its
mechanism more than once (`:101`) and the middle-store comedy running a beat
long. Both were fixed by cutting the narrator, never the material readers
quoted.

## Fixed

- **`:5`** — the opening sentence split; the object inventory and its
  re-seeing become their own sentence, and "she'd brought in with her"
  became "Randi had brought in with her" to preserve the 2026-08-01
  referent fix, which the split would otherwise have broken.
- **`:7`** — "Randi stepped in, delighted." → "Randi stepped in." (the
  dialogue is the delight; same ruling as {{All Told}} `:29`/`:57`).
- **`:9`** — "the way she'd been seen in his mirror" → "as she'd been seen".
- **`:15`** — "Vee turned the way she'd turned" → "Vee turned as she'd turned".
- **`:19`** — the abstract list and the telegraph cut: "The cool press of the
  silk … the small stillness, the pleasure of being looked at — and one faint
  thing under it she didn't stop to name." → "Vee held still under the hand,
  the silk pressed where Randi had settled it." Removes the announcement of a
  withheld thing (the machinery at the surface) and the third "cool" in three
  paragraphs.
- **`:21`** — "take in the whole of it again" → "take in all of it again"
  (`whole-of` tic; also echoed `:5`'s "taking the whole double in").
- **`:39`** — cut "as hunts do when they stop paying out" (gloss on a turn
  the next four paragraphs dramatize).
- **`:43`** — cut "Making herself enormous, apparently, was the answer to
  being called big-footed." (interiority reading the joke aloud).
- **`:51`** — cut "and she let it go before it could be anything" (second
  statement of one dismissal; Vee's own cover story kept).
- **`:65`** — cut "delighted with the pair of them".
- **`:79`** — cut "and the floor did a quiet thing under her" (see the
  designed-rhyme note below).
- **`:83`** — the gloss stack around *lover* cut to "The word was more than
  Vee had ever let herself call him, and Pace rearranged in her own chest
  into something adult and lit."
- **`:85`** — "a heel like a held breath" → "a heel that looked like it could
  not possibly hold her" (echo ruling #6 FIX-AT; the replacement also sets up
  `:97`'s "being stood up by one").
- **`:101`** — cut "A small betrayal of the whole of it."
- **`:105`** — cut "with nothing behind it but next month".
- **`:109`** — the price stated three times reduced to the anchored form:
  "a multiple of Vee's — the price of the shoes Vee was white-knuckling, and
  then several times that again". **Inverts the reader-quote evidence** on
  purpose (gpt-5.5's account uses "several times it"); the anchored form makes
  the reader run the arithmetic against the card Vee just signed for.
- **`:113`** — cut "and her mouth went dry and fond" (author ruling: a dry
  mouth is a sensation Vee cannot perceive on Randi, and "fond" is a visible
  expression — the clause welded the two and attributed both to the non-POV
  character; the next line of dialogue *is* dry and fond).
- **`:117`** — "real and whole and unrehearsed, the kind that bends you at the
  middle, the kind you cannot perform" → "whole and unrehearsed, the kind that
  bends you at the middle". Protects `:49`'s reader-quoted "the real laugh".

## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **`:93` "felt the floor of her tip, a small swing of the room"** — the
  editor recommended varying this instance of the twice-used floor figure.
  **Inverted:** at `:93` the moving floor is load-bearing (it is what her hand
  shoots out to *stop*, and the reach for Randi's shoulder is praised by three
  readers); at `:79` it was decorative. The `:79` instance was cut instead.
  The `a-small` linter hit here is sanctioned by the same reasoning.
- **`:7`, `:13`, `:81`, `:95` — the remaining `the way` constructions.**
  Argued and kept: `:13` is the paragraph's best figure, `:81` is the class
  observation the boutique section rests on, `:7` is inside reader-quoted
  territory (Cassie's bed), and `:9`/`:15`/`:95` are a designed three-beat
  callback to the mirror in {{Peekaboo}}. Only the *density* in the first nine
  lines was the problem, and `:9`/`:15` fixed it.
- **`:9` "she heard herself say"** — `filter-verbs` hit left standing: the
  wanting getting there before the plan, and Vee catching her own voice
  already doing it, is the sentence's whole subject. Body before mind working,
  not a filter.
- **`:83` "in her own chest"** — the `in-her-chest` locus tic (13 book-wide,
  logged at the {{All Told}} review). Reader-praised here by two models as the
  exact phrasing for what *lover* does to Pace; stands pending the
  end-of-pass count.
- **`:61` "Something delighted broke over Randi's whole face"** — an instance
  of the `Something [verb]ed in [X]'s [body part]` frame, an unruled echo
  candidate. Not touched; awaits the end-of-pass inventory.
