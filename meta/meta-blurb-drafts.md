> Vee has to have a woman. She cannot risk Randi. Randi and Pace already know who it will be.
>
> Summer empties the campus. Cassie leaves for an internship out of state, and Vee and Randi stay to dig for Dr. Marsh, at separate sites, in long hard days they love. Vee all but lives at Pace's house now. The van ride out to the field is the only time she gets with Randi, and every minute of it is precious.
>
> Vivienne Thorne has said the want aloud once, to Pace alone. Now she asks him for a stranger, with him there to keep her safe, on her own terms. She won't be told the night. She'll be blindfolded, and the woman won't say a word until it's over. Pace adds one thing, and asks first: her hands, bound. She says yes.
>
> Randi, who hears everything, takes Vee shopping and plays the game Pace once played with her, until Vee is nervous and impatient and hungry for it. Laughing, she moves the plan from Pace watching to Pace joining in.
>
> Randi and Pace believe the truth, when it comes, will set all three of them free. They have loved her for a year.
