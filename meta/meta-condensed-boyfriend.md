# Condensed — Boyfriend (the threesome plant)

*Scene · Unwritten · late January · that same Saturday night → into Sunday, immediately after {{On Her Floor}} · Vee POV*

The mixer kiss ({{On Her Floor}}) has nowhere to go: Vee can't take it to Randi (framed as a show for Harrison, so naming it exposes her own want), and Cassie can't hold the bodily/queer charge. So she brings it to Pace — the only door left. Processing it turns hot; *afterward*, cooled, he floats the seed. But the confession is **also a concealment**, and the scene is where Vee stops being only the routed one and becomes a moral agent in her own right.

## Function

- **The threesome plant (first, *failed* tread).** After she's come down, Pace asks — flat, idle, daylight register — whether she's ever thought about being with a woman. Deniable, real curiosity AND the move at once. This is his *Pointing-Game technique* run on Vee: her body already answered (in the recounting), so the plain question fishes for **the container she'll build.** Unlike Randi in {{The Pointing Game}}, who handed him silk (*"only in a threesome, with you"*), Vee **can't build it yet** — she door-slams and accuses. Ends open, nothing agreed. The clean conversion is hers, a later day ({{A Threesome, With You}}); her growth across spring is measured by how she answers the *same* question the second time. Full arc: [[meta-note-threesome-reveal]].
- **Vee's Vol-2 moral-agent turn.** She lies to Pace in two moves. *Small first:* the fast, tenderness-less arrival sex was *"just our night — my body was expecting you"* — said while she's picturing Randi's hand on her (the reader watches the migration run under the words). *Then the **big lie**:* when he asks whether there's anything else he should know and whether she wants to sleep with a woman, she denies it. Two people concealing Randi in one bed — his by generalizing off the name, hers by denying — and the reader watches both. This grays her.
- **Motivates the still-unannounced {{Forbidden Kiss}}** (taste-thread gate #1 — keep the coated kiss deniable): gives Vee's *"I'm not gay"* door-slam a concrete referent (the just-happened Randi kiss).

## The opening — a conscious inversion of {{Nothing Underneath}}

Ch50 ran this exact journey (a charged-underwear decision → a night drive to Pace's → waking him late at his door) on **courage aimed at Pace.** This scene runs the same skeleton on **cowardice fleeing toward Pace** — she's afraid to tell Randi, afraid to feel it, so she launders the want through the boyfriend. The reader measures her against her own bravest night and catches her hiding. Make the echo *deliberate* via four inversions:

1. **Brave → coward** (the spine).
2. **Clean-and-bare-for-him → stained-and-still-on.** She's home in the dorm after the coda; starts to peel the green thong, her own mess stops her (the shame-flip: would've horrified her once, now it only tells her she meant it), and she leaves it *on* because she's fleeing, not dressing. The thong never comes off — that's what keeps it from being icky.
3. **Self-denial as power → self-denial as evasion.** Ch50 she stops herself on the edge *so Pace can be the one* ("it left her feeling, of all things, powerful"). Here she starts to touch herself — and stops with *"I have a boyfriend, he's better at it, I don't need to do this myself."* The identical act of not-finishing, now **evasion, not power** — the migration engine with a believable alibi bolted on.
4. **Pace's smoothness → Randi's trespass.** The self-touch is *ambushed*: she catches the fresh wax and the imagined hand there is **Randi's**, where Pace should be. The bareness is the one thing she made *purely for him* ({{Barely Stings}} — *"the submission is Pace's alone"*); Randi breaking into it is the deepest transgression of the opening. *"I have a boyfriend"* is her **failed eviction.** Render the trespass *as* trespass (her register clocks it as his), and vary the gesture from the Barely-Stings coda — an ambush, not the staged three-hands ritual — so it's development, not repeat. ({{On Her Floor}} gave *Randi's mouth*; here it's *Randi's touch on the new smoothness*.)

Then: she pulls **Randi's borrowed skirt** back on — telling herself *"he'd want to see me in the skirt,"* wrapping herself in Randi to go to Pace and calling it loyalty (a shift from the coda, where the skirt was "the only thing that misbehaved" — now she *chooses* it). Night drive. Wakes him.

## The sex — two movements, and the doubling

**Movement one (arrival):** fast, and — the tell Pace reads — *missing their usual tenderness even inside the urgency.* It's aimed past him. **His challenge to her about that absence is what opens the reveal** (Pace-initiated; he catches the vector and names it). Her first, small lie answers the challenge: *just our night, my body was expecting you* — spoken while she thinks of Randi's hand on the new smoothness.

**Movement two (the recounting):** the talk re-arouses them into a second round. She ends up on top, him inside her, recounting the kiss — Randi's actual {{On Her Floor}} choreography (the slow handful of her ass, breasts crushed together, the hip-press) — and reliving it re-arouses her; she feels him go hard again. The heat is the **doubling** ({{Nothing Underneath}}'s engine): reader and body strung between the man she's fucking and the woman she's describing, the absent third overlaid on the present partner. **Structural mirror of {{Gone}}** (there Pace + Randi get off on the absent Vee; here Pace + Vee on the absent Randi — Pace the constant corner who knows all three sides; echo one concrete gesture so the rhyme lands). What leaks isn't just Saturday — it's the whole fall's want and every goodbye-kiss. She does **not** think *I'm using Pace to relive Randi* (body before mind; the prose never names the migration). Register: a hard 3, explicit via specificity and overlay, never clinical.

The **joke-deflect fires after, not during** — too far in to deflect while she's in it; the deflection is her *recovery* mechanism. *"My sex brain took it too far"* — her voice, not narration. And the guilt, voiced with the alibi built into the sentence: ***"It wasn't cheating. But it felt like cheating."***

## The name, the misfile, the probe, the tell

She **must** name Randi in the telling (he already knows she was at a party with Randi tonight; the punch requires the word), and his arousal spikes at *Randi* specifically — Vee reads it as him lit by the *story*, files it under *the usual, a guy who wants two girls* (**concealment done by Vee, for him**). She minimizes: *it was a joke, Randi's my best friend, it meant nothing.*

Because she's just stressed the **friendship**, Pace's probe **generalizes** — *anything else I should know? … do you want to sleep with a woman?* — **"a woman," not "Randi."** Three things at once: tact, the deniable threesome plant, and his own concealment (steering the heat off the one name he can't be idle about). The **big lie** is Vee's answer: she denies it — the *"I'm not gay / it was a joke"* door-slam. Then she flips it outward, and in the accusing she's the one who re-speaks the name: *you want this — you want Randi.* And Pace **defends himself** — *"You're the one telling me you want Randi."* True (she does), deflecting (off himself), and the crack: **he has never defended himself before** — he over-guards the very name he'd just generalized away from (the generalization + the over-defense together are the tell). Vee reads a boyfriend batting away a jealous jab; the reader, fifty chapters into his serene wall, clocks it. **Hairline only** — warmth holds.

## The landing — relief, then the wire

**Immediate relief: she got away with it.** Then, lying in the dark next to Pace — warm, safe, *his* — her mind goes straight back to Randi: what she'll say at the next brunch, Randi's smiling face across the table, Randi's lipstick always done so well, Randi's mouth on her. She just had great sex with her boyfriend, asleep an arm's length away; she's had sex **five times in a day** — and it's *still Randi*. **The flight failed** — the sex didn't discharge the want, it compounded it (canon: the appetite builds, never satisfies). Close on the *frightened, unproductive* shame — not fuel this time but bewilderment she can't answer: ***What's wrong with me?*** Keep it bewildered — she names **nothing**, diagnoses nothing; the reader assembles the migration she can't (body before mind). The live wire for spring: guilt she lied to bury, want she can't fuck away, and — the cold she can't see — she's now the one keeping a wall, safe *because he believed her.*

## The word-withholding triangle (the graying)

This scene is where **Vee joins the withholders**, which retroactively grays her whole case against Pace's unsaid *"I love you."* The triangle is **asymmetric** (thesis: "Operational Muteness") — three *different* withheld sentences:

- **Pace → Vee:** can't say **"I love you"** (the appetite can't run in the plain register).
- **Vee → Randi:** can't say **"I want you"** — canonically *appetite-structural* (the received version defuses the charge; "bare is empty") **and**, from here on, *cowardice.* Both true; let the scene hold both, don't overwrite the appetite frame with fear.
- **Randi → Vee:** *says* "I love you" freely (agape over eros — she means it doubly, no qualifier); withholds only **"I want you"** in a register that forces Vee to decide ({{Fairytale}}: "the word kept its cover").

She demands from Pace the one plain sentence she herself cannot produce (to Randi) — different words, identical refusal of the dangerous sentence. Works as a **plant**: the reader banks Vee's lie and cowardice here, and it quietly undercuts her future grievance about the word — exactly when (per {{Nothing Underneath}}) she's already "stopped asking."

## Register law

Warm on both reads; the cold lives only in what the reader knows (he's Randi's lover). Pace's *"it wasn't cheating"* and his blessing must read **true and warm on the page** — the defends-himself beat is the *one* permitted crack, small enough not to tip first-read warmth into sinister. Body before mind — she names none of the machinery.
