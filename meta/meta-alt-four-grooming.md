## The problem it addresses

Randi's alibi as drafted reads as grooming — not as dread the reader assembles, but as
operation stated plainly in a character's mouth, which is the one thing
`meta-craft-pace.md`'s Console rules forbid: *"the manipulation stays structurally intact
and reread-visible in the facts … it must never live in the tone."* Four features carry
the read, two of them inessential: **"get her used to"** (a desensitization verb, making
Vee the material), **"for when she's ready"** (someone else assessing her readiness),
"Somebody has to" (assignment), and "it's work" (load-bearing — the alibi that
*"you don't have to do it for me"* must demolish).

## Alternate — primacy and possession instead of conditioning (author phrasing 2026-10-05)

> "I'm doing it for *you,*" Randi said.
>
> "I didn't ask who it was for."
>
> "Because you want her — us, together." She was shoving back onto him and not waiting to be given it. "And when it happens somebody is going to be the first woman she ever kisses like that, and it is going to be *me*—" and she heard it and hauled it back in. "Which is *work.* It's not a treat, Pace, it's—"

Vee stops being processed and becomes contested. The truth escapes mid-sentence and she
scrambles back to the alibi — the chapter's established pattern — so the architecture is
unchanged and *"You don't have to do it for me"* still lands on a restored lie. The
*"us, together"* pre-echoes her later *"For us"* at the inspired-by-her beat.

**Minimal third option, if the full rewrite is too much:** keep the cadence, cut only
*"For when she's ready"*, and change the verb — *"Somebody has to be the mouth she
already knows."* Familiarity instead of desensitization; loses perhaps a third of the
grooming read.

## The counter-argument for standing pat — and it is strong

The live clause is the single most effective sentence the capture instrument has ever
measured on this book. **"Somebody has to get her used to my mouth." is the verbatim
ALMOST-STOPPED line in six lanes across six different models** (claude-opus-5,
claude-opus-5-5, claude-fable-5-1, gpt-5.6-sol, glm-5.3, gemini-3.8-flash) — and every
one attached it to a 9 or a 10 and kept reading. gemini's fsog-refugee lane, at
ROMANCE 0: *"seeing the premeditated grooming laid completely bare made my stomach turn
… there is no way I'm putting the book down now."* She named it grooming and read on
because of it.

So the question is not whether the line works. It is whether the dread should arrive
**spoken** or **inferred**. Standing pat keeps the most-flagged sentence in the book;
the alternate moves the same dread back into inference, where the craft rules put it,
at the cost of the measured spike.
