Craft-level line-edit pass (`audits/line-edit/the-induction.md`, 13 findings +

and the 2026-07-28 cold-read triage below were all applied as filters first.
**Verdicts here re-open only on a text edit to the passage.**

## Fixed

- **The room's comprehension rendered twice (`:23`)** — "the small still beat
  where a warm dull room worked out that there was nothing between it and her
  but the silk" cut; the run now ends "and she let it travel down the rows."
  The audible version forty words later ("a murmur went through the crowd as
  she came, the sound a dull room makes when it wakes up") is the stronger and
  now the only one. **Note this partially reverses the 2026-07-28 fix below**,
  which installed the concrete-fact sentence in response to panel feedback;
  the author accepted that trade deliberately, to avoid pre-spending the
  murmur, and **the cold readers will be re-run on the chapter before this is
  considered settled.** Also removed the third "nothing" and the second
  "silk", and cleared the `there-was-were` linter hit as a side effect.
- **Third rendering of the decision/body beat (`:35`)** — "— the one where
  she'd decided, and her body had come up to meet the decision" cut; now
  "knew to the heartbeat what second it was." The beat is acted at `:21`–`:23`
  and glossed at `:25`; the third was a weaker paraphrase of `:25`.
- **The send over-prepared (`:37`)** — cut the motive clause ("because he'd
  been sorry to miss it and this was the nearest thing to handing it to him")
  and the frame re-inventory ("the leg and the shoulder and the woman who had
  it handled", which re-listed `:33` and respent its "handled" coinage).
  "Uncropped" kept — load-bearing ({{Cropped}} origin). Clears the run-up to
  "He would have no idea what else was in the frame. That part was hers." —
  the pair all six readers named as the chapter's best.
- **"the single thing he'd built it to do" → "the thing…" (`:37`)** — bonus
  item, flagged by gpt-5.6-sol (the phrasing credits Pace with a predetermined
  purpose at the moment the chapter argues the choice was Vee's). The
  attribution is kept — it is free indirect, and the pivot needs something for
  "That part was hers" to take back — but "single" overclaimed, and sits badly
  against `:13`'s "the most anyone had ever made for her." Canon note: the
  slit is never proposed or explained anywhere in the book; Vee discovers it
  alone at `peekaboo:43`.
- **"The danger of it was behind her now" → "The dread of it" (`:41`)** — a
  precision error, not a rhythm one: "danger" describes the *room*, and the
  chapter has spent forty lines establishing the room is warm, dull and kind
  with no danger in it. What was behind her was her own state. Lore check
  confirmed direct naming of Vee's exposure-fear has extensive precedent
  (`a-round:152`, `:166`, `:168`, `:198`; `vee-on-the-bench:63`, `:107`,
  `:459`; `burn:159`, `:161`; `grace:189`; `fairytale:133`;
  `nothing-underneath:7`) — the book names the emotion *alongside* the somatic
  rendering, not instead of it. "Dread" specifically, to rhyme with
  `a-round:198` ("The dread drained out and something warmer and bolder rushed
  in… a kind of power she'd never been issued") — same noun, same job, the
  fear named as it departs at the hinge into power. PROTECTED as a designed
  echo: `audits/line-edit/echo-rulings.md` #61. "Terror" rejected as
  overshooting the chapter's managed register.
- **"about it" cut (`:41`)** — trailing weak "it" in the run-up to the closing
  sentence. The seeded phrase "didn't make herself small" is untouched.
- **CONTINUITY ERROR — "up the mountain the day she named the dress" cut
  (`:15`).** The line-audit missed this. On the mountain (`leave-no-trace`,
  Sun Sep 27) Vee names a *magazine* dress she cannot afford — silk, "russet,
  like the red of a brick" — and sets it down ("There's a black dress in the
  back of my closet"). There is **no slit** (it enters the book at
  `peekaboo:43`, Thu Oct 15, discovered in his mirror), **no ownership**, and
  **no anticipatory fantasy of any kind** in that scene. Pace deliberately did
  not make the dress she described — `two-towels:103` ("he hadn't matched the
  dress she'd described. He'd matched *her*"). She therefore could not have
  pictured the dramatic entrance there. The mountain also already fired two
  lines earlier at `:13`. The whole parenthetical was cut (author chose the
  clean version over retaining the correct Peekaboo citation); `:7` already
  cites that daydream twice, and the canonical source is `peekaboo:67`.
- **"the same highlight the silk gave up when it shimmered" cut (`:11`)** —
  the metaphor explained after it lands. "A frosted plum that woke and threw
  the light when it moved" makes the rhyme available; kimi-k3's praise
  attaches to that clause, not the gloss.
- **"the give and take of her in the good light" cut (`:23`)** — the one vague
  figure in a paragraph of exact body coordinates, and the third "give" in the
  run ("gave the room her leg" / "giving them away" / "give and take").
  Redundant besides: "giving them away **a step at a time**" already names the
  alternation. Puts gpt-5.6-terra's named peak ("She stalked across the stage
  giving them away a step at a time") against "and did not once make herself
  small."
- **"The eyes found the leg because she'd handed it to them." → "The eyes
  found her leg." (`:25`)** — the because-clause re-explained a motive given
  eight lines above in the same terms. Author changed the article as well: the
  chapter says "her leg" everywhere it matters (`:23`, `:41`), and with the
  `:37` inventory cut this was the lone definite-article instance. Restores
  possession at the moment the room takes it.
- **"stood there resettled" → "stood there and resettled" (`:11`)** — the
  participle buried the sentence's one physical event (the weight shifting
  forward) as a condition rather than a motion. Likely a seam from the
  2026-07-28 cut that removed this sentence's tail.

## Added

- **Cassie states the impact (`:33`).** "Like a movie star. **You sure got
  their attention.** Somebody had to document it." Author's design, replacing
  a "stop the room" callback that was considered and rejected. Vee was on the
  stage facing front and did not see the room turn; Cassie was in the seats
  and did, so this is new information rather than a restatement of `:23`. And
  because **Cassie does not know how high the slit went**, she names the
  effect and never the cause — which protects `:25` ("not a soul in it could
  have said so out loud") rather than breaching it. `:31`'s "How did I look?"
  now reads as Vee unable to take in what she is handed. Full reasoning:
  `audits/line-edit/echo-rulings.md` #62.

## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **"the small choreography of a woman with a secret keeping it" (`:9`)** —
  re-flagged by the line editor as overwork; **dropped without presenting it**,
  already ruled 2026-07-28 (below) and quoted admiringly by kimi-k3.
- **"it was only a ___" twice (`:13`)** — not repetition but a designed
  asymmetry: the first is a thing she *refused* to say (she would not diminish
  the dress), the second a thing she *did* say and failed at ("it had come out
  thin"), with the reason she could not do the first sitting between them
  ("the most anyone had ever made for her"). The second sentence is also the
  settled product of the 2026-07-28 parse-stumble fix.
- **"no performance in it" (`:13`)** — kimi-k3 quoted it as landing sincere.
  The editor's supporting count is stale: it cited a third "sorry" at `:37`,
  which this same pass cut. Two remain, in one sentence, as deliberate
  anaphora. The clause also does distinct work — "the quiet way he was sorry"
  describes his manner; "no performance in it" is Vee's appraisal of it.
- **"a moment longer" (`:37`)** — bare comparative, ruled idiom, not the
  `held-past` tic. Five instances in Volume One (two in consecutive chapters)
  and no cold reader flagged any. The linter rule was narrowed rather than the
  prose edited; see echo-rulings #63.
- **"longer than the walking took" (`:15`)** — the genuine `held-past` figure
  and load-bearing (it measures the room's indifference in exactly the units
  the long step overturns). `--ack`ed in `style/style-allow.toml`.
- **No "stop the room" callback.** The phrase stays out of the chapter
  permanently; the payoff is enacted and then reported by Cassie. Ledger #62,
  which also corrects the report's over-count (two Sorority setups, not four —
  and `sorority:55` is Vee pricing a shoe she does not buy).

## Cold-read verification of the line edit (2026-08-09)

`claude-opus-4-8`, `gpt-5.5`, `gpt-5.6-sol`, `gpt-5.6-terra`. (Kimi skipped —

**The `:23` cut is settled — do not re-litigate.** 4/4 registered the room's
response to the walk; 0/4 showed confusion or a gap. Three of four promoted the
surviving line to best-in-chapter (opus on the murmur: "that's the sentence
I'll keep"; gpt-5.5 and sol on "the room knew, and not a soul in it could have

this pass had been quoted approvingly** — the pre-spend risk did not

Also confirmed: the `:15` continuity cut cost nothing (no reader groped for the
missing anticipation); "dread" at `:41` landed (terra absorbed it into its own
vocabulary, unprompted); the `:37` trim of "single" removed sol's only stated
friction.

**The two new criticisms point in opposite directions and cancel.** terra: the
crowd reads "more collectively attuned than I fully believe" — a texture note
about the crowd as one mind, *not* an objection to the room noticing. opus:
"the room stays an abstraction… I briefly wanted a beat of her actually
noticing an individual face." Same passage, inverse objections. Neither should
drive a change on its own; the calibration is sitting in the middle.

**Cassie's "You sure got their attention" stands — author ruling.** It drew no
reaction praise (gpt-5.5 and sol logged it only in the chapter record; opus
quoted six other Cassie lines and not that one) and terra's reviewer guessed it
contributed to a room-notices-her repetition. The author rejects that reading:
Cassie is a friend at the coffee afterward, not the room, and the chapter's
premise *is* that Vee intentionally shows more and gets the room to notice.

**`:35` — resolved by relocation, not restoration.** The cut clause ("the one
where she'd decided, and her body had come up to meet the decision") was the
most-cited loss across the panel. It was not restored: `:25` already renders
decision-then-body at the moment it happens, so restoring `:35` would double
it. Instead the stronger phrasing moved into `:25` —
"catching up to a thing she'd decided" → **"coming up to meet a thing she'd
decided."** Point kept, position of maximum force, no doubling.

Correction for the record: opus's report that the body reads as *lagging* the
decision derives from `:25`'s "catching up," which was never cut — it was not
evidence of loss from the `:35` edit.

## Fixed

- **Thesis said aloud** ("she had stopped being able to tell the difference
  between what she wanted and what the dress wanted for her") — both Claude
  readers quoted it back as the book's tagline (Fable: "the tagline wearing a
  dress"; Opus: "That's the tagline, isn't it"). Lore check confirmed the line
  was a draft invention, not a planned beat, and a breach of the scene's Guards
  ("stays embodied, not thesis"); it also pre-stated the planned ending's idea,
  demoting the close to a reprise. Cut: the sentence now ends at "because the
  dress wanted the height" — the personification carries the conflation
  unglossed, and the reread charge survives. The panel proved the mechanism:
  both readers composed the cut sentence themselves.
- **The room's comprehension over-narrated** ("worked out exactly what it was
  looking at" — gpt-5.6-sol: "presses a little hard"; gpt-5.5 flagged the
  adjacent "the room knew" as telling the reader how to read the room; style
  linter warned on `register` as verb in the same sentence). Author's intent —
  the room deduces the concrete fact of bare-under-silk via the open slit — was
  under-stated, which is what let readers take it as a mood claim. Recast to
  the fact: "she let it travel down the rows, the small still beat where a warm
  dull room worked out that there was nothing between it and her but the silk."
  Line 25's "the room knew, and not a soul in it could have said so out loud"
  kept untouched — with the knowledge specific, the unspeakability line lands.
  "Translucent/thin silk" variants considered and rejected on canon (charmeuse,
  "heavier than it looked," "a good weight"; the lined bodice/hips) and on
  mechanism (the knowledge arrives through the slit, not through the fabric).
- **Parse stumble** ("She'd tried only a ceremony" — gpt-5.6-sol: "took me a
  beat to parse"). Recast plainly: "She'd tried telling him it was only a
  ceremony." (Italics-as-tried-speech option offered per house convention;
  author chose the plain recast.)
