# The Eros Ladder — feeling-registers across the trilogy

**What this doc owns.** The vocabulary and architecture of erotic feeling in the novel:
the tiers of wanting (heat → want/yearning → love), the forces that ride on them
(shame, apprehension), the two reserved threshold words (`desire`, `yearning`), and the
mutual-disguise contract that drives the SATC track. It is the *how-to-render* companion
to `meta-arch-vivienne.md` (Vee's arc, the tell-rule) and `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md`
(the Randi relationship). The chronology owns scene order; this doc owns the feeling-words.

**Spoiler-grade.** Do not migrate any of this into `CLAUDE.md` (inherited by the blind
cold-readers). It stays in `meta/`.

Built descriptively-then-prescriptively: a full usage audit of `scenes/` (2026-08-31)
established what each word already does before any rule was written. Where a rule departs
from current usage it says so.

## 1. The ladder — Axis 1: *what the wanting is*

| Tier | What it is | Threatens | Pace's relation | Vee's stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Heat** | Bodily sexual arousal — involuntary, present-tense, **transient** | Her **sexuality** (the "not normal / perverted" identity fear) | **Sanctioned** (the threesome plant, "I don't mind") **and extinguishable by him** | Can wait it out; heat passes |
| **2 · Want** (present) / **Yearning** (time-spanning) | Directed desire for a *specific person* — durable; survives the heat passing and survives Pace | The line between friendship and more | **Cannot** extinguish it — *the tell that it isn't heat* | Refuses to see it / can't name it |
| **3 · Love (eros)** | The want's true name — erotic love | Her **monogamy with Pace** (the real infidelity — exclusivity of the heart) + the friendship, irrevocably | The tier his frame can't contain | The word she will not say |

The tiers are the same feeling at rising altitude, not four different feelings. **The two
axes are independent:** *heat is the permitted, extinguishable tier; love is the forbidden,
inextinguishable one; want is the ambiguous middle readable as either* — which is the
equivocation the whole track lives inside.

**The book theorizes its own ladder once**, from Pace's POV — the keystone:
> "not a woman who **hungered for another woman**, which was *unsurvivable*, but a
> girlfriend offering her man a gift, which was *merely sexy*." — `the-pointing-game.md:47`

Tier-2 (durable, directed) vs tier-1 (a gift, transient) named as the exact *survivability*
problem. And the tier-2 self-definition, from Vee's first sight of Randi:
> "the want you keep for a thing you've only ever seen from a distance" — `see-you-later.md:113`

## 2. The two forces on the ladder — *not* a clean second axis

Shame and apprehension are **coupled to the tiers**, not read beside them:

- **Shame is fuel, fused into tier-1 heat.** For Vee, **shame *feeds* arousal** (the engine
  at `my-friend-randi.md:93` — "the wanting coming up hand in hand with the mortification").
  It amplifies, it does not only brake. And it is **gendered and asymmetric**: same-sex heat
  carries *more* shame than het heat, because het is "correct" and a woman is "always wrong"
  in Vee's culture — so her involuntary body (hard nipples, wet for Randi) is **more shameful
  and therefore more arousing** than the same signals for Pace. Shame lives *inside* tier-1. The shame *mechanics* are owned by `meta-craft-vivienne.md`
  (the Three Charge-Sources; the Arousal–Humiliation Loop); the ladder adds only the
  **tier-coupling** and the **same-sex surcharge** — don't relitigate the mechanics here.
- **Apprehension is cost, attending the higher tiers.** The fear of what the want/love
  *means* and what it *costs* (losing Pace or Randi) rides on tiers 2–3. {{Coming Due}}'s
  close is built from it. Its rare positive pole is `all-the-time.md:53` — Pace naming the
  wanting as "the thing gone finally *right*."

So: **shame-as-fuel down low, apprehension-as-cost up high.**

## 3. The umbrella word and the two reserved words

- **`the wanting` / `the want` — the umbrella.** The book's most interior, most
  character-close term; it already slides across all three tiers *without registering as
  clinical*, because it sounds like Vee's own cognition, not the narrator's taxonomy. **Build
  interiority around it.** Do not swap in tier-specific nouns to "clarify" a tier — that
  breaks the voice. The tier is set by context, not by a label word.
- **`desire` — scarce, formal, held in reserve.** Only two instances in all of `scenes/`,
  both Pace-POV, both narrator: `the-pointing-game.md:29` ("a desire too new and bright to
  have a name yet" — the *pre-nameable*) and `the-bench.md:403` ("proof of his desire" —
  arousal named *clinically*, inside a script). Rule: **keep `desire` rare; it marks the
  unnameable or the clinical.** Do **not** give Vee an interior "desire" — it would
  domesticate the word, whose whole charge is that "the wanting" owns her interior register.
- **`yearning` — Vee's private, *late* coinage.** Appears **zero times** currently — a clean
  slate. It is **not** narrator taxonomy and not a prose word until Vee mints it herself, as
  **her own tag** for the more-than-friendship missing, **before Spring Break**. Until then
  the prose renders the *gap* where it belongs — the inadequacy of "missing," her sensing a
  truer word and not reaching for it. Because it arrives as *her* coinage, it is never
  out-of-voice. It joins the withheld-word family alongside "the word" (= love, Pace track).

## 4. Vocabulary registry (from the usage audit)

**Tier-1 carriers, cleanest first:**
- **wet / slick / soaked** — ~95% clean tier-1; the truest signal.
- **pulse / throb** — ~95% clean (`coming-due.md:25` is the key Randi hit).
- **ring / clamor** — peak-intensity; the sharpest hits are Randi-directed (`on-her-floor.md:173,179`).
- **flush** — ~45% arousal, rest is social/moral shame.
- **heat / hot** — the primary word, but **shame-blended**; rarely clean arousal (by design).
- **hum / buzz** — tier-1 *residue* (post-arousal carry-forward).
- **keyed up** — used sparingly, always load-bearing.

**Do NOT use as a tier-1 signal: `warm` / `warmth`.** ~80 hits, only ~5% tier-1 — it is
ambient temperature and emotional texture. (The style linter already flags it; the flag is
warranted.)

**Tier-2 carriers:**
- **want / wanting / wanted** — richly established, both objects (present-tense reach).
- Time-spanning ("yearning") register is currently **lexically unconsolidated** — carried
  ad hoc by **miss / missed / missing** (dominant), plus "the old wanting" (`believe-me.md:13`),
  "hungry for him with the whole state of Virginia between them" (`among-friends.md:111`),
  "ached for the right one" (`barely-stings.md:67`), and {{Coming Due}}'s "for a long time" /
  "all along." Keep rendering it through this family; reserve the *word* "yearning" per §3.

**Tier-3 carriers:**
- **love** — Pace track: full evasion grammar (see §5). Randi track: spoken freely *as
  philos* (that is the disguise, not a leak — see §6).
- **cherish** (1 hit, Pace's interior, `in-her-place.md:5`); **adore** (state-of-being-received,
  not a feeling Vee names).

## 5. The Pace baseline — eros acknowledged, declaration withheld

The Pace track is the control case: **mutual eros both parties know**, with the *declaration*
withheld, gated by who-says-*I-love-you*-first. Its machinery is a complete evasion grammar:
- "the word," "the big one" — `under-the-rug.md:71,95`
- "the silence at the center where the word should be" — `bare.md:19`
- the italic *I love you* that is meant and cannot be said — `seconds.md:11,61`
- named-then-refused: "She loved him. There." — `above-him.md:21`
- external naming (Cassie) — `school-nights.md:31`
- Pace's displacement: "I love how *good we are together*" — `seconds.md:33`

The category is safe; only the saying is dangerous.

## 6. The two psychologies — the mutual-disguise contract

Both tracks are **mutual eros**. The difference is *what* is disguised. On the Randi track,
the **category itself** (eros vs philos) is disguised — by **both women, for different
reasons.** Same philos cover, opposite interiors: **Randi's disguise is a performance over a
thing she sees; Vee's is a blindfold over a thing she won't.**

### Vee
- **Self-deception about the category** — files heat + eros as *philos*. Self-deception
  about desire is entirely in character for her.
- **Heat-denial timeline:** denied *even the heat* for Randi until {{On Her Floor}} and
  {{Boyfriend}} (where it became undeniable). By {{Coming Due}} she is *reconciling* the heat —
  attributing it to sex-brain + missing-Pace, excuses **she doesn't actually buy** — while
  still self-deceiving about the desire underneath.
- **Fear hierarchy:** primary = **losing Pace or Randi**; second but *strong* = **being a
  lesbian / doing something morally repugnant.**
- **Shame:** **moral** (lesbianism is *wrong* in her culture) **+ perversion** ("not
  normal"). And it **feeds** her arousal (§2).
- **The philos naming is the disguise, not a leak.** Vee can say "loved her so much it
  ached" (`among-friends.md:77`) *precisely because* she routes it through friend-love. The
  open naming *is* the cover. **Do not build Pace-style "won't-say-the-word" refusal grammar
  on the Randi track** — the guard here is **misnaming**, not silence.

### Randi
- **Never self-deceived that she is hot for Vee** — the heat was always owned.
- **Her self-deception is narrower and it *moves*:** it begins as heat **+ need**, and she
  tells herself it's *only heat* — the lie is denying the **need.** It evolves into eros.
- **Fear:** her **need** for Vee (the dependency/exposure) — *not* the desire, *not*
  morality.
- **Shame:** **social convention**, not morality. No internal issue with lesbianism at all —
  which is why the threesome frame to Pace ({{The Pointing Game}}) is socially survivable to
  her where "hungering for another woman" is not.
- **Render her POV as knowing-but-concealing** (need-hiding), not self-blind.

### The arcs (stages; exact story-order per `meta-plan-chronology.md`)
**Vee:** denies even heat → heat undeniable ({{On Her Floor}}, {{Boyfriend}}) → reconciles heat,
self-deceives about desire ({{Coming Due}}) → the pre-Spring-Break braid ripens the
recognition (§8) → mints "yearning" before Spring Break → (eros, later).

**Randi:** "only heat," need denied → evolves toward eros → lies to self **and** Pace,
reframing as heat / *for Pace's benefit* ({{The Pointing Game}}) — **fools Pace not at all**,
fools **herself only for a little while** → fully admits it to herself, "her gorgeous"
({{Fairytale}}) → settled, knows it's eros ({{Gone}}).

## 7. The Vol 3 threesome trap

The structural payoff of the ladder. The threesome is offered and accepted as **heat** —
the tier Pace sanctioned. Vee says yes to *tier 1*. But the woman is **Randi**, whom she
**loves** (tier 3) — so the act she frames as spending heat actually **consummates eros.**
She agrees to the one tier Pace allows and delivers the one he doesn't: **unintentional
infidelity.** And it is where **both covers cash out** — Randi has spent the whole track
framing her want-for-Vee as "for Pace's benefit," the identical threesome cover, so the bed
is where her performance and Vee's blindfold both resolve into the real thing. Same act,
three tiers, everyone equivocating.

## 8. {{Coming Due}} on the ladder + the pre-Spring-Break braid

{{Coming Due}} is the **heat-reconciliation + self-deception** chapter:
- Heat named and its excuses dissolving ("went through her hands like water," `:9`); Pace
  cannot extinguish it ("not even with him, not even after," `:9`) — the tier-1-fails tell.
- The heat/want boundary named in prose (`:31`–`:33`) — the marked seam.
- The close is built from **desire + confusion + inability to resolve so many challenging
  ideas at once** (`:57`). The final "It wanted a name. She wouldn't give it one" is the
  **flinch of self-deception** — the look-away — *not* a serene, knowing withholding. Keep
  it that way; do not clean it into a tidy recognition.

**The braid (this chapter → Spring Break).** Vee needs Randi's counsel repeatedly and can't
disentangle four strands: (1) needing her **advice**, (2) the **sexy SATC extractions**,
(3) **wanting** her, (4) needing her as a **friend.** The inability to separate them *is* the
recognition deepening — the philos cover fails not in one beat but because "my friend's
counsel" keeps arriving as all four at once. **The coining of "yearning" is the braid's
output**, when "friend" can no longer hold the four strands.

## 9. Rendering rules (quick reference)

1. Build Vee's interior on **"the wanting"**; let context set the tier, not a label word.
2. **`desire`** stays scarce/formal/unnameable; never domesticated in Vee's interior.
3. **`yearning`** is withheld until Vee mints it herself (before Spring Break); until then
   render the *gap*.
4. Tier-1 heat is **shame-fused**; same-sex heat is **more shameful → more arousing.**
5. Never use **`warm`** as a tier-1 signal. Cleanest tier-1: **wet/slick, pulse/throb,
   ring/clamor.**
6. On the Randi track, **misnaming (philos) is the guard**, not silence — no Pace-style
   word-refusal grammar for Vee's Randi-feeling.
7. Render **Randi** as knowing-but-concealing (need-hiding); render **Vee** as self-blind.
