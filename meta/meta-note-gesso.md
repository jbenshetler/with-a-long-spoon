## The photograph — pride and feared disgust

Keep both readings alive. Under Pace’s gaze Vee was glorious, abundant, and fiercely proud, with no shame. Outside that room she cannot hold his reading by herself and fears what another woman will see: excess, wetness, disgust. **“Isn’t it gross?”** is a test of Randi’s reading, not a report that the experience felt gross with Pace. Vee is asking whether Randi will confirm or destroy the meaning Pace gave the image, and asking her to hold a question Vee cannot yet state directly.

Randi simply does not agree that it is gross. That non-agreement is the second spring reframe: enough to let curiosity outrun shame, not enough to settle the question. Do not turn it into an abstract sex-positive lecture or let Randi tell Vee what she tastes like.
