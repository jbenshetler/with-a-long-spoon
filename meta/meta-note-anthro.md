# Note — The Anthropology Class (the spring mirror; the Vee/Randi binding; Cassie's drift)

*Device + thread · spring semester · successor to the fall stats-mirror · Vee POV · companion to `meta-note-among-friends.md`, `meta-condensed-all-told.md`, `meta-note-outlier.md`*

## What it is

The **spring mirror-class**, the fall stats room's successor. Same craft device — a course whose subject silently diagrams the plot, transcribed by Vee and connected to nothing (subtle, never explained; observation before cognition). The classroom vehicle turns over with the semester; the mirror *function* is carried all year. Where stats gave the **aggregate that lies** (Simpson's paradox — the whole reverses the parts, the deception forming), anthropology gives **exchange, taboo, ritual** — the consummation. Anthropology is almost too on-the-nose in the best way, so keep it buried exactly like the stats mirror.

## The binding (why it's this class, with these people)

Fall stats was **accidental** — Vee, Randi, and Cassie happened into one room. Spring anthro is **chosen**: Vee and Randi sign up together, on purpose. The accidental → chosen escalation is the point — Vee electing more Randi is the sealed room beginning at the level of her schedule.

- **Vee suggests it.** She floats and embraces the shared class; Randi simply accepts, her calculation withheld — Randi stays clean, Vee delivers herself (she keeps unknowingly authoring her own trap).
- **No Cassie — and blamelessly.** Cassie is pre-nursing; her sciences-locked track (bio, chem, A&P) had her clear her one humanities elective early — **she already did it, with the oddball Dr. Wardy** — so she has no reason to be in the spring anthro section. (In `all-the-time.md` Vee reports it in one offhand line: *"already did hers, with that oddball Wardy."*) So **divergent tracks, not a snub** — the schedule itself peels the truth-teller off Vee and pairs Vee with the operator two nights a week, plus study sessions and group work. On reread: the schedule separated Cassie from Vee and handed Vee to Randi, and no one did a single wrong thing (the book's engine — *"it was all there,"* no legible villain).
- **The cost, and why it's fine.** We lose an anthro-room group-project Cassandra beat (as `outlier.md` gave in stats). That device already fired once, memorably; repeating it would dull it. Cassie stays the Cassandra **from the dorm**, watching the drift she can't stop — which sharpens the late *"you saw it and I didn't."*
- **Telegraph guard.** Never narrate "Vee was drifting from Cassie." On the page it's just: Vee and Randi have anthro this term; Cassie has clinical-track science. Warm on first read (friends taking a fun class); the contraction visible only in the pattern.

## The subjects (pick one per scene, plant, never gloss)

Vee reads the theory without seeing she's the case. The concepts that rhyme hardest with this plot:

- **Lévi-Strauss — the exchange of women.** Women circulated between men to bind them; Vee as the object trafficked toward the threesome. The devastating one.
- **Douglas — purity and danger / taboo.** The forbidden structuring desire; dirt as matter-out-of-place. Straight into the shame engine.
- **Mauss — the gift.** The gift that obligates, that must be reciprocated; Randi's "friendship" as the gift with a hook, the reciprocity trap. (Sahlins refines it: a *generalized-reciprocity* mask over *negative* reciprocity — taking without equivalent return.)
- **Van Gennep / Turner — liminality, rites of passage.** The initiand stripped of status in the threshold phase. Her whole year; the blindfold is the liminal veil.
- **Participant observation — the observer who is never neutral.** The ethnographer changes what she studies by joining it; Randi *participates* in Vee's life while steering it, Vee the unwitting informant. (Optional companion: "going native" — losing oneself in the culture one entered — for Vee absorbed into Pace's sealed world.)

**Marsh on the page (author rulings 2026-08-21).** A **rock-star lecturer** — the charisma behind *"even her eight a.m. fills up"* is real and rendered. Physical: tall, of indeterminate age, **built like a marathon**; dressed as if about to go on a hike, in a style that — if it were a style — went out of date half a generation ago; **short silver hair with a single black streak through her bangs**; moves with quick energy.

**Her opening question (decided 2026-08-21) — the hau.** Marsh opens the first class with Mauss via the Māori **hau**: a gift carries its giver's spirit, and the spirit wants to return home — dismiss it as mysticism, then explain why you can't regift your grandmother's sweater, why selling a wedding ring feels like selling a person; *something is riding inside the object* — "by the end of this semester you'll be able to tell me what it is." This is her signature move (mechanic 5: the question she opens with and won't answer; ethnographer named before the finding), and the promise makes it a semester-long refrain. **Framing is bond, not power** — the question is about the *receiver's* side (what rides in the thing, why you're held), never "generosity as strategy." That framing is load-bearing for the novel's engine: the gift binds *regardless of the giver's intent* — Pace's generosity is sincere and binds anyway; Randi's is planned *and* loving, interested and sincere at once. Candidates rejected on the way here: incest-taboo opener (spends the Lévi-Strauss deniability budget on the marquee slot; telegraphs), spit-in-the-glass/Douglas (author distaste), potlatch-as-aggression (power frame fights the thesis — nobody in this book wears warmth over calculation like a mask).

## Dr. Marsh is engaging, not a boring clone (author ruling, 2026-08-09)

**This reverses the earlier decision** (commit `f7a3502`), which made Marsh a flat easy-A vehicle who is half-attended and fades out, explicitly cloned from the fall stats prop. That is no longer the design. Marsh is **exciting and engaging** — a lecturer Vee is genuinely lit up by.

**Why the reversal is worth its cost.** Marsh runs the summer two-site excavation (`meta-plan-summer.md:15–22`), where both women are diggers. With a boring Marsh, Vee signing up for a summer in the dirt is arbitrary and needs propping; with a Marsh who lit her up in February, it is *motivated* — and it becomes Vee choosing her own mind, which is precisely what the book is taking from her. No flat-vehicle version buys that.

**What breaks, and must be replaced.** The old concealment was *half-attention* — the mirror seeped in because nobody was listening. An engaging professor cannot be tuned out, and **anthropology has no deniability budget**: stats had cover (numbers are about numbers), but Lévi-Strauss on the exchange of women is a plain-English description of this plot. So the concealment burden rises exactly as the old mechanism disappears.

**The replacement — the irony inverts from passive to active.**

| | Stats prop | Dr. Marsh |
|---|---|---|
| Irony is | **passive** — Vee doesn't notice | **active** — Vee notices, engages hard, and aims it at the wrong target |
| Requires | a narrator nudge to land | nothing; the misfire *is* the demonstration |
| Vee is | absent from her own coursework | brilliant at her coursework, on everyone but herself |

This is the better instrument. It never needs the nudge (see the Bible's nudge ban, and `meta-note-stats-professor.md`), because the reader watches her apply Mauss beautifully to a sorority anecdote or to Cassie's family and never once to the friendship that is buying her. It upgrades the recorded rule *"Vee's misfiled transcription is the punchline"* (`meta-triage-outlier.md:52`) from a note into a scene.

**Mechanics:**

1. **Cases, never principles.** Marsh's engagingness is *anecdotal* — a named people, a concrete practice, a story. She never states the abstraction; the reader assembles it. (The stats prop stated abstractions — *"never all the way gone"* — which is exactly what makes them quotable as thesis.) This is the highest-leverage constraint and it preserves rule (1), *delivers method, never meaning*.
2. **Vee raises her hand.** She is good at this. Her contribution is sharp, correct, and pointed away from herself.
3. **Randi in the room inverts.** In stats, Randi pulls Vee *off* the material. Here Vee is pulled genuinely *toward* it, so Randi must compete — or, better, **Randi is fluent too, and good at it.** Deniable, chilling, free.
4. **Spend the deniability budget deliberately.** The most on-the-nose subject (Lévi-Strauss, "the devastating one") gets the least stage time, arrives earliest — before the reader has the shape — or comes out of a clumsy *student's* mouth rather than Marsh's. Save Marsh's set-piece for the least self-evident material (participant observation; Douglas on dirt).
5. **The tic set flips register but keeps its function.** The prop had rotating flat similes. A star needs a signature *move* — a question she opens with and won't answer; naming the ethnographer before the finding. Still rotating vehicles, still collectible on reread: the surface flips, the reread-charge mechanism does not.
6. **Marsh never addresses Vee's life.** An engaging professor who engages Vee *personally* drifts into a Cassandra figure, which has its own rules and has already fired. Keep the mirror strictly inside the material.
