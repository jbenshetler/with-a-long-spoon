# Condensed — Rock

*Draft complete (`scenes/rock.md`) · Tue Oct 27, a weeknight stay; post-sex, the porch couch after dark. Vee POV. The first time the name "Peter" reaches the page.*

- **Setting.** A cold night on the enclosed porch after sex: Vee in Pace's robe, Pace in boxers, both under his quilt on the porch couch. Herbal tea, not coffee; firelight/heat from the den fireplace behind them; the yard and the mountains dark through the wall of glass, stars over the ridgeline. The porch is the calm-truth room (it does this twice in the book — here with his truth, in December with hers); the structural opposite of the SATC restaurants, where truth is *extracted.*
- **The robe, uncovered.** It falls open, she doesn't mind; Pace rests a hand on her bare breast — comfort more than heat — and pulls the quilt up. Her ease is the physical twin of the porch-calm; his comfort in her body is part of what opens him.
- **The "who are you" talk.** The ordinary new-couple inventory — food/home, born where, how many siblings, what parents called them, how he got to college at fifteen — rendered only enough to let the name surface in its flow. Not dug for; it just comes out because he's calm here.
- **Food, the seed.** Her Ohio childhood food was made with love and plentiful but never enough; she tells Pace so as praise of his cooking that is really praise of him (*"…the thing you wanted. Then you cooked for me."*). The crackers-and-meal figure stays for December's {{Porch Scene}} — this is only its lighter first seed.
- **Peter surfaces.** His real name lands in the talk — *Peter* — received, not confessed. He gives it with a faint **set-aside**: someone he put down, not someone he guards. The self under it stays withheld (never explained).
- **She's allowed it here.** She tries the name softly; he lets it sit. *Peter* becomes the name permitted in the calm of the porch — which is exactly what won't travel to the cold step at First Weekend Back.
- **The first love, contained.** On the college-at-fifteen thread, first loves come up: Vee offers hers light — a high-school-band trumpet player, sweet and non-formative — and Pace reciprocates with the tutor, told plain and undersold, the ages explicit (assigned a "tutor" at fifteen; she was nineteen; two years; she left at twenty-one for ordinary reasons, no one at fault). **More than a hairline** of feeling shows — his voice goes careful and over-exact, held too level, the containment itself the tell — but it does **not** *break*: the affect stays held and the declaration layer stays sealed. Vee does the arithmetic (15/19), it drops low in her stomach, she nearly says the clean ugly grown-up word and holds it down, and lets out only *"You were so young"* — protective ache for the boy he was, not condemnation, no forensic word in any register. His gut-punch is the non-seeing: he defends the memory and names it luck. Motivates the wall at {{The Stranger}} (reads as pain, not cruelty); does not explain it.
- **Register.** Low heat, tender; the warm courtship phase, before the kink staircase ramps. No unresolved cloud yet (that's December's porch) — this one is clean intimacy. The first-love ache Vee carries is private and unspoken — trust, not a rift — so between them the scene stays warm intimacy and still closes warm on *Peter* + his hand over hers.

**Banked, never stated:** the porch faces the rock of the mountains; *Peter* means rock/foundation — the true name surfacing in the one room that looks onto bedrock. **Taste thread: carry nothing** (pre-Lip-Lick/Refusal window; the name owns the scene). **Sets up:** the failed *Peter*-reach at First Weekend Back (regression, not first use); and, via the contained first-love telling, the wall at {{The Stranger}} (endorsed misattribution — pain, not cruelty). **Two-register first-love telling:** the **facts** are contained here; the **break** — the affect spilling, the declaration layer (*"Once"*), and the vow's reclassification from technique to wound — is reserved for {{Excavations}} (`meta-note-first-love.md`).
