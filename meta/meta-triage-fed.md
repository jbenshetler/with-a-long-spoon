## Fixed

- `:9` — cut "the way you order a thing you have ordered a thousand times and
  will a thousand more." Third of three "the way" hinges in three consecutive
  narrative paragraphs; the clause glossed *familiarity*, which "without
  looking at the card at all" had already shown. `:7` (the road in fog) and
  `:13` (Randi turning cost into a story that cost nothing) both kept.
- `:23` — cut "her face open and glad." Randi's face "opens" three times
  (`:5`, `:23`, `:29`); this was the redundant one, in a sentence already
  carrying "still and full listening" and "got there first." **The editor
  recommended cutting `:29` instead; overridden on reader evidence** —
  claude-opus-4-8 quoted `:29` ("open and warm and glad for her friend") as
  the chapter's sharpest dramatic irony, and "Randi held her flute and
  listened" alone carries none of it.
- `:23` — "she heard the other version of it … and the heat started up her
  throat" → "the other version of it was right there …". Two changes in one:
  the flush was a verbatim twin of `:37` ("The heat came up in her throat"),
  and cutting it left `she heard` as an unsupported filter verb, so the filter
  went too. `:37` is now the chapter's only flush and lands harder for it.
  The existential the recast introduced was itself recast ("was right there")
  at the linter sweep.
- `:23` — "gone still and full listening" → "gone still, all listening."
  Keeps the fullness (Randi's attention register, echo ruling #18) as a state
  in apposition rather than a strained adjectival. *Fully / attentive /
  focused* were all considered and declined: the first measures a degree
  instead of naming a state; the latter two are diagnosis words that put
  technique on the page and make Randi visible as operating.
- `:23` — cut "it was all over her" from "and meant it, it was all over her."
  See the echo ruling below. "And meant it" alone is the colder claim and
  leaves the reader to price it.
- `:25` — cut "warm" from "Then she pointed her flute, warm." One half of a
  reflex pair with `:39` ("'Mm,' Randi said, warm"); the following sentence
  supplies the tone and the tease works better unlabeled.
- `:45` — cut "the same afternoon she'd bought the bra and the panties." The
  shared shopping afternoon was stated twice; `:61` keeps it and now delivers
  it as a reveal. The Ulta grounding stays at `:45`. **Ulta cannot move to
  `:61`** — per `famished:7` the green set came from the store with the low
  lamps, the polish from a different shop on the same trip.
- `:63` — cut "warm" from "She looked at her, warm" ("like the planning was
  the part she liked best" *is* the warmth) and "A beat, warm." → "A beat."
  The paired beat-tags ("A beat, fond." / "A beat, warm.") were the same
  invented construction twice; `:31`'s is now unique.
- `:65` — "who'd half-expected teasing" → "who'd braced for teasing." `:71`
  keeps "half-expected" (claude-opus-4-8 quoted it; the 2026-08-01 audit
  already ruled that line), and the *half* was inert at `:65` anyway — Randi
  has just been described as pointedly not teasing.
- `:69` — cut the tail "— and how much she'd wanted exactly that, she didn't
  quite admit either." Repeated `:61`'s not-admitting move, its "either" had

## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **`:13` "everything that had cost her into a story that had cost her nothing"
  — stands (line edit, cost-vague, 2026-08-11).** Raised in the book-wide
  `cost-vague` back-sweep (vague "cost something/nothing/anything"; rule + author
  ruling in `style/style-rules.toml`). This is the closest of the protected cases
  to the peekaboo one that WAS recast — Randi takes real pain (that cost her) and
  performs it as costless — but it stands because both poles are *in the sentence*:
  the antithesis is stated, not left as a bare "cost nothing." "everything that had
  cost her" immediately undercuts "a story that had cost her nothing," so the
  costlessness reads explicitly as the performance, which is the point (Randi's
  alchemy — turning real cost into costless story). The stated contrast is what
  licenses the idiom here; a lone "cost nothing" would not survive. Do not
  re-raise.
- **Randi covers Vee's hand twice (`:51`, `:67`) — finding declined, not
  merely left standing.** gpt-5.6-sol *counted* the repetition ("Randi covers
  Vee's painted hand twice") and logged it as charged and effective;
  claude-fable-5 called it "a hand pressing a wound closed while checking how
  deep it goes"; gpt-5.5 and gpt-5.6-terra both have it in their motif ledgers
  positively. Five readers, zero friction. Same logic as echo ruling #10
  (`randi pulled her into`): the repeated gesture *is* the escalation ladder —
  and these are not the same move (`:51` interrupts, `:67` settles and does
  not let go).
- **"Fingers for the room. But the toes." (`:35`)** — claude-fable-5's only
  line-level friction ("one beat too diagrammed"), pardoned by fable-5 itself
  in the next clause as in character for a woman who "could put a name to what
  Vee was only feeling and hand it back to her clean" (which is `:69`, this
  chapter). No other reader seconded it; opus and sol both logged the exchange
  as the chapter's central alchemy. A reader noticing the cleanness *and*
  recognizing it as the character's is the double-register working.
- **"You drove over there sure." (`:63`)** — gpt-5.6-sol is factually right
  that this overstates Vee's certainty (`famished:13` "told herself none of
  this meant anything in particular"; `toenails:9` the deliberately unpacked
  bag). **The overstatement is Randi's and is the operation** — a caught woman
  can recant, a woman who chose is stuck with the choosing — and `:65` is Vee
  accepting the cleaner version as her own history. sol names the pressure and
  reports enjoying the release; opus praises the same line as Randi's real
  mechanism. **Do not "correct" this toward accuracy in a later pass.**
- **The mirrored unspoken answers (`:45`, `:61`)** — two questions Randi asks,
  two answers Vee can't give, each opening the same afternoon from a different
  end. The architecture is designed; only the doubled clause was cut.
