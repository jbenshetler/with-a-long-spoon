## The system

  matter of degree ("already tight").
- **Vee — barely-there pale pink: the camouflage made flesh.** The color is

  cf. the alive-and-changing cure and Pace's devoted-lover precision, both

collision point, if ever wanted, is the threesome (undecided — not canon).
