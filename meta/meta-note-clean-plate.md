## Charge (the engine) — added 2026-08-25

The scene runs on **Vee's eagerness to give, met by Randi's appetite to receive** — not on Vee brooding over a burden. The failure mode (diagnosed 2026-08-25) is a passive/melancholy Vee who *receives* the whole meal (Randi's Pace verdict, then Randi's reframe) while carrying the question like homework. Fix = give Vee the forward drive.

**The overflow structure + Vee's self-misread (author, 2026-08-26).** Vee has more to tell than one dinner holds — it starts on the walk. She *believes* her motive is the respectable social one (get Randi's read on last night's party, talk about her boyfriend). So Randi starts to deliver the verdict — *"Pace was so good with Meg and Kayla—"* — and **Vee rolls right over her**, because what she actually came for is the **charge of confessing/bragging** and, underneath, **affirmation about the wet kiss.** Her body's agenda outranks her social one and she doesn't know it: she thinks she's here to listen, she's here to perform and be affirmed. This is dramatic irony run on *Vee herself*, not just the reader — and it silently marks her arc: last fall she lived for Randi's verdict (`all-the-time`; "wanted the verdict since Wednesday… hadn't known how much until it was in"); now her own charge outranks it, and she interrupts the very thing she used to wait all week for. The interruption *is* the arc beat; never narrate the shift.

**Two tranches, two settings** (the overflow is genuinely two events deep):
- **Walk + table opening (last night — {{Among Friends}}, Wed Jan 20, the parts Randi didn't see):** Randi left the lot before the truck. So Vee tells **what happened after her taillights** — the truck blowjob (Vee-initiated start to finish; `among-friends.md:87–93`) and the drive-home orgasm (**Pace's hand, Vee directing — she pulls the lace aside herself**; the mirror-inversion of {{Nothing Underneath}}, her hand denied → his hand completed; `among-friends.md:111–117`). NOT Vee fingering herself — keep it his hand / her control or the mirror breaks. **Split across the restaurant threshold:** the walk carries the vivid blowjob and is **cut off mid-word by the restaurant arriving** (she never reaches "take me to bed"); the table *resumes* it — Randi hands the filthy line back low and hoarse (*"You'd taken it all and didn't spill a drop"*), Vee **shivers**, hearing only in Randi's voice how hungry/slutty she'd sounded — then the drive-home gets its vivid telling at the booth. Vee doesn't argue Randi's Pace-praise; she **fails to hear it**, already back across the bench. Randi draws it out, all-the-time register (minimal prompts, total attention). They order the special blind (dazed comedy). **Food = no rave** — Vee likes it, cleans the plate (the title), no gushing over a student place; the taste-greed setup lives in the *retell* (Pace's cooking), not here.
- **Dinner (last weekend — the unfinished Tuesday story):** the pleasure-princess Saturday morning (pointing, kissing, the orange). The oral drawn out — Randi refuses the tasteful fade: *"Don't you dare fade to firelight and leave me wondering."* Then the wet kiss → *"I don't."*

**Verdict disposition:** the Kayla-dress setpiece is **interrupted out of existence** (Randi gets half a sentence). *"You picked well"* relocates to the **goodbye/goodnight** (author ruling 2026-08-27) — lands hardest at parting, recontextualizing the hug/neck beat.
