# Notes — Tannin (craft)

*Companion to `meta-condensed-tannin.md`.*

## What the chapter is for

Reader-ahead access in the early fall, where the Vee track is still courtship and the
reader would otherwise go several chapters without a window on the architecture. It is
the **baseline** instance of the Pace/Randi two-hander: {{Swim Lanes}} is the same scene
one riser up, {{Portion}} is where it ruptures, {{Space}} is where it is warm again.

## The structure

Her demand → his withholding → her escalation (the hands under the wire) → his one
concrete gift → **two body tells, one read right and one read wrong** → his warmth →
her bright cover → the close on the glass.

## Guards

- **Never inside Randi.** The whole chapter is assembled from surface: the thighs, the
  hands in his hair, the late corner of the mouth, the brightness of *"I already do."*
  No interior access, no narrator gloss on what she feels.
- **He reads the want and misses the jealousy — and the miss must not look stupid.** The
  text gives him an accurate reading twice; the second reading is wrong about the cause,
  not blind to the behavior. That is the catch-and-set-down discipline
  (`meta-craft-pace.md`: never render him *missing* a tell).
- **His privacy rule is live from here.** He gives what he saw, never what Vee said:
  *"It was hers."* Formalized later by author ruling at {{Gone}}; this is the first
  instance and must not be contradicted backward.
- **The close belongs to this chapter and may not be reused.** Finishing the glass in one
  long swallow, taking his face in both hands, kissing him with the last of the wine,
  getting up and holding out her hand — {{Space}} reproduced six elements of that
  sequence and was recast on 2026-10-03 as an accidental repeat. Tannin owns it.
- **He drinks nothing.** The wine reaches him only through her mouth, and the last line
  depends on that holding — do not give him a glass.
- **House constants:** the warm house kept warm for her, Steely Dan low in the front
  rooms, the den couch and the low table (`meta-plan-pace-house.md`, Den).
- **The glass is handled, not fidgeted.** The nail-press is not in this chapter and should
  not be added retroactively; her tells here are her thighs, her hands and her mouth.
  (Gesture canon: `meta-craft-randi.md`, The Cup.)
