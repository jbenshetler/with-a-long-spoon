# Notes — Tannin (craft)

*Companion to `meta-condensed-tannin.md`.*

## What the chapter is for

Reader-ahead access in the early fall, where the Vee track is still courtship and the
reader would otherwise go several chapters without a window on the architecture. It is
the **baseline** instance of the Pace/Randi two-hander: {{Swim Lanes}} is the same scene
one riser up, {{Portion}} is where it ruptures, {{Space}} is where it is warm again.

## The structure

Her demand → his withholding → her escalation (the hands under the wire) → his one
concrete gift → **two body tells, one read right and one read wrong** → his warmth →
her bright cover → the close on the glass.

## Guards

- **Never inside Randi.** The whole chapter is assembled from surface: the thighs, the
  hands in his hair, the late corner of the mouth, the brightness of *"I already do."*
  No interior access, no narrator gloss on what she feels.
- **He reads the want and misses the jealousy — and the miss must not look stupid.** The
  text gives him an accurate reading twice; the second reading is wrong about the cause,
  not blind to the behavior. That is the catch-and-set-down discipline
  (`meta-craft-pace.md`: never render him *missing* a tell).
- **His privacy rule is live from here.** He gives what he saw, never what Vee said:
  *"It was hers."* Formalized later by author ruling at {{Gone}}; this is the first
  instance and must not be contradicted backward.
- **The close belongs to this chapter and may not be reused.** Finishing the glass in one
  long swallow, taking his face in both hands, kissing him with the last of the wine,
  getting up and holding out her hand — {{Space}} reproduced six elements of that
  sequence and was recast on 2026-10-03 as an accidental repeat. Tannin owns it.
- **He drinks nothing.** The wine reaches him only through her mouth, and the last line
  depends on that holding — do not give him a glass.
- **House constants:** the warm house kept warm for her, Steely Dan low in the front
  rooms, the den couch and the low table (`meta-plan-pace-house.md`, Den).
- **The glass is handled, not fidgeted.** The nail-press is not in this chapter and should
  not be added retroactively; her tells here are her thighs, her hands and her mouth.
  (Gesture canon: `meta-craft-randi.md`, The Cup.)

## Addendum — second-statement pass (2026-10-10)

First revision pass on this chapter. Driven by an audit of the Pace/Randi
two-handers for the **second-statement defect** — the page stating once what it
has already rendered. The operative test, carried over from
{{The Pointing Game}}'s appetite pass (2026-10-09): the reading earns its place
when it **exposes** Pace; it robs the reader when it only **restates**. Four
sites cut, 44 words; 1,077 → 1,033.

**Cut.**

- The music's habitual tag — *"and Steely Dan was going low in the front rooms,
  as it always was."* The house constants stay (`meta-plan-pace-house.md`, Den);
  the certification does not. *"as he kept it"* on the warm house is retained,
  because it is agentive — he pre-warms the house for her — where the music's
  tag only told the reader what ch1 had already given.
- The connective middle step — *"He did not want one."* The first sentence is
  protected (he drinks nothing) and the third is the figure; the middle was the
  step between them said out loud.
- In the first body tell: *"He knew what it was."* (grades his accuracy, where
  the next sentence demonstrates it); *"It had grown"* (restates *"it was larger
  now"*); and *"plainly"* (the narrator vouching for him). The reading itself
  and *"he had done nothing to grow it but leave it alone"* — the only new
  information in the tail — are untouched.
- In the second body tell, the misread: the explanatory bridge *"Held harder
  than it had been on the lawn, because there was more of it to hold."* and the
  re-staging *"Randi, sitting on him in his front room with"*. **The guard
  holds**: the reading is still present and still attributed (*"He read it: the
  wanting, held"*), and *"It moved him"* still lands the damning part — he finds
  the holding moving.

**Corroboration.** The `claude-fable-5-1` line-editor pass (whole-volume,
2026-09-30) had independently flagged two of these four in the same terms — the
*as it always was* as "the book noticing its own repeat," and the
*sitting on him in his front room* clause as **explains**: "the set thighs and
the late smile had done it." It also praised the close as earned and un-gilded.

**Cost, recorded.** `deepseek-v4-pro-0813`'s cold read quoted the cut bridge
sentence and built its reading from it, and quoted *"glad of it, plainly"* as
"the engine of the scene." The gladness survives; the bridge and the adverb do
not. The line editor's complaint names the clause that was **kept**, so that
objection is not fully answered and may return.

**Flagged, not ruled — the condensed doc's "what only this chapter
establishes."** It claims the deadbolt-first habit as this chapter's
establishment. `the-bench.md:9` already carries *"she always locked it behind
her, first thing, before anything else"*, and `:7` here repeats it nearly
verbatim six chapters on — the line editor flagged it as a repeat. Either the
claim is wrong or the repeat is deliberate; author's call, untouched pending it.
