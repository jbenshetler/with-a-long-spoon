## The beat

- **Occasion:** the morning after. Vee's hangover is real and mild; the frame is domestic (his kitchen or hers per staging needs — Pace's kitchen preferred, continuity with the {{Among Friends}} kitchen panel's role-inversion).
- **The question:** having met his one friend, Vee asks for the rest — *where are the guy friends* — half-wondering if he's holding a world back from her. Pre-suspicious register: curiosity and hope, not interrogation.
- **"This is it."** — the answer to *the rest of his friends*. Flat, no apology.
- **The answer, when she presses (gently):** not fluent, not a prepared account — the flat biographical grammar of the tutor telling. College at fifteen among men. Men make friends by competing, and he couldn't enter a single arena they competed in — not size, not sex, not money, not age — and by the time he could, the window was closed.
- **The wound-sentence,** under-temperature, his own voice: when he was learning how this worked, **men either weren't safe or weren't welcoming.** He does not say his father's name. He doesn't have to — Vee holds {{Rock}} and does the arithmetic on *weren't safe*; so does the reader.
- **The deepening-barrier, as concrete instance not abstraction:** what the gaps around *"What are you doing for Christmas?"* cost — said as fact, not lament. Friendships deepen through casually exchanged childhoods; his can't be casually exchanged.
- **Sheri re-framed:** not evidence of a missing world but the whole of a hard-won one — the one friendship that got past the questions (her disowning the matched hole).

## Guards
