## What the scene is

## The beat

- **Occasion / cold open:** the morning after, **his house, his bed**. Vee wakes with her head thick; Pace is already up (his constant). A **glass of ice water** is waiting on the bed beside her — she drinks it gladly. The end of the night comes back in **fragments, nonlinear and incomplete**: she fell asleep in the truck → he got her from truck to bathroom → stripped her, ibuprofen and water → put her in one of his **oversized soft white undershirts** → put her to bed → kissed her forehead. Let the fragments surface out of order, with a gap where the truck-to-door walk should be, so the tenderest images (the shirt over her head, the forehead kiss) land *last* — the ones that stuck.
  - *Function:* the sympathy dose's first move, before a word of disclosure — the reader watches Pace *tend* her, so the injured-boy-doing-his-best reading is already warm when "This is it" lands. Earn-the-dark from the first paragraph.
  - *Cashes Sheri's testimony:* last night Sheri told Vee what he *is* — *"next morning he's on my porch with coffee and a box of donuts… He was there."* Now Vee wakes into exactly that (ice water, ibuprofen, the shirt, the forehead kiss), and she has the vocabulary for it. She is receiving what his one friend swore he was — which points her morning-after softness straight at the guy-friends question.
  - *Body register (R3):* she's the one undressed, and it renders as **caretaking, not display** — competence and tenderness (a drunk woman handled with unembarrassing care), never eros or exposure. The only heat is her POV warmth at being *kept*.
- **Into the kitchen → the question:** she follows the smell out and finds that **while she slept Pace baked cinnamon-raisin bread**. He serves her **hot tea with honey, buttered bread, and scrambled eggs** — the caretaking cash continues (he was *there*, again; the {{Among Friends}} kitchen role-inversion, and Sheri's "on my porch with donuts" now lived, not reported).
  - She pads out **in his oversize undershirt and his terry robe** — enveloped in him, which keeps the caretaking warmth on her body through the whole hard conversation to come.
  - The conversation arrives by nature, not interrogation. Vee starts warm: **how much she likes Sheri.** Pace: **"She liked you too."** This is a **deliberate repeat** of the {{Another Round}} truck close (*"I like her." / "She likes you."*) — the night-before version was *drunk-earnest*, small and drink-tailored; the morning version is the sober, hungover, wry replay (**drunks repeat**; foldable into her fragmentary recall — she half-knows she already said it). The repetition *is* the humor.
  - Vee **guffaws** — the memory of Sheri's frank, appreciative once-over (the sexual look, `another-round.md` :51–57: it "was *enjoying* her," and produced this same involuntary laugh — the morning guffaw is that taproom laugh echoing back). Pace *catches it* (R4 — noticing, never missing) and doesn't dodge: **"That way too. She can be like that."** Vee, easy: **"No — we're good."** Beat. Then the ask, hopeful and open: **"I'd love to meet your other friends."**
  - The register is **pre-suspicious**: curiosity and hope, half-wondering if there's a whole kept world she hasn't gotten to yet — *not* interrogation. The all-women shape of his world stays in her POV, **privately noticed, uncashed** — she asks for "other friends," and lets *his* answer reveal the shape. This is the radar ping (a concealed world) that will resolve innocent.
- **The sealed world — a two-pass misunderstanding (the "This is it." beat, realized as dialogue):**
  - Vee: **"When can I meet your other friends?"**
  - Pace: **"Sheri is my friend."** — flat, honest, *complete*; but it reads to Vee as a non-answer (why name Sheri when she asked about the *others*?).
  - Vee, not understanding, cashing Sheri's testimony back to him: **"Of course she is. She told me how good you were when she came out. You mean the world to her, do you know that? And I want to meet your *other* friends."**
  - Pace: **"Sheri's my friend. My one friend. There isn't anyone else."**
  - *The engine:* Vee **cannot parse the honest answer as complete** — a normal person's ear refuses "one friend" as the whole set, so he has to say it twice, plainer the second time. Her non-understanding is the reader's proxy: the sealed world is so far outside ordinary that it doesn't register until spelled out. **"There isn't anyone else."** is the sealed-world line — flat, no apology, no self-pity; the temperature's first drop after the warm banter.
  - *Note (asymmetry softens):* Vee references Sheri's coming-out here — so Pace learns Sheri told her that much (last night Vee withheld *which* things; this morning one surfaces). Warm, not a breach.
- **Vee stunned — the protest (cashes {{Among Friends}} *on the page*):** she can't square it. *"But — everybody likes you. You're so comfortable with people. I saw you. People you've never met, people who aren't like you. You could have friends. Why? Why don't you have friends?"* This names the charm she witnessed (Danny bench-redirect, Theo jazz question, the room-working) **out loud** — so the re-key ("capacity without history") lands on first read *through Vee*, not only on reread. Her protest is what forces the fuller answer.
- **Women friends first — the lighter half, cleared before the meat:** Pace, deflecting-plain: *women misunderstand friendship; it gets awkward.* He does **not** sharpen it (never-diagnosis; he under-states). **Vee sharpens it in her own head:** they misunderstand *your* friendship — a woman can't stay just-a-friend to him; the attention reads as courtship and curdles. She thinks of **friendzoning Theo** — she knows the awkwardness from the other seat, the one doing the gentle declining.
  - *This is the general rule Sheri is the exception to:* Sheri is the one woman friendship that holds precisely because the erotic channel is closed (she's gay, came out) — no courtship-misread possible. Corroborates `meta-arch-sheri.md` ("formed in his own format with the erotic channel closed"). Feeds "Sheri re-framed" below.
  - *Uncashed reread irony (optional to seed, do not state):* Vee couldn't stay just-friends with him either — she's the living case of the rule she's decoding.
- **Then the guys — the meat, delivered as Q&A not a speech (craft directive):** keep **Vee asking**, one question pulling out each piece, so it never reads as a prepared account — the flat biographical grammar of the tutor telling, not fluent. The channels arrive *as answers to her*: college at fifteen among men; men make friends by competing, and he couldn't enter a single arena they competed in — *not size, not sex, not money, not age* — and by the time he could, the window had closed.
- **The wound-sentence,** under-temperature, his own voice: when he was learning how this worked, **men either weren't safe or weren't welcoming.** He does not say his father's name. He doesn't have to — Vee holds {{Rock}} and does the arithmetic on *weren't safe*; so does the reader.
- **The deepening-barrier, as concrete instance not abstraction:** what the gaps around *"What are you doing for Christmas?"* cost — said as fact, not lament. Friendships deepen through casually exchanged childhoods; his can't be casually exchanged.
- **Vee processes it — the enormity washes over her (earn-the-dark, the sympathy dose *lands*):** she takes it in over the domestic wreckage — hot tea, remnants of scrambled eggs, cinnamon-raisin toast, his shirt, his robe — and measures his absence against her own abundance, the four friends rendered by **specific role, not a list** (motivated, each doing a different job): **Meg** (whose closet she raids for anything braver than she is — canon, {{Another Round}}'s borrowed keyhole shirt), **Kayla** (takes half her fries as tax across a dining-hall tray), **Cassie** (hears out her whole heart, *"oh, Vee"*), **Randi** (shoe-store laughs, the secrets, triage on every heartbreak). The realization is arithmetic that *won't run*: **one person can't be all four.** He'd never had *any* of it. The sadness is **hers (and the reader's), never staged by him** — he stated facts; she supplies the grief.
  - *Reread payload (do not cash):* Randi named here among the four — as the heartbreak-confidante — in the very scene about concealed worlds, and it doesn't trip. Innocent now; loaded later. (Radar-guard below.)
- **Sheri re-framed:** not evidence of a missing world but the whole of a hard-won one — the one friendship that got past the questions (her disowning the matched hole).
- **The close — Vee's silent arithmetic → the hug (pays off "does the arithmetic on *weren't safe*"):** she can't help but wonder about his parents, and three `{{Rock}}` threads re-sort themselves against what she now knows:
  - **The mother, taken out of the answer.** `{{Rock}}` :79–85 — she'd asked *what does your mother call you*, and he answered *"My real name is Peter"* — his own name, not the mother's word for it; *"He had taken his mother out of the answer. Vee let him."* Now that deflection reads heavier — the mother is a void he routes around, not just a flat fact.
  - **Leaving at fifteen now cashes.** `{{Rock}}` :129–139 — *"Weren't you scared?" / "Of leaving home. You were fifteen." / "Less than staying." / "I was not a good fit where I was."* Set beside Sheri's *"went bad with his daddy… hasn't been home since he was sixteen"* ({{Another Round}} :115), *"less than staying"* resolves: the father is the **"weren't safe."** Vee assembles it; **the father stays unnamed on the page** — she does the arithmetic, exactly as designed.
  - **Daphne re-scaled (the dark heart).** With no friends *and* an unsafe home, the tutor (`{{Rock}}` :207–227, two years, 19/15) was **the only one** — doing the work Vee's whole four do. And her authority over him was *already inflated by the very thing that was wrong* (the ugly, still-unnamed word) — so Vee holds two irreconcilable readings at once: it as *he* framed it (the best two years he'd had, luck) vs. the wrong shape it actually was; both true, and it hurts to hold both. Then **when Daphne left, he had no one** — no one left to take *that* heartbreak to (Vee has Randi; he had nobody). Deepens the `{{Rock}}` reread without ever re-cashing the word.
  - **The undergraduate offset made concrete (fixes the earlier compression):** carried in Vee's *interior*, not Pace's mouth (keeps his dialogue lean). She contrasts the **preening, insecure freshman boys** (measuring by the hour — who's biggest, who's been with a girl, who'll matter) against Pace, who stood off the side: *less than all of them in every arena they competed in, except the two that counted for nothing there — he was smarter than the room, and he'd already been with Daphne.* And the timing that sealed it: when his age-peers carried trays through a cafeteria and fell asleep over freshman English, **Pace was a graduate student with a patent** (canon: `two-towels.md` :87–89, Vee knows of it — *"I license a patent. It pays a little."*), **his own money, and a house, teaching himself to cook alone** — handed a whole life years before the growing, and left to eat it by himself.
  - **The hug.** Out of all that, she **crosses the kitchen, around the counter that had been between them, and presses herself bodily against him** — literalizing the closing of distance. He is **pensive — not happy, not sad** (R5: feeling and present, never flat/blank; he *receives* it, not soothed — same as at the rock). The scene lands here: warm, un-resolved, the sadness left as hers and the reader's.

## Guards

- **The diagnosis never.** The never-name rule holds absolutely — temperament unlabeled, no self-analysis, no naming of his own machinery. The *biography* and the *wound-sentence* are what's licensed here (dosage guard, `meta-arch-pace.md`, revised 2026-08-03). "Weren't safe or weren't welcoming" is the same species as *"he thought it would be funny."*
- **No self-pity.** He answers because she asked and because she's earned it, not to be consoled. If Vee moves to comfort, he receives it the way he received her at the rock — still, not soothed.
- **Radar-trained-to-dismiss reread payload:** Vee's half-wondering *if he's holding back* is the one ping that resolves innocent — her radar fires at the right category (a concealed world) and the honest answer closes it, while the actually-concealed thing (Randi) never trips it. Invisible on first read; loaded on reread.
- **Re-keys {{Among Friends}}:** the live renders (Danny bench-redirect, Theo jazz question) now read as *capacity without history* — he can charm men; he never got to keep any. Not choice-not-incapacity.
- **Earn the dark:** this is a warm scene — morning light, aspirin, the answer given plainly. The sadness is the reader's, not staged.
