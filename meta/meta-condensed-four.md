## Summary

The only Pace/Randi scene between {{Space}} (Oct 23) and the Christmas break, written to
carry the reader-ahead current through Volume B's long Vee-track stretch.

Randi arrives mid-afternoon, deadbolt first. They have had each other three times before
the scene opens — the counter (fast, her demand), then his bed twice, slowly. Past nine,
she is on the den couch in a dark red shot-silk negligee she brought, and she cannot stop
talking about Vee.

**What she reports.** Vee came to the eleven o'clock stats lecture straight from Pace's
bed: hair half-pinned and wet, having missed her earlier classes, carrying his coffee in a
steel cup, failing to hide her grin. Randi tucked Vee's hair behind her ear — *"because it
was an excuse and I took it"* — and told her she had come to class still full of him; Vee
put her hands in her lap and went scarlet. Saturday's brunch was the best they have had:
Vee talked for twenty minutes about his cooking, said her mouth had been asleep since she
was born, and said she *sleeps* out here, whole nights, having not known she could.

**The pointing game, reopened.** Randi asks how he knew to point at Vee. He didn't — he
read *her*: the hand going slack at the two who went everywhere together, the breath
changing and the *maybe* at the girl on the library steps, the fast *God, no* at the one
running the room. He knew when Vee came out of the doors laughing. But the reading was
never the whole of it, and the rest he keeps: he had never once pointed at a woman he did
not want, and the picture that arrived on the quad was the two of them in his bed — Randi
bound and sleek against Vee's sprawl, himself kissing the redhead with his hand on Randi's
face. He had already chosen; the pointing was to learn whether they had chosen the same.
*"I'd have taken you right there on the quad, if you'd let me."* And the part that was not
method: her face said yes before the rest of her got there.

**The leak.** Pace says Vee waits all week for the brunches — the only thing she will
leave the house for on a weekend — and the compliment opens Randi up. He asks whether Vee
is happy; the question grinds her (*"I know exactly what I'm doing"*). She reaches the
goodbye and stops. He has his hands under the silk; she loses a minute; he waits her out
and returns to the sentence. She volunteers the kiss herself, escalates to kneeling and
facing away mid-sentence, and describes it slowly while he is in her — hug, mouth, the
coming-off, back once more, and she has to hold herself back from going for more. Her body
interrupts the telling throughout. He asks **"Does she want it?"** and will not take the
dodge; she gives him Saturday's new fact: *Vee wouldn't let it end. She came after me.*

**Close.** Her alibi (*it's work, for the threesome*) is undone by his kindness, not a cut
— *"You don't have to do it for me"* — and she comes on it. The fourth takes work from
both of them; she sets the angle, goes over a second time, and that finishes him. Then the
count she will not say aloud (*"That's more than three"*), his quiet pride (*"With the
right encouragement"*), and the admission that arrives unwrapped — *"I'm inspired by her
… God, I want her"* — re-wrapped a beat too late as *"For us."* The chill, the blanket
fetched before she asks, her answer to his pointing-game reading (*"You see more of me than
anybody ever has … some nights it frightens me"*), his *"Then I'll be careful with you,"*
and *"Say something nice to me."* He takes her grip on his arm for a compliment.
