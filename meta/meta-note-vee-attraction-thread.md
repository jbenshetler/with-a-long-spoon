## The one word the thesis rides on

Vee's attraction is **latent-real, threshold-lowered — NEVER implanted.** They did not *create* a want; they **lowered the bar until an always-hers want crossed into action.** If it ever reads as "made to want what she never would have," she becomes a pure victim and the ownership arc (liberation = owning chosen shame) collapses.

## The mechanism (retain all three; the durability is the sex-brain's doing)

Her attraction becomes **available and durable** through the convergence of:

- **The sex-brain** — long-term heightened arousal from frequent, intensely pleasurable sex: a standing state that lowers the threshold and makes the latent want durable and available. The **durability/availability specifically is the sex-brain's doing.**
- **Pace drawing it out** — the erotic track.
- **Randi fueling it via SATC** — the confidante track.

Two tracks over the sex-brain substrate **converge** on a manufactured *occasion* for an always-hers want.

## The renunciation chain — want to blindfold (author 2026-10-04)

The spring thread's outcome, and the cause of everything the summer does. Recorded
because without it the step from *she wants Randi* to *she asks Pace for a threesome with
someone else* has no written cause.

1. **Spring — the hiding fails.** Vee can no longer hide from herself that she wants
   Randi.
2. **Spring — the renunciation.** She decides she cannot have her, **because Randi is her
   best friend.** The want is not resolved or outgrown; it is set aside on loyalty
   grounds.
3. **Summer opens — the answer.** Vee answers Pace's question from {{Boyfriend}} — *do
   you want to taste a woman?* — **yes**, and **redirects from Randi to other women.** The
   redirect is the renunciation enacted.
4. **She tells Randi** she has asked Pace for a threesome.
5. **Randi helps Vee scout women.**
6. **{{The Threesome and Reveal}} happens with Vee blindfolded at her own request**
   (mechanics: `meta-note-threesome-reveal.md`, `meta-condensed-threesome-reveal.md`).
7. **Only at the end does Vee learn her first woman was Randi**, and understands the
   deception.

## Scope and guard

- **A thread, not a scene.** It spans most of the spring semester, across multiple chapters.
- **Guard:** in each scene it must read as **genuine self-discovery, warm and hers**; the convergence stays **invisible in the moment, reread-only.** Never render the sex-brain (or the tracks) as *creating* the wanting — too mechanical, wrong cause. Accelerant on a pre-lit ember, body-first, never named (cf. flag 19b, the Telling as accelerant-not-origin).

Later consent/permission language must preserve that distinction. This guard does **not** reopen `scenes/boyfriend.md`.
