the 4-model repair probe (`reviews/authorship-audit/*/…--repair.md`), the cold-read
reviews, and the per-scene triage docs. One line per fix, grouped by scene, in line
order. **Status: awaiting author rulings** — nothing below is applied. Line numbers

**Governing pattern (both cross-checks):** cold-reader praise and authorship tells
live in almost entirely different populations of lines — readers quote psychology,
consent beats, and frame-analysis; the tells are color-swatch and inventory clauses
nobody quotes. Where a fix cuts only the unquoted clause, praise and repair are
compatible. Four genuine collisions are marked **AUTHOR CALL**.

Actions: **CUT** (delete span) · **TRIM** (convergent shorter version) · **SWAP**
(drop-in replacement) · **LEAVE** (recommend no change) · **SKIP** (not worth
touching) · **AUTHOR CALL** (flagged text is praised/canon/previously adjudicated).
