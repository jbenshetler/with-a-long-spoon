# Notes — Space (craft)

*Companion to `meta-condensed-space.md`.*

## What the chapter is for

The fall's warm Pace/Randi two-hander, and the volume-B opening diptych's second panel:
Vee reports to Randi at {{Broken In}}, Randi carries it to Pace here. {{Four}} rhymes that
pair one rung higher in November, and depends on this chapter having established the
pattern.

## The gauge, running backstage

The scene's real transaction is informational and neither party remarks on it: **Randi
hands over what Vee told her; Pace adds nothing of what Vee said to him.** His privacy
rule (author ruling 2026-09-27, recorded at {{Gone}}) holds all the way through — he gives
his own account of Thursday, his impressions, his gladness, and none of Vee's words.
Randi gives everything, including the morning, and laundered counsel on top.

## Guards

- **Pace's response to Vee's crying is rendered as feeling, not speech** (author ruling):
  he is moved thinking of Vee, and the point is that he *shows* it while Randi is talking
  about Vee being emotional — not that he says much. *"I'm glad it was you"* is the whole
  verbal content, and *"He felt that land"* and the relieved/glad paragraph carry the rest.
  Do not give him a speech here, and do not flatten him either.
- **Two style-linter acks stand on this chapter:** *"He felt that land"* and Randi's
  *"I saw her from the top of the stairs"* (the latter re-acked 2026-10-04 after the
  glass-tell pass changed the following line and re-armed the fingerprint). Both are
  author-signed; a re-flag after an edit is correct, not a regression.
- **The glass tell, as revised 2026-10-03/04** (`meta-craft-randi.md`, The Cup): the turn
  that used to sit at `:39` is **cut** — it fired on nothing and was the second gesture in
  one short scene — and `:53` is now the **nail-press**, the glass brought down against her
  knee and the rim worked in under her thumbnail on *"The heart on her, Pace."* Jealousy at
  Vee being cherished. Do not restore the turn and do not convert the press.
- **The close was recast off {{Tannin}}** (author ruling 2026-10-03, accidental repeat):
  the finish-the-glass / take-his-face / kiss-with-the-wine / hold-out-her-hand sequence
  belongs to {{Tannin}} alone. `:61` is now the glass set down half full and her hand along
  his jaw until he turns his face into it. Keep them distinct.
- **The blue toenails are placed away from the climax** — {{Gone}} and {{In Her Place}} own
  that beat. Keep the placement.
- **She stays**, and he gets the blanket before she has to reach for it. The contrast is
  {{Swim Lanes}}, where she leaves and takes the blanket herself; and {{Portion}}, where she
  rolls away and he does not put his hand in her hair. Here the hand goes in.
- **Her eyewitness gifts are rendered with her body answering as she tells them** — the
  arousal rises in her while she speaks, in Pace's hands, and he feels both the giving and
  the taking at once. The same mechanism {{Four}} runs on the goodbye kiss; this is its
  first instance and the earlier, milder rung.
- **Never inside Randi.** Her hand flat on the folded clothes, the glass stopped halfway
  while she watches his face, the voice going to *the place it went* — surface only.
- **Open ruling, unresolved:** Randi says Vee *cried*, and {{Broken In}} has no crying
  anywhere on the page. Either Randi is embellishing (in character, and she is reporting to
  a man who was not there) or {{Broken In}} wants the beat. Not yet ruled; do not "fix"
  either side without the author.
