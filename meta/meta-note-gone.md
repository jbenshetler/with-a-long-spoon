## What the scene is

Post-coital Pace and Randi, a week after {{Portion}}. She stays down; asks to be fed; takes his undershirt; eats; hands him back his own sentence; stays and sleeps. Both **buzzing about how great Vee is** — the happy-romantic-friendship view of the configuration, *from the inside.* Warm and true on first read; cold only on reread — and now a truce nobody names, made entirely of reversals against the two nights before it (left / stayed cold / stayed warm).

**Pace's privacy rule (author ruling 2026-09-27).** Pace keeps Vee's private actions and statements. His impressions and his analysis of her are his to give — *"She's ashamed. Somebody taught her to be ashamed of her body"* stays; *"And she talks about you"* stays (he is not asking what she said); *"I like taking my time with her"* stays. He does not ask what Vee told Randi; that is Vee's.

She surfaces her attraction the way she talks — **open, frank, sexual** — never by declaring it: *she's so much fun; those curves under that cardigan — but you already know.* That single line is the villain-guard's warm-leak in her register: the desire **leaks**, the wrap is instant (deflected onto Pace's shared knowledge, folded into the shared-plan frame). ## What Pace knows / doesn't

He has known Randi **wants** Vee since {{The Pointing Game}} — that layer is open between them; they can speak the shared wanting warmly. He is blind to the **sadism**, the **cost / need-not-want depth**, and the **hate.** Randi gives him the **sanitized** friendship version (canon: `meta-arch-randi.md`); the **omission of the sadism** is load-bearing on reread — the reader watches her curate what she hands him.

## The three razors (make or break)

1. **Admiration as *love*, not *acquisition*.** "How great she is" must read as *I'm falling for her / she's the best friend I've had* — never two hunters cataloging prey ("perfect for the plan"). Delight, not appetite-for-the-plan.
2. **No awareness-of-the-deception on the page.** No "she has no idea" wink. Their happiness is sincere and non-cruel; the cold is only in what the reader knows and they don't. The instant either enjoys Vee's ignorance, it curdles to villainy.
3. **Pace hot, never calculating.** His interior runs warm/loving (canon: hot fantasist, not cool strategist). One computed line cools him — and the `pov-reliability` oracle showed his POV already reads *cooler* than Vee's view of him, so guard it hard.

## Beat order (as drafted, sixth draft, 2026-09-27; opener and dress beat 2026-09-28)

1. **Bed.** Astride him, down on his chest, loose — *she had stayed down tonight*; he notices it and lets it be. Sade low in the front rooms. **Pace opens:** *"Vee was here yesterday. Sounds like you two had a day."* (Vee at his house Sun Oct 18, off-page; he puts Vee between them first, warmly, and it gives Randi a thing to answer.) Her answer is the shoe store — Randi's embellished {{Sorority}}, cut to the swap (author ruling 2026-09-28: texture out, the lie kept catchable): Vee in the canoe doing the bigfoot, the older couple, Vee red — *"That was kind of hot."* One chapter after {{Sorority}}, so the recap texture goes and the altered account stays. Then, quieter, out of the story: *"I didn't think I'd like her this much… She got past me before I could help it."*
2. **The ask.** The chill takes her; he reaches for the blanket; she catches his wrist: *"Is there anything to eat in this house?"* She has never asked him that. The second drawer down without looking for it; one of his white undershirts to mid-thigh; she waits in the door. He pulls on jeans.
3. **Kitchen.** Kettle, chicken breast split and flattened under the heavy pan, salt from the dish by the stove, couscous under a lid, asparagus steamed in the small pan; he turns the chicken by the sound. *Fifteen minutes, all of it, and nothing in it he had to think about* — his everyday register (bible: sautéed chicken and vegetables), not the craft-for-pleasure cooking he does for Vee. She takes the kitchen chair, feet up on the lacquered seat, the shirt over her knees, the blue of her toes.
4. **At his back.** She resumes from the chair — *"And she'll listen to you like you're the only thing in the room…"* Then *"And she talks about you"* → *"The whole hour… Pace this, Pace that. She's got it bad. It's a little pathetic. I love it."* (Her brunch report trimmed to the general line; the specifics stay off the page.)
5. **She eats.** Half the chicken sliced open, a spoon of couscous, the asparagus; a glass of water. She eats it to a ruler line, fork across the plate. She does not reach into his (canon: Randi never takes from another plate). *The most he had seen her eat at his table. He did not say so.*
6. **The migrated sentence.** *"She's not ready." Not a question, and not the thing it had been a week ago. "You want her to get there on her own."* It was his sentence; he takes her keeping it exactly for her coming round. *"I like taking my time with her." / "I know you do."* Something crossed her face and was gone. *"God, you two."*
7. **The dress → cardigan → shame → the lip kiss.** She turns the water glass. *"She's stunning in it, Pace. I'm around beautiful girls every day. She's extraordinary. How she looks. Who she is."* — her own eyewitness from {{Sorority}}, hers to give; the leak folded into praise of his work; the cost audible to the reader, a compliment to him. **Pace:** he sees Vee in it, glowing, coming through the den to him; then, before he has decided to, all three at this table — Vee wrapped in his sheet, Randi across from her in his shirt, both smiling, *the way they already smiled about each other*. *He wanted to tell Vee. He could not, not yet.* (The benefactor's gladness — *she'll love this* — and the threesome anticipated as shared delight, never efficiency; the arrangement felt as impatience, not guilt; no word for the warmth.) The dress shows what the cardigan hides, so: *"I don't know why she hides in that cardigan." / "She's ashamed. Somebody taught her to be ashamed of her body."* She went quiet at that, and softened past it (the approved interior brush, below). *"And you're showing her she doesn't have to be."* The soft, brief kiss on his lower lip across the little table. *"That's why she's so gone over you."*
8. **The lamp speech, at the sink.** *"You know what she does to a room." / "Tell me."* — the third scene's *tell me*, hers, in his mouth's answer. Behind him the chair makes no sound; her stillness, *the kind that takes holding*; the tightening in her shoulders under his shirt. *"Yeah. She is."* (Jealousy, lit at {{How It's Done}}; Pace reads it as nothing he can name.)
9. **The turn, in the kitchen.** She finds him through the jeans — *"So soon?"* — takes the undershirt off onto the kitchen floor, and the *criminal* beat: her body drawn down his as the picture of Vee's. *Not the picture of Vee she'd handed him, but Randi… wanting something out loud in his kitchen.* He doesn't take up the rest of what she set down; that was hers to hold. He turns off the light over the stove; she goes ahead of him down the hall, bare; the shirt stays where it lay.
10. **The ride and the close.** As before — slow, small and pale above him, the blue on her toes the last composed thing — and then: *the chill took her fast, and he drew the blanket up over the two of them, and she stayed where she was, and slept.*
