## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **"laid out like something prepared and waiting"** (the top-drawer inventory)
  — the report called the simile an explanation of a figure that already
  landed. **Declined, and the report is wrong.** This phrase is the chapter's
  dread mechanism, already recorded in the 2026-07-28 panel triage above: the
  dread "arrived entirely from carry-forward, not the page." "Room for more of
  her" alone is warm and finished; *prepared and waiting* is what lets a reader
  carrying suspicion feel the floor tilt while a reader without it feels only
  tenderness. It is the sole double-reading available in an otherwise entirely
  warm chapter. kimi-k3 quoted the clause verbatim. Cutting it would make the
  chapter safe.
- **"He had to feel it."** (:23) — the report read it as swallowed by "He felt
  it in everything he did." **It is not a restatement, it is a different speech
  act.** "A man didn't do all of that…" is the evidence; "He had to feel it" is
  Vee *arguing* — the modal is her needing it true, reasoning to a conclusion
  she cannot verify; "He felt it in everything he did" is her having arrived.
  Argument, then rest. Cutting it removes the only place a reader can hear her
  needing it and leaves her serene from the start. gpt-5.6-sol's pushback ("I do
  not accept it as proof") was only possible because the page lets you hear her
  making a case — that sentence is what makes it a case.
- **"he smiled, the small private one"** (:45) — **PROTECTED**, ruled at echo
  #65 (see `audits/line-edit/echo-rulings.md`). Pace's tell. The ledger's claim
  of a three-instance cluster was wrong: `ignition-scalding:49` is *Randi's*
  smile, not Pace's, so the set is two across ~137,700 words. Never flag it.
- **"unhurried"** (:19) — the 2026-07-28 ruling thins the word drastically but
  carves out load-bearing Pace beats. One use, on Pace, in the sentence that is
  *about* his unhurriedness. Acked.
- **"the way he near always did"** (:17) and **"the way he did everything"**
  (:51) — both stand. Ruling #33's recast-to-"as" precedent was offered and
  **declined**: "as he near always did" is a lateral move to stiffer diction.
  The second is characterization that pays off at the drawer. Both acked.
- **"before it registered"** (:51) — stands. The `register-verb` rule misfires:
  this is the intransitive idiom on Vee, not the transitive collector's sense
  the rule targets (which exists to stop Pace/Randi reading as cold
  cataloguers). The redundancy with "her fingers closed on nothing" was raised
  and declined; the pre-announcement is wanted. Acked.
- **"she heard the sound that came out of her"** (:13) — stands. The filter is
  the point: she is a bystander to her own noise, which is what licenses "a
  bright stupid sound she'd have died of anywhere else." kimi-k3 quoted the
  image. Acked.
- **"He felt it in everything he did"** (:23) — stands; `filter-verbs` misfire
  (feel-as-emotion, not feel-as-perception). Reader-praised by kimi-k3 and
  gpt-5.6-terra. Acked.
- **"She watched his eyes open. She watched them move…"** (:41) — anaphora, not
  a tic; her holding still and tracking him is the paragraph's action. Acked.

## Applied

1. **:61 — the closing paragraph cut to the posture and one clause.** Was: "She
   stood with a hand on each open drawer and understood that she'd asked him a
   question last weekend without meaning to, standing right here with her hair
   full of pins, and that he hadn't answered it the way she'd waited all week
   to be answered. He hadn't told her it didn't matter. He'd gone and made her
   a place instead, squared his own life down small to clear it, and left it
   for her to find with nothing said over it at all." Now: "She stood with a

   2026-08-02 verdict on "waited all week to be answered" was a *continuity*
   ruling and did not protect the sentence from a rhythm cut.) Also removes the
   chapter's fourth "squared."
2. **:21 — the scar's location dropped** (echo #4 FIX-AT, pre-ruled). "the old

3. **:43 — four appositives cut to two.** Kept the ring-on-good-wood figure and

4. **:37 — "the bag" three times → twice.** "She'd started bringing the bag,

   was off the ground." One door, one opening, hunger at full height at the
   moment she leaves the ground.
6. **:57 — tightness stated three times → twice.** Cut "crammed" and the

    line does **not** fire `not-x-but-y`.
14. **:13 — "he must have heard the car" cut.** Left "Then the door swung wide
    before she reached it and she was off the ground." Removes a doubled *heard*
