## What this scene is

The warm two-hander Volume Two has been missing. Volume One's edge came from Pace and Randi alone, co-conspirators, buzzing about Vee ({{The Bench}}, {{The Pointing Game}}, {{Gone}}) — the reader seeing the apparatus Vee can't. That track goes dark across the spring until {{In Her Place}} (~April). **Me Too re-lights it early, the night of {{A Clean Plate}}:** Vee leaves the loft (→ {{The Reach}}, same night, alone), Randi comes to Pace's.

It is the **spring recurrence of the Pointing Game's engine.** *"Only in a threesome, with you"* was Randi handing Pace the want so she wouldn't have to hold it as hers. Here she does it again, months on — with Vee now real, close, and *curious* — the need with teeth it didn't have on the quad.

## The hall of mirrors (the payload)

Two women who cannot own the same want, laundering it in opposite directions, and the man between them calls the result a gift:
- **Vee** can't admit she wants Randi → launders it as *curiosity*, hands it to Randi as a retell.
- **Randi** can't admit she needs Vee → launders it as *a threesome for Pace*, hands it to him as a gift.
- **Pace** has understood from the start that Randi curates — he **sees** her hide a want and, tender, gives her the room not to say it (**author ruling A, 2026-08-29:** Pace is *not* blind to the hiding). He is blind only to *how deep it runs* — he reads it as a woman shy to own a big desire, not the Bench need-not-want or the hate. He thinks he's building a present for the two women he loves; the reader sees the floor he can't.

## The small sadism — mirror, not cruelty

What arouses Randi in the reporting is Vee's helplessness: Vee's embarrassment, Vee's need outrunning her management, Vee hot for Randi and unable to say so. Each is **Randi's own weakness worn on someone else's face** — she gets off on the exact trap she is in. This is **not** the cruel operational sadism (the humiliations, the extractions) — that stays concealed from Pace and is absent from this page. It is the tender, identifying kind, and it reads warm because it is self-recognition, not contempt.

## Guards (for the draft)

- **Register — warmth is primary (volume-governing).** Plays first as warm, sexy, loving — a couple lit up about the woman they both want; the reader should *want* the configuration and be torn. The apparatus (steering, leak, mirror) is the thin reread-cold *under* that surface, never the emphasis. If it reads as a cold scheming scene, it has failed the volume, not just the beat. Keep the steering *light* — Pace sees and allows (ruling A); he does not stage-manage on the page.
- **POV.** Pace close third. Randi's need, her scheme, and the mirror-identification are all **performed** — body, voice, dialogue — never narrated. No *she needed Vee*, no *she savored it because it was her own weakness*. The reader supplies the why from {{The Bench}}. (The containers rule: Randi is assembled from surface whenever Pace is on the page.)
- **Villain razor.** Loving and true on first read; cold only on reread. A woman lit up about her friend, not a plotter. If the surface reads "scheming," it's broken.
- **Concealment boundary.** The cruel track is absent here; Pace never sees past the warm. Only the small mirror-sadism is present. (Explicit surfaces; sadism does not.)
- **No deception-wink.** The happiness is sincere; no one enjoys Vee's ignorance.
- **Dialogue sparse, broken by breath** — the fragmentation *is* the leak; a fluent briefing reads as calculation.
- **Register:** explicit, embodied, heat-3.
