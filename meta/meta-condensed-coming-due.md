## What the scene is

The scene opens on a **three-day runway** off the mixer weekend (Sunday at Pace's, Monday night alone) and lands on the first Tuesday class, where the standing post-class SATC dinner is *declined* — the ritual withheld, which loads time + dread into {{Gesso}}. The charge is **detonation + anxiety**, not erotic-confession: Randi, all innocence, hands Vee a word that goes off in her; Vee bails the dinner — then her own mouth reaches for the next date anyway; and the goodbye kiss, always frictionless, becomes the night's second charge.

9. **The goodbye — the kiss-question (load-bearing).** The goodbye that was always frictionless is suddenly a thing to get through — **for Vee only.** **Randi has no hesitation:** hug + mouth-kiss are what friends do at the end of a good night, "natural as the light going at the end of a day," already reaching. The hitch is all Vee's: she **wants the kiss and refuses it**, because after Saturday (the dancefloor) and the small hours of Sunday spent explaining Saturday to Pace *from up on top of him*, every "French" goodbye kiss has been "called up for review" — she can't say what they were, what she let them be, what some part of her wanted them to be. Unsure what a kiss would be **to Randi** (the mixer kiss was, to Randi's knowledge, a *show* — *"I almost believed it"*), more afraid what it would be **to herself.** So she gives a few degrees of turn and the kiss lands at the corner of her mouth and slides onto her cheek — *"a miss got up to look like an aim."* Randi recovers it into lightness (half-clocks, misreads; stays unknowing/warm). *Symmetry: her mouth reached toward Randi with "Brunch?"; her body refuses the kiss — want and refusal in one minute.*
10. **The close — the walk home.** The heat doesn't go out; it **banks and settles in to simmer, unaddressed** — nothing done, nothing decided. And the heat is the least of it: *"the heat she could have waited out. Under it was the want, greater and more durable,"* walking home "with the apprehension by the hand." **Refusal-to-name (the fuse into {{Gesso}}):** *"It wanted a name. She wouldn't give it one — not on the walk, not yet."* Active refusal (she withholds the name the heat wants), not an absence of one — and POV-clean (no narrator forecast past the walk).

## The engine: the word "have" — a three-point verbal hinge

The detonation is anchored on a single deniable word, **"have,"** returning for the third time:

1. **Invitation** — `on-her-floor.md:13`, Randi over matcha, winking: *"Guys you have to manage all night. You I just get to* have.*"* Planted playful and deniable (= you're easy company, no managing).
2. **Confession** — `boyfriend.md:87`, Vee to Pace: *"She said guys you have to manage all night, and me she just gets to* have.*"* — the **italic is Vee's own**; the word has already snagged in her.
3. **Coming Due** — Randi, winding her scarf: *"**Having** you at the dance was so great. I've wanted to do that for the longest time."*

**The line is exactly as deniable as it always was** — Randi means *I loved having you as my date / I've wanted to bring you along for ages*, pure warm-friend, **sexually unmarked**. What has changed is **Vee's ear, not Randi's mouth.** After the kiss, after confessing *"I wanted her mouth on me"* (`boyfriend.md:185–189`), after that telltale italic, Vee can no longer hear "have" as *company* — she hears *possession, desire, being had.* The collapse is entirely inside Vee; Randi stays innocent on the surface, and that is the cruelty (there is nothing Vee can point to — you can't accuse a woman of saying she had fun). Same move as *"you picked well"* in {{A Clean Plate}}. **Keep "having" verbatim as the trip-wire — the echo lives in the repetition.**

## The register at the detonation — and deferred analysis (re-keyed 2026-09-01)

- **Fever, not sickness.** She knows she has to bail *"the way you feel a fever coming on"* — the image welds heat + illness (the sickness *is* the wanting), is involuntary/incoming (body-before-mind), and quietly foreshadows the reckoning (a fever crests and breaks; *coming due*). Use the prodrome sense, not "feverish with desire."
- **The under-feeling is NOT "cold"/regret** (retired 2026-09-01, author ruling — a *cold* reads as too much negativity to feel *toward an attraction*; the desire itself is clean and half-welcome). What comes up under the heat is **fear of what it means and what it costs**, felt as sensation, never itemized. At the detonation: the **heat** ("she had no say in it"); **the wanting she can bear** — "a door onto herself standing cracked, and every part of her that wasn't afraid wanted it open"; and, behind that door, **the thing she can't look at** — "the apprehension of something that had already happened and hadn't yet shown its size, a thing that reached, in that half second, everything she had." Note **"already happened"** (swept up), *not* "already done" (which reads as chosen — wrong for where her head is). The apprehension carries the Pace/Randi/whole-world stakes as *"everything she had,"* never a list.
- **She EXPERIENCES here; she ANALYZES later** (author ruling 2026-08-30, holds). She does **not** connect it to anything — in particular not to the lie to Pace (*"nothing,"* `boyfriend.md:217`). She feels its *size and durability*, and precisely because of that she **refuses to name it.**
- **The fuse into {{Gesso}}:** the unnamed want + apprehension rides home with her — *"It wanted a name. She wouldn't give it one — not yet."* The **refusal-to-name** is the fuse (the way {{A Clean Plate}} sent home the thing the joke "had only been a way to swallow… and it wouldn't stay down"). Register throughout: **anxiety**, not outright fear/terror.

## Reference rendering — drafted (2026-09-01)

The scene is drafted (`coming-due.md`). The detonation and the close as written:

> It reached Vee a half-beat late, the same lag it had had in his bed — the word arriving after the woman who'd said it had already moved on to her coat. *Having.* And behind the word, riding in on it, the thing it carried: that she was something Randi got to have. Had wanted to have, and for a long time. In his bed there had been him for it — the whole solid fact of him under her to pour the heat into, to set it against and call it his. Here there was a scarf being knotted and a bright emptying hall and a woman smiling at her over nothing she couldn't have defended out loud to a priest, and Vee's body went hunting for somewhere to put the heat and came back with nowhere at all. The heat came up anyway — she had no say in it. The wanting she could bear; the wanting was a door onto herself standing cracked, and every part of her that wasn't afraid wanted it open. It was what stood behind the door she couldn't look at — only the apprehension of something that had already happened and hadn't yet shown its size, a thing that reached, in that half second, everything she had. She was on her feet before she'd decided to be, because she wasn't going to be able to sit across a table from this tonight and hold still under it. She knew it the way you feel a fever coming on — the body certain of it a beat before the head got the news.

> Vee turned the other way into the wind. She'd half thought the walk would put the heat out; it didn't. It banked, and settled in low, and went on simmering, unaddressed — nothing done about it, nothing decided. And the heat was the least of it, besides; the heat she could have waited out. Under it was the want, greater and more durable, and it had the apprehension by the hand. The two of them walked her home. It wanted a name. She wouldn't give it one — not on the walk, not yet.
