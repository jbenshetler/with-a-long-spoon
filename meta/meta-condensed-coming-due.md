## What the scene is

The **dodged reunion** — the one {{Boyfriend}} spends its last page bracing for, and Vee doesn't have it. A **three-day runway** off the mixer weekend (Sunday at Pace's; Monday night alone) lands on the first Tuesday class, where the standing post-class SATC dinner is **declined** — the ritual withheld, loading time + dread into {{Gesso}}. The charge is **detonation + anxiety**, not erotic-confession: Randi, all innocence, hands Vee a word — *having* — that goes off in her. Vee bails the dinner; but **Randi won't give her up** and walks her home, so the dodge buys *more* Randi, not less, and the **dorm goodbye** becomes the night's second, hotter charge — a **neck-kiss payback** that leaves Vee's body ringing, out of which her own mouth reaches for the next date. It **ends on Vee's non-answer**: Cassie, just in from the cold (she walked past the kiss unseen), asks the plain direct thing about what she saw — and Vee, who genuinely doesn't know, can't answer. The beat is a **handoff into a forthcoming Cassie–Vee scene**, not a self-contained button.

The beg-off would be the **sorority-officer skip lever** (`meta-plan-satc-tracks.md`) — except Randi doesn't take it. The **standards-board Thursday** is repurposed into her **"discipline a bad girl"** line inside the hug, which primes the neck-kiss.

## The engine: the word "have" — a three-point verbal hinge

The detonation is anchored on a single deniable word, **"have,"** returning for the third time:

1. **Invitation** — `on-her-floor.md:13`, Randi over matcha, winking: *"Guys you have to manage all night. You I just get to* have.*"* Planted playful and deniable (= you're easy company, no managing).
2. **Confession** — `boyfriend.md:87`, Vee to Pace: *"She said guys you have to manage all night, and me she just gets to* have.*"* — the **italic is Vee's own**; the word has already snagged in her.
3. **Coming Due** — Randi, gathering her bag: *"**Having** you at the dance was so great. I've wanted to do that for the longest time."*

**The line is exactly as deniable as it always was** — Randi means *I loved having you as my date / I've wanted to bring you along for ages*, pure warm-friend, **sexually unmarked**. What has changed is **Vee's ear, not Randi's mouth.** After the kiss, after confessing *"I wanted her mouth on me"* (`boyfriend.md:185–189`), after that telltale italic, Vee can no longer hear "have" as *company* — she hears *possession, desire, being had.* The collapse is entirely inside Vee; Randi stays innocent on the surface, and that is the cruelty (there is nothing Vee can point to — you can't accuse a woman of saying she had fun). **Keep "having" verbatim as the trip-wire — the echo lives in the repetition.**

## The register at the detonation — and deferred analysis

- **Fever, not sickness — and recast off body-before-mind (2026-09-02).** She knows she has to bail *"the way you know a fever coming: not here yet, and already past arguing with."* Keep the prodrome/incoming-inevitability sense (a fever crests and breaks; *coming due*), **not** "the body certain of it before the head got the news" — that body-before-mind phrasing is retired here and the device is **saved for the "Brunch" blurt** (see Guards). Not "feverish with desire."
- **The under-feeling is NOT "cold"/regret** (author ruling — a *cold* reads as too much negativity toward an attraction; the desire itself is clean and half-welcome). What comes up is **fear of what it means and what it costs**, felt as sensation, never itemized. As drafted, the detonation moves: **heat** ("the heat came up anyway; she had no say in it") → **best-friend vs heat** (*it's Randi, her friend, and her body wants her anyway, with nowhere to put it and no one to charge it to*) → **"Saturday's joke had cracked open a door, and behind the door stood something larger than heat and more durable … while the other half would not look"** → **"only apprehension: of something that had already happened … a thing that reached, in that one word, everything she had."** Note **"already happened"** (swept up), *not* "already done" (chosen); **"in that one word"** (the word *having*), *not* "half second."
- **She EXPERIENCES here; she ANALYZES later** (author ruling 2026-08-30, holds). She does **not** connect it to anything — in particular not to the lie to Pace (*"nothing,"* `boyfriend.md:217`). She feels its size and durability, and precisely because of that she **refuses to name it.**
- **The fuse into {{Gesso}}:** now the **ringing "Brunch"** + the unnamed want/apprehension up the stairs + **Cassie's unanswerable question.** The refusal-to-name is now also a **can't-name** — externalized by Cassie ("she could not answer it"). Register throughout: **anxiety**, not terror.

- **Vee chooses the pull-away.** The neck-kiss is a prank sprung on her; her body answers (goosebumps, the sound) and she **pulls away** — her boundary. Keep it a prank, not a predatory advance (anti-trope armor; "Vee chooses at every gate").
- **The neck-kiss is precise payback** — same spot ({{Covering}}, {{A Clean Plate}}), "twice and been told not to weaponize"; her body **can't deflect** the way Randi's did — the asymmetry is that Vee is the more caught.
- **Cassie is structural, not eros-knowing** — distrusts Randi/the cost, sees Vee receding, but has **no** prior physical sighting and **no** knowledge of the mixer kiss; the neck-kiss she catches is her **first physical evidence.** Register: flat/exact — here a **plain direct question about what she saw** ("What was that with you and Randi?"), the "?" carrying **worry + slight disbelief** (a crack in her usual flat). The line points at the witnessed kiss (cashing the "coat still on, cold still coming off her" seed), not at the whole Pace/Randi configuration; it **opens the following scene** rather than buttoning this one. She is Vee's **oldest** friend — **Randi is now the *best* friend** (the displacement is deliberate). Grounding: `meta-arch-cassie.md`.
- **Vee can't answer because she doesn't know** — the refusal-to-name doubles as a can't-name.
- **Body-before-mind is spent on the "Brunch" blurt** (out of the ringing) — deliberately saved from the fever beat, so it lands once, hard.
- **"have"/"having" verbatim; the wrist-charge is not "like electricity."**
- **The candy roll is Vee's** (canon: `clean-plate.md:53`).
- Not a full SATC-dinner beat — no dinner-table confession, no bathroom-flee; a compressed Sun-sex + Mon-withheld-solo runway, then the dread, the dodge, the neck-payback, the blurt, and Cassie.
