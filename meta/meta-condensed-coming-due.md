## What the scene is

The **dodged reunion** — the reunion {{Boyfriend}} spends its last page bracing for, and Vee doesn't have it. A **down-beat / skipped dinner**: the standing Tuesday SATC dinner is *declined*, which is itself a variation (the ritual withheld), and it loads time + dread into {{Gesso}}. The scene's charge is not erotic-confession but **detonation + dread**: Randi, all innocence, hands Vee a word that goes off in her, and Vee bails — then her own mouth reaches for the next date anyway.

Structurally this is the **sorority-officer skip lever** in `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` (Randi's standards-board Thursday) *plus* Vee's avoidance — the two reasons braided so the reschedule reads as ordinary friend-logistics on the surface.

2. **Dressing, nerves not anticipation.** {{A Clean Plate}}'s "keyed-up gladness she saved for getting dressed for Pace" flips — she armors up, can't settle; dressing to *face* something, not to be seen.

Close on extended dread: she scheduled the reckoning herself.

## The engine: the word "have" — a three-point verbal hinge

The detonation is anchored on a single deniable word, **"have,"** returning for the third time:

1. **Invitation** — `on-her-floor.md:13`, Randi over matcha, winking: *"Guys you have to manage all night. You I just get to* have.*"* Planted playful and deniable (= you're easy company, no managing).
2. **Confession** — `boyfriend.md:87`, Vee to Pace: *"She said guys you have to manage all night, and me she just gets to* have.*"* — the **italic is Vee's own**; the word has already snagged in her.
3. **Coming Due** — Randi, packing up: *"**Having** you at the dance was great. I've wanted to do that for a long time."*

**The line is exactly as deniable as it always was** — Randi means *I loved having you as my date / I've wanted to bring you along for ages*, pure warm-friend, fully claimable, **sexually unmarked**. What has changed is **Vee's ear, not Randi's mouth.** After the kiss, after confessing *"I wanted her mouth on me"* (`boyfriend.md:185–189`), after that telltale italic, Vee can no longer hear "have" as *company* — she hears *possession, desire, being had.* The collapse is entirely inside Vee; Randi stays innocent on the surface, and that is the cruelty (there is nothing Vee can point to — you can't accuse a woman of saying she had fun). Same move as *"you picked well"* in {{A Clean Plate}}. **Keep "having" verbatim as the trip-wire — do not paraphrase it away; the echo lives in the repetition.**

## The callback to Boyfriend's "again, worse" mechanic

`boyfriend.md` already runs — and labels — this exact beat, twice: hearing a word land a half-beat late, *"warmer and worse than she'd said it."*
- `:85` — she hears *"date"* land: *"the inch made her hear the word again… it came back warmer and worse than she'd said it."*
- `:89` — **"And it happened again, worse: she heard what she'd just said, what it meant that Randi just got to *have* her,"** heat climbing, *"she could not have told you how much of it was the thought and how much was him buried to the root in her."*

**Coming Due is the third turn of the same screw**, with the escalation built in: **in Pace's bed the heat had an alibi — "him buried to the root." In the lecture hall there is no Pace.** The word lands, the heat climbs, and there is nothing to charge it to but the word and the woman who said it. The alibi is gone — *that* is the "worse." (Shadow `:89`'s clause — *"what it meant: that Randi just got to have her"* — do **not** repeat it verbatim.)

## Fever, and deferred analysis

- **Fever, not sickness.** She knows she has to bail *"the way you feel a fever coming on"* — the image welds heat + illness (the sickness *is* the wanting), is involuntary/incoming (body-before-mind), and quietly foreshadows the reckoning (a fever crests and breaks; *coming due*). Not "about to be sick" (aversion-only, one note). Use the prodrome sense, not "feverish with desire" (dodges the cliché).
- **She EXPERIENCES here; she ANALYZES later** (author ruling 2026-08-30). At the detonation Vee feels the triad as pure sensation — **heat** (named), **fear** (the fever-dread), **regret** (a **cold she cannot name**) — and does **not** connect it to anything. In particular the lie to Pace (*"nothing,"* `boyfriend.md:217`) is **not** articulated here — that's analysis, and it belongs to a later beat (the walk home / a beat before {{Gesso}}). *"She could not have said what the cold was"* **plants the fuse**: the unnamed cold rides with her into {{Gesso}}, the way {{A Clean Plate}} sent "the case that wouldn't stay closed" home with her.

## Reference rendering — the detonation (workshop draft, 2026-08-30)

> Randi said it easily, folding her scarf. "Having you at the dance was so great. I've wanted to do that for the longest time."
>
> And it came a half-beat late, the way it had come in his bed — the word landing after Randi had already moved past it. *Having.* And behind it what it meant: that Randi just got to have her. Had wanted to have her, a long time. Only in his bed there'd been *him* — the plain fact of him to fold the heat into, to blame it on. Here there was a folded scarf and a fluorescent hall and nothing to charge it to but the word and the woman saying it, smiling, meaning nothing she couldn't defend. The heat came up anyway, and something cold came up under it, and she could not have said what the cold was — only that she was not going to be able to sit across a table from it tonight. She knew it the way you feel a fever coming on.

## Guards

- **Randi stays unknowing and warm.** Her enthusiasm is real; she does not clock the damage, or half-clocks that Vee's gone odd and misreads it — never calculating here. The want leaks as warmth, not strategy.
- **POV: experience, not analysis** (above). Render the flush in the body; no mental recap of {{Boyfriend}}. The reader supplies the collision.
- **Signposting:** *"his bed,"* not "Pace's bed" — it's Vee's association reaching back two nights, not a narrator footnote; the Pace-then / no-Pace-now contrast is the escalation and needs the anchor.
- **The "have"/"having" word is load-bearing verbatim** in all three scenes; the wrist-charge is **not** "like electricity."
- Down-beat: no confession, no bathroom trip; the charge is dread + the blurt, not sex.
