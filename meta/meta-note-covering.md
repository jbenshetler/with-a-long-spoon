## The table — beat order (ruled 2026-08-21)

1. The first *scheduled* SATC — the twice-a-week ritual installing itself without anyone deciding.
2. **Vee's debrief owes only the weekend** (no five-week download — see the chronology entry): Friday compressed — arrival, sex, and food; **pancakes at midnight, nothing on**. Vee **blushes, takes a drink of cold water**.
3. **Randi's story — entered unscripted (ruled 2026-08-22; supersedes "told as a joke to soften the ground").** The motivation is the rhyme: Vee's *on his knee, on the floor* pulls out of Randi the **lawn-boy knee story** — the boy who did the lawns at the rented shore house went down on one knee in the grass to ask her out a second time ("You can't say no to that"); boardwalk fair, merry-go-round, teddy bear; "unbelievably sweet, and it worked on me." She offers it as a *finished, sweet* thing. It is **Vee's earnest follow-up** — "Oh! That's so sweet… What happened with him?" — that opens the catastrophe: a question Randi could not have prepared for, and is too committed to the brightness to refuse ("Well. Since you ask."). The lawn boy **is** the boyfriend of the panties dinner (identity merge, canon in `meta-arch-randi.md`); the good-bedroom afternoon was their **first time as a couple** ("first time as an *us*"). The aftermath lands after "Nothing.": he sat through the rest of dinner barely eating, answered when spoken to; **he quit that week; she never saw him again** — the one place the floor shows before the brightness switches back on. Backstory canon in `meta-arch-randi.md` (The Mother — Active Cruelty, and the Panties Dinner).
4. **The prepared question vs. the one that arrives.** Randi curates by anticipating questions; she is armored for "Did they find them?" Vee — aghast *for her friend* — asks: **"How did they tell you they'd found them?"** Not a plot question; a question about her family. Vee's question must read as innocent care with her characteristic precision, never as perceptive probing — the curation is defeated by warmth, not interrogation.
5. **The slip.** Because Randi is in love and this is the first real story she has told, the question pulls her into the dinner scene itself — the boy at Sunday dinner, the mother, the laundered robin's-egg panties, the line. The cost lands here (flush as texture; the control-slip rung stays the neck beat above). **Updated 2026-08-22 with the unscripted entry (beat 3):** the whole story is unplanned — Vee's "What happened with him?" is the slip's trigger, and Randi's only choice mid-flight is whether to break the brightness (she doesn't, until "Nothing."). The instrumentality question is moot — there is none; Thursday's reframe reads as reflex because it is one.
6. **Recovery = extraction (one gesture).** *"You went in with nothing under the coat. How long before Pace let you put clothes on again?"* — wink. In character: the turn away from her own pain to Vee's embarrassment; the ask disguised as teasing (an earnest ask would be need on the table — see the need engine, `meta-arch-randi.md`); "let you" keeps Pace in the authoring position.
7. **Round two — explicit: Vee tells Saturday morning** — the pleasure-slave → pleasure-princess arc in its canonical comic register (`forbidden-kiss.md:27–31`; the hallway coinage kept, never promoted; the breakfast, the apron, the burned toast), with Randi's questions extracting past the romance cut — peaking at **the toe-on-the-chair beat** ("Paint it for me. 8. **The stall (rung 4 of the orgasm-migration ladder, landed early — author ruling 2026-08-22; full ruling in the chronology entry).** Charged past holding in the painting of the toe beat, Vee excuses herself ("Hold that thought") and completes in the restroom — going *through* Randi's imagined kind eyes, filed as about Pace ("obviously"). **The count toehold:** *"The third restroom she'd touched herself in — she counted the practice room; of course she counted the practice room."* The arithmetic is wrong by the page (`practice-room.md:121` — the caught hand) and right by her character: **she catastrophizes herself in memory and is kind to others**; the discrepancy is deliberate — never "fix" it, and the "of course" is the careful reader's only toehold. She **promises the mirror she'll control herself next time** — the promise that re-arms Thursday's brake.
9. **The return.** The restaurant closed while she was gone — lights off below, chairs going up, the check already down; the telling never resumes, so the kiss's turn never comes (the story ended jointly by her body and the clock). Randi, pouring the last of the wine: **"Things were really heated. It's a good thing you got relief."** — Vee's face stops — **"After driving out to see Pace. He took care of you, but good. Tell me I'm wrong."** Grenade-and-frame (the established fall pattern; ruled *not* a new overtness rung — Thursday keeps rung 1); Vee takes the offered frame: *"You're not wrong."*

You're gone for that boy."* — Vee glows — *"After all you've told me, I want to see him again."* Micro-canon: **Randi was invited to Wednesday's dinner off-page, by text, before tonight.** Double registers: *gone for that boy*; *all you've told me* = the corpus; *again* honors the met-once cover — and the reader is meant to **suspect she's headed to Pace's right now. The suspicion gets zero staging help** (no direction, no car, no glance): the line plus the reader's knowledge of her week does all of it. "Properly meet" is Randi's word first; Vee innocently repeats the errand at {{Among Friends}}.

This opens the new goodbye rung (see Rung clarification above).
