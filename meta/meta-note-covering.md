## The table — beat order (ruled 2026-08-21)

1. The first *scheduled* SATC — the twice-a-week ritual installing itself without anyone deciding.
2. **Vee's debrief owes only the weekend** (no five-week download — see the chronology entry): Friday compressed — arrival, sex, and food; **pancakes at midnight, nothing on**. Vee **blushes, takes a drink of cold water**.
3. **Randi's story — the panties dinner, told as a joke to soften the ground.** She clocked the blush (the real story surfacing and being swallowed); she knows Vee was mortified to perform {{Nothing Underneath}} and wants the real story under the triumph. Backstory canon in `meta-arch-randi.md` (The Mother — Active Cruelty, and the Panties Dinner).
4. **The prepared question vs. the one that arrives.** Randi curates by anticipating questions; she is armored for "Did they find them?" Vee — aghast *for her friend* — asks: **"How did they tell you they'd found them?"** Not a plot question; a question about her family. Vee's question must read as innocent care with her characteristic precision, never as perceptive probing — the curation is defeated by warmth, not interrogation.
5. **The slip.** Because Randi is in love and this is the first real story she has told, the question pulls her into the dinner scene itself — the boy at Sunday dinner, the mother, the laundered robin's-egg panties, the line. The cost lands here (flush as texture; the control-slip rung stays the neck beat above). The story *begins* deployed and *becomes* real mid-flight — curated in intent, breached in execution; the instrumentality is never legible on the page (she never glances at Vee mid-story to check how it's landing), and Thursday's reframe must read as reflex, not preparation.
6. **Recovery = extraction (one gesture).** *"You went in with nothing under the coat. How long before Pace let you put clothes on again?"* — wink. In character: the turn away from her own pain to Vee's embarrassment; the ask disguised as teasing (an earnest ask would be need on the table — see the need engine, `meta-arch-randi.md`); "let you" keeps Pace in the authoring position.
7. **Vee tells Saturday morning** — the pleasure-slave → pleasure-princess arc in its canonical comic register (`forbidden-kiss.md:27–31`; the hallway coinage kept, never promoted; the breakfast, the apron, the burned toast) — the story's own chronology walking toward the kiss. Note the weekend's two foods stay distinct: Friday's midnight pancakes ({{Nothing Underneath}}) vs. Saturday's breakfast ({{Forbidden Kiss}}).
8. **The restaurant is closing — just as the story's breakfast is finishing** — chairs going up, the check — the external clock ends the story at the drafted material's own hinge, leaving untold exactly the half she most needs Randi's counsel on (until Thursday). Double duty: duration made visible (they've shut the place down on a school night, and will now do this twice a week), and the withholding never becomes an on-page decision. The story is told *short*, not swerved.
9. **The goodnight (the neck beat).** The goodbye kiss lands exactly as always — then, still hugging, Randi giggles. *"What?"* *"When you came to my room after Christmas, you made my neck wet — here"* — pointing awkwardly inside the hug. Vee — full of gratitude for the help that got Pace back, the charged telling, Randi's soul-baring, a glass of wine or two — *"I'm sorry for making a mess,"* and a light kiss where Randi points, without thinking. **Randi flinches** (the control-slip, first instance; the hard guard in the chronology entry — Vee must not file it — stands). This opens the new goodbye rung (see Rung clarification above).
10. On the way home, the guilt seed: she notices the clock gave her an out — and that she was relieved to take it. Her mind stays on the untold half; the flinch belongs to the reader alone.

- **No callback to the lecture at the table.** The hau resonances (the coat; the laundered panties coming home) are never drawn; the reader's memory does the work.
