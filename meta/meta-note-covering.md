## The table — Randi's story (the panties dinner)

Backstory canon lives in `meta-arch-randi.md` (The Mother — Active Cruelty, and the Panties Dinner). Scene mechanics:

- Randi knows Vee was mortified to perform {{Nothing Underneath}}; she wants the *real* story under the triumph. She opens the exchange by telling the panties dinner **as a joke** — the sheets, ha ha, never thought about it.
- **The prepared question vs. the one that arrives.** Randi curates by anticipating questions; she is armored for "Did they find them?" Vee — aghast *for her friend* — asks: **"How did they tell you they'd found them?"** Not a plot question; a question about her family. Vee's question must read as innocent care with her characteristic precision, never as perceptive probing — the curation is defeated by warmth, not interrogation.
- **The slip.** Because Randi is in love and this is the first real story she has told, the question pulls her into the dinner scene itself — the boy at Sunday dinner, the mother, the laundered robin's-egg panties, the line. The cost lands here; the **neck flush** (the entry's control-slip rung — first thing Vee sees that Randi didn't mean to show) climbs at the true telling.
- **The recovery.** Randi makes light of it at the end. The mortification must stay **undecidable on reread** — real breach vs. modeled disclosure — so she never glances at Vee mid-story to check how it's landing; Thursday's reframe must read as reflex, not preparation.
- **The ask.** Then she needs to hear Vee's story — the real {{Nothing Underneath}}, which hands the table to the coat and, per the chronology entry, to the swerve away from {{Forbidden Kiss}}.
- **No callback to the lecture at the table.** The hau resonances (the coat; the laundered panties coming home) are never drawn; the reader's memory does the work.
