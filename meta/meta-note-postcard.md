## The governing inversion

**The photograph is cheap; the fantasy is expensive.** Handing over an object costs less than saying a sentence about your own appetite. So the picture is not the climax — it is the down payment, and Vee gives Randi's body away *because it is cheaper than confessing her own imagination.*

She sacrifices her friend's privacy to protect her own. That is the chapter.

## The five stalls — a ladder that undoes *"this"*

She erased a person into a demonstrative; he pays her inch by inch to put the woman back. It reads as intimacy and is the opposite.

1. **The occasion.** *Nothing, just — this afternoon.* → held. **First hold → *Again?*** True answer is the caption in her own mouth: *wish you were here.* Randi's voice, said aloud, in Pace's bed.
2. **The body.** *The picture.* → held. True answer names a part.
3. **The act.** *You saw.* (Pointing at the evidence to dodge the words.) → held. True answer is her own hand, said in her own mouth — the hand he has just spent five minutes tasting. *(The flicker lands here: **I have been here before.** Shoved down.)*
4. **The name.** He asks nothing. **He simply does not pay for a pronoun.** She has run on *she / her / it / this* since the photograph; the inches stop and she cannot work out why until she hears herself. *Randi.* He never demands it; he declines to purchase the erasure.
5. **The last coin — what it was to her,** per {{Boyfriend}}'s law (*"Not the kiss. What the kiss had been to her"*). Not that she wanted Randi's mouth on her — **that she wanted to be the one doing it.** Her nipple in Vee's mouth; *I put*, not *I wanted*. Being done to can be filed as passivity, sex-brain, a body that didn't get the memo. **Wanting to do it cannot be filed at all**, which is why *I'm not gay* is rubble at beat 22.

## Mechanisms

**The unbroken mouth.** Pace does not stop kissing her fingers — not when she stills herself, not while she explains, not when she shows him. **His silence has a physical cause:** his mouth is full of her hand, so he is mechanically unable to interrupt, and never has to be written as *choosing* not to. Vee reads the continuation as proof he is not jealous and not bothered, and keeps going. On reread the patience is the terrible thing: he is tasting what she did while she explains why she did it.

**The self-stilling.** The first stillness in the chapter is **hers**, and she does it out of care. It performs exactly the office his holds perform later — *"the stopping was worse than any question, because it turned her back to look at the thing she'd smoothed over"* — so the gesture arrives as love before it arrives as extraction. Same physical fact, opposite function, ninety seconds apart.

**"It was this."** The writing-off rendered as grammar: a person converted into a demonstrative, distance bought with a pronoun. It is the sentence that hands Randi over, and she delivers it while refusing to say the name. Prefigures the threesome's *a woman* — Vee's own grammar gets there first. Never point at it.

**His pulse.** Vee's entire architecture is organized around the shame of a body that announces itself without leave. Here that body is **his**, once: he is inside her, she shows him the photograph, and he answers involuntarily. He has something to hide and gets away with it because she has a cultural explanation ready — *ordinary greed, boys are like that*, the exact {{Boyfriend}} file. The one time her instrument points at the right man, her own habit covers for him.

**The photograph is the permission gate.** No rationing may begin before the reveal: while a rival reading is available, any withholding reads as a man reacting to a possible man. The instant it is Randi, jealousy is off the table — he settled that in January (*"It's all right. I don't mind"*) — so every hold afterward can only read as appetite.

**Rationing responds to tidying, never to content.** He never stalls a true thing; true things are paid. He stalls the smoothed version, exactly as at {{Boyfriend}}'s *"we danced bigger"* / *"she moved me around"* / *"the skirt rode up some."* Enforcing honesty, never punishing truth.

**Payment makes her hear her own word.** The engine, not decoration: *"he let her down an inch for it, one exact inch, and the inch made her hear the word again, hear it the way he'd just heard it."*
