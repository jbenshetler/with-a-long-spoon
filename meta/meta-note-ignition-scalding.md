# Companion note — Ignition (`ignition-scalding`)

Scene-specific design for `scenes/ignition-scalding.md`. Structure and craft authority for this chapter; defers to the Bible on character and to `meta-plan-chronology.md` on placement.

## Structure

A diptych, Pace's POV throughout. Two movements, two days, one turn:

- **Movement I — The Arrangement** (Sunday afternoon, post-coital). Randi seduces back some of the time she has ceded to Vee, and in the heat of it says one truer thing than she planned: *"I can't wait to taste her,"* then covers it with the *"sloppy seconds"* leak. Pace hears a request she does not know she has made and, without telling her the shape of it, sets Tuesday. The turn opens here and is left suspended.
- **Movement II — After She Leaves** (the Tuesday two days later). Vee leaves at eleven, sated and comforted (the first night she can't stay over). Randi arrives at midnight to run the transgression she came for and gets more of it than she budgeted. The turn completes at the kneel.

The two-day gap is *internal to the turn* — the interval in which a spoken promise ripens into a deed — so the unit sits contiguous on the timeline; nothing slots into the gap. On disk the two movements are separated by a plain scene rule, no titled headers (see **De-scaffolding**).

## Function

Ignition of Randi's **scalding** register: her private jealousy crosses here from controlled/cool/appetitive to scalding — the envy of the aftermath (Vee can take the full sequence and be *comforted*; Randi runs it and the comfort won't land) made flesh. This is **ignition-1** (the private heat breaking), which precedes and enables the later shift to acts of sexual humiliation directed at Vee (ignition-2, downstream). Surface read = a hot, fun, slightly wicked two-night sequence; the dread is reread-only.

## The taste-ladder (Movement II, the kneel) — load-bearing

Randi came for the transgression she *chose* — Vee-was-here, Vee's scent on him, the frisson she names at the door (*"I can smell her on you"*). What ambushes her is the **taste**: Pace has not washed the afternoon off — Vee is literally still on him — so when Randi goes down for the known taste of *him*, she meets a woman's, and then Vee's. The recognition runs as a ladder, rendered **only from outside** (see containers, below), through her body and Pace's inference because he built it:

> anticipated his musk → *something's different* → *that's not his taste* → *a woman* → *Vee* → intense shame ∥ intense arousal, at once, neither winning.

Then — per the ruling — **she proceeds anyway, and the proceeding is enormous**: overtaken and not stopping, she goes back down and takes all of it. The `muh` (the body voting out loud ahead of the deciding part) is preserved as the hinge sound.

The emotional logic under the surprise *is* the scald: Vee has gotten everywhere — even into the one taste of him Randi thought was still just hers. Vee displacing Randi in the place she didn't think she could be displaced.

**Pace's register** is gift-logic / pride-in-effect, never the operator: he made her a gift (heard her wish, delivered it sooner and more wholly than she knew to ask) and gets to watch it land. Not "his evening going to plan" — the plan *was* the surprise. (Retired possessive/having-been-right register per `meta-craft-pace.md`.)
