  staging was physically impossible — her hand stopped *at the waistband*

  the sweep-away — defensible as her later framing; no edits needed.

# Triage — Famished (line-edit pass, 2026-08-07)

Source: `audits/line-edit/famished.md` (13 findings) plus cold-read enrichment

Context for later passes: this is the most heavily reader-praised chapter in
the pass so far. All four cold readers independently named the same four
passages — the "Famished" exchange (:29), "Would you be bothered if I was too
hard?" (:63), "the shame ran the whole length of it and never closed her"

## Fixed

- `:7` — "deep and cool and a little serious" → "deep and quiet and a little
  serious" ("cool" twice in one sentence; "cool going warm in her hand" is the
  working use). Also cut "It was a beautiful color, and that was reason
  enough." (see the reader-praise note below).
- `:21` — "garlic crushed" → "garlic minced" (undesigned "crushed" twice in
  one sentence; "crushed tomatoes" is the product term and had to stay). Also
  "a small bowl" → "a bowl" (linter `a-small` warn; *measured* already carries
  the precision).
- `:23` — cut "He'd opened it before she arrived." "Already breathing" is the
  information; the second sentence restated the inference as flat fact in a
  paragraph whose whole method is showing.
- `:25` — cut "without asking" from "He handed her a glass without asking."
  Flagged by two independent instruments for opposite reasons: the linter
  (`without-asking`, warn) objects to the *label* telegraphing the consent
  theme; gpt-5.5 flagged the *behavior* cold — "it pricks slightly because
  asking is so central to him." Cutting the label fixes the first and makes
  the second invisible.
- `:47` — cut "The body of a man who used himself continuously and without
  comment." A caption under its own photograph: "the mass a man builds moving
  weight" and "made by tools and not for show" had already said it. The
  paragraph now lands on her palm and his heart.
- `:53` — cut "He was getting the lay of her." The land-survey figure ran
  twice in one paragraph; the closing simile ("like a man learning ground he
  meant to come back to") is reader-praised by gpt-5.5 as a peak of erotic
  charge, and the idiom pre-announced the metaphor it was about to make good.
- `:65` — cut "Something shifted in her chest." Flagged by both instruments
  (editor: stock interiority; linter: `in-her-chest`, a 13-occurrence locus
  tic). The paragraph had already rendered the shift concretely, and "No,"
  arriving straight off "the good thing, the right thing" makes the answer
  itself the evidence.
- `:69`, `:71` — "the round of her" thinned from three consecutive paragraphs
  to one. `:69` → "his hands coming up the backs of her thighs to hold her
  open to his mouth"; `:71` → "his hand was under her". `:73` ("the round of
  her filling his palm") keeps it — the only use that makes the roundness do
  something, and it sits in the climax.
- `:71` — cut "through the slick of her" (both that figure and "the wet of
  her" doubled across :69/:71; at :71 they landed four words apart).
- `:71` — "and her hand was in his hair and she was not watching it be there"
  → "and she was not watching herself do any of it." The hair-arc is :67
  (stopped hand converts to holding hand) → :73 ("fisted"); :71 restated :67
  without advancing it. **Note the editor's recommendation was unusable as
  written** — it proposed cutting the hair clause and keeping "she was not
  watching it be there," which orphans *it* (same failure mode as
  `how-its-done:17`). The chosen form keeps the not-monitoring beat, which
  :81 pays off.
- `:79` — cut "She'd been wrong in the best possible way." Off-the-shelf idiom
  capping the chapter's most precise quiet passage ("stored and approximate…
  the actual thing behind the proxy"). Ends on "She'd thought she knew it."
- `:101` — "catching the kitchen light with a soft diffuse glow" → "catching
  the kitchen light." Doubled modifier, and the only vague item in a four-item
  list of precise physical observations.

## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **`:71`/`:81` "the shame was still running" ×2 — finding *withdrawn*, not
  merely left standing.** The editor wanted :71 varied so :81 would land
  fresh. The reverse is true: the fixed wording is the constant against which
  the two clauses that follow it measure the change (running while she burns
  through it → running while she cannot find the top of it). Cold-read
  evidence is unusually direct — claude-opus-4-8 quoted *both* halves together
  as one unit ("I felt that as a genuine release, not a trick… I was moved")
  and claude-fable-5 did the same, calling :83 "the sentence I think the whole
  book has been building toward." Same logic as the "randi pulled her into"
  protection: the repeated thing recurring *is* the content.
- **`:69` "He stayed in the wet of her"** — kept after the :71 fix, which
  leaves "the wet of her" doubled two paragraphs apart (much quieter than the
  same-clause collision that was fixed). The author proposed "He stayed in the
  **shame** of her"; argued down and declined on two counts: it hands Pace the
  diagnosis (the paragraph is Vee's POV and everything he does in it is
  physical and deniable — naming the shame as the thing he stays in makes him
  demonstrably know it, pre-empting "nothing in how he held her wanted her to
  be any less than she was"), and it abstracts the one place the prose is most
  concrete (the shame is never touchable; what is in his mouth is her body).
  "He did not move past it," one sentence earlier, already states the
  shame-meeting at the right distance and in her voice.
- **`:11` "Like a short story, she told the mirror"** — flagged by two models
  as slightly writerly (claude-opus-4-8: "slightly writerly for Vee's
  interior"; claude-fable-5: "a hair cute") and pardoned by both in the same
  breath, because the text does it first ("a nervous thing to say to an empty
  room, kidding herself"). Two readers noticing and both self-resolving is the
  signature of a line doing its job; opus also called it "a lovely nervous
  line."
- **The repeated *floozie* (`:5`, `:37`, `:49`, `:59`)** — gpt-5.6-sol called
  it "the one moment where I felt the prose pressing a little hard," then
  conceded "the orgasm earns the repetition's payoff when shame fails to close
  her down." No other model seconded it, and the other three named that payoff
  as the chapter's peak. The fixed wording ("quick and flat," twice) is the
  loop's design — an intrusive phrase does not paraphrase itself.
- **`:91` "The evening had used her well"** — two readers noted the bluntness
  and both landed on accepting it (gpt-5.5: "I bought it from inside Vee's
  body… spent without being diminished"; gpt-5.6-sol: "I believe it as her
  bodily satisfaction rather than degradation"). Neither asked for a change.
- **`:77` the scar phrase — Famished is the FIX-AT #4 keeper** (ruled
  2026-08-07). Tactile discovery in the dark; the flat coordinate is what a
  hand reports. `rock`, `above-him`, `we-find-out` vary at their reviews. See
  `audits/line-edit/echo-rulings.md` #4.

## Cut against reader praise — noted so it is not "restored" later

- `:7` **"It was a beautiful color, and that was reason enough."** —
  claude-opus-4-8 quoted "for the color" *and* "reason enough" and read the
  cluster (green set → toenails "without thinking too hard about why") as "her
  one small rebellion grown up into deliberate seduction she won't quite admit

  argued.
