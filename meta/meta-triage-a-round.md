## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **Bare breast against his jaw vs. the bodice built "from the top down"** —
  what's on her is a pin-fitting, not a garment: a single front panel pinned
  at the shoulder covers the front line and leaves the sides open; the contact
  is the side swell past the panel's edge. The height works because the lean
  is hers and already on the page ("she bent into it more than the dress
  asked"), and he is shown rising mid-work. Explicating the geometry would
  certify an accident whose deniability is the scene's engine.
- **"The whole time she'd been dying he hadn't looked down once" vs. his
  earlier glance at her chest** — the temporal clause bounds the claim to the
  crisis stretch (kneeling at her hip, her eyes closed), and the surrounding
  paragraph supplies the evidence (face craned up, waiting for her eyes). Her
  free-indirect relief, not a session-wide narrator claim; the slight
  exorbitance is the emotion.
- **"Went to her head like the wine the color of the cloth" with no wine in
  the scene** — figurative compression, not a missing prop: intoxication like
  wine, and the imagined wine is burgundy like the silk (":110, :242" confirm
  the color). The definite article binds the intoxication to the dress; the
  figurativeness is legible.
- **Outside seam measured hip-to-floor vs. inside seam only to the knee** —
  the asymmetry is load-bearing: the inside-of-thigh measurement is the
  scene's most charged contact, and stopping at the knee is his restraint
  (no further than the method requires). Not trouser seams; endpoints need
  not agree, and normalizing them would spend the restraint beat.
- **Free-floating "Wryly." after the cold-air alibi** — her free-indirect
  tone-tag on her own punctured joke, not a modifier of his turning up the
  heat; the compressed self-commentary is her register. (Nearest-verb
  misparse acknowledged and accepted.)
