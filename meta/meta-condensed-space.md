## Summary

The fall's warm Pace/Randi scene, two chapters after {{Lesson}}'s blowup and the night
before the reconciliation. Vee has asked him for space and he has given it.

Pace is in the shop with his own music on when an engine comes up the gravel that he was
not expecting. Randi is at the door and plainly happy to be there; after the kiss she
laughs and swipes the shavings off his shoulder. She asks for a drink and he brings a dry
white, modestly priced.

**She gives him Vee's morning.** Brooke let her in at nine; Vee had put her face back on
over the top of the night before and done a decent job of it, with her coat buttoned to
the throat *"like she was hiding a body under it."* — *"I saw her from the top of the
stairs and that was the whole story. Oh, honey. Come up."* Vee was a wreck. She cried.
Randi dressed her out of her own wardrobe and loaned her the shoes, and sent her back
toward Pace with counsel. *"That's what a blonde is for."* / *"She knew she'd been wrong
by the time she got to me. She knew it before she sat down."* — and then the glass comes
down against her knee and she works the rim in under her thumbnail: ***"The heart on her,
Pace. She doesn't do anything halfway. Not even being sorry."***

**Pace's side is emotion, not speech.** It lands on him when he hears Vee cried — Vee in
a strange room crying over what she said to him, having asked him to leave her alone and
all day the leaving being the hard part. He is relieved there was somewhere for her to go,
and warmed that the two of them have that between them without him; he is glad she went to
Randi. He looks at the table for a moment because he has to, and when he looks up she is
watching him with the glass stopped halfway. *"I'm glad it was you."*

He gives his account of Thursday: *"We'd had a lovely night to that point. When I went for
drinks, everything was great. By the time I came back, everything was wrong."* Her answer
is *"She'll come. Give her a day."* — which is the counsel she gave Vee that morning
(*he leaves you the room*), laundered into a prediction. She has cleared the Friday for
herself.

**Bed: warm, extended, fully explicit.** She undresses him first, then herself, folds her
clothes onto the table and keeps her hand flat on the surface a moment in a way that is
not how you look at a table. She pulls him up off her before she goes over — *"Not like
that. Not yet. Lie down."* — and takes him from above. Then the two eyewitness gifts, each
delivered mid-act with her body answering as she tells it: **Vee standing in her room that
morning in a black lace bra, hands at her sides, letting Randi look and walk all the way
around her**; and then **Randi kneeling under the long skirt to change her shoes, catching
her when she tipped, a thumb in the soft place behind her knee** — *"She made a sound,
Pace. She made a sound and hoped I didn't hear it."* / *"I heard it."* Her blue toenails
are on the white sheet, placed away from the climax.

**She stays.** The chill takes her and he has the blanket up before she has to reach. She
turns into his side with her hand over his heart and he puts his hand in her hair.
Friday, and Vee will come or she won't. Morning is the ride — {{Hills and Valleys}}.
