## Fixed

- **Echo ruling #3 mis-attribution corrected, then applied.** `:37`'s "the
  small private smile" was listed in the ledger among four occurrences of
  *Pace's* recurring tell. **It is the stats professor.** It therefore cannot
  inherit a protection granted on the grounds that the smile is Pace's — and
  spending a Pace signature on a walk-on is the argument against keeping it.
  Ledger corrected; occurrence moved to FIX-AT and varied here: "He smiled the
  smile of a man who had laid the same trap a hundred times." The open Pace
  question narrows to three (`new-ordinary`, `above-him`, `ignition-scalding`).
- **`:21` — echo ruling #5, "warm weight of" (FIX-AT; this chapter varies).**
  "the whole warm weight of her attention" → "the full warmth of her
  attention". The lamp figure is untouched (PROTECTED under #18, and
  gpt-5.6-terra smiled at it); dropping the `the whole ___ weight` frame also
  breaks the unruled `practice-room` near-twin flagged in #18's sub-note.
- **`:39` — the professor's reversal no longer spent before he delivers it.**
  Cut "the man ahead in each season coming out behind across the two of them",
  which stated the paradox in full twenty-eight lines ahead of `:67`. Vee now
  registers only that the arithmetic misbehaves. Direct answer to the
  three-reader on-the-nose complaint; `:67` was deliberately NOT thinned, since
  it is where the thesis line has to be planted.
- **`:43` — Cassie's not-watching stated once, not twice.** Cut "and nowhere
  near the front of the room". The professor sentence is the keeper and is
  separately protected by the 2026-08-01 triage.
- **`:53` — bed-as-legible-text thinned from three passes to two.** Cut "like
  Cassie had read the bed"; `:5` (reader-praised by claude-opus-4-8, "a lovely
  small image") and `:43` keep the figure. The cut clause sat inside the
  soreness paragraph, the chapter's most purely physical writing and the
  passage two readers named as its peak.
- **"delighted" ×3 for Randi reduced to one — keeper inverted against the
  editor's pick.** The report recommended keeping `:57`; `:51` ("low and
  delighted from the aisle") is quoted verbatim by claude-opus-4-8 and sits in
  the chapter's most-discussed moment, so it is the keeper. `:29` and `:57`
  each already contained the concrete rendering, so both were straight cuts of
  the bare adjective ("like she'd cracked a case", "the problem already solved
  in her hands").
- **`:57` — "Mock-stern then, brooking nothing, loving every inch of it." →
  "Mock-stern then."** One edit resolving two findings: "brooking nothing" was
  the abstract pre-statement of "Don't you dare tell me no" (a line three
  readers flag as double-edged, which requires it arrive undiluted), and
  "loving every inch of it" was the near-twin of `:25`'s "loving every second
  of it" — a swapped noun being more conspicuous than a straight repeat.
- **`:59` — "Something turned over in Vee's chest, grateful and warmed" → "It
  went through her warm".** Three flags in one sentence: the
  `Something-[verb]ed-in-[body part]` frame twinning `:49` ten lines up, the
  doubled abstract modifier, and `chest` (the `in-her-chest` locus tic, 13
  occurrences, cut from `famished:65` on the same grounds). `:49` is the keeper
  — claude-opus-4-8 quotes it ("That's love with no agenda"), gpt-5.5 seconds.
- **`:31` — "started to tell them" said once; the unfound word said once.**
  Second "She started to tell them" → "She told them" (the *started* is carried
  structurally by the professor's interruption at `:33`); the paragraph's
  closing pair collapsed to one clause that keeps the duration and ends on "the
  glass" instead of a slack participial.
- **`:39` — "coming up for air out of" → "coming up for air from".** One word,
  chosen over the editor's recommendation to recast `:41`. **`:41` is
  load-bearing and was deliberately left alone:** its up/down mirror ("came

- **`:71` — "the place that was hers" → "which was hers".** "Place" collided

- **`:7` — "The day went topsy-turvy after that." cut.** Half of a quaint-idiom

  other half — **`:3` "it didn't bother her a whit" — is reader-praised and was

## Left standing — do not re-litigate

- **`:43` "because the friend was the only part of any of it Cassie had ever
  been able to see" — finding declined on overwhelming reader evidence.** The

  experienced as a gloss. It is experienced as the line.

  excited. "Tell me everything" is separately her line at `fed:15` ("Be cruel.
  Tell me everything") — refrain, not accident; logged as an echo candidate for
  the end-of-pass harvest rather than touched here.
- **`:41` the up/down mirror** — see the `:39` entry above. Protected as the
  setup for the chapter's most-quoted realization.
- **`:71` "still shown to not one living soul" — left standing as a
