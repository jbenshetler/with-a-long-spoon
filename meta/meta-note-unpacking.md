## What this scene is

The cool-down and the grounding — Volume Two's first beat back in Vee's own world after {{Forbidden Kiss}} and {{Back}}. The happy reunion with Cassie, both freshly returned (Cassie "not due back till the weekend," `my-friend-randi.md:11` — her duffel half-emptied; **both of them unpacking**, the title's innocuous surface; the second read is the weekend unpacked minus one item). Picks up *after* the "Hmm" — the return itself stays Volume One's closing image and is never re-shown. Vee showered, night clothes and robe, back in her own skin (the wardrobe arc's landing: bare Saturday → bare Sunday → his sweats to leave → **her own robe here** → her own clothes Monday).

## The seen/unseen mechanism (the scene's engine)

Vee *knows* Cassie saw her come in — the coda has her blushing under the one-pass read. What she's too absorbed to compute is **what the outfit told**: coat + his sweats + Randi's black spike heels + **no bag** (canon-explicit — "the coat was the one thing she'd brought," `nothing-underneath.md:109`) = the whole story, arithmetic Cassie has already done. She thinks the outfit said *I stayed over*; it said everything. So Cassie's opener is **the eyebrow in sentence form**, and Vee sails past the irony and answers it earnestly. The comedy is the gap; the affection is that Cassie, holding the whole story, lets the curated version be told and receives it well.

4. **The dry response — aimed at logistics, never the wound.** *"Didn't bring a bag?"* / "No, I forgot." / *"And your clothes?"* / the **eep** — the same eep and the same "forgot to pack a bag" she gave Pace on the couch (`nothing-underneath.md:109`); **the rhyme is free and never pointed at** (the two people who actually see her get the same confession in one weekend). Then Cassie laughs — at Vee, the sanctioned direction (she never laughs at her own jokes; laughs readily at Vee) — and Vee laughs too, and **no explanation follows**. Cassie shows Vee she's been read, then lets her keep the story. The eyebrow at half through "And your clothes?" — pure logistics on the surface, the tell withheld a beat before the laugh confirms it.
5. **Cassie's break.** Vee asks — *"How was your break?"* Wrapping presents; the Christmas cookies (*"pretty, but they'd have been better made out of Play-Doh"*); the brother-in-law's snowshoes worn in the house, the scratched floors (deadpan, zero editorializing — {{Cropped}}'s "a little bit of a dork" proven one month later); still treated like she's in high school (the {{Cropped}} refrain). Then **the mother beat (new canon 2026-08-20, recorded in `meta-arch-cassie.md`):** *"My mother says thank you for the card. She wants to know when she's getting to see you again."* Affection handed through the family channel, nothing asked back — the anti-extractive contrast with Randi **structural, never narrated**. Vee warm in a way she doesn't examine. The mother stays unnamed and off-page; a relay, not a character.
6. **Close small.** A page turning, the weekend put away, ordinary dorm sound. Grounded.
