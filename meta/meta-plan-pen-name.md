## The net of the decision (2026-07-30 discussion)

- **Firewall, not garment.** The name is fully compartmentalized from the
  author — no public linkage, ever, no biographical echoes. For a male author
  under a female erotica byline, "written by a woman" is part of the POV
  promise to this readership; an unmasking reads as betrayal, not reveal.
  Dedicated email under the pen-name domain, never a personal address.
- **Full woman's name over initials.** Given warmth-first positioning and the
  serialization-first launch, a full name does the interiority work on the
  byline and builds parasocial warmth on Literotica/AO3 in a way initials
  (E. L. James / A. N. Roquelaure pattern) don't.
- **Register: warm-approachable, not elegant-literary.** Under the 2026-07
  positioning (`meta-blurb.md`), the name belongs to the acquisition surface —
  its job is to make a *Beauty* reader trust the byline on sight. The literary
  claim is made by the prose after purchase, never pre-announced by the name.
- **Construction rule:** distinctiveness in the first name, spellability in
  the surname; the pair must be search-ownable and say itself aloud
  (word-of-mouth is the retention engine).
- **Resonance:** to the book, not the location — the surname joins the book's
  running water-name vein (private resonance only the author and the book
  know; invisible from outside, which is what a firewall name can afford).
  *Marsh* was rejected: already a character (spring-semester professor).
- **Excluded:** first names Charles, Amy, Roger, Clara, Iris; lead-character
  echoes (Vivienne/Miranda/Pace/Thorne-adjacent — self-insert reading);
  hard-to-spell surnames (Weir, Lachaise-type imports).
- **Collision check (2026-07-30):** only existing "Helen Rivers" author is a
  minor self-published health writer — easily outranked. Adjacency: Francine
  Rivers (large Christian-romance author) surfaces on "Rivers"+romance
  searches; noted, not a confusion risk.
- **Domains (DNS check 2026-07-30):** `helenrivers.com` is taken
  (Cloudflare-fronted). Unregistered at check time: **helenriversbooks.com
  (recommended primary)**, helenriverswrites.com, helenrivers.net/.co/.ink,
  readhelenrivers.com (register as redirect). Canonical email lives on the
  domain (`helen@helenriversbooks.com`), with any private inbox behind it —
  never tied to Gmail-handle availability.

## FSoG crossover strategy

The reader drawn to FSoG but unsatisfied by it is core crossover audience —
the book's strengths (prose, real consent, genuine interiority, psychological
stakes) map one-to-one onto FSoG's documented dissatisfactions, and the
Rice/*Beauty* comp already performs the handshake obliquely (*Beauty* is
FSoG's ancestor). **Capture the crossover through targeting, never through
comparison on the jacket:** (1) naming FSoG imports the romance HEA contract
the closed ending violates; (2) defining the book against FSoG is the
author's thumb on the jacket — let "it's what that book pretended to be" be
discovered by word-of-mouth. FSoG belongs in ad keywords, also-bought
seeding, and interview language — the machinery layer, not the promise layer.
