## Left standing — do not re-litigate (line edit 2026-08-06)

  ("I just filed it away") — the repetition is the thread, not a tic.** The
  report recommended varying one. **Reversed by the author.** Vee's habit is
  to register an anomaly about Pace and decline to conclude from it — the

## Applied, with rationale worth keeping

- **`:19` "A beat." cut** (kept at `:133`, the chapter's best turn). Beyond the
  repetition: the unnarrated sit-then-stand now matches the opening bag
  business, which gpt-5.5 singled out ("exactly the body of someone who has

  echo-rulings #2 has the cousin phrase "a beat past" as FIX-AT book-wide.

- **`:165` "It was about deciding to say it." cut** — the narration named the

  Cassie's "with great delicacy" (`:111`), which stands as the first and
