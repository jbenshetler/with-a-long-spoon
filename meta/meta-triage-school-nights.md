## Soup burn rebuilt (2026-08-12)

The **soup-burn payoff** deferred above (the `:17` clarity TODO) was resolved by
replacing the whole burn beat, with the author in session:

- **Dish + placement.** Soup → a **baked-tomato** dish (breadcrumbs, cheese,
  herbs — consistent with the fra-diavolo / baking-stone canon; lore-keeper
  cleared it). The burn moves from "down her front" to **the side of her breast** —
  a spot only bare skin could take, so the placement itself proves the bareness.
- **The leak is now a gesture, not a line.** Vee no longer *says* "not enough on."
  Her hand drifts unknowingly to the spot as she tells it; **Cassie** reads the
  placement — *"There's only one way that burn happens. Not enough on."* Cassie

  genuinely unwitting on Vee's side.
- **Care, not surveillance.** The `:17` payoff — old "how he'd kept the burn
  watched two days after, asking" — became **"fussed over the burn for days, like
  the small hurt was a big thing."** Author ruling: "watch/watched" read as
  surveillance; the beat is a lover's tender (faintly comic) fussing, and "asked
  after" was rejected as too neighborly. Warm-house close left untouched.
- **Structural relief supersedes the "keep unbroken" ruling for this portion.** The
  2026-08-11 pass had kept the soup anecdote unbroken (the one-breath spill). The
  author found the folded rebuild **muddy — "too many unrelieved words"** — so the
  anecdote body is now **broken into short paragraphs**: the hand-to-the-spot

  he… How he…* triad above is untouched; only the anecdote body was relieved.
- **Untipped double reading preserved** (tenderness AND calibration) — the hard
  protected note still holds: Cassie reads it *wry* (warm ribbing, not the

  puncture. "wry" was chosen over "dry"/"amused" deliberately: "dry" is a near-synonym
  of her later "flat"/"no face on at all"/"Kindly" tags (line-audit finding #11's
  deadpan cluster), so it thickens that pile-up; "wry" is a *different* color that
  suits the line's built-in performance of deduction and diversifies her tags.

  "knowing" register the double-reading keeps submerged.
