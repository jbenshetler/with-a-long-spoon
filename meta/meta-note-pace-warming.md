# Pace interior-warming pass — decision ledger

**Scope:** `the-bench`, `the-pointing-game` (both Pace POV).
**Mandate (author, workshop 2026-08-10):** Pace currently reads too dark — "closer to a serial killer than a skilled lover who wants good things for his partner but is misguided." The problem is his *interior*, not his actions (actions are largely correct). Warm the interior; the harm stays structural and lives only in the **facts** (what he does), never in the **tone**. Reader goal: fall in love with Pace on first read, with only two stones in the shoe — the **information asymmetry** and his **inability to say "I love you."**

**This does NOT change his architecture.** Canonical target (per `meta-thesis.md` / `meta-arch-pace.md`): "a man of unusual virtue whose virtue is the instrument of the harm"; flaw-word **hubris**, not malice; first-read clean, horror is reread-structural.

## Locked global decisions

- **Remove possessiveness / acquisitive register** from his interior. Does not break architecture (the manipulation is in the facts, not ownership tone; possession was already retired book-wide 2026-07-31). Author: fully open to reconsidering earlier interior rulings.
- **Reduce over-confident reads** — readers already find him too sure. But only the **ungrounded** ones (see grounding test). **Keep the body reads** (his real, canonical skill).
- **Grounding test** (author, 2026-08-10): a read stays if Pace has a legitimate basis — **something Randi disclosed** (her mother/egg-whites, Brooke, her food-signals) **OR** something inferable from **established character** (she curates her looks: buffs/polishes/paints/waxes, cheer + dance training, top of a looks-paramount sorority). Cut only reads that are pure narrative omniscience with no such basis.
- **Keep the mistaken triumph.** Pace must still close `the-bench` believing the night went purely well — this is load-bearing dramatic irony. Warm it; strip only the *smug certainty*, not the *wrong contentment*.
- **The single wound = the tutor, named Daphne.** Gift-source AND wound-source (she gave him his cultivation epistemology and made him refuse the vow). **No other wounds** (more wounds = too dark). Seed the name in `the-bench` at the bathroom door; re-touch in a later scene (working name *The Rock* — not yet in the chronology); the vow reveal is held for {{Excavations}}.
- **Cut `the-bench` :99** — "He had begun building it seven months ago. He had met her three weeks ago." Author: nothing requires the plan to predate the woman; the line was added to build dread and works too well. Accepts that the bench now reads as built *for* Randi (and later, for Vee, a bench built-for-Randi rather than pre-existing) — stronger, and Pace can survive the end-of-Vol-2 darkness after 700 pp. of lovable-but-misguided. **Reverses the prior KEEP ruling in `meta-triage-the-bench.md`; log there so it isn't re-flagged as a regression.**
