# Notes — Swim Lanes (craft)

*Companion to `meta-condensed-swim-lanes.md`.*

## What the chapter is for

The second rung of the fall Pace/Randi ladder — {{Tannin}}'s shape, escalated: full sex
with no cut, a plainer admission, and the first fracture. It also sets the three
structural facts the later vignettes work against: the by-inches descent, the afterglow
she keeps for herself, and that she always stays.

## Guards

- **Not being fed is the point.** He is hungry before she arrives, offers to cook, is
  refused, and eats alone and insufficiently at the end. The contrast is with
  {{Famished}} — do not resolve his hunger here, and do not let him cook for her.
- **The empty chair and the leaving are a matched pair he cannot assemble.** He notices
  each and sets each aside; the reader puts them together. Never let the narration join
  them for him.
- **He reads the want fully and gladly, and misses the jealousy and the hurt.** The three
  carriers are the **thin laugh**, the **hard clench with her eyes shut**, and the
  **skipped afterglow**. All three are rendered and none is explained.
- ***"I want us"* stays unglossed.** No narration telling the reader what it means, and no
  follow-up from Pace. It is torn out of her and then the scene moves.
- **The question she asked is never answered** (*"How was it?"*), and nobody notices but
  the reader. Keep the one-line observation and no more.
- **Never inside Randi** — surface only (`meta-craft-randi.md`, Console rules).
- **Her leaving must not read as coldness.** She kisses him thoroughly twice and says
  *"See you"* warm and easy. The hurt is in the departure itself, not in any chill toward
  him; the warmth is what makes it unreadable to him.
- **The chill after is hers, never Vee's** (`meta-plan-pace-house.md`, Atmosphere). Here
  she takes the blanket herself rather than being covered — the inverse of {{Space}} and
  {{Four}}, where he gets it before she reaches.
- **Body canon in play:** the freckles across her chest, the cocoa-brown nipples gone tight
  and dark (*cocoa* used once, at the anchor look — `meta-craft-randi.md`).
- **He dressed for her and put her music on.** Small, and it is the measure of what the
  refusal costs: the house was arranged for a woman who then would not stay.
