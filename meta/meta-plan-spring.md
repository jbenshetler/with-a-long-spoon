# Spring — Plan (Volume Two frame)

*The arc and engine for Volume Two (spring 2027 — the middle act: the apparatus at full extraction, the bloom before the souring). Companion to `meta-plan-chronology.md` (which owns the spring scene order and dates — this frame carries none), `meta-note-taste-thread.md` (the self-taste staircase), `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` (the Randi track), `meta-note-grace.md` (the pincer), `meta-note-unpacking.md` / `meta-arch-cassie.md` (the Cassie line), and `meta-plan-summer.md` (the act this volume hands off to). The scenes arise in drafting; here is the arc they sit in. Volume title:* ***A Warm Reception*** *(decided; `meta-blurb.md`).*

## Where the volume sits — the three-rung ladder

Volume Two is the middle rung of one arc, not a self-contained story:

- **Fall / *A Polite Invitation* — the body.** Vee claims her body, through Pace's acceptance.
- **Spring / *A Warm Reception* — the appetite.** Vee accepts her *wanting* — the taste, and the attraction to women — through Randi's reframe and Pace's engineering. **Feeling, not acting.** The bloom is real, and it is being grown for the cutting.
- **Summer — the act.** Vee decides to consummate: the Agreement, the threesome, the reveal (`meta-plan-summer.md`).

The load-bearing boundary is **Spring / Summer = attraction / consummation.** Feeling sexually drawn to women (spring) is a different gate from deciding to have a woman (summer). Spring carries Vee all the way to accepting the want without her having chosen the deed — which is why the Agreement belongs at the **top of Volume Three**, not in this curtain.

## The dramatic question

*Can Vee come home to her own appetite — including a want she'd refused to name — and is that homecoming hers, or grown in her for the cutting?*

Both readings must hold at once. First read: Randi and Pace **help** her arrive at a truer, larger desire — a genuine flowering, continuous with the fall's body-claiming. Reread: *help* is *steer* — the want she "accepts" is the one being cultivated in her for the harvest (the thesis already says it of {{Secret Plans — The Ask}}: her authorship and the machinery that runs her are the same act). Spring's growth is **more compromised than the fall's**, not merely larger: two helpers now, and one is the trap.

## Shape — two axes, one dip (Randi's)

Volume Two runs its two relationship axes on **different curves**, and the volume's mid-turn belongs to only one of them:

- **The Pace axis — unbroken ascent, no dip.** {{Grace}} (fullest flower; the key; the pincer closes — below) → {{First Taste}} (the crossing — the volume's dramatic act) → {{Vee on the Bench}} (the curtain, the last full bloom). The Vee–Pace relationship only deepens across the volume. It is the continuous warmth the curtain lands on — and the stable ground the Randi dip registers against. **We identified no Pace dip.**
- **The Randi axis — bloom → dip → higher bloom.** The attraction / SATC climb → the **crisis** (jealousy — the one rupture) → repair-and-escalate *past* where it stood. The volume's dramatic turn lives **entirely here**: the dip is Randi's, on the axis that is secretly the trap.

So the volume is not a single bloom-dip-bloom curve — it is a monotonic Pace bloom with a Randi dip folded inside it. The crisis earns the volume its turn without cooling the love or the heat, so the warm curtain survives.

## The three staircases — the backbone

Three parallel climbs run the volume, each with a named engine. Two extract; the third makes the extraction legible.

**1. Pace / the self-taste (kink) climb.** *Engine: trust + cultivated hunger + endorphins sublimating the withheld word.* The vow means Pace never says it; Vee stopped *demanding* the word in the fall ("demand dead, hope alive"), but the **need** went underground into the body — so the kink escalates because the endorphin hit is the nearest thing to the declaration she isn't getting. The long spoon feeds her everything but the word, and the hunger for the word drives her onto the next rung. Gates (owned by `meta-note-taste-thread.md`): {{Missed a Spot}} (the Refusal) → {{The Reach}} → *Whiskey and Wine* → *The Almost* → {{First Taste}} (the crossing). **{{First Taste}} moves into spring** from its former summer slot as the volume's dramatic act — the whole self-taste staircase now opens and closes inside Volume Two.

**2. Randi / the attraction (SATC) climb.** *Engine: attraction + trust — both real, both farmed.* The goodbye-kiss staircase climbs; the debriefs grow loaded; {{A Clean Plate}} reframes the taste ("not gay — just yours") and {{A Threesome, With You}} converts *a woman* into *a threesome, with you* (Vee's move); {{Boyfriend}} probes and Vee names Randi. (The scrunchie campaign, the Social Price vignettes, {{In Her Place}}, and the found-hair trace run alongside — owned by `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` and the chronology.) Running underneath, the **masturbation staircase** goes explicitly physical — *bathroom (to completion) → show-me-your-hands → under the tablecloth, Randi presiding* — powered by the Telling loophole (below): Randi's voice reaching into Vee's body across a room. The *show-me-your-hands* rung touches the self-taste staircase at the fingertips — Randi inspecting the evidence Vee won't put in her own mouth.

**3. Cassie / Vee's withholding climb.** *Engine: the anti-extractive mirror.* Cassie gives affection through the family channel and asks nothing back (`meta-note-unpacking.md`, `meta-arch-cassie.md`); Vee gives Cassie the **curated cut** while pouring the raw feed into Randi. The reader's ache is the **misallocation**: the friendship that asks nothing gets the least of Vee's true self; the two that extract get all of it. The mechanism is the **container** (`meta-arch-cassie.md`, The Hallmark Register): asking is how the container for the untellable gets made — Randi manufactures one (the extraction machine *is* one), the dorm floor has a funny-sexy one, and Cassie, who never asks, has none; the withholding is translation failure, not choice, and its escalation is the register gap widening toward silence, never Vee lying better. Cassie is the **control variable** that makes Randi's extraction visible — and the **breath** between the escalation rungs (the cold readers named the breathing as load-bearing). She stays **present and warm all spring** — *not* displaced, *not* isolated; the cost is the widening thing Vee can't say, never distance. Vee's withholding opens on the first Vol 2 Cassie beat ({{Unpacking}}: {{Nothing Underneath}} and the {{Missed a Spot}} kiss edited out) — the first rung of the **three-night disclosure gradient** (Cassie's hole → the girls' flash at {{Across}} → Randi's full coat debrief at {{Covering}}; the same event sized to each listener, the fullest cut to the most dangerous one, the calendar doing the narration — mechanism in `meta-note-across.md`) — and **resolves in the Volume Three coda** (the Cassie Scene — Vee finally tells her everything). Cassie departs warm at the **spring / summer seam** (the nursing internship) — the goodbye that breathes to the edge and then arms summer's isolation (`meta-plan-summer.md`).

## The pincer — {{Grace}}

{{Grace}} (the cabin trip — `meta-note-grace.md`) is not only the Vee–Pace peak; it is the **hinge where Pace's rule becomes Randi's weapon.** In one weekend Pace gives Vee two gifts that are both traps:

- **The key** — a *house* key (*"You're more than a guest in my house. I've never given a key to anyone."* — *house*, not *home*, the one withheld syllable). It converts Vee from expected-guest to **resident**; it is his object-answer in place of the word — and it hands her free entry to the set where she is being orchestrated. It also makes the spring dinner unrefusable (below).
- **The Telling rule** — *tell me when you touch yourself thinking of me* — with a **loophole**: post-brunch relief reclassified as *the exposure* (caught wanting by Randi at the table), not thoughts of Pace, so it needs no telling. The loophole **routes Randi's gaze into Vee's solitude, license-free**, and is the engine under the masturbation staircase; the *under-the-tablecloth* rung is the loophole's language made literal.

The key makes her resident; the rule's loophole makes her Randi's. Both are given in love; both are the apparatus.

## The midpoint and the crisis

**A contained crisis — Randi–Vee axis, jealousy, resolving upward.** It is the theme detonating, not an external obstacle: Vee's attraction, unowned, surfaces as jealousy she has no frame for (*"I'm not gay — so why does this gut me?"*), and the crisis is what forces the question she has been dodging.

Working model — **two jaws** (exact configuration still weighing, below):

- **Vee's jaw — displaced jealousy.** Pointed *away from Pace* (Vee cannot know the Randi–Pace history) — at Randi's other attentions. The **instigator is separation**: over Spring Break the two are apart (they do *not* travel together), and the wrongness of how much Vee misses Randi — sharpened by Randi's **topless sunbathing photos** (the photo thread reversed: Vee→Randi in Vol 1 and after {{Still Life}} becomes Randi→Vee) — primes her jaw. Vee, staying with Pace, receives the photo lying naked beside him, and **ultimately shows it to him** → a displaced-sex beat, this time with **more honesty to Pace**: they have sex while she thinks, specifically, of **Randi's breasts** (his kink fed by her honesty; the attraction going semi-conscious, semi-shared). The breasts as the cognitive object mirror {{Me Too}}'s Pace-guided breast-fantasy, reversed onto Vee — **the desire made mutual** (sub-beats author-affirmed 2026-08-29).
- **Randi's jaw — submerged jealousy.** Her envy crystallizes at the **spring dinner** (below): she walks, spatially, through the domestic bliss she engineered and structurally cannot inhabit — the needer who cannot need, in the home of the woman who blooms where she broke. Aimed cleanest at **Cassie** (the anti-extractive friendship she can neither farm nor replicate). This is also the volume's one **humanizing aperture on Randi** — the Vol-2 counterpart to {{Fairytale}}: the reader glimpses that her coldness is need she can't show, not indifference.

Working sequence: {{Grace}} (key + Telling) → {{Cabin Debrief Brunch}} → Spring Break (the photo; Vee's jaw primed) → reconvene → the spring dinner (the flashpoint) → the crisis ignites → repair-and-climb. **Guardrail:** the crisis *repairs* (Vol 3's reveal is the rupture that cannot); the repair escalates the SATC track *past* where it stood, resolving into the bloom, not away from it — so the warm curtain survives, and watching this rupture mend makes the final unmendable one hit harder.

## The dinner — the spring counterpart to `served`

The spring dinner is Vee hosting Randi in her domestic bliss at Pace's house — a **spring counterpart to the summer dinner** (`served`, currently Volume Three, post-Agreement). The two **bracket the Agreement**: spring plants Randi's envy (pre-Agreement); summer is the bomb with dinner service (the signed third at the table). No second tour is needed in summer — Randi is *already at home* in those rooms because we watched her catalogue them in spring.

The spring dinner runs as a **duel in a language Vee can't hear**:

- **Vee** — proud host (and, post-break, privately churning about the woman across the table).
- **Randi** — narrating Vee's intimate life back to her, room by room, from **two knowledge-sources**: what Vee overshared in SATC (the sunroom — *"this must be where you fit the dress"* — {{A Round}}; the kitchen) and her **own body's memory** (the bedroom — the *"unusual proportions"* of the table that is the **bench** she broke on: `the-bench.md`, `meta-plan-pace-house.md`). She **baits Pace**, walking the line with things she knows Vee told her and suspects Pace does not know Vee told her — a live demonstration of her pipeline's bandwidth.
- **Pace** — present and cooking (Randi's presence in the house is routine to him after eighteen months; the charge is *both his lovers at one table, only one of the two knowing the truth*), holding his face while recomputing what Randi knows and how.
- **The key / keyless irony:** Vee holds the key (resident); Randi has come to this house keyless, bolting the door behind her, longer than Vee has known it exists. Vee plays hostess on turf Randi secretly owns.

The bench itself carries a four-beat object-arc: {{The Bench}} (Randi breaks) → {{The New Ordinary}} (Vee throws a thong on it, not knowing) → the spring dinner (Randi returns as guest, names it) → {{Vee on the Bench}} (Vee blooms on it).

## The curtain

{{First Taste}} (the crossing — the dramatic act), then **{{Vee on the Bench}}** (the curtain — the last full bloom; multi-orgasmic where Randi was wrung once; she blooms where Randi broke; the bench brackets the volume). The volume lands **warm**, the taboo just quietly crossed, the **threesome raised but not agreed** — the one door left open across the seam. Volume Three opens on the yes.

## Guards

- **Earn the bloom.** Dig Vee in deep — the social bloom is load-bearing so the spring / summer escalation reads as believable, not folly.
- **Attraction, not consummation.** Spring renders the *wanting* owned; the deed is summer's. Don't let "more honesty to Pace" drift toward anything that would make Vee ask how Randi and Pace really know each other.
- **Cassie stays warm.** The cost is the withholding, never coldness; her breathing is structural, not decoration.
- **Randi's aperture stays narrow.** The crisis cracks her once (need she cannot show) — it does not convert her into a girlfriend or explain her on the page.
