# Condensed — Among Friends

*SCENE · Architecture complete, prose not drafted · ~late January (a mid-week night soon after First Weekend Back) · an old roadhouse outside town, Pace's choice · Vee POV · warm/social register · [craft](meta-note-among-friends.md)*

The first of the paired world-openings: Pace enters **Vee's** world here; {{Another Round}} reciprocates (Vee into his). It is his deed-answer to the *{{Under the Rug}}* visibility wound — *will we ever be seen in public?* — given in the grammar he lives by (deed, not word): he comes to a gathering of her friends **without being asked**, in his bomber jacket and broadcloth, and is seen with her. Volume Two's warm opener — the bloom before the souring. Warm and fun on first read; the cold is the reader's, on reread. (Title provisional; author may rename.)

### The engine — being seen
The visibility wound answered in deed: he lets himself be claimed publicly. But only *her* world opens — his stays sealed (the gym goes wrong at `The Burn`; his *people* wait for {{Another Round}}). He answers the "seen in public" axis while the enclosure of his own life holds; the answer soothes the wound and deepens the cage at once.

### Panel one — the kitchen (the bridge)
The outing is planned in **Pace's kitchen**, after dinner — Vee at the table, Pace cleaning up (role-trope inversion): Vee finishes a Randi story and Pace times *"I want to meet your people"* onto it (she hears the specific in the generic; he knows she'll offer the Randi dinner — elicits the dangerous offer so he can bury it); Vee offers **Randi** first (the two-on-one he can't afford; he never declines, only adds); **"Bring Cassie, too"**; Vee hems (Cassie *would* come, for Vee — but no restaurant she can picture holds both Cassie and Randi at dinner; the `dear` own-friends thought resurfacing in reverse, the connector over the launderer); his name-drops (Meg, "Kayla, the one with the—?") land the swoon, and the **group catches fire as Vee's own idea**. Chili's remembered (the event, never the diagnosis) → *"you pick the place"* → **"Let me handle it"** (deed, not word; name and location handed over off-page). Every escalation his, every decision hers. Full machine: craft note.

### The room
Vee assembles an unlikely mix — 2–3 of her **dorm** friends (seed: flag 21) + **Randi** (crossing status lines for Vee, as she does) + **Pace**. The mix exists only because Vee connects worlds no one else would — a render of her aliveness (Vivienne), and the mechanism that unknowingly seats her boyfriend and her orchestrator in one room. Cassie present.

### Pace working the room
Genuine, warm, easy — the joke-setter who sets up the lines and lets others land them, makes himself useful, defers (canon charm mechanism). **Venue guard:** the roadhouse is ground Pace can hold — old, outside town, neutral-to-students, no TVs, C&W on the speakers, mid-week, tables reserved, restaurant-not-bar — chosen by *him* off Vee's "you pick" (she learned from the Chili's wreck; neither names why). His **register, not his world**: nobody there knows him ({{Another Round}} owns the world-opening). No spec inventory at arrival — let one or two land via the friends' reactions. Don't write him effortlessly working a loud chaotic room — that contradicts the un-named temperament; keep the format doable or let the cost show.

### The night's shape (staging)
Weathered-colorless roadhouse, too-warm pine inside (matte-scrubbed, fifty years), tile floor; **three round reserved tables; hot buffet, no waitress** — the format circulates the room, and **Pace floats between tables** as host logistics (his generosity in motion; intro-with-a-handle; the venue's quiet restores his ears — the acoustic dividend, never named; drain-not-strain, edges only). **The introduction ("Vee's boyfriend") is the night's first social interaction**; the **Cassie exchange** (the witness won honestly — she arrived charmed by Vee's stories; plain register; glimpsed across the room); the twinge mid-evening; then the party thins on weeknight logic (Cassie out with the dorm cluster) and the **triad contracts to one table** for the late beat: **the compliment through Randi** — Pace sets it up (how Vee lights up a room) and steps back; Randi toasts *"To my beautiful best friend."* (no "love" — ruled; the lovers' duet aimed at Vee, the threesome's give-through-Randi architecture in miniature); Randi quiet, glassy-eyed, turning her glass; Vee's generous misread (*so happy for me*). **The goodbye:** the hug-and-kiss where Pace can see, *"Love you, gorgeous."* — Vee's feather-light wondering what Pace thinks (plant → {{Vee Tells Pace About the Kiss}}). Full staging: craft note.

### Randi present — the concealment (met once)
Pace and Randi hold the meet-cute line: met once at the coffee shop, nothing more. Concealment is **uniformity, not distance** — he charms Randi at room temperature; the absent thing is *desire*, not warmth. Cassie does not re-clock the no-attraction. This is the one social scene where **Pace is the operator and Randi is the audience**, watching him work a room the way she works Vee alone (loaded on reread; invisible on first). Low reveal-cost: Vee already banked the meet-cute, so nothing new to un-know.

### "They share an interest"
Vee later asks what he and Randi talked about; his light **"they share an interest"** (= Vee) is the scene's blade — real AND instrumental in one line: his love he can't declare AND the threesome he still wants. He knows exactly what he means; delivered warm, no smirk. She has no frame (the threesome plant is ~a week off, at {{Vee Tells Pace About the Kiss}}), so she hears only that her two people adore her; on reread it pre-seeds the threesome. Her question springs from hope, not suspicion.

### The bomber jacket (the claim, unasked)
Pace's own bomber jacket + broadcloth (bible wardrobe). He comes into her world **without being asked**; Vee gets the **proprietary feeling she hasn't earned the right to** — to present him as hers, publicly — while he still won't **name** her. The public claim is the named status she couldn't get verbally; continues the shirt-scenting / Top Drawer clothing-as-proxy staircase.

### Guards
- **Earn the dark by being light:** a warm, fun party where a girlfriend's two favorite people get along. Cut any signal that Randi is sinister or that the two already know each other beyond the meet-cute.
- **Real AND instrumental:** the "shared interest" line is genuine affection and live plan at once; never a knowing wink at the concealment.
- **Never name, never explain:** the temperament (venue), the threesome pre-seed, the enclosure-deepening — all resonance.
- **Continuity:** dorm friends unaffiliated (flag 21); Pace/Randi met-once + Cassie no re-clock (flag 22); precedes the threesome plant.
