Rulings and status are recorded here as each chapter is done; the prose edits live on the branch until merged.*

## The test (author ruling 2026-09-28)

**Processing is different from retelling.** Vee working out what an event meant — *it was like his eyes asked, May I look*; *like I was supposed to be there*; the heat turned up hours before — is processing and stays. The listener's reaction, probe, reframe, or refusal is reception and stays. **Retelling** is the prose re-narrating the mechanics of an event the reader has already seen (the sequence, the procedure, the inventory). That is what gets cut or reduced to a sentence, unless a reception beat depends on the reader hearing it again in Vee's voice.

Standing discipline this pass applies: `meta-arch-cassie.md` §The Retell Chapters (elide the recount, keep the reception; one unheeded true sentence; vary the shape; distance) and `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` §The Cassie/Randi Sort (the same event told to both confidantes is SORTED, never duplicated).

## Dependencies a cut must not break

- {{Toenails}} names *frosted plum* before {{Fed}} asks its name; {{Fed}} ties it to the silk. Cut the dinner list in {{Fed}}, never in {{Toenails}}.
- {{Off Six-Fourteen}} *I did it on purpose. Me.* / *a few days* ↔ {{Dear}} *Because of him* / *Two days*. Both sides stay.
- {{Dear}} *May I choose* gloss → {{Turned Up}} *May I look* → the asking motif.
- {{Rye}} *he just looked* ↔ {{Turned Up}} *I locked eyes with him. And then I let him look.* Both stay.
- {{Turned Up}} "he made one" (the shirt) is the reveal; {{Rye}} *I kept the shirt* is its setup.
- {{The Practice Room}} Randi's counting script → {{One Bite}} *It wasn't quite like you said*; *love tap* → *the little love tap*.
- {{School Nights}} *fine* → {{All the Time}} *calling it fine*.
- Kiss shape (hand at the hinge, second press) and *See you soon, gorgeous*: designed escalation across {{How It's Done}}, {{Fed}}, {{Broken In}}, {{The Practice Room}}, {{One Bite}}, {{All the Time}}. Not repetition to cut.
- Randi's scent unnamed ({{How It's Done}}, {{One Bite}}) → *bitter orange* ({{Broken In}}, {{All the Time}}).
