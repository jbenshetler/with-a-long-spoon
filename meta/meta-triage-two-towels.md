## Flagged, unresolved — architecture, not craft

- **Pace's stacked competencies.** The strongest convergent reader signal in
  the four cold reads: two of four models independently named the same
  friction and located it at the same place — the dress reveal.

  > gpt-5.5: *"The only moment where I felt a tiny authorial glow on the scale
  > was how many extraordinary competencies stacked up at once — doctorate,
  > powerlifting, cooking, furniture, patent, sewing."*

  > gpt-5.6-sol: *"My one point of friction is how comprehensively ideal Pace
  > becomes. He is a mathematician, powerlifter, cook, furniture maker,
  > tailor, patent holder, and apparently compassionate medical innovator… by
  > the dress reveal I am close to feeling the author's thumb pressing hard."*

  Not answerable with a cut — the list *is* the chapter's structure; the tour
  is the competence inventory. The open question is whether "too good to be
  true" is landing as **ominous** (design working) or as **wish-fulfilment**
  (calibration miss); both readers reported the latter.

  **Author's assessment (2026-08-06): all of them are load-bearing.** The
  patent and the medical application look the most disposable and are really
  the same item — but cutting them removes the explanation for the income,
  which the house, the workshop, and the silk all depend on. No change made;
  recorded so the next pass does not rediscover the same dead end. Re-test at
  the **end-of-Volume-One cold-read rerun** — whether this friction moves is
  the evidence worth having.
