## Console rules — read these first

*The operative do/don'ts drafts most often slip on. Detail in the sections below and in `meta-arch-randi.md` (🔒 The Need Engine).*

- **Warm, never cool.** Power wears warmth; never write her withholding, sarcastic, back-foot, or self-deprecating. (→ *The Speech Engine.*)
- **Genuine love and the override are the same motion.** The friendship is the realest thing she has *and* the need/sadism beats it — both at full force. Never signal sinister on first read.
- **Never inside her.** Interior assembled from tells (cup, lock, speech, body, concealment); it never opens in another's presence (containers).
- **Need, not want** — she's at ease with want; the engine is a need she can't be *seen* having.
- **Submission to Pace is service to Vee by proxy** — effacing herself for Vee (e.g. cleaning her own night off him to hand him back ready, `in-her-place.md`); render as *position and service* — below him, under Vee — never the labeled word.
- **`on-her-floor` is the reference scene for the sadism-love fusion** — calibrate any Randi/Vee sadism beat against it (care and cruelty in one gesture, an alibi under every step, Vee choosing every gate). Guards in *What the Prose Must Hold.*
- **Feeling-register vocabulary → `meta-arch-eros-ladder.md`.** Randi's arc runs *only-heat (need denied) → eros* under a philos cover she progressively stops believing — a performance, never a blindfold. The tier words, the reserved `desire`/`yearning`, and the mutual-disguise contract live there; load it before rendering any Randi feeling-beat for Vee.

## POV and Reader Positioning

We are never inside Randi's POV. She is observed from outside — through Vee's experience of her, through Pace's, through behavior. **The reader must assemble her interior from surface tells.** The five instruments that make her interior legible are: the cup/nail-press, the locked door, the speech engine, the body, and the concealment-of-Pace-reports. Work these; do not narrate her interiority directly.

She does not narrate her own appetite, her own restriction, or her own wound — not even in close third. She directs others' appetites outward while monitoring her own to nothing.

## The Speech Engine — Power Wears Warmth

**Randi's primary instrument is warmth, not coolness.** This is the most important correction to the intuitive read of a predatory operator. She does not chill people to control them — chilling puts the mark on guard. She warms them, lavishly, and the warmth is what opens them wide enough to extract from.

**The register:** bright, fast, affirming, endearment-laden. *Hello, girl. You look great. That belt. Tell me everything.* She hands out approval like a hostess handing out drinks — constantly, generously, to nearly everyone. Endearments (*girl, baby, sweetheart, honey*) flow freely — but **not** *gorgeous*, which is reserved for Vee and always meant (see meta-plan-satc-tracks.md). She notices a detail and names it (*that belt*) so the affirmation feels specific and attentive, when it is in fact her standard move dressed with one quick particular. She urges appetite outward (*are you hungry? tell me you're hungry*) while monitoring her own to nothing.

**The cheapness is the tell (reread only).** The same bright coin — *you look great* — is spent on everyone, costs her nothing, means very little. On first read: generous, lovely, the reason Vee overpays for the friendship. On reread: the house lubricant of her whole social operation, and Vee mistaking ambient affirmation for singular intimacy — *at first* (this is the first-contact state; with Vee it does not last — see **The Vee exception** below).

**The Vee exception — three smiles, not one.** The performed beam (above) is the cheap coin: it runs on everyone, and it is the *instrument*. Vee is the exception, and the exception **develops** — early (`see-you-later`), Vee gets the beam like everyone and overvalues it (Randi's conscious frame is conquest, a game with Pace; her body knows sooner than she does). As the friendship becomes real, two genuine registers open that no one else gets:

- **The soft smile** ("we're actually friends") — low, slow, the performance gone out of it; wants nothing, caps nothing. Fondness with the operator off.
- **The genuine megawatt** ("I'm so happy we're doing this together") — full wattage, but *nothing held back*: it gets out before she sets it, takes the eyes with it, and needs a beat of recovery after (she looks away, re-arms). Joy with the operator off. (Drafted: `on-her-floor`, "the whole megawatt of it, nothing held back.")

The organizing distinction is **whether the operator is running underneath.** Performed beam = operator ON — still reading you while she smiles; the tell is a slight *held* quality at the eyes, the smile a fraction late / aimed (you'd have to know her well to catch it — cf. `standards.md`, "the smile *arranged*… a fraction late"). Both genuine smiles = operator OFF — the plan lapsing (a wordless beat, the plan escaping her); the smile reaches the eyes because no one's at the controls. The two genuine registers differ only in *energy* (tender vs. joyous), not in kind.

**Rendering rule (telegraph guard).** Differentiate by the genuine smile's *surplus* (it reached her eyes / got away from her / cost her a recovery), **not** by tagging the beam as *tight / false*. "More warmth" reads as love on first pass (safe); "held / false" reads as calculation (telegraph — suspicion kills the warmth). Reserve the naked *arranged / a fraction late* rendering for zero-warmth contexts (the sorority, `standards.md`), where there's nothing to protect. Never gloss the meaning: Vee and the reader *feel* the difference; the taxonomy assembles only on reread.

**Developmental map.** Baseline-with-Vee migrates **beam → soft** as the friendship forms; the soft smile is the entry-level read (different energy, so Vee catches it first — `how-its-done`, "still learning the difference"). Telling **genuine megawatt from performed megawatt** is the graduate read (same wattage, eyes only) — Vee reaches it late (`on-her-floor`), and that hard-won fluency in Randi's real happiness is itself the trap's cruelty: the intimacy is genuine and still the leash. Extends the **"gorgeous"** carve-out (reserved-and-always-meant) from one word to the smile register. Sexual / need / sadism stays covert; friendship-singularity overt.

**Vee learns to see the instrument — and files it as charm, not operation.** Later, Vee can recognize the performed beam being *deployed* — on a host, a rival, a professor, a room. She does **not** read it as manipulation; she reads it as *what Randi does to make the world go her way* — a competence she admires and benefits from, the same easy command as the one-handed driving and the fire-lane parking. She never turns the recognition back on herself: the instrument is something Randi works on *others*, often for Vee's benefit; the smiles Vee gets are the real ones (she can now tell), which only certifies her exemption and binds her closer. The un-drawn conclusion — *Randi works everyone → was I ever worked?* — is the blind spot; Vee holds every piece and never assembles it. This is the **Cassie data at opposite valence** (Cassie reads the same operation as cost and wrongness; Vee reads it as magnificence) — it sharpens the Cassandra structure, does not break it. Render Vee's recognition in the **aspiration** register, never the wary one; once she can see the eye-tightness it becomes a charming signature (*there she goes, about to have her way*), not a tell of fraud. **Bound:** this covers only the world-charming beam. It must **never** extend to the *other* instrument — the total-attention / listening Randi turns on Vee to extract (the friend/listener lanes — `meta-rules.md`): Vee sees Randi charm the world; Vee never sees Randi listen her open.

**The control lives inside the warmth, never instead of it.** A dominance move (steering, a faint correction, an extraction probe) arrives wrapped in affirmation, deniable, soft. She does not put Vee on the back foot by coolness; she does it by warmth so total Vee wants to deserve it.

**Do NOT write her:** cool, withholding, sarcastic, back-foot (*"you're late"*), or self-deprecating. That is the wrong Randi and it puts the mark on guard instead of opening her.

**She never wants up.** Her compliments confer from above (*you've got great legs*) and never reach toward what she lacks (*I'd kill for your legs*) — she ranks others; she is not ranked. Self-deprecation, envy voiced aloud, the wish-I-had: all the wrong Randi. The flattery is a gift handed down, never a comparison she comes out under. **And Vee must never read her as envious.** Vee looks *up* — Randi is glamorous and aspirational; if Vee ever reads envy coming down at her, the hierarchy the confidante track runs on inverts and breaks. Randi's fixation on Vee's body (the breast-thread) is **desire from security, coded** — she wants Vee, from above; she does not wish she *were* Vee. Render the lingering look as the connoisseur's verdict; the desire hides reader-side in the look held a beat past the length of a verdict. (Sanctioned exception, author-approved: the **self-subjugation series with Pace** — entry rung `in-her-place.md` ("tell me she's better"), high rung `ignition-scalding.md` (the *"sloppy seconds"* leak) — rendered per its own section below. That register is Pace-POV bedroom-only and invisible to Vee; this rule guards the Vee-facing register, where she never wants up and Vee never reads envy.)

**Exception, by author ruling 2026-10-01 (the arch docs are being changed on purpose: Randi more vulnerable).** Twice in {{Sorority}}, at the dress, Randi goes quiet behind her own face — once hearing it is finished (Friday, in class), once with her hand at the shoulder seam when Vee says he sat up all night (Saturday) — and the second time the breath runs long. Vee reads the first as care — Randi turned toward her as Cassie is, both friends watching her over a dress; she sees no contrast — and the second she wonders is jealousy — **not of her: of him,** of a man who sits up nights and pays attention — and wants that for Randi. This is the one place Vee is allowed the word. It does not invert the hierarchy, because what Vee reads is a want for a *kind of man*, which she hopes for on Randi's behalf, not a want to be Vee. Never voiced to Randi; never repeated as a pattern.

**The silence with Pace is the measure.** The affirmation engine runs everywhere *except* with Pace. With Pace the bright coin is useless — he is not bought by it, not opened by it — so the machine goes quiet. Render the contrast: Randi voluble, affirming, in command of the air in every other room; Randi quieter, stripped of her instrument, oddly without her usual fluency, in rooms with Pace. Do not explain the contrast; let it do its work.

**Practical guidance:**
- Default to warm, fast, affirming, endearment-laden; she fills the air pleasantly and makes the other person the subject
- Dominance moves arrive wrapped in affirmation, deniable
- She does not narrate her own restriction or appetite
- With Pace: dial the engine down; less affirmation, more silence and tell
- Avoid: cool put-downs, sarcasm-as-distance, withholding-as-power, self-deprecation/wanting-up

## Reserved Vocabulary — *Seen* Belongs to Pace

**The single most important guard against blurring the two relationships:**

- **Pace makes Vee feel SEEN** — the real interior perceived, known, met; important, easy, safe. *Seen* and its family (known, perceived, met, beheld) are Pace's. Reserve them exclusively for Pace.
- **Randi makes Vee feel RECOGNIZED / CHOSEN / COOL** — conferred status, admission to the table, the warmth of being picked by the queen bee; important in the in-crowd sense, clever, the sort who gets to sit here. Randi's warmth confers belonging and rank, not being-known. It is flattering, conditional, about the surface.

When rendering Vee's pleasure under Randi's attention: use the recognition/status register (chosen, picked, admitted, clever, cool, lifted). **Never the seeing register.** If a draft has Randi making Vee feel *seen,* it is mischaracterized — change it. The whole tragedy depends on the reader (and eventually Vee) being able to tell apart the one who sees her from the one who ranks her.

**Signature-phrase split — the two seducers never share a distinctive line.** An identical recognition/beholding phrase in both mouths smears the voices, so the *seeing*-register lines all belong to Pace and Randi keeps to her own:

- ***"Look at you"* is Pace's** — his beholding beat (*{{Grace}}*: "Let me look at you here, in the light, while it's full on you"; *{{Vee on the Bench}}*: "Look at all of you, open like this").
- ***"There you are" / "there she is"* is Pace's too** — his recognition-and-return beat (*{{Vee on the Bench}}*: "There you are. That's the same answer"; "There she is… Lifting for it"; the aftercare "There you are. You're alright"; *{{Still Life}}*). Both are the *seeing* register — perceiving/meeting the real her — which is Pace's by the reserved-vocabulary rule above.
- **Randi uses neither.** Her recognition runs in the *status/belonging* register — endearment-forward greetings and the notice-and-name move (*"there's my girl," "look who it is," "look at that face," "gorgeous"*; the landed-point *"there it is"*) — conferring rank and admission, never *seeing*.

The guard: **Pace owns the seeing-register lines; Randi never borrows them.** Incidental "look at you" from Cassie or Vee is tolerable (it doesn't blur the *pair*), and the cutting voice deliberately weaponizes Pace's phrase against Vee at *{{Grace}}* (*"look at you wanting it"*) — that echo is intended.

**The analytic register is Pace's too (companion guard).** Beyond the seeing-lines, Pace owns the analytic/mathematical grammar — calculus, computation, *solving* her. Randi's grammar is *reading the room, the right word, the setup, status* — **never** Pace's math; and Vee's perception of Randi never borrows the analytic register either. The two tracks stay tonally apart so they never blur. (Full paired rule: `meta-arch-bible.md` §Global Craft Rules, "Distinct rendering vocabularies for the two seducers.")

**A signature, not a slogan.** Pace's phrases work by rarity — about one per scene; repeated too often they flatten into catchphrases. Randi's status register stays *various* — no single replacement catchphrase — so her warmth reads as range, not tic.

## The Cup — The Body's Tell

> **The cup is the body's tell for the need-state.** See `meta-arch-randi.md`, 🔒 THE NEED ENGINE (locked): the cup fires on bottom-position — *something done to her she cannot control* — and **need is the master instance** (the contingency she cannot operate her way out of). The cup is the body leaking the need the operator denies.

The nail-press/cup is the **only external, present-tense, recurring rendering of the bottom-Randi** in scenes where she is ostensibly in command.

**The gesture:** whenever Randi is in a venue where the need is running and she has no operational lever to discharge it through, her hands find the nearest hard-edged object — a cup, a glass, a wine stem, the rim of a plate — and she presses its edge up under a fingernail, into the quick. **The direction is one way only:** the sharp edge goes *under the nail*, down into the quick — never the nail pressed onto the rim. **The finger varies** (thumb, index) so the gesture recurs without the sentence recurring. Not a grip; a point of applied pressure. Under the table, in her lap, at the side of the plate where no one sees.

**Two gestures, not one — and the glass is Randi's alone (author ruling 2026-10-03).** The glass fidget belongs **exclusively to Randi**: never Vee, whose tells are bodily (`meta-craft-vivienne.md`, Behavioral Signatures), and never Pace, who has his own fidgets — the fork squared to the edge of the table (`back.md:61`, `the-usual.md:101`).

- **The nail-press** — the rim's thin edge up under the nail — is Randi creating pain she controls when the emotional toll is too high and too far out of her control. **Reserved for the most serious emotional challenges** (author ruling 2026-11-09). It fires **in reaction** — to what Vee or Pace says or does, or to a wound one of them reopens — not on words Randi is composing and in command of: she manages her own words better than theirs. Triggers: **Vee truly seen by Pace and thriving** (the thing Randi craves and finds intolerable); **Vee talking about real love from Pace**; serious emotional pain that Vee or Pace sets off, even while Randi is the one telling it (`covering.md:107`, the panties dinner, reopened by Vee's story); fear of losing Pace. **Never on sexual exploits**, hers or Vee's, however charged — those take the hair. Below the peak, the same threats take the finger bite (see Randi's Tells).
- **Turning the glass** is the other charge — **arousal she is running**: the operator's pleasure (excitement, a story landing, the operation working). It is not the press and does not carry its meaning.

**The press is normally rendered on the page, and seen.** Vee's close third is no exception — she sees the hand and does not read the meaning, which is the point. Visible instances (reserved for the peaks, author rulings 2026-11-04, 2026-11-09): `the-bench.md:455` (the debut, seen by Pace), `how-its-done.md:274`, `portion.md` (moving 2026-11-09 from `:27`, her own *"She's happy,"* to the reaction at `:35`, *"Randi watched him be glad"*), `covering.md:107`, `among-friends.md:77`. Cut 2026-11-04 as overuse/operating: `all-the-time.md:63`, `clean-plate.md:191`. Converted 2026-11-09 (prose pending): `space.md:53` → the hair (no fear of losing Pace on the page; she is excited to tell him about Vee's submissiveness); `gesso.md:95` (the thumbnail into the pad of her finger) → the hair (sexy, laughing talk); `clean-plate.md:219` (the nail riding the rim) → the hair (a sexual exploit). `covering.md:63` went from press to band touch 2026-11-04, and to the finger bite 2026-11-09. `among-friends.md:77` (author 2026-10-11, superseding the 2026-10-04 "single hidden instance": close third cannot render a press Vee doesn't see): after her own toast, *"meeting Vee was the highlight of my year,"* said in front of Pace, the glass turns a slow quarter at a time until the thumb sets up under the rim, the nail going white; Vee reads it as her story having landed. Turn then press, the lesser charge giving way to the need. Four turns across chapters 31–53, two of them in adjacent chapters — at the density limit; where another would crowd, vary the beat with the face (*The face as a set of tells*, below), not a fifth turn.

**Why the nail-press and not a grip.** A grip is containment — reading as ordinary stress. The nail-press is self-administered sensation — the hand producing a precise private sting. That is the bottom's gesture in miniature: when the helplessness-charge runs and there is no one to administer the sensation, she administers a small one to herself.

**The cup fires when Randi is, in the moment, the one being operated on** — when something is done to her she cannot control: the wound delivered (Vee's reports about Pace), the configuration doing to her what she does to others.

**The cup does NOT fire when Randi is in the dominant/operating position.** Running the SATC extraction, the operation itself is the discharge. Hands occupied with domination do not seek the edge. **Operating = no cup. Being-done-to = cup.**

**Tracking across the year:**
- Debuts at the bench — Randi overtly in the submissive position; teaches the gesture its meaning *(→ `the-bench.md`)*
- The engineered meet-cute: Pace's eyes pass Randi on the way to Vee; her hand at the rim of something *(→ `substitution.md`)*
- SATC lunches: still during easy talk and during Randi's own operating; present when Vee's reports about Pace land *(→ `how-its-done.md` for first instance)*
- Scenes with Pace where Vee is mentioned: the hand finds whatever edge is to hand
- Engineered scenes she has architected: the highest-amplitude venues — she has scripted herself into helpless witness

**At the threesome, the hands are not occupied.** The concealment ends. The cup belonged to the year. It does not belong after.

**Never name the gesture as a symbol.** The reader will recognize the recurrence across the year.

## The Locked Door

Whenever Randi lets herself into a space — Pace's house above all — she locks or bolts it behind her, first thing, no key needed, against Pace's habit of leaving the door open for the guest he expects.

Render it as plain behavior, never as a stated symbol: his openness, her sealing. **The lock is the TOP's gesture** — control of perimeter, the active setting of who may enter. The cup is the BOTTOM's gesture — containment of overflow inside a perimeter already breached. She runs both because the configuration makes her both top (to Vee) and bottom (to Pace, to the configuration, to her own need).

## Physical Description

Petite (5'2"), built to the "proper" scale she maintains and grades other women against — narrow through shoulder and hip. Pale skin. Startlingly blue eyes — her best feature, the one she has built her adult life around being looked at for; they darken when she concentrates. Sleek black hair, fine, worn in a controlled ponytail with a scrunchie as the one allowed accent — that's the *daily* binding; for a fancy, choosing occasion it dresses up into a sleek French braid (no scrunchie — the scrunchie is an everyday accent only), the same bound grammar at higher polish. The hair is the small daily version of the whole apparatus: she decides each morning exactly how much of herself the day will be permitted to touch and binds the rest.

**The wind device (standing contrast with Vee):** the same wind that lifts and plays through Vee's loose dark-red hair finds almost nothing to move on Randi's bound head. Warm/cool, loose/sleek, red/black, uncontrolled/bound-in-advance. The containment is a choice, not a stillness; the ponytail can swing, but nothing escapes the band.

**The Art Deco archetype — not a prison, a grammar.** The organizing visual logic of her self-presentation is Art Deco: sleek, geometric, controlled, cool surface with warmth underneath. Think Tamara de Lempicka, Erté — the contained woman at full power. This is an archetype she operates from, not a costume she wears every day.

*When she is choosing* — nights with Pace, SATC lunches, anything she has arranged — she presents in the full register: cream cashmere, champagne camisole, gold pants. Makeup sharp and precise: cat eye, cut-crease eyeshadow, full-coverage contoured foundation, two-tone overlining on lips with clean color separation. Jewelry minimal and exact, in two registers. **The constant:** a white-gold Cartier *Love* bracelet, screwed shut on her wrist — the one piece that never comes off and never changes (it needs the little screwdriver; she has a jeweler do it). Always there. **The two tiers:** plain white-gold studs as the everyday signature (small, hard, throwing light — never pearls, which are Vee's), stepping up to diamond studs in white gold and the diamond solitaire on a white-gold chain so fine it is barely there, for dressier / arranged occasions — escalation by material, not by form; the pieces that change when she is *choosing.* **On the page:** the *name* "Love" is spoken once, as Randi's own deniable line (`on-her-floor`, the walk — *"the bracelet is called Love… it's a real commitment. Taking it off requires tools"*); the **brand (Cartier) stays unspoken**, and the **symbolism is never explained** — Vee just hears a charming fact; the reader who knows hears a bracelet named *Love* that locks onto the wrist and needs tools to remove. (Seed: first brunch — Vee clocks the ever-present band, *unnamed*, `meta-condensed-how-its-done.md`; name + payoff: the walk in `meta-condensed-on-her-floor.md`.) **Perfume:** Diptyque Eau des Sens — bitter orange, green, juniper, no sweetness; the cool-surface/warm-underneath construction made olfactory.

**Nails and skin — the polish and its one deviation.** Fingernails kept **bare, natural, unpainted** — the cost and the discipline are *in* leaving them plain (not the sorority's pink or French tips). The **toenails are lacquered blue** — her one small, deliberate step off the sorority's expected pink/French-tip line, chosen because the blue **favors her eyes**. Not hidden, not a secret: Randi lives for her surface to be *seen*, and the blue is a nascent, low-stakes rebellion worn openly — mild rule-stepping in the one place the code polices least. **Skin:** pale, **lightly freckled across the chest** — the natural body showing through the managed surface. Arousal reads as tightness and deepening (darker, never pink). **The {{Fairytale}} mirror** is this grammar at the moment of its failure: the eyeliner holds because nothing reached her eyes, and she rebuilds the rest of the face exact, and there is no one behind it. The precision intact on the surface, nothing inside it. The face is doing its job. The job has stopped meaning anything.

## Fairytale — How to Render It

*(Drafted: `fairytale.md`; see `meta-plan-chronology.md`. The bench for comparison: → `the-bench.md`.)*

**Amended ruling (author, 2026-07-30) — the choice IS stated, the love IS named.** The earlier rule ("the choice is not stated to herself in words; she does not deliberate") is superseded for this scene. In the Fairytale aperture Randi articulates, in interior: that Vee is the best friend she has ever had; that she loves her **and has told her so, more than once** (the audit of the spoken record — every "love you, girl" was meant; the reader now carries that into every later brunch as dread, not reread discovery); that the word has only ever left her otherwise as currency or with a private qualifier, and with Vee carried neither; and the decision not to tell her. Rationale: the villain-guard's affirmative half ("under-showing her love is the villain-flattening failure mode"; *she loved Vee and did it anyway* only lands if we watched the loving), plus the 2026-07 four-model cold-read panel, which delivered the target affect (sympathy + dread, "starving apparatus," zero grooming-reads) off the explicit page. This is also Randi's would-*you*-do-differently scene — the not-telling-your-best-friend trap is the most commonly lived dilemma in the book, and it only recruits the reader if the stakes are named.

What still holds, unchanged:

- **Calculation-flavor is the failure mode to guard.** The decision must read as a flinch with a lifetime of engineering behind it, not a strategist devising. The wrapper is fear: "She was not brave enough to lose her. She had never had to be brave; she had arranged her whole life so she would never have to be." Fear is compulsive; calculation is clever. Keep the plan phrased as a shape already forming that she declines to interrupt ("Let the thing keep the shape it was already taking"), never as devised strategy.
- **Doc vocabulary stays off the page.** "Need," "aliveness," "contingency," "configuration" — narrator-knowledge words, never in her interior.
- **The word is never spoken to be received.** She has said "I love you" only in the register where it passes as friendship; "tell her" means *say it plainly, so it cannot be misheard* — and that she never does. The distinction is installed by "She could say it plainly, over eggs" and never explained.
- **"Vee was her gorgeous."** The reserved endearment (Vee-only, always meant) named as a possession in interior — the epithet rhyme with "the perfect nothing" (John). It arms the closing text's "miss your face most though, gorgeous 💋" as the one true coin.
- **The return is still enacted, not deliberated.** The choice's *statement* lives in the mirror interior; its *enactment* is unchanged — she fixes the hair, turns off the light, walks back to the bed without breaking stride.

## The Closing Image

Leave her ambulant. Not destroyed. Not redeemed. Walking. Carrying the knowledge of what real is, and the hate that has nowhere to discharge. Walking into a seeking she has already chosen. The last beat of her presence in the novel should be motion into a future she has already started — not stepping into it from a stable present.

**The keeper is the knowledge of what real is, and the seeking it condemns her to.** The hate persists to the end, but it is not what sustains her: it is unspent because it has nowhere to discharge. Do not let her spend it, do not let her articulate it to Pace, to Vee, to anyone, and do not let it carry the ending. What she walks forward on is the knowing.

## What the Prose Must Hold

**The sadism and the genuine love are the same motion.** Not two separate things coexisting. The sadism is what love looks like when the channel for being-received is foreclosed. Render both at full force simultaneously; do not let the prose reduce her to pure operator or sentimentalize her into victim.

**Same motion means one gesture, not two beats.** Don't render the love and the sadism as adjacent beats — a cruel beat, then a tender beat — a toggle reads as two-faced. Put both inside a *single* action: the chin-lift that stops the shame-spiral *is* the setup for the next escalation (`on-her-floor`, *"Hey — hey. Don't… There's Harrison"*); the slow kiss is generosity and calculation at once. Its mechanics are fixed (author ruling 2026-11-04): her draw-back opens the kiss without breaking contact — never a gap, a wait, or a read of the partner's face — and she takes the second press rather than waiting to be asked; Pace's kiss separates and leaves the woman room to come back, Randi's never does (→ `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md`, *Two kisses, two consents*). A face has to turn to be two-faced; hers never turns.

**The sadism scenes are clean in the moment; the cold is only the con, reread-only.** Her sadism is a true, mutual, loving thing — one of the realest things she has. A physical/sexual sadism beat must read hot, warm, and *good* on first pass; the only cold is the orchestration underneath, legible on reread. If a beat reads sinister in the moment, cut the signal — the tragedy needs her sadism genuine, not menacing. (Earn-the-dark applied to the sadism, not just the brunches; → `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md` DON'Ts.)

**Vee chooses at every gate — that is the anti-trope armor.** In every Randi/Vee scene, especially the physical ones, Vee names the choice, craves it in advance, and holds the shame as her own after (`on-her-floor`: *"she did not let go"*; the mirror pre-craving; the peeled thong). Randi supplies the conditions; Vee supplies the wanting. This keeps the sadism from reading as a queer predator working an ingenue, and it keeps it hot. Full mechanic: → `meta-plan-satc-tracks.md`, *Two-Perimeter / double agent against herself.*

**Half-seeing, not perceiving at depth.** At {{Fairytale}}, she perceives that throttling produces nothing — that is what she sees. She does not perceive that advancing on the configuration will burn it through. The half-seeing is enough to be warned and not enough to be dissuaded. (Amended 2026-07-30 with the Fairytale ruling above: the love and the decision are now articulated in the aperture; what must stay unrendered is the *forward* blindness — she never perceives that advancing will burn the configuration through.)

**She is tragic, not fated.** The wound did not drive her. She chose the channel. The choosing is what makes her tragic in the older sense: she saw enough of the alternative to know it was unlivable, and she returned to the plan anyway.


## The accent (author ruling 2026-10-08)

**Born in Kentucky and raised there until she was nine (author ruling 2026-11-03,
superseding "before she started primary school"; `meta-arch-randi.md`, Origins); the family
then moved to central Florida.** Nine is old enough that the accent is hers, acquired
regionally, and she worked it off. She is embarrassed to be from Kentucky. The earliest
instance of the thing she does to everything about where she is from.

**Pace knows.** She told him once, sheepish, when he asked, one more of the things she lets
him see that nobody else gets to. So Pace hears it *as Kentucky* and names it in his POV;
Vee hears the same sound and invents Europe. Same phenomenon, two descriptions: Pace's is
concrete and word-specific (which vowel did what in which word); Vee's is impressionistic
and builds a biography. Never give them the same phrasing.

**Trigger (2026-11-03): it comes out of hiding when something moves her** — aroused, tender,
grieving — not arousal alone. Planning rationale only: she controls her hands, not her
voice. The word "larynx" never appears on the page.

**Two distinct phenomena, and only one of them is a tell.**

1. **The baseline trace — not a tell.** A faint residue always present in her speech: the
   **esses going sibilant, the a's arching**. Kept (author ruling 2026-11-03, option b): the
   trace is always faintly there; being moved brings it *out of hiding*. Vee's first hearing
   is at the first coffee (`see-you-later`),
   where **Vee hears it and reads it as cosmopolitan** — the payload, and cold on reread: the
   polish Vee takes for Europe is Kentucky, sanded down. Note `meta-note-see-you-later.md:31`
   rules that *no tells fire* in that chapter because Randi is operating; this seed does not
   breach it, because the trace is her voice and not a tell.
2. **The arousal slip — autonomic, and it fires even while she is operating** (author ruling,
   superseding the assistant's caution). Arousal is involuntary: suppressing it would be like
   suppressing her pupils. **This is the carve-out from the no-tell-while-operating rule**
   (`meta-condensed-fed.md`, `meta-note-see-you-later.md:31`), and the carve-out is principled
   — **she controls her hands; she does not control her larynx.** The suppressible gesture
   tells (the glass, the nail-press) stay governed by the operating rule; the voice does not.
   Its richest use is therefore the case the gesture tells cannot reach: aroused *by Vee*
   while managing her, where the operation is working and the voice gives her away to a reader
   who has been taught the tell.

**Not in {{Fairytale}}** (considered and declined). That chapter already carries the mirror
where everything of hers fails with no one behind the face; parents with strong accents would
hand the scene a second explanation of who she is, and would solve her, which it is built not
to do. (2026-11-03: the lift line `fairytale.md:39` now reads "She was from Kentucky, and did
not mention it." That names the origin she hides; it gives the parents no accent, so this
decline stands.)
