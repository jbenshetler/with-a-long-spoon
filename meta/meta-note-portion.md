# Notes — Portion (craft)

*Companion to `meta-condensed-portion.md`.*

## What the chapter is for

The fall's rupture, and the place the jealousy becomes behaviour. Lit at
{{How It's Done}}; the accusation here is its **laundered form** — she cannot say *I am
jealous*, so she says *you are selfish*, which is sayable and nearly true. Resolved later
as the unspoken truce at {{Gone}}; never discussed by either of them.

## The shape

Everything in the first half is **portioning**, and the title carries all of it: the small
bowl, the spoon drawn along the top of the sauce, the salad eaten and the stew refused,
the glass she pours herself, and the measure she is really objecting to — how much of Vee
Pace is taking and how long he intends to take.

## Guards

- **The bowl is the argument.** Hers is the small one, it is served, it is never eaten, and
  the last image is the skin over it with her one line still drawn through. Do not let any
  character comment on it. He covers it and puts it away *for her*, which is the whole of
  his answer.
- **The absence of *"Tell me"* is the scene's loudest sound.** It is established in
  {{Tannin}} and {{Swim Lanes}} as the standing demand of every night in this house. Here
  it does not come, Pace waits for it, and he understands only that whatever this is, it
  is not for him. Never explain it.
- **"It's wonderful"** is the bright coin she spends on everyone except him — the
  compliment is the warning, and he cannot place it. Keep his inability to place it.
- **The nail-press is on the page here** (`:27`, the rim in under her thumbnail at
  *"She's happy"*) and is one of the gesture's canonical visible instances — jealousy and
  insecurity about Vee being cherished. See `meta-craft-randi.md`, The Cup; do not convert
  it to a turn.
- **His one flash is exact, not loud.** *"How do I get her from where she is to us, on your
  schedule?"* The stress-tell is precision, never volume (`meta-arch-bible.md`, Pace). He
  does not raise his voice and does not take it back.
- **Vee's agency is the thing he will not trade** — *"She chooses it, Randi. Every step of
  it. Or it isn't anything."* This is the sentence the whole book is written against; it
  must read as conviction, not as an excuse, and he must not be softened into negotiating
  it.
- **Randi's accusation is the laundered jealousy and must never be named as jealousy** —
  not by her, not by him, not by the narration. The reader supplies it.
- **Her interior stays closed.** The only tells are the spoon, the untouched bowl, the flat
  repetition, the bare nails and the white gold on the cloth, the bottle, the shut eyes and
  the back. Nothing else.
- **He clocks every piece and assembles none**, and the misattribution is given in his own
  words — *the want, with an edge on it tonight, from somewhere.* That is the two-layer
  behaviour/cause doctrine; do not let the narration know better than he does.
- **He does not put his hand in her hair**, having done so at {{Space}} and elsewhere. The
  withheld gesture is the measure of how far outside it he is.
