# Change map — cut `The Stranger`, drop the Peter-reach

*Branch: `cut-stranger-pace-sympathy`. Purpose: remove the book's highest-risk
manipulator-read beat (the morning erasure) to raise Pace sympathy at the winter
reconciliation, per the oracle de-confound (Pace sympathy 3, wariness deepening at
the finale, `stranger` the coldest beat). Nothing merges to `main` until the author
signs off; plan is to draft, then cold-read for a fresh verdict.*

## The structural move

**Before:** reconciliation day = 3 beats (Fri Jan 22): `The Stranger` (morning, door
one, honest reach fails — Vee reaches "Peter" to his cold face, it fails to thaw him)
→ `My Friend Randi` (afternoon, coat counsel) → `Nothing Underneath` (night, door two,
wordless offering works). Thesis carried as a **diurnal arc**: daylight reach fails,
night offering succeeds.

**After:** reconciliation day = 2 beats. The morning door-visit is **gone**. The
failed-word→wordless-offering contrast now spans the break: **December's worded demand
ruptures** (`Believe Me`, "words are cheap," she walks out) → **silence across the
break** → **January's wordless offering succeeds** (`Nothing Underneath`). The
**Peter-reach is dropped entirely** — not relocated. `Rock` (name permitted, warm) +
`The Top Drawer` (whispered to sleeping Pace) keep the name alive; `Excavations` and
the reckoning re-anchor to the withheld word / `Rock`, not to a failed reach.

New Jan 22 shape: red-eye lands early → Vee cries on the drive home to **no-Pace** →
empty dorm (Cassie not back) through the morning → afternoon she breaks and drives to
Randi's (`My Friend Randi`, beat 1 of 2) → night reunion (`Nothing Underneath`, beat 2
of 2). The morning airport-drive grief replaces `The Stranger`'s function (delivering
Vee wrecked to Randi); the wound driving her is now `Believe Me` + the two-week silence,
not a fresh rejection.
