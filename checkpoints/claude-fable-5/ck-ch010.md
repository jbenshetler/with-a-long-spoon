### Impression
- **Vee** I'm entirely for. Her hunger — for food, for being chosen, for her own yes — is rendered so nakedly it hurts, and her pride in "I did it on purpose" lands as triumph even though I know how much of the runway was built for her. The jacket promised nothing would be taken that she didn't hand over; I'm watching her hand it over gladly, and I dread and want the rest.
- **Pace** pulls me two ways at once and that's the book working. Every individual act is genuinely kind — the pencil handed back, the room left for choosing, the closed-door discipline learned from Daphne at real cost — and the whole is an architecture around an unknowing girl. I believe his tenderness is real; I don't yet trust what it's in service of. "He's hard to say no to" in Randi's private key is the warning I keep hearing.
- **Randi** I feel tenderness for against my better judgment. The bench and the mirror showed me the starved girl under the face, and the bottle cap under the thumbnail scared me. She is running a seduction on Vee — and some of it (the "dear friend," the helpless staring on the quad) is escaping her own management. I trust nothing she performs and quite a lot of what leaks.
- **Cassie** I trust completely — the one person with no angle. The "Goodbye, Vee" sits in me like a bruise; whether it's love or just clear sight, she's the reader's proxy and I fear she'll be right.
- **Brooke** I have no evidence against — she was warm and let Randi go — but her warmth works like an instrument, and I'd rather she never looked closely at any of this.
