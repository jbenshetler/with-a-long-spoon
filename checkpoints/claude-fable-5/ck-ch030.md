### Impression
- **Vee** I'm wholly with. She's the warmest thing on the page — funny, hungry, brave in increments (opening her eyes on the riser; the humble pie; "I wanted it. And I took it"). Watching her flourish under attention that the jacket told me was seeded as a game is the book's whole ache: everything she feels is real, and the frame around it isn't hers. Tender toward her; afraid for her Thursday-math finances and her Thursday-math heart.
- **Pace** confounds my wariness. Scene by scene he is the most careful, most generous man in the book — nothing he's done to Vee reads as taking; his tenderness looks indistinguishable from love, and in ch24/28 it may simply be love. But I can't unknow the design: the game, the staged meeting, the two women at once, the connoisseur's patience of the bench. He is either the best thing that ever happened to her or the most beautifully made trap, and the book keeps letting both be true. Trust: high in each scene, low in the aggregate.
- **Randi** frightens me and moves me in equal measure. The ch1 mirror is the saddest thing in the book — a woman who chose, permanently, not to be found — and everything she does to Vee since (the warmth, the debriefs, the soft doors, the stories told to arouse) is both grooming and, increasingly, real attachment leaking past the design. Her jealous stillness in ch24 and "she got past me" make me think the plan is no longer fully hers to run. I don't trust her hands; I believe her feelings.
- **Cassie** I trust completely — the book's one person with no angle. Her watching has grief in it I can't yet name, and I dread the scene where she sees what I see.
- **Brooke** I read as weather: warm, pressurized, gathering.
- **Sheri** I liked immediately — the only person who says the plain thing out loud.
- Overall: the seduction is working on me exactly as the jacket promised — I keep forgetting to be suspicious because every individual kindness is real. The stats professor keeps reminding me: same numbers, opposite verdicts; and the second kind of outlier is already in everything that comes after.
