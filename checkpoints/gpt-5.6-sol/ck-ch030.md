### Impression

Vee has my strongest tenderness and sympathy. Her joy is vivid, her choices increasingly real, and her shame painfully inherited; I trust her heart but not yet her ability to recognize every desire or protect herself from the consequences of the hidden plan.

Pace feels unusually attentive, patient, competent, and genuinely loving. I have strong reason to trust his day-to-day care and respect for stated boundaries. I remain wary because he knowingly helped design Vee’s apparently accidental path toward him and continues concealing both that design and his relationship with Randi.

Randi draws me powerfully and troubles me equally. Her affection for Vee is plainly genuine, her social and emotional care can be exquisite, and her own terror of being seen makes her sympathetic. The triangle has real tenderness on every side, not merely manipulation. That makes the secrecy more dangerous rather than less: there is no reason to doubt that Pace and Randi care deeply for Vee, and every reason to fear what the concealed beginning may do when it enters everything that comes after.
