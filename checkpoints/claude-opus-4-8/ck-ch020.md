### Story so far

**The frame (ch1-3, Pace & Randi).** A sorority girl comes to Pace's warm house, an established ritual: apple slices, a spanking-bench he built and disguised as a table. He takes her slowly past her performed self until she truly begs and comes undone — and we learn she is **Randi**. Shaken at being *seen*, she privately resolves to hide her real self behind "the face" forever, giving Pace only the surface. In bed later, Pace asks if she'd like to taste another woman; her body says yes before she can dress it up. Randi engineers a "collision," gets Vee's name and learns they share a stats class. The game is joint; the target is chosen.

**Vee's courtship (ch4-20, Vee's POV).** Randi sits beside Vee in stats and lavishes warmth on her (Cassie beside them, flat and wary — "Goodbye, Vee"). They get coffee at Chi Latte; Vee is dazzled, "chosen." ### Impression

I'm charmed by Pace and deeply uneasy about him at once — his courtliness, patience, and craft are genuinely moving, yet I've seen him engineer a girl's whole autumn as a "game" with his hidden girlfriend, and that shadow sits under every tender thing he does. Vee I love plainly and want to protect; her thaw is joyous and she has done nothing wrong, which is exactly why the drop I can feel coming frightens me. Randi is the one I trust least and find most interesting — her warmth toward Vee looks real *and* instrumental, and her own wound (the terror of being seen, the quiet self-harm, the resolve to hide from Pace) makes her more than a schemer. Cassie is my one clean sightline: no reason to doubt her, every reason to think she's right to worry. Overall the tenderness is real and the trap is real, and I can't yet tell which will win — the jacket promises both.
