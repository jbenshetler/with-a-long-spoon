"She lifted it out clean as she went, the way a ladle takes soup from the pot and the surface closes without a hole, and told herself it was a kindness. It was a kindness. She believed it the way you believe the thing that lets you keep what you want to keep." That's the whole chapter. Vee editing the night for Cassie, keeping "the middle" — not out of shame this time but out of *hoarding*. The clean-eyed girl is now the one managing the seam, and Cassie, who reads everything "fast and whole," feels something cross her face at the door and then chooses not to pull the thread. That half-second on Cassie's face before the grin — I clocked it. She senses the shape and lets it stand. The asymmetry between them widened again, and it's Vee widening it now.

The panties beat is the sharpest thing here. She reaches for the zipper to shower, and stops — because she gave them away, and the next thing off her body "would not be a story she was editing. It would be on the floor, and there would be nothing left to say." She zips back up rather than be bare in front of her closest friend. The trade she made in his palm has literally reorganized her body's availability — she can hide from Pace nothing and from Cassie everything. That inversion is doing a lot.

And *floozie* — the mother's word — comes up as the toenails ("a word for" the frosted-plum in October), and this time she notices she "didn't much mind it this morning." The shame-that-doesn't-close from last chapter holds. Still can't tell if that's freedom or the last check gone.

Cassie calling him "your boyfriend," "lover" — kindly, certainly, **wrong**, the page says so outright. That's the tell I trust: the word is wrong and Vee doesn't correct it.

No heat on the page — it's all afterglow and concealment. No Randi, no Pace present. Trust/suspicion: nothing new indicts anyone; the unease is structural, the widening seam between Vee and the one person who loves her clean.

Small, bodily, a mark left on her that she chose. It points at exactly the chapter's quiet: the private evidence of the night, worn where a friend can see and read wrong.

What I want next: Randi back, and that scar. Dread: that Cassie's kept silence is the last clean thing, and Vee's now spending it.
