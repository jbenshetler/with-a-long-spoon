## Reader reaction

This whole chapter is a brunch, and I was happy and uneasy in almost exactly the same proportion as Vee was — which frightens me a little, because the design worked on me too. The sun porch, the mimosa she priced and then the second one she "did not price at all" — that small lift of the lifelong arithmetic, handed to her by Randi choosing the place and ordering without looking at the card. I felt the warmth of it as warmth. "There's my girl." I believe Vee feels chosen because I felt chosen on her behalf.

And then the debrief unfolds and I can see the shape of every question. "You have to tell me… How was he?" — the elicitation again, exact same hand as the Mercedes: confess first, something self-deprecating and disarming (the sad story of the boy in a hurry, "at least I got dinner out of it"), then harvest. And what gets harvested this time is everything the design didn't yet have: the toes ("You paint the toes for a man who's going to have your feet bare in his hands. You did those Friday. For him."), the bought underwear, and finally the creed handed back as blessing — "You're not caught, sweetheart. You're lucky." She names Vee's premeditation, reframes her shame as good fortune, and Vee says the true thing out loud: "I wanted it. And I took it. For once." And Randi says "Good," and lays her hand over Vee's, and I felt the word settle into Vee and stay — and I couldn't tell whether I was watching a friend midwife a woman's self-permission or a handler closing the loop. Both. The page insists on both, and the hand is warm either way.

The thing that actually ached: Vee gave Randi *more* than she gave Cassie, and noticed it as a kindness — "because Randi would not flinch" — and the one thing she still folded back into its tissue, the trade, the middle, stays hers. The wet spot remains the secret she shares only with Pace. The silks under the dress came up as the answer she couldn't give about the polish — and Randi watched it arrive in her face and harvested *that* instead. Even what Vee withholds gets read.

And the kiss at the curb — the second fire-lane kiss, now half-expected, met, and Vee "didn't look down the street for who might have seen them." The anchor re-set, in daylight, in the ordinary noise. Randi's "See you soon, gorgeous" and Vee driving home lighter, radio up. The chapter is called "Fed" and I take it three ways at once: Vee fed (the meal, the attention, the being-told), Randi feeding on the telling ("Give me something to live on… I'm running on fumes here" — she says it plainly, as a joke, and it isn't), and the design feeding on them both. It did not give anything away and it recolored everything.

Friction: almost none. If anything, the chapter is *smooth*, and the smoothness is the point. My dread is now specific: the sentence "Randi was the person you got to *tell*" — the one undesigned witness edited, the designed one given the fuller cut.
