## Reader reaction

This one swept me almost clean off my feet, and I let it. The dress hanging in the window, lit from inside itself — "he'd taken all that trouble and then taken the further trouble to hide the trouble" — that got me the way it got her. And then the chapter doubled it: the slit to the hip, the underwear coming off *by her own decision*, the shame crossing into wanting "almost before it was shame at all," and her striding to him to show him what he'd made. This is the most fully owned desire the book has given her. Nobody elicited it, nobody blessed it, nobody harvested it. The *floozy* flare came and she "stayed anyway" — and this time there was no Randi in the room to name it for her. She named herself: "a lot of woman who knew exactly how much she was."

Pace lands on me here as close to purely good as he ever has. The grin he couldn't hold in, the kiss broken not as power but as a kid with a surprise; the two-handed care hanging the dress mid-everything; the arm coming down around her in his sleep. And I noticed — the chapter is almost free of my usual double-vision. Almost. The pedestal and the *newly hung mirror, screw heads still bright* — he built a stage for her to see herself on, and she experienced it as a gift, and it IS a gift, and it is also the design's next room. I hold both, but honestly, this chapter made the gift side heavy. The daydream of the induction — her late, on purpose, the room turning, "it just felt like what she was for" — is the first time her exhibitionism has been hers alone, no pointing required. "Getting away with something" as the last line: she means the daydream, maybe the whole affair. I heard it darker — she doesn't know what she's inside of — but the page let it stay happy, and I'll let it.

The title: "Peekaboo" — the slit that gives and takes back, the lining set into "the only parts of her the dress had decided not to give away," conceal-and-reveal as the dress's whole grammar. Also, uncomfortably, the design itself: showing her exactly what it wants her to see. Heat: the door kiss going deep, the stride, the leg hooked around him — then a fade to after. Charged and explicit-adjacent rather than sustained. I want the induction now — and I dread it in equal measure (guess from here: that's where Randi, the dress, and the pointing all converge).
