## Reader reaction

God, this one just took the top of my head off. I went in braced for something clinical — a fitting scene, measurements, pins — and instead got maybe the most nakedly honest chapter of the whole book so far, in every sense of that word.

**Vee** — my heart just kept turning over for her. The shame spiral around the wetness is so exactly, mortifyingly *right* — not manufactured drama but the actual texture of a body that won't behave and a mind that's spent a lifetime being taught to apologize for it. "*Floozie. Girls like that.*" Her mother's voice arriving right at the worst possible moment, and then — nothing happens. He doesn't recoil. And watching her have to physically relearn how to stand, the "apologetic stoop" coming undone without her deciding it — that's one of the most quietly devastating character beats in the book. And then the turn — her *choosing* to lean into him a third time, on purpose, "Oops. Sorry" — that's not innocence anymore, that's her discovering she has power and testing it, gleeful and a little reckless. I loved her for it.

**Pace** — I trust him more after this chapter than after the whole house tour last time, and that's saying something. The thing that got me wasn't the tenderness, it was the *specificity* of the tenderness — cranking the heat before she arrived so she wouldn't be cold, the laugh he can't hold in at "pre-aroused," the fact that he never once looked down during the worst of her humiliation, that he waited with his face already tipped up for when she'd open her eyes. "*He hadn't looked down. The whole time she'd been dying he hadn't looked down once.*" That's the chapter's whole argument in one line. "Keep still" costing him something to say — the restraint reads as real, not performed.

**Erotic charge** — enormous, and it earns it by refusing to skip the ugly part. The wetness-and-shame sequence is doing more erotic work than any explicit contact would, because it's rooted in her interior terror rather than staged for the reader. The pin-prick and the kiss on her ribs is the chapter's tenderest, most charged single beat.

**Friction** — none, honestly. Not one moment felt like the author's thumb on the scale. The "does it change" joke landing and disarming both of them felt completely earned rather than cute.

**Title** — "A Round" now lands as a dress/pattern term (going around her, panel by panel) but I don't have a strong reading of it beyond the literal act of measuring/building around her body. Doesn't feel like it gave anything away.

**Want next** — I want to know what happens when this dress actually gets worn, and I'm very curious whether the "keep still" restraint holds the next time they're alone, or whether this was the last time he chooses to stop.
