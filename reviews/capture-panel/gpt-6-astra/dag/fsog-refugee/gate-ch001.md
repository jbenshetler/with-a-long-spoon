REACTION

The apple slices got me. Before the bench, before anything: he knows how she’ll actually eat them, and she climbs into his lap and feeds him a bite. That’s the sort of attention I came for. Her moving his hands where she wants them, him enjoying being directed by her—I relaxed into that. There are two people here who want each other, and she has somewhere to put that wanting.

And yes, the slow undressing and the waiting worked on me. But the moment I liked him most was when he came around to her face and said “Hi.” That little laugh between them, right in the middle of something so exposing. His hand on her cheek. I want that man: the one who can take charge and still come down where she can see him.

Then I hit “it was not the right *please* yet,” and something in me pulled back. She has told him what she wants. He’s waiting for her to ask in a voice he considers real enough. I can enjoy begging in a scene, absolutely, but I got uneasy about him deciding which part of her counts as her. Especially when he wants to hear that particular cry again. I was still caught up, still feeling the heat, but I wasn’t resting in his judgment anymore.

And then—*Randi.* Oh. I had assumed this was Vee. Now the woman who is going to help arrange Vee’s experience has already been here herself, and I suddenly want to reread the jacket with considerably less trust.

The bathroom hurt. Finding out that she really did feel that alive matters to me; I needed to hear it from her. But the memory of begging her mother, and her terror of letting him be tender afterward, changed the whole evening in my stomach. The bottle cap under her thumbnail was where I wanted him to notice something beyond how far she’d gone. He notices the thickness of an apple slice. Does he understand what he’s seeing now?

I don’t think his warmth is fake. That makes the ending worse. He’s sitting there happy, ready to feed her and hold her, and she’s deciding he must never see her again while staying tucked against him. I’m sad for her, and I’m worried about Vee before I’ve even met her. My one-a.m. text would be: “He’s tender and I’m still scared of how certain he is that he knows what she needs.”
