Oh, Randi. I wanted to trust those arms. Then the empty dorm caught her, and Randi was there with exactly the comfort she needed and advice I desperately wanted her to refuse.

I’m reading on, urgently, but I’m braced. Please let someone remember what Vee actually asked for when that coat opens.
