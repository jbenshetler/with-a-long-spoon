The elevator nearly shutting on her because she’s busy looking at him—yes. That got me. I like Vee when she’s allowed to be funny and a little ridiculous with wanting. But I got restless in the middle. I wanted to hear their dinner conversation. What does he say when he isn’t asking the perfect question? What makes her laugh? I understand that she feels fed and safe and can stop monitoring herself. I felt it before I finished being told it. The food sounded lovely; by the tea I was ready for somebody to do something unexpected.

Then she refuses the kiss, he accepts it, and *she stops him with their joined hands*. There. That brought me right back. Her having to stand in the awkwardness of revising her own rule was more intimate to me than the thought that her hand had been waiting for his all her life. And smiling inside the kiss is lovely. I wanted that kiss for her.

Which is an uncomfortable thing to want when Randi is still sitting in the back of my head. Cassie declaring him a man, Vee deciding she trusts where he takes her—I can’t settle into those entirely. He can respect the boundary she names while keeping her ignorant of the situation she’s entering. I’m attracted to his patience and suspicious of it at exactly the same time.

I’m continuing, but I’m hungry now. The wine chapter gave me a little charge, the pool gave me Vee, and this gave me a sweet first kiss. I’ve enjoyed those things. I’m ready for the wanting to get messier and more physical again, and for Vee to learn something about him that isn’t another reason he’s perfect for her.
