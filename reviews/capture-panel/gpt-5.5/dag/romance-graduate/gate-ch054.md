I am not immune to this. I am extremely not immune. This is the kind of female intimacy romance keeps teasing and then flattening, and here it is allowed to be messy and charged and morally complicated and tender all at once.

Vee going to him wordless may get them back into bed, may even get them back into tenderness, but it does not solve the thing. And Randi knows enough to know that. She is helping Vee and also steering her toward the version of repair that preserves the structure Randi and Pace already understand.

Brooke sees the whole room. Finally, someone sees Randi.

And Randi now has a kiss in her mouth and Brooke’s warning in her ear. This is not stable. Thank God.
