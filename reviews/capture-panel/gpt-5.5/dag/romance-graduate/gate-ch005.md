I hated how well it worked on me.

She has no idea she is walking into a structure already built around her. And the chapter makes that feel both morally prickly and horribly romantic. That is the awful little engine of this book for me: I want the thing to happen and I want someone to protect her from it, and those wants are fighting in my chest.
