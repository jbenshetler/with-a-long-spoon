REACTION — Oh, this one got me in three different places and I’m annoyed about all of them in the way that means I’m absolutely not putting the book down.

First, Vee on the plane and then driving back through the same fields wrecked me. That feeling of *I survived home, I’m going back to myself* and then the road betrays her because the body remembers where Fridays used to end. That was so painfully true. I was proud of her for knowing the old room wasn’t home anymore, but also sad for her because that kind of freedom still leaves you nowhere to be held.

And Randi. God. Randi is so good with her when she is good. She doesn’t just soothe her, she sorts the fear from the facts. She lets Vee cry, gets her behind a door, puts the pieces back on the table one by one. I loved her calling out the imaginary Sheri, because that is exactly what panic does: builds a woman at three in the morning and gives her your whole life. Randi saw it and didn’t shame her for it. But also: Randi is steering. Warmly, expertly, lovingly, and still steering. The coat idea is hot, I won’t lie. It is very much my kind of dangerous, especially because Vee finds the wanting inside the shame and chooses it. I believe Vee wants to go. I believe the thought catches fire in her. I want Pace to come with words. I don’t want Vee to have to arrive naked under a coat to make him brave.

And then the kiss. The kiss. Vee turning back because thank you is not enough, taking Randi’s face in both hands, kissing her under all those framed women. That was the chapter’s real electric current for me. Not because it was the most explicit thing, but because it was hers. She initiated. She didn’t wait to be leaned into. She put her hands on Randi’s face like she meant it, and Randi finally gets the thing she’s been starving for: Vee coming back.

I am desperate for tonight now, but not peacefully. I want Vee at Pace’s door, yes. I want the coat and the shoes and the shock of him seeing her. But I need him to understand what she is actually offering and not let her body substitute for the answer she asked for. If he only takes the gift and still won’t name her, I’m going to be furious.
