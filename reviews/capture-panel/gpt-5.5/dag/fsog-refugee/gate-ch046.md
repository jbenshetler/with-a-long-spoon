Oh, this one got me in that quiet, stomach-level way. Not because anything huge happens, but because Vee is trying to translate this whole strange, gorgeous, overwhelming life she has with Pace into language her parents can survive, and every version is cropped. The picture is cropped. The boyfriend story is cropped. Pace himself is cropped down to “math PhD candidate, hiking, dancing,” and somehow that makes me ache more than if she were lying outright.

The dress thing especially. My god. That he made it. I knew it mattered when he gave it to her, but hearing Vee imagine her father finding that stranger than sex made it land differently.
