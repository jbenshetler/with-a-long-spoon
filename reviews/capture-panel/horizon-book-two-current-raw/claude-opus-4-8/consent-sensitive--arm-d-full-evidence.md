F4 — **The shape answers the accountability problem. The delay is at the outermost edge of tolerable, and the mechanism is the highest-risk move in the book.**

The ending packet is, point for point, the anti-con ending I said I was owed: reckoning finally crosses from interior to exterior because *she knows*; no compressed forgiveness; no road back; Vee ascendant, keeps the no, gives them nothing, leaves looking at the objects and not the people. Two reservations. First, "reckoning" here is still Vee's knowledge and Vee's exit — Pace loses *her*, Randi loses the friendship — but nothing in the *world* touches them. That's the natural terminus of the relationship, and I've decided it's enough, but only because Vee's ascendance is doing the moral work; if her ascendance is a consolation prize, it's not a reckoning, it's a breakup. Second, and larger: **reveal-by-her-own-authored-blindfold-threesome** is the single most dangerous mechanism available. The book has spent Book Two teaching Vee to *want* this and to *author* it herself (Secret Plans, the whole bench/cabin surrender arc). Spring the reveal there and you are one bad sentence away from "she asked for it" — grooming-arc-as-courtship with a bow on it. The packet's defenses are the right ones ("clean on its face, materially deceived," "no road back"). Whether they hold is pure execution I can't see.

5. **The curtain must land as dread, not plateau.** "Want named, deed unframed, truth hidden" has to close on the shape tightening — the same key as Volume One's coat-lining and cruiser — not on a happy domestic exhale.
