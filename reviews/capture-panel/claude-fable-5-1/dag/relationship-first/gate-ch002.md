Brooke. God. "The not-standing was the move." I've been in that room. Last night the one thing she couldn't do was pick her *please*; this morning she can't even pick her laugh. The book doesn't say that. Because I don't think it's about him at all. I think it's about what she did last night that she can't say no to, and she doesn't have the words yet, and neither does the book, and that's fine. I'm in.
