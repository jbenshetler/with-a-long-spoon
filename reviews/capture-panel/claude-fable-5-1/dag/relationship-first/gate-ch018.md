Forty pages of two people talking in a kitchen, except it's a dorm room with the window cracked two inches and a radiator that clicks. I'd have read it if it went twice as long.

What got me first was that Cassie already knew about the shirt. "You wore it to breakfast on Tuesday." Vee's been sleeping in it a week with the answer in her hands and she filed it under weird things about him and stopped looking, and Cassie — four words a day Cassie — had been watching her not look.
