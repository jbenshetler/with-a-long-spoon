The practice room, the shoes, the goodbye with one more beat in it every time, "I have always known what I'm doing" said flat to Pace two nights ago — all of it hers, all of it built. "God, I want her" into the wood of a couch, and now this. Pace said *I'll be careful with you*. Nobody's being careful with Vee, and now I'm not sure anybody's left who *can* be. Everyone in this book knows one thing the others don't, and this chapter added another, and it's Vee holding it. Vee. The one who's never held anything.
