The boot. God, the boot. The cherry pit undid me more than I expected. "We take nothing and leave nothing," and he picks it up, and then lays his own beside hers on the wax paper without looking. I know that voice she's got — the *common* word, the *no one taught you* word — I have one of my own, and I've been corrected by men who enjoyed the correcting.
