WHAT'S FADING

The three-step itself — back right, back right, back left, one-and-two three-four — is a rhythm now, not a thing I could teach. The couscous dinner is "she ate" and the chicken; I'd lose the asparagus if you asked tomorrow. The grandsupervisor and the chapter meeting about flowers are already gone to a feeling of him talking at the stove while she held out a half-full glass. The paint shape and the coastline have collapsed into "math is beautiful and he turned it toward her like light"; I couldn't tell you which was the small stuff. Her brother's branch — Army, I think. Which Sinatra, still "Witchcraft." Meg's shirt and Dana's boots are "borrowed, black, a keyhole." The Rusty Nail's name I have; the women at the rail are a hug.
