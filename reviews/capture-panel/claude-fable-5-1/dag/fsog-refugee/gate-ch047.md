She took the whole book's metaphor and handed it to him across a quilt. She'll never say the cracker sentence again. That's the line I'd text at one in the morning. She retired it. The thing she built for weeks, said once, and buried alive in her own body because he didn't catch it. The porch went gold and she didn't say anything else and I wanted to reach through the glass.
