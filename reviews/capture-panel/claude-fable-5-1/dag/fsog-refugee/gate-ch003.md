REACTION

Okay. Okay. I had to put the book face-down on my chest for a second after "Would you like to taste another woman?" — not because of what he asked, because of what her body did with it. She came *on the question*. Before she could decide what it meant. And then that line — he knew her body's answer, "He did not take it for her answer" — I actually read that twice. That's the thing. That is the exact thing I've been reading around for years and not finding: a man who can feel a yes in a woman and still doesn't treat it as the yes. He waits for the afternoon to go ordinary, he asks again when she's back in herself, and when she hands him the silk version — "only in a threesome, with you" — he sees straight through it and *lets her keep it*. Doesn't peel it off her. Doesn't make her say the uglier truer thing. I was braced for him to push, and he just said "Okay," and kissed her forehead. God.

And the pointing game — I was embarrassed for her for exactly as long as she was embarrassed, that first beat where he's pointing at a stranger in daylight and it's *wrong*, and then it resolved in me about when it resolved in her. "I point. You pick." The choosing was hers. I loved watching her stop being a good sport and start shopping. The kiss with her own taste still on his mouth, on the quad, in front of God and the frisbee people — that got me more than the bed did, honestly. The bed was quick and a little from his control tower; the kiss I felt.

But here's what I'd text you at 1 a.m.: I'm scared for Randi and he isn't. He's "quietly happy." He thinks the best idea he's had in a month is the day after she locked a bathroom door and decided, with a deadbolt sound, never to come out again. He's so attentive and he's being *handled*, and the one thing all that attention is missing is that the face came back on Saturday and hasn't come off. "Let's go feed you" nearly undid me — "I can't eat that," the automatic no, and he just clocks it and carries her toward food anyway — and that's the man I want her to let in. And she's not going to. Not without something breaking.

And then Vee. I knew from the jacket. I bought the book *because* of the jacket — I wanted to be ahead of her. But reading Randi fold a decade of dance into a three-second shoulder-bump so clean that the girl apologized — that shocked laugh with her head all the way back, "a person who had genuinely forgotten she could be seen" — and then Randi comes back grinning and says "Do you think she'll like the bench?" I went cold. That's the one line in three chapters where I felt the book might be winking. A girl who doesn't know she's been picked, and these two are already putting her on furniture. The only thing that held me was that everything *between the two of them* in this chapter was asked for. Every step. If they carry that over to her — if Vee gets the question and the room to answer, the way Randi did — I'll follow them anywhere. If she gets the collision and nothing else, I'm out, and I won't be gentle about it.

Also: three chapters in and the heroine on the jacket just said her name. I'm not mad — this was a very good prologue — but I want her chapter *now*.
