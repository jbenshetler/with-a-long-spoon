The bag with her half-life in it, the drawer's twin, left on the passenger seat. She knew in the driveway. I've been keeping a ledger of everything he's never said and that's the biggest line he's ever put on it, and I know something she doesn't, which is that the nice thing he found for the other woman on the couch was "extraordinary," and this is bigger than that.
