REACTION —

Oh, this one hurt in a totally different place. I came off that high of Friday night with Vee — like I was still glowing with her on Saturday morning — and walked straight into this cold kitchen and it was like getting the wind knocked out of me.

The bowl did me in. The small one, her apples bowl, and that one little line she draws through the top and then doesn't eat? I have *been* that girl at a table, and I've watched that girl, and I hated watching Randi do it to him. The pouring her own wine — god. That tiny detail felt louder than the whole fight. He pours for her, always, that's their thing, and her just grabbing the bottle by the neck... I felt him feel it.

And I'm mad at her and I also get it so bad it makes my stomach ache. She sat across from happy-morning-after Vee at brunch and had to smile and be the good girlfriend and listen to *our* night told back as Vee's romantic comedy, and then she drives out to him and he's just... glad. Like beaming-dad glad. Of course she snapped. "How long are you going to take?" — that wasn't about Vee being ready, that was *when do I get to stop being the girl in the hallway?*

The sex was the coldest thing we've had in this book and that's saying something. No inch-and-stop, no eyes on him, no *tell me* — I kept waiting for it the way he did, and when it didn't come I wanted to throw the book. She used him to get off and then turned her back and rolled up in the blanket like a little pillbug. And him just lying there behind her doing math on her body trying to make it add up? "He had all of it... and they would not go together" — yeah, baby, because she's not telling you the piece.

What gutted me most is the end. He covers her uneaten bowl with a plate and puts it in the fridge *for her.* Like she'll want it later. Like care is all he's got. I'm sitting here wanting to shake both of them — her for starving herself to punish him, him for thinking patience fixes everything — and also I can't look away. I need Vee to walk into this. I need the three of them in a room before someone breaks something that won't go back.
