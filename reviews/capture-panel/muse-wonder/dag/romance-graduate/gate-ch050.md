REACTION —

Okay, this is a quiet one and I know it's a quiet one, but I didn't resent it. Mostly because Vee in the middle of that sunny porch, with Meg and Kayla loud on either side of her, eating eggs like a normal twenty-one-year-old with friends — I needed to actually *see* that life before Pace walks into it on Wednesday. She's not just the girl in his house anymore. She has this. She rescues the morning, she knows a place, she orders the mimosas, she's the center and she knows she's the center and she likes it. That matters.

And God, the editing. I sat there watching her do it in real time and my stomach did the little thing. To Cassie it's "we talked" and a sweater that shrank in the dye. To these two it's the truth but *cut* — the coat, the flash, the laugh, him in the doorway, "it's late, is everything alright?" in that flat courtly voice, which — yes, that did get me, that's exactly him, I heard it. But not the drive. Not winding herself up twice and stopping on purpose. Not the cold wet boards or the pancakes or the knee on the floor or — obviously — Randi's heels in Randi's hands first. She told a story that was "exactly true" at the size she brought it, and she *knew* that, the book let me watch her know it, and that's the bit that kept me reading instead of skimming for Pace. She's getting so good at this. Fluent. Pleasurable, even. She took a sip and set the flute down and let Kayla's shriek do the rest of the scene for her so she never had to say one word about the drive. That's craft in a character, not just in the prose, and it's also kind of frightening. Every audience gets a version. Randi gets nearly everything. Cassie gets the cute lie. Meg and Kayla get the porch-story with the middle taken out. Who gets the whole thing? Nobody, including — I'm starting to think — Vee.

Meg. *Meg.* "Sometimes you have to make it right in their language" and the maple bacon and sitting through all the Matrix movies again for a man who brought her a heating pad and never took the bait. I laughed out loud, genuinely, and then it sat there a second too long, because that's the framing Vee is being handed and happily accepting: Friday night was *her apology*. Her grand gesture in his language. And it was, partly, she chose it, I've not forgotten the mirror and "last thing, only thing," that was hers. But he was the one who let her walk out in December rather than spend one cheap word, and somehow the story at the brunch table is the girl flashing her way back. Nobody at that table knows enough to push back on it, and Vee isn't pushing back on it herself, and that December question — *what am I to you out there* — is still sitting unanswered under all this butter and light. I notice I'm the only one at the table keeping hold of it.

The one moment I snagged on, honestly: she floats "is that okay, it's a little expensive," she's got Christmas money earmarked, this is her treat, her rescue, her occasion — and then the check comes and she asks for it split three ways and they all tap their phones deliberating tip buttons. It's a tiny thing and it's probably just how friends do it, nobody letting anybody cover anybody, fine. But it dinged because Pace's dinner is the opposite — "he's handling it, it's his treat, all of it, he was very clear" — and she *liked* saying that, I heard how much she liked it. His money gets to be a whole gesture. Hers gets split three ways at a terminal. I don't know if the book is doing that on purpose. I suspect it is, which is why I'm still here.

Wednesday, though. Wednesday is the thing. Two days. He's picking the place — a place he can hear in, I remember, tables have ends — and he's putting Cassie and Randi in the same room with Meg's level stare and Theo-who-once-asked-her-out possibly at the table, and Vee just announced it in the middle of breakfast like it's a fun plan and not a lit fuse. Kayla is already planning hair. Randi hasn't even heard yet, not in front of me — Tuesday with Randi comes first, and now Tuesday isn't just the coat debrief, it's Vee telling her she's been seated at Pace's dinner as "friend." I want that conversation maybe more than I want the dinner.

So: a bridge, yes, I'll call it one, but a bridge with Vee standing in the middle of it editing her own life at brunch while the ground is being wired. One quiet chapter. I know my own rule about the second one. Wednesday better be coming.
