REACTION — God, I loved her in this room and it also made my stomach drop a little.

Not because anything bad happened — because I got to watch what Vee does with the truck when it's not Randi she's telling. To Meg and Kayla she skips the middle. No unbuttoning her wet shirt while he watched. No his cold, shaking hand on her belly under his flannel. She turns it into a cute almost-kiss interrupted by a cop, *I wasn't going to kiss a man about to go hypothermic,* ha ha, next fry. And I get it — I get it completely, some things you don't hand across a dining hall table — but I've already seen her let Randi talk her out of *I made him stop, I kissed him, me* and into *because of him*, and now here's another version, cleaner, funnier, more tellable. She's learning how to edit the parts where she was the one wanting. That scares me for her more than Pace inviting her over does.

Because the rest of her here? She's incandescent. Laughing with her whole body, stealing fries, doing his voice — *my writing's no good after that* — so proud of him she can't even pretend not to be. Twelve days. She didn't have to count. Of course she didn't have to count. I have been that girl pretending twelve days is "a couple weeks" like vagueness will make it sound less obsessed.

And Meg saying, so gently, *everybody's different at the start. I said it about Danny. Word for word* — that just slid right off her. Didn't catch on anything. That's exactly right and exactly terrifying. Vee isn't stupid, she's *in* it, and Pace asking what she wants and then actually waiting for the real answer has rewired something in her that "he's different" can't even describe, so of course a warning phrased as a cliché can't reach her.

Then that text. *Would you come to my house Saturday. Two o'clock.* That's it. That's the whole message from this courtly, exact man who says *May I* and *don't answer tonight* and leaves the last inch every single time — and it works on me exactly like it works on her. I read it twice too. Two o'clock is not a meal, Kayla is right, there is no meal at two, and he's never had her through his door, and she has his shirt folded on her pillow *right now* while she's sitting there with her heart going stupid under the table. First time at his house, no occasion given, no what-to-wear, nothing to prepare with except wanting. After the bench in chapter one and that table I know is in that house and she doesn't — oh, I am not calm about Saturday.

I also noticed I trust these two friends. Meg with her fork set down like punctuation, Kayla announcing hair on Saturday to the entire gold dining hall. They're not Cassie — nobody's said the plain dangerous thing — but they're warm and they're *hers* and they see the wanting on her face before she can arrange it. I don't want her world to narrow down to Randi's high table and Pace's house. Keep these girls.
