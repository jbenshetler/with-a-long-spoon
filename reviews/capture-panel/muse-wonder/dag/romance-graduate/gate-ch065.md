REACTION

Okay, that was — wait. That was *it*? I turned the page expecting the room to keep going and it just... stopped on the cereal box. I've never had a chapter do that to me before. Two pages. I sat there for a second actually annoyed, like, excuse me, you cannot ask me *Is this what you want?* and then hand the girl Cocoa Pebbles and leave.

And then it landed. Because of course that's the only question she can answer.

Cassie. God. I love her more every time she refuses to play the game Vee lays out for her. Vee came in ready to do the whole sorority-thing, she's-like-that-with-everyone stack of words — I could hear her building the cover story in real time, I've watched her get so good at that filing system — and Cassie just steps *over* it. Doesn't argue about Randi, doesn't ask what happened, doesn't take the bait about not liking her. Just: is this what you want?

And Vee *looks*. That's the part that got me in my chest. She actually tries to answer honestly, because Cassie asked it so plainly, and she hits the edge of something huge and steps back off it so fast. Her neck is still saying yes — that detail, her staying aware of it like a burn, convinced it must be visible on her skin like a postmark, that is so exactly it. That feeling that everyone can see it on you. Her body has been voting all night, all *month*, and she keeps insisting the body doesn't count, it's just skin, just static, just ticklish. The question has "no floor under it she was willing to stand on" — I had to stop and reread that. That's her whole life right now in one sentence.

I also — the deflection to "you don't like her" stung because I recognized it. That's what you do when someone who loves you asks the one clean question and you'd rather have the old familiar fight than the new terrifying answer. And Cassie knows it, and she doesn't punish her for it. She just feeds her. *You didn't have dinner. Do you want some of my Cocoa Pebbles?* After all the Chambord nobody can taste and the forty-dollar bottles poured into a tub and Randi knowing the price of everything in the air — here's a want the size of a bowl. Childish cereal at god knows what hour in a cold dorm room, offered without a single condition attached. No extracting a confession first. No inch-by-inch toll like Pace charged her, which — I haven't forgotten that, or that she *liked* paying it. Cassie just holds out the box.

It's strange, for a chapter with basically nothing happening in it, my heart was going the whole time. Maybe because it's the first time someone has asked Vee the question straight, without wanting anything from the answer. Pace asked *do you want to sleep with a woman* and stepped around the name like a gentleman and she walked straight into the room he left her. Randi never asks at all, she just arranges. Cassie asks and then lets the silence sit there to see what Vee puts in it, and Vee puts nothing, and Cassie lets *that* be the answer for tonight.

I don't know if the shortness is a trick or not. Part of me feels a little cheated — I was braced for the big reckoning and got a bowl of cereal. But I keep thinking about her standing there in her coat, unable to even take it off, saying *yeah* fast and whole to the one want small enough to hold in two hands, while the other question stays open on the desk. That image is going to stay with me longer than a longer chapter might have. I just wish I trusted it was deliberate and not the book catching its breath. Saturday brunch is circled now and I have no idea what face she's going to wear into it, and neither does she, and apparently neither of us gets to find out tonight.
