REACTION

Okay, this one got under my skin in a way I wasn't expecting from what is — let's be honest — mostly Vee retelling a date I already watched happen.

Because with Cassie? She knows exactly what happened. *I stopped. I made him stop. I kissed him. Me.* She spins in the dorm room, she throws a pillow, she's lit up and clumsy and completely in possession of herself. "I did it because I wanted to and I did it on purpose." I loved her so much in that scene. That's the girl from the pool, the girl who goes under the water where nobody wants anything from her. Unguarded. Certain.

And then Randi gets her alone for forty responsible-free minutes and just... gently re-edits her.

It's so small I almost missed it the first time through my own feelings about it. *You let him choose for you. And you liked it.* Not a question. *Because you couldn't not. Because of him.* And Vee had the true version right there — I heard her say it to Cassie, I heard her say "I'm making an exception" in the actual moment — and she trades it in for Randi's smaller, truer-sounding version because Randi says it soft and tender, like she's agreeing with something Vee hasn't said yet. That made my stomach drop. That's not a friend hyping you up. That's someone teaching you how to remember yourself.

Same with Cassie getting tidied out of the frame. "It isn't really her place, the smoothie one. All the—" and that little hand gesture, and Vee comforting *herself* with "it's fine to have your own people" while something turns over underneath that she lets lie. I wanted to shake her. Not because she's stupid — she's not, she's painfully perceptive about rooms and people — but because being chosen feels so good to her that she'll do the excluding herself and call it healthy. Randi doesn't even have to push. She just opens the door and Vee walks through it smiling.

And Brooke. God. The whole Smoothie Bird scene — Vee up on that high chair with her feet not reaching the floor, in the pilled cardigan, feeling herself go "the wrong size for the room" — I felt that in my body. And then *Vee's my dear friend* with the hand held down on the table in plain view, and Vee experiencing it as being rescued, being claimed, somebody finally spending social capital on her out loud. Which it is. It's also a flag being planted in front of exactly the two women whose opinion counts. Both things. At the same time. That's what's making me a little feral about this book — nobody's doing just one thing.

Pace barely appears in this chapter and I still felt him all through it, which is either very good writing or I'm already gone on him. Probably both. "May I choose?" hitting *again*, secondhand, through Vee's mouth, and landing completely differently on Randi than it did at that table. Randi hears the whole soft night — stopped keeping track of herself, stopped watching herself — and her smile goes up a whole notch. I noticed that. I'm supposed to notice that.

The hiking talk should have been the hot part, honestly — hair down, mountain man, ravish against a tree, and Vee's quiet "He'd ask, though. Before he ravished me" is somehow the most erotic line in the chapter, that certainty she has about him already. But what stayed with me wasn't heat. It was Vee, half up an imaginary trail, with Randi's voice dropping down to match hers for the first time all day. *Ask and then ravish.* A beat late. Low. Like she'd followed her there.

I'm suspicious of everyone and I trust Pace and Cassie, and those two lists worry me because Randi is exactly the person who'd benefit from me trusting that fast.
