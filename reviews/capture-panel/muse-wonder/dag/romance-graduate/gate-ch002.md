REACTION

Okay so that was *not* what I expected to get after that bench scene, and I mean that in the good, kind of unsettling way.

Chapter 1 left me braced for either afterglow or a blowup, and instead I'm in a sorority common room at 9:40 on a Saturday morning watching Brooke smile Randi to death in under four minutes. And I felt it in my stomach the whole time. Not because anything "happened" — nobody raised their voice, nobody even stood up — but because I *know* that room. I've been the girl coming in in last night's clothes trying to make my face do normal, and I've been on the couch pretending not to watch. Brooke never asking a real question while asking every real question? *Pace. That's actually his name?* The little downgrade to engineering, then fishing to see if he's her TA, then "cute — like short-cute?" Jesus. That's not manufactured BookTok conflict, that's social murder with perfect teeth.

What got me is Randi. Last night she was literally undone — that real *please* that clearly scared the shit out of her — and this morning I watched the face click back on a fraction of a second late, over and over. Her laugh matching Kinsey's before she's even chosen it. Saying "He's hard to say no to" in her head in a completely different key and then just... not being able to follow the thought any further. That stopped me cold.

And then her room. The not-sitting, not-reading the phone, opening the closet and forgetting why, lying on top of a made bed in her shoes and cardigan under a duvet and *not* crying — not bravely holding it in, just there being no door to where the crying is. That's the part I would text someone about at 1 a.m. Because I've read a hundred heroines who have a big cry and a shower and decide to be empowered by breakfast. This felt truer and worse. She got seen last night in a way she cannot afford to be seen, and now she's back in the house where being seen wrong costs her everything, and she's just... shutting the whole interior room down again.

Is it hot? No. This chapter is cold rooms and cereal bowls. But I'm not bored, and that's the test for me right now. The writing isn't showing off, it's just noticing exactly the right half-second. I do feel the risk though — if the next chapter is another quiet room where she doesn't cry and doesn't call him and nothing charged moves, I'm going to start skimming. One bridge chapter after *that* scene, I will happily follow. I need to know what she does with this hum in her ribs before it turns into wallpaper.

I don't trust Brooke for one second, I don't think Randi is safe in that house at all, and I'm weirdly protective of her in a way I wasn't yesterday when she was just the pretty sorority girl on Pace's lap.
