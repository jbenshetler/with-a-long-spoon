REACTION

Okay, so this is the date chapter, and it's lovely. It's also the one where I realize I've stopped being on Vee's side and started being on Vee's side *against the book*. That's the point, I think, and it works.

The restaurant is the best sustained thing since the bench. "May I choose?" lands exactly how the jacket promised: she hands the choice across and the relief is erotic in itself. The nine words, seven of them food. The soft unnamed dishes as "closed doors with something behind it." The bread, the drip of baba ghanoush off his hand to his mouth, and she loses the thread of the conversation and nothing bad happens. That's a girl being fed, and I felt the hollow go all the way down. It rhymes with Randi's apple slices, cut side up, and I don't think that's an accident. Pace feeds both women. Whatever he is, he's good at hunger.

And I'm uneasy in exactly the right way. Everything he does is lovely and every bit of it is *measured*. The right hand always, never the left, "a rule he kept without seeming to think." The hand he holds out "no ceremony, no hurry." The mint tea in the brass cages. And the line that sat in my stomach: "it felt less like something beginning than something resolving." She's being fitted. She even says she lost the thread and "let him carry it. It hadn't occurred to her that she could." That's the happiest sentence in the chapter and also the most frightening. He asked her not to answer, and she answered anyway, and that's the one place she steered, and I love her for it.

The kiss is the real turn. "I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days," then standing there with the taut hand and choosing the exception. He leaves the last inch where it is, and she crosses it. Of course he does. He gives her the first kiss small and waits, so the second one is hers to take, and the choosing is the erotic part, same as the jacket. It's very good, and it's very hot in a clothed, mint-in-both-mouths way. I wanted more of it, and I'm aware that wanting more is what the book is doing to me too.

Where's Randi? Gone entirely, except in the grid of the thing, and I missed her. Vee mentioning Carole King and "I'd love to" with no thought of the girl who steered her here. The ending has her glowing, and I know what that kiss costs the one watching from the other side of this. I want to see Randi's face when she hears. That's what the next chapter has to be.

Complaints, small: the narration still explains ("the old reflex, the part of her that..."), and the lobby paragraph stretches the "he showered, he dressed for her" beat past what it needs. "Beautiful nonsense" with the menu was twee. But it earns its pace. Third dinner-and-feelings chapter-ish stretch isn't a problem yet because the kiss pays.
