The kiss beat is the best thing here. "I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days," then the hand pulling taut, then "I'm making an exception." Then the small kiss, then her taking the bigger one herself. That's consent as foreplay, which is what the blurb promised. It made my stomach go.

But here's my real feeling. I now know exactly what Pace and Randi are doing, because I watched them plan it, and Vee's sweetness is dramatic irony that has teeth. Every lovely thing he does lands doubled: is it real, or is it technique? The book knows I'm asking. That's the hook, and it's a good one. I'm also suspicious of how perfect he is. He's never wrong, and he's never awkward.

The cost is that this is the second or third quiet, near-chaste chapter running (froyo, the pool, the coffee shop, now dinner), and the only heat is the one kiss. Chapter 1 spent me, and since then it's been all wanting and no payoff. I can feel the payoff coming, so I'm not skimming, but I'm close to the line. The prose is doing real work, but a few lines explain her feelings to me ("it felt less like something beginning than something resolving"), which I don't love.

I'd text my friend: "He's going to ruin her and it's going to be so good. Also where is Randi."
