REACTION

Okay, this is the first chapter in a while where I felt the book *breathe*, and I hate how much I liked it. It's a bridge chapter. Ch23 was Vee's wreck and this is the repair. But it's a bridge that happens to be a dress-up scene with a woman kneeling under a long skirt, and I did not mind.

The shoe fitting is where it lived. Vee can't see any of it because the skirt covers it. She only feels hands, a strap closing, a thumb in the hollow behind her knee. That's a smart way to write it. It's the best heat in the chapter, and nobody takes anything off. "Being given no say in where her foot went made her surer of it." Yes. That line is the whole book, and it's also the whole trap, and the book knows it.

Randi is quietly doing damage. She turns Vee's jealous meltdown into "you're in this." She says "Not today," which sounds like wise advice and is also just control. Then she picks the outfit, the shoes, the scarf and the script for Pace's makeup scene. She's also steering the apology, and she's Pace's lover. I read every "he won't text you, he leaves you the room" line as someone who knows exactly what he'll do. The "Evil Queen" joke made me wince. So did "shoe whore" and the "sensible ones you tell yourself you bought for the style." That one was brutal and loving at once.

Vee still files the elongated "Sleep, gorgeous" "across an ocean." I'm screaming. She didn't brace for the kiss and she noticed that she didn't. The reader is two steps ahead of her and it's delicious and awful.

Edna Mode was a genuinely fun beat, and the laugh did real work for the friendship. A couple of lines are a touch over-written, like "the cold went through her like a verdict." I notice, but I don't care much.

I'm still waiting on Randi and Vee in an actual room with heat, and this teased it hard. Another quiet chapter might test me, but this one earned its keep. I'm reading on.
