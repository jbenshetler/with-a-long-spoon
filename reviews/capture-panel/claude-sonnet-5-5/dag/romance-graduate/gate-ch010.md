REACTION

Okay. This one worked on me in a way I didn't expect, and not because it was hot. It's a hiking chapter, and I was mostly gripped by the boot.

Pace kneeling to fix her blister while her mother's voice screamed in her head was the best moment. The moleskin, the relacing, and "I'm sorry, I didn't think to ask about the boots." He keeps taking care of her without making her small, and the cherry pits are the same move. The dress is another one. She says the russet silk out loud, he says only "that sounds like a beautiful dress," and he never picks it up again. I clocked it as a Chekhov's dress immediately. If it shows up later, I'm lost.

The truck scene is where the chapter earns its place. She strips on purpose, in her mother's voice, and she's choosing it. Then she reaches for his hand and finds him shaking. That reversal got me. The would-be seduction turns into "you're freezing," and the heat turns into tenderness. It's a real turn in the bond. It's also the second chapter of Vee where the heat is all anticipation. I wanted more than a stripped shirt and a sheer bra. But the restraint is doing something here. I trust that he asks first, and I want to see what happens when he finally does.

The siren cutting her off mid-"Pace—" is a cheap beat. I know exactly what it is. I'll forgive it because the series trained me to love "the couple apart."

What's in my head now is the structure. We know, from Chapter 1 and the jacket, that Randi and Pace are running this. Vee is still calling Randi her dear friend. The dramatic irony is eating me. Pace being this decent while he's in a game he started in bed makes him more worrying, not less. "Every yes was freely given" is a threat at this point. I'm uneasy about him in a way I enjoy.

I'm also a little tired of the quiet chapters. This is the third gentle Vee chapter in a row, with no explicit scene since Chapter 3. I'm giving it credit for the prose and for the flannel, but I need payoff soon.
