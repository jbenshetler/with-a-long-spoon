REACTION

Oh, this is the chapter where the book makes me pay for the last one. Ch31 was the best thing in the volume. Vee at the table going molten while Randi watched, the bathroom, the pastry glaze on her lip. Then this opens with Vee telling Pace about Randi, and I wanted it to be the other half of that loop. It isn't, quite. It's a very soft chapter, and I felt the air go out.

What works is the dramatic irony. Vee tells Pace all about "a new friend," the accent, the funny goodbye kiss, and he mimes the French cheek kisses. He plays dumb in his own kitchen. I know he knows, and she doesn't, and she's even teasing him about "that kind of French." That's the only spark of heat or menace here. It's good. But the chapter doesn't do anything with it. He doesn't react and nothing leaks. It sits there.

The "I love him" beat is fine. "Peter," whispered to a sleeping man, is the book's best line in the chapter. It's a little sad and a little stupid, and that's exactly her. But "he never said it, so I'll count the salmon" is the same move she's made for thirty chapters. I'm exasperated, because the jacket already told me that's the engine.

The drawer ending is the sweetest thing Pace has done. He didn't say a word and gave her the easy drawer and folded himself into the hard one. I did go "aw." And I distrust it at the same moment, because he gives her everything but the word. It's a gift that doubles as a way not to answer. Good, but it's a small chapter ending on a small note.

The heat is the problem. It's a recap of a hot night ("the couch, unhurried") and nothing on the page. After ch31's bathroom this reads as the closed door I hate. Randi is absent. This is the second quiet chapter running if I count the gentle ending of ch31, and I'm already restless. The weight-loss paragraph also snagged me. She's eating "like a field hand" and the bands are loose, and it's possibly a flag the book wants me to notice, but it's dropped just as fast.

I'll go on, mostly because of the bathroom and the irony. But the book needs to put Randi and Vee in a room soon.
