REACTION

I'm a little deflated, and I'm not sure the book meant me to be. I've been waiting for the induction since ch14. I expected it to break there, with the dress walking into a room, Randi and Pace both circling it, and the game starting to buckle. What I got was a short, solitary, nearly pleasant chapter. The best thing in it is the walk. Vee takes the first step small, decides on the second, and lets the slit open on a bare leg while a dull room wakes up. That's a good beat, and the line about the murmur is lovely. The photo is good too: "only she would ever see it there." Sending the uncropped frame to Pace in Berkeley, with all that hidden charge in it, is exactly the dramatic irony I came for.

But it's also nearly weightless. Nobody is in the room who matters. Randi is absent, Pace is absent, and her mother isn't there either. The heat is entirely internal and clothed, a 1 or maybe a 2. I like that she's doing it for herself now, and I like that she's wearing the shame like a coat and choosing not to hide. It reads like a victory lap with no one watching, though. Cassie's "You look—" is the best line, the standing ovation.

What's bugging me is the structure. The last chapter was a practice-room scene where Randi told Vee about being spanked and Vee fled to a bathroom. This one is a reflective, mostly alone set-piece. Two softer chapters in a row, and I'm at the edge of "has it stalled?" The thing I'm owed is Randi and Vee in a room with the heat, and Vee still hasn't named what the spanking means. I'm also waiting on the credit card, the Pace and Randi lies, and Cassie finding out. The ending is calm, with a camera-ready image and no hook.

I'm still holding on because the prose is good and I trust this book to pay off. But my thumb doesn't hurry.
