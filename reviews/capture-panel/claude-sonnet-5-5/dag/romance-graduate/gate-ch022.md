REACTION

Well. That's a hard left turn, and I think I'm glad of it, but I'm braced.

Two quiet chapters in a row, then. Ch21 was Pace and Randi in bed, and this one is Vee and Pace talking under a quilt. The difference is that this one is the best two-people-talking scene I've had in a while, so I'm not skimming. The cumin and the unopened saffron are funny and exact. "Whatever was in it may have been old enough to vote" made me snort. Then he goes "affectionate" and I felt it land, because he goes for the hand under the thing. The paint-and-coastline monologue could have been a nerd-boy lecture. Instead it comes out as grief and love, and he's the only man in this book who makes math sound like sex. The line about the poem and most people never reading a line of it nearly got me.

Then Daphne. Nineteen and fifteen, and he calls it "the best two years I'd had" and says "nobody did anything wrong." Vee's reaction to the arithmetic is the one I'd have had: she holds the word under her tongue and lets him have his story. This is the first time Pace has been a person to me and not a smug surveillance machine. It also explains him. He was chosen at fifteen by someone older, and he loved it, and now he's the one doing the choosing. That's a hell of a mirror for "every yes was freely given." I'm putting that together without the book telling me, which is what I want.

The scar under his hand, "Not tonight," a door with a time on it. Good. I want that. The lifting-as-presence line is the one place where Vee explains the subtext to him a bit. It's a little on the nose, but it works because she's discovering it.

Then the ending, which wrecked me in the intended way. Vee is lying there so happy and so grateful, calling Randi "her glamorous friend," with "she had stumbled into her," and the reader knows the whole thing was a setup. "She had never been so fortunate." Oh, honey. And after ch21, where Randi lay on him describing Vee, that dramatic irony is vicious.

Heat is low, though. There's a hand on a breast and a bare hip, but it's all warm and no charge. I came for heat and I'm getting two chapters of tenderness. I'll allow it, because it's earning its keep, but I'll want the induction or Randi and Vee soon.
