REACTION

Oh, this is a cold-water chapter, and I mean that as a compliment, mostly. I came off ch 11 with Randi leaving his bed without staying, Pace standing in his own doorway with that low plain ache, and I wanted to know what that did to her. Instead I get Vee at a dining hall table with two girls I've never met. Kayla and Meg? Where is Cassie? Vee told her best friend's story to *new people*, and the one who tells the truth in every scene isn't at the table. That's the thing that stuck in me. "Goodbye, Vee" is getting louder.

But Vee herself is a delight here. Loud, laughing with her whole body, easy in a way I haven't seen since the early chapters. The shirt story getting better in the telling, and she knows it. "Twelve days," and she didn't have to count. Her hand gripping her own arm while she tries to explain that a man asking what she wants for dinner is wrecking her. Yes. That's the whole book's engine and she says it better than the narrator does. And Meg's "Sometimes they even are" almost lands, then "It didn't catch on anything." I felt that one.

What I'm itchy about is the dramatic irony getting thick. Pace's invitation is a bare ransom note, and we know there's a plan behind it, and Randi isn't even in the room this time. Eleven chapters and Vee and Pace still haven't been behind a closed door. Saturday, two o'clock, his house. I want it badly. I also notice she told them *he asked her about the sandwich order*. Nobody's pushing. Good.

Also, the chlorine and gardenia bit from last chapter is still in my head. Randi was in that bed with Vee's smell, and I'm worried about what she's carrying. This chapter doesn't touch it. The gap between Randi and the page is getting long.
