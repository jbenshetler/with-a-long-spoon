REACTION

I feel sick. I mean that in the best, most thrilling, utterly horrified way possible—my stomach is in knots. This was diabolical. Absolute, surgical cruelty masquerading as the sweetest, most tender campus meet-cute imaginable.

Watching the trap snap shut in real time while Vee radiates pure, uncomplicated joy made me want to scream through the page. The coordination between Randi and Pace! Randi moving them to "the place on Wilson"—the exact coffee shop she lied to Brooke about going to in Chapter Two—and then texting under the table with that quick, absorbed focus. *That was Pace.* She was literally feeding him the coordinates, telling him when to walk in, setting Vee up like a bowling pin.

And then Pace’s entrance. Jesus Christ, Pace. The sheer psychopathic discipline of this man. He walks over, completely ignores Randi—giving her that dismissive little stranger-nod, treating her, as Cassie brilliantly points out, "the way you'd look at somebody's great-aunt"—and zeroes in on Vee. And the way he seduces her isn't with swagger or pickup lines; it's with *attention*. Grown-up, spacious, courtly attention. He rolls his sleeves. He doesn't take her pencil or talk down to her; he gives her Euler’s formula, gives her back her own agency, lets her solve the problem herself. It’s the exact intellectual version of what he did on the walnut bench: finding where she’s stuck, giving her just enough structure, and making her believe *she* chose it.

And Randi sitting right there! Randi holding her coffee cup, taking phantom sips, asking that tiny math question just to be acknowledged, and watching her lover systematically charm another woman using the exact techniques he used on her. You could see the hairline fractures in Randi’s composure: *"Randi's hand went to her coffee cup, held it with both hands a second longer than the sip required, then set it down."* Randi engineered this entire circus to keep Pace’s devastating spotlight off herself, and now that the spotlight is blinding Vee, Randi is already choking on the reality of it. "He wants to see you naked." God, Randi, you shoved him into her arms and you’re already bleeding.

Then the frozen yogurt. That crushed me. Vee stacking her cup too high without her mother’s voice shaming her for once, confessing that this is the nicest anyone has been to her in a long time... and Pace gently absorbing it with *"Your tart's holding up."* It’s so tender it breaks your heart, because you know every millimeter of this sweetness was designed in a bedroom between two people who intend to put her on an interrogation bench.

The psychological weight Helen Rivers is building here is staggering. Not a stitch of clothing came off, but the emotional voyeurism and predatory intimacy made it burn hotter than most full-blown sex scenes. I am dying for the next chapter.
