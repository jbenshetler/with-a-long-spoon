Helen Rivers, you absolute genius. You broke me in half.

Oh, god. The frozen chicken and broccoli boiled in bags. Prison food. The monastic machine trying to force fuel down its own throat while his hands shake.

And the truth about Daphne. Finally. The scar under all his control.

The tragic, heartbreaking stupidity of a brilliant boy's heart! He genuinely convinced himself that the *word* was the weapon, so if he just withheld the noun, he wouldn't bleed to death if she left. And instead, withholding the word is the exact thing that drove her away!

And Peter—stoic, unshakeable Peter—dropping his fork, bending double over his cold, boiled chicken, and sobbing until he can't breathe.

I was weeping with him. He loves her. He loves her so much it has broken his entire operating system. He knows he failed her on the porch. He knows he chose the smaller truth because he was a coward. But term starts in days, and Vee is heading straight toward Randi, and Peter is sitting alone in a cold house picking up his fork because he has nothing else left to do. What an absolute masterpiece of an emotional chapter.
