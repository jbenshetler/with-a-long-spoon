God, my heart aches for Vee. Absolutely aches.

The contrast between the predatory, calculated intimacy of the last chapter—Randi straddling Pace on the couch, demanding every scrap of Vee’s afternoon like a voyeuristic drug, while Pace kept Vee’s secret tucked away in his pocket—and this chapter’s quiet, aching humanity is stunning.

Seeing Vee at the pool broke me open a little bit. The sheer *labor* of having a lavish, lush body in a world of college boys who yell out gross, entitled garbage from the bleachers (*"You won't need floaties with those!"*), and how she has to deploy a game face just to walk twenty feet to the water. And then that moment she sinks under the surface: *"Nobody up there could see her. Nobody down here wanted anything. She had loved this before she had a body to be a problem, and the water was the one part of it that had never been taken."* That sentence is so breathtakingly sad and true. She’s twenty-one years old and her own physical form has felt like public property, an inconvenience, or an object of maternal shame her whole life. The water is the only place where she gets to just *be*.

And Cassie! God, I love Cassie so much. Cassie is the anchor Vee doesn’t even realize she’s going to desperately need when this whole thing blows up. Cassie standing chest-deep in the water like a fence, waiting for Vee to surface with her sad face, and instantly splashing her to snap her out of it—not interrogating, not demanding, just being a true, unpretentious, fiercely loyal friend.

Then we get Vee in the locker room, steaming from the shower, turning Pace over in her head. And *of course* he asked her to dinner and then told her not to answer tonight. *Of course he did.* The jacket said it from the start: *“Pace sees her completely. Courtly and exact, he asks before he takes, until choosing becomes the most erotic thing she has ever done.”* He is already training her nervous system. He’s deliberately withholding the immediate gratification, forcing her to sit in the wanting, making the *yes* build up in her until it's aching and heavy.

And it’s working! She’s sitting on that locker room bench in a damp towel, shivering, thinking about his thick knuckles and wondering what else he does with his hands. She is completely, utterly charmed by this man who treats her with deliberate, spacious reverence—and she has no idea that the dinner she's about to say yes to was conceived in a bedroom on a walnut spanking bench. The tragedy of how pure her feelings are against the machinery running underneath them is just masterclass writing.
