REACTION

Oh, Cassie. Thank god for Cassie. In a book full of people speaking in intoxicating half-sentences and setting exquisitely choreographed psychological traps, Cassie is just sitting in a bean bag with the window cracked two inches, being a solid wall of real life.

There is something so genuinely sweet, and completely terrifying, about watching Vee try to explain what’s happening to her. When she tries to talk about her past boyfriends and all she has is the word *fine*—God, I felt that in my chest. That’s the exact thing that made Fifty Shades hit so hard back in the day, that contrast between the boring, lukewarm, take-it-or-leave-it stuff and a man who looks at you like you are the only object in the entire universe. Pace cooks for her, he fusses over a tiny burn from a hot tomato on her bare breast like she’s made of porcelain, he clears out his own drawer so she has a place. It looks like absolute devotion. It feels like the safest, most tender kind of worship.

And then Cassie drops the anchor: *“You don’t come home school nights.”*

It’s such a simple, quiet reality check, but it hit me like a slap. Because Cassie isn't jealous or cruel; she’s just marking the territory Vee is giving away without noticing. Vee thinks she's just being loved better than she's ever been loved, but her actual life—her bed, her routines, her classes—is quietly dissolving around the edges. She’s being enclosed.

And when Cassie asks her, flat-out, *“Do you love him,”* Vee’s sudden retreat into “Maybe... I think so... It’s all so fast” broke my heart a little. In the quiet of his bed a chapter ago, she could admit it to herself in the dark: *She loved him.* But here, under the fluorescent dorm lights in front of a girl who actually knows her, the truth sounds dizzy and dangerous even to her own ears. It *is* fast. It’s breathtakingly fast. I love the warmth between her and Pace so much, and the tenderness is completely real to me, but the fact that Cassie is the only person holding up a mirror to the sheer velocity of this makes my stomach tight. I need to see what happens when that bubble gets pricked.
