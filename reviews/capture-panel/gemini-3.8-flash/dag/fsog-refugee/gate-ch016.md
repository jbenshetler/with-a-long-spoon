That balance—her agency rising up to meet his restraint—is pure perfection.

I don't care. I am so utterly hooked. The banter, the warmth, the psychological precision—I could read ten chapters of them just talking in this dorm room, but God, I want that dress to be finished. I want to see what happens when she puts it on.
