REACTION

God, my heart aches for her. The sheer, dizzy, spinning-in-a-dorm-room joy of being twenty-one and falling headlong into a man who actually *waited* for you. The way she has to defend it to Cassie: *"Nobody did anything to me. I wasn't talked into it. I did it because I wanted to and I did it on purpose."*

That line hit me right in the chest, because she's so proud of it. She has spent her life being managed, being scrutinized, having boys push past her boundaries or having to perform for them, and here she got to hold the steering wheel for once. She thinks she broke the rules; she thinks she chose him. And the terrible, beautiful, sickening thing is that she *did* choose it—her wanting is completely real, completely clean, completely hers—and yet every single inch of that path was cleared, paved, and lit by Pace and Randi before she ever set foot on it.

I love Cassie so much in this chapter. She’s the anchor. She’s the girl who doesn’t get swept up in the Greek-row shine, who asks the blunt, protective questions (*"How much did he give you to drink?"* and *"What's his name, so I know what to tell the state troopers"*). Cassie represents the real world, the safe world, the world where a girl watches out for her friend. And Vee is already floating so far above it that an address like "off six-fourteen, past where it goes to gravel" sounds like romantic poetry instead of an isolated house in the woods with a custom-built spanking bench sitting under a false tabletop.

It’s a short chapter, a quiet landing after the date, but it does exactly what it needs to do. It lets Vee's happiness breathe so that the trap feels all the more heartbreakingly tender. He's taking her hiking. We already know from earlier that hiking is one of his "unnamed things." Every step she takes into his world feels like her own idea, and I am desperately, hopelessly hooked on watching it unfold.
