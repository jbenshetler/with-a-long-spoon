REACTION

Oh, this broke my heart. It completely, utterly broke my heart.

Just a few chapters ago, in chapter 41, she was so proud of herself for washing that shirt and asking him to wear it for the weekend so it would smell like him again. It felt so tender, like such a sweet, intimate little ritual of young love—him putting on two shirts in his own kitchen just because she asked. And now, to watch that exact same talisman turn to ash in her hands in the middle of the night? That hurt so much to read.

"The heat in the shirt was her own, given back. He wasn't in it." God. That line knocked the wind right out of me. Because that is the exact, brutal reality of where she finds herself. He gives her everything—he feeds her like a queen, he worships her body, he lets her unravel him in bed, he listens to her, he adjusts for her—but he will not give her the word. He won't say *I love you*, he won't say *girlfriend*, he won't give a name to what they are. And without that name, all the exquisite tenderness in the world suddenly feels like a cage, or worse, an illusion she’s maintaining entirely on her own heat.

I felt so suffocated with her in that top bunk. That awful feeling of hovering your thumb over a blank text field, knowing there is literally nothing you can type that won't either sound pathetic or shrink the massive, aching question into something polite and easily deflected. You can't text someone: *Are you my life, or am I just an erotic project to you?* Because deep down, the fact that you even have to ask already gives you the answer you're terrified of.

And then taking the shirt off—stripping completely bare in a freezing dorm room just to punish herself with the cold, leaving his beloved shirt balled up in a heap like garbage—is such a sharp, agonizingly real physical manifestation of heartbreak. She’s finally waking up to the asymmetry. She loves him with her whole entire soul, she has handed over every scrap of her pride, and he’s across town sleeping like a baby because he’s in complete control.

I’m terrified for what happens next. When a girl gets this cold and this hollowed out, she either breaks down and demands the truth, or she runs straight into the arms of the other person circling her—and Randi is right there waiting.
