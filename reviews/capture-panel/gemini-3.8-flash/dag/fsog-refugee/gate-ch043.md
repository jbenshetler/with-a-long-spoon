The intimacy between Pace and Randi at the end was breathtaking—he really does know her, he really is tender with her—which makes it a thousand times worse. They aren't villains twirling mustaches; they are two gorgeous, controlling, intense people who thought they could play with fire, and now they're all going to burn down together.

I cannot put this book down. I need the explosion. I dread it, but I need it.
