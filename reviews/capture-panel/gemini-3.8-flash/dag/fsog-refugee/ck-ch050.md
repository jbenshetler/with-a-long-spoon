WHO'S WHO

*   **Vee (Vivienne):** My heart breaks for her, and my respect for her just hit the ceiling. She gave Pace everything—her body, her vulnerable pinup photo, her weekends, her heart—and then she gave him her best poetic sentence: *"I had been eating crackers my whole life, and you fed me a meal."* When he ducked it, she didn't just swallow the pain and let his incredible cooking and earth-shattering touch numb her into submission. She picked a fight at Chili's to test the walls of the terrarium, took off his comforting flannel in the freezing dorm because the scent was a ghost of a commitment he wouldn't make, and finally stood on his mat with her coat buttoned and demanded a name for what they are in the daylight. When he offered his hands instead of words, she walked out into the cold. She is hurting, but she refused to be reduced to a warm secret behind a closed door.
*   **Pace (Peter):** The ultimate tragic hero of his own making. He is everything I ever wanted in an alpha romance hero—tender, utterly devoted, building custom tables and sewing bespoke silk dresses, putting on two flannel shirts in a warm kitchen without a drop of mockery just so his girl can have his scent all weekend. But his devotion is trapped inside his own iron silence. His answer to her soul-baring confession was the lethal corporate dodge: *"I love how good we are together... What your body does."* When she demanded daylight clarity, he hid behind math and deeds: *"Words are cheap. Believe what I do."* He built her an astonishing, secure paradise, but because he and Randi set this whole thing up before Vee ever crossed the threshold, he cannot afford to speak the daylight truth without detonating the foundation. He watched her walk out into the snow because his control couldn't yield the one thing she needed.
*   **Randi (Miranda):** Getting inside her head in Gstaad completely changed everything. She isn't just a sociopathic, silk-clad puppeteer; she is a deeply tragic, cowardly, hollowed-out woman suffocating inside her father's criminal wealth (that awful, fraudulent PPP loan named *MIRA* after her). With John—the faultless society bachelor in white tie—she got the exact "acceptable" romance the genre often pedals (the guy who takes, who assumes, who leads without asking), and it left her utterly freezing in a luxury bed. She knows Pace is the real thing because he sees past her armor. And more importantly: *Randi is genuinely, desperately in love with Vee.* She is hoarding Vee’s unconditional warmth like a starving castaway, terrified to confess because she can't bear to lose the only uncalculated affection she’s ever had. She gives Vee double-edged words and fake fairytale texts because she's too cowardly to be brave.
*   **Cassie:** Still the clear, quiet bell of truth. She pulled the PPP data that broke Randi's illusions; she sat across from Vee in the dining hall and gently, without prying, pointed out that Vee can't explain Pace because he doesn't fit into the daylight world. She gives Vee the space to keep her secrets, even while Vee is drowning in the silence.
*   **John:** The society mannequin from Yale/New Haven. Cotillion dancer, ski-line charmer, sleigh-ride provider. The classic romance hero archetype who assumes consent, takes what he wants, brings a woman off, and falls asleep without ever seeing her. The perfect foil who proved why Pace's watchful, asking care is so infinitely superior.

HOW I FEEL ABOUT EACH OF THEM

*   **Vee:** Pure, unadulterated admiration. It is so easy for a heroine in this genre to get swept away by a big, gorgeous, capable man who cooks gourmet meals and takes her to the heights of ecstasy in bed. It is intoxicating, addictive comfort. But Vee knows who she is. She demanded to be seen in the light of day. Walking out that door into the cold when he offered his hands instead of the word took terrifying, magnificent courage.
*   **Pace:** I am furious with him and I ache for him. He is suffocating inside his own principles. He genuinely believes that building the table, keeping the fire lit, and tending her body is the purest truth, and in many ways it is—but a woman cannot live on actions alone when the actions stop at the edge of his property. He is paralyzed because giving her the title means confronting the secret pact he made with Randi. He chose his wall, and he lost his girl. Now he's standing alone in a warm house with two glasses of wine, a pot of chicken, and an empty bed.
*   **Randi:** I went from wanting to strangle her to wanting to wrap her in a blanket and shake her by the shoulders. She is pitiful in the most tragic, aristocratic way. She lives in a world of pure performance where love is transaction, and she finally stumbled across two people who are real—Pace and Vee—and she is ruining them both because she won't risk being vulnerable. Her love for Vee is real, but her cowardice is poisonous.
*   **Cassie:** An absolute treasure. The one person whose hands are entirely clean, holding down the anchor of reality while everyone else drifts into high-stakes psychological drama.
