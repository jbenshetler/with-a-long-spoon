REACTION

Oh, Vee. Oh, honey, *no*. My stomach completely dropped through the floorboards.

Watching her self-sabotage is like watching a car crash in slow motion where you can see the black ice three miles back. The first half of this chapter was pure, dripping romance heaven—the boom box wrapped in electrical tape, the empty concrete pad under the buzzing field lights, him making her push against his shoulder to feel the frame, that breathtaking moment where she finally stops fighting and just *gives him her weight* and lets him lead. Pace is so thoroughly, deeply competent at everything he touches that it makes your chest ache. Country dancing, tailoring silk, powerlifting, cooking coq au vin—the man is practically a bespoke fantasy machine.

And then they step into The Rusty Nail, and the phantom voice of her mother screaming *floozie*—combined with a catty blonde marking her territory with a sniper rifle of a compliment—just detonates Vee’s entire insecure psyche. "Does your *service* include getting the girls home safe from here, too?" The immediate, visceral bile of it! I literally gasped. It was so mean, so petty, so profoundly unfair to a man who literally answers honestly: *"Yes. Sometimes. When it's required."* Pace doesn't yell, doesn't chase her, doesn't put on a show; the light just completely drains out of him, he sets down his untouched beer, and he walks into the cold. That hurt worse than a screaming match.

And the bartender delivering the autopsy: *"Wasn't him they were jealous of, honey."* God, the humiliation. Sitting in that rotting cab, burning money she doesn't have on a ride home after throwing away the best thing that ever happened to her because she couldn't handle feeling common.

AND THEN. The final sentence. The absolute terrifying cherry on top of this impending disaster: *"She was going to fix it. When it was light she knew what she was going to do... go to Randi's."*

NO. DO NOT GO TO RANDI'S. I want to physically tackle her in the hallway! She is running straight into the wolf's den with her guts already spilled out! Randi is going to feast on this. Randi was already sitting in Pace's kitchen, wearing his undershirt, silently seething with jealousy because Pace was falling in love with Vee's light. If Vee walks into Randi's sorority castle weeping about how she blew it with Pace at the honky-tonk, Randi is going to play her like a cheap fiddle.

I am sweating. My heart is pounding. The tension is unbearable.
