WHO'S WHO

- **Vee (Vivienne Thorne):** Twenty-one, junior. She walked out. After months of letting Peter feed, dress, spank, and reshape her, she finally hit the wall when he tried to swap a plate of food and good sex for actual emotional standing. She spent Christmas in Ohio curled in a childhood twin bed she’s literally outgrown, suffocating under fabric softener and lavender plug-ins, convinced she’s a discarded toy and that Peter has already replaced her with a tanned blonde dancer. She’s clinging to Randi’s "fairytale" texts as her one lifeline for spring, utterly blind to the truth of both people she loves.
- **Pace / Peter:** Twenty-two. The math prodigy's armor just cracked clean down the center. He convinced himself years ago—after his older tutor/first love Daphne left him gutted—that withholding "I love you" was the mathematical formula for making loss survivable. It was a lie. After Vee walked out and his friend Sheri dragged him for his arrogant bullshit, he got violently, blindly drunk on whiskey, puked in his own pristine hallway, bleached the floor, and broke down sobbing at his kitchen table because a passing truck on the highway wasn't her car. He knows he loves her. He knows he was a coward on the porch.
- **Randi (Miranda):** The golden sorority queen is living in an absolute cryogenic chamber. On vacation in Gstaad, her father is a corrupt fraud eating rare steak while dodging the PPP fallout, her mother is an ice-pick in diamonds, and the "billionaire" law student John she bagged is a cotillion-trained empty suit who gave her a textbook romance-novel night (sleigh, white-tie ball, champagne, sex) that felt like eating a dry sandwich. She is violently, desperately in love with Vee—Vee is the only person in her life who isn't currency—but she's too much of a coward to risk losing the brunches, so she feeds Vee emojis and lies from a cold luxury suite.
- **Cassie:** Sane, grounded, working-class roommate. Over cheap cafeteria trays, she asked Vee the only question that mattered: *"What is he... to you?"* And Vee couldn't answer. Cassie didn't pry, letting Vee keep her secrets, but the distance is growing.
- **Sheri:** Pace's cycling buddy. Five feet of brassy, sharp-tongued, bleached-blonde country girl who eats double cheeseburgers, drinks milkshakes, and gives Pace zero passes. She saw through his high-minded "philosophy of food" immediately and told him flat out that letting Vee walk over three unsaid words makes him look like absolute shit.

WHERE THINGS STAND

- **The Crack and The Walkout:** Vee tried to give Peter her heart on his porch with a rehearsed metaphor ("I had been eating crackers... you fed me a meal"), and he calculated the trap and deflected straight to her body ("I love how good we are together... what your body does"). A few days later, after a miserable, overstimulating dinner at Chili's proved he can't handle the real world, Vee cornered him in his kitchen. He told her "Words are cheap, believe what I do," and she hit him with *"Words are cheap, and you still won't spend them on me"*—refused his reaching hand, and walked out into the cold.
- **The Winter Break Silence:** Neither of them texted. Vee spent New Year's Eve spiraling in Ohio, stripping off his flannel because its warmth was only her own body heat reflected back, imagining him with other women. Peter spent it drowning in whiskey, getting called out by Sheri, and weeping over cold, vacuum-sealed chicken in an empty house.
- **The Triangle’s Hidden Stakes:** Randi is in Switzerland realizing that no perfect, wealthy man can touch the nerve Peter reaches or the warmth Vee brings. She texted Vee calling her "gorgeous," and Vee took it as effortless glamour. They are all heading back to campus on a catastrophic collision course.

HOW I FEEL ABOUT EACH OF THEM

- **Vee:** God, I could have cheered when she walked out that door. She has taken so much, surrendered so much of her autonomy and pride to this man, but refusing to let a hot plate of food and skilled hands stand in for basic emotional dignity was her finest moment. Her late-night holiday spiral was agonizingly, humiliatingly relatable—that midnight brain that replaces you with a prettier girl at his stove—but she held her line.
- **Peter:** The arrogant, beautiful idiot. I wanted to shake him by the hair during the Chili's and kitchen scenes, and then chapter 50 completely ripped my heart out. The Daphne reveal finally makes his rigid control make sense: he thought he could out-math heartbreak by keeping the words hostage. Seeing him hungover, smelling like bleach, shaking over boiled broccoli, and weeping over a passing truck was the exact humbling he had coming. He's not playing a game anymore; he's bleeding.
- **Randi:** My god, chapter 47 made her human in the most tragic way. Watching her stand in front of a luxury mirror after a flawless, textbook "fairytale" night with an eligible rich boy, feeling completely hollow and cold, made me ache for her. She is trapped in the gilded cage she built. She loves Vee, but she's such an emotional coward she'll settle for being the voyeur bestie rather than risk the truth.
- **Sheri:** Give this woman a medal. She is the only person in Peter's universe who doesn't treat him like a prodigy or an erotic god. Calling him out for his pretentious "philosophy" and telling him he's throwing away a girl over his own stubbornness was pure gold.

WHAT I'M CARRYING

- *"Words are cheap, and you still won't spend them on me."* The absolute executioner's line. Vee delivering that while Peter stood frozen with his arms crossed will ring in my head for the rest of the book.
- The flannel coming off in the dorm: sitting in the dark, realizing the warmth in the cloth wasn't Peter's—it was just her own heat given back. Stripping it off and lying bare and cold in the twin bed.
- Peter at Chili's: the four screens blaring, the fork being turned and squared against the table over and over, the sensory overload choking him until he couldn't hear her. The cage doors swinging shut in the public glare.
- The Gstaad hangover: Randi lying beside a flawless, sleeping white-tie bachelor, feeling like she just ate a dry sandwich on a busy afternoon, wiping off her lipstick while staring at a locked white-gold bracelet.
- The passing truck: Peter at his kitchen table, fork stopped in midair, his entire body leaning toward the gravel drive, and then sobbing into his hands when the engine doesn't turn in.

WHAT I'M WAITING FOR

- The reunion back on campus. Vee thinks it's over; Peter is sitting in a house that smells of bleach, starving for her. Who moves first? Does Peter finally break his Daphne rule and say the words out loud?
- Randi's return. She’s coming back from Switzerland hungry for Vee, totally unaware that Vee and Peter just had a seismic rupture. What happens when Vee cries on Randi's shoulder about Peter's silence? Does Randi push them apart, or does she slip?
- The stats project bomb. Cassie still has her teeth in that PPP loan data. When the semester starts, Randi's corrupt family business is still sitting on that spreadsheet.
- The photograph. It's still sitting on Randi's phone. Peter still thinks it was only for him.

WHAT'S FADING

- Whatever lingering respect I had for Peter's "quiet, noble control"—it's officially diagnosed as pure, unadulterated trauma-cowardice.
- Any memory of the academic stuff Vee is doing besides stats.
- John from Gstaad—he served his purpose as the hollow romance-trope foil, and he's already gone.
