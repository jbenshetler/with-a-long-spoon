Pace. *Pace.* You arrogant, monstrous, completely hypocritical bastard.

The sheer, sickening delusion of that logic made my jaw drop. Because of Friday. Because he didn't want Vee to see another woman's teeth marks on his skin when he undresses for her.

And then the ending. Standing over the burgundy silk in the dark. Driving through the cold mountain cut at dawn. Texting her from the idling truck: *Dinner Friday?*
And her instant, pure, trusting answer waiting for him in Chicago: *Yes.*

*Every yes was freely given. That was the problem.*
The trap is officially set. Friday is coming, and Vee is walking straight into the slaughterhouse believing she's being courted by a prince. I am shaking.
