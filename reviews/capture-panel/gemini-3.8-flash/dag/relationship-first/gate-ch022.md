REACTION

I feel sick. That is the honest, physical response to reading this: a terrible, fascinated, creeping nausea.

Because on the surface, this is the brunch scene every woman in her early twenties has lived. You sit in the sun, you calculate the brunch arithmetic to make sure you have enough in your checking account to survive until Thursday, you get slightly tipsy on cheap champagne and orange juice, and you debrief the man who finally gave you what you were starving for. It feels like sisterhood. It feels like the glamorous older girl welcoming you into the club where women aren't ashamed of their hunger.

And the entire time, my stomach was in a knot, because we know where Randi was on Friday night before this. We know Randi knows *exactly* what Pace does, how he moves, what he looks like at that stove, what he smells like. When Randi leans across that table and says, *"You’re not caught, sweetheart. You’re lucky,"* the sheer, layered cruelty of that line took the breath right out of my lungs. She is *literally* caught. She is caught in a trap that Randi helped build, and Randi is patting her hand, validating her agency, convincing her that handing herself over was an act of radical feminist empowerment. *"You wanted it, and you took it. For once."* God, Randi is evil. Not cartoon evil—worse. The intimate, beautiful, smiling kind of evil that feels like the best friend you prayed for at fifteen.

And Vee just eats it out of her hand. She leaves out the middle because she thinks it’s sacred to her, not realizing Randi doesn't even need the middle—Randi is looking at the edges, checking the inventory. She notices the toes. She asks about the underwear. And Vee—sweet, doomed Vee—thinks she’s sharing girl-talk, while Randi is essentially doing an autopsy on Vee’s submission to the man they share.

Then the kiss at the curb. The two-part kiss, the hand on the jaw. The fact that Vee *expected* it this time, leaned into it, didn't look around to see who was watching. Randi is folding Vee into her own mouth right after Pace had her. The circulation of fluids and intimacy between these three, with Vee being the only one blindfolded, is getting suffocating. I cannot look away.
