REACTION

Oh, Cassie. My heart just dropped into my stomach over one word.

*“Goodbye, Vee.”*

Not "see ya," not "text me later." Just *Goodbye, Vee.* That gave me absolute chills. It was so quiet and so heavy, like watching someone step off a curb into oncoming traffic while looking at a pretty bird in a tree. Vee has no idea what she’s walking into, but Cassie felt the shift immediately. You know that specific kind of friend who has an almost animal instinct for when the air changes? Cassie smells the danger, or at least the profound artificiality of it, and she just quietly, mournfully steps out of the blast radius. It’s devastating.

And then seeing Vee from the inside—God, it’s so much more painful than seeing her from Pace’s perspective on the quad. The jacket promised she was "quietly starving," and here it is: the cardigan kept on over the better clothes, her mother’s vicious little voice hissing *“floozies”* in her head, the deep, secret vanity warring with the deep, secret shame. She’s built on this lavish, athletic scale, but she’s constantly shrinking herself, buttoning up, terrified of taking up the space she naturally commands.

So when Randi swoops in—Randi, who is petite and polished and holds the keys to the kingdom—and basically says *I choose you, I want you in my window, you belong with me,* Vee doesn't stand a chance. It’s like watching a heat-seeking missile hit a completely defenseless target. The detail about Vee trying to remember what they talked about and realizing she has zero words, only the *feeling* of being chosen? That is so psychologically sharp it hurts. Randi didn't have a conversation with her; Randi performed an enchantment. She gave her the Greek letters, the high table, the oat-milk latte, the casual *“love you, girl.”*

And the sickest part, the part that gives this book its terrifying momentum, is that we know Randi isn't just an evil sorority queen doing a bit. Randi was weeping on a custom-built spanking bench forty-eight hours ago, completely undone by a man who looked right through her mask. Randi went hunting for Vee because she is trying to manage her own terrifying appetite, trying to gift-wrap a transgression for Pace so she doesn't have to face what broke open inside her. There are so many layers of hunger and denial operating at this coffee table. Vee thinks she’s being invited into the sunlight, and she’s actually being groomed for the basement. I am completely hooked.
