REACTION

My stomach actually hurt during the middle of this. It’s the kind of ache you get watching someone happily count out counterfeit bills, convinced they’ve just struck it rich.

Randi calling her *dear friend* in front of Brooke was brilliant and vile. Because Vee feels the cost of it—she knows, instinctively, that Randi just staked social capital to claim her in front of the apex predator of their house—and so Vee interprets it as the purest, most generous gift of loyalty imaginable. When in reality, it’s a leash. It’s Randi marking her territory, performing benevolence, and tying Vee so tightly to her that Vee won’t be able to turn around and look at what’s actually happening. And that line: *“Ask and then ravish.”* Randi whispering that back to her at Vee’s own volume, letting her own mouth taste Pace’s methodology right there across the smoothie table… God. It’s sick. Randi is curating Vee’s surrender to Pace step by step, getting vicariously fed on it while simultaneously steering her away from Cassie.

And that opening moment with Cassie broke my heart a little. Cassie’s radar is so clean. She looks past Vee, sees Randi already lunging for her wrist, and just folds her face up and walks away. *“Goodbye, Vee.”* She doesn't fight for the space because she has too much dignity to beg, and Vee just lets her go because she’s so drunk on being *chosen* by the pretty girl and the older man. The way Vee immediately justified Cassie’s absence—telling herself it’s totally healthy to have separate lives while simultaneously writing off Cassie’s entire existence as "not her thing"—is such a classic, quiet betrayal.

The physical staging in the smoothie shop was suffocatingly good: Vee perched up on that high stool like an awkward display piece, clutching her oat-milk smoothie (ordered just to mirror Randi), feeling her pilled cardigan like an apology. And then Randi casually dropping the prompt—*Tell me you're going full nature girl up there. Hair down, no bra, let your mountain man ravish you against a tree*—planting the exact erotic script into Vee’s head so that when Pace reaches for her on that mountain, Vee will think the hunger was entirely her own idea.

It’s psychological horror disguised as a college girl getting a makeover. I need Sunday to get here immediately.
