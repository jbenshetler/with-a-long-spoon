That scene in the bathroom looking in the mirror—realizing she had broken her cardinal rule of keeping herself behind the mask, realizing she has begged a man her sorority sisters don't even know, and the absolute panic of: *"The one he had found was going back behind it, and she was not coming out again."*

If Randi is terrified of being seen by Pace, what is the best way to divert his laser-focused, terrifyingly patient gaze away from herself? You bring him an offering. I am entirely hooked.
