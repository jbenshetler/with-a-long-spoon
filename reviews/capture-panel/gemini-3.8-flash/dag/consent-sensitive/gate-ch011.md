I feel sick. Genuinely, physically sick to my stomach, and I mean that as the highest possible praise for what Helen Rivers is pulling off here.

This chapter is a quiet masterclass in predatory intimacy. There’s no kink here, no sex, not even Pace in the room—just two girls on barstools drinking smoothies—and it is easily one of the most alarming stretches of prose I’ve read all year.

Look at what Randi is doing. She plays the "my love life is a disaster" card immediately to establish false equality, making herself seem messy and relatable so Vee will drop her guard. And Vee spills *everything*. And then Brooke walks up.

God, the social violence of that sorority dynamic is rendered so cleanly. The way Brooke and the tall girl just *scanned* Vee, pricing her out like an ugly thrift store coat. But the horror—the absolute, devastating horror—is when Randi takes Vee’s hand and says, *"Vee’s my dear friend."*
