She has been reading his silence as ease, and here he is bruised by her absence and deciding, as usual, that whatever hurts can simply “keep.” It makes me ache for both of them while also making me want to shake him. His silence is not emptiness, but she cannot live on secret abundance.

Sheri is wonderful. I need to see whether he pays it—or whether he keeps treating endurance as integrity while Vee freezes outside.
