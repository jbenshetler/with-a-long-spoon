REACTION — Oh, Randi. “I know so” made my whole body go cold, because she does know. She knows Pace misses Vee. She knows Sheri is not the imaginary blonde rival—she has actually met Sheri—and she knows exactly why Pace has kept silent. Vee believes she is receiving a brilliant act of sympathetic interpretation; Randi is laundering privileged information into intuition. That is such a profound counterfeit of intimacy. Vee comes to her because she feels safely, uniquely understood, and Randi can understand her this perfectly because she has access to both sides of the locked door.

And then she takes Vee’s entirely legitimate demand—give this relationship a name, let it exist outside your house—and turns it into something Vee did *to Pace*. “You backed him into a corner in his own house” made me furious. Instead she teaches Vee to treat Pace’s refusal as the vulnerable thing that must be protected and her own need as aggression.

Randi is not helping them resolve the conflict; she is helping Pace get Vee back without answering her.

I am very, very in. But I’m also at the point where the reveal needs to hurt the people who engineered this, not merely Vee. Randi just used her secret knowledge to send a devastated woman back into the plan wearing nothing but Randi’s shoes. The book plainly knows what that means. I need it to keep knowing.
