Every individual moment can be tender and consensual while the larger structure remains concealed from her. That tension is the book’s live wire, and so far it is absolutely not looking away from it.

The kiss worked on me completely. She states a boundary; he accepts it immediately and actually begins moving on. She is the one who stops him. Then he leaves her the final inch, answers only what she brings, and waits again.
