That is exactly the knot the jacket promised, and it’s tightening beautifully.

I love Cassie. “How much did he give you to drink?” is funny, but it’s also the question a good friend asks, and Vee being able to answer it with such radiant certainty makes the whole thing hurt more. Cassie is watching out for her without puncturing her happiness. The pillow, the stupid little spin, “Look at me”—that all felt so young and genuinely delighted that I wanted to hug her. And then: off six-fourteen. I got a chill. I’m not worried he’s going to physically harm her; the more frightening thing is how carefully he’s going to care for her while guiding her toward something she cannot yet see.

Now I desperately want her at that house, seeing the long table without knowing what it is, while I know exactly what it is. That dramatic irony is doing filthy work already.
