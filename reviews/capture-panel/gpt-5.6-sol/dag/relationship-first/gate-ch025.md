Oh, this is the Randi chapter I’ve been waiting for, and it made me feel wonderful and ill.

Cassie asking only, “Was it a good night?” nearly undid me. She does not care about the dress, or Pace as an impressive object, or the erotic details she could extract. And then the money. No. No, sweetheart. Vee is living each column separately. She still thinks Pace and Randi independently see her, while I’m watching their combined attention produce a result she cannot calculate.

That last thought—*I have never had a friend like this*—landed like a blade. True. Completely true. Just not in the way she means.
