Oh, the drawer got me. Completely. Him folding his own careful things into too little space so she can have the easy drawer—the one a hand reaches for without thinking—is exactly the kind of love I believe. I wanted to put the book against my chest for a minute.

And “Peter” into his sleeping ear. God. She has named the love privately because she thinks his actions are enough to make the silence safe. The door swinging open, the ridiculous happy spin, feeding her, returning to her, the scrunchies, the drawer: yes, of course she believes him. I believe the love too. That is what hurts. I think he loves hearing her speak about Randi and is pleased that their plan is “working,” which may be worse. Now Vee is carrying that kiss home as a charming foreign custom and Pace grins. The two of them are standing on either side of her innocence enjoying what it gives them.
