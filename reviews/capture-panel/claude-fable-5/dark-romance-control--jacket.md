VERDICT

MAILING LIST: yes — but I'm warning you, Helen Rivers, if book two turns into everyone processing their feelings healthily, I'm out.
TELL A FRIEND: Okay so you know how I DNF anything where the man asks permission? Listen anyway. This one's a bait-and-switch in a good way — the boyfriend is the softest dom alive, folds her clothes, checks her shoulders mid-scene, I almost quit twice — but he is NOT the predator. The predator is the girlfriend. Cruel Intentions energy, except literary and slow-burn: she and the boyfriend literally walk around campus picking out a girl like they're shopping, and then you spend a whole chapter inside the target's head while she gets love-bombed and it's SO warm and SO sinister at the same time. Chapter one has a handmade spanking bench and a begging scene that actually earns it, chapter two is skippable sorority stuff, chapter three is where it gets evil, chapter four made me feel complicit. It's dark like a hand on the back of your neck, not dark like a knife — manipulation instead of menace — so if you need your danger to have a body count, temper expectations. But the wrongness is delicious and the girl doing it is the best villainess I've met all year. Skim the literary bits, stay for Randi.
