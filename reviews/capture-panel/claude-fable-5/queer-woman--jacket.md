TELL A FRIEND: Okay, so — I found a thing, and I need you to hear the whole sentence before you make the face. It is, on paper, the exact book we don't read: a couple runs a seduction game on an unknowing college girl, and yes, the man is one of those serenely omniscient doms who is never wrong about anything and I wanted to flick his ear by chapter three. But. The jacket tells you the whole scheme up front — it's not hiding the predatory shape, it's *about* the predatory shape — and the women underneath it are so precisely written I kept forgetting to keep my guard up. There's a chapter-one scene where the schemer girlfriend stands in a bathroom rebuilding her face after a man accidentally found the person behind it, and it's the best thing about closets I've read in an erotica in years — she literally thinks that wanting a woman herself would be "unsurvivable," so she regifts her own appetite to her boyfriend as a threesome offer, and the book *names* that move. And the mark, the girl they pick? The book quietly shows you her wanting was there first — the too-long looks, the cardigan over the better shirt, the helpless staring — before anyone ever pointed at her. So the question the series is asking is my question: whose desire is it, when someone else builds the door? It might still botch the landing and give the man authorship of both women, in which case I'll throw it across the room and you'll hear about that too. But four chapters in it's hot, it's mean in the right ways, and there's a side character who says "Goodbye, Vee" like a bell tolling and I haven't stopped thinking about it. Read the sample at least.
