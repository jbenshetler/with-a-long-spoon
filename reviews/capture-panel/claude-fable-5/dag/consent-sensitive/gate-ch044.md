That's the whole novel in four words and she gets one flash of it and doesn't follow the flash, because following it means seeing that a Chili's skillet used to be a fine night and *fine used to be plenty* and the man asleep against her back has burned her old world down to the studs, palate first. She asked for outside and he said, level as a floor, "Where do you want to go tonight?" — handed her the planning, and her map only had Chili's on it, because Chili's is what her old life could afford, and the night failed on schedule, and now the outside world tastes like a billboard. He never once said *let me pick somewhere quiet.* A man who pours Nebbiolo and unwraps buffalo mozzarella from paper knows the candlelit rooms exist. The door he opened led to Chili's or home.
