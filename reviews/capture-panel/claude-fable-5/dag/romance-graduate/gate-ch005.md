Okay so this is the chapter where the trap closes, and I got to watch it close in real time knowing everything Vee doesn't, and I have to say — the jacket promised me "ahead of her from the start, fall for it exactly as she does," and that is precisely the drug this chapter is dealing. I know Pace built a spanking bench with his own hands. I know Randi picked this girl off a lawn like fruit. And here he comes across a coffee shop doing the humble-tutor bit, "It's pretty hard, it's calculus," to a man we know is about to finish a math PhD, and Vee's little "did I just warn a math PhD that my calculus was hard?" — I actually laughed and then felt sick, because it's adorable and it's ALSO the whole machine working exactly as designed. She thinks she got lucky. She got selected.

The thing that got me most: Randi's hands. The cup that comes up to her mouth twice and no sip happens. The other hand in her lap. Vee's brain files it as "she's bored" and I sat there going oh honey, she is the furthest thing from bored, she is watching the thing she set in motion and she is not okay, and I don't even fully know which way she's not okay — turned on? jealous? of him? of Vee?? I see you, Helen Rivers.

Cassie. CASSIE.
