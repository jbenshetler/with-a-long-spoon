WHO'S WHO

- **Pace** — the mathematician. PhD-almost-done, lives off six-fourteen past where it goes to gravel, in a warm house full of furniture he built with his own hands. Including the bench. Courtly, patient, "may I" — the most seductive man I've read in years and I've watched him use every kindness as an instrument. Feeds women. That's his whole tell: he finds the hunger a woman has starved down her entire life and answers it, and the answering feels like love and works like a leash. Still not sure which one it actually is. Maybe both. Old wound named Daphne — a door he opened that he shouldn't have; now he over-respects closed doors, which is going to matter.
- **Randi** — black hair, blue eyes, sorority Standards board, single room, the "glamorous friend."
