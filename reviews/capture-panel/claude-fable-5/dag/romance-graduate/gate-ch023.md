Frosted plum. *Chosen to catch the light as the silk did.* Which means Vee painted her toes to match a dress a man made her — that's Pace's machine still running — but it's RANDI who reads it, Randi who runs one thumb across the nail, and Vee's hand shoots out to Randi's shoulder because the floor tipped. "Randi looked up at her and smiled and said nothing at all." NOTHING AT ALL. She logged it. She logged the gasp the way Pace logs hungers, and she filed it, and she went back to the buckle. The book told me in the jacket she'd want Randi too and I still wasn't ready for it to arrive through a FOOT.

And here's what's making me sick in the good way: this whole chapter is Pace's chapter with Randi wearing it. Look at the shape. He finds the hunger before the flinch finishes — she finds "it doesn't stop the room" before Vee can finish talking herself into the tan heel.
