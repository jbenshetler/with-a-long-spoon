It hurt. Two women, six time zones apart, each lying awake envying the life the other one is lying awake unable to feel. Randi cold in a warm bed; Vee cold in a cold one. "Miss your face most though, gorgeous" — the one line Randi meant, and it's the one line Vee reads as decoration.
