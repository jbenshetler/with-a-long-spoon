Okay so Randi just performed emergency surgery on her own operation with her heart lying open on the table and I need everyone to be awake for this.

Because look at what this chapter actually IS. Three chapters ago I watched Randi lie in a perfect man's perfect bed in Gstaad and admit to herself — to ME — that Vee is the thing, that the word has to keep its cover, that she's not brave enough to lose the brunches. Both of them starving through one embrace and each one thinks she's the only one stealing. I had to put the book down. That's the closest these two have ever come to the truth and it happened wordless, in a doorway, disguised as comfort.

And then — THEN — Randi sits down and does the most agonizing thing she could possibly do: she gives Vee back to Pace. Competently. Beautifully.
