**WHO'S WHO**

- **Vee** — my girl, and she's come through the worst stretch of the book and out the other side standing up. The fight was hers, said *well* — she made room for all the good before asking for the one missing thing, and "words are cheap, and you still won't spend them on me" is her best sentence in fifty chapters. Then she survived the break (the room that didn't know her, the blonde she built at midnight), came back wrecked, and — with Randi's coaching — drove out through January in a coat and heels and nothing else, as her own decision, wound and unspent on purpose, to hand him the one thing that wasn't words. She took his kiss back mid-shame-spiral and came the instant she did. BUT — my honest books: the word never landed, and she declared herself satisfied ("no room in her for wanting anything he hadn't given") and I don't trust that any more than I trusted "she had forgiven him." She's telling Cassie the reworked sweater version now — a small invented lie to the clean witness. The pile is still there under all the happiness. And she's still wrong about Sheri.
