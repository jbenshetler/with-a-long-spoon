REACTION — Okay, this one put a fist in my chest and I'm still kind of sitting with it.

The dance lesson on the concrete pad is the whole book in miniature, and the book *knows* it — "give me something to push back against," "being jelly doesn't work," the lead arriving before her feet know what to do. That's Pace. That's the entire courtship: he won't carry a girl who won't give her weight. And then Vee names it herself later — *I'm doing well / he's making me* — which is the blurb's whole thesis in three lines, on a dance floor, and she lets the floor take the question because the dancing is too good. God, that's exactly how complicity works. You don't ignore the doubt; you have it, and the music is loud.

And then the blonde. That mean little "he's so good about getting a girl home safe" — that was the knife, delivered with a smile, and it worked exactly as designed. I don't think she was lying — I think she was telling the truth *as venom*, which is worse, because a lie Vee could have survived. The thing that wrecked me is how Vee detonated: "spread her legs like a slut" — that's her MOTHER's voice again, the *floozie* voice, arriving at full force exactly at the moment she's happiest. The shame never left; it was just waiting for a door. And she punished him for being the man she wanted him to be. She *knows* it, in the dark, in his washed-out shirt, and she still lies there with both things, the shame and the phantom of his hand on her shoulder blade. That's interiority. That's the grown-up writing I'm starving for.

The bartender is my new favorite minor character. "Wasn't him they were jealous of, honey." Flat, done being asked for things, and she hands Vee the whole reframe in one line. And the detail that he *doesn't bring dates* — Vee is the exception, factually, from a hostile witness who'd have said otherwise if there were otherwise to say.

But here's what I'm watching with my jaw tight: the morning plan is *go to Randi's*. Randi — who is literally sleeping with him, who is running the operation, whose last on-page scene had her wanting Vee out loud in his bed and barely able to stop it. Vee is walking the detonation straight into the hands of the person who benefits from her being unsteady. And I have no idea whether Randi will build her back up honestly or use the crack. That's the most afraid of the next chapter I've been since I started this book.
