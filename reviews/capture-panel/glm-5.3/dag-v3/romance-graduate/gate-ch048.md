The old Vee would have been managing him. The new spine doesn't even reach for the management anymore. And the Chili's picture — she didn't argue with it, didn't swallow it, just let it stand there until she'd seen it and then handed him the problem: *you pick the place.* And him drying his hands slowly, turning around with happiness he'd barely let out, saying "Let me handle it." He's going to pick somewhere he can hear her. He's going to build her a room where nobody gets seated at the end.

Nothing happened this chapter. Two people at a sink. It's one of the best things the book has done.
