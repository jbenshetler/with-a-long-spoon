Peter. His real name is Peter. Twenty-two — he's TWENTY-TWO, a year older than her, a doctorate under his belt, and the whole time I've been reading "mathematics graduate student, finishing more or less" I've been picturing a man in his late twenties running an intricate hunt — and the "more or less" I've been carrying as a splinter since chapter five was the *timeline of a child*, fifteen in a dormitory full of grown men, teaching himself trigonometry and getting moved into the hall for it. The hallway. "They took the thing you loved and made it the punishment." I had to set the book down. This book just told me where the bench was built. A boy whose entire experience of being extraordinary was that the world's answer to it was a desk in the hallway — and then a woman found him and *paid attention on purpose*, ceremonially, and it was the first time in his life being seen didn't cost anything.
