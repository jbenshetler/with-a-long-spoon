REACTION — Okay, so this is the book showing me what it does between the knives. No heat at all this chapter, and I didn't even care, because that common-room scene was one of the best four minutes of social fencing I've read in this genre in years. Brooke staying seated. "Pace. That's actually his name?" filed away like a round in a chamber. The "Engineering, isn't it" — Randi correcting to *math, PhD* and Brooke "absorbing it without conceding the notch" — I know that woman. I've *been* in that room. Nobody says a single hostile thing and the whole scene is a strip search. And Randi's smile arriving "a fraction late" over and over, and her laugh matching Kinsey's pitch before she'd chosen it — the mask working on autopilot while the woman inside it is still lying on a ceiling somewhere in last night's clothes.

What wrecked me was the second half, though. The girl who portioned herself thin apple slices now lying on top of her made bed, cardigan buttoned, *shoes still on*, under a duvet she registers only as "it was a duvet" — after a night sleeping under *his* cashmere, in his evenly-heated house. The closet she opens and can't remember why. The phone turned facedown, two notifications unread — those are from Pace, they have to be, and she can't even look. "He's hard to say no to," replayed in a key she can't sing in. And the not-crying that doesn't feel like holding a door shut, "it felt like there being no door." That's the exact counterweight to the bathroom's deadbolt. Last night she locked a door. Now there isn't one. God.

I did clock the shape, though — this is the first quiet chapter. The book earned it; the quiet IS the content here, it's not throat-clearing, and I'd argue that bed scene is the most explicit thing in the book so far emotionally. But I'm now watching: if chapter 3 is also Randi alone not-crying, I'll start to get itchy. One note of uncertainty — the section is short and feels almost like a fragment. Whether that's technique or the author rationing pages, I can't tell yet. I'm willing to find out.
