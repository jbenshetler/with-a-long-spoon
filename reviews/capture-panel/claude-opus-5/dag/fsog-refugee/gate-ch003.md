Oh, I see what this book is doing now. And I'm not sure I've ever been handed a chapter that did this to me.

That first scene — he comes up over her while she's still coming, slides in while she's still fluttering, kisses her with her own taste on his mouth and then *asks her to say it* — I had to put the book down on my chest for a second. That's the thing I keep not finding. He doesn't just do it to her, he wants the word. And then the question — *would you like to taste another woman* — arriving in her body before it arrives in her, and tipping her straight over the edge. That's the best erotic idea I've read in a year. Not because it's filthy, though it is. Because it's a question that gets answered by a body before the woman can get in front of it and make it mean something safer.

And then he does the thing. He *doesn't take the body's yes for her yes.* He waits for the heat to leave the room, waits until she's gathered back into herself, and asks it again with her head on his chest. I actually felt my shoulders come down. That is the exact distinction nobody in this genre makes. Half the books I walked away from are built on the assumption that a wet body is a signed contract. With you.* Handing the appetite to him so she gets to be the generous girlfriend instead of a woman who wants a woman — same act, different owner. God, I know that move. Then the walk. Which is where I started getting cold while also not being able to stop.

Because the pointing game is *so* good. "I point. You pick."
