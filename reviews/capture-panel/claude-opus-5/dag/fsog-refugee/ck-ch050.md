WHAT'S FADING

The lemon chicken chapter is a pan's talk changing and zest coming off in a bright rain; the Chilean white is gone except *grapefruit.* The pizza night has kept the hallway and the flannel and lost the pizza — *water buffalo* and a handful of sand on the stone is all that's left, and the second taste-awakening aria has blurred into the first (cotton candy and strawberries, eating with the sound off — I can't say which belonged where, only that I got it three times in four chapters and stopped needing it).
