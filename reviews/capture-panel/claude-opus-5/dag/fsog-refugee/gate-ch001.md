I sat down expecting to meet Vee, because the back cover is all about Vee, and instead I got a man taking the top off a table in a room nobody's in yet, and by the end I understood I'd been handed the *other* woman — the one the blurb told me is going to do something to Vee — and I watched her get taken apart. Here's the thing I've been hunting for years and this chapter just did it in the first thirty pages: he asks. He asks *twice*. "Tell me your word." "Tell me again if you forget. I won't be upset. Tell me anytime." And then he keeps coming around the bench to her face — how's your back, hands and shoulders, you with me — and when she says "They're *fine*, Pace" half-exasperated, he says "just checking" and goes back to work. And he doesn't come. "That's not what this night was for." He's been hard since she sat on him in the kitchen and he just... sets it down. I have read so many books where the man's orgasm is the point of the architecture. Here the architecture is for her and he means it.

The apple. God, the apple. Sliced thin because she won't eat thick ones, cut sides up because she eats faster that way, and he's *sorry about* the thing behind her face that polices what she's allowed. The mother on egg whites "a thing she had told him with a knife in it." That's not foreplay, that's someone paying attention for months. The water bottle left out in advance. The cashmere blanket she brought in week two because she gets cold. This man's love language is noticing, and it's the most erotic thing in the chapter, more than the spanking.

Which — the spanking worked on me, fine, I'm not going to pretend. The slow passes up her ribs that keep turning back before they arrive, her forgetting the apple mid-bite, "You're doing that on purpose" / "Doing what?" and he does it again — that's flirting written by someone who knows that teasing is a *conversation*. And the edging on the bench, the hand taken away and laid wet against her spine, "her own heat, returned to her in the wrong place" — okay, yes, that's cruel and I loved it and she never once said red.

She begged in a voice she hasn't used since she was a child asking her mother for something her mother wasn't going to give her. She came so hard her body did something she couldn't have produced. She's going back in and she's not coming out again.

And he's on the other side of that door being *good*. Respecting it. Because of Daphne, whoever Daphne is, because he learned that you don't open a door just because you can hear through it.
