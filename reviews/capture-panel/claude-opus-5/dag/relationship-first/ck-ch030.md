WHO'S WHO

**Randi.** Still the white-gold band at her wrist, thirty chapters, nobody has said one word. **Cassie.** Cleats, a game, a tight-made bed, a cracked window every night because she likes the cold. Reads Vee off a made bed before Vee opens her mouth. WHERE THINGS STAND

HOW I FEEL ABOUT EACH OF THEM

Pace broke open and it's so much worse than a flaw. He is running Daphne.
