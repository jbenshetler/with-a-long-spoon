Oh, the shoes. The *shoes*.

Size seven. That's what I couldn't stop thinking about the whole way through. And the fitting. God. I want to say plainly that this was the most erotic thing in the book since the burgundy pins, and nobody took off anything but a shirt. The long skirt falling over the work so Vee can't see any of it, just cool hands somewhere past the hem, her foot guided in, the strap closing — a hand coming up under the skirt to the outside of her thigh to steady her, a thumb pressed once into the hollow behind her knee on the way down. *Vee's breath did something she hoped wasn't out loud.* This book is deliberately doing the fitting twice now, once with a man who craned his chin all the way up and never looked down, and once with a woman who did the whole thing where Vee couldn't watch. Same with the kiss in the foyer — catching up a half-beat late that she hadn't braced, that her mouth just met Randi's, *it was only what they did.* Nothing has ever been said about it by either of them.
