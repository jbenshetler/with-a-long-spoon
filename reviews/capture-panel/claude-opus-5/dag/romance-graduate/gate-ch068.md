Now the ask. I want to be honest about how close this came to losing me, because a man saying *I'd like you waxed, bare, by the weekend* in a kitchen is the single most secondhand beat in the entire genre — I have read that line forty times, usually from a man with a helicopter. And then the hand. God, the hand. *"Her body knew that grammar.
