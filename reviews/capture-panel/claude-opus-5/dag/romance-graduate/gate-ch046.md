Because here's what she's actually telling Cassie: she has three versions of this man and none of them is him. The induction photo she sent Pace uncropped and kept the inside of. The one she gave Randi, half first, then all of it, because she wouldn't be the girl who flinched. Her parents get the waist up. Cassie gets "the parts that fit," and then not even that. She's got a different crop for every person in her life and she's gotten *good* at it.

Her mother touching her own collarbone. Not saying anything. *You know how she does.* / *I know how she does.* That's four words of history between these two girls and I'd read a whole book about it.
