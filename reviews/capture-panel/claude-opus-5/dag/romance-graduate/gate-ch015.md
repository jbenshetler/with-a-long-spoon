That's what got me. She gave that to Cassie and she's keeping it from these two, and nobody in the book points at it, and I sat up. Vee has started having things that are *hers*. That's new. That's the same move Pace made with the froyo line and the same move Randi makes with everything, and the girl who didn't know she was allowed to want a cup of frozen yogurt is now curating. I'm weirdly proud of her and it also means she's getting good at exactly the skill that's going to hurt her.

The other thing: Randi is not in this chapter. Randi, who gets *every* detail, who sits on the aisle side and boxes her in — Vee took the biggest development yet to two girls in an athletic dining hall instead. Two in the afternoon, no meal, no dress code, and I know what's in that bedroom because I've read the chapter where the top comes off the table.
