Okay so this is the one that made me put the book down for a second and just sit there, and nothing happened in it. Nobody touched anybody. A girl went to the pool with her friend.

But "she had loved this before she had a body to be a problem" — I actually made a noise. That's the whole book in one line, and it's the first time I've been inside Vee at full depth instead of watching her get chosen by people. The underwater passage did something physical to me: the room switching off, the sculling her hands remembered without being asked, the thing in her chest that had stood at attention all afternoon quietly sitting down. That's the shame the jacket promised me and it's not being explained to me, it's just — there, in the water, in the size of the suit.

And the bleachers. God. "You won't need floaties with those" and she gives it *a long blink, half her mouth pursing, less than a grimace* — she has a whole trained micro-repertoire for being yelled at about her chest, she's been doing this since she was what, thirteen? She put her face on to walk fifty yards past a set of bleachers. The line that got me was "fifty yards of held breath going out at once." She was holding her breath the whole walk and I didn't know it until she laughed.

Cassie. Cassie is going to wreck me.
