And she *coached the apology.* Don't go in sorry, go in sure. The black dress, neckline down where it ought to be, the real earrings, these shoes, this scarf. That is a woman dressing another woman to walk through a door she herself has walked through a hundred times, and if you'd told me in chapter fourteen that the erotic center of this book would be a small dark woman kneeling on a carpet putting shoes on a half-dressed girl I'd have said sure, fine, but I would not have understood how it was going to *feel.*
