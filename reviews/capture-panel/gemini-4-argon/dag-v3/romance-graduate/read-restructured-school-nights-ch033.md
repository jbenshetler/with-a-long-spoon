REACTION

Wait—look at the date! *Monday, November 9!* This chapter sits *inside* the gap of Chapter 32, right between Saturday morning November 7 (when Vee left her brush and pins scattered across Pace's bathroom counter and wondered *"Had he minded?"*) and Friday the 13th (when she comes back and finds he gave her the top drawer)! And putting this quiet, gorgeously written chapter right here—so we already know the answer to *"Did he mind"* before Vee swims her heart out trying to outrun the question—made the whole thing ache in the best possible way.

First, can we talk about Cassie in the dorm room?! God, I missed Cassie and Vee actually talking! Look at the details Vee spills when she spins her desk chair around: the baked tomatoes with breadcrumbs, cheese, and fresh herbs, and Vee getting greedy and leaning in so a hot tomato slides off her fork and burns the side of her bare breast! And Cassie's dry one-liner: *"There's only one way that burn happens... Not enough on."* I laughed out loud! And Vee doesn't even blush or stop to explain because she's so used to being naked or half-naked in Pace's warm house now (*"warm enough that she never once thought about how much she had on"*).

Wait a second, though: *"You don't come home school nights."*
Vee has been staying over at Pace's house *more nights than not* during the week?! No wonder we haven't seen Randi at Pace's house lately! If Vee's dorm bed has been empty more school nights than not, when is Randi even seeing Pace?! Is Randi getting shut out of the weeknights *and* the weekends now?!

And then Cassie drops the flat question: *"Do you love him."*
In Chapter 32, lying in Pace's dark bedroom on Friday November 6, Vee admitted to herself in her own chest: *She loved him.* Not a maybe, not an "I think so." Yet here, three nights later on Monday, when Cassie asks her out loud, Vee hears herself go small and say: *"Maybe. I think so... It's all so fast."* Why did it come out as *maybe* when the real answer was sitting right there in her chest?!

And the Tuesday afternoon swim scene gives us the exact answer, and it is *identical* to Pace's description of powerlifting in Chapter 22! Remember in Chapter 22 when Pace told Vee why he lifts—that under a heavy bar, his mind finally has to shut up because there's no room left for thoughts?! Here is Vee in the third lane at 4 p.m. on a Tuesday, doing her seventy-five-minute set that she knows like the second verse of a hymn:
*"Nobody was counting but her and nothing was riding on it, and her body, which had opinions all day long every day of her life, had none at all down here. It only worked... It took the words off things one at a time until what was left was the shape underneath, and a shape you could carry around all evening without it asking you for anything."*
They are so much alike it makes my chest hurt! Pace lifts to take the words off things; Vee swims to take the words off things!

And look at what happens in the seventh piece of the set, right when the words come off:
*"*Do you love him* was a pressure under her ribs with a dread riding it... and the thing about Pace turned over once in the quiet and she could not tell, for about four strokes, whether the man she was turning over was Pace or was somebody she had built out of wanting him — whether he was that good or she had made him that good, out of wanting, out of two months of being looked at like that."*
THAT'S why she said *maybe* to Cassie! That's the exact fear that poisoned her at The Rusty Nail in Chapter 23 (*"wish laid over man so neatly she couldn't find the join"*), and it's STILL riding under her ribs as a dread! Part of her is terrified to say "I love him" out loud to Cassie because he hasn't given her a word for what they are (just like the jacket blurb says!), she still carries the Tuesday dread of *"Did he mind"* about the bathroom counter (because she hasn't seen Friday the 13th's top drawer yet!), and deep down her intuition is still whispering that there is something about Pace she can't quite see!

I know a bridge chapter when I see one, and this was a quiet, zero-heat interior chapter, but the writing in that pool sequence—the rhythm of *"Off the wall. Breathe every third"* stripping her anxious thoughts down to raw physical peace, and the line about walking out into the cold *"with her arms feeling like they belonged to somebody generous"*—was so good I didn't skim a single word. Now take me back past Friday the 13th—where is Randi while Vee is living at Pace's on school nights?!
