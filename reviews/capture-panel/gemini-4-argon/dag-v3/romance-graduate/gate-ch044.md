REACTION

Oh, my god. Putting Chapter 43 ("Fairytale") and Chapter 44 ("Old Acquaintances") back-to-back on the exact same New Year's Eve—so we watch the text message leave Ohio and land in Gstaad from BOTH sides of the screen—is one of the most devastatingly brilliant things I have ever read in a romance novel. My heart is aching so badly for both of these women that I feel bruised all over.

First of all, my suspicion from Chapter 43 about Vee's text (*"thinking about you 💛 hope it's beautiful out there. i'm here if you want to talk. or not."*) was 1000% CONFIRMED!
Look at that paragraph:
*"She wrote to Randi, warm, and made sure it demanded nothing — an open hand offered. Not the thing — the thing she'd folded away in the glass room and never once let on she'd seen, had let Randi cover and never said, not then and not after."*
Vee was lying in her outgrown childhood twin bed in Ohio, looking at Randi's Instagram feed of the Gstaad chalet, knowing full well that *the money that bought that dream came from her father's crooked `MIRANDA` PPP loans* ("even a poisoned Gstaad was more life than she had"), and gently offering Randi an open hand in case being trapped in a chalet with that father was hurting her!
And look at the tragedy of the double misread across six time zones:
In Chapter 43, Randi didn't know Vee knew about `MIRANDA`, and thought Vee was sitting in a warm, happy kitchen with parents who loved her out loud, expecting Randi to be living a golden fairytale—so Randi sent back the fake fairytale (*"dad's got us in this insane chalet"*) while lying next to a man who left her freezing cold inside!
And here in Chapter 44, Vee isn't in a warm kitchen at all—she's freezing alone in a dark bedroom that smells like old-lady lavender plugins, having heard *not one word from Pace since she walked out of his house on December 11*! And when Randi's text comes back at midnight, Vee actually believes Randi IS having the time of her life with a "devastatingly handsome billionaire" (which John literally was on paper!) and feels pathetically grateful that "Randi, the year's gift she had literally stumbled into" took thirty seconds out of her fairytale to text her back!
*"Randi, the year's gift she had literally stumbled into. Randi, the only girl she could talk to about real things."*
I wanted to cry when I read that line. "Literally stumbled into"—Chapter 3's pointing game!

And let's talk about what has happened between Vee and Pace since Friday, December 11 (Chapter 42):
NOTHING.
Twenty whole days of silence! Because Vee walked out the door when he wouldn't spend a word on her, and what is Pace's fatal, defining flaw? *He never pushes past a closed door. He always leaves you the room.*
*"He left you the room. He always left you the room — a thing she'd appreciated once, and hated now."*
When she types *"are you—"* into the text field and deletes it letter by letter, glad he doesn't live on his phone so he won't see the typing bubbles start and stop, I could barely stand it. Think about Pace sitting in his quiet house in the woods over Christmas while Vee is in Ohio and Randi is in Switzerland: does Pace think he's "respecting Vee's space" the way Randi told him to in Chapter 25 ("he doesn't sulk, he leaves you the room"), OR is Pace sitting there brokenhearted thinking Vee dumped him for good on December 11?!

And then—oh god—the sequence where Vee reaches for her favorite memory to stay warm, and her own insecurity turns it against her:
In Chapter 17, when Vee described her first night with Pace (wrapped in the top sheet at midnight, drinking the Chianti and watching him cook the spicy *fra diavolo*), Randi called it "a painting." And in Chapter 21, when Randi was eaten alive by jealousy, *Randi* secretly recreated that exact midnight kitchen painting at Pace's table!
And now here in Chapter 44, Vee tries to climb back inside her own *fra diavolo* painting to get warm at midnight on New Year's Eve, and because she has no name for what she is to Pace—and knows he cooked for Sheri at Thanksgiving—her mind replaces her in the falling bedsheet with a tan, blonde, fashion-model woman (built out of the Rusty Nail blonde and Sheri!) while she dismisses her own curvy body as "a stripper's"!
*"The one at the table in the falling sheet stopped being her. The hair went gold and the leg went tan and the blonde sat where Vee had sat in the wine light, the sheet slipping off a body that had never in its life needed building for... She could not keep her own night."*
Look at the irony of that: she is torturing herself picturing a tall, tan blonde in her bedsheet at Pace's kitchen table, when the woman who *actually* sat in her place at that table in Chapter 21 was petite, black-haired, pale Randi—the very woman Vee is clinging to right now as "the one sure thing the new year held"!

And that opening description of Vee's childhood bedroom in Ohio—the bent knees because she outgrew her twin bed in middle school, the synchronized-swimming trophies with their pointed toes, the clarinet in the closet, the high-school friends on the corkboard who haven't texted in a year, the Wheel of Fortune puzzles downstairs, the beige Christmas food that was love and yet never what she was hungry for:
*"She lay in the cold lavender dark of a room that loved a girl who no longer lived inside her."*
Who hasn't felt that exact claustrophobia of going home to your parents' house after you've become a different person and realizing the room only knows the high-school version of you?!

Look at where we are at the end of this chapter as the year turns over:
Both Randi (in Gstaad) and Vee (in Ohio) are lying in cold beds at midnight on New Year's Eve, both of them aching for Pace AND for each other! Randi decided in Chapter 43 that she can't lose Vee and will keep the brunches going; Vee falls asleep here thinking that *Randi* is her only sure thing when the new term starts ("a day she could circle for the one and nothing at all for the other") because she and Randi have their Tuesday/Thursday night Anthropology class together and she doesn't even know if she and Pace are over!
What happens when everyone comes back to town in January?! Where has Pace been for these three weeks?!
