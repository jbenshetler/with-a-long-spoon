REACTION

Oh, my God. One single page, and it gutted me completely.

Look at the arc of that handmade flannel shirt across the entire book! In Chapter 10, he took his only dry shirt out of his pack on a mountain in the rain and handed it to her, and she refused to give it back ("Keep the shirt"). In Chapter 15, Cassie noted she’d been sleeping in it all week. In Chapter 23, after the Rusty Nail fight, she had to wash it because she’d rationed his scent until it went stale, and in Chapter 24 she folded it back onto her pillow like a sacred relic before walking to Randi's. And in Chapter 37, just three weeks ago, she handed it back to him in his kitchen and made him wear it over his own shirt all weekend so she could have it back smelling of him again!

And now it’s after midnight on Friday, December 11—wait: why is she in her dorm bed on a Friday night (or early Friday morning before Friday night?!) instead of at Pace's house?!—and look at what she has to do with the shirt first:
1. She wears it *over* her own sleep shirt in the dorm so Cassie won't look at her and see "a girl too far gone in a boy." Even her talisman has to be worn as camouflage!
2. Then, when staring at the empty text field on her phone gives her nothing because no text she could send would ever extract the word from him, she strips both shirts off and puts his flannel straight onto her bare skin so she can breathe him in—the sawdust, the salt of his work, the rooms he keeps warm, the food he cooks.
3. And then the realization hits her like cold water:
*"And it was warm because she was warm. That was the thing she couldn't stop feeling once she'd felt it — the heat in the shirt was her own, given back. He wasn't in it."*

READ THAT SENTENCE AGAIN. *"The heat in the shirt was her own, given back."*
That isn't just about the flannel shirt in her cold dorm bunk! That is the terrifying realization she has been swimming away from since Chapter 23 ("how much of this was Pace, and how much was the Pace she'd been building out of wanting him") and Chapter 32 ("whether he was that good or she had made him that good, out of wanting")! The warmth she thought was him holding her in the dark is *her own body heat reflected back at her by an empty shirt*, while he is across town sleeping deeply and easily with nothing grinding in him! (Or worse—is he even alone across town, or is it Thursday night going into Friday morning?!)

And when she pulls his shirt off over her head and chooses to lie *bare* ("Bare"!) and shivering in the freezing draft from Cassie's cracked window until dawn because the cold truth is better than the false warmth of her own heat reflected back at her... and leaves his handmade shirt in an unfolded heap where it fell beside her instead of folding it onto her pillow... I actually felt something break in my chest. For the first time since Chapter 10, she has taken off his armor and left it on the floor.

We are at Friday, December 11, at the very end of the semester. She hasn't slept all night, she is raw and bare and freezing, and either Friday night with Pace or Saturday brunch with Randi is hours away. Give me the next chapter right now!
