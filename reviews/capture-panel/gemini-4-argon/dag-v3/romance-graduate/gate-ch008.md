REACTION

Oh, I am so in trouble with these two. I am *so* in trouble.

Can we talk about the kiss at the end?! Because it is the exact move he does with Randi at the front door in Chapter 1—he gives a brief, warm kiss and then backs off to let her decide if she wants more—except here, with Vee, we get to feel what that move actually *does* to a woman who has spent her whole life managing the room and obeying her mother's rules in her head!

When she blurted out *"I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days,"* I actually winced for her, because you can hear her mother's voice in it just like the froyo cup and the girls at Chi Latte. And any normal romance hero would have smirked and said *good thing it's been three days* or leaned in closer to tempt her into breaking her rule. Not Pace! Pace just smiles like she told him something lovely about herself, accepts the boundary completely, and starts walking again! So that when she plants her feet and stops him and says *"But I'm making an exception,"* nobody coaxed her into it. Nobody tricked her. And even THEN, he leaves the last inch to her, gives her back only the small kiss she brings him, and eases back a breath so she has to stand there in the dark smelling soap and restaurant smoke on him and *choose* to go back in for the real kiss! By the time she went back in with the grin on her mouth, my toes were curling. That had more genuine erotic charge in a first kiss than most five-flame explicit scenes I've read this year.

And the dinner! God, this book knows how to write food as foreplay. The whole sequence at Christine's restaurant—*"May I choose?"*—was hypnotic. I noticed right away how the prose did the exact opposite of Vee's coffee date with Randi in Chapter 4. With Randi, Vee came away with the "transcript blank" because she was performing so hard, leaning in so hard, dazzled by the surface while Randi gave her zero actual content. With Pace, Vee loses the thread of what he's saying because for the first time in her adult life, *she doesn't have to work.* She doesn't have to police how much she's eating, she doesn't have to keep up her end of the conversation like a social tennis match—she just eats hot blistered bread with her right hand (she noticed him only using his right hand!) and sinks into a literal food-and-trust trance until the mint tea wakes her up! And wait—shelost the thread right when he was licking a drip of smoky baba ghanoush off the side of his hand! Of course she lost the thread!

Also, two details that made me scream:
1. Cassie! *"That's not a boy. That's a man."* When Cassie approves of someone, you know it's real. And Vee analyzing why "Don't answer tonight" felt so different: *"The other ones always made you answer. Right then, on the phone... So they'd know where they stood. He just left it with me. Like it could keep."* That is such grown-up emotional intelligence on the page.
2. He's a powerlifter! ("Badly. Best I've ever done is third.") THAT explains the forearms and the thick chest under the henley in Chapter 1 without him being some generic six-pack gym bro!

And yet, underneath how ridiculously happy and gooey this chapter made me, there is this growing, aching dread in the back of my throat. Because look at how real this is for Vee—feeling like her hand had been wrong all its life until he held it—and look at how real Pace is being with her. He took her to *Christine's*, a place that clearly means something to him, where the owner knows him by name. Does Randi go to Christine's? Somehow I cannot picture bone-to-camel-to-oatmeal Randi tearing blistered pita bread and eating baba ghanoush with her hands! And tomorrow (or tonight?!) Randi is going to want the report. Or Vee is going to tell Randi everything over lunch. My heart is in my mouth.
