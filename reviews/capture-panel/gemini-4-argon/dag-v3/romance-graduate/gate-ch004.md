Vee looks at Randi and sees the undisputed queen of the campus, someone who isn't even competing with the other girls in Chi Latte because she's so effortlessly above it, someone who probably went to boarding school in Switzerland or has a French mother because of the way she arches her *a*'s and holds her *s*'s. Randi gives away *nothing* of herself while making the other person feel like they just got handed the keys to the kingdom.

The blurb said *Vee never lets herself notice how much she wants Randi too*, and watching her misread her own crush as social dazzle is delicious.

Also—the professor ending the lecture on *"latent variables"* right as Randi swoops in to take Vee to coffee? And the spoons ringing against the insides of all the cups in Chi Latte (*With a Long Spoon*!)? I see what you're doing, book, and I love it. Now bring on Pace.
