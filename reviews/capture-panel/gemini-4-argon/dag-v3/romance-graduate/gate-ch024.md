Let me back up before I start screaming in all caps, because what Randi just did in this room is simultaneously the hottest, funniest, most tender, and most diabolically brilliant power play of the entire book so far. Remember back in Chapter 18 when Randi confronted Pace over the untouched cream stew and said, *"How long are you going to take?"* and accused him of keeping Fridays and Saturdays with Vee to himself? And Randi stops her dead in her tracks: *"Not today... Tomorrow night."*

Saturday night! Randi just took control of the calendar! Is Randi clearing Friday night for herself with Pace, OR is Randi setting up Saturday night so that *she* is at Pace's house when Vee walks in wearing *Randi's* cognac heels and *Randi's* green-and-gold silk scarf?! *"See you soon, gorgeous"*—not "Tell me how it goes on Sunday," not "Call me after," but *See you soon, gorgeous*, right as her secret emotional tell (the long *s*'s and the arched *a*'s!) flares up louder than usual! And the scarf! Remember Chapter 15 at lunch when Randi told that story about a man tying her wrists to a headboard with an Hermès scarf?! And here she says: *"I'd have to make you cover up for room inspection — a scarf, at least. And I've got one that'd be criminal on you"*—Echoing Pace's exact word from Chapter 21 (*"It's criminal, Pace"*)! And then she teaches Vee how to tie it with *"Tension. It has to hold its shape or it'll go limp on you by the second drink. Snug — there."* She is literally dressing Vee in her own silk and her own shoes to send her to Pace's door!

And yet—and this is why Helen Rivers is a complete genius and why I am obsessed with this book—it doesn't read like a cold villain twirling a mustache! Every bit of Randi's affection and desire for Vee in that bedroom is *so* real it made my skin prickle. Look at Randi waking up five minutes ago with no makeup on, a sparse cocoa scatter of freckles across her nose (while Vee spent ten minutes burying her own freckles under foundation!), bare feet with her sky-blue toenails tucked under her, listening to Vee's disaster at The Rusty Nail. When Randi turns Vee's shame inside out—*"You were so far ahead you couldn't see the race, and you ran it anyway, because you're in this, and he saw that. That's not the disaster, honey. And the "Edna Mode" laugh! Vee bending over helplessly laughing in her skirt and boots doing the clipped little Edna Mode voice (*"I have to see what I'm working with"*), and Randi cracking up with her, and then Vee giggling into her hand picturing herself living in Randi's bone-to-camel-to-oxblood closet in nothing but Randi's size-7 shoes (*"You'd be my very own shoe whore"*). Vee standing there in her bra and her long winter skirt with bare legs underneath, not able to see Randi crouching at her feet because the skirt falls over the work—only *feeling* Randi's cool hands slipping up under the hem of her skirt! Being given no say in where her foot went made her surer of it, not less."* That is the thesis of the entire book in a pair of heels!

What is going to happen tomorrow night when Vee walks into Pace's house in the little black dress, Randi's cognac heels, and Randi's green-and-gold silk scarf at her throat?! Is Randi going to be there?! Or is Pace going to recognize Randi's scarf and shoes on Vee's body?! I cannot hit next fast enough.
