REACTION

Oh, Randi. My chest actually hurts for her right now.

We are used to seeing Randi be the one who runs the room, the girl in the Mercedes and the cognac heels who never has to look at the right-hand side of the menu, who plays the whole world with this effortless, glossy certainty. And to watch her walk right into that glass study room so sure of her father—defending him, aiming the whole project at her home county to prove that *her* people did the right thing—only to click on that outlier and see her own name (*MIRANDA*) sitting on top of the worst fraud in the county... God, the way all the motion just went out of her in the chair. *"Cheating bastards,"* ragged and unpracticed, said for the very first time in her life while she stares at the proof that everything she's wearing and driving was paid for by her father taking that money and cutting the workers anyway.

And what really got me—what made my throat go tight—is Vee. For thirty-six chapters we've watched Pace and Randi be a step ahead of Vee, managing her, holding her secrets, looking after her without her knowing the half of it. And here, for the first time, *Vee* is the one who sees Randi when Randi doesn't know she's being looked at. Vee sees the name, sees the color drain out of Randi's face, hears the truth in those two ragged words while Cassie is completely oblivious on the other side of the table—and Vee just quietly steps in and covers her. Doesn't ask, doesn't make her explain, doesn't let Cassie look at the screen; just slides the laptop over and gives Randi busywork to hold onto until her color comes back, and decides she will never make her friend carry that shame out loud.

It's such a quiet, short chapter after the absolute inferno of Chapter 36, and notice the date header—it starts on November 4th, before the break, and finishes "the week before break" (so right around now, mid-November). Which makes me wonder: is Randi going to go to Pace with this? Or is she going to lock it behind her teeth the way she tries to lock everything that hurts? And how much worse is it going to hurt when the secret between the three of them finally blows open, now that we've seen how fiercely, quietly loyal Vee is to Randi when Randi is the one exposed?
