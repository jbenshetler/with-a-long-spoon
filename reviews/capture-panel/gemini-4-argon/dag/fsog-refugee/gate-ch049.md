REACTION

Oh, my heart just melted into a puddle on the kitchen floor. *This* is how Pace answers the December fight! Not by giving a speech he still isn't ready or clean enough to give, and not by pretending the coat on Friday night erased what she asked him for—when she stood at his door before break and cried *Out there, what are we? How do I say what this is to Cassie?* he actually took that in, carried it through the whole awful month of chicken and broccoli, and now, with his back turned at the sink so it doesn't feel like a grand performance, he says, *"I want to meet your people."*

And the way he knows every single one of them! Meg's boots from the hike in October (*"Your heel came up out of the right one when you pushed off"*—not only did he remember the name she dropped once, he was watching her heel slip while they walked!), Kayla talking the paint off a wall, Cassie. When a man listens to your rambling at his back while he washes dishes and keeps a whole shelf of your friends' names in his head, and then isn't even slightly threatened when you nervously bring up Theo who asked you out last spring (*"Bring Theo"*), that is security and devotion on a level that makes my chest ache. And *"Tables have ends. Somebody ends up at one"*—he's already thinking about how not to leave anyone out!

Better still is what Vee does right back at the end. She remembers Chili's—the four screens, the roar of the room, him squaring his fork to the table edge because he couldn't hear her—and instead of making it a big thing or reminding him of a bad night, she just quietly hands him the choice of venue so he'll be somewhere he can actually breathe and hear. That mutual, unspoken care—where he steps out into her "out there" world for her, and she protects his ears and his peace inside it—is the purest romance beat they've ever had.

Of course, sitting right underneath all my melting is the giant, ticking bomb of this gathering: he deflected her immediate *"We could do a dinner. The three of us"* with Randi by expanding it to Cassie and Meg and Kayla, which is smart of him (can you imagine the three of them at a small table with "real napkins" pretending Randi and Pace only ever said nine words about math at a coffee shop?!), *and* it fulfills her real need to bring him into her whole life with Cassie. But Randi and Pace and Cassie and Vee are all going to be in the same room together! After Vee just kissed Randi with both hands on her face on Friday, and tasted her own salt on Pace's mouth on Saturday, and is going to Tuesday night Anthropology + dinner with Randi in forty-eight hours! I cannot get to the next chapter fast enough.
