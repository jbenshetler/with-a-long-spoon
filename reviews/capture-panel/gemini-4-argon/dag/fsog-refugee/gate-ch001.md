REACTION

Wait. Wait a second. *"There you are, Randi."*

I literally gasped out loud when he said her name. Because of the jacket blurb, I spent that entire scene—from the moment she walked through the door, the apple in the kitchen, the bench, all of it—completely assuming this was Vee! The twenty-one-year-old sorority girl, the one who has been "quietly starving"—and then at the absolute peak of the scene, when she breaks open and cries, he calls her *Randi*.

Oh my god. That changes *everything* about what happens in the bathroom after. Because the jacket says Randi and Pace are lovers who plan a game for Vee in bed, and that Vee is the first woman Randi has ever let herself want. And now I'm watching Randi—not Vee!—get cracked wide open by Pace's tenderness and his patience, and she goes into the bathroom and looks in the mirror and decides *she can never let him see her like that again.* She literally locks the door inside herself ("The one he had found was going back behind it, and she was not coming out again") because being seen and being tenderly held by him is too terrifying for her to bear. Is *that* why they bring Vee in?! Is Vee going to be Randi's shield so Pace stops looking directly at *her*?! My head is spinning, what a way to open a book.

And Pace. God, Pace is *everything* I read this genre hoping to find and almost never actually get. From the first five pages—the way he kisses her when she comes in and then backs off slightly so she can decide whether to chase his mouth; the honeycrisp apple sliced thin with the cut sides up because he just quietly notices how she eats and wants to make it easy for her; the way she circles her finger and he turns the chair and sits where she wants him, pleased to be directed by her. There is zero posturing in this man. None of that fake-alpha barking or cruelty dressed up as control.

When they got to the bedroom and he went down on one knee to undress her while she was still buttoned up, my stomach flipped. And then on the bench—"Tell me your word. Tell me again if you forget. I won't be upset. Tell me anytime." And the way he notices that she's bracing because every guy before him has been in a hurry, and he just tells her to breathe and waits her out. The twenty-second pause at her head between strikes just holding her face and scratching her scalp, taking her heels off her feet and holding her bare arch in his hands... and then at the end, when he's still hard from the kitchen and he just sets that awareness down internally (*That's not what this night was for*) and wraps her in cashmere and holds her for twenty minutes. That combination of total, relentless physical control (making her beg in her real voice, not her pretty performative voice) and absolute unhurried care is *devastatingly* hot.

And it breaks my heart a little bit already, because he's sitting on that bed thinking he just had a breakthrough with the woman he's falling for, respecting her closed bathroom door because of whatever happened in his past with "Daphne," and meanwhile Randi is in the mirror putting her mask back on for good and digging a bottle cap under her thumbnail to ground herself in pain instead of letting him comfort her. I am *hooked*.
