REACTION

Oh my god, this chapter had my pulse in my throat from three different directions at once.

First of all, Dr. Marsh walking in on the first night of Anthro and dropping Marcel Mauss’s *The Gift* and the concept of *hau*—that a gift carries the spirit of the giver and wants to go home—right while Randi is sitting there wearing the green-and-gold silk scarf she gave Vee in October, and right after Vee spent the weekend in Randi's borrowed stilettos?! That is how you use a college lecture in a novel without it feeling like homework. Every single object in this triangle—the scarf, the shoes, the flannel shirt, the cashmere blanket—is pulsing with *hau*.

And then that dinner in the loft. Every time I think I have Randi figured out, this book peels back another layer of her and breaks my heart. That story about the lawn boy at the shore house—starting out so bright and wicked and funny about forgetting to count her underwear after using her parents' bed, and then the bottom just dropping out of it when her mother brings the robin's-egg blue panties to Sunday dinner, laundered and folded, and sets them next to her plate in front of the boy: *"They're not mine. They're your color."* You can see Randi’s entire pathology born at that table! Why she keeps her real self hidden, why she thought John in Gstaad was the only "acceptable" life, why her mother's points-and-edges hug in Chapter 51 made her hide her bracelet. Watching her fingernail dig into the rim of her wineglass while she tells Vee about the midnight pancakes (*"On his knee. On the floor"*)—she is so genuinely happy for Vee and so quietly starving to death at the exact same time.

And speaking of starving: Vee turning the olive church coat around on the hook to show Randi the dark bloom soaked into the pale satin lining?! And Randi's eyes going dark and liquid, her voice hoarse with *"Wow"*?! The erotic charge at that booth was thicker than half the actual sex scenes on BookTok. When Randi took Vee's hand across the table and asked *"How much could he see? ... Could he see you were ready?"* and Vee literally had to excuse herself to the restaurant bathroom stall to get off against the cold partition—and at the peak of it, it's *Randi's* dark, liquid eyes behind her eyelids saying *"He could see you were ready. There's no way he couldn't"* while Vee furiously tells herself afterward *"Because that had been about Pace, obviously. All of it. About Pace."* GIRL. Who are you trying to convince?!

And then coming back up the stairs into Randi's deadpan *"Things were really heated. It's a good thing you got relief... After driving out to see Pace."* Randi 1000% knew what Vee just did in that bathroom and let her off the hook with a double entendre you could drive a truck through!

Wait—and the ending on the sidewalk! When Randi points to the side of her neck where Vee cried on her, and Vee just unthinkingly bends and kisses her bare neck, and Randi *flinches* and claims she's ticklish?! Randi didn't flinch because it tickled, she flinched because either her whole body jolted with how badly she wants Vee, or Brooke's creepy sorority-house warning from Chapter 54 just flashed in her head out in public, or both! And meanwhile Vee walks home relieved she didn't have to tell Randi about tasting herself on Pace's mouth yet. And tomorrow is Wednesday—the big group dinner where Pace and Randi finally "properly meet" in front of Cassie, Kayla, and Meg. I cannot turn the page fast enough.
