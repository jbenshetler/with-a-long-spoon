REACTION

Wait—a *two-page* chapter?! I don't think I've ever seen a chapter this short in my life, and my first instinct was *wait, that's it?* followed immediately by *oh, that was a sniper shot.*

Because for twenty-seven chapters we have been living exclusively inside this airtight three-person greenhouse—Pace, Randi, Vee, with Cassie on the outside looking only at Vee—and suddenly the window gets cracked open and we find out Pace has a *Cassie*. A tiny, bleached-blonde, queer cycling buddy named Sheri who has been riding hills with him on Saturdays for two years, who was *at the Rusty Nail on Thursday night*, who kept an eye on Vee for him after he walked out, and who knows about Randi!

I love how much this tiny scene does without breaking a sweat. First of all, learning that someone was watching over Vee at that bar while she nursed her drink and turned down guys—and reporting back to Pace that she left alone—gives me this warm, protective ache, even with Sheri tossing in *"Hell of a backside on her, though."* Second, watching Mr. Compartmentalized, Mr. "I only answer the exact question asked," open his mouth to say *"Vivienne"* and just *spill* down the hill about Vee running her thumb over the walnut chair joint he spent a weekend on and picking up his math book without dropping it like it burned her... *"Listen to you,"* is right! He doesn't even hear himself! He is so stupidly, helplessly in love with Vee that even his sarcastic cycling friend clocks it in thirty seconds flat.

And then the ending—hearing their whole secret arrangement said out loud to an normal, outside person for the very first time:
*"She know about the redhead?"*
*"She knows. She likes her."*
*"And the redhead?"*
*"Not yet."*
*"If that's not already complicated, it will be. But I bet it's worth it."*

Hearing it stripped of all the sun-porch romance and sorority-room seduction and reduced to plain English across two handlebars on a cold Saturday morning made my stomach drop in the best way. And notice what today is: *Saturday, October 24*. Tonight is the night Vee walks through his door in the little black dress, Randi's cognac heels, and Randi's green-and-gold silk scarf to make it right. Bring on Saturday night immediately.
