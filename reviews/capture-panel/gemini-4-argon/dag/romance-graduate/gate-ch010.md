Was it a move to lock Vee in deeper, or was it Randi flipping off Brooke's whole world and claiming the girl *she* chose on the quad? Both. It's definitely both, and that's why this book is eating me alive.

Now bring on Sunday and the mountain. I want them on that trail immediately.
