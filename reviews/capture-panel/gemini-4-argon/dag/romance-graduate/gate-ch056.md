REACTION

God, I love Cassie. And I love how this book uses Cassie as the exact moral and emotional tuning fork for Vee's life.

That whole sequence where Vee is trying to explain why she came home in Pace's grey sweatpants and Randi's strappy stilettos—"We *talked*, Cass, we *talked*"—while completely fabricating a story about Pace shrinking a cranberry wool sweater to doll-size and dyeing her pants in the wash... and Cassie just sits there, turns her highlighter over, and says deadpan: *"Pace. Shrank your sweater."* And then puts her head back and laughs because they *both* know Pace is a human micrometer who has never shrunk a garment in his life, and Cassie lets her keep the cover story without making her confess what she actually did under that coat! It is such a warm, funny, real best-friend moment.

And yet, right underneath the warmth and the comedy, you can feel the exact cost of what happened this weekend. Notice what Vee just told Cassie: *"We talked."* They didn't talk! Vee took Randi's advice—*"You don't do it with words. Words are the whole war"*—and showed up naked under her coat to bypass the conversation completely. She surrendered the question she walked out over in December, and right now she is so high on the relief of having him back, on the mushrooms on toast and the salted persimmons and the three orgasms on the cashmere blanket, that she thinks the tent pole of the conflict just vanished. Even putting the flannel shirt back on at the end—in December she stripped it off in the freezing dark because she realized her own body heat was all that was in it, and tonight she pulls it right back over her head, touches the cold cinderblock wall, and sleeps easy because she feels secure again.

Also, wait a second: what was that beat about Cassie's mother?! *"My mother says thank you for the card. She wants to know when she gets to see you again."* Why did Vee's hand stop mid-air and go to the collar of her robe, and why did something go through her middle *"that she had no intention of examining tonight, warm and specific, like a hand cupped briefly at the back of her neck"*? Did something happen when Vee visited Cassie's family earlier, or is it just the contrast of a mother who sees her warmly without her own mother's suffocating smallness? Whatever it is, the way Cassie clocks the exact size of Vee's reaction and drops it before it asks anything of her is pure gold.

That’s two quiet, domestic bridge chapters in a row now after the massive heat of 55 and 56—57 in Pace's kitchen and 58 in the dorm room—and while both of them were genuinely lovely and full of character work, I am *very* ready for Tuesday to hit. We have the first Tuesday night Anthro class with Randi, the post-coat debrief over dinner (where Vee is debating whether to tell Randi about tasting herself on Pace's mouth!), and this big group hangout Pace is planning for all of Vee's friends where Randi and Pace are going to have to "properly meet" in front of Cassie, Meg, and Kayla. Bring on Tuesday!
