REACTION

*“Nothing at all under the silk, and nobody in this room who had ever taught her what to be ashamed of.”*

God, that line hit me right in the chest. That is the entire arc of Vivienne Thorne in a single sentence. When she told Cassie at the doors that she was a little relieved her mother wasn't there, and Cassie tried to be a good friend and make it about folding chairs and watching her mother's face—no, THIS was the truth she couldn't hand Cassie. If her mother had been sitting in that room, Vee would have been in the "good-enough black" from the back of her closet, or mincing across that stage with her hand clamped to her hip trying not to be a *floozie*. Because her parents couldn't afford the flight, she got to walk into that room completely unpoliced by her childhood.

And I love that the ceremony didn't go the way her fantasy went! She'd had that whole daydream earlier about walking in late and stopping the room, and instead it's just a warm, dull afternoon full of navy dresses and bad suits where nobody turns around on their own. So if she wants the moment the dress was built for—the dress Pace stayed up all night making *to* her, the four-inch heels Randi buckled onto her feet that she blew her emergency credit card on—she has to *choose* it in broad daylight. That first tiny careful step, and then taking a breath and making the second step long on purpose, stalking across the stage flashing bare hip to a room full of strangers' parents until a murmur goes through the crowd and she gets wet right there on the stage? YES, VEE. Look at her! She isn't just reacting to Pace and Randi anymore; her pride and her arousal have braided together into the same thing, and it belongs to *her*.

Also, Cassie continues to be the most quietly wonderful friend on earth ("Come on, streetlight. I'll buy you a terrible sandwich and you can be famous at me" made me tear up a little, especially after Vee ditched her in the previous chapter for the music practice room with Randi). And Cassie snapping that photo at the exact second of the long stride!

Wait, though—Pace is in *Berkeley* for three days?! Hold on. My brain is immediately doing the math. Pace is across the country in a hotel room opening that devastating uncropped photo of Vee's bare hip in the burgundy silk... and Vee is back on campus, glowing, buzzing with exhibitionist heat in that dress and those heels, with Pace gone for three days and Randi right here. Oh, my god. Is she going to go show Randi the dress in person tonight?!
