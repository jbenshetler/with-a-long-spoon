REACTION

Oh, my god. I am sitting here with my hand over my mouth because this chapter just broke my heart in two completely opposite directions at once.

First of all, the conversation in the kitchen while he’s cooking the salmon?! I was practically holding my breath through every single line. Vee sitting there in his shirt, swinging her feet, happily telling Pace all about her "new friend" Randi—"You remember Randi? From the froyo day"—and Pace playing completely dumb with *"Short brunette?"* When Vee tells him how she bumped into Randi outside the dining hall at the beginning of the semester ("It's a small world") and how Randi can't seem to find what she wants with boys, and then describes Randi's double mouth-kiss while brushing her own lower lip (where we just watched her taste Randi's pastry glaze!), and Pace's grin spreads before he puts down the spatula to come kiss her and cup her breast... I wanted to shake him and kiss him at the exact same time. The dramatic irony is so thick you could cut it with a knife. He's standing there flipping fish while his girlfriend tells him about his other lover kissing her on the mouth, and instead of confessing, he gets turned on and kisses her!

And yet—how am I supposed to stay mad at him when he does what he does in this chapter? Watching Vee realize she’s losing weight without trying while eating whole plates of his food ("She ate like a field hand at his table now, she who'd spent years pushing a plate away half done")—especially right after we remember Randi forcing herself to swallow every bite at that same table—hit me so hard. And then Vee lying in the dark looking at his chest and the pale line of his scar, admitting to herself that she loves him, and whispering *"Peter"* into his ear while he's asleep because he gave her his real name.

And then the bathroom drawer on Friday the 13th. When she reached for the scrunchies and the counter was wiped bare, my stomach dropped right through the floor—not just because I thought he'd minded her mess, but because my first paranoid thought was *he cleared the counter so Randi wouldn't see Vee's things during the week!* Wait—DID he put them in the drawer initially because of Randi, or because he likes a clean counter, or both?! No, look at what he actually did: he emptied out his own top drawer, moved all of his carefully ordered things into the bottom drawer where they're crammed too tight together, put her pins in a little dish, stood her face cream up, and left clean liner with room for more of her in the *top* drawer. "He'd folded his whole ordered self down into a single drawer. He'd given her the top one. The easy one. The one your hand goes to first." That is one of the most romantic things I have ever read in my life, and simultaneously a ticking time bomb, because what happens if Randi opens the top bathroom drawer in the house where she knows which bedroom drawer keeps the white undershirts?!
