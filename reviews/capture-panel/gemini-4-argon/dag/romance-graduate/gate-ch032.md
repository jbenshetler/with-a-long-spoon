Oh, my god. I am so torn in half by this chapter I don't even know whether to melt into a puddle or scream into a pillow.

First of all: that scene at the stove where Vee casually chats to Pace about her "new friend Randi" from stats class while he turns the salmon?! My stomach was doing backflips the entire time. Watching Pace stand there with a spatula and play completely dumb—*"Sure."* *"You like her."*—and even mime two dramatic European air-kisses across the kitchen while Vee tells him how she "accidentally" bumped into Randi outside the dining hall and how Randi kisses her on the mouth twice! And when Vee says *"No, on the mouth. And then she comes back and does it again,"* and brushes her lower lip with her tongue, and he goes rock-hard on the spot and gets his bedroom eyes?! Of course he does, because Randi has probably already told him about the pastry-glaze kiss, or he's picturing his two lovers kissing each other while Vee thinks she's just telling a cute story! And Vee playful-teasing him—*"I'll pretend it was that kind of French. After you feed me"*—not knowing how close to the bone she's cutting. The dramatic irony is almost unbearable.

And then she lies in the dark looking at his scar, admits to herself that she loves him, and whispers *"Peter"* into his ear while he sleeps. My heart broke for her right there. Because she really, truly believes that actions speak louder than words and that what he's giving her is simple and whole. After a lifetime of her mother policing her body, Pace feeds her until she's full and her body just settles into itself.

And then the bathroom drawer. Wait—let's talk about the bathroom drawer on Friday the 13th! When she reached for the scrunchies and the counter was wiped bare, my stomach plummeted right along with hers—and my very first dark thought was: *Did he hide her stuff because Randi was over here during the week?!* And maybe that's STILL half true! What if he cleared the counter so Randi wouldn't walk into the bathroom on a Monday or Wednesday and see Vee's scrunchy tower and face cream colonizing the sink, AND at the exact same time, he genuinely gave Vee the top drawer?! Because cramming all of his own neatly ordered razors and combs into the bottom drawer so she can have the top one—with a little dish for her pins and empty shelf liner waiting for more of her—is the most devastatingly romantic thing a silent man could ever do. *unless* that top drawer is also a way to keep her invisible when she isn't there! Wait—no, if Randi opens the top drawer looking for something, she's going to see a shrine to Vee! Which one is it?! Is he clearing a permanent space for Vee, or hiding her off the counter, or is Randi About To Open That Top Drawer?!

Everything in this house is a ticking time bomb wrapped in a love story, and Friday, November 13th is a hell of a date to land on.
