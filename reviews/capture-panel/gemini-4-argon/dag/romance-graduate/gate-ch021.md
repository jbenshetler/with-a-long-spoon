REACTION

Wait, a two-page chapter! And honestly? After the massive, breathless high of Chapter 20, this was the exact right way to handle the morning-after dorm recap without slowing the book down. In fact, I just said in my notes that I was ready for a break from the long dorm-room recaps with Cassie, and the book gave me a tight, sharp sixty-second vignette where the whole point is that Vee *can't* recap it for Cassie anymore.

That image of Vee lifting the whole middle out of the story "the way a ladle takes soup from the pot and the surface closes without a hole"—and telling herself it's a kindness to Cassie, when really she's hoarding it—is so real. Especially because we know who she *will* tell the middle to. She edits Pace down to "sweet" and "a good kisser" and a plate of chicken fra diavolo for her actual best friend, while saving the real, filthy, terrifyingly intimate stuff for Randi. And what was that half-second look that crossed Cassie's face when Vee first walked in? Did Cassie feel a pang of being left behind, or was she worried for a second before she saw Vee's grin?

Also, the comedy and the heat of Vee pulling her skirt zipper halfway down with her towel in hand and suddenly remembering she has no panties on underneath because she traded them to Pace! "The next thing she took off would not be a story she was editing. It would be on the floor, and there would be nothing left to say." I laughed out loud. She drove all the way home in October with bare thighs under her skirt and frosted-plum toenails, and even her mother's shaming voice has lost its teeth this morning ("The word could come; she found she didn't much mind it this morning").

And then that last line: *"She had never in her life been so happy."* Oh god. Reading that right after the jacket blurb's promise sits in the back of my head makes my stomach drop in the best, most delicious way. She is floating on air, completely bare in her cold dorm room, and we still haven't seen Randi's reaction to Friday night. Give me the next chapter right now.
