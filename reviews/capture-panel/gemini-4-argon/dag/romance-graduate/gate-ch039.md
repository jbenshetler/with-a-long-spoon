Oh, my chest hurts. That title drop—*She had been received. She had been received warmly, she would remind herself, in the small private way you remind yourself of evidence whose weight you are not sure of*—hit me right in the ribs.

This whole chapter is a masterclass in the quiet, excruciating geometry of an unequal secret, and both halves of it—the cafeteria with Cassie and the porch with Pace—are doing the exact same thing from different angles: showing us everything Vee has to crop out of frame so the picture doesn't break.

First, that dinner with Cassie. I loved every detail of Vee's Thanksgiving in Ohio—her dad burning the rolls on purpose, her mom touching her own collarbone and putting her "nurse face" on because Vee has gotten thin from shedding all that shame and stress, her dad pricing the burgundy silk dress in his head and looking for the catch. And Vee realizing that her working-class dad would understand Pace taking the dress *off* her way better than he'd ever understand Pace *sewing* it on his sun porch! That is so sharp and true about masculinity and class. And then Cassie—God bless Cassie—just stopping with her fork and asking, *"So what is he. Not to your dad. To you."* And Vee having to admit it's both that she can't say and doesn't want to.

And then Monday morning on the porch. Watching Vee spend weeks building a proxy sentence around *"I love you"* because she bumped into the invisible rule from the inside that she isn't allowed to say it first... I was holding my breath right along with her. *"I had been eating crackers my whole life. And you fed me a meal. I didn't know what a meal was until you fed me one. And now I know."* Coming right off the homemade buffalo-mozzarella pizza in the last chapter where she tasted every separate ingredient like a bowl of cut strawberries instead of cotton candy, it's the most vulnerable, gorgeous thing she could possibly hand him.

And what does Pace do? He takes *two* breaths. His hand starts two different responses on her shin and puts both of them down. And then he gives her: *"I love how good we are together"* and *"What you do — what your body does — I've never. I'm glad you feel it too."*

I actually gasped in disgust—not because Pace is being cruel on purpose, but because you can hear the exact translation happening in his head! Either he heard her say *I love you* and deliberately stepped sideways into *"I love how good we are together"* and *"what your body does"* to keep it inside the bounds of what he and Randi agreed to, OR he genuinely reduced her soul-baring metaphor to sex! And watching Vee sit on the folding table in the dorm laundry room two days later, running her sentence and his sentence side by side and realizing *either end of it hurt*—that either he understood and deflected, or it went right past him—and then filing it away in the growing pile of "small flickers" she doesn't look at... I am aching for her. And you *know* she's going to take this straight to Saturday brunch with Randi, or worse, *not* take it to Randi because it's retired into her body now.
