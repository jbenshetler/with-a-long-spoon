REACTION

What a smart, sharp little chapter. Calling it "Cropped" is such a great tell, because it's not just the induction photo Vee had to cut off at the waist before sending it home to Ohio (Just like she tried to show Randi the waist-up version of the pinup before giving her the whole uncropped length of it!). She had to crop *Pace* and she had to crop *herself* just to fit through her parents' front door.

Every single detail of that Thanksgiving visit felt so lived-in and exact. Her mother touching her own collarbone while looking at the photo, and then touching Vee's collarbone at the door before even saying hello because Vee's gotten thin—after all the chapters of her mother's shaming voice in Vee's head, seeing the real woman put on her "nurse face" and watch Vee eat seconds like it might be a trick (because she thinks Vee is making herself sick, when really she's eating like a field hand and burning it all off in Pace's bed!) was almost darkly funny and a little heartbreaking. And her mechanic father pricing the silk dress with his eyes and going quiet because an expensive gift means his daughter is in deep with an older man, when the reality is so much stranger and more intimate: *Honestly, my dad would take it better if I told him Pace takes that dress off me than that he made it. Sex he understands. The sewing he never would.* That observation about "weird" being worse than "wrong" to a man like her father—because wrong fits in his world and weird doesn't—is so dead-on.

And then the gut-punch right in the middle of it: *"I told them he was my boyfriend. I think I put a question mark on it. Out loud. Like I was asking them."*

That's the jacket blurb ("He gives her everything but a word for what they are") hitting the brick wall of normal family life. In the woods, in the dark, with his flannel shirt and her top bathroom drawer, she doesn't need the word. The second she has to sit at a Thanksgiving table and explain why she's glowing and losing weight and wearing couture silk, the missing word turns into a question mark out loud. And Cassie—god, I love Cassie—doesn't laugh. She just waits until Vee finishes listing all the things she couldn't tell her dad, stops eating, and asks the only question that matters: *"So what is he. Not to your dad. To you."* And when Vee admits she neither can say nor wants to, Cassie lets her off the hook, and that final line—*Vee was grateful. She was also, a little, not*—says everything. Part of Vee wants someone who isn't inside the spell to force her to look at the shape of what she's in. Also, wait: Thanksgiving just happened! That means Pace just had his Thanksgiving with Sheri, and now everyone is back on campus for the final stretch before the end of the term, and Tuesday/Thursday night Anthro with Randi ("Just us") is coming up.
