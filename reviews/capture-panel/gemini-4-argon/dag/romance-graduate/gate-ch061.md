Oh, I *loved* this. God, this is why I'm still reading this book and why it puts ninety-nine percent of what's on my Kindle to shame. A solo masturbation scene in a romance novel is almost always throwaway filler—five paragraphs of "she thought of his abs and came" before the plot moves on—and instead this is a whole psychological event.

Everything from the dinner with Randi in the last chapter comes home to roost here in the quiet of her dorm room. The detail of closing Cassie's two-inch window so the radiator turns the room into a shut hothouse, putting on Pace's giant worn flannel with all the buttons left open... you can feel the exact temperature of that room. And what she's actually doing here isn't just getting off; she's auditing her own shame. That line—*He had looked at the whole slick fact of her as if it were true, as if it were fine. As if the plenty of her required no pardon before he brought it to her mouth*—hit me right in the chest. And then the way Randi's voice braids right into the middle of her fantasy alongside Pace's! *Had Randi's looked like this? Sticky too. Shining. Had she meant a mouth carrying the wet back to her? Or her own fingers, lifted to her mouth?* Both of them are in bed with her in her head now, whether she's ready to name that or not.

And the best decision the author made was having her *stop* a breath's width away from tasting her own fingers. In a lesser book she'd just do it, check the box, lesson learned, shame cured. Here, she can sniff it—she can realize the boys' jokes were a lie and that she smells like "a hothouse with its doors shut, the air around a tropical flower without its sweetness"—and she can get her tongue right to the edge, and then *she can't cross it*. Not alone, not yet. She wipes her hand on the sheet and comes to the smell of herself and the memory of Pace's face instead. And then at the very end, after she's aired the room out for Cassie, she finds the cool wet spot she wiped on the sheet, settles her bare hip right into her own mess, and lies there with her mouth "still empty." What a killer final line. It leaves the whole circuit unclosed and buzzing for next time.
