TELL A FRIEND: Okay, so. You know how I keep saying I want a book that's actually *written* and still makes me put my phone facedown on the table? This is that, and I'm annoyed about how right I was. It's a man, a sorority girl, and a redhead with a body her mother taught her to apologize for, and the first two are running a game on the third, and the book tells you that on the jacket and then makes you fall for it exactly as she does — I hated myself a little at every lunch and leaned in anyway. The people have insides. The man is tender and *wrong* and both are true. Now the bad: it's built in this weird rhythm — a long charged chapter, then a two-page debrief on a dorm bunk, and by the third debrief you're skimming for dialogue. There's a stretch around the middle where every good scene gets retold twice. New named friends keep showing up late. And it ends at thirty-one chapters with the three of them still not in a room together and the redhead still not knowing, which is either a gorgeous held breath or a cliffhanger sale depending on my mood. But I'm buying Book Two, so. Read the fitting chapter first if you don't believe me, then go back and start at the bench.
