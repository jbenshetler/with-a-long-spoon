REACTION

Ten chapters I've been asking for this room and the book gave it to me with his mouth already on her and no runway at all. No scene-setting, no whose-house, no how-did-we-get-here. First line, she's coming. Good. That's the only way this chapter could have been written without feeling like a reveal, and it isn't a reveal — it's a Tuesday.

And then the thing that made me put the phone face-down on my chest for a second: **he lifted his head and gave her time to see it.** Deliberately. And she said *come here* and took her own taste off his mouth with no hesitation whatsoever, licked his lip clean, and then wiped the corner of his mouth with her thumb and put the thumb between her lips and said **"Now it's mutual."**

Chapter 53. Chapter 60. A girl with her fingers a breath's width from her own mouth until her lungs hurt and *nothing in the room told her why*. And here is the woman who caused it doing it as a joke, as a courtesy, as *tidying up*. The book did not point. It never points. It just ran the same act through a different body and let me sit in the difference, and the difference is the whole novel. Vee couldn't close half an inch alone in a locked room. Randi closes it as punctuation.

And now I know what *"I don't."* cost her, which was nothing. She wasn't being kind at that table and she wasn't being cruel. She was being accurate. She removed the floorboard because she genuinely does not believe there is a floor there. That's somehow worse.

Then: they talk about her while he's inside her. **"She kisses me first now."** — a beat of stillness — **"Does she."** — **"She looks at you, too."** — *her body tightened around him.* They are feeding each other intelligence on this girl as foreplay, and it is the hottest thing in the chapter and the coldest thing in the book, at the same time, out of the same mouths, which is the trick this series does that nothing else on my Kindle can do.

But here's what actually got me. I came in braced for a conspiracy scene — logistics, timelines, *when she's ready*, the kitchen tableau, two people planning a girl. **There was no plan.** Not one word of it. It's two people who cannot stop saying her name at each other. *I miss her during the day. More at night.* **"I nearly turned around."** And his hands stopping her dead with him buried, and *"You liked it."* That's not handlers comparing notes. That's two addicts.

And the breast. God. *Her breast in my hand. All that softness. The weight of it.* And then offering him her own nipple and saying **"And her nipple in my mouth"** — using her own body as the proxy because the real one isn't in the room, *as if the wanting could cross through the body she offered him.* I've read a thousand threesome-adjacent scenes and I have never seen want displaced that cleanly. She came with her own breast in her hand. That's a woman fucking a ghost and knowing it.

And then Vee's misread, which is the detail I'm going to be chewing on all week: **"She made me laugh. She does it on purpose. She knows exactly when I'm about to ask for more."** Randi thinks Vee is *playing*. Thinks the deflection is timing, is craft, is a girl running a game back at her. It isn't. It's the vault. It's the same reflex that built a cranberry sweater and filed a bathroom stall under *obviously*. Randi — who reads everyone, who has never once misjudged a room — has got this exactly backwards because she cannot conceive of a person who doesn't know what she wants. She's courting someone she believes is courting her. That's the trapdoor and it's under *her*.

And then the ending of it, which I did not expect to break me. **"You know she loves you, right?"** — *"She hasn't said it."* He's been counting. He's been counting the whole time, the man who had it out of the gate on a doorstep and got it stopped with a coat, and he's sitting there keeping the ledger like a boy. *"She doesn't have to."* And his face softened before he could do anything about it, **and Randi saw it.**

**"She's not the only one in love."**

And *he couldn't tell her she was wrong.* And she gave him **no smile and no rescue from the sentence** — the exact same move she ran on Vee at the table. Say nothing. Eat the bread. Let it stand. Except this time I can't call it ruthless, because she left it there with her thumb moving on the center of his palm, and she'd just kissed his palm, and the sentence was true, and *Vee's name stayed in the room between their breathing.* The open sentence stayed with it. That's three books of withholding turned, for once, into the opposite thing.

Also: she kissed the center of his palm. Nobody feeds that man. Somebody just did.

What I'm nervous about: the register. The Vee chapters are written from inside a nervous system — the strands in the lamplight, the hothouse with its doors shut. This was spare and plain and mostly verbs, and the one flourish was a joke (*Subtle. / Elegant.*). I think that's Pace and I think it's deliberate, because ch 50 inside him was bags in rows and a bleach bucket — the man doesn't narrate himself. But I noticed the cleanup stretch ran a while past its own climax, and the real peak of the chapter was eight lines earlier, and I felt the gap.

What I'd text at 1 a.m.: *they're not scheming. THAT'S the problem. If they were scheming she could survive finding out.*
