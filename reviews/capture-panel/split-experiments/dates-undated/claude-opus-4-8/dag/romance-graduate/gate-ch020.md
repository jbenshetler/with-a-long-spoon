A whole pot since three. And the salad she ate all of, down to the drowned leaves at the bottom. She ate the thing that wasn't the thing he made for her. I could not breathe through that detail. He feeds people. I want to text someone RIGHT NOW: she wouldn't eat the stew.

And he poured every glass of wine she's ever drunk in that house.
