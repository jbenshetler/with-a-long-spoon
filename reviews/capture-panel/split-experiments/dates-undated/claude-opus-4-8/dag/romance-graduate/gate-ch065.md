REACTION

Okay so I'm still vibrating from 61 — the thing the jacket promised and I half-refused to fully believe, and then there it was, Randi astride him saying Vee's name without saying it, both of them in love with her and feeding each other the wanting like it's dinner. "I want her to know exactly what she does to me." That broke the chapter open. So I came into 65 carrying *that*, which changes everything about this little wax appointment, and I think the book knows it does.

Because here's what got me, cold, in the honey-lit waiting room: "barely stings." Randi said barely stings, and poured her the wine story, and "the most expensive thing in the room," and left out the entire back half — the roll-over, the rest of it, the part Vee didn't know she'd agreed to. And two weeks ago I'd have read that as Randi being warm and a little breezy. Now I read it as Randi sending her lamb somewhere to be opened up smooth, *for Pace*, without the whole truth, exactly the way they chose her without the whole truth. The withholding is the whole relationship in miniature. I don't even think it's cruelty. That's what's awful. It's just how Randi loves — one road ahead, handing back the map with a corner torn off.

And then the chapter does the thing I keep saying this book does that saves it from being the fourth identical solo scene: the wrong hand. "Her body knew that grammar." The flat impersonal palm pressing her thigh down, *don't move*, holding her the exact shape she's learned to want and wanting nothing from her — and it tears a mile-wide hole where the right hand should be. That's not choreography. That's a real idea about what their room did to her nervous system, that she can't be pinned by anyone now without her whole body reaching for Pace. I felt that in my teeth. And the lit-up wrongness of being turned on *here*, bored stranger, bright cruel light, appalled and aroused at once — that's the grown-up thing I came for. The body telling on her before she can get a hand over its mouth.

But — and I have to be honest with myself — this is another chapter alone inside Vee's head doing the private-arousal arithmetic, and we're a lot of these deep now. What keeps me is that 61 just reloaded the whole gun. I'm not here for Vee's interiority on a table anymore; I'm here because I know what she doesn't, and every scene is now unbearable in the good way. The mother's voice, "the kind of girl who," shutting her eyes on it and it staying — that's good too, the shame she can't curate away.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: *they're TOGETHER together and they talk about her while they do it and now she's getting waxed bare because he asked and Randi "forgot" to mention the worst part and I think I'm going to be sick, send the next book.*

The one thing holding this at a notch below a peak: I want a collision now, bodies in a room, the three of them, or at least Vee catching one true thing. I've been inside her head a long time. I'll follow — but I'm ready for the wall to come down.
