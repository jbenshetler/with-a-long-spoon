REACTION

Oh, I needed this. After that truck I was wound all the way up and this is Vee walking it off with the one honest person in the book — and it let me breathe without making me bored, which is the trick most debrief chapters can't do.

Here's what got me, though, and it's quiet: Vee tells Cassie *almost everything* and I watched exactly where the edit happened. The sandwich gets the full "and then we ate" reverence — rye, horseradish, no pit — which is sweet and a little funny, the way she says it "the way other girls said *and then we kissed.*" But when she gets to the shirt and the truck, she tells it. She actually tells it. "I took my wet shirt off in front of him. On purpose. Me." She's not hiding this one. That's the thing that pulled me — she's *proud.* The girl who portions herself down to apple slices and keeps the cardigan over the better shirt on purpose just narrated taking her top off to a man she's known three dates, out loud, to her friend, and the word she keeps circling is *me, me, I did that.* That's the arc the whole book is building and I felt it land in one line.

And Cassie. God, Cassie. She does the bit — "I had your blood type ready," "No windowless van" — and then the second Vee slows down, "Cassie, who had been doing the dry thing, the file-revising thing, went still and stayed that way." She goes *rapt.* That's the line I'd text a friend. Because Cassie is the Cassandra, Cassie said "Goodbye, Vee" off the pier, Cassie clocked Pace in two minutes — and here she doesn't warn, she doesn't do the *oh Vee.* She just says her name "like setting something down where it wouldn't break," and then "Borrow real boots next time." That's love surrendering. Cassie can see the whole shape of it and she's decided Vee gets to have this anyway. "Keep the shirt." It broke my heart a little, honestly — the one clean instrument in the book just lowered her guard, which means either this is real and good, or Cassie's wrong for the first time and nobody's watching the door anymore.

What I'm suspicious of: nothing new here, and that's fine — this chapter isn't trying to turn anything. It's the exhale. But I clocked that it's the second shortish, quiet-ish beat if I count the truck's back half, and I'm aware the next one needs to *move.* I don't resent this at all — a two-page scene of two people talking is exactly the thing I've said is the best thing a book can do — but my thumb knows a third quiet one would start to cost. Give me Randi. I want to know what Vee does *not* say to Randi over oat milk, now that I've seen what she's willing to say to Cassie.
