**REACTION**

Okay. Two things happened in this chapter and they're not the same size, and I need to talk about the smaller-seeming one first because it's actually the biggest thing this book has done in fifty chapters.

The sex was — fine, it was more than fine, the whole princess/slave reversal was genuinely fun and I loved Vee working out that *he's* the pleasure slave, naked and gleeful and counting his servitude off on her fingers in a hallway. That's the Vee I want, the one with appetite and a sense of humor about it. Him burning the toast because she was examining her own toes — I actually laughed. The "I got mushrooms" as the sum total of a broken man. Good. That's the register that made me stay with this series.

But then. *Then.* He comes up her body and kisses her with her still on his mouth, and her tongue goes looking *before her mind gets there* — "curious, unforbidden, hers" — and then the installed voice slams shut over it and she says "ugh, gross," and he — the way he handles that is the most loving thing he's done in the whole book. Reads which no is which. Doesn't push, doesn't sulk, doesn't *explain*. Leaves her mouth for her until she comes and takes it back. And when she takes it back her tongue goes hunting for the taste *again*, and then AGAIN on her own lip afterward, lying there telling herself a story about how he "must have forgotten to wipe."

That's the chapter. That's the whole book, actually — Vee's body knowing things her mother's voice forbids, and Vee building a comfortable lie over the top of what she just did. And the narrator is too good to let the lie sit flat: "he must have forgotten to wipe. This once" — when she *knows*, she just counted, it's been dozens, hundreds of times. She's been tasting herself on him for months and never knew, or never let herself know, and the moment she's *told* — the moment it's named — the machinery of shame fires. And it doesn't fully win! She chases the salt. "Minor-key, waiting." That little light staying on is doing more work than the entire doorway scene from last chapter, hot as that was.

And Tuesday's coming. She's going to tell Randi "nearly everything," and she'll have to find a way to say it that doesn't sound like what it sounds like — which is exactly the sentence Randi lives inside every single day. Two women, each sitting on the same unspoken want, about to be across a little table from each other. Meanwhile Brooke just put a clock on Randi with that ice-cold "choose" in the foyer, so Randi's coming into Tuesday with a hand tied behind her back. I can feel the book winding these three threads toward the same afternoon and I genuinely don't know which one gives first.

Only wobble: this is the second consecutive chapter that's essentially "Vee is naked and serviced at length in Pace's house," and I've noticed. The food writing is carrying it — the blood orange done "the most him thing she had ever seen done to food," the persimmon with the salt, all of it doubling for him, obviously — and the kiss thing was real plot, so I'm not bored. But if chapter 54 is another morning of being fed, I'll start skimming the descriptions of mushroom cookery. The outside world has to knock on that door soon: the anthro class, Brooke's ultimatum, the MIRA grenade still in somebody's pocket. The house is warm; the book needs to remember the cold exists.
