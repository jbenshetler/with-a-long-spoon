And "that's not a message, that's a ransom note" — I'd text that to my group chat, honestly.

The best beat in the chapter is "twelve." She hadn't even had to count. That's the control I keep staying for.

What I felt under the fun: the book is skipping Cassie. Vee reports everything to Randi, reports everything to Kayla and Meg now, and the girl who had her blood type ready isn't in this chapter at all. My carry-forward dread is doing its work — every new warm body around Vee is another layer between her and the truth, and I don't think it's an accident that the chapter where she's most surrounded by friendliness is the one where the door to Pace's house finally opens.

And that last third. "Would you come to my house Saturday. Two o'clock. That's it."
