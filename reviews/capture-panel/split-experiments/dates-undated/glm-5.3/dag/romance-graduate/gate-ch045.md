The bedroom voice at the wrong size — "Is this what we are now?" coming out too loud because she can't calibrate desperation — that's exactly what it feels like when you've been swallowing a question for weeks and it exits at the wrong pressure. That's the tragedy of Pace, and the book knows it: he's not withholding to be cruel, he's withholding because the word is load-bearing for something she doesn't know about yet, and every honest thing he does give her makes the missing word louder.

The Chili's scene wrecked me a little. She needs a shape she can rest in. *Forgiven* as a warm coat she puts on. I know that woman. I've been that woman.

Either her, or Randi, or the calendar. The "when she's ready" plan has a shelf life and I think she's expiring it faster than they know.
