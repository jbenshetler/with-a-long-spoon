REACTION — Okay, this is the "two people talking" chapter, and I've said a hundred times that the best scene in a book can be exactly that, and this one mostly earns it. The mother touching her own collarbone — I know that woman. I had a mother-in-law like that. "You lied to your mother." / "How am I supposed to tell her it's the sex?" is the laugh-out-loud beat, but the line that got me is "I told them he was my boyfriend. I think I put a question mark on it." Because we, the jacket-readers, know the question mark is *structurally correct* — she genuinely doesn't know what she is — and Vee thinks the question mark is her being girlish. Ouch, ouch.

And Cassie. Cassie asking "is it that you can't say, or that you don't want to" is the sharpest anyone has been to Vee's face in forty-three chapters, including Dr. Marsh, including Randi. Everyone else keeps Vee in the dark *for* her; Cassie just holds the flashlight steady and doesn't swing it around.
