REACTION

Oh, she edited the story.

Cassie got the wet bra, the deliberate undressing, the way Pace looked at her, his shaking hand against her belly. Meg and Kayla get the shirt, the rain, the almost-kiss, the campus cop. It isn’t exactly a lie, but Cassie still has the version with Vee’s real hunger in it—and that made me weirdly relieved after watching Vee carry so much of herself to Randi instead. Cassie remains the person she trusts with what actually happened inside her.

And yes, I am completely gone for Vee being driven out of her mind by a man who waits for the real answer. She knows it sounds absurd because “he gives me what I ask for” should be ordinary, and it isn’t ordinary in her life. That landed harder than any generic speech about him being different could have. She isn’t dazzled by the doctorate or the lifting, though she is adorably proud of both. She’s starving because he makes wanting possible.

But that invitation made my stomach drop even while hers lifted. His house. Saturday. Two o’clock. I know what is in that house. I know what was imagined on that bench before Vee knew either of them. She thinks a door has opened because Pace wants her there now; I know Randi and Pace built the doorway around her weeks ago. If this is the bench chapter, I am already braced to be extremely turned on and extremely angry.

Also: after Randi used Vee’s body to get off, fled without staying, and left Pace hungry in every sense, Pace immediately asks Vee into the house? I do not know whether this is part of their agreed plan, his attempt to move it forward, or something he has chosen because Vee’s undressing in the truck got under his skin. Every possibility is dangerous. And poor Vee is sitting in that lovely gold room letting herself be happy while the reader can see the trapdoor.

Kayla declaring there is no meal at two made me laugh, but I wanted to grab Vee by both shoulders. Babe, there is absolutely something waiting for you at two. You just do not know it was custom-built.
