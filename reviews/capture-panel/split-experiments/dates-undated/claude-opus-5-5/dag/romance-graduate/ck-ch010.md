**WHO'S WHO**

**Randi.** She's the girl on the bench in chapter one. I thought it was Vee for the whole scene, and I still haven't really gotten over that reveal. Black hair, startling blue eyes, a resting face that always looks a little surprised, cold-citrus perfume. She's a sorority girl and sits on the board as Standards chair, so grading other girls is literally her job, and she gets a single room for it. She cuts apples thin and polices every bite, and her mother lives on egg whites. She has a slight accent that Vee assumes is European. I'm still suspicious of it; it could be part of the act. Her safeword is "Red." She's the one running the game, and she's also the one who got broken first.

**Pace.** Math PhD, "finishing, more or less," and young for it. He looks ordinary until you look at all of him. He builds furniture with his hands, including the bench, which hides under a tabletop in his bedroom. He keeps the house warm, plays Steely Dan, drives a white pickup, and lives out west off 614 where it turns to gravel. He's courtly: "May I," he asks before he takes, and he hands the pencil back. Somebody named Daphne is in his past. He once opened a door she had closed, and now he swears he never will. He's decent, I really believe that. But he sets moments up ahead of time ("He had decided this beforehand"), and he reads women with total confidence when I think he's sometimes wrong.

**Vee.** Vivienne Thorne, V.T., "like the university." Econ major, smart, and she reads a whole room at once. She's built bigger than the sorority scale and covers it with the cardigan over the better shirt. Her mother's voice lives in her head ("floozies," "that's plenty"). She did synchronized swimming as a kid. She's starving in every sense and doesn't know it yet.

**Cassie.** Vee's roommate and the only honest person in the book. Boxy, ball cap, says about four words a day. She keeps the window cracked, plays rec sports, and has a lab. "Tossers. They will never know the love of a woman." I'd die for her.

**Brooke.** Chapter president, voice like a perfume, and she doesn't stand up when she wants to stop you. She has filed Pace's name. There's also a tall, silent friend of hers who scared me more than Brooke did.

**Others.** Sloane and Kinsey from the house. Christine runs the little Middle Eastern restaurant and is fond of Pace. Meg is the girl Vee was with when Randi bumped into her.

**HOW I FEEL ABOUT EACH OF THEM**

**Randi** is the one I can't stop watching. I don't trust her, and I hurt for her. She engineered all of it, and she's also the one who's cracking. "Dear friend" in front of Brooke cost her something real, and I don't think it was strategy. I think she wants Vee for herself and has only let herself want her dressed up as a present for Pace. She owes Vee the truth and will never give it.

**Pace.** I trust him more than most romance heroes and less every chapter. He respects every door except the ones he builds himself. Standing at a light pole watching his girlfriend reel in a stranger, then asking Vee things "like her grandfather" did: both are true, and that's what keeps me reading. He has Randi's real self wrong, I'm almost sure of it.

**Vee.** I'm protective of her and sick about it. She's prey and she feels chosen. I love that she has appetite and vanity and her mother in her head all at once. She's slowly trading Cassie for Randi and calling it healthy.

**Cassie.** Pure love. I'm braced for the book to cut her out.

**Brooke and the tall one.** Danger. They know his name, and now they've seen the hand on the table.
