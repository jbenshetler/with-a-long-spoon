**REACTION**

Okay. I need a minute.

The fourth one, with the pleasure stretched so thin it starts to hurt, and Randi folded over the couch, is hot. Really hot, and explicit in the way I came here for. The book doesn't flinch at the wet, at the sound, at the smell of a whole day in the room. But what actually got me came before that and after it.

First: "He had already chosen; the pointing was only to find out whether they had chosen the same." I've been reading Pace as the kind one who can't see himself, the boy Daphne picked, now stuck in the same setup from the other side. Now I learn that on the quad he was reading Randi the way he reads Vee, one breath at a time. Her hand going slack, her *maybe*, her *God, no*. He picked Vee first, then steered Randi until she thought the pick was hers. Randi says "I was had." She was. He just nicely leaves out by how much. "He did not add that he had taken his own time over every one of them." So he's managing her too. Gently, lovingly, and completely. That changes the whole ground under the book for me.

Then he redeems himself, kind of, and it made me angry that it worked. "She consents — every time — or I'm done. It isn't you I'm checking on." And "Is she happy?" against "She is *radiant*." "That isn't the same answer." He's the only one in this whole setup asking the right question. But he asks it in a room Vee doesn't know exists, about stories she doesn't know are being told. Her consent to the bed, sure. Her consent to *this* couch? He's checking the gauge on a machine he built. I appreciate that the book lets him be both things.

And Randi. "Since October." "I got my hand in it once in October, before I knew better." The kiss Vee stole on the sidewalk, which Randi "had carried for two days, waiting to share it with him." "She told me first." She's jealous of Pace for having Vee's stories first. Then "I'm doing it for *you*" while she comes on "you don't have to." Then "That's more than three." She's counting him against Vee's "two, three" from brunch. And then "God, I want her," followed by the scramble: "For us. When she's ready." HE FELT HER HEAR IT. I texted my friend in all caps about that one.

Then the ending wrecked me. She pulls his arm tighter, then tighter again. "Say something nice to me." He gives her "extraordinary," and Vee got *wonderful, wonderful* while falling asleep with her head in his lap. The book doesn't spell out the difference, thank God. It just lets the two words sit there. "Some nights it frightens me." "Then I'll be careful with you." She's the one I'm most scared for now, maybe more than Vee.

Things that grated a little. "Still full of your come" in stats class is more porn-logic than the rest of the book. The mirror she forgot was a nice touch, though. "She had not given the answer to him. She had given it to the Saturdays" explains a beat I'd already caught. And yes, it's the Randi/Pace frame again: sex while she narrates Vee, then the blanket, then the hold. That's the fourth time. It survives because every line in it gave me new information, but I can feel the book leaning on it.

Also, the pastry glaze from ch34 is still sitting on Vee's lip in my head, and Randi still hasn't mentioned it to Pace. What else is she keeping from him?
