Okay. That's the shortest chapter I've ever read, and I sat there afterward with the Kindle on my chest for a minute. I'm not sure if that was because it hit me or because I was waiting for the rest of it.

It hit me. Mostly. The shirt finally paid off. I'd been waiting since ch42, when she gave it back to him to "fill." Back then it was the sweetest thing in the book and I wanted to cry. Now it turns on her: "it was warm because she was warm." The heat in the shirt is her own, handed back to her. That stopped me. It's the whole relationship in one object. Everything he does is in that cloth, the sawdust and the salt, the rooms he keeps warm, the plate, and none of it says what she is to him. She's been feeding herself on her own body heat and calling it him. Then she takes it off, and the cold is worse, and she *stays in it*. She doesn't fold it. She leaves it "where a dropped thing lands." That's the first time she's chosen the cold over the substitute. It's a turn, a quiet one. She's starting to refuse.

I also loved the shirt-over-shirt thing for the hall, so Cassie "wouldn't have to say anything." She's even hiding her longing from her roommate.
