REACTION

Wait. Wait a second. Hold everything.

First of all, this little two-page domestic scene in the kitchen—Vee sitting naked on the lacquered chair he made, talking at his back while he washes dishes—is so tender it physically hurts, and at the exact same time it has every alarm bell in my head ringing at once.

Because on one level, Pace is giving her the exact thing she walked out into the cold over in December! Not the word *boyfriend*, not *I love you* (though we saw him start to say *"Vee, I l—"* at the door in Chapter 52 before she opened her coat and stopped his mouth!), but *"Out there. What am I to you out there?"* He is volunteering—out of nowhere, while scrubbing a pot—to step out of his house in the woods and meet her entire world. Not just a private little table, either. And the way he remembers Meg's boots from one throwaway sentence in October (*"Your heel came up out of the right one when you pushed off"*) and quotes her own line about Kayla talking the paint off a wall back to her... God, any woman who has ever talked at a man's back while he does the dishes knows how devastatingly romantic it is to realize he didn't just let your voice wash over him, he *filed every name*. And then the mutual caretaking: Vee immediately realizing a fancy dinner would make Cassie have to "be a good sport" (and flashes back without looking at it to when *she* had to be a good sport, or when Pace did), and then remembering Chili's—him squaring his fork under the roaring TVs, *"I listen as hard as I know how and I still can't hear you"*—and handing him the choice of venue so he won't get sensory-overloaded while meeting her loud friends. That is real, grown-up love on both sides.

*Except.* Look underneath the surface of what Pace just did. Vee has been chattering about Randi for ten minutes, so when Pace says *"I want to meet your people,"* Vee immediately leaps to: *"We could do a dinner. The three of us."* A quiet dinner for three—Pace, Vee, and Randi, who he is *secretly sleeping with and plotted this whole thing with*! Can you imagine the two of them having to sit across a tiny table with real napkins pretending they barely met for "nine words at the coffee shop" for two solid hours while Vee beams at her two favorite people?! Did Pace throw Cassie, Meg, and Kayla in purely because he wanted to give Vee her whole world "out there," OR did he also pivot *fast* away from a three-person dinner table with Randi ("Bring Cassie, too... Then it can't be a dinner table. Tables have ends") because a crowd is way safer cover—or because he has a very specific place in mind?

Wait. *"Let me handle it."* Where is he going to take them?! Not his house—or wait, WOULD he bring them to his house, or to the dance hall, or somewhere else? And what happens when Randi, Cassie, Kayla, Meg, Danny, and Theo are all in one room watching Pace and Vee together—and watching Pace and *Randi* together?! I am equal parts melted into a puddle and clutching the armrests.
