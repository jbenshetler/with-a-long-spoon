REACTION

*“Words are cheap, and you still won't spend them on me.”*

I literally just put my hand over my mouth. Oh, Vee. My heart is in pieces on the floor, and at the exact same time I have never been prouder of a heroine in my life.

This is the beat from the jacket—*"When she asks what she is to him out in the world, he tells her to believe what he does, and says nothing more"*—and watching it actually happen after forty-six chapters of slow, gorgeous build is devastating. She didn't even bring her bag inside! Leaving the bag in the car, keeping her coat buttoned to the throat, keeping her hands in her pockets on the mat and refusing the hello kiss while the house smells like spiced chicken and sawdust and warm piano music... you can feel every ounce of what it costs her to hold her ground against the pull of that house.

And when he first answers *"I thought we'd have the evening. I've got the chicken going"*—oh, that maddening, literal, compartmentalizing man! And then when he says *"You're the most important person in my life,"* my stomach actually lurched, because I instantly heard Randi in the dark in Chapter 40 begging him *"Say something nice to me"* and getting *"You are extraordinary."* Does he even hear himself? Maybe he actually means it when he says Vee is the most important person in his life now—which is Randi's worst nightmare come true—and he *still* won't give Vee a word they can use outside his front door because "out there" he isn't hers alone!

Every single line of Vee's speech hit like a hammer because it's completely fair. She doesn't play games, she doesn't diminish what he gives her—she lays out every good, tender, life-changing thing he does for her inside that house, and then asks the one question he cannot answer without blowing up his entire secret architecture: *"Out there. What is this relationship? What am I to you out there?"* When he tries to math-logic his way around it with *"Do any of your friends have a relationship you'd rather have than this one?"* and she refuses to let the true thing pull her off the point, and then hits his *"Words are cheap. Believe what I do"* with *"Words are cheap, and you still won't spend them on me"*... I wanted to stand up and cheer through my tears.

And that ending! *"Not the hand in place of the word."* And the echo of the end of Chapter 46 when she took off his shirt in the freezing dorm room: *"She went out into the cold, and it was worse, and she did not go back in."* She walked out! And where is she going to go now—or who is she going to tell on Saturday morning?!
