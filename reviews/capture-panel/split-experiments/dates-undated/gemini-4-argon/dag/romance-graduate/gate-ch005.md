And Vee's little answer about why she likes econ—*"the gap between what they say they're doing and what they're doing"*—sitting right there in the middle of a room where the two people she likes best are running a silent game on her.

I am so completely hooked. I need to see the first date, AND I need to see what happens the second Randi and Pace are alone after this.
