REACTION

Oh, the contrast between Vee’s normal dorm friends and the sorority girls is doing so much work here, and I didn't realize how badly I needed to see Vee just be twenty-one and giddy at a dining hall table until it happened. Meg and Kayla are warm and loud and normal—eating plates of cheese and fries in the golden hour light—and watching Vee hold court with them makes my chest ache a little. Look at what she *doesn't* tell them. She told Cassie the real story about knocking on the truck window and unbuttoning her wet shirt, and she spills her darkest fantasies to Randi over smoothies, but with Meg and Kayla she edits out the strip-tease in the cab and makes it a cute rom-com story about him standing in the rain and being too cold to kiss. She's keeping the erotic charge of it secret from the regular girls, saving that side of herself for Pace (and, unknowingly, for Randi).

Also, when Vee says the line from the jacket blurb—how he asks her what she wants and waits for the real answer, and how it's making her climb the walls—and Meg just smiles into her cheese and says every guy is different at the start until they aren't... *oof*. Vee lets it roll right off her because she's floating, and in one way Vee is right (Pace *is* genuinely wired like that), but in the bigger way Meg has no idea how right she should be to warn her, because the whole thing is a setup!

And then the text lands. *Would you come to my house Saturday. Two o'clock.*

Kayla calling it a ransom note made me laugh out loud, and then her pointing out that two o'clock isn't lunch and isn't dinner—"He wants you for the afternoon"—sent a straight shot of anticipation right down my spine. We just saw Pace standing in his doorway in Chapter 13, cold and lonely and starving after Randi used him and bolted, and now he's inviting Vee into his house off 614. The house with the walnut table in the bedroom! Wait—is Randi going to be there, or is this just Pace and Vee for the first time in his bed, or is she about to walk into the next stage of their game? And why did Kayla say "What to wear?" right after Vee told Pace about the russet silk dress on the mountain? Is that dress going to be waiting for her, or am I getting ahead of myself? Either way, Saturday at two o'clock cannot get here fast enough.
