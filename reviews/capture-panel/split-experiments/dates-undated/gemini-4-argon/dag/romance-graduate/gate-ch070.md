REACTION

God, Cassie is the absolute MVP of this book. Also—what just happened here? That was barely two pages! I've never seen this book do a chapter that short, or honestly read a book that drops a two-page chapter right on the seam of a cliffhanger, and for a second when the page ended I blinked like, *wait, that's the whole chapter?*

But it completely worked. Having Chapter 69 end in the doorway on Cassie's question, and then making this entire miniature chapter just the thirty seconds of Vee standing in her coat trying to answer it, felt like holding a breath. Look at what Cassie asks her: not *are you gay*, not *what are you doing behind Pace's back*, not *what happened down there*, just: *"Is this what you want?"* Because Cassie knows Vee better than anyone, and Cassie always sees the actual shape of the thing. And watching Vee actually go looking for the answer inside herself—coming right up against that huge, terrifying shape in the dark while her neck is still burning from Randi's mouth, and then panicking and throwing the old "you just don't like sororities / you don't like her" fight on the floor like a plank to walk out on—broke my heart a little.

Even better is Cassie refusing to step on the plank. She doesn't take the bait, she doesn't make Vee fight her, she just clocks that Vee skipped her Tuesday dinner with Randi and offers her the box of Cocoa Pebbles. *"Here was a want she could find — a want the size of a bowl."* In two pages, that tiny domestic grace note said more about female friendship and how lost Vee is right now than a ten-page fight ever could. Now I'm dying to see what Thursday and Saturday brunch look like now that Cassie has seen them with her own eyes and Vee has nowhere left to hide from the question.
