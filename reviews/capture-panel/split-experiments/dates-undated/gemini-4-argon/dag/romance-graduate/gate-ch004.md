REACTION

Oh, Cassie. *"Goodbye, Vee."* That gave me a literal chill down my spine. Cassie clocked the whole thing in thirty seconds flat—she looked at Randi's polished little intro bit, refused to play, and then looked at her friend and basically said *have fun in the cult, I'll miss you.* I love Cassie already, and I am so nervous for Vee.

Being inside Vee's head for the first time is fascinating because we just spent three chapters seeing how the machinery works, and now we're watching the target walk right into it, beaming. What kills me is how *real* Vee's interiority is. That whole beat in the coffee shop where she looks at the girls in sports bras and her mother's voice says *floozies*, and then right underneath it is her own suppressed heat looking at their stomachs, and then her own secret pride that her body is fuller and better than theirs even while she hides it under a cardigan... God, that is such specific, grown-up character writing. In a normal KU romance, the curvy redhead heroine is just "insecure" until the hero calls her beautiful. Vee isn't insecure—she *knows* what she has, she's just locked in a cage her mother built and starving for permission to take up space.

And the way she looks at Randi! She thinks it's social awe—that flat, helpless want of finally sitting at the cool girls' table—especially after we just saw Randi come apart in Chapters 1 and 2, so watching Vee look at Randi and think *she isn't performing like the rest of them, she's just effortlessly Randi* is delicious and tragic at the same time. Girl, she is performing for her life! And the detail that Vee couldn't remember a single word Randi actually said afterward—that the "transcript was blank" and she only remembered how Randi made her *feel*—is the most exact description of being love-bombed by a charismatic person I've ever read. Even the little detail about Randi's accent ("the esses ran a half-beat long, and the a's came up arched") makes me wonder what Randi's actual backstory is, or if even her voice is constructed.

There's no explicit sex in this chapter, obviously, it's a pure setup/seduction beat, a total bridge into Vee's POV, and I didn't miss the heat for a second because the psychological charge is humming so loud. Now I need to see how Randi brings her to Pace, or how Randi reports back to Pace after this coffee date.
