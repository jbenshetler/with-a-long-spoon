Oh, that ending just gave me full-body chills! Cassie still had her coat on and the cold coming off her—she must have come home right behind them, or seen them from the steps or the window! And when Cassie looks up with actual worry and disbelief and asks, *"What was that with you and Randi?"*—and Vee can't even summon a cover story because she doesn't have an answer anymore—the outside world has officially breached the bubble.

I loved every single paragraph of this chapter because after the explosive physical peaks of the last few chapters (the body-painting, the mixer kiss, the 1 a.m. confession ride at Pace's), this is the real psychological fallout. You can't un-ring that bell, and watching Vee try to keep the lid on the pot is so excruciatingly well-written. That opening paragraph—initiation after initiation with Pace all day Sunday, taking the book right out of his hands, driving home *"full of him and not, somehow, filled"*—and then Monday night in the top bunk keeping her hand flat on her stomach like a lid on a pot *"because as long as she didn't give it the thing that would make it true, it could stay only wanting."* God, the interiority in this book puts 99% of romance novels to shame.

And then Tuesday! Every tiny detail that used to be their normal routine is suddenly radioactive. Standing in the closet with her arms caught half-in the plum sweater, not knowing if she's dressing to be looked at or covered up. Randi's knee against hers for a whole hour. Let's — let's have brunch this weekend"* before her brain even catches up! Now Cassie is staring right at her waiting for an answer. I cannot click next fast enough.
