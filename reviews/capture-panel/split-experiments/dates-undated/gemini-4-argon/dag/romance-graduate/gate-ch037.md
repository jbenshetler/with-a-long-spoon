REACTION

I really love how physical the writing of this swimming set is—the rhythm of *Breathe every third. Hand in front of the shoulder, not past it*, and the way her body slowly burns the words off her thoughts until she's just left with *him, and the ache, and the count*, and then only the count. And it broke my heart a little when, right in the middle of the set, she has four strokes of wondering *whether he was that good or she had made him that good, out of wanting, out of two months of being looked at like that*. Because we know he *is* that good to her when they're alone, and simultaneously that he is hiding a massive, devastating lie from her!

Also, I just noticed something with the timeline: she's still turning over *Did he mind* about the bathroom counter since Saturday, which means this Tuesday swim is happening *before* "the next Friday" from Chapter 35 when she finds the top drawer cleared out for her! I actually really like seeing the week inside the gap between sections ii and iii of Chapter 35, and seeing that Vee has a life and a discipline—swimming laps since she was twelve, the old green-and-white suit—that belongs entirely to her and has nothing to do with Pace or Randi.

That said, that's two short, quiet chapters in a row now (first the dorm chat with Cassie, now a solo lap swim where nothing actually moves forward in the plot). The prose is still gorgeous—*"with her ears ringing pleasantly and her arms feeling like they belonged to somebody generous"* is such a great line—so I'm not going anywhere, especially knowing that Friday with the bathroom drawer is coming, or whatever happens after that. Move me back to the three of them!
