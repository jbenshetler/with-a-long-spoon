REACTION

Oh, that broke my heart in about fifty places. Remember two chapters ago when she made him wear that flannel all weekend so it would smell like him again, and he put it on over his own shirt in the kitchen without making her feel foolish, and it was the sweetest, softest thing in the world? To take *that exact shirt* and turn it inside out like this—where she realizes the warmth inside the cloth is just her own body heat coming back at her, and the smell is just a promise of closeness with nobody actually there—is devastating.

I’m also noticing how the last three chapters have been hammering this one bruise harder and harder: *Seconds* on the porch with the cracker-and-meal speech he didn't answer in kind, *Under the Rug* with the fight after Chili's and her lying awake in his bed realizing she has all of his body and still no word for what they are, and now *Bare* in the top bunk. The writing is so good and so quiet that it hurts,especially the detail of her wearing her own sleep shirt *under* his flannel so Cassie won't see how far gone she is, then stripping hers off to have his against her bare skin, and then taking his off too and choosing to lie there freezing in the two-inch draft rather than be comforted by something that won't name her.

It’s a short, sharp little gut-punch of a chapter—almost like an epilogue or a coda to the night before—and because she finally stopped letting the comfort of his actions (or his scent) drown out the question, something *has* to give next. She didn't fold the shirt. She left it in a heap and stayed bare in the cold until morning. You don't wake up from a night like that and just go back to normal.
