I love how this book uses Vee's friends to gently hold a mirror up to what she's actually doing, without the author ever jumping in to lecture us.

First of all, watching Vee edit the story for Kayla and Meg is fascinating—just like she edited it for Cassie last night (*"we talked"*), except here she gives them the spicy version (*"I flashed him on his front porch"*) while completely inventing a polite, level line of dialogue for Pace when he opened the door (*"'It's late. Is everything alright?'"*) instead of the real moment where his face cracked in pain and he literally started to say *"Vee, I l—"* before she cut him off with the coat! Why did she invent that line for him? Because even to her friends, she's framing it as: we had a fight, I was the one who went too far, and I showed up naked to apologize.

And then Meg, sweet level-headed Meg, validates it by comparing Vee showing up naked in a trench coat after a month of silence over *"What am I to you?"* to... Meg being a jerk on her period when Danny brought her a heating pad, and buying him maple bacon donuts to apologize! *"That's a big apology."* / *"It was a big fight."* My stomach dropped a little right there. Because Vee didn't do anything wrong in December! Asking the man you're in love with what you are to him outside his bedroom isn't "being a hag" when someone brings you a heating pad! Randi spun Vee around so thoroughly in Chapter 51 that Vee genuinely believes *she* was the one who owed Pace a grand peace offering, and now she's telling her friends *"We talked all night"* when in reality they didn't talk about December at all.
