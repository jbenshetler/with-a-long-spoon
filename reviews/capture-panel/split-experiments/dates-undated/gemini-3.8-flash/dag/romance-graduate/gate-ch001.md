REACTION

Holy shit. That naming beat at the end of the scene completely knocked the wind out of me.

Going in from the jacket, I assumed—naturally, because of every romance trope wired into my brain—that the girl walking through his door in chapter one was Vivienne. I was reading her as Vee: the sorority girl, the rigid control around food, the hunger, the performance of being looked at. And then, right at the climax of this excruciatingly slow, masterfully calibrated scene, he whispers: *"There you are, Randi."*

My jaw literally dropped. The *twist* of realizing that this intensely stripped-bare, emotionally wrecked woman isn't the innocent target of the blurb, but the other half of the scheming couple? That Randi—the "glamorous new friend" who is supposed to coldly, playfully steer Vee into Pace's bed—is *this* undone by him? It recasts the entire blurb in an instant. This isn't just a kinky couple casually hunting a third for fun. This is a woman whose armor just got cracked wide open by a man who sees straight through her, panicking in the bathroom mirror because she got seen, while he sits outside calmly thinking he just gave her a breakthrough.

And the craft of the heat here—my God. The patience. A lot of books think explicit means fast and aggressive; this made every single button, the thin-sliced apple, the sound of leather, the way she pressed the bottle cap into her thumbnail to ground herself, feel practically seismic. Pace isn't some cartoon Dom barking orders; he's quiet, architectural, maddeningly observant. But what makes it electric is that Randi isn't yielding; she's *retreating* while pretending to yield. That moment where she puts the mask back on in the mirror to protect herself from his tenderness—"Tenderness was something the face received and passed inward... Out there tonight there would be no deciding"—that is the exact grown-up psychological meat I am starving for.

I need to know how the hell Vivienne enters this dynamic now. If Randi is already this desperate to divert his terrifying, all-consuming gaze off herself, is that where Vee comes from? A decoy? A shield? I'm entirely hooked.
