REACTION

Oh, this was a gut punch of a different kind entirely.

Zero heat in the room—pure, gray institutional chill—and yet the tension was suffocating. Watching Randi, the untouchable sorority predator who has spent chapters meticulously dismantling Vee's defenses, completely crumble over a single data point in a glass study room was mesmerizing. It exposes the rotten foundation Randi’s entire gilded life is built on. She genuinely believed her daddy was the heroic exception who kept people paid, and finding her own formal name stamped over the stark, cold evidence of wage theft and pandemic grift broke something inside her.

And then there's Vee. Sweet, blind Vee, caught between the girl whose father got cut loose and the girl whose father did the cutting. Her instinct isn’t spite; it’s pure, protective mercy. She shields Randi in real time, covers the wound, absorbs the awkwardness, and actively chooses to carry the secret *for* her so Randi doesn't have to bleed in front of Cassie. It breaks my heart because it proves, once again, what a deeply generous, loving person Vee is. She’s giving that absolute grace to a woman who is currently running a psychological ambush on her behind Pace’s locked door.

Cassie sitting on the other side, completely unbothered, delivering the fatal truth with that flat certainty while having no idea she just drove a knife through Randi's ribs—perfection. The dynamics here are so tight, so grounded, and so smart. It’s a quiet bridge chapter, but the emotional intelligence in how the room shifted, how the silence landed, and how Vee instinctively stepped in front of the blow was top tier.
