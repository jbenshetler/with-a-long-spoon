REACTION

Oh, Cassie. God bless Cassie. Every girl who has ever had a body that arrived too early and too loudly needs a Cassie to tell her that the guys catcalling from the bleachers will never know the love of a woman, and then shoot a sheet of pool water directly into her face before she can sink into herself.

This chapter made me ache for Vee in a completely different way than the previous ones did. "She had loved this before she had a body to be a problem, and the water was the one part of it that had never been taken." That hit like a physical punch. It explains *so much* about why she let herself be led by Randi, why Pace’s intense, quiet, specific looking is going to absolutely ruin her, and why she wears that cardigan armor up to her collarbone even when she knows she has a better shape than the girls in the Mauve crop tops. She is exhausted by her own visibility. Men yell at her from the bleachers, her mother polices her portions, the world looks at her like she’s an appetizer, and down at the bottom of the pool—sculling in the cold green quiet—nobody wants anything from her.

And that’s the tragedy of what’s coming, isn't it? Because Pace and Randi *do* want something from her. They’ve picked her out like a prize at a fair. Pace’s quiet, respectful, unhurried looking that felt like such a relief to her over frozen yogurt isn’t neutral safety; it’s an entirely different, infinitely more dangerous kind of hunger. She thinks she's finally being seen as a person who solves calculus problems and talks about applied economics, but behind the curtain, Randi is asking if she’ll like the bench and Pace is imagining her mouth on Randi’s skin.

It was short, yes, and completely uncharged erotically—pure friendship and quiet grief—but it grounded Vee’s humanity right before the jaws snap shut. It gives weight to what’s about to be broken or remade. I felt the water, I felt the relief of that ugly shriek-laugh with Cassie, and I felt the dread of knowing how little of this safe, quiet territory she actually has left.
