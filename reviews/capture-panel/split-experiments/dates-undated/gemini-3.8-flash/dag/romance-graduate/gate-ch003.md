REACTION

Holy shit. Holy *shit*.

The way this book is built is diabolical. The first chapter set up this quiet, hyper-controlled dynamic where Pace feels almost entirely like a benevolent, attentive dom coaxing this locked-down sorority girl into her body. Then chapter two showed the cold, terrifying social cage Randi actually lives in, where losing control for an hour is a lethal liability. And now—chapter three—you pull the pin on the grenade promised on the back jacket.

The transition from her orgasm at the mere *idea* of tasting another woman to the immediate, desperate psychological pivot: *“Only in a threesome. With you.”* The sheer brilliance of that defense mechanism! If she wants a woman, she’s broken, degenerate, outside the lines. If she gets a woman *for her boyfriend*, she’s just the ultimate cool, game, sexually adventurous girlfriend. It’s devastating and it’s so psychologically true it hurts.

And then the pointing game. God, the pointing game. The campus walk felt crisp and alive, the tension building with every single girl they evaluated. Pace isn’t just indulging her; he’s studying her, mapping her desires while she pretends she’s doing him a favor. And the contrast between the girls Randi usually measures herself against—the sleek, pastel athleisure, the controlled hair, the tiny waists—and then Vee. *Vee*.

The description of Vee stepping out of the dining hall was electric. Big, unbothered, laughing with her throat completely bared, clothes that don't fit the curated sorority mold—she is the living antithesis of everything Randi starves herself to be. The moment Randi saw her and just said *“Yes”* with no polish, no game, just raw, involuntary appetite… my stomach dropped.

And then watching Randi hunt! The calculated, graceful little “accident” of a collision, using every drop of social poise she owns as a weapon to rope this open, laughing, unsuspecting girl into their orbit. It was predatory and thrilling and deeply unsettling all at once.

And that final line from Randi: *“Do you think she’ll like the bench?”*
Chills. Actual chills. The victim of the bench instantly looking to become the architect of someone else’s undoing on it. The trap is sprung, and Vee doesn’t even know she’s in a game yet. I am completely hooked.
