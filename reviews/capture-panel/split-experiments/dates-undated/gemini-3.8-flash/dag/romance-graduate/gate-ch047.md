REACTION

God, my stomach dropped. She actually did it. She left the bag in the car, kept her coat on, and didn’t let him kiss her into complacence. After watching her swallow her own needs for weeks, watching her drown every terrifying instinct in his bed and convince herself that being fed gourmet pizza was the same thing as being loved, she finally stood on the mat and demanded the truth.

And his answers made my blood run cold. *"You're the most important person in my life"* is the ultimate non-answer an avoidant man hands you when he wants the benefits of devotion without the liability of a label. But when he tried to pull that condescending, math-brain logic puzzle on her—asking if any of her friends had a relationship she’d trade for—I wanted to reach through the page and slap him. It’s such an arrogant deflection, treating her heart like an optimization problem instead of answering a basic human question.

Then came the line from the jacket: *"Words are cheap. Believe what I do."* And Vee slicing right back through his bullshit with *"Words are cheap, and you still won't spend them on me"* took my breath away. What makes it devastating—what makes this whole book feel like watching someone walk into traffic—is that we *know* what he does behind that door. We know about Randi. We know this warm, curated sanctuary is a trap he built with another woman. When he reaches out with his hands at the end to "settle" her, it felt suffocating, and the relief of her actually getting that door open and walking out into the cold was immense. But now she’s out in the dark, crying, vulnerable, and having no idea that the "best friend" she’s likely to run to next is the architect of the whole nightmare. My chest hurts. I need the next chapter right now.
