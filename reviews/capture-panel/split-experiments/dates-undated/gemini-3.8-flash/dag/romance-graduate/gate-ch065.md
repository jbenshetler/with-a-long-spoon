REACTION

*“The wrong hand. It held her the exact way she wanted to be held and it did not want her at all, and what it did, pinning her under the flat of its palm, was open a hole in her a mile across where the right hand should have been.”*

Jesus. Rivers took a standard, mundane, almost clichéd romance/erotica trope beat—the heroine getting her first Brazilian wax—and turned it into an absolute masterclass in psychological tension, somatic memory, and submissive surrender.

What completely floored me here was the contrast between the sterile, clinical apathy of the esthetician and Vee’s internal erotic wire. Having Sondra put a firm, totally disinterested hand on Vee’s thigh to keep her from squirming, and having Vee's body immediately recognize the *shape* of that touch from Pace—only hollowed out of any actual desire—was brilliant. It’s so sharp, so accurate to how touch works when you’re utterly consumed by someone. You become Pavlovian. A hand on your thigh isn't just pressure; it's a prompt, an ache for the one person whose weight actually means something.

And the layers of manipulation surrounding this! Pace didn't just casually ask; he framed it as an exact, quiet request, knowing damn well Vee would leap into the abyss just to hear him ask it. And Randi! “Barely stings.” Randi intentionally omitted the reality of the pain, and *definitely* omitted the fact that you have to flip onto your stomach and have the back done. Randi stage-managed this entire initiation down to the salon and the aesthetician’s name, knowing Vee would walk through that fire because Randi gave her the permission slip. Randi is curating Vee’s body for Pace, and for herself, and Vee is just blissfully, terrifyingly letting them sculpt her.

The shame, too—her mother’s kitchen voice whispering *“the kind of girl who”* while she lies face down in loud crinkling paper, totally exposed. Vee giving up her lines one by one, realizing that once you've crossed the border, trying to draw a line in the sand is just a joke.

And then that final moment alone in the room. Touching herself, feeling the shock of that raw, brand-new smoothness, and the immediate, devouring fantasy of *his* hand finding it. The anticipation for what Pace is going to do when he sees her this weekend is suffocating.
