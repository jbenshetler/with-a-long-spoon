REACTION

Oh, Cassie. God bless Cassie. That girl is the only ballast keeping this entire manic, hyper-aroused, self-deluding ship from completely capsizing into the ocean.

This was a short chapter, but it hit like a bucket of ice water straight out of a January gutter. After the sheer, unhinged, multi-chapter sensory overload we’ve been drowning in—the explicit threeway-in-spirit sex in Pace’s bed, the paint and the fruit, the dancefloor grind, the neck-kissing on the cold walk—having Vee walk straight into the dorm room and face Cassie’s totally unadorned, devastatingly clear eyes was *everything*.

"Is this what you want?"

Not "are you hooking up," not "what did I just see on the sidewalk," but *is this what you want?* It’s the exact question Vee has been running from at eighty miles an hour. She can handle Pace maneuvering her with hot/cold bedroom games; she can handle Randi feeding her lines and expensive perfume; she can even handle her own puritanical guilt by turning it into a kink. But Cassie asking a plain, flat, honest question with worry underneath it? Vee’s whole defensive infrastructure completely collapsed. She tried to throw the old "you just hate sororities/you hate Randi" deflection out there, and Cassie didn't even dignify it with a breath. She just stepped right over the bait and offered her a box of Cocoa Pebbles.

I felt that in my chest. That is real female friendship. That’s the friend who sees you wrecking your life, refuses to fight with you about your obvious lies, and feeds you cheap dry cereal out of the box because she knows you’re starving and spinning out.

Vee taking that box because it was "a want the size of a bowl"—a want she could actually admit to without having her entire reality shatter—was such a gorgeous, quiet beat. She is so deep in the thicket. She dodged dinner with Randi only to offer up Saturday brunch; she’s terrified of what she’s feeling, terrified of what Cassie saw, and Pace and Randi are out there like twin gravitational pulls she can't escape. The tension is so thick right now I can barely breathe.
