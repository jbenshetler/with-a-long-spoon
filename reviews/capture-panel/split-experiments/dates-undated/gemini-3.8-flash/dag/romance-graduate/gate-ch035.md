REACTION

Oh, my God. This chapter hurt. It physically hurt to read, in that exquisite, terrible way where you are watching someone you love walk barefoot straight toward an open bear trap with a smile on her face.

She whispered his real name to him in the dark. *Peter.* And she told herself she loves him. She is counting every single domestic grace—the dill sauce, the warm tomatoes, the way he lets her burn through him all weekend, the fact that he comes back to her an hour later on the couch—as currency toward a future that is fundamentally built on a lie. She’s living "clear inside the everything-he-did and not go hungry for the saying." That line gutted me, because every woman who has ever settled for breadcrumbs from an emotionally withholding man has told herself that exact same lie: *He shows it, he just doesn't say it.* Except Pace *isn't* just an emotionally withholding guy; he's an architect of an entire orchestrated trap with Randi, and Vee has no idea she's being seasoned like that damn salmon.

And then the drawers. The goddamn top drawer.

When she saw the counter cleared, I felt that exact cold drop in the stomach she did—that sickening realization of *oh, I took up too much space, I was too much, I'm just a guest who overstayed.* And then she opens the drawer and finds he didn't throw her out; he folded his entire rigid, hyper-controlled life down into the bottom drawer to give her the top one. The easy one. The one your hand goes to first. It is the most devastatingly romantic gesture in the world, and it completely breaks my heart because the more space he makes for her in his real life, the more real this is becoming to him, too. He isn't just playing Randi's game anymore. He is carving out pieces of his sacred, hermetic sanctuary and surrendering them to her.

The domesticity here was so lush and so heavy with impending doom. There’s almost no explicit heat compared to the last two chapters, but the intimacy was off the charts. It felt stolen and fragile. I am terrified of what happens when Randi finds out he gave her that drawer. Because Randi will ask, Vee will tell her, and Randi is going to see the walls of her little dollhouse starting to crack.
