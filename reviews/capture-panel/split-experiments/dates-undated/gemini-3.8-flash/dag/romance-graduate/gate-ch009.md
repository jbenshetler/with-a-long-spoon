REACTION

Oh, god. The dramatic irony in this book is actually sick. I have a knot in my stomach and I cannot put this down.

Look at the contrast Helen Rivers just set up. In chapter 1, we watch Pace dismantle Randi—who measures her food in paper-thin apple slices and polices every calorie—on a literal spanking bench, breaking her down until she's weeping and begging. And here is Vee, who has clearly been starved in a completely different way (by her mother, by the culture, by men who only want the "legs for days" spectacle of her), and Pace takes her out and *feeds her*. The scene with the food—the blistering bread, the tabbouleh, the baba ghanoush, the mint tea—was so intensely sensual. Watching him eat with his right hand, watching him choose for her because she *asked* him to, the sheer relief she felt at being cared for by a grown-up who doesn't demand performance.

"May I choose?"
God. The way he operates. He asks permission, he gives her the agency, and the very act of choosing to yield to him becomes the most intoxicating thing she's ever experienced. That line from the jacket blurb—*until choosing becomes the most erotic thing she has ever done*—is landing with absolute sledgehammer force right now.

And what kills me is how utterly genuine Pace feels in the moment. He isn’t playing a sneering cartoon villain; he is entirely present with her, respectful, patient, taking his time, not rushing the kiss, letting her hold the reins. But we *know* the blueprint. We know that while Vee is floating home, tasting mint and feeling like her hand finally fits where it belongs, Randi is back in her room holding that glass of red wine, asking "Did she make you laugh?" and "Do you think she'll like the bench?"

It’s intoxicating, it’s deeply tender, and it is horrifying because Vee is walking right into the snare with her whole heart open. When she said, "I'm making an exception," I actually gasped. She gave that up so easily because he made the room safe enough for her to do it. The psychological architecture here is incredible. I need chapter 10 immediately.
