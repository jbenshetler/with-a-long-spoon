REACTION

*Oh, my god.*

I am grinning like an idiot and clutching my chest, and simultaneously I want to throw up because I know what’s coming. The psychological whip-saw of this book is incredible.

First off: seeing Pace alone in his house rocking out to *AC/DC* and doing terrible air guitar with a protein shake?! That is the single most endearing, humanizing glimpse of this man we have had in thirty-two chapters. It completely shattered the rigid, austere craftsman shell and reminded me that he is *twenty-two*. He’s a kid who lifts heavy things and listens to loud dad-rock and gets clumsy when a pretty girl ambushes him. And the playfulness between them! The tackle, the stealing of the shorts, the flashing, the wrestling on the floor—it felt so young and alive and normal.

And then the shift. God, the shift into the spanking.

This author understands female somatic response better than 99% of people writing romance today. That terrifying, humiliating betrayal of your own anatomy—where your mind is still clinging to polite, good-girl denial ("It's not. I don't—"), but your body has already signed the paperwork, opened the doors, and rolled out the red carpet. The wetness on his finger. The absolute *plenty* of it. It felt like *them*.

And the aftercare. The juice! Bringing her a ridiculous glass of sweet, cold juice because her blood sugar dropped, and pulling her head into his lap, stroking her hair and just repeating that she’s wonderful until she falls asleep. The green polka-dot scrunchies on the counter. The curry with toasted spices. He is building a life for her in that cabin.

Which is why the dread is currently eating a hole in my stomach. She lies there thinking: *"Shame wants a witness, and in the small lit country of his bed there was no such thing as a witness, anywhere."*

VEE. Sweetheart. There is *always* a witness. Every single thing that happened in that bedroom is going straight to Randi over brunch, or worse, Randi already choreographed the blueprint for it. Vee thinks she's safe in this private country of two, and she has no idea the border is completely wide open. The sheer emotional fall from this height is going to break my heart. Give me the next chapter immediately.
