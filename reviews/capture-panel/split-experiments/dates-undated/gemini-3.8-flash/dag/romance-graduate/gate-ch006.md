REACTION

Okay, first of all: that was tiny. Literally two pages. I blinked and it was over, and normally a chapter that short makes me raise an eyebrow like an author just ran out of breath, but damn if every single line didn't hit clean.

What gets me right in the chest is the dramatic irony of it all. We *know* what Pace and Randi did. We were in that bedroom with the leather bench; we watched them pick Vee like a piece of fruit on the quad. And now here is Vee, so completely unaware, so starved for basic human consideration that a guy listening to her finish a sentence without interrupting feels like a revelation. When she built that leaning tower of froyo and realized her mother’s voice wasn’t in her head for once? God. That hurt. And Pace with his quiet, methodical care—he feeds Randi thin apple slices, and he lets Vee pile on the sugar. He knows exactly how to make a woman feel seen, and that makes him equal parts delicious and terrifying.

Also, Cassie continues to be an absolute treasure. "As long as he doesn't make you roll a healer again" gave me more backstory about Vee's pathetic dating history in eight words than two paragraphs of exposition ever could. She's always been the girl accommodating everyone else, putting herself in service of someone else's game. And now she's stepping right into Pace and Randi's game, thinking it’s the first time someone is accommodating *her*.

It’s completely chaste—two quiet, clothed chapters in a row now—so my spicy romance brain is tapping its watch waiting for the tension to snap back into physical heat. But psychologically? I am locked in.
