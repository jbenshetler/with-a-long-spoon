REACTION

Wait. Who the hell is Sheri?!

We just drop into a Saturday morning bike ride with a totally unintroduced character who talks like a truck driver and clocks women’s asses, and Pace just casually lays out the entire ménage to her across the handlebars? *"And the redhead?" "Not yet."*

Excuse me? After the suffocating, claustrophobic brilliance of chapters 26 and 27—where Randi was literally *using* her tactile memory of Vee’s bare skin and thighs to fuck Pace out of her mind, and Vee was falling completely in love with Randi’s validation—this felt like walking into a cold shower. A short, chilly, weirdly detached scene where an outsider just chuckles and goes, *"Two of them at once. How do you sleep? ... But I bet it's worth it."*

No. Absolutely not. The entire devastating engine of this book is that Pace, Randi, and Vee are caught in an intense, psychologically nuanced, agonizingly private pressure cooker. Randi is unraveling while pretending to be an architect. Pace is lying to himself about his "ethics" while hoarding Vee's soul. Vee is blindly handing herself over to both of them. To suddenly have some cycling buddy pop up out of nowhere just to summarize the plot like a casual high-five (*"Two of them at once... complicated, but worth it"*) felt like a total betrayal of the tone. It flattened Pace into a guy bragging to a pal on a trail ride, and reduced the terrifying web of manipulation to a buddy-comedy trope.

I get what it was *trying* to do: show that Pace is talking about Vee out loud to people in his normal life, proving he's falling for her mind and her presence (the chair, the math book) instead of just the sex. But having a brand-new spectator arrive out of thin air just to hand-wave the central, agonizing secret of the novel with a laugh made me recoil.

I’m not quitting, because tonight is supposed to be Vee showing up at his door in the black dress, the cognac shoes, and the Hermès scarf, completely unaware that Randi literally scripted her arrival and then rode Pace to exhaustion talking about her bare thighs. *That* is the collision I'm dying for. But this chapter was an unwelcome speed bump that took the air right out of the room.
