REACTION

Oh, this is gorgeous and it is making me utterly, terribly sick to my stomach.

The contrast between Vee and Randi is being drawn so exquisitely here that it physically hurts. In chapter one, we watched Pace carefully manage Randi’s hyper-policed relationship with food—the honeycrisp sliced paper-thin, cut-side up, because her mother and her sorority had turned eating into a minefield. And here is Vee, starving, literally hollowed out from a lifetime of her mother whispering *that's plenty, that's enough now*, sitting across from this man and having him order for her. *May I choose?* The sheer psychological relief of that question. For a girl who has spent her whole life managing the space she takes up, being fed—being given permission to just *eat*, to lose the thread of conversation, to stop performing—is practically an erotic act before they even touch.

And Pace is doing what he does. The exactness of him. He is patient, courtly, and utterly lethal because he *does not push*. Every time he backs off, every time he respects a boundary or leaves a choice in her hands, he is just laying down another inch of track that she is going to sprint along. When she threw out that defensive little rule—"I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days"—and he just nodded, smiled, and started walking? *God.* The emotional intelligence of that move. He didn't sulk, he didn't cajole, he just let her own the boundary, which instantly made her want to tear it down herself. *Every yes was freely given.* The jacket blurb is hovering over this entire scene like a ghost at the feast. She thinks she's making an exception; she thinks she's taking the lead. She has no idea she is walking straight into a room they built for her weeks ago.

It's delicious, it's romantic, the sensory details of the food and the cold air and the mint tea are so grounded, but the dramatic irony is ratcheting so tight I can barely breathe. Randi is waiting back at his house, or back at her single room, practically vibrating with the idea of this girl, and Vee is just falling. Wholly, beautifully, and without a net.
