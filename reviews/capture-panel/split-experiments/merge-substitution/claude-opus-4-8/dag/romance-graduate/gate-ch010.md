Just Vee. And God, she's so alive in it. The pool chapter earlier told me she had an interior — the going-under, the grief she couldn't name — and this cashes it.

The boot. I want to talk about the boot. He takes her sock off on a rock in the woods and her whole body clenches with the mother-voice, *what kind of girl lets a man*, and he just — fixes the blister. Moleskin, warms it with his thumb, relaces the boot differently, pats the toe, *done.* And the clench lets go without her catching it go. That's the whole machine of this book in miniature and it's *so good* and it *terrifies* me because I know what it's for. He is teaching her nervous system, over and over, that handing herself over to him is safe. The cherry pits. "We take nothing and leave nothing." He corrects her without making her small — she said that line herself, *he hadn't made her small to give it to her* — and I believe it, I believe he's gentle, and that's precisely the problem, because the blurb told me she's livestock. She was chosen in a game. Every one of these tender competent little gifts is grooming and it is working on *me*, the reader, not just on her, and I resent how well it works and I cannot stop reading.

The russet dress. The dress she puts back on the shelf. *You put a thing back on the shelf and you keep walking.* I underlined that in my head. And he doesn't pick it up — "that sounds like a beautiful dress" and he lets it lie there. Which means it's coming back. That dress is going to show up and I already know it and I'm going to feel something when it does and I'll hate that too.

And then the truck. THE TRUCK. He gives her his only dry shirt and gets out and stands in the rain with his back turned. His shirt AND the truck, both. And she's shivering and the want comes up — *I want him to see me* — and she knocks on the glass and crooks her finger and unbuttons her wet shirt with her eyes locked on his the whole way down, feeding him the mother-voice *floozie* as heat, her thighs pressing on their own. He gave her the shirt and he's been sitting there shivering while she undresses and he hasn't said a word because he was "a long way from the question of whether he was cold." And she stops.
