WHO'S WHO

Pace — the man. Grad student, math PhD, "more or less" finishing. Lives out past where the road turns to gravel, 614. Small house, cheap white laminate counters he's never replaced, but he *built* the furniture himself, walnut, and he built a bench — a sex bench — that hides under a tabletop in his bedroom. Older-seeming than his age. Courtly. Says "may I." Fixes blisters, slices apples thin, feeds starving girls. The most calibrated man I've ever read and I still can't decide if that's love or a program running. There's a Daphne in his past — a woman whose closed door he didn't respect once, and he "learned." That line is the whole halo keeping him from being a monster.

Randi — black hair, startling blue eyes, the glamour queen, sits on Standards board at her sorority, has the single room. The jacket's "planner." But Chapter 1 she's the one on the bench, begging, crying, deciding in the mirror to go back behind the face *forever*. She and Pace are lovers running a game. She steered Vee into him on the quad (the pointing game), and she's grooming Vee while visibly, horribly, starting to actually want her. The constructed accent Vee keeps mis-explaining as Switzerland/Paris — there's a hidden self there.

Vee / Vivienne Thorne — the redhead. Twenty-one, econ major, sharp (reads rooms like a strategist), quietly starving, a mother's voice living in her mouth policing food and want. The mark. Doesn't know it. Thinks she's the luckiest girl in the valley.

Cassie — Vee's real friend. Boxy, blunt, says four words, ball cap, swims. The one honest instrument in the book. Clocks Randi and Pace cold and declines the game. Said "Goodbye, Vee" like she saw a cliff.

Brooke — chapter president. Warm voice, stays seated, outranks Randi. The audit in human form. Minor but teeth.

WHERE THINGS STAND

Timeline matters and I keep it: the con runs in order. Randi got the bench first (Sept 4). Next morning (Sept 5) she lies to Brooke about where she was, goes up to her cold room, can't cry. Sept 8, the pointing game on the quad — Pace points, Randi picks, lands on Vee, "her name is Vee, we've got stats together." Sept 9, Randi love-bombs Vee in stats and at Chi Latte. Sept 17 is the big hinge day: Pace "happens" into the coffee shop, fixes Vee's calculus, takes her number, froyo date — AND that same night Randi comes to his house and demands the full report, feeds him wine from her mouth, "tell me." Sept 18 the dinner (may I choose). Sept 19 Vee tells Cassie everything; later the smoothie place with Randi and Brooke, "Vee's my dear friend," cool hand on the table. Sept 27 the hike — blister, cherry pits, the russet dress she shelves, the truck striptease, his shaking hand, she takes care of him, cop lights cut her off.

So: the trap is fully built and sprung. Vee is in it and glowing. Randi is running it and losing to her own body. Pace has both women and tastes neither clean.

HOW I FEEL ABOUT EACH OF THEM

Pace — suspicious as hell and still half-seduced, which is exactly where the book wants me. Nobody is this perfect by accident. He's a hunter who builds benches. But the shaking hand in the truck, the giving up his only dry shirt — I can't fully hate him and I resent that.

Randi — I came in expecting a villain and got the most defended person I've ever read. I'm almost protective of her. I think the "game" is her armor — pointing him at Vee to keep him off *her* after the bench cracked her. She owes Vee everything and is paying it in a currency that will bankrupt all three of them.

Vee — I love her and I'm scared for her. She's livestock and she thinks she's chosen. Every tender thing she's fed is grooming. I'm complicit in how nice it looks.

Cassie — I trust her completely, which means she's the one I'll lose. Vee quietly demoted her at the smoothie place ("not her thing") and that was the first real casualty. Protect Cassie, book. You won't.
