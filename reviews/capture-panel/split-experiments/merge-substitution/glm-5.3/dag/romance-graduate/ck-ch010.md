CARRY-FORWARD — *A Polite Invitation*, ch. 1–10

WHO'S WHO
- **Pace** — the man. Math PhD, builds furniture, small plain house with cheap counters west of town off 614. Patient in a way I've never seen a romance hero be patient *without it being a performance of patience* — except it might be a performance, because chapter 1 told me he can stage things. Slice apples thin, cut-side up. Built the bench for her. He did NOT go to that coffee shop by accident (ch. 5, "Substitution" — he was in line on purpose, aimed at Vee). Kept Vee's froyo confession secret from Randi, "it was hers," which is either the most decent or most alarming thing he's done. Past: a woman named Daphne, a door he didn't respect, cost him "more than he knew anything could cost." That's all I've got and I want more. The shadow hand thing — always the right, never the other. Filed. Not explained yet.
- **Randi** — sorority girl, Standards board, single room, expensive-quiet everything, mother lives on egg whites and tofu, a face she built at thirteen and runs like a mask. Was GENUINELY cracked open by the bench night (ch. 1) — cried, saw herself, then went in the bathroom, looked in the mirror, and *decided the real one goes back behind the face. Not ever.* That decision is the engine of this whole book. Then she and Pace took Vee's yes as a game. Now she's in free fall with excellent posture — wet on his lap from hearing about Vee's froyo cup, thighs tightening at "she's easy to listen to," "I already do." She wants Vee. First woman she's ever let herself want. She's lying to everyone, including herself, and the "dear friend" hand was real AND the plan. Both at once.
- **Vee / Vivienne Thorne** — the mark. Econ, stats, honor society induction coming, mother's voice in her head (*floozies*), synchronized swimmer, tall lush body she hides under cardigans. Starving — literally, the food thing runs through everything, nobody's said the word yet and I'm scared of it. Chose "May I choose?" over any explicit thing. Said it herself without knowing: *Nobody did anything to me, I did it because I wanted to, on purpose.* Jacket's last line is aimed straight at that sentence.
- **Cassie** — Vee's roommate/friend, flat-voiced prophet. "Goodbye, Vee" (the whole word). "He looked at her the way you'd look at somebody's great-aunt." Reads Randi correctly, gets slid aside as Randi absorbs Vee ("it isn't really her place"). I'm protective of her already. She's the measuring stick.
- **Brooke** — chapter president, warm voice deployed like artillery, the not-standing move, interrogation about "Pace" over a fake paper. Keep an eye on her.
- **Daphne** — Pace's past. Door. Unresolved.

WHERE THINGS STAND
- Bench night (ch. 1): Pace took Randi further than she'd gone, made her beg past her own performed *please*, she cried, he said "There you are, Randi," and she sealed shut in the bathroom. That's why everything after is happening.
- Ch. 3: in bed, "Would you like to taste another woman?" — Randi's body answered before she could. Reframed as "only a threesome, with you." The pointing game on campus; she rejected the polished mirrors; the bare "Yes" at the laughing redhead. "Do you think she'll like the bench?"
- Ch. 4–5: Randi intercepts Vee (the "collision" — staged clean), stats class, Chi Latte; Pace "happens" into the coffee shop, helps with her integral, "May I call you?" — she gives the number.
- Ch. 8: dinner. "May I choose?" She stopped watching herself eat. She initiated the first kiss — her rule, her exception.
- Ch. 10: the hike. Summit, cherries, the russet silk dress she set down on the rock (HEARD THAT, book — it's coming back). Rain, the truck, she unbuttons her wet shirt for him with the mother-voice running — "the voice was the heat and the heat was the voice" — and his hand is *shaking* because he stood in the rain so she could change. She puts his cold hand on her belly. Says "please, get warm." Then the cop siren eats her goodbye at the dorm — she had a BIG thing to say and didn't get to. She still has his flannel.

HOW I FEEL ABOUT EACH OF THEM
- **Pace** — half in love, half on guard. The courtliness might be the disguise or might be the real man the con is borrowing. The shaking hand and the secret-keeping tilt me toward real, and that scares me more, not less. If he's genuinely good AND genuinely running a game, the book breaks right.
- **Randi** — fascinated, furious, aching. She sealed herself shut after the one night she was actually seen, and now she's outsourcing her desire through Vee. I understand her and I hate the machine she's building with Pace. She owes Vee the truth. Vee owes her nothing.
- **Vee** — pure protective tenderness. She's being fed — food, attention, choice — by people with a hidden ledger, and her joy is real and that's what makes it hurt to read. "She hadn't known she was allowed." Come ON.
- **Cassie** — my girl. I trust her completely. Something in her clocked the whole thing in ninety seconds and I'm dreading the moment Randi's orbit fully closes over Vee and Cassie's on the outside.
