The hangover scene wrecked me, though. The freezer rows of chicken and broccoli, the engine on the highway, his body leaning toward the gravel, the sobbing, and then "He ate anyway." The suspense has moved: not will he love her, but will he spend it.

Two things snag me. Randi is gone from his life since the green panties, and I wanted that thread touched harder. And "Daphne left" is still unexplained; the scar too. I'm still waiting on both. Also zero heat for the fourth chapter running, and the whiskey stretch gets a bit long.
