TELL A FRIEND: Okay, put down whatever you're reading. It's literary erotica but don't let that scare you off — it's not the closed-door beautiful-sentences-going-nowhere kind, it's genuinely explicit AND genuinely written, which I did not think existed anymore. Here's the hook: the blurb sells you a sweet innocent named Vee getting seduced by a courtly older math genius, and by chapter three you realize the whole seduction is a *game between two lovers* who picked her out on a quad like she was on a menu — and then the horror is watching all three of them actually fall in love inside the trap they built. The man is the strangest, most tender predator I've ever read; you'll be furious that you're rooting for him. There's a chapter that is just him measuring her for a dress and it's hotter than entire books I've DNF'd. My one complaint: the "girl comes home glowing and tells someone everything" scene runs a couple times too close together in the middle, and once I clocked the pattern I read a little faster through them — but even those keep turning the screw, because she tells her safe friend the clean version and tells the huntress the *real* version, and you watch which one is feeding on her. It made me ache about a friendship, which erotica is not supposed to be able to do. Warning: it will leave you wrecked and immediately wanting Book Two. I already do.
