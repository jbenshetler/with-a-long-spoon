The panties. Thirty-odd chapters of me asking "who are those panties for," and they've been lying flat at the bottom of his undershirt drawer all along, folded in thirds the way Vee folded them standing in his kitchen. Randi found them in the drawer she always goes to, where she took his undershirt from back in chapter 21. She came out naked-to-dressed in his shirt with Vee's green satin balled in her fist. She didn't say one word, and she fucked him with it pressed flat between her palm and his chest, never opening her hand. Then she cried. "It's hormones." And he let it be hormones. I had to put the Kindle face down on my chest for a minute.

Pace's read on her tears is "he kept a thing of Vee's, and Randi had never once offered him anything to keep." Maybe. I don't think that's it, or not all of it. I think she found proof that Vee gets kept and she doesn't. I also think "God, I want her" got out of her the night before and she heard herself say it. And I think something in her broke when he said "You don't have to do it for me" and she came on it. That moment was the most precise thing in the book. He took away her alibi kindly, and her body answered before she could. "But I'm *doing the work*." She's been calling her own longing a job so she's allowed to have it. Randi, honey.

Then the last paragraph. Vee's drawer standing out an inch. The tap ran and stopped and "for a while after that he heard nothing at all." She was in there a long time, twice. She went through Vee's drawer. The scrunchies, the pins in their little dish, the room "for more of her." And Pace pushes it shut with two fingers and blames the house settling in autumn. The man who reads Randi's breath on a quad from one slack hand can't read an open drawer. I screamed a little. Best last line in the book.

The middle of the chapter rearranged Pace for me, and not in his favor. "He had already chosen; the pointing was only to find out whether they had chosen the same." So the pointing game was never Randi's pick. He found Vee, got hard picturing her on his pillows with Randi, and then ran Randi through a test with her hand in his. "He had never once pointed at a woman he did not want." My "patient in a way that's a little frightening" note from chapter one just got a lot less "a little." And then "She consents — every time — or I'm done." I believe he means it, and that's what makes me angry. She can't consent to what she doesn't know exists. He's checking the gauge on the wrong tank. I liked him asking "Is she happy?" and "That isn't the same answer," and I hated that he got "she's choosing it, every Saturday" and said "All right."

The stats scene I never got to see, told by Randi: Vee scrubbed pink, hair wet, his coffee in the steel cup, "still full of your come," and Randi leaning over in class to *say that to her*. Wait, out loud, to Vee? That's Randi pushing past the line where she's just collecting. Vee hid her hands in her lap. I want that scene from Vee's side so badly. It's also exactly the both-at-once that makes this book work on me: it was filthy and hot and it made me furious on Vee's behalf, and I couldn't separate the two.

The sex itself was the most explicit in the book, and none of it was summary. Thank you. The mirror on the wall giving him her face when she thought she'd hidden it is my riser/mirror suspicion paying off in a way I didn't expect: she's the one being watched. "Four" as a title made me laugh, and the fourth crossing over into hurt was a real detail and not a brag. Randi saying "Here. Like this" and taking over, then "That's more than three," then "I'm inspired by her," is the most of herself she's ever shown.

Gripes, small: "took her holding on for the compliment it was" is the book winking at me over his head. I see it. "Somebody has to get her used to my mouth" was a bit too clean a confession of the plan. And "She had pressed Vee between them this morning, and it had excited her as much as it fired him" got me as Pace misreading, but it brushes close to telling. Her untouched black coffee, plus the five eggs scraped into the trash and then three, is the food thread breathing again without anyone saying so. Good.
