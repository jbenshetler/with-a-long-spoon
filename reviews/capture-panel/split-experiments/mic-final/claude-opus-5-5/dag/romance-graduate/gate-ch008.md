## REACTION

Okay, so this is a first-date chapter. I've read about four thousand of those. This one got me anyway, and I think it's because I know what Vee doesn't, and the book never once winks at me about it.

"May I choose?" That line made my stomach drop. On the quad Pace told Randi "I point. You pick," and now he's at a table asking Vee if he can pick for her, and she hands it over with relief, like setting down something heavy. In the moment it's lovely. Over the whole book it's chilling. I felt both at once and the book didn't explain either one. That's what I want from it.

Then the food. Two chapters ago Randi was eating thin apple slices, cut side up, with that calculator running behind her face. Now Vee has gone all day without eating, and she's tearing hot bread and eating with her hands. She stops watching herself eat and doesn't notice she's stopped. Pace feeds women. That's his move. With Randi it was tender and sad, and with Vee it's tender and kind of ominous, because I watched Randi report back on the couch last night. Is "she built a cup like nobody had ever told her when to stop" going to get a sequel tonight? "She ate like she'd been empty for years"? I'd honestly like to read that scene, and I feel a bit gross about wanting it, and I like the book for making me feel that.

What I really noticed is that we never get inside Pace's head this chapter. For the whole evening he's only what Vee sees: showered, shaved, polished boots, the eat-with-your-right-hand thing, leaving her the last inch. Every move is perfect, which is exactly what bothers me. Is he enjoying this for its own sake, or is he collecting stories for Randi? I think the book is keeping him closed on purpose and I'm itching to know.

The kiss worked. "I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days" sounded a bit put-on in her mouth. But then she doesn't walk on, the hand-hold goes taut and stops him, and she says "But I'm making an exception." He doesn't take the opening, she has to come back for the second kiss, and she grins into it. That was sweet, and the pull between them felt real. It's still a closed-mouth, PG kiss, though.

A few things bugged me. Cassie's "That's not a boy, that's a man" is straight off a TikTok caption. The bit about his care running "down to the part of him she'd only get close enough to smell" is trying too hard. And her hand having been wrong "all its life" until his took it is the purple version of something the book had already shown me better with the bread.

Here's the pacing honestly. The pool chapter was quiet and this one is quiet, so that's two in a row from Vee's side. The suspense under it kept me reading, but I'm counting. If chapter 9 is also soft I'll start flipping ahead for the bench.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: "the guy is SO good at this and that's the scary part. she's getting fed and she has no idea she's being served up."
