**REACTION**

Okay. After Chapter 1 I needed a breather, and this was a good one. It wasn't a nothing chapter either, because Brooke is terrifying. She never stands up and she never says anything you could quote back at her. She files "Pace," calls him an engineer to knock him down a notch, takes the math PhD without giving the notch back, and then lets Randi go when *she* decides it's over. I've watched that exact person run a room. The guess that he's "short-cute," the line about not caring about cars, the pickup truck behind Hahn: it's a whole class system done in four minutes of smiling, and Randi plays her part perfectly and still loses. The detail I loved was Randi getting cut off before she could use the three lines she'd lined up.

I don't believe the "not his section" answer at all. The pause was too long. I'm filing that away the same way Brooke filed his name.

The bedroom part got to me more than I expected. She walks to the vanity and doesn't sit. She walks to the bed and doesn't sit. She lifts her hand toward the closet and puts it down. Then she lies under that cheap duvet in her shoes. The duvet is the knife: "It was not — it was a duvet." She's thinking about his blanket, the too-warm house, the man, and the book doesn't spell that out, which I appreciated. "He's hard to say no to," in a different key, made my stomach drop a little.

Where it lost me a bit: "fraction of a second" comes back three or four times, and lines like "The not-standing was the move" and "The release was the cleanest hit" sometimes explain the power game instead of just showing it. The no-door/held-breath paragraph at the end is lovely, but it does tell me what I'd already felt. It's not enough to put me off, but I'm watching for it.

What really has my attention is the jacket. It promises Vee, and two chapters in she hasn't shown up. Now I get it, though. Randi just decided she'll never come out from behind the face again, and somebody who can't feel things herself goes looking for someone to feel them for her. I think I just watched the "game" get born. That's the hook, and it's a good one. One more quiet chapter would test me, but this one earned its place.
