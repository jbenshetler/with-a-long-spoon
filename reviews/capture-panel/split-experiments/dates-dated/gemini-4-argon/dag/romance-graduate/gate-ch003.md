REACTION

God, this book is so smart it actually makes my chest ache a little.

First of all, the opening of this chapter—walking past the covered bench and saying *"Let's do something different. Today."*—after the absolute emotional devastation of Chapter 1 and 2? Genius. You don't need a paragraph of internal monologue explaining that Randi is terrified of how deeply he saw her on Friday night; she just looks at the table and pivots. And then the sex scene immediately after... *"Would you like to taste another woman?"* making her come on the spot before her brain can even put up its guard? That is *such* a hot beat, and psychologically it does ten things at once. The way she immediately dresses her own terrifying, genuine desire up as a sexy little gift for him (*"Only in a threesome. With you."*) so she can survive wanting it—I swear, the character work here is operating on a completely different level than 99% of what's on my Kindle. Randi is finding a way to keep playing with Pace without ever having to be the one on the bench crying again. If they're hunting someone else, the spotlight isn't solely on her. And *"Do you think she'll like the bench?"* at the very end! Passing the vulnerability off to the new girl like a hot potato while getting off on the power of it. Chills.

And the campus walk! Turning the blurb premise ("she was chosen in a game Randi and Pace started in bed") into this sunlit, slow-burn afternoon of people-watching was so unexpectedly magnetic. When he stopped and kissed her on the path without having wiped his face from earlier, so she tasted herself in the middle of the quad while looking at a girl she said "maybe" to? I literally had to put my hand over my mouth. *Filthy* and completely effortless.

Then Vee walks out of the dining hall. It’s wild how much dread and excitement I felt at the exact same time. Because Vee is everything Randi won't let herself be—lush, uncurated, taking up space, laughing with her head thrown back like she doesn't know she's being watched—and watching Randi's decade of dance and sorority training weaponize itself into a three-second "accidental" shoulder bump where *Vee* apologizes to *her*? It was like watching a leopard in tall grass. And yet, when Randi comes back doing that little involuntary happy footwork on the brick and squeaking *"Yes"*, she's so genuinely lit up and twenty-one that you can't even hate her for it. Neither she nor Pace thinks they're the villains here. Pace thinks he's liberating his girlfriend and feeding her starved appetites; Randi thinks she's found a way to have heat and aliveness while keeping her armor on. And poor Vee just walked into a stats class trap. I am *hooked*.
