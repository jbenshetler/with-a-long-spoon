Okay, the title. *How It's Done.* I read the chapter and then I went back and looked at the title and said a word out loud in my empty kitchen.

Because that line — *nobody measures over a bra, sweetheart, it's not a thing, it's just how it's done* — is the single most frightening sentence in this book so far, and it's delivered in the voice you'd use to explain the salad fork. It is not true. I have had one thing in my life altered by an actual tailor and she measured me in my actual bra, because the bra is *part of the garment math*. Randi knows that. Randi, who has had a great many things made. She reached across a velvet booth and handed Vee a false piece of worldly common knowledge for one reason: it was the key that unlocked the door. *If it's ordinary, I didn't do anything, so I can say it.* And Vee's chest let go and she said it. That is a lie told to produce a confession, and it is also, horribly, a kindness — it took the shame off a thing Vee had been carrying. Both. At the same time. I don't know what to do with that and I love that I don't.

And then she retroactively laundered Pace. That's what gets me. Whatever Pace was doing with *it'll lie to me* — and in chapter sixteen I believed him, I believed the craft of it, I still mostly do — Randi just went back and stamped it APPROVED PROCEDURE in Vee's memory. Vee will never again be able to wonder about that moment. It's been foreclosed. That's the most efficient thing anyone has done to her yet.

The interrogation itself I could barely sit still through. *Were there any — challenges?* With the little hook in it. And Vee's **"It was cold in the house"** — the fastest, flattest, most obviously untrue sentence in the chapter, and the house was *warm*, we were there, we watched him turn the thermostat up hours early. Randi puts the word *cold* down on the table and turns it over once and says *hmm* — and that *hmm* is Pace's noise. That's his sound, over her heel on the trail, over her rising onto her toes. These two have the same tells and I don't think either of them has noticed. Then *which hip* — *the left one* — *oh, all the best ones start at the left hip,* which means absolutely nothing and is pure hypnotist patter, a fake confirmation to keep the yeses coming.
