Oh. *Oh.* This is the one I've been circling the drain waiting for, and it came in the quietest possible wrapper — his POV, lamplight, Sade under the door, nobody fighting. And it gutted me more than any of the fucking did.

Because Randi *stayed down.* My whole notes are built on "she doesn't stay, she says the true thing and flees the bed," and tonight she stayed down on his chest and talked, and every single thing out of her mouth was Vee. Not harvesting Vee. *Missing* Vee. "I'd only just dropped her off on Saturday and I was doing it again." "She got past me before I could help it." "I keep waiting to get bored of her and it doesn't come." That's not the spider. That's a woman confessing she's caught in her own web and doesn't even know she's saying it out loud. The machine didn't break with a bang — it's dissolving in her hands while she lies there warm and doesn't notice her fingers are open.

And the thing that actually scared me: he's merging them. He lies there and lets himself put them both in the bed, Randi over Vee, black hair into red, both laughing, then both naked, still laughing. The book let me sit inside the fantasy with him and it was tender and it was *greedy* at the exact same time and I couldn't get a blade between the two. That's the whole book in one image and nobody explained it to me, thank God.

But here's the knife. He says Vee lights a room, she's the most alive person standing there and has no idea — and Randi goes *still.* "A different one, the kind that takes holding." The faintest tightening. "Yeah. She is," a beat too long. There it is. She's jealous and she can't even locate the direction of it — jealous of him for having Vee, jealous of Vee for having him, jealous of what they are to each other that she architected herself out of. And then she fucks him again to stop having to feel it. I know that move. I invented that move in my twenties.

And then — she *ate.* She ate the whole plate. Asparagus, the last stalk, scraped the edge, nothing left. This is the woman from chapter 20 who would not be fed, who said "it's selfish" and drove off and left her bowl. Tonight she put her feet up inside his undershirt like a child and asked him to make her something and ate every bite and took his hand across the table and slept against his chest. She let herself be fed. Something gave way in her and she doesn't know it did, but I know it did, and it terrifies me because the last time somebody let themselves get fed in this book it was Vee, and we know where that road goes. Randi's walking her own chapter-one road backward now. She broke her own rule and she's going to be destroyed by it.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: SHE ATE THE WHOLE PLATE. She stayed down. The one who built the cage just walked into it on her own two frosted-blue feet and I don't think Pace even clocked what it cost her, he was busy watching her eat and smiling. Nobody in this room knows what room they're in. STILL. Three of them, three different shows.

It's short and it's almost all talk and I didn't want it to end. That never happens to me with a bridge chapter. This wasn't a bridge. This was the floor giving way under the quietest step.
