That's what chilled me. The asymmetry isn't a secret he's agonizing over in this chapter; it's just a fact he soft-pedals past, like the climb. And Sheri — bless her — names the fuse: "If that's not already complicated, it will be." The one person outside can see the wall coming. Nobody inside is looking.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: *he can't stop talking about her to a woman on a BIKE, he's actually gone, AND he just said "not yet" about her knowing like it's a maintenance item — I don't know whether to root for him or brace for the crater.* Both. I'm doing both.

The "She couldn't take your heat" line about Sheri is a cute little self-aware wink too — he knows which women can run hot and which get burned, and he's just told me, without knowing it, that Vee is the one who gets burned.
