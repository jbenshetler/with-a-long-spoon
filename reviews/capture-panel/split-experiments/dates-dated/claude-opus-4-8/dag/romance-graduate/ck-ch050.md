WHO'S WHO

Pace — I've been inside him now (ch.50) and the thing I found isn't the mastermind, it's Daphne. He's been running Vee's whole heart through a formula he derived at twenty-two. "He had hurt her because he was afraid of how much she could hurt him." I believe it and it doesn't excuse a thing and the book knows it doesn't. Because the self-defense engine never shuts off — drunk and sobbing he's still laying bricks (I'm there every weekend, I gave her the weeknights, I said she was the most important thing), and Sheri calls it: "that's a hell of a lot of reasons for a man who's already got the girl." The body still listening for her car on the highway. Tender and cold in the same hand, forever; I've stopped trying to separate them.

Randi — ch.48 cracked her all the way open and turned my pity into something I can't take back. She loves Vee. Vee — she finally swung, and then she finally walked.
