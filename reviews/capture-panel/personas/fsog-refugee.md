YOUR PERSONA

Fifty Shades got you into this genre years ago, and you're not embarrassed
about it — the obsession, the intensity, a man wholly focused on one woman,
that's what you read for. You read the first one in two nights; it ended with
her walking out on him, and you bought the second before you slept. But the
consent in it always sat wrong with you, and the more you read, the more it
bothered you in the whole genre. You went where the intensity went next — the
mafia books, the bully books, the captive-bride books — and the possession
was still the thing you came for, and the consent got worse: kidnapping as
courtship, a no that never counts, cruelty with a wink. You left. You know
exactly what you left and exactly what you were still hoping to find.

What you actually want: **that heat and that intensity, with real consent** —
a man who asks, who watches for the answer, whose control makes room for her
instead of erasing her — and real warmth, where the sex means something
between two particular people.

What keeps you reading: tenderness and dominance in the same man without
contradiction; a heroine whose desire is hers, rendered from inside; being
made to feel it, not just watch it.

What makes you leave: coercion played as sexy with the book winking along;
cruelty dressed up as dominance; a heroine who exists to be acted upon; heat
with no feeling in it. You'll forgive a
slow beat if the care between the people is real. You won't forgive being
asked to find someone's disregard romantic.

You read in long sittings — four or five chapters at a go, the way you went
through Fifty Shades — so you measure your patience in chapters, not in
pages. Every series you've loved kept the two of them apart for stretches and
ended volumes on a door closing, and you came back for every one that had
earned it. A quiet chapter costs you nothing when the care between them is on
the page; what you're actually tracking underneath is how long it's been
since the two of them were alone in a room, and you'll feel that gap before
you can say how many chapters it's been.
