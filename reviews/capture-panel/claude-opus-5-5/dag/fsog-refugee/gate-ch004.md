Okay. So now I'm standing inside the girl they picked.

I knew the switch to Vee's head was coming, since the jacket is basically her story. It still did something to my stomach to go from Randi pressed up against Pace saying *Do you think she'll like the bench?* straight to Vee in the third minute of not looking at the door. I know what the door is. She doesn't. Everything sweet in this chapter is spoiled for me in advance, and I can't decide if that's cruel or delicious. It's probably both, which I suspect is the whole point.

What got me is how real Vee's hunger is. "The right pen" warming her like praise she'd worked for. Making herself wait one beat so the yes doesn't come out the size it actually is. I've been that girl at the edge of someone's brighter life. And the cardigan stuff hit hard: she knows she has the best body in the room and covers it to the collarbone anyway, with her mother's voice moving into her own mouth. She has a real interior and real shame, and that's the heroine I want. Her desire is hers, rendered from inside, which is exactly what I'm reading for. Except right now it's being cultivated by someone who chose her from a lineup. Randi's "Love you, girl" landed on me like a cold hand.

Cassie is the person I trust most in this book right now, and she said maybe fifteen words. "Goodbye, Vee." God. I wanted to grab Vee's sleeve.

My unease is about whether the book wants me to find this romantic. Chapter 3 had Pace pointing at strangers on a quad, rating women for a game he hadn't named, and I went along with it because Randi's want was so raw. Seeing it from the other side, it's a love-bomb. Vee can't remember one word Randi said, only the feeling. That blank transcript scared me a little, the way it's supposed to. What's holding me is that nobody is being forced. Randi isn't cruel. She's warm, and maybe she means some of it. Vee is walking toward the warmth because she's starving. The jacket promised "every yes was freely given, that was the problem," and this is the first chapter where I feel that problem in my body.

I miss Pace. It's been a full chapter since he was in a room with anyone. And the Pace I've been falling for is the one with Randi, who waits outside a locked bathroom door. I need to see him meet Vee. I need to see whether he asks her the way he asked Randi, knowing what he knows and she doesn't.
