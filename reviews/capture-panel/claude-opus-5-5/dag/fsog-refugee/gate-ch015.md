## REACTION

The thing I keep thinking about is that she lied to them.

Cassie got the whole truck scene, on purpose: "I took my wet shirt off in front of him. On purpose. Me." Meg and Kayla, over cheese fries in the gold room, get a cleaned-up version: she was about to kiss him, he was too cold, so she had him drive her home. The knock on the glass, the crooked finger, every one of the buttons, the way he looked at her like she'd handed him something are all gone. She kept that for herself. I was proud of her for it, and right under the pride something went sour, because one chapter ago I watched Pace on his back with Randi riding him say "Her stomach is soft." The narration let him off because he "did not say how he knew." But he still handed it over. The one thing Vee wouldn't give her own friends at dinner, he gave away for Randi to use in bed. She's guarding it and he's spending it, and she has no idea anyone else has it. I sat with that for a minute before I could keep reading.

I loved getting to see Vee with her own people. She has a life: Meg's calm fork, Kayla's curls and her running commentary, Theo, who she let down kindly. And the boots were Meg's! It's a relief to see that Randi isn't her whole world, and that the "dear friend" hand on the table at the Smoothie Bird didn't replace everyone else. I liked Vee laughing loud enough that the next table wanted in. That's not a girl who exists to be acted on.

"He asks me first... and then that's exactly what happens, the way I said it." Then: "climbing the walls over a man asking me what I want for dinner." Yes, Vee. That's exactly why I'm here, and the book knows it, and so does she. Meg's "everybody's different at the start" landed on me even though it slid right past Vee.

Then the text: *would you come to my house Saturday. Two o'clock.* Kayla calls it a ransom note and she's not wrong. My stomach dropped. The house means the long table, and the bench under the top. And after chapter 14 I'm suspicious: is that text Pace, or is it Randi's appetite, sent from Pace's phone? Randi left his bed without staying for the first time ever, her belly went tight as a board, and she said "I want us." Pace read all of it as want, and I think it was pain. Now Vee is being asked to the same room. I want her through that door so badly, and I'm scared of who else might be there, or who'll be told about it afterward.

It's been twelve days since that kiss, and the truck since then. I'm not starved yet, because the truck fed me. But Saturday is the payoff and I'm not putting the book down before it.
