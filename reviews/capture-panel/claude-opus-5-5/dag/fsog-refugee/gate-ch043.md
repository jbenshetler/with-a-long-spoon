I put the phone face down on the duvet and said "oh, you *liar*" out loud to an empty bedroom.

It isn't anything in this chapter that got me. It's chapter forty-one, two chapters back, which went off in my chest about a page in. She's lying on her back thinking *he's all mine*, and she asks who does this to him when she's not around. He takes that pause he always takes "when he meant to answer a thing properly." Then he says "Sometimes I take care of it myself." I had stamped that pause as proof he was honest. It turns out he's honest the way a lawyer is. Every word he said was true, and none of it was the truth. Then the deadbolt in this chapter "the way it always did," with the heat turned up two hours ahead of time. That's the ritual I'd been reading as hers. *Always.* How many Mondays has this been going on?

The jacket told me. It said three games, and I bought the book on that line, so I can't act ambushed. But knowing a thing from a blurb is different from watching Randi pin down the timing: "still full of your come." She knows when Vee left his house because she arrives after.
