**REACTION**

Okay, this one was short and I liked it more than I expected to.

"I want to meet your people," said in a grocery-list voice with his back to her. That's the first time in fifty-seven chapters he's asked to come *out*. The whole book he's been the closed door and the house up the gravel cut, and she's the one who has to drive to him. Then the heel coming up out of Meg's boot on the trail in October, and "the one who can talk the paint off a wall," handed back in her own words. I melted a little. I've read a hundred heroes who "notice everything," and it's always eye color or her coffee order. This was a half-sentence she'd forgotten saying, and he'd kept it on a shelf. That's a better love declaration than the actual word would have been, and I think the book knows it.

Which is also my suspicion. Sheri told him straight out: *say it*. What he went home and did is the thing he always does, *believe what I do*, only bigger. He's still not saying it, he's just doing it in public now. Vee doesn't notice that, and right now I don't think she needs to. She got the coat, the pancakes, "I missed you so much" with his voice cracking. But I'm keeping count.

I loved Vee here. She builds a dinner for Randi first, sees she'd be stuffing Cassie into a place with real napkins, and won't do it. "She'd watched somebody be fine about it before. She wasn't going to look at when." That's Chili's and she's protecting *him*, quietly, without making a speech about it. Then "Will you pick the place?" She's learned his noise thing without either of them ever having to say it. And "Tables have ends. Somebody ends up at one" is the most Pace sentence there is, and kind.

The Theo bit: she over-explains, he says "Bring Theo" and reaches for the next pan. No jealousy, no bit. After a Kindle full of men who growl at a guy from English class, that felt like a grown-up, and I liked it.

The party itself is what scares me. Randi in the middle of everybody, Pace across the room, Cassie with her arms crossed, *and* Brooke's ultimatum, *choose*, still hanging over Randi from three chapters ago. Pace says "let me handle it," and Pace handling things is exactly how this whole spring got arranged. I'll bet you money this party is part of the plan. And Vee still hasn't had her Tuesday dinner with Randi about the taste thing.

Heat-wise it's basically nothing, just her naked on the lacquered chair with her wine like it's normal. That's a sexy detail precisely because nobody comments on it. After two big chapters I don't mind one quiet one. A second in a row and I'll start skimming.
