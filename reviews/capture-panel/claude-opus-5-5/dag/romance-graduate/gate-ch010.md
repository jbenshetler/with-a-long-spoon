I'd filed it as backstory, a wound to be healed. Then here it turns into the engine of the heat. The voice says *girls in trucks, three dates, your mother would*, and her body answers every line with heat, because the heat *knows the voice is right* and wants to be exactly that, with him watching. I've read a hundred "good girl unleashed" scenes, and they always treat shame as something to get past. This one makes the shame the turn-on, and doesn't apologize for it or fix it. Her thighs press together when she hears her mother's word. That's a grown-up, slightly ugly, true thing about desire, and I sat up in bed for it.

And it's *hers*. She knocks on the glass and crooks a finger. She holds his eyes so he knows something is coming, then looks down on purpose so his eyes go with hers. He doesn't move or lean in. He just takes it, and the stillness is the answer. It's all clothed, nobody touches anybody, and it's hotter than half the full scenes on my Kindle. Then the shaking hand got me. She reaches for him to start the next thing and finds him shivering. He'd been standing in the rain for her the whole time and forgot he was cold. She pulls his hand under the flannel against her belly. That's where the bond actually turned for me, more than the striptease did.
