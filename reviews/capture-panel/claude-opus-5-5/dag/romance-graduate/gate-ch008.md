**REACTION**

Okay. I'll admit it. The kiss under the tree got me grinning at my Kindle like an idiot. Her "I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days," him not arguing, her feet staying put, the hand pulling taught between them. That's a real beat. She makes the first move and then makes the second one bigger, and he just stands in the shadow and lets the last inch be hers. Then he finally meets her "all the way," with the mint still in both their mouths, and she goes back for more with the grin still on her mouth. That's a first kiss I'll actually remember, which almost never happens for me anymore. It hits so hard because nothing in the scene is pushing.

But I can't read Pace straight anymore, and I don't think the book wants me to. "May I choose?" is the whole chapter, isn't it. I already watched him slice Randi's apple thin, cut side up, because she eats faster that way. Now he's choosing Vee's food, and she feels the relief of handing over the choice as a *surrender*. That's the word she uses. Two girls with mothers living in their heads about food, and he feeds both of them. I don't know yet if he's a man who's honestly good at this or a man who's very good at running a play, and the not-knowing is delicious. Then there's the line "she was already learning she could trust where he was taking her." Babe. He told his girlfriend about your froyo two nights ago while she rode him on the couch. The dramatic irony makes everything sweet in this chapter sit on a blade, and that keeps the sweetness from going soggy.

Vee is great, though. She's someone with a real inner life: the lobby moment where she forgets to get off the elevator and has to beg the doors, the shoes borrowed from a girl down the hall, the low readiness she carries everywhere for the bill to come due, and then it just isn't there. I believe her. I like her a lot, and I'm scared for her.

Where it lost me a little: the dinner runs long. I was already drifting at the pool chapter, and this is the second quiet Vee chapter in a row. Even if the chapters run short, I'm starting to feel how little heat there's been since the couch. And the book tells me too much at moments. The hollow "had been empty a lot longer than since noon," and then the hand feeling like it had been wrong "all its life maybe" for want of his. I got it, I promise. Mostly, though, it trusts me.

What I'd text at 1 a.m.: "she KISSED him and he waited and I'm screaming but also he's 100% feeding her like he fed Randi and Randi is going to want a full report tonight."
