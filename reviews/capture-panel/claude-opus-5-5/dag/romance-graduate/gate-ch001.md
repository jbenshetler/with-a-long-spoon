I'm still sitting here with my mouth open. I read that whole chapter as Vee. Sorority girl, junior, black hair, blue eyes, the cardigan, the apple. I'd text my friend at 1 a.m.: "the first chapter of the vee book isn't vee and I'm devastated about the OTHER girl."
