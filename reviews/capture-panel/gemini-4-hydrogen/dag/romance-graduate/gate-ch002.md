REACTION

God, the contrast between Pace’s house and this place is like walking into a walk-in freezer. Last night she was in a too-warm room with a man who noticed how she eats apples; this morning she’s sneaking into her own sorority house in last night’s clothes, getting cornered by three girls who smile like they’re sharpening knives.

Brooke not even standing up from the table to stop her—just sitting there with her chapter spreadsheet and her warm, deadly voice—made my skin crawl. I’ve known women like Brooke. The way they do it is so smooth you can’t even call it out without looking crazy: *Oh, Pace? I thought you said Chase. Engineering, isn’t it?* And Sloane chiming in about the white pickup, and Kinsey calling him *short-cute*—and Randi just *takes* it. She lies about where she was, she matches Kinsey’s laugh, she lets them shrink Pace down into some harmless, slightly embarrassing grad student she’s slumming it with.

It hurt to watch, honestly. Especially knowing how undone she was on that bench twelve hours ago. You can see why she has the mask, though. In a house like that, if they ever smelled that she was actually *vulnerable* with someone, they’d eat her alive.

And then the ending, with her lying on top of her made bed in last night’s clothes with her shoes still on, under a cold synthetic duvet, not even able to cry—just holding her breath because she’s been doing it for twenty-one years. It’s bleak. There’s zero heat in this chapter, obviously, and I’m ready to get back to Pace (and finally meet Vee!), but as a character chapter it’s razor-sharp.
