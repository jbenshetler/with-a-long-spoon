Watching Randi text under the table—and Vee just assuming it’s some boy—and then two minutes later Pace walks through the door on cue? No wonder Vee is completely cooked before he even stands up to leave.

And that froyo walk at the end? Brutal in the sweetest way. He’s not even faking the kindness, which is what makes the whole thing so dangerous.
