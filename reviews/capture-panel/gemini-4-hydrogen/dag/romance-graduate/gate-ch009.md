Oh, *man*. That chapter was a rollercoaster in the best way.

First of all, coming back to the dorm room with Vee still glowing from Saturday night—spinning around in a space too small to hold her, throwing a pillow at Cassie, completely lit up because *she* was the one who pulled Pace back and kissed him—was pure joy. And Cassie is just the best friend anyone could ask for: *"What's his name, so I know what to tell the state troopers when you go missing on a mountain with a man you met at a coffee shop?"*

And then six days later, we’re right back in the Smoothie Bird, and watching Randi operate is honestly chilling. The way she snaps her fingers and pretends she can’t remember Pace’s name—*"Paul?"*—when we know she’s been in his bed talking about Vee? And the fake story about her Friday date with some lacrosse guy just to get Vee to open up? She is *so* smooth. You could feel Vee shrinking on that high stool, suddenly feeling too big and underdressed in her pilled cardigan. And then Randi—Randi, who spent all of Chapter 2 letting Brooke and Kinsey shrink Pace down to nothing just to keep her standing in the house—reaches across the table, takes Vee’s hand in plain view of Brooke, and says, *"Vee's my dear friend."*

She *spent* something on Vee right in front of the chapter president. She risked looking uncool, risked Brooke’s little spreadsheet smile, just to make sure Vee didn't feel small. That’s not just the game anymore. You don't put your own social capital on the line like that for a mark unless you’re already halfway gone yourself.

Vee walking home feeling *picked* broke my heart a little, because she still has no idea what’s actually happening—and now I’m dying to see what happens on Sunday’s hike.
