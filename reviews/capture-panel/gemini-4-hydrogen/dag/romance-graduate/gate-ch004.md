Oh, *man*. Watching the trap close from inside Vee’s head is a completely different kind of thriller.

We just spent three chapters watching Randi lock herself behind a mask and then weaponize her sorority-girl polish to hunt Vee down on the quad—and now we’re sitting in a stats lecture with Vee, who has no idea she’s being hunted, just desperately trying not to look at the door because she’s hoping the cool, glowing girl from yesterday actually meant it when she said *we should sit together*. And Cassie! God, I loved Cassie in two minutes flat. Randi’s whole rehearsed *I’m Randi, yes like the adjective* bit bounces right off her like rain off a raincoat, and then at the end of class she just puts her ball cap on and says, *"Goodbye, Vee."* Not *see you later*, not *bye*. Just *goodbye*, like she’s watching her friend walk into a house she’s not coming back out of.

The Chi Latte scene is so sharp. I love how Vee reads the room—clocking the uniformed girls, clocking the two underdressed girls at the bar, hearing her mother’s voice in her head calling them *floozies* while her own body is quietly aware that she’s got more than they do under that cardigan. There’s no real heat here yet, just that low, unexamined crush Vee has on Randi and that *You're a total fox* on the sidewalk, which is plenty to keep the hum going. I’m dying to see what happens when Pace finally walks into Vee’s line of sight.
