REACTION

Oh, *Cassie*. "Goodbye, Vee." Not *see you later*, just flat-out *Goodbye, Vee* before walking out of the row with her ball cap pulled low! Cassie clocked Randi in roughly four seconds flat — didn't take the rehearsed name-joke bait, didn't get dazzled by the four-hundred-word-a-day charm offensive, just looked at her friend getting swept off to Chi Latte by the shiny sorority queen and quietly said goodbye to the version of Vee she knew. That landed in my stomach like a stone, especially paired with Randi's breezy *"See you later, okay?"* at the end.

And oh god, being inside Vee's head is *achy* in the best way. Watching her pretend three times not to look at the lecture hall door while writing the date just to give her hands somewhere to be! And the whole Chi Latte sequence — hearing *Floozies* in her mother's voice, then catching herself looking a beat too long at the sprinter's stomach and low waistband, secretly knowing she's got more body than any of those girls in their sage quarter-zips while keeping her cardigan buttoned to the collarbone because "nice girls" don't invite that kind of looking! And then looking at Randi with "a flat helpless want" that she completely misfiles as wanting to *be* near her, wanting to belong at the high window stool, when every single line of it reads like a crush she doesn't have the vocabulary to recognize yet. And thinking Randi's polished-over Kentucky vowels are *a boarding school in Switzerland or a year in Paris*!! Oh, honey. We saw Randi's freezing single room and her egg-white-and-tofu mother and Brooke slicing her up on Saturday morning — Vee thinks Randi isn't performing like the other girls in that café, when Randi is actually just performing at an Olympic level!

The detail that really gave me chills, though, was the blank transcript. That Vee walks home glowing, feeling chosen and lit up with all those little spoons ringing against the coffee cups (hello, series title?! *With a Long Spoon*?!), and when she tries to remember a single actual thing Randi told her about herself, *nothing is there*. Just the feeling, and a line that went in "like a key in a lock" — the exact same phrase from Chapter 1 when Randi lay against Pace's chest plotting her way out! Randi gave away zero real pieces of herself in an entire hour and walked off with Vee completely spellbound.

I loved finally meeting Vee from the inside, and I already want to wrap her up and protect her, *and* I want to watch Pace look at her when she isn't buttoning a cardigan up to her neck. Now put Pace and Vee in a room together, please!
