TELL A FRIEND: Okay, this one is *weird* and I mean that as the highest compliment. It's not my usual — there's no cage, no villain snarling, it's all silk and no visible knife for whole stretches, and Chapter 2 nearly lost me because it goes full literary-sad-girl. BUT. The trick it pulls is that the *reader* is the one being seduced along with the victim, and the predators are a couple doing it as a *game*, and one of them — Randi — is secretly a wreck who got hollowed out on a spanking bench in chapter one and is now doing to an innocent exactly what was done to her. It's capture-and-obsession but the cage is made of kindness and coffee dates, which honestly disturbed me more than chains would. The dread is unbelievable — there's a quiet roommate named Cassie who says "goodbye" like she's watching a girl walk off a cliff and I got actual chills. It's slower and colder than what we usually devour, so don't start it tired. But if you want to feel genuinely complicit while a sweet lonely girl gets love-bombed toward something awful — this is it. I bought Book One.
