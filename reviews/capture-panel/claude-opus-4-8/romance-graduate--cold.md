GATE 1 — The Bench
DECISION: CONTINUE
CAPTURE: 9
ALMOST-STOPPED: The opening paragraph, honestly — "the small economy of two people who did not perform for each other settled across the house like a sheet being drawn." I braced for one of those precious literary throat-clearings where a man keeps a house warm and I'm supposed to be impressed by furniture. If the apple slices hadn't turned into something with actual teeth in it fast, I'd have bailed by the second oak cabinet.
WHY: But it didn't go coy and it didn't go clinical — it went *deep* and stayed explicit at the same time, which is the exact thing I've been unable to find. The bench scene is genuinely erotic AND it's a character study; the heat is the mechanism of the interiority, not a break from it. That final movement — Randi in the bathroom deciding, "not ever," choosing the face over being seen — gutted me. A sex scene that's actually about the terror of being known. I have not read this before.
