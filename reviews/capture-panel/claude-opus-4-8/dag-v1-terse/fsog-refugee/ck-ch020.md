Then the fitting (ch14–16) — he made her the burgundy silk dress she'd named once on the summit and thrown away, measured her down to the bare skin on a riser he built, matched the cloth to *her* not the magazine. HOW I FEEL ABOUT EACH OF THEM

Pace — still the knife. Every single thing he does is the thing I've hunted for years: "Would you be bothered if I was too hard?" asked as a real question with the answer left to her; the kneeling; choosing doing-it-right over the easy yes and letting me feel it cost him. If I didn't know it was aimed I'd be gone on him like she is. But I know, so the tenderness is real *and* engineered on the same wire, and I can't put it down and I resent how well he's built. Randi — this is where my dread lives now.
