Oh, this is the one. This is the chapter my notes kept circling — I wrote "I'm waiting for the register of the sticky-fingers brunch to come back — depth AND charge in the same minute," and here it is, and it paid. There's no scene in it. Nobody's over a knee, nobody's in cognac heels, and it's still the hottest thing in the whole last stretch, because the heat is *Vee talking*, and watching the talk land on Randi.

What got me, actually got me, was the shape of the confession. For forty chapters Vee's been handed her own appetite a week in advance — Randi plants the recipe, Vee "discovers" it. I sat up. Vee reads it as *pride* — "a Saturday of hers could do that to a woman like Randi" — and the dramatic irony is almost unbearable, because I know what I'm watching and Vee thinks she's flattered a glamorous friend.

And that's the drowning-woman read my notes kept flagging, confirmed and then some. Randi set this table. She chose this girl *with Pace, before Vee was anything to either of them* (the jacket). She built a creature who would walk into a dark green booth and describe, in loving helpless detail, exactly how thoroughly another person has woken her up — and now she has to sit across from it and absorb it. "He woke you up." "Just us" coming out "softer than the plan called for, a small roughness under it." "I gave him *all* of it." I'd text my friend: *she finally broke Randi just by being happy and she thinks she gave her a GIFT.*
