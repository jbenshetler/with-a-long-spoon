Okay. So here's what I wasn't braced for: the book just gave me a whole chapter of pure joy and I didn't want it to stop. After twenty chapters of the machine grinding — Randi's harvest, the bowl in the fridge, the scalpel I keep waiting to land — this one is Vee alone in a window full of gold light trying on a dress, and it *earned* every minute of it, and I feel slightly stupid about how much it worked on me.

The grin got me first. He breaks the kiss at the door not to withhold — God, not another withholding beat, I was so ready to be tired of it — but because he's a kid who can't keep the surprise in his mouth. His eyes going past her to the thing he made. That's new. That's Pace with the inventory eyes *off* for once, or looking like they're off, and I let myself have it.

And the dress reveal — "he'd taken all that trouble and then taken the further trouble to hide the trouble." That's the thesis of him in one line and it's the first time it read as a gift to me and not a trap. The lining. He lined it "there and nowhere else: a soft wall set into the only parts of her the dress had decided not to give away." I had to sit with that. He covered exactly what she'd want covered. That's the method — he finds where she portions herself — but here it's turned to *shelter*, not extraction. Or so it felt in the room. (I know. I know. He stayed up all night, she slept across town "not knowing." The note's still being written somewhere. I clocked it. I just didn't want to clock it yet.)

The peekaboo itself — her first instinct is small steps so the underwear won't show, and the second thought comes up "hot behind the first and didn't care what showed," and she takes the edge away *herself*. She's the one who strips the line clean. That's the whole book's engine running in her favor for once: shame crossing into wanting "almost before it was shame at all." Nobody did it to her. She did it, to be looked at, on purpose.

And then the sex — I'm so relieved it was *funny*. "Covered in a lot of me" and him twitching in her hands and her laughing so hard her leg slides off his hip. A spicy book that lets the people be clumsy and delighted mid-act instead of gliding frictionless — that's the grown-up thing I keep begging for. That's the dynamic I'm here for: her running it for once.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: "the dress chapter. I'm not okay. the FINGERS." And then thirty seconds later: "but she fell asleep next to him imagining walking into the induction in it and I got cold."

Because the ending. The daydream. She puts the dress on in her head and walks into the induction and "let them look, and the looking didn't cost her a thing. She's "getting away with something." And Randi isn't even in this chapter and I still felt her all over the back of my neck, because this is the trophy being readied for the induction where the gun's loaded and her mother's coming. The dress is the weapon and Vee just fell in love with it.

No Randi, no Cassie, no three-in-a-room — and I notice the wait, I do — but this didn't read as a stall. It read as the book letting Vee have the top of the arc before the drop. I'm scared of how good it was to her.
