Okay, so this is the quiet chapter — and it's the second quiet one in a row if I count the pool chapter and the dinner afterglow, but it doesn't read as a stall, because the *dramatic irony* is doing the heavy lifting and the book knows exactly how much I know that Vee doesn't. That's the whole engine here and it's working on me. I'm reading Vee gush to Cassie about kissing a man she'd known two days, *because of him, on purpose, me, look at me* — and I know about the bench. I know Randi cried facedown on it and went back behind her face and decided *not ever*. I know "her" was a point Pace made on a quad. So Vee spinning in her tiny dorm room nearly taking out the closet door is just — god. It's unbearable in the good way. The gladness is real and I can see the drop coming and she can't.

What I actually love is how the book is building Vee's exact vulnerability as the mechanism of her own capture. She let him choose the food. She lost the thread and nothing bad happened. She stopped watching herself eat. Every single thing that felt like liberation on the dinner date is, from where I'm sitting, the lever. "May I choose" — she's told the whole story to Randi now, handed it over, *wants* to hand it over, and Randi is sitting there going "you let him choose for you. And you liked it." holding it up to the light. Randi's doing the exact reading-and-filing I watched her do on the quad. The hand on the table — "Vee's my dear friend" — the cool damp fingers held in plain view that "cost something." It costs something because it's the con deepening, and Vee reads it as being chosen out loud. Both things are true at once and that's the sick, excellent trick of this book.

Cassie is the thing I'm clutching. "That's not a boy, that's a man" — and then "how much did he give you to drink," the dry floor under the gladness. And the hiking dossier: "off six-fourteen, that's the whole address," "is he rich, rich people hike, poor people walk." Cassie is the only person triangulating this man and she's doing it as a joke and nobody's listening.
