REACTION

Oh, they switched the camera. Seventy-four chapters inside Vee's skin and now I'm behind Pace's eyes for the whole thing — and I didn't resent it, I leaned in, because I've been *starving* to know what he's actually doing when she's not in the room. And the answer is: he's exactly what I accused him of being, and worse, and also — damn it — a little more than that.

So Randi drove Vee home drunk, tucked her in, and came straight here to lie on Vee's green sheets, naked, as Vee's substitute. The green he put on *for Vee*, that he stripped the white marriage-bed down for. That staging gutted me before anything even happened — she picked the one color that isn't hers to be ruined on. And the whole "you have an hour" was a lie, there was never a clock, she invented the emergency so he'd race it, so he'd punish her against a deadline that didn't exist. She built a fake knife to her own throat because she wanted to be made to pay for walking into the other girl's place. I *saw* that read in him — "the punishment was the first, the wanting was the second" — and it's the truest thing the book has told me about Randi yet. She came to be made to hurt for the thing she can't have.

And the clamps, the spanking down toward the one place, the whole scene — fine, it's hot, it's precise, the warmth-then-cold thing is genuinely good. But what made me go cold is what he *says*. "Is she better. Tell me she's better than me." And he gives her: "She's perfect. For your first woman." That's a scalpel. He knew exactly what she was asking — not about sex, about whether Vee will ever be *hers* — and he handed her the thing that gets her off and guts her in the same breath. He's not jealous and he's not in love with either of them the simple way; he's the one holding both leashes and he *likes* the weight of two women in his bed and one name in the room. "Vee's name still in the room with them both." He came early, undone, buried on the ruined green — so he's not above it either. Good. I wanted him not above it.

But then — the sobbing. Randi folding down boneless, crying for real, not performed, and him understanding it "wasn't really about anything he'd done to her body." That's the architect with the blueprints on fire, the Love bracelet welded shut, breaking from the inside exactly like I've been waiting for. She got the most careful man she knows to spank her raw and it still didn't touch the actual wound, which is that Vee is never going to be hers and she drove the proof home with her own two hands. I felt that. I almost forgave her the whole stunt for that one paragraph.

And then the Sunday morning. God. Vee on the phone, hungover, *grateful*, "I don't know what I'd do without her" — while Randi slides under the sheet and cleans herself off him with her mouth. Not to savor it. To *erase* it. To hand him back washed, put herself under the other girl, make him clean for Vee's use. "He lay in the middle of the two of them and let it happen." That's the three-games thing from the jacket landing with full weight — Vee thanking Randi for the tenderness that was the cover for this, in real time, down a phone line, while the erasing happens. It's the cruelest, most intimate image in the book so far and I had to put the phone down (mine, not his).

What I'm sitting with, at 1 a.m., texting a friend: *he stripped the bed TWICE. Started the chapter erasing Randi for Vee and ended it erasing Randi for Vee, and in between let Randi erase herself. This man does the laundry of his own deceptions.* And nobody in this triangle is the victim and nobody's clean and I can't look away.

The one thing nagging me: this was all his POV and his read of Randi, handed to me pretty complete — "she'd built the whole afternoon to earn it," "the punishment was first." A hair more explained than I want. I liked it better when I had to build Randi myself from the outside. But it's a small complaint against how hard this pulled.
