REACTION

Okay, so this is the chapter where I finally get to be *inside* it with Vee instead of watching Randi manage her want on a couch, and I have to say — the shape of this book is doing something to me that I did not expect when I started Chapter 1 on that bench.

The thing that got me tonight is the fear that isn't there. That's the whole chapter, really: the absence of a thing. Vee walking down the bleachers with her face on — I already had that from the pool chapter — and then at the table, the moment she names it to herself, *that low background readiness she carried everywhere, the part of her always braced for the bill of a thing to come due, and it just wasn't there, and she let it not be there.* That landed in my actual chest. Because I know that woman. I've *been* that woman, bracing at a dinner for the moment it turns, and the book knows exactly what it feels like when it stops. That's the emotional intelligence I've been starving for and DNF'd forty books looking for. It's not decorated. It's just precise.

And the eating. God, the eating. This is a book that has decided food is sex and it's *right*. The soft foreign words she can't read — "each soft sound a closed door with something behind it, and she sat there with her mouth watering at promises she couldn't have read aloud, wanting things she had no names for" — come ON. That's the whole seduction in one image and it's not even about a body yet. The hollow that "kept going down and down into something that had been empty a lot longer than since noon." I underlined that in my head. This is a girl being fed for the first time and the book trusts me to feel how starved she was without spelling out the mother, because it already spelled out the mother two chapters back with Randi's egg whites and thin apple slices. The architecture is *tight*.

The "May I choose" — yes, I heard the callback to Randi's whole training on the bench, the asking-before-taking, and here it is being run on Vee gentle and daylight and she doesn't know it's a technique because she's never been handled by someone who has one. And "May I call you" back in the coffee shop, and now "May I choose," and I — this is where I get uneasy in the *good* way the jacket promised. I'm ahead of her. I know Pace built a bench. I know Randi begged on it and then locked a bathroom door and decided *not ever*. I know these two picked Vee off a lawn like produce. So watching Vee melt over the courtliness, watching her think "that's not a boy, that's a man" — I'm falling for it *with* her and also watching the trap close and the doubleness is genuinely the most interesting thing a romance has done to me in a year.

Now. Craft-honest: this is a soft chapter. It's the froyo energy stretched to dinner length — a lot of interior, a lot of "she surfaced into the mint," and the actual event count is low. A restaurant, a walk, a first kiss under a tree. I'd normally start tapping my foot at the third page of *she stopped doing the things she normally did*, because the book has told me Vee-goes-quiet-and-is-fed maybe four times now across froyo, the wine chapter (from Randi's side), and here. But the kiss saved the shape for me. That "I don't kiss a man I've known a couple of days" — and then *she didn't go*, her feet stayed, the hand pulling taut — and she makes the exception herself, leans in, takes it, and he leaves her the last inch. That's the same choreography as the bench (he backs off, she comes back for it) and now I *see* it as choreography and it's chilling and hot at once. Every yes freely given. That's the problem. The jacket told me and I still felt the pull of the kiss.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: "he is running the exact same playbook on the sweet one that he ran on the mean one and I am ROOTING for it to work while knowing it's a con, what is wrong with me." And also: "the guy has a RULE about which hand touches food and it made me feral, I don't know why."

Slight cool note — I want to get back to Randi. Vee's chapters are lovely but they're the honey; the vinegar is Randi holding a want too big for her face and finishing a wine glass she never tasted. Two Vee chapters in a row on this soft register and I'm ready for the plot to turn. But I'm not tired. I'm baited.
