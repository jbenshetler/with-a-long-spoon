Oh, this one. This is the chapter the whole book has been quietly loading, and it fired without a single piece of clothing coming off, and I sat here with my hand over my mouth.

The one where he says it first or nobody says it, and you learn the fence is there by the shock.

He put *love* in the sentence and attached it to the thing between them, the performance, the "what your body does," and not to *her.* She handed him a meal and he complimented the restaurant.
