Held her off him, an inch at a time, "tell me the real story and I'll give you what you want." I sat up. The interrogation-as-foreplay is genuinely the hottest structural thing this book has done since the doorstep, because for once the heat and the horror are the same gesture and I didn't have to choose.

And it's monstrous. Let me be clear that I clocked it. The gentleman-around-the-puddle line — I wanted to scream at her.

Two people talking, and one of them ringing.
