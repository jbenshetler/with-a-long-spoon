Okay. Okay. She didn't go further than the mat. That's the whole chapter right there — the bag's in the car, the coat's buttoned to the throat, and she stands on the mat level with the chair where her bag goes on the nights she stays, and she does not cross the floor. And he does the thing. God, he does the thing I've been clocking the whole book and this time she names it to his face: "Words are cheap. Believe what I do." And she hands it right back — "Words are cheap, and you still won't spend them on me." That's the best line she's had in the whole book and it's hers, clean, not something Randi planted a week earlier in a practice room. THAT'S what I've been starving for — her reaching that's actually hers. The confrontation she drove over to have. No scene, no dress, no staging. Just a girl at a door refusing the warm.

The "do any of your friends have a relationship you'd rather have than this one" move made me want to throw the book, in the good way, because it's exactly what he does — he answers the question next to the question, he makes the true thing pull against the point, and she SEES it: "that's not what I asked," "you've made the true thing pull against the point." She's not fooled anymore. That's the growth. She can feel the deflection land and name it even while it works on her a little.

And then he folds his arms. He goes cold and holds the line "at cost," and says nothing. The man who cooked with his whole self down in a ball of dough, who cleared a drawer, who sewed a dress — he will fold his arms and let her cry rather than say one word. This one is *holding a line.* He has a reason not to say it and the reason is not shyness. It's the plan. It's Randi across his couch counting to four. He can't say "girlfriend," can't say "I love you," because she was never going to be only his and he knew it before she was anything.

His hand lifting at the end — "the hand in place of the word" — chilled me exactly right. That's the whole relationship in one gesture. He'll settle her body because settling her body is the thing he's good at and the thing that keeps her. The word costs something he won't pay.
