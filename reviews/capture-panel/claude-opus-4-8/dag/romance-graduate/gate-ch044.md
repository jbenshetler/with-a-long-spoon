Oh, this one got me in the throat, and it did it clean, no hand on my shoulder telling me to feel it.

The whole chapter is one sentence and what it does to a man and what the man does back. "I had been eating crackers my whole life, and you fed me a meal." She built that for weeks. She load-bore-tested it — checked that it wasn't *I love you* in any shape he could flinch from, filed it down until it was safe to say out loud, which is the most Vee thing in the world and also the saddest, because look at the labor. She has to engineer a way to tell the man she loves him that he can't rule inadmissible. She's lawyering her own heart around a rule he never stated and she only found by walking into it from the inside. That line — "she did not know the rule was a rule until she had bumped against it from the inside" — yeah. That's forty chapters of training rendered in one clause.

And then he answers. "I love how good we are together." I actually made a sound. Because I clocked it the same half-second she did — and I love that the book let me be a half-second ahead of her and no more, didn't crow about it. He took her *meal* and handed back the *mechanism*. She said: you are the thing. He said: the thing we do is good. She offered him a person and he complimented the machinery. "What your body does — I've never." It isn't *you*, it's what your body does. Same grammar as "You are extraordinary." The instrument talk. He even does the thing where he "assembles something he means rather than producing something easy," and the meaning he assembles is *still* about the sensation, the function, the good-togetherness. He meant it and it was still a dodge. That's the horror I keep signing back up for — his sincerity is the dodge.

The craft I actually loved: the two breaths. "He took a second breath. She had not heard him do that before." He *heard* it. He got the sentence, it landed, he had to take a second breath — and then he still didn't say it back. That's worse than if it went past him. The chapter even floats both options later in the laundry room — maybe he understood and chose, maybe it sailed over — and refuses to tell me, because Vee can't know, and I'm pinned to exactly her level of knowing. Except I'm not, because I know Randi. I know what travels. And a sentence that good, that vulnerable, is going to go somewhere, isn't it. Is he going to hand *this* to Randi too? The meal line? God. I don't want it harvested. It's the one thing she made that was hers.

The laundry room is where it turned from tender to bleak for me. "She put it where she put things she did not know what to do with. The place was not empty." The *pile.* The little flickers all fall, the repair work her knowledge of him keeps doing to stay functional. She's got a drawer in herself now for the things that don't resolve, same as she's got a drawer in his bathroom, and this one goes on top. And the last beat — "The cracker-and-meal would not return to her speech." She's retiring the best sentence she ever wrote because he didn't catch it. She's going to get smaller again. That's the cost the jacket promised and here's a degree of it, quiet, no spanking, no Randi's mouth, just a woman folding laundry deciding never to say her best thing again.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: *he TOOK the second breath. he heard it. and he still said "we're so good together" like she'd complimented the sex. I want to throw the book and I'm picking up the next chapter.*

Is this the second quiet chapter in a row — 43 was the cafeteria debrief, also talk, also no event. Two in a row. Normally by now I'd be watching for the stall. But these two aren't the same quiet — 43 was her failing to find the word for him to Cassie, 44 is her failing to make *him* say the word. They rhyme on purpose. She can't name it down to Cassie, she can't get it named up from Pace, she's the hinge with no word on either side. That's not a stall, that's the vise closing. I'm fine. I'm more than fine. I'm scared of the next brunch, because she's not bringing this to Randi — or is she — and that gap is the whole fuse.
