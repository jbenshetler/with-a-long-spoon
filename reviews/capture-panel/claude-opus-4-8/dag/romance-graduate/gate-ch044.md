Ragged, said for the first time, not worn smooth. That's *exactly* what Cassie's been doing for Vee the whole book, three feet away in the dark. Now Vee's doing it for Randi. The witness position is contagious in this book and everybody's protecting the person they love from the thing that'd cost them the friendship. Nobody says the true thing. That's the whole weather system.

What I'm sitting with: Vee now holds a secret about Randi, and it's the first time the power's tipped that direction even an inch — except it hasn't really, because the secret Vee's holding is the *decoy* secret. She's protecting Randi from the small shame to keep from ever learning the large one. The architecture is exquisite and I feel slightly sick and I want the next chapter.

No heat in this one at all, and I notice I don't mind, which surprises me — because the thing underneath finally moved.
