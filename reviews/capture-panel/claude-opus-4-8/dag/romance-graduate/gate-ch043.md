REACTION

Oh. *Oh.* There it is. The whole thing I've been circling for forty-some chapters, finally on the page — and it landed harder than I let myself hope, because I was braced for a reveal and instead I got a *wound.*

First the two warm-up chapters did exactly what I needed them to. Forty-one was Pace cooking and the photo, and I'll be honest, I almost checked out — I've had this dinner three times now, the lemon rain and the pan's "talk," and I kept thinking *I know whose doing that is, you don't have to tell me.* But then the picture. "I'd never share your photograph" — I clocked that lie in my notes already as *share with whom, Pace*, and here in forty-two Vee literally hands it across a brunch table to Randi. He said never and it's already circulating. Good. The book knows what it's doing with that line even if Vee doesn't.

And forty-two gutted me a little, because it's the first brunch with no *scene* to perform — just Vee happy, formless happy, "my mouth had been asleep since I was born," and I watched the harvest happen without a single prop. Randi going bright and unfixed, the catch in her breath, "it doesn't" — that's not the architect anymore, that's a woman taking a bullet across a table and calling it a jewel Vee set down. The cropped photo, then "I gave him all of it" — Vee reaching past her own nerve again to not be the girl who flinched in front of Randi. Of course she gave all of it. She gives Randi everything, she just thinks it's her idea.

But forty-three. *Forty-three.* This is the chapter I've been owed and didn't know would come like this. We're finally behind the other door — Pace and Randi, the two of them, after, and the whole con laid bare in the pillow talk. "You taught me. By what you showed me about the other girls… so I pointed at her and watched your face." There it is, flat, no music under it: they chose her together, he read Randi's taste off the earlier doors and aimed. The two sad-boyfriend "tuning fork" warmups, the froyo, the bench — all of it was *casting.* I knew. I've known since the jacket. But hearing him say it in bed while she works herself on him through his boxers made my stomach drop anyway.

And then the real thing, the thing the whole series has been withholding: *Randi is in love with Vee and it's eating her alive.* "That's the one that got me. Twenty-one years old and she found out this autumn that a person can sleep." The architect, undone by Vee learning she can *sleep.* Every time Pace pinched harder he pulled another confession out of her — the goodbye kiss, "soft, slow, like I've got nowhere else to be all day, and she lets me," the waiting with her eyes shut in the sun, "I have to hold myself back from going for more." She turned to face the back of the couch so he couldn't see her face and *forgot the mirror* — that detail. The book let Pace watch her fall apart in a mirror she forgot about. God.

And the clench coming on the *word.* Her body answering "and then I kiss her mouth" before she decided to. "I'm doing it for *you.* For the threesome. Somebody has to get her used to my mouth. It's work." And Pace, quiet, lethal: "You don't have to do it for me." And she *comes* — "Oh, but I'm doing the work" — comes on the lie, because he took the cover away from her. That's the hottest, cruelest, most psychologically exact thing in the whole book and it's the two of them together, which I didn't expect. The heat and the depth in the same minute, finally, which is the exact thing I wrote I was starving for after two chapters of depth on an empty plate.

"God, I want her." Said down into the wood, after four, with nothing around it. "She heard it leave her. He felt her hear it." And then the retreat — "for us, when she's ready, that's all I meant" — and she needs him to say he wants it too, and he does, and I don't believe his "I am" the way I believe her "I want her." That's the asymmetry that scares me now. Randi's in it for real. Pace is in it for — what? The gauge-checking ("is she happy," "that isn't the same answer," "is she all right") is the one place he looked almost decent, and even that reads like a technician confirming the equipment is sound.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: *the villain is the one with a heart and she's drowning and she comes every time he makes her admit she loves the girl they're farming. I am not okay.* And: *he said he'd never share the photo and she showed it to Randi at brunch TWELVE HOURS later.* And: *"say something nice to me." "You are extraordinary." she held his arm and didn't let go for a long time and took it for the compliment it was. these two monsters are so lonely.*

The "not yet" countdown Sheri named is now in the room. Randi *wants her* out loud, Pace said "I am" to the threesome, and the only person who hasn't been told the game exists is Vee, who's at home in the trees already wanting the drive back. I'm terrified and I have never wanted the next chapter more.
