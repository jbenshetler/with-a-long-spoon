Pace doing "Thunderstruck" with no shape to his arms, caught being a complete dork when nobody's due for an hour — and then 33 turns that same man into furniture. He does the dishes with his back to her and lets her talk to it, and it's domestic and warm and *earned*, and I didn't resent a single quiet beat of it because I've watched them fight to get here. She brought no overnight bag and she brought *the dress.* That detail is the whole book in one gesture — she won't let herself plan on the night but she packs the thing she wants him to see. Nobody coached this. She built it in her own body on the drive over. That's the growth I've been waiting for and it came with heat on it instead of instead of heat.

"The other is for the world. This is just for me." — fine, that's the line, he's good, he always has the line. And it lands because she set it up so he couldn't *not* say it, which is what makes it hers and not his.

What I'm sick about, still, underneath all this: the dress is Randi's shoes. The induction walk she's restaging was choreographed out of what Randi dressed her in, and every tender thing in this room is going to travel back to Randi across Saturday brunch, and Pace already knows the thing Vee doesn't. The induction chapter had that too — she sent Pace the uncropped photo thinking "that part was hers," the secret of what the walk really was. She keeps finding these private rooms inside herself and every one of them has a door she can't see that opens onto Randi.

Where's Randi this whole stretch, by the way? Three chapters — induction, spanking night, Halloween — and Randi's offstage. That quiet is doing something. The harvest table is coming Saturday and I can feel the book saving it.
