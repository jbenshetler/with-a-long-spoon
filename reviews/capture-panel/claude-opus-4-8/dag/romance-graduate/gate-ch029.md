Okay so this is the morning-after-the-disaster chapter and I came into it braced, because 28 was the first time this book let Vee actually swing at something and I wanted to see what the swing cost. Here's what the book did to me. It handed Vee her best instinct — she woke with a plan, she washed the funk off with cold water "like a verdict," she fixed the sad-clown face, she walked across a show-off morning eating an apple with a plan, and I was genuinely rooting for her, which the book knows. *I'm going to fix this. Because watch what Randi actually does here. That's Vee's.
