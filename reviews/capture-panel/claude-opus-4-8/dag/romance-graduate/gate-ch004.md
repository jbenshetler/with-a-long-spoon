REACTION

Oh, this one got under my skin in a completely different way. We're inside Vee now, and that's the move — after two chapters of watching Randi and Pace *build* her like a piece of furniture, here's the girl herself, and she is so happy. That's the horror of it. She's not naive-stupid, she's naive-*starving* — she reads the whole Chi Latte room in one sweep, she clocks every fabric choice and every worked-for body, she's got her mother's voice installed in her throat policing the cardigan. She's got an inner life a mile deep. And all that intelligence is pointed at the wrong question. She's spending it on *did Randi mean it* while I'm sitting here going: honey, it was choreographed on a lawn yesterday while you laughed with your whole throat.

The dramatic irony is the engine and the book knows it. "There you are" — Randi says it to Vee coming in the door of the lecture hall, and it's the *exact* phrase Pace breathed into Randi on the bench. "There you are, Randi." That reuse is deliberate and it made the back of my neck go cold. The predator's tenderness handed down the line. And the transcript-goes-blank thing — Vee keeping the feeling and losing the words — that's not lazy writing skipping the dialogue, that's the mechanism of the seduction shown from inside. You can't defend against what you can't remember was said to you.

And *Cassie.* Cassie is the whole ballgame in this chapter. Four words a day, the flat "Cassie," refusing the bit's open door, and then "Goodbye, Vee." Not bye, not see you later — *goodbye.* The one person in the room who isn't being warmed up, who won't be worked, and she says the word for a long parting over a two-hour coffee. She sees it. She's the reader, planted in the text. I want to text a friend: THE QUIET ROOMMATE KNOWS. And Vee smoothing it over — "It's *coffee*, weirdo" — because smoothing wrongness is the exact reflex that's going to walk her all the way in.

What I don't have: heat. None. This is my second chapter running with nothing carnal on the page — ch 3 at least had Pace kissing her mouth still wet from her on a public quad, that charge. This was a pure con-artist chapter, and I felt the pattern: the queen bestows warmth, the girl spins. I loved it *as dread*. But I bought this book for the both-at-once, and right now the depth is carrying the whole load and the door's been shut on the heat for a while. I trust it's coming back — the bench is loaded, Randi asked "do you think she'll like the bench," I know where this points. I just noticed the wait.
