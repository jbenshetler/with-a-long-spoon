WHO'S WHO

I've finally been inside it: shoes paired on the closet floor, one right pair per thing; the kitchen that actually cooks; furniture he built with joints that have no screws; a wall of books from cracked fantasy paperbacks up to math I'll never read. Licenses a patent — the MRI one, getting a scared kid out of the tube faster, the part he likes. Makes his own clothes (the shirt, no tag) and now a gown. Randi — his. The architect, the first casualty. Blue eyes, black hair, the built face, the white-gold band she never takes off, now a dark-green Mercedes with the self-sealing windows.
