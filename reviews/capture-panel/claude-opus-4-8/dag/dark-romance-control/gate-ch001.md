REACTION

Okay so first two-thirds of this I was doing the thing where I'm skimming for teeth and getting cashmere instead. The apple slices. The face cradle. "You okay?" "Tell me your word." "Tell me anytime." "You're doing so well." He asks, he checks, he backs off and lets her decide, he unbuckles her heels one at a time like a man defusing a bomb he loves. And I felt my hand drifting toward the exit, because a careful man is my least favorite kind of man. I don't want to watch someone do informed-consent artisanal woodworking on a girl. Give me the knife. I kept waiting for the knife.

And then — okay. Then the knife came from a direction I did not have my guard up for. Because the woman on the bench is *Randi*. The one from the jacket who's supposed to be the huntress. The one running the game on Vee. And this whole scene isn't a seduction, it's a *dismantling*. He didn't spank her open, he *saw* her open, and the entire horror of the last third is a woman realizing she has been located behind the face she's used as armor since she was thirteen, and deciding — cold, quiet, "the size of a key turning in a lock" — that she is going back behind it and never coming out again. The water bottle cap under the thumbnail. The lock she's never turned before clicking over. The smile that "settled" and "was lying" and "had been lying for years." THAT'S the thing under the silk. That's actually mean and I sat up.

So here's my problem and my hook, both at once. The man himself is too gentle for my blood. Pace with his safewords and his cashmere blanket he keeps warm for her — if this book is his POV all the way down I'm going to starve, because tenderness is his whole weapon and I need at least one person in the room who could actually hurt someone. BUT. The predator in this scene isn't him. It's her. Randi is the dangerous one, the one with the wound and the deadbolt and the plan, and watching her get caught by the exact thing she does to other people — that's a threat I can feel. The jacket promised "psychologically merciless in its tenderness" and I rolled my eyes at that, but this last section actually was merciless. He is doing something to her that is worse than cruelty because it's kind and she can't survive it.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: "ok the sub on the bench is the VILLAIN and she's spiraling because he was NICE to her, this book is doing something weird and I might be into it, also every 'you okay?' made me want to throw it across the room but stay with me." I'm suspicious it'll be too soft. I'm staying because Randi scares me and I want to see the game she's running on Vee now that I know what's rotting inside her.
