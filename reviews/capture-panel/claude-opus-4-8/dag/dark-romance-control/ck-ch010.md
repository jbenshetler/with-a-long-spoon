WHO'S WHO

Pace — the man. Grad-student mathematician, finishing a PhD, lives out west of town off six-fourteen past where the road goes to gravel. Builds his own furniture, keeps his house too warm, wears soft washed henleys and good boots he's already broken in. Says "may I." Never last name to anyone. This is the one everybody keeps telling me is the spider and who keeps behaving like the gentlest man alive. He built a BENCH — a bondage bench disguised as a massage table — in his bedroom, and lied about it by not correcting the lie. He runs a program: reads you like a problem, feeds the hunger you keep starved, asks permission so the yes is yours, and takes you apart with the taking dressed as giving. He did it to Randi in ch.1. He's running the exact same routine on Vee now. There's a Daphne in his past — a door he didn't respect once, went into her feelings uninvited, cost him more than he knew. He learned. Now he respects the closed door — which is somehow colder.

Randi — the huntress who is also, secretly, the wound. Sorority girl, Standards board, single room, black hair, startling blue eyes, the face she's weaponized since she was thirteen. She's Pace's — girlfriend? accomplice? both. On the bench in ch.1 he found the real voice under her face and she BROKE, begged, cried, and then locked herself in his bathroom and decided, deadbolt-quiet, to go back behind the face forever and never come out. She and Pace point at girls on the quad like shopping. They picked Vee. Now Randi is love-bombing Vee — "dear friend," "love you girl," the hand flat between the shoulder blades, the cold-citrus perfume. She staged the "collision" on the lawn with a decade of dance training.

Vee (Vivienne Thorne) — the mark. 21, econ major, lonely, sharp about numbers and people but blind to the two hunting her. Red hair, freckles, lush build she's ashamed of, covers to the collarbone, her mother's starving voice in her head. Thinks she got lucky — chosen by the queen, chosen by the courtly math boy. Kissed Pace on purpose after two days and is PROUD of it, proud of the exact thing being farmed out of her.

Cassie — Vee's real best friend. Ball cap, plain navy suit, four words a day, no angle. The only person with eyes. Said "Goodbye, Vee" — the whole flat finished word. Doing all my dread for me. Being quietly amputated out of Vee's life.

Brooke — sorority president, warm-voiced, sits while others stop walking. Ran a status-demotion on Pace (engineering→math). Clocked Vee at the smoothie place and handed back nothing but smile.
