Oh, this one got me. This is the chapter I was afraid of and the chapter I wanted, and it turned out to be the trail only he knows, and it didn't do a single thing I braced for.

Here's what I keep turning over: nothing in this chapter is a move. The boot. God, the boot. He kneels in the dirt and takes her bare foot in his hand in the cold air and her whole body clenches with her mother's voice coming up — *what kind of girl lets a man* — and he just finds the hot spot, says "hmm," warms the moleskin with his thumb so it sticks, laces the boot differently, pats the toe: *done.* And then: "Should've done that at the lot. The russet dress.
