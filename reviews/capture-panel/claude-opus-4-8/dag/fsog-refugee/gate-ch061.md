REACTION

So this is the one where she's alone, and I didn't expect the book to spend a whole chapter on her by herself with her own hand — and I'm oddly moved that it did. Because this is the inside of the thing I've been tracking for ten chapters. The salt. The "ugh, gross" she performed for Randi as a party trick and couldn't quite land. Here it is with nobody to perform for, nobody steering, the window shut, and she reaches her own fingers to her own mouth and *can't do it.* One breath's width and she stops and there's nothing in the room telling her why. That's the whole book right there. She can take it off his mouth and clench so hard it whites her out, she can hear Randi say *it was powerful, it turned me on* and feel the pulse answer — but alone, on her own authority, the reach won't close. God, that's honest. That's the counter-voice made into a body. The thing I keep saying I'll leave over — being asked to call her erasure her bravery — the book keeps *not* doing it, and this chapter is the clearest proof yet: it put her completely alone and let her desire be genuinely hers and genuinely unfinished. Nobody's hand on the small of her back here. She wants it and she can't and both are true and the book doesn't resolve it for her.

What I'm suspicious of, still, is Randi. Because Randi's not in the room and Randi is *all over* this room. *It was powerful. It turned me on. He's enthusiastic* — those are Randi's words doing Randi's work while Randi sleeps twenty minutes away. The seed went in Thursday and it's germinating in a dorm bed Friday. That's the machine, running on remote. And yet — the thing Vee reaches toward isn't actually Randi's permission, it's her own. The "greedy / hers to lie in / her mess" — that shame-staging is the one place I felt the book pressing a thumb, settling her hip over the cold wet patch like a penance she's half-enjoying, and it's a hair too arranged for me. But even that she chose. She opened the window herself. She shut it herself. She lay back down in the spot herself.

The smell beat got me. Expecting the stink the boys promised and finding a hothouse instead — hers, unmistakably hers, alive and not sorry. That's the whole shame being dismantled brick by brick by her own nose, and it's tender in a way I didn't see coming. And still the reach won't close. She can believe it's fine and not be able to taste it. That gap is the truest thing in the chapter.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: *she literally cannot lick her own fingers but she'll swallow it off him proud and the book KNOWS that's the whole mystery and won't explain it and I think I love this terrible book.*

The ache underneath, though — it's been Pace-in-memory and Randi-in-memory for two chapters now. I want one of them actually in the room. Preferably him, actually in a scene, so I can see what the reach looks like with the real man on the other end of it and not just his untroubled glad face replayed off a flannel that's lost his smell.
