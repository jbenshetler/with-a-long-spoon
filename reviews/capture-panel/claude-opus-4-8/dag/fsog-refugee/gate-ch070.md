Okay so the whole chapter she's alone — and that's the thing. That's what got me. She's enjoying this. The power of it. The neck's been a crack in HER twice now and here it's a tool again.

The first lie from last night is still sitting in her cold, "under all the rest of it." I haven't forgotten it and neither has she. They count. I'm waiting for the floor to come out from under that.

What I'd text a friend at 1 a.m.: *she turned her mouth away on purpose AND invited her to brunch in the same thirty seconds and that's the whole book right there.*
