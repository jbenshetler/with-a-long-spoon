REACTION

Okay, this one got me right in the throat, and not the way the bench chapter did — this is the other knife the book keeps in the drawer. Because I *know*. I know Randi steered her, I know Pace was pointed at her like a rifle on that quad, I know "off six-fourteen" is a house with a bench in it that turns back into a table. And here's Vee, spinning around her tiny dorm room, nearly taking out the closet door, saying the exact sentence — *nobody did anything to me, I wasn't talked into it, I did it because I wanted to and I did it on purpose* — that the whole book is built to make me sick about. That's the trick, right? Every yes freely given. She's *right*. She did pull him back under that tree. That was hers. And it was also completely, invisibly arranged. Both true. I sat there feeling glad for her and cold for her in the same breath and I hated how well it worked.

And here's the thing — I'm not fully cold, and that scares me a little. Because the froyo, the tea in the little brass cages, the *may I choose*, him not taking the kiss further than she brought it — that's the man I actually came to this genre for. He asks. He leaves her the room. If I didn't have the jacket whispering in my ear I'd be swooning with her. The book knows that. It's making me fall for it exactly as she does and then holding up the receipt.

Cassie. Oh, I love Cassie. Cassie is my heart in this book right now. "How much did he give you to drink?" — that's the friend I want everyone to have, the one whose gladness has a dry floor under it. And she's *asking the right questions* — is he rich, what's his name, where does he live, borrow good ones — she's doing exactly the safety-check a real friend does, and it's landing on nothing, because the danger here isn't the danger she's screening for. "Off six-fourteen. That's the whole address." Cassie's instincts are perfect and pointed at the wrong threat, and that's its own quiet dread. Please, book, do not do something to Cassie. She's the only person in this whole thing looking at Vee and seeing *Vee*.

What I noticed: no Randi in this chapter, and I felt the absence like a held breath. The last time I saw Randi she was on Pace's lap drinking wine and interrogating him about the girl, holding a want too big for her face. So I'm reading Vee's happiness through that. Vee thinks she has a boyfriend and a fabulous new friend. She has two people who chose her together. The bench chapter told me what Randi does behind a locked bathroom door. I keep waiting for the moment these two threads touch and Vee's inside it.
