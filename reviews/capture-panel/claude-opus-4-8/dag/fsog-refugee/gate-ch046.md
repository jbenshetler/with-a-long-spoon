And because the book just handed me its whole thesis in reverse: here is the perfect safe man who does everything well and reaches nothing, and here is Randi telling me — from inside, unprompted — that Pace on his worst night reaches further than this man managed at his best, and that she wishes it weren't true.

The book is telling me, cold and exact, that being asked, being made to say it, being left the work — the thing Vee's currently resenting — is the thing that isn't nothing. This didn't ask me to cheer anything closing. It cracked the trap open toward Randi and made her the collateral too. That's the frame that keeps me here.
