VERDICT
BOOK TWO: yes — immediately, before I'd finished the last page. The whole thing is a loaded gun that hasn't gone off: Vee still doesn't know there was a plan, Randi is in love and cornered by her own con, the unshared photo and the fraud secret are both live wires, and the "with a long spoon" title promises the supping-with-the-devil bill is still to come. I need to know what the invitation actually costs.

MAILING LIST: yes. Someone who can write the bench chapter, the Gstaad chapter, and the laundered-flannel chapter has earned my email. I want to know the second this person publishes anything.

TELL A FRIEND: Okay so you know how I've been complaining for two years that it's always either gorgeous sentences with the bedroom door politely shut, or the fortieth hockey captain who communicates exclusively in one-word growls and a manufactured misunderstanding about a text? This is the thing I said didn't exist. It's genuinely filthy — like, put-the-book-down filthy — AND every single person in it has a whole interior life you could get lost in. It's a seduction told from *inside* three heads at once: the golden-girl operator who started it as a game and is now catastrophically in love with the girl she's conning, the mark who's smarter and sadder than she looks and keeps guarding secret rooms even the reader almost misses, and the "perfect" gentle mathematician who is, when you actually watch him, kind of a monster — the book *knows* he's a monster, that's the point, it never once lets him off the hook even while the prose is halfway in love with him. Fair warning: it's structured as scene / then-she-tells-a-friend-about-the-scene, over and over, and by the middle the "and then Vee described it at brunch" chapters start to drag — I could've lost a third of the debriefs. And it's the first half of one story, so it just *stops*, no resolution, come back next volume, which will annoy you if you hate that. But there's a chapter where a naked woman gets measured for a dress that's hotter than most actual sex I've read, and a chapter where the villain has the most perfect night of her life with the most perfect man and feels absolutely nothing and understands *exactly why*, and I have not stopped thinking about either. Read it. Text me when you get to the bench.
