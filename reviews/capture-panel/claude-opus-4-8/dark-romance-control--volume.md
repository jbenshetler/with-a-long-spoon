TELL A FRIEND: Okay so — this is NOT a normal dark romance and you have to know that going in or it'll drive you insane. There's no capture, no cage, no villain snarling in your face; the man asks permission for EVERYTHING and cooks salmon and sews her a dress, and I almost bailed like four times in the middle because I thought I'd been handed a wholesome slow-burn by mistake. BUT. The trick is chapter one shows you the whole thing is a HUNT — two people picked this girl off a quad like dinner and are running a designed seduction on her, and once you know that, every tender "may I" turns sinister and you're the one supplying the menace. And the real love story isn't even the couple — it's RANDI, the mean-girl handler who's supposed to be grooming Vee for the man and instead falls for her herself and can't say it, and there's a brunch chapter where she gets Vee off ACROSS A TABLE just by talking, and a Gstaad chapter about the loneliest perfect night of her life, that are the best things I've read all month. My complaints: it doubles back constantly — big scene, then a whole chapter of the girl telling her roommate about the big scene you just read — and there's more cooking than a Food Network binge. And it's Volume One so the trap never actually springs, which will either kill you or make you buy Book Two immediately; it made me buy Book Two. Read it for the psychology and the wrongness, not for the whips. If you need teeth in the first chapter you'll get them — chapter one is a woman begging on a bench and then locking a bathroom door to hide that it was real — just know the teeth go UNDER the silk for a long stretch after and you have to trust it.
