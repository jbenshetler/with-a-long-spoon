CAPTURE and NEXT are different questions and they often diverge in both
directions. A quiet chapter can leave you keen for the next one; a hot chapter
can leave you tired of a pattern and happy to stop. Answer each on its own and
do not average them toward each other — where they split is the most useful
thing you can tell us.

changed, write `LEDGER` and then `none`. Nothing after the LEDGER.
