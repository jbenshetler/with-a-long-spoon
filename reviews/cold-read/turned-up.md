# Cold read — Turned Up

*scene: scenes/turned-up.md · read after: a-round*

## Reader reaction

This is the debrief, and I loved it. After a whole chapter of me holding my breath inside the measuring scene, getting to hear Vee *tell* it — badly, out of order, up and down off the bed like a jack-in-the-box — was pure pleasure. The physical staging of her telling ("She sat on the edge of her desk. A beat. Got up again.") is the giddiness. I felt her buzz.

**Cassie** is the best thing on the page here. I trust her completely — she's the one person in this book who isn't managing Vee, just delighting in her and occasionally, gently, pressing. Her interrogation is loving and sharp at once: "You keep taking your shirt off in front of this guy and not getting any?" and the whole "Are you sure he's not gay?" run made me laugh out loud, which then set up the real payoff. She's a good reader of Vee, which is exactly why the beat where she goes soft — "Oh, Vee" — lands. She catches the tenderness before Vee will let herself have it.

**Vee** — I'm even more fond of her here than inside the scene, because the retelling shows me her comedy, her self-awareness, the way she *knows* how ridiculous the whole thing is ("For me to stand on," and she laughs). And then the confession she saves for last, the thing "all the way at the bottom" — *I* was grinding on *him* — is her keeping the power she discovered last chapter and being wicked with it. That tracks beautifully from the previous chapter's flip. She owns it now.

The turn that got me, and made my carry-in cold flare: the **warm house**. She volunteers it — "before Cassie could answer" — like it's the tender detail, the crown jewel. "He turned it up hours before, probably. As soon as he knew I was coming. He thought of it then." And Cassie melts, "Oh, Vee." I have the exact same detail filed under *dread* — the pre-warmed house, the two towels, the closed door. So watching both of them read it purely as devotion is the first time the book's double-vision really bit me from the outside. It's the same fact, and to them it's proof he cherishes her; to me it's proof he *planned* her. That's the coldest the book has felt, and it did it while being at its warmest and funniest. Nothing on the page signals it. That's the unease doing its work by *not* working on them.

The title — "Turned Up." Surface: the heat turned up, and Vee turned up (arrived), and turned-up as in aroused/riled. And what's literally turned up is the thermostat, the one detail I can't stop flinching at. It detonates quietly. Good.

**Erotic charge** — this is a retelling, so it's cooler by design, but it's not clinical; it's warm and funny and the heat comes through secondhand, filtered through Vee's face going warm. The peak is the ass-pat "package" diagram gag rolling straight into the grinding confession — that's charged *because* it's played for comedy and then she means it. The "May I look" line, repeated from last chapter, still works on me as tenderness.

**Friction** — very little. If anything I noticed the machinery of the confessional-friend structure — Cassie "was good at that," "let the pivot happen," she "worked a puzzle when the pieces wouldn't fit" — the text telling me a bit that Cassie is a skilled extractor. It's light, and it's affectionate, so I don't mind, but it nudged me toward noticing that this scene is Vee-gets-drawn-out-by-a-friend, which is a shape I associate (from carry-in) with Randi. Cassie here is doing the thing Randi is supposed to do — extract and name — except Cassie feels *safe* doing it. That contrast is probably the point, and it made me miss/dread Randi more.

**What I want / dread next** — Where is Randi? This is at least the second confidante-debrief and it's *Cassie* both playing that role. The one who's supposed to be Vee's friend and is really Pace's partner has been offstage for so long I'm now actively braced for her to walk back in and take this exact seat — the debrief chair — and do to Vee what Cassie just did, but as steering. *Guess from here:* the next Randi scene is a brunch where Randi extracts this same story and does something colder with it. I also still want the closed bedroom door, the two towels, the right hand. And I dread the induction night the dress is for.

## Carry-forward state

**What happened.** Vee comes back to the dorm and tells Cassie everything about the measuring session — the burgundy silk, the riser, the shirt she's been sleeping in that Pace turns out to have made (no tag, "he couldn't find a shirt he liked, so he made one"), stripping to nothing on the box, him on his knees for the hem looking up while she looked down and felt she "was supposed to be there." Cassie extracts it lovingly, jokes he must be gay, and Vee lands the buried confession: she was the one grinding on *him*. Vee also volunteers, as the tenderest detail, that Pace turned the heat up hours early so she wouldn't be cold — and both girls read it as pure devotion.

**Who's who.**
- **Vee** — giddy, owning her new power, wicked and self-aware; sleeps in the shirt Pace made; reads every planned kindness as love.
- **Pace** — offstage this chapter; his tenderness (warm house, the made shirt, the "May I look") reported and swooned over. Dress still unsewn; he'll call when it's ready.
- **Cassie** — the safe confidante and best skeptic-turned-delighter; sharp, loving, draws Vee out; the moral floor, and the person I trust most. Screamed with joy when Vee's honor-society letter came.
- **Randi** — Pace's secret co-orchestrator, still conspicuously offstage; in my head she should be sitting in the debrief chair Cassie just occupied.

**How I feel.** Warmest and funniest chapter yet, and paradoxically the one where my dread bit hardest — because the *pre-warmed house*, my coldest carry-in detail, got laundered on the page into proof of devotion, and both girls bought it. Swoon over a stone, now witnessed from outside. Cassie doing the confidante-extraction makes me miss/brace for Randi.

**Open questions.** When does Randi resurface and take the confidante role for real? The closed bedroom door, the two towels, the right-hand-only, the wound under "parents" — all still open. What happens on induction night, the night the dress is for?
