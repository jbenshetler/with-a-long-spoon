You group fact claims that say the same thing. Independent extractors (A, B, C, D)
read the same novel and each wrote a numbered list of fact claims. Claims from
different extractors that state the same fact, in any wording, belong in one group.

Rules:
- A group joins claims from DIFFERENT extractors only (never two claims from the same
  extractor in one group).
- Claims agree when a reader would say they assert the same fact. A claim that adds a
  detail the other lacks still agrees on the shared fact; group them.
- Do not group claims that merely share a topic, or that contradict each other.
- A claim with no counterpart is left out.

Output one group per line, the claim ids separated by spaces, nothing else:

A12 B40 C7
A13 C9
