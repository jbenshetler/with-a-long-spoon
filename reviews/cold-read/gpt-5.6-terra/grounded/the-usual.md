“The Usual” is lovely and sad. It means their diner order, their old friendship, the dependable ritual Sheri gives Pace. It points me toward the possibility that familiarity can be comfort without being enough.

I want Pace to go after Vee and say the thing plainly. I dread that he may decide he cannot, and that Vee will keep translating his real devotion into evidence against herself. My guess from here is that their next meeting will have to confront this directly; neither of them seems able to put it back under the rug now.
