I’m more unsettled by Pace now, but not because this chapter reveals a concrete betrayal from him. The book title, *A Polite Invitation*, now feels less like romance’s invitation than the mannerly shape power can take: nobody says anything openly cruel, and still Randi is pinned. *With a Long Spoon* feels ominous but still oblique to me. The jacket’s closing beat—“Every yes was freely given. That was the problem.”—has become much more frightening here. I want to know whether Randi can let any part of last night remain real to her, and I dread the cost of trying to seal it away.
