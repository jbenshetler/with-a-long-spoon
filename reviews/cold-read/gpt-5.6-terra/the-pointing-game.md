This hit me with a strange double current: I was genuinely swept up by Randi’s helpless, delighted response to Vee, and at the same time I felt the floor drop out a little under the sweetness of it. The game is so buoyant on the surface—walking in that beautiful weather, laughing, choosing, Randi’s uncontained grin—that I can feel why she goes with it. Her bare “Yes” when Pace points at Vee is thrilling. It’s the first moment in this chapter that she seems to answer from somewhere underneath all her careful translation of herself.

But Pace’s side made me uneasy in a way the page earns. Not because he wants her to want a woman, nor because he facilitates the meeting; that part has real warmth and he does give her room to choose. It is that he already knows the rules of the game before she does, knows exactly how she will use him to make her desire survivable, and is privately pleased to steer her toward a particular woman. “The last thing she needed before she went was to see in his face that it was hers to take” is tender in one register and chilling in another. Randi thinks she is being invited into a shared flirtation; Pace is arranging a path under her feet.

Pace points, but Randi picks; yet he has built the frame, set the rules, and eventually points directly at Vee. The title gave away only that some sort of choosing game was coming, not the emotional force of who would be chosen.
