Vee feels the heels’ architecture hold and steady her; she estimates they cost more than a semester of books but does not calculate further.
- **Scarf:** Randi lends deep-green silk with a thread of gold, loops it at Vee’s bare throat and leaves chest open; then teaches Vee loop, cross, tuck, and firm pull, saying tension must hold its shape. She calls the effect “dinner in Paris.”
- **Mirror/outcome:** In a long mirror Vee sees herself in bra, long skirt, borrowed shoes, and scarf; she no longer sees a sad clown and lets herself like the light she sees. Randi watches her “like her own good work.”
- **Departure:** Randi sends Vee home with the shoes in a soft drawstring bag and scarf folded on top. Beneath the composite portraits, Randi gives Vee a whole-body hug and a soft lingering mouth kiss with a second press; Vee kisses back without bracing. Randi says, “See you soon, gorgeous.” Vee walks home with a missed class, the rest of the day and night ahead, planning for Pace’s door tomorrow night.
