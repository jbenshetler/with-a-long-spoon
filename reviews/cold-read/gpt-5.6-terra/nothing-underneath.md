The heat works very strongly here because it is never just the mechanics of what she does. The near police stop is almost absurd in the best way: her terror of being “caught” turns what she is doing into exactly the old shame she has been trying to outrun, and then she recovers enough to see the mark on the coat as proof that she means it. Their reunion is hot, but what got me was the physical relief in it: him pulling her warm against himself, the kiss full of missing, then the pancakes and finally, unmistakably, “I missed you so much.” That is the sentence I had been waiting with Vee for him to risk.
