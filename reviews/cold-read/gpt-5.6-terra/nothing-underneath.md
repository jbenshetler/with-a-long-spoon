He makes pancakes from flour, sugar, baking powder, melted butter, cold milk, egg, vanilla, and butter for the skillet. He folds the batter only until lumpy, pours it into a circle, and serves gold steaming pancakes with butter, maple syrup, and hot tea.
- Vee watches Pace cook with her chin in her hand. She tells him, “I feel like I haven’t eaten anything real since the last time you cooked for me.”
- Pace comes around the counter, kneels beside Vee’s chair, kisses her forehead, and says, “I missed you so much,” with a cracking voice. Vee puts her hand in his hair.
- Sunday evening, Vee returns to the dorm wearing her long coat, Pace’s loose sweats rolled at the cuffs, and Randi’s black heels. Cassie is at her desk reading. She sees Vee’s clothing, raises an eyebrow, says “Hmm,” and returns to her book. Vee blushes and does not explain.
