- **Bleacher remarks:** An unnamed man calls, “You won't need floaties with *those*!” Another says, “Legs for *days*.” The bleachers laugh.
- **Vee’s responses:** She long-blinks and purses half her mouth at the first remark; she gives the second an eyeroll at the ceiling. She walks with chin level and shoulders open, trying to look as if she meant to arrive.
- **Cassie’s response:** Quietly says, “Tossers.” Then says, “They will never know the love of a woman.” Vee laughs hard enough to lose the game face.
- **Water positions:** Cassie wades only to the middle of her ribs, plants herself with arms spread on the surface, and says she will stand there “Like a person.” Vee crosses the dark stripe, loses the floor, and swims in the deep end.
- **Vee underwater:** She uses an old synchronized-swimming scull, making slow figure-eights with her palms. Underwater she sees green water, cold from below, light through the surface, and hears only her heartbeat. She stays until her lungs ask and one beat beyond, grieving briefly.
- **Cassie’s splash:** From about ten feet away, Cassie cups her hands, sinks them to the wrists, and drives them forward to send a low sheet of water toward Vee’s head. It barely wets Vee.
- **Splash-fight:** Vee swims back into the shallow, gains her footing, and throws water at Cassie. Cassie takes it in the chest without changing expression. Vee shriek-laughs and splashes Cassie again.
