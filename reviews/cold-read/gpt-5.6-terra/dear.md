“Dear” is a lovely title for this chapter because Randi says the word that changes Vee’s whole afternoon: “Vee’s my dear friend.” It names the sweetness and the weight of being publicly selected. *A Polite Invitation* still promises doors opened with exquisite manners—Pace’s “May I choose?” is almost its purest form—and *With a Long Spoon* remains an ominous signal I cannot yet place from the page. The remembered tagline, “Every yes was freely given. That was the problem,” presses hardest against Vee’s gladness now: she is not being pushed; she is being fed, welcomed, and chosen. I want the hike, and I want to see whether this new place beside Randi can hold. My guess from here is that Cassie’s folded-away look will matter, though I don’t yet know how.
