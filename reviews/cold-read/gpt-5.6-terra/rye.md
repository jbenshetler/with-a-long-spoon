This is tender in a way that made me ache. Vee gets to tell the story of the hike and hears herself as someone who climbed a mountain, ate hungrily, wanted, chose. Cassie lets her have it without prying or correcting the scale of it. “Look at you,” landed especially hard.

I’m still pulled toward Vee’s delight and toward Cassie’s gentleness. Pace’s care continues to feel genuinely careful on the facts Vee has, though I can’t forget what Vee doesn’t know. Cassie’s response earns trust: “Keep the shirt” protects the feeling without turning it into a cross-examination.

“Rye” is, plainly, the sandwich Vee remembers almost reverently—the appetite and effort of the day condensed into one word. It makes the chapter feel warm and bodily. *A Polite Invitation*, *With a Long Spoon*, and “Every yes was freely given. That was the trap.” still make me uneasy because Vee’s yeses feel so unmistakably hers here, and yet they are happening inside knowledge withheld from her. I want the next chapter to let her keep this joy for a little while; my guess from here is that the shirt will become difficult once its history is no longer only hers.
