## Reader reaction

I’m smiling with Vee here. Her joy is so physical—“*Look at me*”—and it lands because she keeps insisting on the crucial thing: nobody persuaded her; she wanted the kiss and chose it. That makes the recollection genuinely hot in a soft, afterglowing way rather than explicit: the peak is her stopping Pace after he has accepted her boundary and walked on.

Cassie is wonderful company for it. Her first dry question—“How much did he give you to drink?”—briefly startled me, but Vee’s firm answer and Cassie’s believing “Okay” make it feel like care, not accusation. Her state-trooper joke is funny, though the repeated “Off six-fourteen” leaves a little cold thread under the warmth. Vee thinks knowing a road and that he will bring water is “plenty”; I feel tender toward her, and uneasy on her behalf, because I know much more than she does about Pace’s connection to Randi.

The title initially sounds like Vee’s happy, inadequate answer to where Pace lives; by the end it has a faintly ominous emptiness—an address that is not quite an address. *A Polite Invitation*, *With a Long Spoon*, and “Every yes was freely given. That was the trap.” press harder now precisely because Vee’s yes feels so plainly hers. I want the hike next, and I dread what she will discover there. That’s a guess from here, not knowledge.
