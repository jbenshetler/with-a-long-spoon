## Reader reaction

So this is what I've been waiting for — the inside of Pace's head, or close to it. And it does something to me. It's the first time I've been let past the surface of him, and the surface holds: he's exactly who the bartender said. He goes quiet on the climbs, he watches Sheri drive up the grade and "marvelled at it the way he always did," he's not the kind to remark on a woman's body out loud. The tenderness reads real from in here, not performed for Vee's benefit. That's almost worse for my dread, because it means the warmth isn't a mask he takes off when she's not looking.

**Sheri** is new and I liked her immediately — the fast little climber on the child-sized frame, the bleached ponytail, the two years of remarking on women "the way other people remarked on the weather." She's the friend who'll say the thing. "Hell of a backside on her, though. I'll give her that." She's a relief, honestly — someone who talks straight, who could "turn it on a person inside a breath and keep it burning for years." I trust her the way I trust Cassie, as a person with a spine. Whether Pace deserves that trust is the open question.

The gut-punch is quiet and it's the confirmation I flagged as an inference for chapters now. **"What happened to the pale sorority chick?" "That's not finished."** And then: **"Two of them at once."** — Sheri running the arithmetic. There it is, from the outside of Pace but from someone who knows him. He's got the sorority girl (Randi — "the pale sorority chick," which lands) going and Vee coming on, and it's *deliberate*, and someone who loves him is telling him, gently, that it's going to get complicated. He doesn't answer. That silence is the loudest thing in the chapter. My inference just got a lot of weight under it — though I notice the text still hasn't shown me Pace *and Randi as co-plotters*; Sheri frames it as two women he's involved with, not a scheme they're running together. So the conspiracy is confirmed as "he's working two women at once" but not yet as "she's in on it." That gap stayed open.

What moved me most: **"Listen to you."** He talked about Vee too long — the walnut chair joint, the way she pulled a math book off the shelf and put it back "like it had burned her" — "He heard himself going and didn't stop." Sheri catches him out. He doesn't know "what there was to listen to." That's the tell I didn't expect: he might actually be falling for Vee, past the plan. That complicates my read of him — the calculation and the real thing running at once, and him not able to see the second one in himself. It makes me sadder and it makes the dread sharper, because a man half in love who won't admit it is more dangerous to Vee than a cold one, not less.

**Erotic charge:** almost none, and rightly — this is a cold-morning talking chapter. The one flick is Sheri's line about the backside and, more subtly, Pace watching "the whole small engine of her standing on the pedals," the ponytail swinging — a fond, physical looking that isn't sexual so much as it is worship of competence. It didn't work on me erotically and wasn't trying to.

**Friction:** very little. The prose is spare and I bought all of it. If anything, "she could turn it on a person inside a breath and keep it burning for years; he'd seen her do it, and seen what it was worth on the other side, which was everything" made me stop and reread — it's doing a lot of gesturing at some history between them I don't have, and "which was everything" is a touch overloaded. But it earned the vagueness; I'm curious, not annoyed.

**What I want next:** I want to know who Sheri *is* to him — two years riding, blunt, trusted, no charge between them that I can feel. A sister? An ex who became something better? She's the one person he tells the truth to, apparently, and I want more of that channel. And I dread the arithmetic she named. "Two of them at once… if that's not already complicated, it will be." *Guess from here:* the staged apology (still pending from last chapter) goes off exactly as Randi scripted, and this ride is the last easy morning before the complication Sheri named starts costing him.
