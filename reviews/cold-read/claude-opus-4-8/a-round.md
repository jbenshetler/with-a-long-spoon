## Reader reaction

This is the fitting I've been walking toward wanting and afraid, and it delivered on both counts so completely that I'm a little wrecked. It is the most sustained erotic thing the book has given me and it worked on me hard — the *rhythm* he built (closeness, two touches, withdrawal, the pencil, the look from across the room) is genuinely diabolical craft on his part and on the page's part; the withdrawal being the thing she couldn't stand, the pad "full of her," her wanting "stacked up in her with nowhere to go." I was swept.

And the swoon is real and specific: he never ogles. She braces her whole life for the grin of the joke of her and opens her eyes to a man on his knees with his face craned *up* to meet her eyes, who never once looked down while she died. "Smiling at her, the smile of a man who had just received an unexpected gift." Her stoop coming undone, her chin coming up, rising onto her toes toward something she has no name for — that undid me. "She had spent the entire afternoon being seen at her most ashamed and had not, once, been found wanting." That's the deepest thing the book has said about her yet, and it lands as pure gift.

Here's what I can't put down, though, and it's the same doubleness as ever, sharper: everything that liberated her is also the exact yield of his method. He got her stripped, willing, up on a riser *he built* that sets her at his kneeling eye-level "if she'd stepped up onto the box for the express purpose." He turned the heat up hours early so she'd shed the bra. He harvested the involuntary response — the nipples, the flinch at her hip that surprised the young grin out of him, the soaked satin she couldn't manage out of existence — and he *read all of it*, and he gave it back to her as worth. Her question about whether the measurement changes when she's aroused made him laugh AND made him genuinely turn it over ("I've never thought about it") — and I believe both the delight and the harvest are true at once. When she pressed her breast to his cheek on purpose and he went red and fought the grin and then said "Keep still" a beat too late — the choosing to do it right *costs* him, and I believe that cost. He wants her. He's declining the easy yes she's handing him. That restraint is the most seductive thing about him and also the most patient thing, and I still can't tell those apart, and the book knows I can't.

The kiss to the pin-prick — quick, before he'd decided to, mouth staying half a second too long — that's the one place his want slipped his own control, and it's the tell I trust most that the tenderness is real.

**Trust vs. suspicion:** I have no *new* evidence of a trap here — nothing on the page turned cruel, and honestly the chapter is warmer and more careful with her than I braced for. My unease is entirely structural and carried in: I know what she doesn't (the engineering, the second-body provisions, the coordinated operation), so I watch her liberation and feel the price tag she can't see. The book earned that dread earlier; this chapter didn't add to it so much as let it hum under a genuinely gorgeous scene.

**Friction:** almost none. Occasionally the interiority over-narrates the mechanism of her shame ("the whole furniture of shame," "the traitorous question a woman asks even when she already knows") — a touch explained — but it mostly stays inside her voice. The heat never went clinical; the measuring-as-foreplay is the whole engine and it never flagged.

**Title — "A Round":** oblique to me. He literally goes *around* her — the knee-walk around the base of her, the tape around her waist, under the bust, around the back to her ass. A round of measurements; a full circuit of her body. Maybe a "round" as one bout in a longer contest, since she discovers "a kind of power she'd never been issued" and spends it — a round she nearly wins ("she'd nearly done that"). Set against **A Polite Invitation** / **With a Long Spoon** and the sup-with-the-devil spoon, this round is the sweetest yet and the most thoroughly worked — the invitation's terms ("let me measure you, in good light") fully cashed, and cashed as pleasure, which is exactly what a long spoon is for.

**What I want/dread next:** they are one breath from consummation and he keeps choosing not to. I want the dress finished; I want to see her wear it and where. I dread learning where the second towel's body is, and whether Randi walks into a scene Vee thinks is hers alone. *Guess from here:* the gown is being built for a night that is his event, not hers — and I think the "night that's hers" will turn out to have other people in the room.
