## Reader reaction

**Felt read**

The Cassie scene is pure pleasure and I let it be — the pillow fights, "He's a bodybuilder who makes dresses," "Are you sure he's not gay?" and Vee's deadpan anatomical rebuttal ("His *package*. Right here."), then the confession that *she* was the one grinding. It's the easiest I've felt all book. Cassie is still the clean thing: she catches the shirt, works the puzzle, lands "Oh, Vee" soft as setting something down. I trust her completely and I love that Vee tells her the true versions.

Then the Mercedes pulls up and my chest tightened before anything happened, because I know what Vee doesn't. And the second scene is the most quietly upsetting thing I've read here. Randi's softer smile, the car tucking itself around Vee, the green scent — it's seduction, and it's working on Vee, and I can feel the pull of it even while I'm braced. The lunch is a *performance of intimacy wrapped around an extraction*, and I watched Randi do it one soft "yeah" at a time: "which hip?" "all the best ones start at the left hip" — meaningless, landing like benediction, leading Vee down and down toward the thing she's never told anyone. Randi is debriefing her. I know where this goes (to Pace). And the title said it the whole time: "it's just how it's done," Randi's reassurance that made the bared breasts ordinary so Vee would keep talking — *that's* how it's done, the manipulation named in the comfort.

But the chapter did something I didn't expect: it cracked Randi open mid-hunt. The tightness behind the smile, the thumbnail bearing down under the glass rim — that's the ch-1 self-hurt, the bottle cap under the nail, back. When Vee glows "he looked at me and I was fine," something in Randi goes taut and she has to fold it away. And then, at the exact moment Vee's deepest secret is finally coming out — "I—there was—" — *Randi closes the door herself*: "You don't have to tell me." Gentle, kind, and the first time she hasn't taken the thing offered. I read that as Randi not being able to bear hearing it. The instrument turning live in her hand, exactly as I feared. She's jealous — of Pace's looking, of Vee being cherished, maybe of both — and she can't look at it either.

Then the kiss. On the mouth, soft, the small drawing-back and the second press "that had something in it the first one hadn't." That landed on me — a real swoon-with-dread doubled. Randi *wants* Vee, and it got past the face for one second in a fire lane. And Vee explaining it away ("French, that's just how women like her say goodbye") then the cold scan of the dark windows — who saw — ached. She's terrified of being caught at a thing she won't admit she wanted. Her own unexamined pull, surfacing. I've been waiting for it and it arrived crooked and frightened and I believe every inch.

Vee I love and fear for more than ever. The money shame is rendered without a thumb on the scale — the right-side-of-the-menu reading, the debit card drop she'll "feel on Thursday," the scuffed pump tucked under the booth. She's funny and generous and walking deeper into a design, and now the design includes a woman whose warmth is half-weapon, half-real, and I can't tell anymore which half is winning — which is the book working, not failing.

Trust/suspicion: no new suspicion of anyone, because I already knew. What's new is *pity sharpening toward Randi* — the hunter flinching. Friction: almost none. The one spot I felt the author's hand was "a thing spent rather than handed around" — a touch too neat an articulation of the softened smile, Vee doing the reader's analysis. But it's a whisper.

The book title — *How It's Done* — now reads two ways at once: Randi's kindness-cover, and the method of the whole seduction. *With a long spoon · A Polite Invitation* still hangs over it; the spoon is Randi's warmth, the long handle the distance she keeps while she feeds. The chapter title gave nothing away so much as it *recolored* — I didn't feel the knife in "how it's done" until Vee had swallowed the reassurance whole.

What I want next: Pace's debrief. If Randi relays this lunch to him, what does she do with a jealousy she won't name, and with a kiss she gave Vee that wasn't in the plan? *(Guess from here:* Randi is losing the clean line between running Vee and wanting her, and the kiss is the first visible leak.*)*
