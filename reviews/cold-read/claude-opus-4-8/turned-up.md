# Cold read — Turned Up

*scene: scenes/turned-up.md · model: claude-opus-4-8 · read after: a-round*

## Reader reaction

After a whole hour inside that fitting, this chapter is the exhale — and it's a lovely one. Cassie is *back*, finally, after all those chapters off-page, and I hadn't realized how much I missed her until she put the highlighter down and said "Well?" The whole thing is Vee coming home vibrating with it and Cassie catching her, working the puzzle, cracking the jokes, and — this is the thing — being the one clear-eyed person in Vee's orbit doing exactly what I've been dreading she'd do: circling the truth and landing one inch off it.

Because that's what happens here. "He *sews*." "He's a bodybuilder who makes dresses." "Are you sure he's not gay?" Cassie's puzzle-frown does the small frown, she lines up the finger-count of everything that doesn't add up — the no-tag shirt, the sewing, the tape measure instead of a pass — and her clever brain routes the anomaly to the *one* wrong answer. It's played for laughs and it lands as laughs, genuinely, I laughed at "He doesn't kiss like he's gay" and the deadpan ass-pat "his *package*. Right here." But under the laugh it's the exact mechanism I've been carrying in my dread: the sharpest eye in the room assembles all the right clues and reaches the safe conclusion. She holds every piece and can't see the picture in the noise. Which — God — is his patent. *Finds the picture in the noise.* Cassie can't.

And the reveal that gutted the joke open in the best way: "*I* was grinding on *him*." Vee's been sitting on it the whole conversation, the thing at the bottom, and she surfaces it with her "whole face going warm and delighted and a little wicked." That's the power she found on the riser, still on her, spent now in the telling. She's not ashamed of it. She's *thrilled* by it. She's claiming it — I did that, I leaned, that was me. And I love her for it and I ache because I know the box was sized to his kneeling eye-level and the heat was cranked hours early, and the appetite she's so delighted to own was staged into her. The chapter lets her have the joy fully. I let myself have it too.

The heat here is low and clothed — it's a retelling, not a scene — but two beats caught in my chest. "It was like his eyes asked, *May I look*." And "Like I was supposed to be there." When Cassie asks how it felt and Vee is quiet "long enough that it wasn't about finding the word" — that's the tender center, and it's devastating precisely because *supposed to be there* is exactly what a hunt manufactures. The belonging is the mechanism. But on the page it plays as the sweetest thing that's ever happened to her.

The detail that undid me most, though, wasn't erotic — it was the heat. Vee circling back to it unprompted: "He turned the heat up before I got there. So I wouldn't be cold." "He thought of it then." And Cassie going soft, "Oh, Vee." I *know* what the cranked radiator was for — I logged it, staging to produce the arousal — and watching Vee read it as the tenderest forethought, watching Cassie's face go soft endorsing it, is the whole book in one gesture. Cassie unknowingly endorsing him a third time. Warm, warm, warm, and I'm cold.

**Trust vs. suspicion:** nothing new moved my read of Pace — he's off-page, refracted through Vee's telling. What moved is Cassie: she came *closest yet* to the anomaly and her cleverness is precisely what protects him. That's not her failing, that's the trap's elegance, and it sharpened my dread rather than easing it. I trust Cassie's love completely and I no longer trust her eye to save anyone.

**Friction:** almost none — this is the most purely pleasurable chapter in a while. If I reach: the "Vee. Are you sure he's not gay?" bit is *slightly* convenient as a way to voice-and-dismiss the reader's own "what is he really doing" question, and I noticed the machine of it for half a second. But it's so in-character for Cassie's puzzle-brain, and it earns the grinding reveal, that I forgave it instantly.

**Title — "Turned Up":** oh, it's the radiator. He turned the heat up. And on reread it's her, too — turned up to his door, turned up in every sense, and turning *up* on the box while he knelt below. But the primary charge is the heat he turned up hours early "so I wouldn't be cold," which Vee reads as care and I read as staging. A quiet, cruel little title. It gives nothing away to Vee and everything to me. That's the machine working exactly as the others have.

**The book's titles:** *A Polite Invitation* — "May I look," the door he left her, "He asked" — this chapter is *thick* with the polite-invitation motif, the yes she keeps giving. *With a Long Spoon* — sup with the devil, use a long spoon — Cassie unknowingly passing him the spoon, calling the man who staged the exposure the sweetest thing that ever happened. The tagline I wasn't given one this volume, so nothing to lean on there.

**What I want / dread next:** I want the dress finished and the induction, the set piece it's all been building toward. I dread whoever "he'll call me when it's ready" delivers — because the last logged fact I carry is that *his* restraint is fraying, and now there's a finished dress and a phone call between them and no fitting-frame to hold the line. *Guess from here:* the next Pace-and-Vee scene is where the deferral breaks, and I think it breaks from his side. And Randi is *still* gone — six chapters now — the closer nowhere while the mark falls all the way in. That absence is starting to feel like a held breath.
