### Impression

Vee I love and fear for in equal measure — she's warm, funny, brave in exactly the way that will cost her most, and every yes is genuinely hers, which is what makes the trap sickening. My tenderness for her is total; my alarm is total too, because she's the only principal who doesn't know the shape of the room she's standing in. Pace reads as sincere *and* damning at once: his care is real, his patience is real, and he is still a man who selected her like a problem to solve and cannot give her the one plain word — I trust his feeling and distrust his position. Randi is the one I can't settle on — the architect who genuinely broke open over the girl she was assigned to ruin; I feel her loneliness and her love and still watch her hands. Cassie and Sheri are the two I trust flatly; they're the book's clear eyes. My unease sits almost entirely on the secret — the Pace/Randi arrangement, the game, the debriefs — and it's a deep, waiting dread, because everything tender in the book is built directly on top of it.
