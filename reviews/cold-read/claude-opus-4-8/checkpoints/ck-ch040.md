### Impression
I'm charmed and increasingly uneasy in equal measure — which is exactly the trap. **Vee:** total tenderness; I'm rooting for her waking-up even as I can see the floor is rigged. **Pace:** magnetic, courtly, genuinely attentive — and I don't trust him, because the reader was shown the machinery in chapter 3 before we were allowed to fall for the man; the care reads as real *and* as strategy at once, which is the most disquieting thing about him. The Daphne history (19/15) sits like a stone: he narrates his own grooming as luck, and now runs a courtship on a 21-year-old he and his partner selected. **Randi:** the sharpest ache — a co-conspirator who has genuinely fallen for her mark, so I feel for her and fear her simultaneously; her mouth-kiss ritual and Vee's leaning-in are a slow fuse. **Cassie:** the one clean instrument in the room; I trust her completely and want her closer to the truth. Trust sits low with Pace and Randi (I have concrete reason to doubt them — the plan), high with Cassie, and the dramatic irony is doing the jacket's promised work: I'm falling for it roughly the way Vee is, and noticing that I am.
