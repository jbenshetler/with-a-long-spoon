### Impression
Pace unsettles me precisely because he's so easy to trust on the page — the patience, the "May I," the pencil handed back, the door he won't open. With Vee his care reads real; but Chapter 1 and Chapter 7 show the same tenderness running inside a cold machine he and Randi built in bed, and I can't forget that the froyo "chance" was staged. So: drawn to him, and wary in equal measure.

Randi is the one I most distrust and, oddly, most ache for. The warmth she pours on Vee is a tool, and I've watched her wield it. But I've also seen her break on the bench and flee herself, and I believe the jacket that Vee is the first woman she's let herself want — which means the instrument may be turning live in her hands. Suspicion high; pity underneath.

Vee I feel tenderly and anxiously for — hungry, generous, self-erasing, happier than she's been in months and walking straight into a design she can't see. No reason to doubt *her* at all; every reason to fear for her.

Cassie is the one clean thing: loyal, unfooled, already being edged out. I trust her completely and I don't like how quietly she's going.
