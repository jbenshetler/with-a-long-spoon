### Impression
**Vee:** I'm wholly on her side — tender toward her, and increasingly protective. Her waking hunger and her small triumphs (the froyo, the kiss she chose) are lovely, and that's exactly what makes me uneasy: I can see the room around her that she can't. **Pace:** genuinely magnetic — the restraint, the "may I," feeding her, the returned pencil all read as real care, and I feel the pull. But I know he pointed and Randi hunted, I know about the bench, I know about Daphne, so my warmth toward him sits over a low, steady wariness. **Randi:** the most divided feeling — real sympathy for the wounded, unseen woman on the bench and the thumbnail she doesn't know she's pressing, alongside clear alarm at how deliberately she's steering Vee. **Cassie:** unqualified trust and warmth; she's the one person here with no hidden angle, and I want her kept close. Overall: no reason to doubt Cassie; strong reason to watch Pace and Randi — a seduction that is tender and true on its face and engineered underneath.
