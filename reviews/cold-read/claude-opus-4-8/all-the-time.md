# Cold read — All the Time

## Reader reaction

The brunch. The reporting ritual again — but this time the harvest is total, and I watched the closer get caught in her own trap.

Structurally this is the "Vee pours everything out to Randi" scene I've seen versions of before, and I braced for it to feel like a repeat. It didn't, because two things move that I've been waiting on for chapters. First: **the photograph traveled.** Not siphoned through the con in the dark — Vee handed it over herself, freely, both versions, out of pride and the terror of being "that girl who flinched." Randi thumbs back and clocks the dates ("these are two *weeks* old… You did *nothing* worth a picture last week?"), and that small needle is enough — Vee shows the cropped one, then, stung that Randi might file her as a girl who lost her nerve, pulls up "the real one, the whole uncropped length of her" and puts it in Randi's hand: "I gave him *all* of it." The nude I was dreading is now known, in full, to the one person running her. And the awful part is how *good* it feels to Vee — "the one friend she trusted with all of it… There was no one else, anywhere, she could have shown." The con didn't even have to extract it. She gift-wrapped it.

Second, and this is the one that actually made me sit up: **Randi cracked.** "Like he wants *me*. With the body attached. Not — the other way around." And the story lands on Randi in a way I've never watched before — "her eyes had gone bright and a little unfixed," a head-shake "as if to clear it," "a catch in her breath she didn't quite smooth over." Then: "*It doesn't,*" she said. Low, and not quite even. Vee reads it as pride, that a Saturday of hers could reach a woman like Randi that far in. I read it as the double seam splitting open — Randi wanting Vee, and maybe aching at what Pace gives Vee, both at once, breaking through the "face" against her will. The closer is being got by her mark. That's the most alive Randi has felt to me — precisely because she lost the beat.

And Vee's pull toward Randi surfaced for the *first* time on her own side. The goodbye kiss — "the part Vee had come, without ever quite admitting it, to wait on all week" — and this time *Vee* is the one who won't let it end, "leaning up the last half-inch after Randi as she started to draw back, taking a second of it for herself." She does it before she thinks, notices, decides it's what she wanted, and lets it be. Randi draws back "a beat past what a goodbye needed," looks right into her eyes, then: "See you soon, gorgeous." The jealousy-flickers Vee kept extinguishing toward Sheri have quietly become something aimed the other way — toward Randi, a wanting she isn't calling anything yet.

The **swoon** here is real and doubled: the food is nearly orgasmic ("this is the best part. The middle, when it goes everywhere"), and Vee naming the wanting as the thing gone finally *right* — "You're just finally hungry out loud… It's the least flawed thing about you" — genuinely moved me. Randi's absolution is the warmest thing she's said. But I can't unsee that it's an absolution delivered by the person who cultivated the hunger. That's the whole chord again: the kindest words in the book, spoken by the trap.

The **narrowing** got a concrete new turn I flinched at: the Anthro elective, Tuesdays and Thursdays seven to eight-twenty, "Sign up with me?" — and when Vee asks "Is Cassie in?", Cassie's *out*, already did hers. "So it's just us." "*Just us,*" Randi says, "softer than the plan called for." Two more evenings a week folded into Randi's column, and the one unengineered witness scheduled cleanly out. That "just us" chills me exactly because Vee takes it "for the ordinary pleasure of a plan made."

**Trust vs. suspicion:** no new suspicion needed — the text lays the harvest bare. "Randi listened the way she listened, which was total"; "I'd confess anything, honest, just to have him hear me" (of Pace, but the same faucet runs at this table); "the one friend she trusted with all of it." The irony writes itself. What's *new* and destabilizing is that the runner isn't fully in control — Randi's want for Vee is leaking. I don't know yet whether that cracks the con or deepens it.

**Erotic charge:** working, and warmest at the kiss — Vee leaning in is the first two-sided beat between them and it's charged. The reported sex ("he takes *forever*… I've got the sheets in both hands before he's even really started") and the yolk running gold both simmer. Clothed the whole time, but it hums.

**Friction:** almost none. It's another report scene, and if I'm cold about it, the shape is familiar. But it earns its length by moving both the photo and the Randi-seam, so I didn't feel the drag.

**Title — "All the Time":** on the surface it's the sex — "it doesn't *stop*… the more we do it the more I *want* it," the appetite that won't quench. On the reread it's her *time*: the gray weeks, the saturated Fri-Sun with Pace, now Tues/Thurs and Saturdays with Randi — all her time, portioned out between the two people running her, and Cassie squeezed to the edge. The innocuous face (so much of it) and the charged one (all of her hours claimed) sit right on top of each other. **The book's title, *A Polite Invitation*** — this chapter *is* one: "Sign up with me?", accepted like it costs nothing, and it's Vee who extends it, never seeing the invitation serves the person accepting. **"With a Long Spoon"** got its plainest wink yet: "That was the luck of it. She still couldn't get over being the one Randi picked." She thinks she's supping at the lucky table. I know who brought the spoon.

**What I want / dread:** I want to know what Randi's crack becomes — a lever she pulls back on, or a place the whole thing starts to come apart from the inside. *(Guess from here:* Randi wanting Vee for herself is the fault line that eventually breaks the coordination with Pace.) I dread the photo, now fully seen by the closer. And I ache that Cassie just got scheduled out of the only two evenings that might have let her catch Vee's wrist.

**How I feel — overall**
Still split clean, and the split just got more interesting, because this chapter the *runner* is the one who slipped. On the page it was another warm, funny, tender brunch — Vee named her hunger as rightness for the first time and it moved me, and the little erotic charge of her leaning into that goodbye kiss was lovely. Underneath, the dread deepened by the book's own hand: she showed the closer the whole nude freely, out of pride, and the con didn't even have to reach for it; and Cassie, my one hope, just got scheduled out of the only evenings that mattered. But the thing I can't stop turning over is Randi's face going "bright and a little unfixed," the breath she "didn't quite smooth over" — the trap developing a pulse. I don't know if that saves anyone or damns them further. I ache for Vee handing over everything to the one person mining her, and I'm newly unsure whether the miner knows anymore where the work ends and the wanting begins.
