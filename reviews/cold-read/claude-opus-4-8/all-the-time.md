# Cold read — All the Time

## Reader reaction

This one is almost unbearably tender, and that's exactly why my stomach was in a fist the whole time.

There's no scene to report at this brunch — Vee comes in with "nothing to give, not the way there usually was. There was no scene." No dress, no swat. And that absence is the point: she's stopped bringing Randi episodes and started bringing her the whole *weather* of her life. "It's not a thing that happened... it's just how it *is* now." She's handing over the shape of herself, the ambient totality, and I felt how much more that is to give than a story.

**Vee.** My whole heart, harder than ever, and it aches. The gush about the sex — "We have so much sex, Randi," coming out "plain and a little helpless" — is genuinely lovely and I felt happy *for* her, that a girl who lived clenched and money-scared is ordering the eggs Benedict, "the good one, the one that cost," letting the yolk run, going back for more "before she'd decided anything." The food and the wanting are the same motion now: appetite unclenching. When she asks "is something *wrong* with me? Am I turning into some kind of — sex pervert?" and Randi answers "You're not greedy. You're just finally hungry out loud" — I felt the absolution land, and I also felt the tongs close. Because that's the whole machine: Randi hands her shame back as a gift, and Vee "went warm with something close to pride."

**Randi.** Here is where the chapter did something to me I didn't expect. For thirty-five chapters Randi has been the operator — total attention, never a hair out of place, the un-sipped cup, the story that tips Vee where she wants her. This chapter she *slipped.* When Vee says Pace wants "*me.* With the body attached. Not the other way around," Randi "didn't answer," her "eyes had gone bright and a little unfixed," "a catch in her breath she didn't quite smooth over," and then, "low, and not quite even": "It doesn't." That "It doesn't" is the loudest thing in the book to me right now. It reads as: *no one wants me that way. He wants you like that; no one wants me like that.* For the first time I felt something real and wounded under the polish — jealousy, or grief, or want, aimed God knows where. Vee reads it as pride, that her Saturday could move a woman like Randi. I read it as the operator getting caught by her own instrument. My deep-red distrust of Randi didn't vanish — but it complicated. She might be trapped in this too. The jacket did say *both are falling as hard as she is*, and this is the first place I've *felt* Randi fall rather than been told it.

**The photograph.** Dread with an object, and it arrived exactly where I feared. Vee has "had it ready for days, and didn't ask herself why she still did." The cropped one first — and Randi plays it perfectly: "You don't have to show me," which is the precise lever that makes Vee show it. Then the "*Miss November*" tease, then the needle — "you only gave him *half?*" — engineered so Vee *won't be that girl who flinched* and produces the full nude, "I gave him *all* of it," and puts it *in Randi's hand.* So the photo Pace promised he'd "never share" is now on Randi's palm, over brunch, handed across freely by Vee herself. Nobody broke a promise. Vee delivered it. "Every yes was freely given. That was the trap." I felt that tagline snap shut on the table. And "Showing him your toes, eh?" — Randi knowing to look for the toes — sat oddly intimate, like she already knew the shape of his attention.

**The kiss at the end.** This is the other turn. The goodbye kiss has been "the greeting they do now" — but this time *Vee* is the one who won't let it end, "leaning up the last half-inch after Randi as she started to draw back, taking a second of it for herself." She "did it before she'd thought," then "decided it was what she wanted, and didn't bother about it again." That's Vee's falling for Randi surfacing as *her own* reach, not friendship anymore — she just hasn't let herself name it. And "This was the thing itself," set against Tuesday stats being "public and full of other people" — she's rating the private hunger for Randi above the man she's driving home to. The tug-of-war the chapter opens with ("the man wanting all of her and the friend wanting her piece") is real on both ends now.

**Trust/suspicion.** No new information cracked the game open, but the temperature moved. Randi's "It doesn't" and the thing that "crossed her face and was gone" when Vee says "just us" — those are the text earning unease, not me manufacturing it. She signs up for Anthro "immediately, like it cost nothing," but *something* crossed her face. Whether that's a plan tightening or a woman genuinely wanting more of Vee than she can have, I can't tell — and that ambiguity is doing a lot to me. Cassie being "out" of the shared class ("just us") felt quietly ominous: the conscience routed away.

**Erotic charge.** The sex here is reported, not enacted, so it's warm rather than hot — but the *food* is where the heat actually lives, the yolk breaking and running "thick and gold... down into the sauce," her hand going back before she'd decided. And the real erotic event of the chapter is the photograph passing hand to hand, and that final half-inch of kiss Vee steals. That's a 1 climbing toward 2 in feeling, without a stitch coming off.

**Friction.** Very little thumb on the scale. If anything the "sex brain / I used to be a person with thoughts" line is a touch cute, but it's in Vee's voice and I bought it. Randi's absolution runs a hair close to thesis-statement ("the thing gone finally *right*"), but it's exactly how Randi talks, so it landed as character not authorial nudge.

**Titles.** "**All the Time** " — first it's literal (the sex that "doesn't stop," Tuesday-in-the-library sex brain), then on a second beat it recolors: this isn't an event, it's a *condition* now, continuous, no edges, "no place it began." And it points at the friendship too — the week runs gray, only the weekend's in color, and now Vee wants Randi *all the time*, stealing the extra half-inch. The book title, *With a Long Spoon* — supping with the devil, needing distance to be safe — feels sharper than ever here, because the whole chapter is Vee closing distance on purpose, handing over the last cropped inch of the photo, taking the extra second of the kiss. She keeps *shortening the spoon* and calling it trust. The tagline is just true now.

**What I want / dread next.** I dread that photo's next stop — does it reach Pace's knowledge that Randi's seen it, does the three of them collapse into one room. I want to know what "It doesn't" costs Randi. Guess from here: the Anthro class "just us" is the next tightening — daily proximity to Vee, and Cassie deliberately not in it. And I suspect Vee's reach at the kiss is the first crack of her wanting Randi as she wants Pace — the jacket's third-in-the-bed, arriving from *Vee's* side without her knowing there was ever a plan.
