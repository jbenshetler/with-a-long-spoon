## Reader reaction

This one worked on me as pure warmth for most of its length, and the dread I carry only came up at the very end, on a couple of lines. That's exactly the temperature I've come to expect.

**Vee.** I love her here and I ache for her. The whole confession is her at her most open — "We have so much sex, Randi," said "plain and a little helpless," and then the real thing under it: "Is that — is something wrong with me? Am I turning into some kind of — sex pervert?" That's the shame engine again, but it moved somewhere new for me. It's not shame at a specific indignity this time; it's shame at *the wanting itself*, at appetite as a defect. And when Randi absolves her — "You're just finally hungry out loud… You've been starving your whole life and calling it *fine*" — I felt the pull of it as strongly as Vee did. That's the danger: it's *true*, and it's kind, and it's the exact language a harvester would use to make a woman trust her more. The line about her mouth "asleep since I was born," eating "with the sound off" — Pace woke her palate up; the parallel to being sexually woken is doing a lot, and Vee feels remade at both ends.

The detail that snagged me most tenderly: "Like he wants *me.* With the body attached. Not — the other way around." That's the whole thing Vee's been starved for, said cleanest here. And it's the line that lands on Randi.

**Randi.** Here's where the cold came up through the floor for me, and the text earned it, quietly. Twice she goes still in a way that isn't performance. First: "Randi's eyes had gone bright and a little unfixed… a catch in her breath she didn't quite smooth over… 'It doesn't,' Randi said. Low, and not quite even." Vee reads it as *her story landed that hard* — pride. I read it as something escaping Randi. The word "It doesn't" is unfinished and I can't fully close it — *it doesn't [reach that far], it doesn't [happen to me], he doesn't [look at me that way]* — but whatever it is, it's involuntary, and it's the first crack I've seen in her beam. Then the reflex recovery: "the beam came up full again… the grin arriving like a room light." That word *room light* — a thing switched on — told me the warmth is a fixture she operates.

And the photograph. This is the payoff I've been dreading since it lived on Vee's phone. Vee shows Randi the nude. First the cropped one — "*Miss November*" (which chills me, because that's exactly the calendar-pinup frame Pace *composed* her into, and Randi names it instantly, like she knows the genre) — and then, goaded by "you only gave him *half?*", Vee hands over the *uncropped* full-length one, the real one, into Randi's actual hand. "Randi took it… kept looking — past a glance, past what a verdict needed." That "past what a verdict needed" is the tell that isn't a tell — Vee reads a friend admiring her; I read Randi *studying* something. Pace promised he'd never share her *photograph* — and here Vee has done the sharing herself, to the one person, and thinks she's the safest possible confidante: "There was no one else, anywhere, she could have shown." The dramatic irony there is almost unbearable and the text never once winks at it. That restraint is why it works.

**The Anthro elective.** Small, but it caught me: Vee engineers more time with Randi, and specifically checks whether Cassie's in — Cassie's *out* (already did hers). "So it's just us." "Just us," Randi said, and something crossed her face and was gone." Vee wanted the moral floor removed and doesn't know it; Randi's flicker suggests she clocked exactly what just got arranged. The stats-class mirror is still humming under this.

**The goodbye kiss.** The staircase moved and *Vee* moved it this time — she's the one who won't let the kiss end, "leaning up the last half-inch after Randi as she started to draw back." That's the escalation crossing over: the track is now self-propelling from Vee's side. "Randi drew off and looked at her a moment, close, something in the look Vee didn't try to read." I don't try to read it either, but I feel it.

**Erotic charge.** Strong, and cleverly displaced — the actual sex is all *reported*, Pace absent, filtered through Vee telling it, so the heat is in the telling and in Randi's total listening ("her eyes going to Vee's mouth when she talked and her hands when they moved"). The Benedict is the erotic set-piece: the yolk breaking and running "thick and gold… down into the sauce," the "low sound got out of her before she could help it," her hand going back "before she'd decided anything." Food-as-sex, appetite-she-can't-govern — same body announcing its wanting without her consent. It peaks for me at the photo hand-off, where arousal and dread fuse. It never goes clinical.

**Friction.** Very little. The one spot I felt the machinery was "Randi produces with words what Pace produces with his body" made almost too literal — Randi *naming* things for Vee to see ("He woke you up," "Not a question. Setting it down where Vee could see it") gets close to showing me the device. But it's inside Vee's warmth, so it mostly held. "warm as a bath" and "warm as a room light" are lovely but the *warm* keyword is getting a workout.

**What I want / dread next.** I dread that photograph now more than anything — it's in Randi's memory, maybe her phone, and she looked too long. *Guess from here:* the uncropped image reaches Pace, or is *already meant to*, and the two tracks are closer to touching than Vee can see. I want to know what "It doesn't" was going to be — the one true involuntary thing Randi said. And I'm braced for the Anthro class / "just us" to be the next room the seduction moves into.
