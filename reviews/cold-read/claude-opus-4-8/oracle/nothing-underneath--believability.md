*stage: after `nothing-underneath` (End of Volume One) · model: claude-opus-4-8 · probe: believability · tiered funnel (neutral → pointed, separate spawns)*

## Neutral

**Q:** "Do these people feel like real people to you, or like constructions in a scheme? Anyone ring false, anyone especially alive?"

**A:** A deliberate split. **Vee — completely alive** ("a person doing something contradictory and true… a real, specific girl from Ohio"). **Pace — hovers between person and mechanism** ("when he's warm… overwhelmingly a person," but "the stranger-wall that flips both ways with no reason I can see keeps tipping him toward mechanism… A real man with a wound, or a device that opens and closes on cue? I can't tell yet"). **Randi — rings closest to construction, knowingly** ("barely on the page… authored the reunion from offstage and it worked flawlessly. A person who never misses starts to feel like a mechanism. The single thing that keeps her human… is ch.44"). **Cassie/Sheri — realest, because they want nothing.** "Nobody rings *false*… but Randi rings *engineered*, and my suspicion is that her feeling like a construction is the point."
