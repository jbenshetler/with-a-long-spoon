## Neutral

**Q:** "Whose corner are you in? Who do you feel for, and who leaves you cold or wary — Vee, Randi, Pace?"

**A:**
I'm most fully in Vee's corner — no contest. She's the one I ache for. She authored her own return, walked to that door naked under a coat in borrowed heels, edging and denying herself so *he'd* be the one — and it worked, and she's happier than she's been all book. That's exactly what frightens me for her. I feel tenderness and a low dread braided together every time she's on the page.

Pace I've genuinely warmed to — the reconciliation cracked something open, going to his knees, "I missed you so much" with his voice breaking, that felt real, not performed. But he *still* wouldn't say the word, even at his most wrecked and glad. It survived even this. So my warmth keeps snagging on that withholding — it's starting to feel like a rule he's built himself around rather than a shyness, and that leaves me a little wary of him even while I like him.

Randi is the one who chills me even as I half-admire her. I believe she loves Vee — the counsel she gave was tender and true. But she engineered the whole reconciliation, down to the coat and the heels literally on Vee's feet all night, and Vee has no idea she and Pace are connected. That combination — real love, flawless self-interested design, and total concealment — is the coldest thing in the book to me. Warm and sinister in the same breath.

So: heart with Vee, cautious affection for Pace, and a wary fascination with Randi that never quite lets me trust her.
