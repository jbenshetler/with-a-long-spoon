## Neutral

**Q:** "Is there anyone here you don't fully trust? Anything that feels off to you?"

**A:**
Oh — yes — Randi. She's the one my skin crawls a little around, even though she's charming on the page. I specifically remember she and Pace are secret lovers who *picked* Vee together and steered her into their bed without telling her, and that Randi engineered the whole naked-under-the-coat plan while letting Vee believe it was her own idea. That's the thing that feels genuinely off: I'm watching a girl feel *powerful* and *self-authored* — "no room in her for wanting anything he hadn't given" — while I know the empowerment was handed to her by the two people running the game. That gap between what Vee feels and what I know is the queasy engine of the book so far. The jacket even flagged it — "every yes was freely given, that was the trap" — and I can feel the trap closing.

Pace I trust less as a safe person than the tenderness wants me to. The word-withholding — "he never said," "words are cheap," the rule that he says it first — reads to me less like a quirk and more like control dressed as devotion. And that backstory: college at 15, the 19-year-old "tutor," which I read as grooming even though *he* calls it luck. So there's this pattern where a much older influence shaped him by taking, and now he's the careful one taking-with-permission. The sealed door at "parents" and the inner-thigh scar he won't discuss — those are locked rooms, and locked rooms make me uneasy.

What feels most *off* is the asymmetry of knowledge. Both Pace and Randi genuinely love Vee — I believe the feeling is real, his voice cracked, that landed — but they're loving her from inside a structure she can't see. The person I trust completely is Cassie, precisely because she just says "Hmm" and goes back to her book. She's my clean witness, and the fact that the book keeps her in reserve makes me feel the ground under Vee is not solid.

*Guess from here:* the moment Vee learns Randi and Pace are connected is the floor giving way, and I'm braced for it.
