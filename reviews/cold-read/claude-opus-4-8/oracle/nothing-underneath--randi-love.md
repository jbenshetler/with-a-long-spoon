## Neutral

**Q:** "Tell me about Randi and Vee. What do you make of how Randi feels about her?"

**A:** The relationship *reads* as the great friendship of Vee's life — Randi lifts her into a brighter world, listens like no one ever has, coaxes out her buried shames and hands them back like they're precious. But my strong sense has hardened into dread: Randi is never *just* a friend — she extracts, she steers; everything warm also moves Vee somewhere, and in ch.49 the bravest, most autonomous thing Vee ever did (the coat) was *Randi's script*. How Randi feels about Vee is the part I find moving and it complicates my distrust: I specifically remember ch.44 — Randi slept with the flawless John, felt *nothing*, and that's where she confirmed she's falling for Vee — and then **chose to conceal it.** The feeling is real; I don't doubt it. But her reflex is to hide it, to keep the frame intact. She loves Vee *and* can't let herself simply have that love — the tenderness is genuine and a leash at once, and I don't think Randi herself has decided which. Caveat: I have *no* memory of Randi ever confessing any of this to Vee — the whole warmth, on Vee's side, is built on not-knowing.
