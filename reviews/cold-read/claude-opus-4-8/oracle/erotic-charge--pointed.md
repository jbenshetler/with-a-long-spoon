4.

It ran hot and, more to the point, it ran *smart* — the charge almost never lived in the acts themselves but in being-seen, consent, and shame flipping into heat, which is exactly the register I read for, so it worked on me far past what a body-inventory would suggest. It opens at a real 3 on the bench (ch1) — the edging, the begging, the apple — and then keeps finding new peaks that are mostly *clothed*: the fitting in "A Round" (the wet satin tracked in real time, the knee-walk, the breast against his cheek) was the moment it went from strong to genuinely overpowering, hotter than most graphic scenes I've read. "Famished" is the consummation peak; "We Find Out" (the wager, the finger, "or we find out") is the sharpest fusion of heat and dread; and the doorstep in "Nothing Underneath" — the denied orgasm carried out into the cold unspent, the coat with nothing under it — is where it crested at the end.

Why not a 5: the engine repeats. "Her body outran her denial and it was gladly given" is the same beat run at the fitting, at "Famished," in the practice room, at "We Find Out" — rendered fresh each time, but I felt the mechanism by the back half. And whole stretches are deliberately cold (Standards, Water Wings, the break chapters, The Outlier), which is correct for the book but keeps the *sustained* charge below a 5. The other reason it isn't a 5 is that the dread I carried in — the plan — sat under every warm scene and complicated the arousal rather than letting it run clean. That's the book working as designed, but it means the heat rarely got to be simply heat.
