## Reader reaction

This one worked on me and then quietly gutted me, which I think is exactly the trap. It's another brunch — I know this format now — and knowing it made me read leaning slightly forward, waiting for the mimosa, the parallel story, the goodbye kiss. All three came. But the machinery didn't make it inert; it made the last three lines land harder.

The erotic charge is the strongest sustained thing here, and it builds the way the text says it builds — "one careful confession at a time." Vee retelling the spanking is genuinely hot because her body keeps *reoffending* on her while she talks: "she had to press her thighs quietly together under the cloth," the print of his hand "arriving again," "the wet plain fact of herself was near." That fusion of the telling being its own indignity — "the laying-down was its own hand on her, worse than telling Cassie anything" — is the shame engine I've come to recognize, and it's fresh here because the *occasion* is new: it's not the act, it's the confessing of the act in daylight to Randi. That's a clean new riser.

And then Randi does her thing. She produces with words what Pace produced with his body — I've had that formulation in my head, and here it's just naked: her "young Robert Redford" story, the abandoned Greek-house bedroom, "dress up, panties to the side, knees wide, and me singing the whole song of self-pleasure." That line put Vee over the top and off her chair, and honestly it did a fair amount of work on me too. The porch scene peaks exactly where the text says it does.

The bathroom is the emotional center and where my dread spiked. Vee gets herself soaked, starts, and *Randi's face arrives behind her eyes* — "as though Vee were the best thing in the room" — and she cannot come. She stops. "Frustrated and cowed in the same breath." This is the second time (the practice room was the first) she's fled to a bathroom and refused herself over one of Randi's stories, and this time she gets *further* and still can't finish because of Randi's imagined gaze. Something is happening to Vee's ability to give herself pleasure that is bound up entirely in being *watched by Randi*, and that frightens me more than anything Pace does, because Pace at least gives her the release. Randi installs the wanting and denies the discharge — the engine "running and nobody left to drive it," to steal her own line.

The suspicion the text *earns*: "Sticky fingers?" That's the moment my stomach dropped, and Vee's dropped with it — "she could not tell whether her friend had just handed her a door or shown her there had never been one." That's the cruelest, most precise version of the double register I've felt yet. The text lets it stay ambiguous, and the ambiguity is the knife. Then the final image — the thread of sweetness on Vee's lip from "a pastry she had never once touched," transferred by the goodbye kiss — is doing something I felt in my body before I could name it: Randi took a bite, set it down, closed the subject, drank her coffee black, didn't eat it. She *put* the sweetness on Vee's mouth. It's tender and it's a claiming and it's faintly predatory all at once, and the book didn't tell me which, which is why it works. That's her not eating the pastry — like Pace's right-hand-only eating, a private rule I can't decode, and I love that the text just leaves it sitting there glazed and untouched.

Where it went slightly slack for me: nowhere badly, but the "vanilla" prod at the top is Randi visibly *steering* — "after your last couple of stories I had him filed under spicy. And this is all very. Vanilla." — and I could feel the extraction happening. Because I already suspect her, I watched her bait Vee into the confession ("Walk me," "How was it settled?") and it read as harvesting. But per the light-on-top principle, a first-time reader without my suspicion would just see a fun friend goading a good story out of a shy girl, and the text stays clean enough that I can't quote a line as an overt tell — it's the *pattern* doing it, not the signal. The one place I felt the thumb even a little was "no curiosity anywhere in it, as if she had said nothing at all" when Vee flees — that "as if she had said nothing at all" is nudging me toward *she knows exactly what she did*. It's a small nudge and I think earned by the "Sticky fingers?" payoff, so I forgive it.

What I want / dread next: I want to know what Randi *does* with this. Vee has now confessed the finger-check, the strip-to-heels, the being-seen — the full shape of what Pace did. Given my (still uninside-confirmed) suspicion that Randi and Pace are coordinated, I dread that this brunch is Randi *reporting Vee's readiness back*. Guess from here: the physical Randi track and the confession track are converging on something, and the fact that Vee now literally cannot come without Randi in her head means Randi has hijacked the channel Pace built. I also keep waiting for Cassie to walk into one of these and see it plainly — she's the only person who'd say "your best friend is doing something to you." And I still want the scar, the family, the right-hand rule. This chapter added a matching enigma — Randi's refusal to eat the pastry — and I noticed the rhyme.
