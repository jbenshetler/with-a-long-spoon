## Reader reaction

This one worked on me, plainly and warmly, and I want to say that before I let the shadow-ledger creep in. It's a Vee-and-Pace weekend chapter — she comes back after five nights she imposed on herself, and the whole first half is her body dismantling the careful story she told about "needing time for school." The undressing sequence made me grin: the jeans "the enemy now," the one-legged hopping with "no dignity anywhere," the bra coming off her "unwinding like something off a spool" because he holds it and won't pull. "You're not helping." / "I'm helping." That's charming, and the heat is real — the come-fast, "published the number she'd gone to such trouble to keep private" line is the best thing in the sex: her throat betraying the tally her mouth kept. The erotic charge peaks in that reluctant lean-back against the office wall, the moment "she had never decided to give it." Then it goes soft-slow and tender for the second, and I felt it as tenderness, not just mechanics.

The shirt is where I actually got a lump. She washed his flannel because it stopped smelling like him, and gives it back not to return it but to be re-scented — "I need you to wear it for me. The whole weekend." And he pulls it on over the shirt he's already wearing, in his own kitchen, because she told him to. That's a lovely, exposed, un-guarded ask from her and a graceful yes from him. It swept me up.

And yet — I read this whole chapter through last chapter's memory, so I can't un-know that the flannel "she treasures was made by Pace," that the house runs "too warm," that the drawer of her things "set down in a clean man's house" is the made place narrowing around her. The line that pricked me: "he hadn't made her pay a cent for it... the plain grace of that had cost her more than a fight would have, because some unreasonable corner of her had wanted him to fight." He never fights, never says he wants the nights — "he only opened the door and waited on the far side of it for her to come back through." That's the pattern under the sweetness: he doesn't have to pull; he holds and lets her come out of it herself, the same physical grammar as the bra. She does the towing, the asking, the choosing — and every choice runs toward him. The five-nights-of-study experiment ended with her lasting five and driving back, and I noticed the chapter frames her own faculty (the school she was protecting) losing to the pull.

The food beat: "she could taste all of it," cotton candy vs. cut strawberries — that's the palate-remade motif, and his "small private smile, as if her face had just told him something he'd wanted to know." He watches her taste. It's warm and it's also the thing I've been told to keep an eye on — the appetite credited to him.

No suspicion the *text* newly earned beyond what I carry; this chapter is almost defiantly un-staged, no music, just two people and dough. That's what makes it land and what makes me ache. I let myself be swept, and I'm keeping the pattern-words ticking under it, quietly, the way she keeps the tally her mouth won't read out.
