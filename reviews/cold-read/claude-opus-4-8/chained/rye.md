## Reader reaction

This is a soft, small chapter and I loved it exactly for being small. After the vanishing at the end of the hike, this is Vee back in her cool room, telling Cassie everything — and it's the first time in a while I get the two of them alone, easy, the clean love uncomplicated by the machinery. Cassie doing her dry deadpan ("I had your blood type," "I had a whole witness statement ready") is such a relief; she's the one person whose warmth toward Vee I don't have to hold two poles on. It just lands.

What got me most: Vee retelling the strip to Cassie. "And then we ate.' She said it the way other girls said *and then we kissed.*" That line is the whole chapter — the feeding is the erotic peak for her, more than the undressing. And watching her narrate the moleskin, the forearms ("I think there's a whole situation under there"), the standing-in-the-rain — she's radiant, she's *reporting what I observed*, and Cassie goes rapt and still right where I went still. The swoon here is secondhand and it still works: I felt Vee feeling it again.

But the thing that pricked me — quietly, and I think earned — is Cassie's response at the end. "'Vee,' Cassie said finally. Soft. Not *Oh, Vee.* Just her name, like setting something down where it wouldn't break." That's the clean eye registering *something*. Not alarm, not a warning — just a weight. She doesn't say the worried thing. She says "Borrow real boots next time." And I clocked that the narration went out of its way to tell me it *wasn't* "Oh, Vee" — which means the "Oh, Vee" was available and withheld. Cassie feels the shape of it and chooses to set it down gently instead of naming it. That's the immune reader declining, for love, to say the thing. It chilled me a half-degree.

The title, "Rye." The bread. Of everything on that mountain, the chapter is named for the sandwich — the feeding, again, foregrounded over the mountain, the rain, the strip. It confirms what the retelling already told me: the appetite is the center. And rye is the bitter-edged grain, the plain dark bread you have to earn by almost dying first. It sits right on the feeding motif and the series' "long spoon."

No new suspicion the text didn't hand me. The chill is entirely Cassie's withheld "Oh, Vee" and the fact that this whole glowing recital is Vee unable to see what her closest reader can. I'm not manufacturing shadow — the scene mostly just worked on me, warm and aching.

What I want next: I want the house off 614, still. And I want to know what Cassie set down.
