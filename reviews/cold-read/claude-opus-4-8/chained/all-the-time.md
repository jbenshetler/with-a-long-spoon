## Reader reaction

**The felt read.**

This is a Vee-and-Randi chapter, no Pace on the page, and it's the most nakedly seductive scene the book has given me — except the seduction runs the wrong direction from where Vee thinks. She came to *tell*, and she pours herself out: the amount of the sex, the slowness of him, "there's not a part of me he doesn't want," the palate waking up, sleeping whole nights in the woods. It's a genuinely swoony monologue about Pace, and it worked on me as one — "like I'd been eating with the sound off" is lovely, and "he wants *me*. With the body attached. Not the other way around" ached. But the whole time she's aiming all this warmth and openness across the table at Randi, and Randi is *drinking* it.

The moment that stopped me the way it stopped Vee: Randi breaking. "Her eyes had gone bright and a little unfixed… a catch in her breath she didn't quite smooth over." "It doesn't," low and not quite even. Vee reads it as her story landing, a point of pride. I read something I can't fully close — either the closer moved by her own success, or real want breaking the curated face, or both at once. The book has taught me to distrust the smooth Randi; this is Randi *not* smooth, and that's the first time the caring-ajar read on her has had real air. I'm holding it genuinely open.

Then the photo — the nude Vee "gave him to keep" last chapter, the thing she couldn't retrieve. She shows it here. First cropped, then, stung by "you only gave him *half*," the whole uncropped length, put into Randi's *hand*. "Miss November." Randi looks past what a verdict needs. So the private thing is now in the closer's eyes too, and Vee handed it over as intimacy, proud. That tightened my stomach even as the scene stayed warm.

And the kiss — this is the axis moving. Last chapter's ledger said Vee's desire near Randi is produced, collapses, isn't hers. Here *Vee* is the one who won't let the goodbye kiss end, leaning up the last half-inch, "taking a second of it for herself… decided it was what she wanted." Her own appetite, aimed at Randi, claimed. That's new and it's the chapter's quietest big move. Randi holding the look "a beat past what a goodbye needed" before the smile — the mark being set again, differently.

The Anthro signup is the narrowing made literal: Tuesday/Thursday, "it'd be like stats except we'd have picked it," Cassie already out, "just us." Randi threading into the gray weekdays now, not just Saturdays. "Just us," said "softer than the plan called for" — I clocked that.

**Trust/suspicion:** No new fact, but the shape hardened — Vee handing the closer her most private image and her weekday hours, calling it the luck of being picked. The one crack toward Randi's favor is that breaking-breath, which felt real.

**Erotic charge:** high, sustained, and unusual — it's all report, no bodies in the room, yet the transfer of it onto Randi across the booth is the charge. Peaks at "sheets in both hands before he's even really started" and the extended kiss.

**Title:** "All the Time" — the amount, the wanting that won't stop ("the more we do it the more I want it"), and Randi setting her Saturday down all the time. Innocuous; on the reread it's the engineered appetite that never fills.

**How I feel — overall**
Warm and uneasy in almost equal measure, and closer to helpless about it than before. The confession scene is genuinely swoony — Vee finally hungry out loud, the food and the sex and the whole-night sleep all one glow — and I felt it. But every ounce of that openness pours across the table into the one person the book has taught me is working her, and the private photo and the weekday hours both migrated to Randi this chapter. The one thing that complicates my wariness, and complicates it in the good direction, is Randi's break — the face slipping in public for the first time, the catch she couldn't smooth. I can't tell if that's the truest thing Randi's done or the most efficient, and I like that I can't. And Vee leaning up that last half-inch into the kiss, taking it for herself — that's the first time her desire near Randi read as *hers*, claimed, unflinching, and it moved me even as I wondered who built the wanting. I ache for her, I want the friendship to be what she thinks it is, and I'm fairly sure it isn't.
