Title: "The Pointing Game" — it gave me nothing ahead of time and now it means everything: the game where he points and she picks, and also the pointing as the mechanism that lets wanting exist without an owner. *A Polite Invitation* — Vee is about to receive one, engineered to look like chance. The series title *With a Long Spoon* keeps its flavor: something here is being supped with carefully, at a distance, through instruments.

What I want next: Vee's side of that collision. And I dread — mark it as a guess — watching Randi befriend her while calling it a gift for Pace.
