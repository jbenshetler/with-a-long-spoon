## Reader reaction

This one went cold on me — deliberately, and it worked. The lecture hall cold, the institutional cold, and then the moment where Randi goes *still* — "not a motion, the withdrawal of motion" — I felt that in my chest. I have watched Randi be bright, fluent, generous, possibly predatory for chapters, and this is the first time I've seen her *ambushed by reality*. Her father is the outlier point, MIRH, Miranda Holdings — the fraud she's been defending from the inside without knowing it, or (and this is the ache) knowing it and hoping the graph wouldn't say it out loud.

"Cheating bastards" — two words, ragged, "said for the first time," no plural folded down. I read that as her father being named and unnamed in the same breath. And Vee — watching the person, not the page — hears it land wrong and *says nothing forever*. She builds a shelter of busywork around Randi and decides kindness means never making her carry it in front of anyone. That's the most loving, and the most loaded, thing Vee has done for her. Because now the secret flows the other way: for the first time *Vee* holds something over Randi that Randi doesn't know she holds. The whole ledger of asymmetries just got a counterweight, and it sits in Vee's pocket quietly.

Cassie broke my heart a little — her father's layoff set on the table "no handle left out for anyone to pick it up by," and then being handed the proof and not gloating. She never saw the screen. She still doesn't know the easy-to-see point was sitting to her left the whole time.

The title did its work *after*: the professor defines the outlier as the point you may set aside "if you could account for them" — and the last line lands like a verdict: the biggest point, easy to see if you know to look, invisible if you don't. That's Randi's family on the graph, and it's also, I realize, every hidden thing in this book. The outlier is the thing everyone agrees not to look at. Whether it gave anything away — no; it recolored.

No erotic charge at all this chapter, and I didn't miss it; the intimacy was of a different kind — Vee holding a flinch nobody else saw. What I want next: whether Randi ever tells her, and what "cheating bastards" costs her at home. Does this thaw or harden her? And I keep dreading the T/Th Anthro class against the Thursday watch — still nothing on that.
