### Impression
- **Vee:** I feel strong sympathy and tenderness for her. She is not merely being swept along; she keeps choosing, and the choices are transforming shame into appetite and self-worth. I worry about how dependent she is becoming on two people who know more than she does, and about how quickly she is financially and socially rearranging her life around them.

- **Sheri:** I like her bluntness and loyalty to Pace, even though her report to him about Vee is retrospectively uncomfortable. She is abrasive but not false.
- **Overall trust/suspicion:** The emotional center is warm and often consensual in the immediate moment; the unease comes from concealment, not from visible cruelty. No one currently gives me reason to doubt Cassie; everyone gives me reason to doubt how much of Randi’s friendship is uncomplicated; Pace is tender but still compartmentalized.
