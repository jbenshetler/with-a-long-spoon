## Reader reaction

Randi's back, and the whole chapter glows with her — and I came out of it warm and wrung and quietly rearranged. The brunch is the most openly happy scene the book has given Vee: eggs Benedict she lets herself afford, the confession poured out, "sex brain," the absolution Randi hands her — "You're not greedy. You're just finally hungry out loud." That line did something to me. I *felt* the friendship land as real. And then the chapter keeps working underneath the warmth, and I can't decide which register to live in.

The moment that split me open: "Like he wants *me*. With the body attached." And Randi going bright-eyed and unfixed, a catch in her breath — "It doesn't," low and not even. Vee reads it as her story landing on a sophisticated friend, and goes warm with pride. I read it as Randi *wanting her* — and both readings sit on the page at once and neither is wrong. That's the book's trick at its best.

And then Vee hands Randi the nude. The photograph — the one Pace promised never to share — Vee shares it herself, cropped first, then the whole uncropped length, because "only gave him *half*" stung and she wouldn't be the girl who flinched. The photograph now has a second holder, and Vee put it there. Randi's "God, those *legs*" and "Showing him your toes, eh?" — both of them pink and delighted — it's funny and intimate and genuinely sweet, and also: Vee showed her best friend a naked picture her boyfriend took, and I know who that boyfriend is to Randi. The reader's chord under that warmth is very loud for me now.

The goodbye: Vee steals the extra half-inch of the kiss for herself, decides it's what she wants, and "didn't bother about it again." That is the first time Vee's desire for Randi has moved from body-proven to *chosen*, even if she files it under not-ready-to-give-the-morning-up. And Randi holding her eyes a beat past what a goodbye needed. "Just us," softer than the plan called for. I trust none of my suspicions and all of my swoon at once.

The title — "All the Time" — reads as the sex, the attention ("it's on me the whole time I'm there"), the wanting that doesn't stop. It gave nothing away; it fits like skin. *A Polite Invitation* / *With a Long Spoon* — the spoon image keeps echoing for me every time someone is fed, and now Randi is being fed Vee's whole happiness, secondhand, and something in her is bright-eyed about it.

What I want next: the Anthro class, Tuesdays and Thursdays, "just us" — dread and pull in equal parts. Guess from here: the Thursday watch and the Tuesday-Thursday class are about to collide, and Vee doesn't know anything needs colliding.
