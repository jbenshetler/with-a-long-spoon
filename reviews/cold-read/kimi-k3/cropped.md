## Reader reaction

This one is small and quiet and it got me. After the sugar of the shirt chapter, this is the reckoning-in-miniature: Vee back among ordinary life, trying to hold her weekend-world in one hand and her parents' kitchen table in the other, and discovering the two don't fit in the same frame — literally, since the photo "stops at the waist." The title is the whole chapter: *Cropped* is what she did to the picture and what she does to the story, and to herself, in every telling.

What moved me most: "I told them he was my boyfriend. I think I put a question mark on it. Out loud. Like I was asking them." That's the sound of a person discovering the label doesn't hold what's in her hands — and it's the closest the book has come to admitting the love-word problem out loud, to her parents no less. And the sewing revelation lands like a small detonation: Pace *made the dress* — sewed it on his sun porch because she once mentioned one she couldn't afford. That is the most cherishing fact yet, and her instinct is exactly right and heartbreaking: her father would understand sex and never understand the sewing. "Wrong is something he understands and rejects. Weird is something that doesn't fit anywhere in how he sees the world." She is curating which parts of her love are legible, and she knows it, and says "I'm not ashamed of it. The opposite."

Cassie is doing her quiet best work — "So what is he. Not to your dad. To you" and then the diagnostic that cuts: "Is it that you can't say, or that you don't want to?" And Vee's "Both. Probably both." And then that perfect final beat: Cassie doesn't push, "Vee was grateful. She was also, a little, not." Even her gratitude has a countercurrent — some part of her *wants* to be pushed, wants to be made to say it. Same note as wanting Pace to fight for the five nights. The book keeps showing me a woman whose deepest wish is to be asked.

No heat, no suspicion earned fresh — but the cropped photo now has a fourth context: her mother's purse, shown around to people who'd already seen it. The induction image keeps multiplying its holders and audiences. And the "boyfriend?" with a question mark is going to hang there.
