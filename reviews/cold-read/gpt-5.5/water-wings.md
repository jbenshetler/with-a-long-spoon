Underwater details: the room’s noise disappears; Vee feels cold rising from below and light coming down through the surface in slow sheets; she hears her heartbeat close and steady. Her hands remember the slow scull, palms feathering figure-eights to keep her down and turned. She looks up at the surface from underneath. She remembers loving this before she had “a body to be a problem.” The water feels like a part that has never been taken. She stays until her lungs ask, and a beat past that, and grieves a little.

Vee resurfaces; light, noise, chlorine, and bodily publicness return. Cassie watches her without asking. Cassie sinks her cupped hands to the wrists and drives them forward to shoot a low sheet of water at Vee. It barely reaches Vee. Vee swims ten feet from deep to shallow, gets her feet under her, and splashes back. Cassie takes the wave in the chest without changing expression. Vee makes a shriek-laugh she has not made in a while and splashes her again. The splashing overtakes whatever grief Vee brought up. The chapter ends with the sense that “the body that was such a problem” stayed on deck with the rolled towel.
