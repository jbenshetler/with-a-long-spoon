And yet — the chapter title, **“My Friend Randi,”** starts to sting almost immediately. Vee comes to her friend. Vee experiences this as rescue by her friend. But I know Randi is not only her friend. Randi is counseling her from inside a secret.

The most troubling and most charged part is the coat plan. Erotically, yes, it works on me.
