The erotic charge from the last chapter is still there, but now it has curdled into exposure. I felt protective of her immediately, and also frustrated with her in that helpless way where I can see exactly why she is reaching for the face again.

Brooke scares me a little here, not because she does anything overtly cruel, but because she knows how to make warmth function like authority. “The not-standing was the move” is such a clean little social knife. She can stop Randi from across the room while smiling, while typing, while seeming to ask nothing. I do not think Brooke necessarily knows what happened, but she knows there is something to press on, and she presses with perfect sorority-polished fingers: the paper, where Randi worked, Pace’s name, the truck, whether he teaches. Pace is not physically here, and that matters.
