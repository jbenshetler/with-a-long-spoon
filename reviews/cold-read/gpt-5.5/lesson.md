## Reader reaction

This chapter hurt in a more exposed, social way than the private sexual chapters have. The dancing lesson is very attractive: Pace/Peter is exact, physical, patient, and the erotic charge is all in frame, pressure, resistance, and trust. I felt Vee learning something bodily before she could think it, the same way she has learned desire with him. The peak for me is not the club itself but the moment on the concrete pad when “the pattern came through his body before it arrived in her feet.” That is intimate without being sex.

Then the room turns poisonous because Vee sees that his gift is not hers alone. My sympathy stays with her even while I think she is wrong. The blonde’s line is perfectly barbed: “Knows how to get a girl to do just what he wants her to.” It earns Vee’s spiral because it touches every anxiety already in her — specialness, sluttiness, being one of many, having mistaken attention for choosing. But the bartender’s correction also lands: Pace does not bring dates, Pace gets drunk girls home safely and leaves them at the door, and the women were jealous of Vee. That moved my trust in Pace upward again, and my trust in Vee’s perception temporarily down — not because she is foolish, but because shame can reverse the whole room.

Pace’s straight answer, “Yes. Sometimes. When it’s required,” feels adult and wounded. He does not defend himself theatrically; he just leaves. I wanted Vee to stop him and could feel the terrible momentum of not being able to. Her night afterward is lonely and deservedly awful: the cab, the cost, the stale washed shirt, Cassie breathing nearby but not called. I liked that she reaches the truth herself by morning, not because someone rescues her from it.

Randi being the person she decides to go to next is a strong pull forward and also worrying. It makes emotional sense: Randi is the one who can receive shame and turn it into usable language. Vee thinks she is going to her friend to fix a wrong done to Pace; I know she is walking toward someone who is part of the whole hidden arrangement.
