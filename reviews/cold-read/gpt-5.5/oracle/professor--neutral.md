**Question asked (verbatim):** Was there anything that read as a running joke or a wink — aimed a little over the characters' heads, at you? Anything you caught yourself smiling at on a second or third appearance?

---

Yes. The clearest running wink for me was the book’s habit of making ordinary academic or practical language suddenly describe the erotic/emotional situation too: “latent variables,” substitution, Simpson’s paradox, outliers, “a recognized method.” I sometimes noticed the author’s hand there, but mostly I smiled because the recurrence became part of Vee’s weather: class, math, tailoring, dishes, and desire all kept quietly explaining each other.

The food thread also started to feel like a private joke that turned serious: apple slices, froyo, rye, fra diavolo, eggs Benedict, pancakes, crackers versus a meal. By the time Vee says Pace fed her “a meal,” I wasn’t just reading metaphor; I had been trained to grin at every menu as emotional plot.

And Cassie’s “Hrm”/dry little checks became a kind of running pleasure for me. Not a big joke, but every time she punctured the glow with something plain — windowless vans, school nights, toenails, the eyebrow at the coat — I smiled because she was usually the only person in the room not intoxicated by the scene.
