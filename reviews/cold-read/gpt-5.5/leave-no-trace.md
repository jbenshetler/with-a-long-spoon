The trail begins at a gravel lot and goes steeply into fall mountain woods under silver overcast. The air is wet-edged and cold. Vee stands by Pace’s old white pickup with her hand on the truck door before stepping onto the trail.

Vee wears Meg’s borrowed boots, a half-size too big and stiff at the ankle; the right heel slips and rubs. She has a raw pink hot spot on the back of her right heel, later covered by a square of moleskin.

Pace wears a bright cobalt-blue fitted synthetic shirt that shows his back and movement. He carries a pack with a trail kit, sandwiches, cherries, water, wax paper, and later has a gym bag behind the truck bench with a clean folded dry flannel shirt. He drives the old white pickup with an unbroken bench seat and fold-up console.
