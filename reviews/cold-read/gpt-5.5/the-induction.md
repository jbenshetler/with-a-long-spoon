She crosses the stage, shakes the department woman’s hand, holds the pose for a camera beat, takes the scroll, returns to her chair, sits, and closes the slit again. Afterward Cassie finds her near the coffee, takes her shoulders, calls her “Honor student,” shows her the photo, then steers her by the elbow toward the doors. Vee sends the photo to Pace.

Times, dates, durations: induction takes place in the afternoon, in good afternoon light. Pace is at a mathematics conference in Berkeley for three days, the same three days as the induction. The date conflict became clear earlier. The dress was imagined on the mountain and later in Pace’s bed with it going dark on the door.

Stated facts: honorees sit in roped rows down front while everyone else sits elsewhere. Vee’s name is on a card taped to her folding seat. Pace made the dress specifically for Vee’s induction, for her name being read in a good room, and is sorry to miss it because of the Berkeley math conference. Vee had once imagined walking in late on purpose and making the room turn, but the actual ceremony is warm, dull, and kind, and people do not watch ordinary crossings for long. Cassie’s photo documents the decisive long step. Vee sends the uncropped photo to Pace so he can see the dress doing what he built it to do.
