I felt the pleasure first: Randi choosing her, dressing her, waiting for her instead of Pace, the two of them arm in arm in the cold, Vee being let inside the sorority light. My feeling about Randi moves sharply. She is dazzling, funny, generous with glamour, and the kiss feels real on both sides — she comes back from it “a moment behind,” breathless.
