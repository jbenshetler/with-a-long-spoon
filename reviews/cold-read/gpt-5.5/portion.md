This one lands cold in my stomach. Pace has cooked again, carefully, lovingly, with the same rituals that have felt so erotic and tender before: the bread, the wine, the small bowl, the remembered apples. But because it is Randi at the table, not Vee, the care does not open her. It exposes the gap.

The small bowl, the drawn line through the sauce, the weekend portion of Vee, the portion of truth each person has, the portion of Pace Randi gets and does not get. My guess from here is that Randi’s jealousy is going to make the arrangement less controllable, and that the first real break may come not from Vee discovering the plan, but from Randi no longer being able to bear the shape of it.
