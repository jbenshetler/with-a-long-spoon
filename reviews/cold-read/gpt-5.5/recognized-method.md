This chapter is tender and hot in a much more domestic register than the earlier set-pieces. Suspicion is not about his behavior in the scene so much as the larger hidden system: Vee thinks she is repairing one relationship, but Randi’s hand is at her feet.

- **How I feel**
  - Pace/Peter remains intensely attractive: careful, prepared, practical, restrained until invited, adult, disciplined, strong, brilliant, skilled with math, wood, sewing, cooking, dancing, cycling, and bodily attention. “Lesson” increased my trust in his public safety through the bartender’s testimony; “Broken In” made me ache for him because Vee’s remorse clarified how unfairly she hurt him; “Sheri — First Ride” warmed him further because his feeling for Vee spilled out through her attention to his craft and books. “A Recognized Method” strengthens my trust in his immediate treatment of Vee: he listens, asks for truth, does not punish, does not cheaply wave it away, and answers her practical shame with care.
