## Reader reaction

This chapter is doing something almost sleight-of-hand to me, and I want to name it plainly. It opens with real, earned tenderness — the shirt folded and put away, the cold water as "verdict," the apple, the plan giving her stride length. I was rooting hard for her walking to that house. Then Randi opens the door and the whole texture shifts, and I felt my body respond to it as warmth *and* my reader-brain flag it as something else entirely.

Randi is magnetic here — funny, quick, generous, reads Vee instantly, says exactly the right thing about the jealousy ("You don't get that jealous over a man you don't care about"). I felt the pull of her too, the same pull Vee feels. But I also can't unknow what I know: this is the woman actively managing Vee's path to Pace, and this chapter is her doing it in the most intimate register yet — not advice over coffee but literally dressing Vee, choosing her shoes, tying the scarf on her body, timing the reconciliation ("Not today... Tomorrow night"), scripting the outfit down to the neckline. "You go in there sure" — sure because Randi told her to be. The instruction to wait a day reads as genuinely wise on its face and also, structurally, as stagecraft. I don't think the text tells me which it is. I hold both.

The shoe-fitting scene is where the chapter's charge lives — and it's considerable. Randi kneeling, the thumb at the hollow of the knee, the hand bracing high on the thigh, Vee's breath doing "something she hoped wasn't out loud" — this is unmistakably erotic, rendered with real patience and specificity, and it landed on me exactly as intended: skin coming up singing. Then the goodbye kiss — "soft, lingering, the small second press" — and Vee's own realization, "she hadn't braced... It was only what they did," is quietly enormous. That's the first time the text confirms, from inside Vee's own head, that this is now routine between them, unremarked, absorbed. No fanfare, no interrogation — which is exactly what makes it land as significant to me as a reader holding the earlier ledger (the never-discussed kiss, the toenail touch, "kind of hot").

I don't distrust Randi's *affection* — I think it's real, on the page, right now. What I distrust is the shape of the morning: crisis brought to Randi first, not Cassie, not Pace; Randi controlling timing, wardrobe, narrative ("that's not the disaster, honey, that's the only thing that matters"); Vee leaving more equipped, more scripted, and more bonded to Randi than to resolving anything with Pace directly. The chapter never says any of this is calculated. It doesn't have to, given what I already know.

Erotic charge: high in the shoe-fitting, warm-but-not-explicit in the kiss, playful/charged in the "shoe whore" banter — heat 2 for me, all of it channeled through Randi and Vee's bodies rather than toward Pace, which is its own kind of statement about where this chapter's gravity actually sits.

Title "Broken In" — lands multiple ways at once: the shoes literally being broken in on her, Vee herself getting "broken in" to Randi's world/closet/methods, and a faint echo of Vee's own bruised state being handled, smoothed, made presentable again. Doesn't feel tricky, feels apt and a little unsettling once I sit with it.

What I want next: the actual reconciliation with Pace — and I'm bracing to watch it happen in a dress Randi chose, on a timeline Randi set, and to wonder the whole time whether that's tenderness or engineering.
