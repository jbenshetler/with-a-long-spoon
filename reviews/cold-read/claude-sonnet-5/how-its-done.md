# Cold read — How It's Done

*scene: scenes/how-its-done.md · model: claude-sonnet-5 · read after: turned-up*

## Reader reaction

Okay, this one has more going on under the surface than any chapter so far, and it moved me around a lot while I was in it.

**How I feel about each character right now.** Randi is the whole show here and I don't trust her the way I did an hour ago. She's still magnetic — the car, the "Look at *you*," the wine she doesn't even glance at the price of — but this chapter she does something with Vee that I clocked in real time and didn't love: she pulls the fitting story out of Vee piece by piece, normalizes the thing Vee's ashamed of ("Nobody measures over a bra... it's not a *thing*, it's just how it's done") specifically so Vee will keep talking, gets her all the way to the edge of the actual confession — "I—there was—" — and then, right there, closes the door. *"You don't have to tell me."* That's not kindness, or not only kindness. That's someone who wanted to watch Vee open up and then chose the moment to stop her, and I felt the manipulation of it more than I felt the tenderness. And then thirty seconds later Randi's voice goes "daylight," ordinary, like a switch thrown. That flatness after the hush unsettled me more than anything explicit could have.

And then the ending. Randi kisses her — not a peck, a real one, with a pause and a second press — and drives off calling her "gorgeous," and Vee stands on the curb with her fingers at her mouth explaining it away as *French, that's just how women like her say goodbye,* and then goes cold with fear that someone in the dorm windows saw. That's the chapter's real center of gravity for me. I don't know what Randi *means* by it — affection, another test, habit, genuine want — the text doesn't tell me and I'm not going to decide for it. But I felt the charge of it land in my own body along with Vee's.

Vee I love more every chapter — her reading the menu right-to-left, the scuffed pumps, tipping well because she won't be "the broke girl," the whole quiet arithmetic of money she does at that table while Randi orders wine that costs more than lunch. That's such precise, unglamorous class detail and it made me ache for her a little. And her retelling the thermostat and the fitting to Randi with the same giddy pride she gave Cassie last chapter — except this time the listener isn't safe the way Cassie was, and I felt that mismatch acutely.

**Swoon / pull.** The car itself, honestly — the window sealing shut "like the car was tucking itself in around her," the top down, the sing-along song at the light, Vee not minding being looked at for once. That whole stretch is pure swoon, unguarded pleasure, and I let myself enjoy it with her. And under the manipulation, there's something genuinely erotic in how thoroughly Randi reads Vee — "You *love* being looked at" lands as true and as a gift, even while I distrust the hand that's giving it.

**Trust vs. suspicion.** This is the first chapter that's really earned suspicion of Randi specifically, rather than just holding open questions about her. The tickling story — tied to a headboard, a man who "switches to somewhere else entirely" — reads oddly specific and oddly staged for a stranger, and I can't shake the feeling I'm supposed to wonder who that story is really about, though the text gives me nothing to confirm it (that's a guess from here, not a read). More concretely: the way she extracts Vee's confession and then withholds the payoff is a *choice*, on the page, not my invention.

**Erotic charge.** Peaks twice — once inside Vee's own memory as she tells it (the platform, "which hip," "then he's right in front of you... what did he *see*") which is scorching precisely because it's secondhand, filtered through Randi's coaxing; and once at the very end, the kiss, small and understated and the more charged for it.

**Friction.** None, really — this one held me the whole way. If anything the "tit goddess" line jarred a little, funny but a hair broader than Randi's register elsewhere.

**Titles.** "How It's Done" — I read it first as Randi's line, "it's just how it's done," about the bra, innocuous, instructive. But sitting with it after the kiss, it points wider: how *seduction* is done, how a person is walked, gently, expertly, exactly where you want them, being told the whole way that nothing unusual is happening. It recolors the whole lunch. Sharp title.

**What I want next.** I want to know what that kiss was and whether Vee will tell anyone — Cassie, probably not, given how careful she is now about the windows. I'm bracing a little for Randi, more than I have been, without dreading her outright.
