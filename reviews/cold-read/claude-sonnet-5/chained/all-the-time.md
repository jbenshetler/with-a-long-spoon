## Reader reaction

Oh, this one got its hooks in me differently than the last chapter did. It's Vee and Randi again, brunch again, and on the surface it reads like sheer confection — Vee gushing about sex and food and sleep, Randi drinking it up. And I *did* melt some of the way through, genuinely: "You've been starving your whole life and calling it *fine*, and now you're not, and you've gone and decided that's the flaw" is a gorgeous line, and I felt the real tenderness of Randi giving Vee permission to want without shame. That landed as pure warmth.

But then the photo. God, the photo scene. I already knew from carry-forward that the picture existed, sent to Pace with a named risk — and now Vee is showing it to *Randi*, unprompted, escalating from the cropped one to the full nude because she can't stand to be read as a girl who flinched. That's not really about the photo anymore, that's about wanting Randi's approval, wanting not to disappoint her. And Randi's reaction — "her eyes had gone bright and a little unfixed," the catch in her breath, "It doesn't" said "low, and not quite even" — when Vee describes being wanted "with the body attached" — that's not casual friend-listening. That hit me in the stomach a little. Randi is not neutral here. She is moved by something in this in a way that reads more like longing than vicarious pleasure in a friend's happiness.

And the goodbye kiss — Vee leaning up into it this time, taking the extra second for herself, and Randi holding the look a beat past what a goodbye needs before smiling it off. Vee narrates herself as choosing it, deciding "it was what she wanted" — but she also flattens it immediately after, "one hug to carry her the whole week." I don't fully trust that flattening. The text gave me more than Vee is willing to sit with.

Trust vs. suspicion: I don't distrust Randi's fondness for Vee — it reads as real. But I'm now actively suspicious that Randi's feeling for Vee has weight of its own, separate from and possibly in tension with whatever's going on with Pace ("not finished"). The reader-knowledge gap is wide open and this chapter widened it further without Vee noticing at all.

Erotic charge: the eggs Benedict passage is doing more sensory work than most explicit sex scenes I've read — "her hand had gone back for more before she'd decided anything" — food as displaced/rhyming appetite is working on me hard. The actual described sex (relayed secondhand, "he takes forever," "hold me anywhere he wants me") is warm but a little abstracted by being reported rather than shown — it's Vee performing intimacy for Randi as much as remembering it.

Titles: "All the Time" — points at the sex-frequency confession ("more than I've ever," "it doesn't stop"), but by the end it also reads as pointing at something else — Randi wanting all the time Vee will give her, or the "all the time" quality of attention Vee describes Pace giving her, mirrored uneasily by Randi's own total attention across the table.

What I want next: I want to see whether Vee ever catches what I caught — that Randi's response to the photo and the kiss wasn't ordinary. I'm bracing (not dreading, genuinely curious) for either a scene where Randi's control slips further, or one where this whole morning gets sanded down into "just a fun brunch" by Vee's own narration.
