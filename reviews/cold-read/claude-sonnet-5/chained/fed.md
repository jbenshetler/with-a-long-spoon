## Reader reaction

This one unsettled me steadily the whole way through, in a way that felt earned rather than imposed. Randi is doing something masterful and faintly predatory here and I can feel it happening in real time even while Vee experiences it as intimacy.

**Randi** — I don't trust her, and the text has now given me real grounds, not just lingering suspicion from earlier chapters. Everything about this scene is engineered: she picks the venue, she orders first so Vee follows her lead, she leads with a sad self-deprecating story specifically to "clear the air" and prime Vee to reciprocate, and then she extracts — gently, warmly, expertly — every detail Vee withheld even from Cassie. "Give me something to live on. Be cruel. Tell me everything." That's a seduction of information, not a friend's curiosity. And she reads Vee's tells with surgical precision: the toenail polish, the new underwear, the flinch at "why'd you pick that color" — she catches the fear on Vee's face about being "gone" on Pace and turns it into reassurance ("You're not caught, sweetheart. You're lucky") that functions to keep Vee talking, keep her open, keep her handing things over. Then the goodbye kiss, casually described, met without hesitation this time, no longer even glanced-around-for. That progression alarms me more than the interrogation did.

**Vee** — I ache for her here. She is so happy, so undefended, and she doesn't see what I see: that she's giving Randi exactly the thing she just finished protecting from Cassie ("the rest of it was not Cassie's, was not anybody's, was hers") — except it wasn't hers after all, it was Randi's, the moment Randi asked. The line that got me was "how much she had wanted, all weekend, to set it down on a table in front of someone who would not flinch" — that need is completely understandable and completely being used.

**Pace** — offstage, described only through Vee's retelling, and the retelling still glows: patient, gentle, hungry for her specifically. No new suspicion of him from this chapter directly, though the dramatic irony (what I know that Vee doesn't about how this all started) sits heavier now that I've watched Randi work.

**Swoon vs. unease** — the swoon is real (the sheet, the kitchen, "nothing missing" — that's a genuinely beautiful image and I felt it) but it's wrapped inside a scene that is fundamentally about extraction, and I can't unfeel that frame once Randi's questions start stacking.

**Friction** — none really; the writing is controlled and I believe every beat of Randi's technique because it's shown, not asserted.

**Title** — "Fed" lands multiple ways: literally the meal, but also Randi feeding on the story, feeding Vee lines to complete her own confessions with, Vee's hunger fed. Given the chapter, I read it now as mostly about Randi.

**Want next** — I want to see whether Randi reports any of this back, or to whom, or why she wants it so badly. And I keep waiting for the kiss thread and the extraction thread to collide with something Vee can't unsee.
