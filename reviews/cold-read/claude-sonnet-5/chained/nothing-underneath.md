## Reader reaction

Okay, this chapter is a lot, and I want to be honest about all of it rather than smoothing it into one verdict.

First — the mirror scene, the drive, the traffic stop, the door. That whole sequence swept me completely. The prose does something patient and unhurried that matches Vee's own deliberate pacing — the drawer closing on the pretty bra, the shoes going on first instead of last, the giggle alone in the room, "last thing, only thing." That's such a specific, true little observation of how a woman actually experiences dressing for something momentous, and it made me love her a little, watching her build her own courage detail by detail. The masturbation scenes — in front of the mirror, then in the car — are frank and hot without being clinical, and they're doing real work: she's not performing arousal for the reader, she's using her own body to talk herself into the plan, stopping deliberately both times because she wants him to be the one. That refusal reads as real self-possession, which matters to me given how much of this plan was, per last chapter, Randi's authorship. For a few pages Vee gets her body back as unambiguously her own, even if the plan around it wasn't.

The cop scare made me laugh out loud and then flinch with her — good, unpretentious tension, nothing overwrought about it.

Then the door opens, and this is where the chapter earns its title and its whole shape: "the wall was simply gone, taken down all at once by what she'd done." I felt the swoon here completely — the arms around her waist, "all those weeks of it in one breath," going to his knees on the cold boards. It's generous, desperate, mutual heat, not cold or performative. The couch, the failed carry to the bed, the gracelessness of it — "which was the truth of them tonight" — that line lands. This is Pace unguarded in a way I haven't seen since maybe the earliest chapters, and I believed it.

And then the pancakes. God, the pancakes undid me a little — "made and not poured," him on one knee with his voice cracking, "I missed you so much." That's the tenderness axis maxed out, and it's rendered so specifically (measuring flour without looking, folding just until it comes together) that it reads as devotion in the register this book has taught me to trust from him — competence as love-language.

But — and here's where I have to hold the unease the prior chapters earned rather than manufacture new ones — I can't unsee what I know that Vee doesn't. Her whole confidence walking to that door was built by Randi, sentence by sentence, on zero actual information about what Pace has been doing (the real brunette, unaddressed, unmentioned even in Vee's fear — she's still worried about an invented "Sheri"). What happened at the door was real and warm and clearly is real relief on his side too. But it doesn't resolve anything about the brunette, doesn't touch the actual content of the December rupture (he still hasn't said the word she needed), and the "wall coming down" here was accomplished by a naked woman on a porch, not by him finally being able to name her. I notice she still doesn't get "I love you" — she gets "I missed you so much" and pancakes and his face cracking, which is enormous, but it is still not the word. The chapter is honest enough to let me notice that gap myself; it doesn't pretend the word got said.

The closing beat — Cassie's "Hmm" and going back to her book, Vee too happy to explain — is a nice quiet landing, low-key and a little funny, and it doesn't editorialize the weekend for me. I appreciate that the text doesn't tell me whether this was a triumph or a postponement.

**Swoon / pull:** Very high this chapter — the door, the kneeling, the pancakes. I was fully swept.

**Trust vs. suspicion:** I trust the heat and the tenderness as real. I remain suspicious of the *foundation* — the plan's authorship, the unresolved brunette, the fact that nothing about the actual content of December got addressed, only re-enacted and overwritten by a spectacular reunion.

**Erotic charge:** Very high — the mirror, the car, the door, the couch. It's graphic, present, and never goes slack; even the tenderness after (his mouth at her breast "not to start anything back up") stays sensual without straining.

**Friction:** None structurally — my only hesitation is thematic, not craft: I want to know if this "worked" only because Vee brought her whole body and asked for nothing verbal in return, which is exactly the terms Pace has always preferred.

**Title:** "Nothing Underneath" — obviously the coat, but by the end it also reads as a question about the reunion itself: is there anything underneath the reconciliation besides bodies and relief? I don't think the chapter answers that yet, and I don't think it's supposed to.

**Want next:** I want to see whether the words ever come — whether this weekend becomes the new "warmth held on one seam only," or whether something actually shifted. And I very much want the brunette thread to surface eventually, because right now it's a live grenade nobody in the story knows is in the room.
