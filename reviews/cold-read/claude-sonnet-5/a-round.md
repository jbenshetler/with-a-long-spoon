## Reader reaction

**The felt read**

I came into this chapter already melted from the hike and the truck — and this one goes further, in a way that surprised me with how *slow* it let itself be. The measuring scene is almost unbearably patient. Every beat — neck to shoulder, shoulder to wrist, waist, under the bust, across the bust, inseam — is its own small scene of approach and withdrawal, and by the third or fourth repetition I felt exactly what Vee felt: the rhythm training her body before her mind caught up. That's expert-feeling erotic writing to me — the tape measure doing what a hand alone couldn't get away with, because it has a plausible, decent reason to touch her everywhere.

**Pace** — I still don't fully trust him, and this chapter didn't resolve that, it complicated it further in an interesting way. The dress itself is a gorgeous, generous act — he remembered an offhand, half-embarrassed thing she said at the summit and built weeks of labor around giving it back to her. That's real tenderness, not performance for its own sake; nobody was watching him do it. And yet I can't unknow what the checkpoint told me: that his attentiveness to Vee began as a device, a "pointing game." So even a scene this loving comes with a low hum of unease under it for me — is this *just* him now, or is some of this still the same exquisite calibration that got her into his orbit in the first place? The chapter doesn't editorialize about it at all, which I appreciate — it just lets the tenderness be completely, devastatingly real on the page while I hold my separate knowledge apart from it.

The moment that actually got me was small: he laughs — really laughs, "surprised out of him" — when she asks if the bust measurement changes when her nipples are hard, and he says "I don't know. I've never thought about it," genuinely stumped. That felt like the truest, least performed beat in the whole chapter. A man running a long game doesn't get caught flat-footed and delighted like that. It's the strongest evidence yet, for me, that whatever this started as, it has become something he's actually inside of, not managing.

**Vee** — I ached for her the whole way through, especially the shame spiral over her own wetness. That section is the emotional center of the chapter for me: the "small ugly word" *stripper* arriving in her mother's register, the bargaining ("Maybe it would stop here"), the dread building as his hands walk around her toward the place she's most exposed. It's shame rendered with total specificity — not generic modesty, but the exact machinery of a woman raised to apologize for her own body. And then the pivot when she opens her eyes and finds him grinning up at her, "delighted by her," and something in her posture actually *changes* — she stops apologizing with her spine. That's the chapter's real turn, more than anything sexual: her body starts to believe it's allowed to be wanted without punishment. I was fully with her there, rooting hard.

And then she gets bold — the deliberate "accident" of her breast against his cheek, the "Oops. Sorry." — and it's wonderful and funny and hot in a way that felt earned rather than tacked on, because we'd just watched her spend an entire afternoon learning she had this power. Him going red, fighting the grin, is a lovely reversal — for once he's the one caught out.

**Swoon vs. suspicion**: the swoon is enormous here — the fleece blanket and tea "in the good way," the riser he obviously built with exactly this kind of use in mind (though nothing sinister — it reads as craftsman's habit, not a trap), the way he chooses restraint ("Keep still," costing him something) over the easy yes she's handing him. I don't distrust his *care* in the moment at all. My suspicion is entirely the inherited kind, sitting quietly behind the scene rather than inside it — the jacket copy's "nothing was taken from her that she did not hand over" keeps humming under every gift.

**Erotic charge**: this is where the book is working hardest on me and succeeding. It never once goes graphic — nothing below PG in terms of actual explicit contact — and yet it's the most charged chapter I've read so far, purely through withheld touch, the discipline of the tape-measure ritual, and interiority. The under-the-bust measurement, his knuckles riding the underside of her breast, her breath going shallow because shallow "lifted her into his hands with every inhale" — that's as hot as any explicit scene, maybe hotter, because it's all coiled tension with nowhere to release.

**Friction**: none that bothered me. If anything the chapter risks over-lingering — the measuring sequence is very long and could feel like the author's thumb on the scale toward "make the reader suffer along with her," but it earned its length for me because each measurement adds a new specific sensation and a new specific piece of Vee's psychology rather than repeating the same beat.

**Titles**: "A Round" — I like this a lot once I've read it. It's the tailor's term for a fitting, plain and un-freighted, and it also carries that faint echo of "buying a round," something given, something shared — apt for a chapter that's entirely about Pace giving her something (the dress, the being-seen) and about what she gives back (the deliberate "accident," her own held stillness). It doesn't spoil anything; it just sits there quietly correct once you know what the chapter is. *A Polite Invitation* and *With a Long Spoon* both feel closer to their teeth now — this whole chapter is an invitation rendered as courtesy (he asks, he waits, he stops) laid right over an act of total exposure. The proverb the series title gestures toward (supping with the devil) hasn't shown its edge yet on the page, but I feel it standing just outside this scene, in what I already know and Vee doesn't.

**Want / dread next**: I want to see the dress finished and worn — and I'm bracing, pleasantly, for whatever happens next in that house, since "Keep still" clearly cost him something and I don't think that particular restraint holds forever. Guess from here: I'd bet the pinning/fitting doesn't stay strictly workmanlike much longer, though I have no confirmation of that beyond the temperature of this chapter.
