## Reader reaction

Everything's inline this time, no packet id — so I'll just give the reaction directly.

**How I feel about each character**

Vee is still the one I'm rooting for, and this chapter both deepens and complicates that. Her hunger — for the swim, for the food, for Pace, for the *telling* — is so alive, and I love watching her build her own confidence ("a woman shopped with her eye and not her budget"). But the "clean-plate" title is earning its keep here: she is being consumed just as much as she's consuming, and I felt the first real prickle of worry about *Randi* specifically as something other than a warm confidante this chapter.

Pace remains tender and specific in exactly the ways that have always made him magnetic — the wrist-kissing, the orange-peeling patience, "he's enthusiastic" — and nothing he does in the room is anything but generous. My unease about him hasn't grown here so much as sat quietly, waiting.

Randi is where this chapter really moved something in me. The "missed a spot" recounting and the oral-sex conversation is the most exposed I've seen her be about her *own* desire rather than extracting Vee's — "It was powerful. It turned me on" delivered flat, mild, like a fact set on the cloth. And then the neck kiss: last time it was clearly established as a ticklish, involuntary place. This time Randi tips her head to "settle the hug deeper" and lays that exact spot open, hair brushing Vee's cheek — and her body does "a small quick thing, a catch and then a rise, and the rise went on a beat too long" before she laughs it off as ticklish again. That reads to me, for the first time on the page rather than in my checkpoint-knowledge, like Randi *arranging* to be touched there and then covering it. I don't have proof — I'm working partly off what my memory tells me about her interiority — but this chapter gave the text-level behavior to back the suspicion up, not just prior knowledge.

**Swoon / pull**

The Saturday-morning recap chapter did its work — I'm still floating on some of that. But this chapter's real swoon, oddly, is Vee's *appetite itself*: the swim, the clean plate, "I ate the whole plate and told him so, twice, mouth full." Her own hunger being narrated with such unashamed relish is lovely and feels like real growth from the "floozie" shame-voice era.

**Trust vs. suspicion**

This is the chapter where my suspicion of Randi crossed from checkpoint-knowledge into page-earned. Two moments: the flat, unadorned "I don't" / "It was powerful. It turned me on," delivered with total composure while Vee is unraveling — Randi is clearly not disturbed by the content, only fascinated by Vee's reaction to it, gathering data. And the neck-kiss repeat, which felt engineered in a way the first one (which read as pure accident, "you cried on me") didn't. I don't fully trust that "ticklish" cover anymore.

**Erotic charge**

High through most of it, but interestingly displaced — the actual truck-sex retelling goes *soft-focus* on the page ("My head went to his lap... he came apart... It was a lot") specifically because Vee can't say the real version out loud even to Randi, and the charge instead builds around the *telling itself*, the interrogation, Randi's fingernail on the wine glass rim. The chapter is hottest not during the remembered sex but during the confession about the confession — very in keeping with this book's pattern, but it lands.

**Friction as a reader**

One thing nagged: "you picked well" from Randi, followed immediately by Vee's own dissonant thought — "she hadn't picked Pace so much as looked up... to find him already halfway across the floor at her, the whole thing decided before she'd known there was a thing to decide." That's Vee's memory *contradicting itself* in real time (in my checkpoint, Randi orchestrated the "chance" meeting via the coffee shop, not this floor scene) — but since this is new material to me on the page, I just registered it as an odd, telling little slip that Vee brushes past rather than examines. It's the kind of moment that reads exactly like the "outlier" motif — she notices, and files it, and moves on.

**Titles**

"Clean-Plate" — I initially read it as pure appetite-affirmation (she eats everything, twice, unapologetically), and it is that. But by the end it's doing double duty: she "ate all of it without noticing," the plate "polished clean," right after the oral-sex conversation where she's been fed a truth by Randi that she also swallows whole without quite tasting what it was — "the folder wouldn't sit flat in the drawer." A clean plate that isn't actually settled. It recolors nicely on the way out rather than giving anything away up front. No volume/series title or tagline was supplied with this chapter, so I have nothing fresh to react to there.

**What I want next**

I want to see whether Vee ever consciously catches the neck-kiss oddity, or whether it goes the way of everything else — filed, not examined. And I'm newly, specifically curious what "I don't" from Randi is going to cost either of them. Guess from here: nothing confronts anyone directly yet, but the gap between what Vee tells Cassie/the reader and what she lets herself notice is widening, and this chapter felt like a small crack in the ice over Randi rather than Pace for once.
