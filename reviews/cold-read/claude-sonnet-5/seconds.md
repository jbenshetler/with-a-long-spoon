## Reader reaction

Oh, this one landed differently than I expected. First — Pace on the page again, and a scene with real morning intimacy: the quilt, the coffee positioned for her before she even asks, the choreography of "reaching and not-moving." That's tender and it does swoon me a little — I believe the domestic ease between them completely, more than I've believed it in any other chapter, because it's shown in small habituated gestures rather than declared.

Then the sentence. "I had been eating crackers my whole life... and now I know." I felt that in my chest — it's the most naked thing Vee has done on the page, more naked than anything physical so far, and I loved her for the risk of it and ached for how carefully she'd built it to not-quite-be *I love you*. The prose earns the tension of the silence after: "his stillness for one beat that was a beat longer" — I felt my own held breath there.

And then his answer: "I love how good we are together. I never had this either. What you do — what your body does — I've never. I'm glad you feel it too." My gut reaction was a small drop, a wince. He heard her offering of *meaning* and answered about *sensation* — "how good we are together," what her *body* does. It's warm, it's not nothing, but it's not what she gave him, and the text lets me feel that gap before Vee herself can name it. This is the first time I've felt genuinely uneasy about Pace rather than swept up by him — not suspicious of malice, but suspicious that he may be constitutionally unable, or unwilling, to meet her at the level she's reaching. That tracks with "I don't go back" and the whole architecture of control I've been carrying — but this is the first time it cost her something directly, on the page, in real time.

The dryer scene is devastating in its quietness — no confrontation, no drama, just Vee doing the math alone and choosing not to ask, because "testing it meant saying the words, and the words went through him first." That "small flickers... doing small repair work to keep functioning" line hit hardest — it names, for the first time, that Vee has been *managing* doubts about him all along, not just marveling at him.

**Trust vs. suspicion:** this is the chapter that earns real suspicion for me — not of his intentions, but of his capacity to reciprocate at her depth. It's the first crack I actually feel, not one I'm told to look for.

**Erotic charge:** low this chapter, and rightly so — this is about emotional exposure, not heat. The physical warmth (the quilt, his hand on her shin, the kiss held long against her hair) is doing tenderness work, not arousal work, and it works.

**Title:** "Seconds" — I take it now as her second cup of coffee, the sentence being a kind of second offering after the unspeakable first (*I love you*), and maybe also "seconds" as in a portion — she gave him a full meal of feeling and got a second helping of something smaller back. Oblique, resonant after the fact.

**What I want next:** I want to know whether this gap widens or whether I'm wrong to read it as a gap at all — the text keeps it genuinely ajar ("Maybe he understood and chose to say it like that. Maybe it had gone past him entirely"). I don't dread it exactly, but I'm newly wary of Pace in a way I wasn't before.
