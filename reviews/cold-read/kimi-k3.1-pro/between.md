The ache in this one is that Vee can recognize a woman’s desire with almost perfect clarity—until recognizing it would require her to locate it in herself. Sheri’s leisurely look becomes safe, funny, even glorious: “She never graded me at all, Randi. She was enjoying herself.” I loved Vee’s delight in that, the way being wanted without being assessed felt like a small emancipation. Then she says, “Me, she’d have unwrapped right there on the floor,” Randi’s thumb stops, and everything in me stopped with it. Vee’s “No” arrives too quickly, followed by the smaller “Not my thing,” and I didn’t believe her for a second. Not because she has to want Sheri, but because the chapter has just shown her body and imagination enjoying precisely that possibility.

Randi is becoming almost painful to watch through Vee’s trusting eyes. Her flat “And you liked it” does not sound to me like worry that Sheri might threaten Vee’s relationship with Pace. After hearing Randi tell Pace that she wants Vee’s breast in her hand and her nipple in her mouth, it sounds like jealousy breaking through before she can dress it. She is so tender with Randi—reassuring her, taking her hand, accepting the resumed caress—that I can’t treat Randi’s feelings as merely manipulative. I trust the wanting. I just don’t trust the story either woman is currently telling about it.

Their physical ritual had more charge for me than the waxing plan: Randi turning Vee’s fingers over one at a time, her bare foot settling along Vee’s calf, the goodbye kiss that eases back without leaving and then “took more of it.” Vee is already waiting with her eyes closed before Randi begins.
