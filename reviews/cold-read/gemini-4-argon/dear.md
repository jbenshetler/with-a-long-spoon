This chapter left me with the strangest, most intoxicating double-vision: I am completely swept up in Vee's giddy joy and the genuine heat between her and Randi, while at the exact same time my stomach is knotted with dread over how seamlessly Randi is orchestrating her.

My heart is entirely in Vee's hands right now. Watching her come back from her dinner with Pace and do a clumsy, whole-body spin into a bean bag chair—*"I did it because I wanted to and I did it on purpose"*—was pure, infectious delight. And Cassie! I love Cassie more every chapter. She is the truest friend in this book, genuinely happy for Vee (*"Look at you"*) while immediately asking the exact pragmatic questions nobody else is asking: *"How much did he give you to drink?"* and *"What's his name, so I know what to tell the state troopers when you go missing on a mountain with a man you met at a coffee shop?"* That lands with real teeth because *we* know how much isn't what Vee thinks it is, and Vee really only knows *"off six-fourteen, past where it goes to gravel."* Worse, watching Vee gently drift away from Cassie after stats six days later—telling herself *"that it was fine to have your own people"* while *"underneath it something turned over, small, and she let it lie"*—made me ache for both of them. Cassie sees Randi so clearly, and Vee is already choosing the shiny new warmth over the friend who keeps the window cracked two inches.

And yet, god, it is impossible not to feel why Vee chooses it, because Randi in The Smoothie Bird is dizzyingly magnetic.
