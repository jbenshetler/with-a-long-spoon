### Impression
- **Vee:** Deeply sympathetic, vivid, and easy to root for. Her hunger—for food, for intellectual recognition, for a world with color in it, and for a man who doesn't make her feel like too much or an afterthought—is so honest that every step she takes out from under her mother's shaming voice feels earned and exhilarating. Even when she misses what is happening around her (forgetting Randi's actual words, misreading Randi's tight smile and thumbnail on the glass, explaining away Randi's mouth-kiss as "French"), her blindness comes from how badly she has wanted to be chosen by people like Randi and Pace.
- **Pace:** A genuinely double-edged pull. On one hand, his patience, competence, and courtly restraint on the page—fixing Vee's blister, standing shivering in the rain so she can have his dry shirt, matching the burgundy silk to her coloring, keeping his eyes lifted to her face on the riser so she feels delighted in rather than shamed, and quietly withholding her most vulnerable moments from Randi—feel deeply tender and real, not merely performed. On the other hand, we know from Chapters 1, 3, 7, and 14 that he built the bench, initiated the pointing game, staged the coffee-shop pickup, and has been feeding sensory pieces of Vee to Randi in bed. The tension between his genuine care for Vee (and his past remorse over Daphne) and the deceptive game he started with Randi is sharpening fast.
- **Randi:** Mesmerizing, dangerous, and quietly tragic. In front of Vee she is effortless solar warmth, yet Chapters 1 and 2 showed how brittle and lonely her perfection actually is—a woman terrified of being truly seen or held in tenderness, pressing sharp edges under her thumbnail and lying with her shoes on under a cold duvet. Her physical hunger for Vee is unmistakably real, not just a performance for Pace, which makes her calculating extraction of Vee's secrets in Chapter 19—and the flash of pain when she learns Pace warmed the house and is making Vee a dress—all the more volatile.
- **Cassie:** The clearest-eyed person in Vee's life and a grounded, loyal anchor. She clocked Randi's performance from the first minute in stats class and has never been dazzled by her, while cheering wholeheartedly for the version of Vee that is blooming with Pace—without knowing the trap underneath it.
