### Impression
- **Vee** is immediately endearing and easy to root for: funny, smart, hungry for real connection, and painfully accustomed to shrinking or covering her lush body under her mother's internalized voice and cheap male attention. Watching her come alive—eating without policing herself, diving into the pool with Cassie, and joyfully claiming her own agency when she stops Pace under the tree—makes the trap closing around her ache all the more.
- **Pace** is magnetically calm, patient, and genuinely attentive, which is what makes him so disarming and so dangerous. There is no cruelty in him; he truly cares about Randi's safety and pleasure, respects closed doors because of his past with Daphne, keeps Vee's froyo confession private, and wants both women to choose freely. Yet he is seamlessly running a covert script on Vee—using the exact same courtly restraint, permission-asking, and half-step retreat that disarmed Randi—while remaining completely blind to how badly the bench night fractured Randi behind her bathroom door.
- **Randi** is fascinating, electric, and quietly heartbreaking. Her social and physical grace is lethal when aimed at a target like Vee, and her deception (staging the collision, summoning Pace, faking the "Paul" lapse and the French-date story) is chillingly smooth. At the same time, we have seen her raw terror in the bathroom mirror, the bottle cap pressed into her thumbnail bed, her humiliation in the sorority common room, her staring at the ceiling under a cold duvet unable to cry, and the involuntary orgasm and bare *"Yes"* that betrayed her real, unscripted hunger for Vee. She is using Vee both as an erotic object and as a human shield against Pace's tenderness—yet the flash of stillness when Pace says Vee is *"easy to listen to"* hints that the game is already cutting back into her.
- **Cassie** is a grounded, clear-eyed breath of fresh air—the only person in Vee's life who wants nothing from her body or her social status, and the only observer sharp enough to clock the falseness around Randi.
