God, that *There you are, Randi* dropped the floor right out from under me. Because of the jacket blurb, I spent the first few pages halfway waiting for her name to be Vee — she’s twenty-one, in a sorority, starving herself on thin apple slices — or else holding her unnamed while Pace was named right away. Keeping her just *she* all the way through the scene on the bench, until the exact moment her social armor cracks and he says *"There you are, Randi,"* is a breathtaking move. We aren't starting with Vee's story at all; we're starting inside the relationship between Randi and Pace before the game on the jacket even exists, watching the exact night Randi gets cracked open so deeply that she decides to lock the door from the inside.

And before that turn in the bathroom, the swoon and the erotic charge of this chapter worked on me completely. What makes the last third of the chapter so chilling and so sad is that the terror comes *from* the tenderness, not from any cruelty. I had zero friction with the prose; every physical and psychological detail earned its keep. And the titles are doing dark, fascinating work together now. **"The Bench"** is both the literal piece of furniture Pace built — a domestic table hiding an apparatus of total exposure — and the place where Randi's lifelong defense mechanism breaks. Put that next to the cover's series title **WITH A LONG SPOON · BOOK ONE** (the proverb about supping with the devil!), the volume title **A POLITE INVITATION**, and the blurb's closing line (*"Every yes was freely given. If she can't bear Pace looking past her face with all that patient, stripping tenderness, is she going to put another girl on that bench in her place? I cannot wait to see what she does next.
