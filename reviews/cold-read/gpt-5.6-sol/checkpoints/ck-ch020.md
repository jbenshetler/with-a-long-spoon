### Impression
Vee has my strongest tenderness and sympathy. Her joy is real, her desire is increasingly her own, and it is moving to watch her discover that being visible need not mean being shamed. I am also uneasy because the two people helping her claim that freedom are withholding the fact that they selected her together.

Pace feels deeply attractive, careful, observant, and trustworthy in every direct interaction shown with Vee. His practical tenderness consistently costs him time, comfort, or effort. My suspicion is not about whether his care is real; it is about what he and Randi planned, what he currently knows, and whether his respect for local choices can survive the larger concealed choice.

Randi pulls me in and worries me most. Her warmth toward Vee increasingly feels genuine, her private terror after the bench makes her sympathetic, and her kisses and softer looks carry real desire. At the same time, she is a gifted social guide who can lead Vee almost anywhere while making the path feel self-discovered, and she is hiding the central truth.

Cassie remains the safest person on the page: funny, loyal, perceptive, and undemanding. I trust her instincts, including the unease she has not yet turned into an accusation. I worry that Vee’s edited stories and growing preference for Randi as confidante are beginning to place Cassie outside the life she used to share.
