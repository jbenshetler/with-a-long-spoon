Pace says smaller steps are easier and it is not a race. He demonstrates the woman’s part while facing away from Vee. In frame, his left hand holds Vee’s right extended to the side; his right hand rests firmly against her back, fingers touching her shoulder blade. Vee presses her left hand against his shoulder. He instructs her to supply firm counterpressure, avoid becoming “jelly,” close the space between them, avoid looking at her feet, and feel rather than anticipate the lead.
