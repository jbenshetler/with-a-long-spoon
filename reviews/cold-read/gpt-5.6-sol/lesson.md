Vee heard the troubling imbalance but comforted him without forcing a label. Pace linked lifting to protection and total presence; Vee recognized the same presence in sex. She touched his scar, accepted “Not tonight,” and kept her hand beneath his. She said “Peter” softly.

Now Pace invited Vee by text to Thursday dancing at the CW. Because she did not know how, he changed the pickup time and privately taught her the country three-step on a concrete pad beside the dark gym. He taught her that the frame requires firm resistance, closed distance, and attention to the lead rather than guessing. Once she stopped anticipating, his bodily information reached her feet and they moved together.

At the crowded CW, many regulars greeted Pace warmly. Vee learned he had taught beginner country and swing for two semesters and was responsible for many people learning to dance. On the floor she initially lost the beat under public scrutiny, then gave the frame her weight and recovered. Women watched them. A blonde praised Pace’s lead but insinuated that he controlled women and routinely took “the girls” home. Vee interpreted the women’s affection as evidence that she was merely the latest in a series.

When Pace returned with beers, Vee venomously asked whether his “service” included taking girls home. Pace’s good mood vanished; he answered that he sometimes did when required. Vee refused his protection and walked away. Pace did not argue. After friends consoled him, he left the CW.

Vee stayed until closing, watched other women through her inherited *Floozy* contempt, rejected approaching men, and tortured herself with imagined sexual histories. An older bartender gave her water and explained that Pace takes drunk women home but leaves them at the door, never exploits them, and rarely brings dates to the CW. The women had been jealous of Vee, not competing over Pace.

Vee did not call Cassie because she could not explain what she had done. She waited under a light, took a poorly maintained cab whose driver watched her in the mirror, and spent money intended for other needs. Back in the dorm she wore Pace’s washed flannel, whose scent had disappeared, and lay awake. She recognized that his safety and ability to make women feel real were the very qualities she loved, and that she had punished him for them. Alongside the shame, her body retained the pleasure of his lead. At dawn she decided to go to Randi and fix the rupture.
