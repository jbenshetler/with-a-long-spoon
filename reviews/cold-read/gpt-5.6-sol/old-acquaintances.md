Her longing for him still works on me completely. Then the chapter does something genuinely painful by letting the imagined blonde take Vee’s place inside her own memory. That is worse than ordinary jealousy. Vee cannot keep possession of an experience that actually happened to her, because Pace’s silence has made her feel retrospectively replaceable.
