**Question asked (verbatim):** Was there anything that read as a running joke or a wink — aimed a little over the characters' heads, at you? Anything you caught yourself smiling at on a second or third appearance?

---

The clearest running wink was the series of titles and ordinary objects becoming erotically overqualified: Pace’s “low table of odd proportions” is especially good. Once I knew it was the bench, Vee casually tossing her thong onto it in “The New Ordinary” gave me a dark little smile because the furniture knew more than she did.

I also smiled at the recurring practical tokens—green scrunchies multiplying at Pace’s house, his flannel needing to be “recharged” with his smell, and Randi’s black heels repeatedly carrying her influence into Vee’s encounters with him. Those repetitions felt affectionate and funny while still serving the secrecy. And the food jokes kept returning with increasing confidence: Vee’s catastrophic frozen-yogurt tower, the labeled “HUMBLE PIE,” her “recognized method” of soaking dishes, and the way nearly every emotional crisis eventually becomes something Pace feeds her through. The book seemed knowingly amused by how often appetite became literal.
