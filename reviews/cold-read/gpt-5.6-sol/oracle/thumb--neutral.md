**Question asked (verbatim):** Anywhere the writing lost you — confusing, boring, or a moment you didn't buy?

---

A few times, though I was rarely bored. The clearest continuity break was in “Gone”: Randi’s embellished account of the shoe-store joke contradicts what I remembered actually happening, so I couldn’t tell whether she was deliberately repackaging Vee for Pace or the book had slipped. I was also confused by Vee’s claim in “Leave No Trace” that Pace had “no house here to go to,” and briefly stopped buying Pace’s almost mythic accumulation of talents in “Two Towels.”

More often the writing lost me by underlining meanings I had already felt—“latent variables,” Simpson’s paradox, and occasional declarative explanations after an image had already landed. The one moment I resisted on more than stylistic grounds was “We Find Out”: Vee says “Don’t,” Pace continues and makes confession the price of stopping, then adds terms to the wager afterward. Her desire was unmistakable, but I didn’t buy bodily arousal as sufficient grounds to override her words, especially after the book had made his asking so central.
