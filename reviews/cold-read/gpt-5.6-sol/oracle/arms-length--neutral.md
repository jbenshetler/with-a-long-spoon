# Oracle (grounded) — arms-length · neutral

**Question asked (verbatim):** As you read, did you feel like someone watching these people from the outside, or like someone being drawn in with them? Was there anyone you kept at a distance the whole way?

---

I was drawn in with them—often bodily before I could judge them. Pace’s patience and care made me swoon; Vee’s appetite, shame, and joy became almost painfully immediate; even Randi’s steering worked on me while I distrusted it. The book repeatedly made me enjoy the invitation and only then feel the concealed structure underneath, so I felt complicit rather than safely outside.

I kept the socially appraising figures—Brooke, Randi’s parents, the Rusty Nail blonde—at a distance, but no central character stayed there. Cassie was the person I trusted most consistently. Randi came closest to someone I tried to hold away because her tenderness and manipulation were inseparable, yet “Fairytale,” “Gone,” and her unguarded moments with Vee kept pulling me toward her too. Even when my trust in Pace faltered—especially when he pressed past Vee’s “don’t,” and later refused to name their bond—I never stopped feeling the reality of his care or wanting him and Vee to find their way back.
