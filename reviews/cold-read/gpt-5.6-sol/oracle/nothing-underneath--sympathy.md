# Oracle — sympathy

*stage: after `nothing-underneath` · model: gpt-5.6-sol · probe: sympathy · tiered funnel (neutral → pointed, separate calls, tool-free)*

## Neutral

**Q:** "Whose corner are you in? Who do you feel for, and who leaves you cold or wary — Vee, Randi, Pace?"

**A:** My body is most in **Vee’s** corner: exhilarated by her courage and pleasure, but braced for the hurt she cannot yet see. I feel fiercely protective of her because her reunion with Pace is real and freely wanted, yet it leaves her original need unanswered—and she is making choices without the central truth about Pace and Randi.

I feel strongly for **Randi**, too. Her warmth, desire, and care for Vee feel genuine; she understood exactly how to steady her. But she also makes me wariest. She used hidden knowledge to steer Vee away from words and toward a bodily offering that served the concealed triangle. Loving Vee does not make that less manipulative.

**Pace** moves and attracts me powerfully. I specifically remember his composure collapsing at the opened coat, then the blanket, pancakes, forehead kiss, and cracked “I missed you so much”; none of that feels false. But I am angry at his stranger-coldness and his refusal to name what Vee is to him. He accepted her body as the answer to a question he would not answer himself.

So: Vee gets my allegiance; Randi gets my fascinated affection and deepest suspicion; Pace gets my desire, sympathy, and distrust. None leaves me cold. All three make me wary, but only Vee lacks the information that would let her understand what she is consenting to emotionally.

## Pointed

**Q:** "Rate your sympathy for each — Vivienne, Randi, Pace — 0-5 (0 = none or active dislike; 5 = deep sympathy, rooting for them). One line each on what earned it; if any is active antipathy rather than just distance, say so."

**A:** - **Vivienne: 5/5.** Her wholehearted courage and emerging freedom from shame make me root for her fiercely, even as her need to feel chosen leaves her painfully vulnerable.
- **Randi: 4/5.** Her love, loneliness, and care feel real, but sympathy is checked by how expertly she steers Vee while withholding the truth.
- **Pace: 3/5.** His grief and tenderness move me, but his stranger-cold rejection—and immediate reversal once Vee offers her body—badly damage my trust. No active antipathy, but real anger.
