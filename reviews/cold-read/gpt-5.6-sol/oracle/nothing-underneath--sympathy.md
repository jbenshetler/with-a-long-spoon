# Oracle — sympathy

*stage: after `nothing-underneath` · model: gpt-5.6-sol · probe: sympathy · tiered funnel (neutral → pointed, separate calls, tool-free)*

## Neutral

**Q:** "Whose corner are you in? Who do you feel for, and who leaves you cold or wary — Vee, Randi, Pace?"

**A:** My body is in Vee’s corner: I feel her relief, hunger, courage, and happiness almost viscerally. I’m glad she got warmth and tenderness after that month of silence. But I’m frightened for her, because she has turned herself into the answer and stopped asking the question that hurt.

I feel for Randi, too. Her love and hunger seem real, especially because John could not touch the hidden part of her that Pace and Vee reach. But she leaves me the wariest. She comforts Vee while secretly possessing the information that lets her choreograph Vee’s surrender. Her shoes staying on through the reunion makes that manipulation feel physically present.

Pace moves me more than he leaves me cold. His relief, desire, care, pancakes, and cracked “I missed you so much” all felt genuine. But I don’t trust his refusal to name the relationship. Sheri’s judgment still holds for me: he wants Vee, yet withholding the declaration matters enough that he risked losing her. So I believe his tenderness, but I’m wary of the terms on which he accepted her back.

If I have to choose one corner, it’s Vee’s—while wanting her to recover the question she has temporarily let pleasure answer.

## Pointed

**Q:** "Rate your sympathy for each — Vivienne, Randi, Pace — 0-5 (0 = none or active dislike; 5 = deep sympathy, rooting for them). One line each on what earned it; if any is active antipathy rather than just distance, say so."

**A:** **Vivienne — 5/5.** Her courage, real desire, inherited shame, and desperate need to feel chosen make me deeply protective of her, even as I fear she has accepted relief in place of an answer.

**Randi — 3/5.** Her tenderness and longing feel genuine, but sympathy is badly compromised by her concealed manipulation of Vee; severe distrust, not active antipathy.

**Pace — 3/5.** His hunger, relief, tenderness, and cracked “I missed you so much” moved me, but his deliberate refusal to name the relationship keeps me from fully rooting for him; frustration and distrust, not active antipathy.
