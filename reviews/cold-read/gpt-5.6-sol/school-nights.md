- Vee returns to her dorm room after eleven at night with the warmth of Pace’s car and the previous evening still on her.
- Cassie keeps the dorm window cracked two inches because of the building’s cooking heat; cold air meets Vee at the door.
- Cassie is folded into a bean bag with a book against her knees. She initially does not look up, then places the book facedown.
- Cassie says Vee is “doing the streetlight thing again” and calls her “a public utility.”
- Vee spins her desk chair around, sits backward, and rests her arms along its top.
- Vee describes the shape of her recent weeks with Pace rather than one particular night.
- Pace cooks something different each time, never from a box; Vee cannot name many of the dishes.
- Pace watches Vee eat and notices things before she does.
- Pace made baked tomatoes with breadcrumbs, cheese, and fresh herbs.
- Vee reached for another tomato before swallowing the previous bite; it slid from her fork and landed on the side of her breast, burning her.
- While recounting the burn, Vee unconsciously touches the spot.
- Pace pressed a cold cloth to the burn before Vee finished beginning to swear, bending over her gently.
- Cassie infers from the burn’s location that Vee was not wearing much.
- Pace fussed over the small burn for days.
- Pace’s house now stays warm enough that Vee does not think about how much clothing she has on.
- Cassie listens until Vee talks herself out, then observes that Vee does not come home on school nights.
- Reviewing the uncounted week, Vee realizes her own bed was empty more nights than not, though she says it is not every night.
- Cassie says Vee has had boyfriends before but has never reacted this way to one.
- Vee describes her former boyfriends as “fine,” “nice enough,” and “a good enough time.”
- Vee cannot find a word for Pace beyond “fine”; she gestures at his cooking, watching, and complete attention until the room seems dark around her.
- Cassie observes Vee running out of words.
- Cassie asks, “Do you love him.”
- Vee replies, “Maybe. I think so,” then says, “It’s all so fast.”
- Cassie neither challenges nor confirms Vee’s answer. She returns to her book and tells Vee to sleep because she has class.
