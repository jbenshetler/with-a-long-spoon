Randi enters from a door Vee was not watching, finds her, and sits on Vee’s aisle side. She greets Vee with her whole face lit, touches her forearm, praises her for having “the right pen,” and jokes that she will copy from her. Randi offers Cassie a polished introductory bit; Cassie gives only her name. Vee repairs the stalled rhythm by saying Cassie uses four words a day, and Randi responds with undiminished delight.

During the lecture Vee is distracted by Randi’s scent, movement, shoulder, breath, touch, and request for help. She enjoys becoming “the one who knows things” for Randi but misses material she normally enjoys. The professor ends by introducing “latent variables,” factors that cannot be measured directly and must be inferred.

Randi declares that she needs coffee and invites both women to Chi Latte, saying, “I’m not asking, I’ve decided.” Vee’s yes arrives fully formed, though she hides most of its size. Cassie declines because she has a noon lab. She says “Goodbye, Vee” with odd finality after an unreadable look; Vee smooths over the wrongness and follows Randi.

Chi Latte is a yellow-and-white café identified by a Greek-letter pun, **Χ ΛΑΤΕ**. Vee has passed it many times but never entered alone because it felt like an uninvited party. Entering with Randi makes the joke legible and makes Vee feel admitted. Spoons ring throughout the room as people stir sweetness into their drinks.

Vee surveys the fashionable women inside. Her mother’s voice labels the scantily dressed women “floozies,” but Vee looks at their bodies with interest and knows her own lush athletic body would look better in their clothes. She deliberately conceals herself in a cardigan because she has internalized warnings about what people say and what attention a nice girl should invite.

Randi quiets Vee’s social comparisons simply by being herself. She buys Vee a lavender oat-milk latte and takes her to the high window table Vee associates with belonging. Being seated there also makes Vee feel bodily displayed. Randi reveals she has a single sorority room, which tells Vee that she is near the top of the house.

They talk for an hour. Vee later cannot reconstruct the words, only the shape: Randi is funny, tells a parking-ticket story, asks questions and wants the answers, discusses a guy, and gives a flattering remark that enters Vee “like a key in a lock.” Vee keeps the feeling rather than the transcript—the feeling of being clever, lit up, and chosen. It is the best hour she has had in months.

Randi leaves for a meeting, hugs Vee with a hand between her shoulder blades, holds her upper arms, says “Love you, girl,” and promises “See you later.” Vee interprets this not as a vague social hope but as a factual promise that Randi has already decided there will be a later. She walks home the long way, warm and in no hurry.

**Running memory — how I feel**

- **Randi:** Strong attraction, sympathy, and increasing alarm. Her desire for Vee appears genuinely joyful, and her warmth toward Cassie suggests generosity rather than simple targeting. Yet her polish, touch, praise, commanding invitations, and casual intimacy have enormous force on Vee. Randi may sincerely mean every warm gesture while still failing to understand how completely Vee receives it.
- **Pace:** Highly attractive for his patience, attentiveness, sexual generosity, tenderness, and delight in Randi’s agency; increasingly unsettling because he treats bodily response as truth, asks at unfair moments, accepts defensive framing to enable his plan, and withholds purpose. He has initiated a pursuit whose emotional effect on Vee is already much larger than Vee knows.
- **Vee:** Intense warmth, attraction, sympathy, and protectiveness. She is perceptive, socially fluent, physically confident in some ways, ashamed and concealed in others, and starved for the brighter life Randi seems to offer. Her attraction to Randi is unmistakable, but she does not examine it directly. Her eagerness and loss of verbal detail make her vulnerability painful.
- **Cassie:** Immediate trust and curiosity. Her plain speech and refusal to perform are refreshing. Her “Goodbye” feels like a warning, judgment, or recognition, though its meaning is unknown.
- **Meg:** Mild warmth; so far she seems easygoing and incidental, but she witnessed the first contact.
- **Brooke:** Actively unsettling. Her questions may contain concern, but her warmth, status awareness, and effortless control feel dangerous to Randi.
- **Sloane:** Mild discomfort; status-responsive, watchful, with adaptable laughter.
- **Kinsey:** Less threatening but insecure and eager to align.
- **Randi’s mother:** Unease and dislike because of restriction, judgment, and denied childhood pleading.
- **Vee’s mother:** Strong unease and dislike because her shaming voice now lives inside Vee, policing clothing, desire, display, and “nice girl” behavior.
- **Overall mood:** Bright, sweet, romantic, and ominous. Vee’s happiness is infectious, and Randi’s attention may be sincere, but the initial deception has already become emotionally consequential. Vee is freely saying yes to exactly what she wants while lacking the hidden context that gives those yeses another meaning.
