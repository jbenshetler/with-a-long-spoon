I feel warm and sad at once here. After the almost operatic pleasure of Pace’s house, this ordinary room could have felt like deprivation. Instead it feels like another kind of home. Vee has missed the draft. I love that.

But she lies to Cassie almost immediately, and the lie hurts precisely because their ease is so trustworthy. “We talked,” she says, when the reconciliation was wordless; then she invents the shrunken sweater and dyed pants to hide that she deliberately arrived naked under her coat and remained naked by choice. Cassie understands that Vee is editing her life and chooses not to force the door. I trust Cassie completely; I’m less worried that she believes the lie than that Vee now reflexively lies to the safest person she has.
