That opening took me apart. It is also, let's be honest, the most beautifully executed piece of manipulation I've watched on a page in a while, and I was turned on and slightly appalled at the same time, which I think is where this book wants me.

And then "Only in a threesome. With you." I actually sat back. What she does there is the whole character in one line: she doesn't retract the want — she can't, her body said it twice and he heard — she *relaunders* it. Not a woman who hungers for a woman, which is unsurvivable; a girlfriend offering her man a gift, which is merely sexy. **Titles.** "The Pointing Game" told me a little in advance — I knew a game was coming — but what it gained on arrival is the joke inside it: the pointing is the camouflage, the *picking* is the real transaction, and the game's name lets both of them pretend it's play. "A Polite Invitation" lands hard after this chapter: the invitation to Vee has already begun, and it began as a hand on her arm and an apology she didn't owe. No tagline was supplied in my packet.
