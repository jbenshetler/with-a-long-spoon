### Impression
- **Vee**: deep sympathy and real delight. The fight made her more human, not less: jealous, cruel for a minute, then brave enough to make an honest apology. Watching her find her own desire (the dance, the stage, the bet, the hallway) is joyful. But her new hunger was primed in a room with Randi, and she still can't see the two-person machine around her. My fear for her has deepened even as she flourishes. I worry about the drift from Cassie.
- **Pace**: trust in his decency has grown. The bartender's testimony, his silence about the bar even to Randi, the juice, "wonderful," "always beautiful," the way he withdraws at "Don't" all count for him. He is also endearing (air guitar). Unease remains: he takes Randi's reports of Vee's body to bed and is excited by them, he told Sheri "Not yet" as if Vee's finding out is something to be scheduled, and he doesn't see how much Randi steers. Strong pull.
- **Randi**: charged, magnetic, untrustworthy, and sympathetic all at once. Her rescue of Vee was real kindness, and she truly wants her ("I wanted to keep her"). But she engineered the timing, embellished ("She cried"), carried the bra and the thigh straight into Pace's bed, and fed Vee a spanking story that may be invented to see what Vee would do. I watch her most closely of anyone.
- **Cassie**: full trust. The steady friend being quietly left behind; still showing up, holding the phone, buying the sandwich.
- **Sheri**: I like her. Blunt, loyal to Pace, sees the complication coming. Faint wariness only because a blonde at the bar said cruel things and she was in the room; no evidence ties her to it.
- **Brooke / the sorority**: wary; a watching institution with a folder.
