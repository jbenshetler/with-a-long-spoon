## Reader reaction

That ending left me with my stomach on the floor. The whole last third of this chapter is an absolute masterclass in dramatic irony and emotional devastation, and watching Pace miss every single cue while I had to watch Randi shatter in real time was almost unbearable.

Right after *Above Him* — where Pace gave Vee the top bathroom drawer and compressed his whole ordered life into the bottom one — Randi goes into that exact bathroom. We hear the door, the tap, and then *"for a while after that he heard nothing at all."* And at the very end of the chapter, Pace sees Vee's top drawer standing open an inch and tells himself *"The house was old and shifted each autumn."* No, it didn't! Randi opened that top drawer. She saw the green scrunchies, the dish of pins, the face cream, the permanent space he cleared for Vee at the very top of his life. And then she went straight into the bedroom to get an undershirt from the second drawer and found Vee's first-night green satin panties hidden at the bottom like a relic. Watching her come out clutching those panties, refusing to let him touch her or give to her, riding him in desperate silence with Vee's underwear pressed between her palm and his heart until she breaks into a high, thin sound and sobs on his chest — my heart broke for her. And Pace thinks she's just aroused by pressing Vee between them and maybe a little sad she hasn't given him a keepsake! He scrapes two ruined eggs into the trash and thinks *"He would be hungry long before lunch."* The blindness of this man!

And yet, before the morning broke her, the night on the couch was scorching and deeply moving in a completely different way. The erotic charge when Randi gets up on her knees facing the back of the couch and narrates kissing Vee while Pace takes her from behind is a straight 3 — peak heat, filthy and psychologically loaded. When she clenches around him on the word *"mouth,"* when she insists *"I'm doing it for you... it's work"* and he says gently into her neck, *"You don't have to do it for me,"* and she comes on *"Oh, but I'm doing the work"* — that worked on me completely. Because she is lying to herself just like she did in Chapter 3 ("Only in a threesome. With you"), and he sees right through it and gives her permission to want Vee for herself. And when she finally drops her head onto the wood and says, *"God, I want her,"* and then catches herself and tacks on *"For us,"* and lies in his arms admitting *"You see more of me than anybody ever has... It makes me feel real. And some nights it frightens me"* — that was the most vulnerable, unmasked Randi has ever been with him. I felt a huge pull toward them both right there.

Which makes the morning hurt ten times worse. Just when Randi admits how frightening it is to feel real with him, she walks into the bathroom and discovers that while she's been playing the cool architect of a threesome, Vee has quietly moved into the top drawer and into his heart. Also, what a revelation about the pointing game in September: Pace admits to himself here that *"He had already chosen; the pointing was only to find out whether they had chosen the same."* And we learn Randi lied to Vee in *One Bite*? Or wait — here she tells Pace that in Monday stats she leaned over and told Vee *"she'd come to class still full of you."* Did that actually happen, or is she spinning it for him the way she said Vee cried in Chapter 24? Either way, his insistence on *"She consents — every time — or I'm done"* shows his conscience is still trying to operate, even while he's completely blind to the fact that Vee can't consent to a game she doesn't know she's in, and blind to the fact that Randi is drowning right in front of him.

I had zero reader friction here; every line earned its weight. Against the cover board — **WITH A LONG SPOON · BOOK TWO — A WARM RECEPTION** — the "warm reception" is starting to curdle into something agonizing: every warm space Pace makes for Vee is a cold blade to Randi.

Next, I both want and dread seeing Randi after she walks out that door with her makeup back on ("the face" restored) and her coffee untouched. She has Vee on Tuesday and Thursday nights in Anthro now ("Just us"), and Saturdays at brunch, and she knows about the drawer and the panties.
