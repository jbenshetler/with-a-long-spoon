## Reader reaction

**Felt read**

Oh, thank God. Forty-nine chapters in and I got the reunion, and it delivered — not just the sex, the *shape* of it: she stopped asking with words, which is exactly what Chapter 44 broke on ("Words are cheap. Believe what I do"), and answered him in his own language. She did a *deed*. And it worked instantly — "the wall was simply gone, taken down all at once by what she'd done." That's the fight resolved on his terms, and I felt the relief in my chest before I noticed the irony of it.

The mirror scene is the erotic peak of the book so far for me, and interestingly it's Vee alone. Watching her rehearse his hands on her own body — "doing what his did," "as he made her wait" — and then *stopping*, "denied by no one but herself... It was not hers to finish" — that stopped my breath. "It left her feeling, of all things, powerful" is the whole novel's argument about her in one line, and I believed it. The car scene climbing on top of it, then the cop lights — my stomach genuinely dropped with hers. The false alarm was almost cruel, in the good way; it wound the doorstep scene even tighter.

The doorstep itself: "he looked at her like a starving man, and he looked at her like a man just told he is forgiven, and the two were one look" — that's gorgeous and it earns weeks of his silence. His voice cracking over the pancakes ("I missed you so much") landed harder on me than the sex. And "Vee" — he says her name, the name that wasn't enough in December, and now it *is* enough, and she laughs-and-cries and lets it be. Notice he still didn't say the word. She notices too — "no room in her for wanting anything he hadn't given" — *tonight*. That word "tonight" is doing quiet work and I clocked it.

But here's the thing sitting under all my gladness, and the text put it there: every step of this was **Randi's plan**, executed in **Randi's heels**. "Doing the exact thing Randi had told her to do." Randi's voice literally comes and went under the shower. Vee's most autonomous, bravest act — "she had done this, come out of hiding and driven through the dark" — is also the most steered thing she's ever done, and she experiences it as pure self-authorship. The tagline is breathing down the page: this yes was freely given, and it was manufactured. And Pace never asks how she knew to come, never clocks the heels. Does he guess Randi's hand? I couldn't tell, and that gap itches.

Kayla was a delight — "wearing not one stitch of the underwear Kayla was mourning" made me laugh out loud. Cassie's "Hmm" is a perfect last note. Friction: almost none. Maybe "milking at him" tipped a shade pulpier than the book's register, and "which was the truth of them tonight" is a hair of authorial summary — but I'm quibbling.

The title: "Nothing Underneath" is the coat, plainly — but after reading, it's also her: no armor, no bra of pretense, nothing held in reserve, "the one card with nothing smaller behind it." It gave the plan away in advance (I already knew the plan from ch.48, so no harm), but it recolors as *her* condition, not the coat's. *With a Long Spoon* — sup with the devil. Randi fed her this night with a very long spoon, and Vee ate it as her own cooking.

What I dread next: this happiness has three unexploded charges under it — the threesome plan, the bench, and Randi's love. My guess from here: Randi collects. She'll want into this warmth she just repaired, and Vee will owe her everything.
