## Reader reaction

**Felt read**

This is the chapter I've been braced for since Randi pressed the heels into her hands, and it delivered — I read it fast, heart going, and then went back to savor. What surprised me is how much of it belongs to Vee *alone*. The mirror sequence is the best erotic writing in the book so far, and it's masturbation-as-declaration: "She turned from the glass unfinished on purpose, and it was harder than anything the day had asked of her yet, and it left her feeling, of all things, powerful." That's the girl from the flats-to-church childhood taking possession of the body she spent years apologizing for — "finally use it for the one thing it had always been for." Her mother's word "came up out of old habit and found nothing to hold to." After forty-eight chapters of shame lit into wanting, this is shame simply *failing to attach*, and I felt the click of it as a reader — this is what the whole book has been building in her.

The heat peaks three times and never goes clinical: the mirror, the car (the cop scare is a perfectly-tuned jolt — "license and registration with these hands" made me laugh out loud in the middle of arousal, which is a rare trick), and the doorway. The doorway itself: "he looked at her like a starving man, and he looked at her like a man just told he is forgiven, and the two were one look" — that's the emotional payload of the reunion in one sentence, and it made my chest hurt. He goes to his knees *on the porch, in January*. Acts, not words — and then the word he does give her is "I missed you so much," voice cracking. Still not the word she asked for in December. I noticed that. She noticed it too, I think — "tonight there was no room in her for wanting anything he hadn't given" is happiness, but it's also a very careful sentence. The want is deferred, not dissolved.

Kayla's laundry monologue while Vee stands there bare under wool "smiling and dying by inches" is comic relief that earns its place. The pancakes are pure food-as-eros again — "made and not poured" — and Cassie's one-eyebrow "Hmm" is a perfect landing.

What keeps this from being uncomplicated joy: every beat of this triumph is Randi's. The plan, the coat, the heels, even the interior voice at the mirror is "Randi's warm certainty" — "doing the exact thing Randi had told her to do." Vee experiences this as her own bravest act, and it *is* brave, and it also ran on rails someone else laid. "Every yes was freely given. That was the trap" has never sat so exactly on a chapter. And "Those stay on" — Pace telling her to keep Randi's heels on — gave me a small chill I can't fully justify. Does he know whose they are? The text doesn't say. I'm watching it.

The title gave the game away only as much as chapter 48 already had — "Nothing Underneath" is the coat, obviously, but by the end it recolors: nothing underneath the plan either, no hedge, "one card with nothing smaller behind it." No suspicion of anyone new; my old double-vision on Randi holds, intensified because her fingerprints are on every inch of this happiness.

What I want next: I dread the debrief — Vee will tell Randi everything, and Randi will receive her own architecture back as news. Guess from here: Randi's next move takes them toward the three of them in a room, and February's dance hall is where the wires start showing.
