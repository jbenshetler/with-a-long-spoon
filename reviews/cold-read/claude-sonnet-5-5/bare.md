This one landed as a quiet ache, and it's the first chapter in a while where nothing is withheld from Vee and everything is withheld from her. She cancels on him, and Randi cancels on her. Both answers are kind, and both leave her alone in a cold room.

**Vee.** I feel tender toward her, and a little frightened for her. The pull is in "Of course. Study hard. I'll be here." She reads it twice and finds nothing in it that needs anything from her. She keeps testing him by withdrawing, and he passes by being gracious, which is the one result that tells her nothing. The 2:40 a.m. sequence got to me most. She puts his shirt on bare, notices "the heat in the shirt was her own, given back," then takes it off and chooses the cold. That's a precise, unsentimental piece of self-knowledge. She's still short of the other thing, which is that she was never told she could ask.

**Pace.** My feeling hasn't moved much, but his absence works against him here. "I'll be here" is warm and also costs him nothing. I still can't say whether he's holding something back or simply doesn't have the word. I'm holding that open, as the page does. The "love how good we are together" answer last chapter is still sitting under this one.
