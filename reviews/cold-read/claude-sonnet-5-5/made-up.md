## Reader reaction

The chapter felt like a held breath. It's the warmest I've felt toward Vee in a while. She's doing something for herself, and she isn't bracing against anyone.

**Vee** is the whole chapter, and I'm with her. Halloween is her excuse, "Tonight they're costumes", but the real thing is that she stages her own reveal. The dress was built for a stage and a murmur and then nobody who made it saw it. So she gives him the walk the room got. "You missed the debut. This is the walk the room got." That line made me melt. She isn't being fed, dressed or coached this time. She chooses the order, the heels, the pause, and she puts her hand out flat to stop him standing. That's her first real authorship in the book. The blush "went down and down, and she did not cover one inch of it" is the opposite of the induction scene, where she hid the slit with her hand at her hip.

**Pace** is quiet and good on the page. "His hand came partway up and he put it away again, and the putting-away cost him something she could see." His answer, "The other is for the world. This is just for me", is lovely and also slightly possessive. It lands warm, and I let it. He still asks nothing. I trust him here, more than I did when he was running a bet in the previous chapter.

**Erotic charge** is real but slow and mostly seen. It peaks in the second hallway, where she walks ten steps in heels and nothing else with "the house's warmth moving over every part of her at once." The heat comes from looking and restraint, and I felt it. It stays clean and consenting, and she's the engine. The first costume is nearly as charged as the second. It slackens a little in the setup, with a lot of dress logistics: when the bag was zipped, where the heels sat, "after, and only after, her bet was paid."

**Friction.** The stage-managing sentence "He didn't ask, and she loved that he didn't pry" feels like the narration reassuring me. "A man being handed something and taking his time about the taking" is a bit pat. I also noticed what's absent. Randi is nowhere in this night, and the heels "Randi had knelt to buckle" are the only trace of her. They're the pivot of the costume, Randi's choice completing the thing for Pace. Vee doesn't notice the irony and the page doesn't underline it. I felt a small chill. I'm not calling it suspicion, just that she's performing for Pace in objects Randi chose. The "made-up" of it, a costume built to be looked at, has an edge I can't name yet.

**Trust and suspicion.** I don't suspect Pace or Vee in this chapter. The unease is only structural. I know things Vee doesn't about Randi and Pace, and this tenderness sits on top of that. The page hasn't added anything new to it tonight.

**Titles.** "Made-Up" fits the chapter and doubles. She puts on makeup, makes up costumes, and the dress was made for her. It also means invented, pretend, and that gets quietly sharper. Is the night's honesty also a costume? "Which do you like better?" in a chapter called Made-Up makes her a little unsure which one is the real her. *With a Long Spoon* and *A Warm Reception* both read differently now. The reception is warm indeed, but the spoon implies you shouldn't sup with something. I'm following that thread, and the warmth makes me more wary of it, not less. The tagline isn't in my packet, so I can't weigh it.

**Expect and want.** I want him to finally touch her, and I expect the chapter to go there. A guess: Randi surfaces soon, and Vee's confidence will be tested by learning who has been shaping it.
