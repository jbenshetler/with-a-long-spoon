## Reader reaction

Across was a quiet, bright chapter after a heavy run, and it landed softly on me. After the bare-under-the-coat reunion and the pancakes, I expected more heat. I got a waffle brunch.

**Felt read**

I like Vee a lot here, and I feel protective of her. The chapter is almost entirely her performing "it worked." She tells Kayla and Meg the flashing story and lets Kayla's shriek and sock monologue run, so she "never had to say one word about the drive." That detail made me wince. She hands over the comic version of Friday and keeps the rest: the fight, the month of silence, and the unanswered question *what am I to you?* She offers the version that sits on the table "looking exactly true." Meg's bacon-and-Matrix story about making it right "in their language" lands a little ironically. Vee apologized with her body and Pace never gave her the word, and no one at the table knows that.

The swoon is Vee's contentment. She is paying for the mimosas out of Christmas money and doesn't "do any arithmetic at all." The sun porch is full of butter and light. The three-way check fight is warm, with everyone refusing to let anyone cover anyone. And she ends it with "the middle of it all the whole time," which made me glad for her. I can feel her building a world where Pace is a person her friends will meet. His wanting to meet them is genuinely sweet.

I'm uneasy only in small ways, and the page earned them. "He's handling it... his treat, all of it" is a pleasant phrase, but Pace is arranging the room where her people meet. Randi will be at that table, and Vee still doesn't know the two of them already know each other. I know what's at that dinner and she doesn't. Meg's "That's a big apology" is the one flicker of an outside eye. It passes. Vee still hasn't named the tongue-seeking-salt thing from the last chapter, and she leaves it out here too.

Erotic charge: nearly none, apart from a flash of the doorway story. The chapter doesn't need it. It's a breather, and I took it as one. Romance is there only as warmth at the edges.

Kayla is pure comic chorus, and she's fun. Meg is level and a little perceptive. I wanted more of Cassie, who is missing and who Pace asked to include.

**Titles:** "Across" is oblique to me. It could mean the table across from her, or crossing between her two worlds, her friends on one side and Pace and Randi on the other. It also hints at the dinner's seating, with people facing each other. The volume title, *A Place at the Table*, now reads loud. This chapter is literally a table, and Wednesday is the big one, so the title pays off. *With a Long Spoon* still promises danger in sharing a table with someone you shouldn't, and I can feel that coming.
