## Reader reaction

I'm reading this as a warm, slightly aching chapter. It's the first in a while where Vee is simply happy, and I know things she doesn't.

**Felt read.** The opening got me. The week apart, the door flung open, her feet leaving the ground, "a bright stupid sound she'd have died of anywhere else." The line that stayed with me is "She hadn't known a man could do that. She hadn't known a man would want to." Vee is at her most lovable here, with the salmon, the warm tomatoes, the shirt, the wine. And then she says "Peter" into his sleeping ear, and I felt a small hot tenderness. She doesn't know that he never answers her.

The kitchen scene made my stomach drop in a good way. Vee chatters happily about Randi: "She's a new friend. I walked right into her." I know that collision was staged. She mentions the accent, "she must have lived in Europe", and the kiss that's "French, I guess". Pace mimes the cheek kisses and then his grin "left, and under it was the look she knew from his bed." He says almost nothing. He hears his two women described to each other by one of them, and she kisses him and gets him hard on it. That is the hottest beat for me, and it carries a small chill. I'm unsure what he feels. The page keeps it ajar, and I'm leaving it ajar. His silence is consistent, though. He also says nothing about the bathroom counter, and she reads that as "he never said."

I feel for her. She decides "She loved him" and counts the ways he's said it without saying it. I want it to be true, and I distrust that a little. Her version of him is built from what he does, with no words to check it against. The swim sharpens this: "whether the man she was turning over was Pace or was somebody she had built out of wanting him." It lasts four strokes, and then the wall comes. That's the best moment in the chapter. She brushes up against the doubt and swims away from it. The pool is quieter and more honest than the brunch before it. It gives her a body that wants nothing from her.

Cassie is the steady note again: "You're a public utility," "You don't come home school nights," and "Do you love him." Vee answers "maybe" when the answer is plainly yes, and she can't say why. I'd guess she doesn't want to say it aloud to someone who might say nothing back. I trust Cassie completely.

Cassie says Randi "isn't a fan", and that's the only place the chapter shows any suspicion. I also notice that Vee puts her time with Randi into the "girl stuff" bucket here. She leaves out the brunch confessions, and she mentions "your name might come up." The chapter says nothing about her shame or the bathroom stall.

**Friction.** The chapter is quiet and domestic, with little plot. The food paragraph, the eating-more-and-bras-tighter note, and the "after" aside are a touch tell-y. "There could be an *after*" is slightly muddy. The sex is told in retrospect and summary, so the erotic charge is real but lower than in the previous chapter. The Cassie scene retells material we've mostly had.

**Titles.** "Not in Your Bed" puzzles me. It might point to Pace's bed and the counter that isn't hers, or to how Randi is kept out of the bed Vee sleeps in. It might also be Pace's unspoken rule. I can't tell yet. Given Randi's absence, it probably recolors: someone is "not in" this bed, though everything about her is in the room. "With a Long Spoon" suggests supping with the devil and keeping one's distance, and "A Warm Reception" is welcoming on its face. The warmth here feels earned and possibly dangerous.

**Next.** I dread the moment Vee finds out she was chosen. I want to see Pace and Randi in a room after this. I'm guessing Randi will hear about the "I love him" and react badly.
