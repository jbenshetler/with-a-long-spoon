# Cold read — Bare

*scene: scenes/bare.md · read after: under-the-rug*

## Reader reaction

This is short and almost entirely interior, and it landed on me as a held breath. No dialogue, no Pace on the page, no Randi — just Vee awake at the top of the bunk with his shirt, and the whole chapter is her working the shirt like a problem she can't solve with her hands.

The move that got me is the reversal on the shirt. The whole book she's used that flannel as a way to have him close — she re-scents it, she sleeps in it. Here she finally *sees through her own trick*: "the heat in the shirt was her own, given back. He wasn't in it." That's her figuring out, physically, in the dark, that the comfort was a loop she was running on herself. "It had promised him close and there was no one close." That's the loneliest line in the book so far and it's earned — I've watched her build this exact dependency, so watching her catch herself doing it hurt in the right way.

I noticed the layering she does with the two shirts — his over her own sleep shirt, worn "for the hall," so Cassie's look can't land — then she strips both, drops hers, puts his straight on her skin "the way she wore it when no one could see." Body before mind: she's chasing the real thing, skin contact, no barrier. And it still fails. Then the ending, where she takes it off entirely and lies *bare in the cold that's worse* and stays in it — that's the title. "Bare." She's choosing the worse, truer thing over the warm lie of the smell. I read it as her first small act of not-patching-the-model: instead of soothing herself back to sleep with him, she lies in the actual cold of what she doesn't have. And she leaves the shirt "in a heap where a dropped thing lands," not folded back on the pillow — a tiny mutiny. That detail did a lot without saying anything.

The phone beat is good and quiet: thumb over the empty field, "waited for the words," and there are none — because any message would come back "small and warm, and would leave her exactly here." She's understood that the thing she needs can't be texted out of him. That tracks perfectly with the previous chapter's namelessness.

**Trust vs. suspicion.** Nothing new to be suspicious *of* here — Pace is offstage, described only as sleeping easy, "a man with nothing grinding in him." But that line is quietly damning from where I sit: I know (from the bike-ride chapter) he's working two women, so "nothing grinding in him" reads to me as her innocence, not his innocence. The text doesn't announce it. It just lets "he'd gone down easy" sit next to her not-sleeping, and the asymmetry is the whole point. That's the cold underneath, and it's invisible from inside her POV, which is exactly the register I've come to trust the book to hold.

**Erotic charge.** Not an erotic chapter, and it doesn't try to be — the nakedness is the opposite of erotic, it's exposure and cold. That felt right. I wouldn't want heat here.

**Friction.** Very little. The prose leans hard on repetition-and-accumulation — "not the big one and not the small one," "the way she wore it when no one could see" (used twice), the piling of "the things he made with his hands / the rooms he kept warm / the plate he set down." It's incantatory and mostly works, but there's a point in the long shirt-sniffing paragraph — "she could have gone through it and named every part" — where I felt the author's hand pressing the theme a hair too deliberately, restating the "everything he does except the one thing" idea I already fully have from last chapter. It's the one place the chapter tells me something the earlier image already showed. But it's a small thing and the ending recovers it completely.

Cassie is here only as breathing and the two-inch window, and that's enough — she's the steady baseline Vee is failing to match. I still trust her most.

**What I want next.** I want this small mutiny — the dropped shirt, lying in the cold — to be a hinge, not just a bad night she smooths over by morning. *Guess from here:* I doubt it is; I expect her to put the shirt back on tomorrow and go to him, because that's her pattern, and this night will just be a private crack she tells no one about — maybe not even Randi, or maybe she tells Randi and Randi files it. I'm bracing for the induction/threesome machinery to keep closing while Vee has these solitary clarities that go nowhere. The dread is that she's now *conscious* of the emptiness at the center and still can't move.

## Carry-forward state

**What happened.** A wakeful night, Vee alone up in the bunk, Cassie asleep below, window cracked two inches. She almost texts Pace, realizes no message could get her the thing she needs (any answer would come "small and warm" and leave her where she is), and puts the phone down. She strips off her own sleep shirt and wears his flannel bare against her skin to feel him closer — and instead catches herself: the warmth in the shirt is *her own* body heat given back, he isn't in it, he's across town sleeping easy "with nothing grinding in him." She takes the shirt off entirely, lies naked in the cold "that was worse," and stays in it, leaving his shirt dropped in a heap on the mattress instead of back on the pillow. Doesn't sleep; the window goes grey.

**Who's who.**
- **Vee / Vivienne** — in love, can't name what they are; here she consciously sees through her own self-soothing (the shirt-warmth is hers, not his) and chooses to lie bare in the cold rather than fake his closeness. First small refusal-to-patch.
- **Pace / Peter** — offstage; only "sleeping easy, nothing grinding in him." From my outside knowledge (two women) that ease reads as guilt she can't see.
- **Randi (Miranda)** — confidante/harvester. Absent this chapter.
- **Cassie** — roommate/moral floor; here just steady breathing and the two-inch window. Still the one I trust.
- **Sheri** — Pace's blunt riding friend (from earlier).

**How I feel.** Golden-on-top, cold-underneath, and this chapter was almost all the cold — quiet, aching, no arousal, no menace on the surface. I love Vee more for the small mutiny and dread that it changes nothing. The gap between his easy sleep and her sleepless bareness is the whole book in miniature.

**Open questions.** Will the dropped shirt / the bare-in-the-cold night be a turning point or just a private crack she smooths over by morning? Still: why never the word (tied to the two-women plan?); what she does with what she can't name; the scar, right-hand-only eating, sealed family, the weight loss, the nude photo, induction/threesome timing.
