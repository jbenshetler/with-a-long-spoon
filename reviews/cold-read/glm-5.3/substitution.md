The title did give it away before I read — or rather, the *recent chapters* did: I read "Substitution" knowing what the substitution was for. Euler's formula — two quantities that struggle alone, "put together, at right angles, and they turn into a single thing that's suddenly plain." He is teaching her the shape of the arrangement she's entering. The dread and the flutter sit in the same chest.

What I dread: the moment Vee finds out the collision was staged. What I want: more of her voice — "Your tart's holding up" undid me a little. I want her to have this and I want her to survive it.
