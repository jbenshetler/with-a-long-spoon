That beat landed hard, because she's right and the page knows it: Vee was pointed at, chosen, steered, and never asked — not really, not about *this*. I don't think less of her for it — I think the page doesn't want me to — but I've stopped being able to read her as only the generous one.

Pace wakes to cold fingers that smell of soap and doesn't know what I know. That gap is going to keep me up. The house expects her now. And the induction is a week from Tuesday.
