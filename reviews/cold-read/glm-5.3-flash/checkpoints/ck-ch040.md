### Impression
Pace frightens me a little and disarms me completely. He is patient in a way that reads as both genuine tenderness and total control — he built the bench, he built the dress, he built the opening, and he never once lies to Vee; he simply arranges the truth until she walks into it. I trust that he wants her; I am not sure yet what the wanting costs her, or what it costs anyone when Randi's plan comes due. Randi I find magnetic and genuinely alarming — every warm thing she does is double-edged, and the kisses are no longer ambiguous, and Vee still can't see it, and I want to shake her and also understand why she can't. Vee herself I am entirely gone over; her hunger is the realest thing on the page, and every time she eats, or walks tall in the heels, or says "I wanted it and I took it," something in me cheers. Cassie is the one I'd want in my corner — she sees everything and says nothing, and I don't know yet whether that will save Vee or just break Cassie's heart. No reason yet to doubt anyone's underlying care — Pace's and Randi's care for Vee is not in question on the page, only its architecture. The trap and the love are the same shape, and I can't yet tell which one is holding the other up.
