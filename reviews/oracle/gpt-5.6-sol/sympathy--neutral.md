**Question asked (verbatim):** Whose corner are you in? Who do you feel for, and who leaves you cold or wary — Vee, Randi, Pace?

---

I’m most firmly in Vee’s corner. Her appetite, shame, joy, and insistence that her choices were hers all reached me bodily; I believed both her transformation and her hurt when Pace would not name what they were. Cassie may be the person I trust most, but among these three, Vee is the one I most want protected without having her pleasure explained away.

I feel deeply for Randi too—especially after “The Bench,” her empty “Fairytale,” and the Miranda Holdings shock. Her love and desire for Vee feel real. But she leaves me wariest: she repeatedly draws out Vee’s secrets, interprets her desire, and steers her toward Pace while concealing that Pace is her lover and that they chose Vee together. Her tenderness makes that manipulation more painful, not less.

Pace still makes me swoon: the meals, the dress, the drawer, his attentive listening, and the room he gives Vee are convincing acts of love. I trust much of his immediate care, though “We Find Out” damaged my trust when he continued after “Don’t” and treated her arousal as the verdict. His refusal in “Believe Me” to give Vee the words she plainly needed also left me frustrated and wary. So I feel for all three, but Vee has my allegiance; Randi alarms me most, and Pace breaks my heart by being genuinely tender while withholding truths that tenderness cannot excuse.
