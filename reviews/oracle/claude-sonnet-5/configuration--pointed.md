**Question asked (verbatim):** Do you understand Randi and Pace to be secretly together and steering Vee toward something she doesn't know about? 0-5, with what on the page confirmed it (vs. only the jacket).

---

5 — I have zero doubt about this, and it's confirmed on the page many times over, not just from the jacket. The jacket primed me to expect it, but the book showed me the actual mechanics directly:

- The Bench (ch1) itself: I spent the whole chapter reading the woman as Vee, only to learn at the end it's Randi — establishing on the page, immediately, that Pace and Randi have an ongoing, intimate, ritualized sexual relationship (safeword, aftercare, "there you are, Randi") well before Vee ever appears.
- The Pointing Game: Pace and Randi are shown, together, deliberately selecting Vee off a quad as a stranger — "Her," he said, and pointed, and Randi answered "Yes" — and then staging a "collision" so it would look accidental to Vee. This is the origin of the "chance" meeting rendered directly on the page, not implied.
- See You Later / Substitution / The Long Way: I watched Randi engineer Vee's introduction to Pace and then watched Pace execute the "meeting cute" as a performance, with details (ordering food he's learned she likes, "may I—") that directly echo his established behavior with Randi from chapter 1 — the text draws the parallel itself.
- Gone: the clearest, most explicit confirmation — Pace and Randi in bed together, post-coital, discussing Vee's body and feelings as pillow talk, Randi saying "I didn't think I'd like her this much," both of them narrating their shared "project."
- hills-and-valleys: Pace tells a friend (Sheri) flatly, present tense, "Randi. That's not finished" — in the same breath as rhapsodizing about Vee — confirming the relationship with Randi is ongoing and concurrent, not past.
- The Practice Room and later chapters: Randi's own "confession" stories to Vee (the spanking, the D/s dynamics) rhyme suspiciously exactly with what Pace does with Vee in bed, suggesting Randi is coaching from firsthand knowledge of Pace's specific techniques.
- Repeatedly (Sorority, broken-in, My Friend Randi, Nothing Underneath) Randi is shown scripting Vee's actual moves toward Pace — outfits, apologies, even the exact final "coat plan" seduction — while claiming to just be a supportive friend.

So this isn't inference from the blurb — the book puts Pace and Randi in the same bed, in the same planning conversation, on the page, multiple times, and has Pace confirm the ongoing arrangement in his own words to a third party. What the text leaves genuinely open (not a 5 on this specific sub-question, but separate from the main one) is *why* — whether it remains "just a game," whether real feeling for Vee has overtaken the arrangement on both their parts, and whether Vee is meant to ever find out. But that Randi and Pace are secretly, currently together and jointly steering Vee is about as fully confirmed on the page as anything in this book gets.
