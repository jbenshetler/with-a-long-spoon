**Question asked (verbatim):** As you read, did you feel like someone watching these people from the outside, or like someone being drawn in with them? Was there anyone you kept at a distance the whole way?

---

I felt drawn in with them far more than held outside. The book kept making me feel the desire first in my body, then letting the unease arrive afterward: Randi’s “please” on the bench, Vee’s froyo and dinner and dress, Pace’s drawer, the coat with nothing underneath. I was often ahead of Vee intellectually because I knew about Randi and Pace, but emotionally I kept falling for the same warmth she did.

The person I kept most at a distance was Pace, but not because I found him cold. Almost the opposite: I trusted his hands, his food, his restraint, his care in scene after scene, and that made the hidden arrangement harder to forgive. I never fully let him become innocent. Randi I did not keep at a distance in the same way; I distrusted her more openly, but I was also constantly pulled toward her, especially once her own loneliness and wanting became visible. Cassie was the one I never held away. I trusted her from very early on.
