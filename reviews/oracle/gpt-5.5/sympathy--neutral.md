# Oracle (grounded) — sympathy · neutral

*model: gpt-5.5 · probe: sympathy · tier: neutral · stage: end (whole book) · battery: oracle-battery.json*

**Question asked (verbatim):** Whose corner are you in? Who do you feel for, and who leaves you cold or wary — Vee, Randi, Pace?

---

I’m most in Vee’s corner. I feel for her bodily: her hunger, shame, brightness, need to be named, and the way she keeps choosing with real agency inside a situation she doesn’t fully understand. I loved watching her become more alive, especially through the dress, the induction walk, the drawer, the coat, but the whole time I hurt for how much of herself she gives while still being the one without the map.

I’m also in Randi’s corner, but warily. I believe her feeling for Vee is real, and chapters like “Fairytale” made her loneliness and self-deception ache. But she frightens me because she is so good at naming Vee’s desire, dressing it, steering it, and making manipulation feel like rescue. She is warm and dangerous at once.

Pace leaves me the most divided. In the scene-by-scene body of the book, I often trust him: his care, restraint, food, hands, the dress, the drawer, the juice after spanking, all of that worked on me hard. But I grow wary of him emotionally and structurally: he withholds the word Vee needs, answers adjacent questions, and keeps the hidden arrangement with Randi while Vee falls deeper. He does not leave me cold, but by the end I trust his tenderness more than I trust his honesty.
