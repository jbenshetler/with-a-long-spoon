I also think Pace is still withholding the word and, more seriously, withholding the whole truth of Randi and the original arrangement, so Vee’s trust is beautiful but not fully informed.

Randi is Vee’s confidante, stylist, rescuer, erotic interpreter, and increasingly the person who names Vee’s desires for her. The kisses have become ordinary; Vee reaches for Randi now; Randi’s shoes and voice are literally on Vee when she goes to Pace. I feel Randi genuinely loves or wants Vee, especially after “Fairytale” and “Gone,” but she is also actively managing Vee.

Between Randi and Pace, the secret is still the central rot and charge. They know too much together, talk about Vee behind her back, and are both more emotionally caught than they planned. So the triangle is no longer just a game. Vee is in love or close to it with Pace, desire for Randi is becoming undeniable, Randi is losing control of her “friend” role, and Pace seems to want Vee badly while refusing the full naming and truth that would make the arrangement bearable.
