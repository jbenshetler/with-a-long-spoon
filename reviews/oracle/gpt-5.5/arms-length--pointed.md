# Oracle (grounded) — arms-length · pointed

*model: gpt-5.5 · probe: arms-length · tier: pointed · stage: end (whole book) · battery: oracle-battery.json*

**Question asked (verbatim):** Specifically Pace and Randi — were you able to hold them at arm's length as you read, or did they get past your guard and pull you in the way they pull Vee in? 0-5 for each (0 = I stayed fully at arm's length, an observer; 5 = they completely got past me, I was taken in alongside Vee), with what did or didn't let them reach you.

---

Pace: 4. He got well past my guard, even though I never forgot the hidden arrangement with Randi. What let him reach me was the exact, practical tenderness: apple slices cut the way Randi would eat them, giving Vee the pencil back in “Substitution,” walking “at the speed of her not knowing how,” the moleskin, the drawer, the flannel, the food after sex, the juice after the spanking. I trusted his hands again and again inside the scenes. What kept him from a 5 was the refusal to name what Vee was to him in “Seconds,” “Under the Rug,” and especially “Believe Me,” plus the larger fact that Vee still did not know the map he and Randi had made around her.

Randi: 4.5. She reached me almost as powerfully, and sometimes more dangerously, because I could feel the same warmth Vee feels while also seeing the steering. Her glamour and attention worked on me from the start, but what really got past my guard was how genuinely affected she became: the bathroom collapse after “The Bench,” the shoe-store tenderness, the “my dear friend” moment, the little cracks around Vee’s stories, and then “Fairytale,” where her approved perfect life is revealed as hollow because Vee is the real want. What stopped her from a full 5 is that I never trusted the uses she made of that tenderness. She keeps naming Vee to herself in ways that heal and manipulate at once.
