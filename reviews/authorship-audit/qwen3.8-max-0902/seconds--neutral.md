### POV notes

The POV character is female, so all interiority is naturally hers. However, the candidate tells above survive the defense because they concern *how* the interiority is rendered, not merely that it exists. A male author writing this POV would likely render the emotional processing with more external observation, more explanation of the "rules" rather than the felt collision with them, and would be less likely to locate the emotional reckoning in the laundry room with the dryer as timer, or to render the accumulated "pile" of unresolved moments as a physical space. The tells are in the texture, not the mere fact of female interiority.

### Rationale

The chapter is built almost entirely from interoception and its social consequences. The male character's interiority is minimal and read from outside — we get his hand, his breath, his small sound, but never his thought. The asymmetry is the point, and it is rendered with a specificity and granularity that suggests the author knows this terrain from the inside. The evidence is consistent, multi-layered, and independent across multiple passages.
