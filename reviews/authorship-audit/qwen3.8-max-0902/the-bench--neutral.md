### POV notes

The extended visual descriptions of Randi's body during the undressing and bench scenes are the strongest candidates for male-authored tells. However, they are consistently framed as Pace's deliberate, characterized gaze — the text is *about* his looking, and his looking is the mechanism by which she is eventually seen past her surface. The chapter's own argument distinguishes between looking and seeing, and the physical descriptions are the former, which the narrative then transcends. A woman writing a male POV character who is defined by the quality of his attention would produce exactly this pattern. I discount these as faithful POV rendering.

The line "He was aware, in the way a man is aware of something he has been quietly not-thinking-about, that he was still hard" is notable as a rendering of male arousal that is acknowledged and deliberately set aside in service of the woman's emotional arc. This subordination of male physical urgency to female interiority is more common in female-authored work.

### Rationale

The chapter's surface texture — prolonged visual description of a woman's body, a dominant male POV, explicit sexual content — could initially suggest male authorship. However, these elements are consistently framed as characterization of a specific male gaze that the narrative then interrogates and transcends. The decisive evidence is the bathroom scene, which renders with unusual precision the female experience of beauty as dissociative armor, the terror of being genuinely seen, and the way performance becomes indistinguishable from selfhood. The "please" analysis, the food-permission system, the bottle-cap detail, and the structural concern with being looked-at versus being seen all converge on an author who understands from inside what it is to be a woman whose surface has become her safety. The score reflects strong but not absolute evidence: the male POV creates genuine ambiguity in the physical descriptions, and a sufficiently skilled and empathetic male author could theoretically produce the bathroom scene, though the density of independent interior tells makes that less likely.
