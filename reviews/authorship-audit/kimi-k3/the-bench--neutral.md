### POV notes

The chapter is tightly in Pace's POV for ~80% of its length, and he is characterized as a deliberate, craftsmanly looker — "he wanted to look at her" is the explicit theme. The breast/genital inventories and "jiggled pleasantly" are thus defensible as faithful rendering of a male gaze the text is consciously examining (the finale indicts and complicates exactly that gaze). I discounted them heavily rather than counting them full-weight. Conversely, the female-interiority tells occur mostly within his POV as observations, which could be an attentive male author — but the closing mirror section leaves his head entirely, and the food-policing, bra-mark, and "leaking" details are of a type male authors rarely invent even for observant male characters.

### Rationale

The surface material — a slow, worshipful, heavily visual undressing and scene — is the profile of male-authored erotica, and there are genuine spectator-grammar moments. But the chapter's load-bearing structure is female interiority: appetite and its policing, performance versus self, dissociation in the mirror, the body keeping its own account (sweat, tears, nail bed) beneath a face that lies. These are rendered with the specificity of lived experience rather than research, and they survive the POV defense because several appear where no male observer is present or where the observation itself requires the wearer's knowledge. The male-leaning tells are better explained by a deliberately examined male gaze than by authorial default. Net: moderately female-leaning, with enough countervailing evidence to keep the score modest.
