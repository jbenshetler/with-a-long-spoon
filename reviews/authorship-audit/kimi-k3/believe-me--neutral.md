### POV notes

But the chapter is tightly in Vee's POV, and his opacity *is* the subject — a woman writing a male POV would likely do the same. The sexual content is recounted only as her retrospective evaluation ("it's the best of my life"), never choreographed, so no male-gaze candidate survives scrutiny.

### Rationale

Every load-bearing moment is processed through Vee's interoception — shoulders, jaw, taste, tears felt before known — and the chapter's central wound is precisely the gap between a body treated as "just fine as it is" in private and the shame of an unnamed intimacy in public. There is no external inventorying of the female body anywhere, and the male body exists only as warmth, smell, and hands. This is strongly female-authored signaling on internal evidence alone, held short of the extreme only because a single chapter offers a limited sample and the tells, while consistent, are of one type (interiority) rather than many independent kinds.
