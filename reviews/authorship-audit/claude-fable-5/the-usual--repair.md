## Passage 2 — scenes/the-usual.md:7 ("She was a small thing")

> She was a small thing, five feet of her and every inch in motion even standing still, a round face gone a little sallow under makeup.

**DIAGNOSIS.** I'd half-contest this flag. "A small thing" is live Southern idiom, women write it constantly ("little bitty thing"), and the sentence earns its keep by the end — "every inch in motion even standing still" is affection, not appraisal. What the two models are actually smelling is the *opening move*: the first clause of Pace's first look at a woman he's known two years is a size-and-classification ("small thing… five feet of her"), the appraiser's ledger snapping open before the person arrives. A woman writing this POV would more likely lead with the known quantity — the motion, the dare of the makeup — and let the measurement come in sideways. Note the five-feet figure has a job later ("the single in front of Sheri — all five feet of her"), so it should survive any fix.

**ALTERNATIVES.**

*A — same clauses, reordered so motion leads and size arrives as part of the fondness:*

> Every inch of her in motion even standing still, all five feet of it, a round face gone a little sallow under makeup.

*B — lead with the two-years-known fact of her rather than her dimensions:*

> Sheri was five feet in boots and never once still, a round face gone a little sallow under makeup.

*C — keep "small" but weld it to character instead of category:*

> She was small the way a banty hen is small — five feet of her, none of it ever still — a round face gone a little sallow under makeup.

(C is the riskiest; the simile is in the chapter's country register but adds a flourish the surrounding prose doesn't otherwise indulge.)

**CUT OPTION.** Yes, and here the trim is probably better than any rewrite: deleting the opening clause removes the only flagged words and the sentence stands cleanly, with the measurement still delivered:

> Five feet of her and every inch in motion even standing still, a round face gone a little sallow under makeup.
