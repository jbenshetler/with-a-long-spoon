### POV notes

The mirror scene's male-gaze framing is substantially discounted: the character is deliberately rehearsing being looked at ("She made herself look as he would look"), the breast-cupping is relationally motivated ("her own hands doing what his did"), and the appraisal collapses into doubt ("Were they enough"). A woman writing this POV would plausibly stage exactly this rehearsal. Likewise the heel spec comes at her "first proper look" at borrowed shoes, so the itemizing is partly POV curiosity. The "stripper's body" line is half-defused by its embedding in a body-shame history ("hiding it under long coats... folded small... apologizing for") — the too-muchness framing is female-coded even if the rating is not. These lines still register faintly as tells because the *scenario choices* (mirror display, naked-under-coat, heels-stay-on) belong to a male-fantasy repertoire regardless of who narrates them; but each is executed against the grain of that repertoire.

### Rationale

The chapter's architecture is borrowed from male fantasy — the coat over nothing, the mirror strip-tease, "those stay on" — but nearly every sentence inside that architecture is built interoception-first: cold and satin and ache on skin, wetness with consequences (a stained lining, a shivering aftermath), arousal that is inseparable from shame, forgiveness, and the need to be named. The insider material is dense and independent: makeup-after-crying shorthand, eyeliner as discipline, shoes-on-last ritual, polish maintenance, the mirror verdict qualified by "as no woman ever is about her own," and the Kayla scene's pitch-perfect female social texture. The male-leaning tells are real but each is the *premise*, while the female-leaning tells are the *execution*, and execution is where authorship leaks. A skilled male erotica writer could assemble some of this, which keeps the score off the extreme; but the accumulation of small, unforced, from-the-inside details — none required by the plot — pushes well past balance. Hence a solidly positive score with strong evidence: +30, strength 8.
