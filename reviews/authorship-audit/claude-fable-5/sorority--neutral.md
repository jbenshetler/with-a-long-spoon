### POV notes

The POV is a young woman, so interoception alone can't be scored — a skilled male author writing this POV would also put sensation inside her. What I credited instead is knowledge the POV doesn't require: the failure modes of cheap patent, the tan-goes-with-everything apology shoe, TJMaxx math, polish chosen a week ahead to match silk, size-sharing as intimacy. Those are researchable but dense and unforced here. Conversely, I discounted "the wanting to be *seen* in it as she'd been seen in his mirror" — a woman's pleasure in being looked at, rendered as her own want, is faithful characterization either way and moves nothing. The two male-leaning candidates above only partially survive: the toe-caress swoon is rendered interoceptively and carries relational consequence (her hand on Randi's shoulder, the wordless smile), which is the opposite of spectacle, so it's weak evidence.

### Rationale

The residual caution keeping the score off the ceiling is that a genuinely skilled male author could assemble this from attention and research, and the made-*to*-her frame slightly hedges the picture; but on internal evidence alone, the prose reads strongly female-authored: +35 with substantial, independent tells (strength 8).
