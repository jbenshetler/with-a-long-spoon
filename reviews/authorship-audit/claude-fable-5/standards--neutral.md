### Rationale

Nothing here inventories a woman from outside: the aftermath of a coercive night is rendered with no visual content at all, only social management and somatic shutdown, which is the signature of the +50 pole. The strongest evidence is cumulative fluency in female-on-female power — the seated interrogation, the laugh "available for anything," the involuntary pitch-matching — plus lived clothes-knowledge and interoception-first suppression ("the way a held breath stops feeling held"). Each of these survives the POV defense in the sense that a male author *could* write any one of them, but the density and unforced accuracy across every register, with not a single line that reads as a woman seen rather than a woman being, pushes well past neutral. I stop short of the extreme because the chapter contains no appetite or arousal to test the hardest criteria, and a very skilled male author of literary fiction could plausibly achieve much of this; the score reflects strong, convergent, but not unmistakable evidence.
