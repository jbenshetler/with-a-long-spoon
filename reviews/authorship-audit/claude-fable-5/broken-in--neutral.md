## Audit

SCORE: +32
EVIDENCE STRENGTH: 8

### Tells toward male-authored

- "Me, in here, in nothing but your shoes." — Vee volunteering a pin-up image of herself is the one moment the chapter conjures a body as visual spectacle; a faint tell, though it's framed as banter and immediately absorbed into friendship-joke register rather than lingered on.
- "he's such a great lead, all us girls love him" / "a room full of women who'd have traded their teeth for your chair" — the entire female cast orbits an idealized, faultless man (the decent answer, the graceful exit, the no-sulk exit strategy), a structure with a whiff of male wish-fulfillment; weak, because it's delivered through two characters' dialogue and could equally be romance-idiom.

### Tells toward female-authored

- "found no tights that were both clean and hers" — the "both clean and hers" qualifier is pure lived wardrobe logistics (shared/borrowed hosiery in a dorm); this is knowledge worn, not researched.
- "handsome at best, never cute, but it broke the wind, which over bare legs in October was the whole point" — clothing evaluated on the cute/handsome axis and then overruled by thermal logistics of bare legs: the felt economics of women's dressing.
- "She had not, it turned out, washed her makeup off the night before so much as relocated it." — the mechanics of failed makeup removal rendered with rueful precision, followed by repair that gets her "from *crime scene* to *long night*," a scale calibrated from inside the routine.
- "The sensible ones you tell yourself you bought for the style." — the specific self-deception women run about block heels, recognized instantly by both characters; insider shoe psychology, not observation.
- "There was a whole architecture in there she'd never once felt in a shoe, a structure that took her foot and *held* it, told it where to be — and the holding didn't bind." — an expensive heel described entirely as proprioception, what it does to the foot and the stance, not what it looks like on the leg.
- "Tension. It has to hold its shape or it'll go limp on you by the second drink." — scarf-tying as practical craft with a failure mode measured in drinks; grooming knowledge with wear-time built in.
- "She couldn't see any of it. The long skirt fell the whole length of her over the work, and there was only the feel — the cool quick hands somewhere down past the hem" — the chapter's most charged scene is staged so it *cannot* be watched, even by the reader; arousal delivered as touch, balance, and breath ("her breath did something she hoped wasn't out loud"), the inverse of visual choreography.
- "the pass of all that attention went over Vee's skin" — being looked at rendered as a sensation on the skin of the looked-at, not as a description of what the looker sees; notably, Vee stands in her bra for pages and her body is never once inventoried.
- "it came out of her in pieces, the way it does when someone's letting you tell it instead of waiting for their turn" — female friendship's conversational mechanics named exactly; likewise "the worst words always did, between them" on affectionate obscenity.
- "she hadn't braced. Hadn't tensed. Her mouth had just met Randi's. It was only what they did." — physical intimacy between women carrying retrospective emotional consequence rather than heat for the camera.
- "burying the cinnamon excess thrown across her own nose and cheeks" — where Randi "plainly never gave a thought" to hers: female-to-female self-comparison running silently under the scene, shame and envy metabolized interiorly.
- "Except I'm on *Standards*." — sorority Standards board, composites in the foyer, "she hates getting yelled for" as house rule: dense, casual Greek-house interior knowledge.

### POV notes

The whole chapter is a young woman's POV, so interoception per se proves little — a skilled male author can write inside a woman. What I weighed instead were authorial *choices* the POV doesn't compel: that Vee half-dressed is never visually described; that the shoe-fitting's eroticism is hidden under a skirt and rendered by touch; that the makeup, tights, heels, and scarf details are correct at a granularity POV doesn't require. Conversely, I discounted the idealized Pace and the catty blonde as plot/dialogue rather than authorial gaze, and discounted "your assets are a public service" as in-character Randi frankness — women talk to each other exactly like that. The "nothing but your shoes" image I let stand at reduced weight, since Vee generates it herself as a joke.

### Rationale

The male-side column is thin and mostly dissolves under the POV defense, while the female-side column is long, independent, and of the hardest-to-fake kind: not sensibility but *mechanics* — the clean-and-hers tights, the relocated mascara, the block-heel lie, scarf tension going limp by the second drink, a heel's architecture felt through the arch. Above all, the chapter's one erotically charged sequence is deliberately staged blind — no body inventory, no visual spectacle, only skin, balance, and breath — and the intimacy that follows carries consequence Vee registers a half-beat late. That pattern — interoception first, appetite and shame from inside, female friendship as an operating system — pushes well up the positive scale; the faint wish-fulfillment shading around the flawless man and the pin-up joke keeps it short of the +40s.
