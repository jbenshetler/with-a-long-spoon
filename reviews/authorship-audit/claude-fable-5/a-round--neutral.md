### POV notes

The POV character is a woman, so the symmetric defense applies: could a man have written this female POV faithfully? Several candidate female tells were pressure-tested that way. The nipple and breast-contact material, in isolation, is trope-adjacent and I discounted it accordingly as scenario rather than tell — a skilled male author writes that scene. What survives the defense is *texture*, not scenario: the concealed-gesture observation about bras, the lifetime of managing wetness "out of existence in public," puberty as the end of swimming, the mother's register of shame, hope worn as underwear. Net: strongly female-leaning with one notable male-flavored fault line, hence +30 at evidence strength 8.
