### POV notes

The POV character is a woman, so interoceptive narration alone is not proof — a skilled man could write her from inside. I therefore discounted the general inward stance and weighted only the *technical* details a faithful-POV defense doesn't cover: the bra band as weight-loss gauge, ends-up brushing, sizing "to a guess." Those are knowledge tells, not voice tells. I also considered whether the flawless partner — orgasms first, salmon, dozens of scrunchies, the surrendered drawer — reads as male self-insert wish-fulfillment. It doesn't: he is idealized entirely through acts of accommodation *as received and interpreted by her* ("counted all the ways he'd said it without saying it"), and his body gets one sentence while her inner ledger gets pages. That asymmetry is the opposite of the male-authored pattern, so I did not score him against the female reading, nor for it beyond the tells above.
