### Rationale

The chapter is saturated with interoception: hunger that arrives before it's named, the cold of the room felt at the chin, the social arithmetic of a cropped photograph, the ambivalence of an unanswered message checked twice. The female body appears only as felt and as socially contested (the slit, the heel, the self-scissoring of her own image), never inventoried for the reader's eye. Arousal and longing are inseparable from status, shame, and relational consequence. No male-authored tells survive the POV defense. The prose reads strongly female-authored; the score is high but not maximal because the tells, while numerous, are of one family — interiority and image-management — rather than many independent categories.
