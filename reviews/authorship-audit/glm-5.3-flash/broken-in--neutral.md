### POV notes

The male love interest (Pace) exists almost entirely offstage, so his appeal is mediated through the women's talk — no opportunity for male-gaze inventory of him that would need discounting. The scene of Vee in her bra before Randi could, in lesser hands, become spectacle; here it is explicitly framed as non-event ("it was nothing — it was dress-up, the oldest game there is") and felt through Vee's skin rather than described visually, so it survives the POV defense and lands as a tell rather than a discount. Randi's flirtatious ambiguity ("You'd be my very own *shoe whore*", the goodbye kiss) is characterization of Randi, not authorial.

### Rationale

Nearly every sensory and social detail is routed through Vee's interiority: cold as verdict, clothing as totem and armor, makeup as triage, shoes as architecture. The chapter's central pleasures — dressing, being tended, friend-counsel — are rendered as felt experience with emotional stakes and consequence, the exact signature the scale's positive pole describes. There is no external inventory of any woman's body, no porn-grammar, no anatomical false notes; even the underwear scene is de-spectacularized. The prose's command of wardrobe logistics, sorority-house social coding, and the texture of female friendship is deep and independent. Not a full +50 because the register is polished enough that some tells are stylistic rather than unmistakable, but the weight of evidence is strongly one-directional.
