### POV notes

The chapter is a woman's POV describing sex with a man. A male author could ventriloquize this, but the pleasure is consistently located in what Vee *feels* (sleep, safety, being heard, fed) rather than what the man looks like or performs — no porn-grammar, no external inventory of Vee's body by the narration, and the one nude image is deliberately withheld from the reader while fully rendered emotionally. The friend's charged reactions are handled through Vee's limited, self-flattering perception ("she went warm with something close to pride"), which discounts any "male author writing women for men" reading — the ambiguity reads as authorial design, not gaze.

### Rationale

Nearly every load-bearing passage renders appetite — sexual, gustatory, relational — from inside the body feeling it, with shame, class caution (menu prices first), and female friendship as the governing textures. The one moment a female body could be displayed, the narration turns the reader's gaze away and keeps all attention on Vee's internal negotiation of exposure. The male partner is characterized almost entirely through felt consequence, not spectacle. These are independent, mutually reinforcing tells across food, sex, money, sleep, and friendship, outweighed by nothing on the other side; the score is high but not maximal because a highly fluent ventriloquism can't be fully ruled out from one chapter.
