### POV notes

Several visual-body lines could be defended as her seeing herself through his adoring eyes ("her as his eyes had her"), which the text explicitly thematizes. But the defense is weaker than usual here because the exteriority persists even in unwatched, private moments of her own body-sensation (the areola "coming up bright," breasts "shining"), where a woman narrating her own arousal would more typically report feeling before appearance. The soreness and tongue passages show the author *can* do interoception — which makes the recurring outside-looking-in framing of the sexual body read as authorial habit, not just faithful POV.

### Rationale

The chapter mixes real interoceptive competence (the "worked soreness," the taste-analysis) with a persistent tendency to render the woman's own body as lit, colored, glistening spectacle and to route her self-worth and satisfaction through being-looked-at, culminating in a photo scene composed by and for the male eye. The strongest tells survive the POV defense because the external, visual framing of arousal appears even in her private sensation, not only when she is being watched. The genuine female-coded moments pull the needle back from the extreme, so I land at a clear but not maximal male-authored reading with fairly high evidence strength.
