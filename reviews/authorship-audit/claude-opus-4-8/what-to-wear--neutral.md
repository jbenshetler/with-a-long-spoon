### POV notes

The POV is a woman (Vee), so the absence of an external female-body gaze is partly expected and cannot be over-weighted; a male author writing a close-female POV could suppress it too. I discounted the pure "no male gaze" observation on those grounds and leaned instead on the positive interiority tells (heart "quick and light and stupid, and she let it"; "climbing the walls"; the counted days as felt shame), which are affirmative female-interior signals rather than mere absences. The lone male figure, Pace, is described almost entirely through consequence and Vee's feeling ("He asks me first... he waits for the real answer"), not anatomized — consistent with the POV but also with the authorial pattern.

### Rationale

There are no male-authored tells: no woman is inventoried from outside, no arousal-as-spectacle, no porn-grammar. The positive signals are numerous and mutually independent — interoception placed first, desire rendered as social/emotional destabilization and self-aware shame, and a finely observed grammar of female friendship (the demanded "middle" of the story, the counted days, naming one's own fluster before the table can). Some of this is enabled by the female POV rather than proving authorship, which caps my confidence and keeps me short of the extreme. But the affirmative from-inside rendering of appetite and embarrassment, not just the absence of a gaze, pushes this clearly into female-authored territory at +34 with solid evidence strength.
