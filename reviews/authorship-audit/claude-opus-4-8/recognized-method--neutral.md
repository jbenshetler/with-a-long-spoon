### Rationale

Add the woman-to-woman transmission of apology-craft and the exact logistics of the single bra and the unmanageable hair, and the internal evidence points well toward female-authored. I hold short of the extreme because the male lead's polished lines and the romance framing introduce a faint idealizing register, but nothing there counterweights the pervasive interoceptive signature.
