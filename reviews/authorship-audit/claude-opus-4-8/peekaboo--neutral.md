### Tells toward male-authored

- "the neckline crossed and dipped and set her chest out frank in the gold light" — Brief moment of the body described as external spectacle, though it's immediately routed back through her own appraising gaze in the mirror rather than a lover's.

### Tells toward female-authored

- "That was what her skin knew before her head did: no slack standing off the waist, none of the give she'd worn her whole life without once naming it. Everything she'd ever worn had been cut for no one in particular." — Interoception-first, and a lived knowledge of how off-the-rack clothes actually fit a body; the revelation is a felt mechanical fact, not a look.
- "At her hips it caught the way good cloth does and water doesn't, and she worked it down with a low shimmy, both hands smoothing it over herself until it dropped the last inch and settled." — The real mechanics of getting a fitted garment over hips; the "shimmy" is dressing-from-inside, not choreographed for a viewer.
- "there was a second softness, a doubled, quiet weight through the bodice and over the hips, oyster-pale where it showed at all. He'd lined it, there and nowhere else: a soft wall set into the only parts of her the dress had decided not to give away" — Practical garment construction (lining as opacity/modesty) understood from the wearer's stake in it.
- "*Floozy.* The word flared up automatic and her face went hot ... somewhere behind her ribs the looking stopped being shame." and "The shame of it went through her hot, the shame of having stripped herself bare on purpose to be looked at, and it crossed into wanting almost before it was shame at all" — Arousal braided with shame and social judgment, rendered from inside; appetite carries consequence rather than being pure visual spectacle.
- "she'd dressed to be lovely and soft to touch, without saying why even to herself: the dark dress, the long-sleeved one, feminine and easy, the one she knew she looked good in, her hair down" — The lived logic of choosing an outfit for how it will make her feel and be received, self-knowledge withheld from herself.
- "the sensible pair that went with everything, the leather gone soft and the heels worn low ... On this they'd have read like an apology" — Specific, worn-in relationship to her own shoes as social signals.

### POV notes

The chapter is a close-third on a woman, so most female-interior material is expected and could in principle be a skilled male author's rendering — I weighted the tells for texture that's hard to fake (the lining logic, the "no slack standing off the waist" fit-knowledge, the shame→wanting braid) rather than mere presence of a female POV. The one external-body line ("set her chest out frank") is discounted as a woman appraising herself in a mirror, consistent with female-authored self-regard, not a male gaze imposed from outside.

### Rationale

The prose consistently privileges interoception, the felt mechanics of clothing, and arousal that carries social weight and shame — the body known "before her head did," the shimmy over the hips, the lining as a modesty wall, the "floozy" flush turning to want. These are numerous, independent, and of the hard-to-fake, from-inside variety the female-authored end of the scale describes; crucially the sole moment of external inventory is folded back into her own mirror-gaze rather than a lover's eye. That combination pushes well positive. I hold short of the extreme because the material lives inside a female POV where such interiority is partly expected, leaving a residual possibility of expert ventriloquism, so I land at a strong but not maximal +39.
