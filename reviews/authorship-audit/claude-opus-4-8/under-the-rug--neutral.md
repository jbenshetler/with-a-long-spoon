### POV notes

The POV is female, so most interiority is expected rather than an authorial tell; I weighted the *manner* of it (consequence-laden, self-suspecting) rather than its mere presence. The "soft full weight of it / stood up for him" beat is the reverse case: it briefly adopts his tactile/visual frame, but it reads the same whether authored by a man or a woman writing a woman being touched, so I discounted its force rather than scoring it hard as a male tell.

### Rationale

The chapter is built almost entirely from the inside out: arousal never arrives as visual spectacle but as breath coming apart, heat pooling, and above all as social and emotional consequence — the withheld word, the fear of saying it into a room that won't say it back, the belonging that "stopped at its edges." The single external-gaze moment is faint and survives a POV defense, so it barely counts. This is a strongly female-authored signal, held short of the extreme only because the tells cluster in one register (interior monologue) rather than spanning many independent domains.
