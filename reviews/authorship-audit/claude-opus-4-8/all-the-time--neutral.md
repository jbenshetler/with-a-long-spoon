### Rationale

Nearly every strong signal points one way and they are independent of each other: arousal is interoceptive and consequential rather than visual; appetite (sexual and literal) is fused with shame and then with self-forgiveness; the female-friendship texture carries unspoken ritual and the specific relief of unedited disclosure; and the chapter's emotional climax is an explicit rejection of being wanted "the other way around" — body first. Because the POV is a woman, interiority is expected characterization, so I discount raw interiority somewhat; but the *reversal of the objectifying gaze* and the seamless welding of hunger, shame, and being-seen survive the POV defense, since a male author rendering this same POV would more often reach for spectacle. That pushes the score well positive without going to the extreme, since no single tell is anatomically decisive on its own.
