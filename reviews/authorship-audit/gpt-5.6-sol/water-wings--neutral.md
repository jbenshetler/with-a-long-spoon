### POV notes

The descriptions of Cassie’s “boxy frame” and Vee having “more of her” could superficially resemble external bodily appraisal, but both are embedded in the women’s practical relationships to their swimsuits and self-presentation. The shouted comments “You won't need floaties with *those*!” and “Legs for *days*” are male-gaze dialogue, not authorial gaze; the narration emphasizes Vee’s vigilance, defensive performance, and relief afterward rather than inviting the reader to share the spectators’ view.

### Rationale

The evidence is unusually consistent and comes from several independent domains: clothing mechanics, performed confidence under scrutiny, bodily vigilance and release, remembered physical competence, and tacit friendship. Crucially, the prose repeatedly privileges how inhabiting a scrutinized female body feels over how that body appears. Although none of these features can establish authorship, their density, specificity, and integration justify a strong female-authored signal, with a small reserve for the possibility of exceptionally attentive cross-gender imaginative writing.
