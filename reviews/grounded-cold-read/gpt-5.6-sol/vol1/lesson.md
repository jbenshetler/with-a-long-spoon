I was so happy on that concrete pad that I almost resented the Rusty Nail for existing. The lesson itself worked on me completely: Pace bringing the battered boom box, dressing for the dancing rather than the cold, putting himself in front of Vee to demonstrate her part, and teaching without making her clumsiness embarrassing. “There’s no sorry… unless you take off a foot” is exactly his kind of gentleness—serious enough to make her feel safe, then funny enough to let her breathe. When her body finally understands his body and he says, “There it is,” I melted. It carries the charge of all their earlier moments of recognition without turning the lesson into a seduction trick.

The dancing is erotic because the physical rules are so exact. “Give me something to push back against.” “Close it—come all the way in.” His hand at her shoulder blade transmitting information before her feet understand: all of that has real heat. The peak for me is not even the closeness but the moment she stops anticipating and gives the frame her weight. She is not limp or overruled; she has a job, resistance matters, and then the lead becomes readable. It feels consensual, collaborative, and intensely intimate.

Then the room gets into her head, and I felt the drop. Her jealousy is painfully believable. Pace is not merely popular there; women brighten at him, hug him with their whole bodies, and one tells Vee he has taught half the room. After Vee has felt uniquely awakened by him, discovering that he has made many women feel capable, graceful, and safe hits exactly where she is tender. “She’d let herself feel like an exception. She was looking at the rule” hurt, even though I did not believe it was true.
