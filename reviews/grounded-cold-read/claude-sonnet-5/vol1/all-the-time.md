# Cold read (grounded) — All the Time

## Reader reaction

**The felt read**

I came into this one already warm from the run of chapters before it — "Made-Up" and "In His Hands" had me fully swooning, and "All the Time" opens on the tail end of that glow, so my first feeling was just pleasure: at the mimosa-rim ritual, at Vee ordering the eggs Benedict instead of the soup and feeling "the illicit lift of it," at how plainly happy she is. I like her so much right now. The girl who used to police her own plate and hide under cardigans is choosing the good thing on the menu and describing her own sex life out loud in a booth without flinching, and when Randi tries to needle her about only sending the cropped photo — "Legs like yours, though — and you only gave him *half*?" — and Vee goes back and sends the whole picture rather than be filed as the girl who chickened out, I felt something like pride in her. That's a real turn from the Vee of the early checkpoint chapters, and it landed as growth, not recklessness — she's choosing to be seen, on her own terms, which is the whole throughline of this book's "may I look" motif finally bending her way.

Pace isn't on the page at all, but he comes through vividly in Vee's telling — the slowness, the "he doesn't just go for the parts you'd think," the never-once-heard-the-music-start detail, the phone that's "for the music, and—" trailing into the picture-taking. It's all lovely and warm and I believe every word of Vee's devotion. And yet I can't un-know what the checkpoint told me: that this same patient, generous, wants-nothing-for-himself-first man is the one who, in bed with Randi, agreed to "take his time" and let Vee "get there on her own." So when Vee marvels that he's both crushingly strong and gentle enough to make her cry, "both, the same night," I hear the tenderness and also feel the old unease stir — not because the page did anything wrong, but because I remember what the page hasn't told her yet.

The real jolt of the chapter, though, is Randi. Everything up to that point is her usual mode — greedy, delighted, egging Vee on, capping things with a joke ("Does he have a brother?"). Then Vee says the thing about being wanted "with the body attached, not the other way around," and Randi just — stops. "Her eyes had gone bright and a little unfixed... there was a catch in her breath she didn't quite smooth over." And then, quietly, wrong-footed: "It doesn't." That's the first time in this whole run I've watched Randi's polish actually slip *in front of Vee* rather than only in the privacy the checkpoint told me about (the bench, the bed with Pace). Vee reads it as her own power — "that a Saturday of hers could do that to a woman like Randi" — and I ached a little on her behalf, because I don't think that's what it is at all. It reads to me like something in Vee's description of being wholly wanted touched a nerve in Randi that isn't about admiration. I don't know what — envy, longing, grief for something she doesn't let herself have with Pace, or want with Vee herself — and I'm glad the text didn't resolve it, because I don't think Randi herself has resolved it. I'm just noting: I felt that crack land on me as real and important, the sharpest moment in the chapter.

The goodbye kiss escalation got me too — Vee is the one who won't let it end this time, leaning up after Randi as she starts to draw back, and *noticing herself do it and deciding she wants it.* That's new. Every earlier kiss in the checkpoint was something Randi initiated and Vee absorbed without naming; this is the first time Vee actively reaches for more, however small, and clocks the choice as her own. It's a quiet but real hinge.

**Swoon / pull:** The confession itself is genuinely hot and genuinely warm at once — I was smiling at "I used to be a person with thoughts" and melted a little at "like my mouth had been asleep since I was born and nobody told me." The egg-yolk moment right alongside it — "this is the best part. The middle, when it goes everywhere" — is a lovely bit of doubling, food and sex running the same current, which the checkpoint had already trained me to watch for and which still worked on me instead of feeling like a trick. The photo scene is charged in its own separate way — two women, one showing, one looking "past a glance, past what a verdict needed" — and I felt that as real desire in the room, not just teasing.

**Trust vs. suspicion:** No new suspicion of anyone's intentions was earned here — nothing Pace does through Vee's report reads as manipulative on its face. My unease is entirely carried over from what I already know (the game, the joint courtship, Sheri's "complicated"), and Randi's crack this chapter deepens that unease into something more specific and more sympathetic toward Randi rather than more damning of her — it reads like Randi is not fully in control of what she feels for Vee, which the checkpoint already told me but which I finally *saw* rather than being told.

**Erotic charge:** Working, but at a simmer rather than a boil — this whole chapter is retrospective narration and a kiss, no scene happening in real time between bodies, so I'd call it a warm 1 rather than a 2, though the intensity of the confession and the nude-photo moment push right up against that line.

**Friction:** The chapter is structurally a near-twin of "One Bite" — brunch booth, mimosas, extracted confession, Vee's body responding, the goodbye kiss — and I felt that repetition a little as I read: haven't we been here before? What earns its keep is the new material layered in (the photo, Randi's crack, the class plan), but if I'm honest, the *shape* of the scene didn't surprise me, only its payload did.

**Titles:** "All the Time" reads to me now as pointing at the sheer duration and constancy of it — "the amount of it," sex that "doesn't stop," the weekend as the only saturated color against gray weekdays — but it also, once I sat with it, has a faint double edge given what I know and Vee doesn't: someone else in this story has also had "all the time," a long time, with the same man, and doesn't get named here at all. I don't think the title gave anything away I didn't already know, but it did recolor itself slightly by the end. I wasn't given a cover tagline in this packet to react to, so I'll leave that blank rather than supply one from memory — I do carry the two title-level facts (A Polite Invitation, With a Long Spoon) as given framing rather than packet content, and against them this chapter reads like the "invitation" being extended further and further into Vee's ordinary life — brunch as much as the bedroom — while the "spoon" quietly stays with the two people stirring it.

**What I want next:** I want to watch Randi's crack again — I want to know if it happens twice, whether Vee starts to register it as something other than pride. I'm braced (not dreading, just alert) for the anthro class becoming another vector for Randi to fold Vee further into her orbit and further away from Cassie, who's now gone unmentioned two chapters running while Randi eats up the weekends. And I want, eventually, the ledger to come due — not because I need a punishment, but because I want to see what happens to Vee's hard-won, joyful hunger once she learns what it was built on top of, if she ever does.
